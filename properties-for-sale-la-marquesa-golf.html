The course

Golf at La Marquesa Golf

La Marquesa opened in 1989 as Quesada Golf Club, designed by Justo Quesada, and is one of the most established courses on the Costa Blanca. The Spanish professional Miguel Ángel Jiménez is an honorary member.

Its best-known hole is the 17th, with an island-style green inspired by the 17th at TPC Sawgrass. You can play 18 or 9 holes, rent clubs and buggies, and residents of Ciudad Quesada can join on special memberships.

Living here

Why buy a property at La Marquesa Golf?

La Marquesa is part of Ciudad Quesada, a large, established town with supermarkets, shops, restaurants and bars open all year. You live by a golf course without being cut off in a remote resort.

The beaches of Guardamar del Segura are about 6 km away, and Alicante-Elche airport is easy to reach along the coast road and the motorway. That mix of golf, services and beaches is what makes the area so popular.

La Marquesa Golf: questions and answers

How much does a property cost near La Marquesa Golf?

Our properties in Ciudad Quesada start at €260,900 for an apartment. Villas with a private pool cost from about €395,000 up to about €1.2 million, depending on size and how close they are to the course.

Where is La Marquesa Golf?

In Ciudad Quesada, in the municipality of Rojales, in the south of the province of Alicante. The beaches of Guardamar del Segura are about 6 km away.

What costs are there on top of the price?

For a new build you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty. For a resale home you pay 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million). Add about 1.5–2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer. We give you the exact figure for the home you like.
