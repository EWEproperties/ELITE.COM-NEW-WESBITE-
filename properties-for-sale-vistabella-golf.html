The course

Golf at Vistabella Golf

The 18-hole course was designed under the direction of Manuel Piñero, a former European Tour player who played in the Ryder Cup. It covers more than 40 hectares of Mediterranean landscape with native trees.

Undulating fairways, well-placed bunkers and lakes reward accuracy more than length, so the course works for both good players and beginners. Lessons, practice areas and a clubhouse are available.

Living here

Why buy a property at Vistabella Golf?

Vistabella has a clubhouse, a restaurant, bars and shops within the resort, and San Miguel de Salinas and Los Montesinos have more shops and services a few minutes away.

The beaches are about 15–20 minutes away by car and Alicante-Elche airport about 35–40 minutes. Prices are lower than in the bigger resorts, which makes it popular for holiday homes.

Questions

Vistabella Golf: questions and answers

How much does a property cost in Vistabella Golf?

Our properties in Vistabella start at €269,900 for an apartment. Terraced and semi-detached houses cost from about €325,000, and villas with a private pool from about €357,000 to €650,000.

Where is Vistabella Golf?

Between San Miguel de Salinas and Los Montesinos, inland from Orihuela Costa. The beaches are about 15–20 minutes by car and Alicante-Elche airport about 35–40 minutes.

What costs are there on top of the price?

For a new build you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty. For a resale home you pay 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million). Add about 1.5–2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer. We give you the exact figure for the home you like.
