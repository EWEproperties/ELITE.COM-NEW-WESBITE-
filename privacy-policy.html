3. Where the data comes from

Almost always from you: when you email, call or message us, sign up for the newsletter or work with us on a purchase or sale. Sometimes we receive your details from another agent, a developer or a lawyer who is working with you on the same transaction.

This website has no forms and no user accounts at the moment. Simply visiting it does not give us your name or contact details.

4. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: while we are helping you, and up to two years after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Contracts and transaction files: for the time limits of legal claims (generally five years under Spanish civil law) and six years for accounting records.
  • Anti-money-laundering documents: ten years, as Law 10/2010 requires.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Server logs: a short time, normally a few weeks.

5. Who we share it with

Only with the people and companies needed for the purposes above:

  • Service providers who work for us under a data processing agreement: our property and client management system (eGO Real Estate), our newsletter service, our email provider and the company that hosts this website.
  • The other parties to a transaction, when you ask us to act: the seller or buyer, developers, notaries, lawyers, banks and the land registry.
  • Public authorities, when the law requires it, such as the tax authorities or the anti-money-laundering service (SEPBLAC).

The property photos on this website are loaded from eGO's image servers, so your browser connects to them and they see your IP address, as with any website you visit. If you write to us on WhatsApp, Meta processes the messages under its own privacy policy.

6. Transfers outside the EU

We try to use providers in the European Economic Area. If a provider handles data outside it, we make sure the transfer is protected by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • see the data we hold about you (access);
  • correct it (rectification) or delete it (erasure);
  • limit how we use it (restriction) or object to its use;
  • receive it in a common electronic format (portability);
  • withdraw your consent, for example to the newsletter, without affecting what we did before.

Write to info@eliteworldestates.com or to our address above. We may ask for proof of identity, and we reply within one month. Some data we have to keep by law even if you ask us to delete it, as explained in section 4.

If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es).

8. Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures: the website uses HTTPS encryption, access to client files is limited to the staff who need it, and our providers are bound by confidentiality and security obligations.

9. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 14.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when we change how we use data, for example if we add a contact form or website statistics. The date of the latest version is shown at the bottom of the page.
