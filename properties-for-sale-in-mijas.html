The city

Living in Mijas

Mijas is a large municipality on the Costa del Sol with about 90,000 residents, between Fuengirola and Marbella. It has two very different sides: Mijas Pueblo, a white village on the mountain with views over the coast, and Mijas Costa, a long stretch of beaches and residential areas by the sea.

La Cala de Mijas is the heart of the coast, with a sandy beach, a promenade and a small town centre with shops and restaurants. Inland lies one of the best golf areas in Spain, with courses such as La Cala, Calanova and El Chaparral close together, and new homes built around them.

Mijas has a large international community, schools and health centres, and life goes on all year. Málaga airport is about 25 minutes away by car, and Fuengirola and Marbella are 10 to 20 minutes away.

Where to buy

The main areas of Mijas

La Cala de Mijas

The beach town of Mijas Costa, with a promenade, shops and restaurants. Apartments close to the sea, many with sea views.

Calanova and La Cala Golf

The golf valley a few minutes inland, with several courses. Apartments, townhouses and villas with golf and sea views.

El Chaparral

A golf course on the border with Marbella, close to the beach. Semi-detached houses and villas next to the course.

Calahonda and Riviera del Sol

Green residential areas on the coast between La Cala and Marbella, with apartments and villas near the beach.

Mijas Pueblo

The white village on the mountain, with narrow streets and views over the coast. Apartments and village houses.

Near Fuengirola

The areas of Mijas next to Fuengirola, with shops and services on foot. Modern apartments for living all year.

Budget

What it costs to buy in Mijas

On top of the price you pay tax and fees. For a new home at €482,400, for example, that is €48,240 VAT and €5,789 stamp duty, plus notary, land registry and legal fees. Taxes on resale homes are different, so always ask us for the exact figure for the home you like.

Questions

Buying property in Mijas: questions and answers

How much does an apartment cost in Mijas?

Our new apartments in Mijas start at €256,600 today. Most apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms we sell cost between €380,000 and €800,000, depending on the views and the distance to the beach. Townhouses cost from about €650,000 and villas up to €1.86 million in Calanova Golf.

Which area of Mijas is best to buy in?

It depends on how you will use the home. La Cala de Mijas suits buyers who want the beach and a small town centre. Calanova, La Cala Golf and El Chaparral are for golfers, and Mijas Pueblo for village life with views.

How far is Mijas from the airport?

Málaga airport is about 25 minutes by car from La Cala de Mijas, and the train from nearby Fuengirola goes straight to the airport.

What costs are there on top of the price?

In Andalusia you pay 10% VAT and 1.2% stamp duty on a new build, and 7% transfer tax on a resale home. Notary, land registry and legal fees add about 1.5–2.5%.

Can foreigners buy property in Mijas?

Yes. Anyone can buy property in Spain. You need a Spanish NIE tax number and usually a Spanish bank account, and we help you with both. See our guide to the NIE number.
