Sea view apartments

What counts as a sea view

We list a home here when its details or title in our system say it has sea views. Some see the sea from the whole terrace, others only from one corner, so always ask us for photos taken from the terrace, or a video call.

Questions

Questions and answers

Is a sea view apartment worth the extra cost?

For many buyers, yes: sea views are the feature that sells and rents most easily on the coast. Check where the view comes from, whether a new building could block it, and how high the floor is.
