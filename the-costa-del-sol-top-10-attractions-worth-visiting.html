What to See on the Costa del Sol: Top 10 Attractions

By Aleksandra Kobus · 19 May 2025 · updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: The Costa del Sol runs for about 150 km along the coast of Málaga province. Its top attractions are in Málaga city (the Moorish Alcazaba, the cathedral, the Picasso Museum and the Atarazanas market), in Benalmádena (the cable car up Calamorro and Puerto Marina), in Fuengirola (Bioparc zoo), in Estepona (the flower-filled old town) and in the east (the Nerja caves and the white village of Frigiliana). All are within about an hour of Málaga airport.

The Costa del Sol is best known for beaches and golf, but it also has Roman, Moorish and modern Spain within a short drive. Málaga has become one of the most interesting cities in the country, and the white villages in the mountains are only half an hour from the sea.

These ten places, from west to east, give a good picture of the coast for a holiday or a scouting trip before buying.

1. The Alcazaba of Málaga

This Moorish fortress-palace in the centre of Málaga was built mainly in the 11th century, with courtyards, fountains and gardens and views over the port. At its foot is a Roman theatre from the 1st century BC, and a path climbs to the 14th-century Gibralfaro castle for the best panorama of the city.

2. Málaga Cathedral

Work on the cathedral began in the 16th century and continued for more than 250 years, which explains its mix of Renaissance and Baroque. One of its towers was never finished, giving it the nickname La Manquita, the "one-armed lady". A rooftop tour gives great views over the old town.

3. Picasso Museum Málaga

Pablo Picasso was born in Málaga in 1881. The museum, opened in 2003 in the 16th-century Buenavista Palace, shows a large collection of his work donated by his family. His birthplace, on Plaza de la Merced a few minutes away, is also a small museum.

4. Atarazanas market

Málaga's central market is housed in a 19th-century iron building that kept the Moorish gate of the old shipyard. Come in the morning for fish, fruit, olives and cured ham, and have fried fish or tapas at one of the bars inside.

5. Benalmádena cable car

The cable car climbs in about 15 minutes to the top of Mount Calamorro (771 m). From the viewpoints you can see the whole coast, and on clear days Gibraltar and the mountains of North Africa. There are walking trails and bird of prey shows at the top.

6. Puerto Marina, Benalmádena

Benalmádena's marina has around 1,000 berths and unusual, colourful architecture with apartments on small islands. It is full of restaurants and bars, and home to the Sea Life aquarium, a good choice for families.

7. Bioparc Fuengirola

A zoo in the centre of Fuengirola where animals from the tropical forests of Africa, Madagascar and Southeast Asia live in spaces that recreate their habitats, without bars. It is compact, shady and ideal for children. See our homes for sale in Fuengirola.

8. Estepona

Estepona is called the "garden of the Costa del Sol": the old town's white streets are decorated with thousands of flower pots, each street in a different colour. Add a long promenade, large murals, a botanical garden and a relaxed marina. See our properties in Estepona.

9. The Nerja caves

Discovered by chance in 1959, the Cueva de Nerja, near the village of Maro, is a huge cave system with giant stalactites and stalagmites and prehistoric paintings. A walkway route takes about 45 minutes, and in summer the caves host a music festival.

10. Frigiliana

A few kilometres above Nerja, Frigiliana is one of the prettiest white villages in Andalusia, with steep cobbled streets, flowers, Moorish history and views to the sea. Come early in the morning or late in the day, when the day-trip crowds have gone.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Costa del Sol best known for?

Beaches, golf and around 300 days of sun a year, but also Málaga's museums and Moorish monuments and the white villages in the mountains.

What is the most beautiful town on the Costa del Sol?

Many visitors pick Frigiliana in the east and Estepona's flower-filled old town in the west. Marbella's old town is also very attractive.

How long does it take to drive the Costa del Sol?

From Nerja to Estepona is about 140 km, around 1 hour 30 minutes on the motorway.

Is the Costa del Sol good for families?

Yes. Bioparc Fuengirola, the Sea Life aquarium in Benalmádena, the cable car and the Nerja caves are all very popular with children.
