Altea in Spain – What Is There to See on the Costa Blanca?

By Lucyna Gacek · 18 April 2025 · updated 24 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: Altea is a town of about 22,000 people on the northern Costa Blanca, 11 km from Benidorm and about an hour from Alicante airport. It is known for its white hilltop old town, the blue-domed church of Nuestra Señora del Consuelo, pebble beaches and quiet coves, and an artistic, relaxed feel. Altea Hills and Mascarat are among the most sought-after areas for villas on the coast.

Altea is one of the prettiest towns on the Costa Blanca. It is calm, yet close to everything, which is why many visitors come back to buy a home here. This is what to see and do, and what life in Altea is like.

Where is Altea?

Altea lies in the province of Alicante, on the bay where the River Algar meets the Mediterranean, between the Serra Gelada and the Sierra de Bernia mountains. It is:

  • about 11 km from Benidorm,
  • about 20 km from Calpe,
  • about 60 km from Alicante and its airport (around an hour by car).

The coastal tram (TRAM line 9) and buses link Altea with Benidorm, Calpe and Dénia.

The old town

Altea is called the white town of the Costa Blanca, and its old town (Casco Antiguo) shows why. It sits on a hill above the sea, with narrow cobbled streets, whitewashed houses, flower-filled balconies and small squares.

At the top stands the church of Nuestra Señora del Consuelo, the symbol of the town. Its blue and white tiled domes, finished in the early 20th century, can be seen from far along the coast. The square in front of it, Plaza de la Iglesia, is lined with cafés.

Walk to the viewpoints around the old town for some of the best views on the coast, such as the Mirador de los Cronistas and the Mirador de la Plaza de la Iglesia.

Beaches and coves

Most of Altea's beaches are made of smooth pebbles, which keeps the water very clear. The best known are:

  • Playa de la Roda, the main beach below the old town, with a promenade, cafés and water sports.
  • Playa de la Olla, quieter, facing the small island of l'Olla.
  • Cap Negret, a calm beach between rocks, good for snorkelling.
  • Cala del Soio and the coves around Mascarat, small and rocky, popular with divers.

The seafront promenade runs from the old town along the bay and is perfect for an evening walk.

Things to do

  • Water sports. Sailing, paddle boarding, kayaking and diving from the Club Náutico and the beaches.
  • Art. Altea has long attracted artists. It is home to the Faculty of Fine Arts of Miguel Hernández University and many small galleries and studios in the old town.
  • Festivals. In August, the Castell de l'Olla fills the bay with fireworks over the island. At the end of September, the Moors and Christians festival brings parades and music to the streets.
  • Nature. Hike in the Serra Gelada Natural Park, which protects cliffs and sea between Altea and Benidorm, or visit the waterfalls and pools of the Fonts de l'Algar, about 20 minutes inland.
  • Day trips. Guadalest, Calpe, Jávea and the vineyards of the Jalón valley are all within easy reach.

Food in Altea

Altea's cooking is based on fresh fish, seafood and rice. Besides paella, try:

  • arròs a banda, rice cooked in a rich fish stock,
  • suquet de peix, a fish and potato stew,
  • arròs al forn, oven-baked rice with pork, chickpeas and sausage,
  • coca, the local flatbread with toppings.

The old town has many small restaurants and terraces with views over the bay, and the seafront is lined with fish restaurants.

Living in Altea

Many people who visit Altea come back to live. The town has an international community, good healthcare nearby and international schools within driving distance. Popular areas include:

  • Altea Hills, a gated estate with large villas and sea views,
  • Mascarat, with the Greenwich marina and modern apartments,
  • Sierra de Altea and the countryside around Altea la Vella, for quiet villas with gardens,
  • the area near the old town and the seafront, for apartments within walking distance of everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is Altea worth visiting?

Yes. Its white old town, blue-domed church, clear-water beaches and relaxed, artistic feel make it one of the most attractive towns on the Costa Blanca.

How far is Altea from Alicante airport?

About 60 km, or around an hour by car.

Does Altea have sandy beaches?

Most beaches in Altea are pebble beaches, which keeps the water very clear. For sand, Albir and Benidorm are a short drive away.

Where are the best areas to buy in Altea?

Altea Hills and Mascarat for luxury villas and sea views, the Sierra de Altea for quiet homes with gardens, and the area around the old town and seafront for apartments.
