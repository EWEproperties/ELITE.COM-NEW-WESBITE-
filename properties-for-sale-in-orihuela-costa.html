The city

Living in Orihuela Costa

Orihuela Costa is the coastal part of the municipality of Orihuela, in the south of the Costa Blanca between Torrevieja and Pilar de la Horadada. It is a string of residential areas along the sea, each with its own beach or cove, such as Punta Prima, Playa Flamenca, La Zenia, Cabo Roig and Campoamor.

It is one of the best places in Spain for golf. Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, Villamartín, Las Ramblas and Campoamor are all within a few minutes of the coast, and many new developments are built right on the courses.

Life is easy here all year, with a large international community, the La Zenia Boulevard shopping centre, restaurants, health centres and international schools. Alicante-Elche airport is about 45 minutes away by car, and Región de Murcia airport about 40 minutes.

Where to buy

The main areas of Orihuela Costa

Las Colinas Golf

A large gated golf and country club in a green valley a few minutes inland, with security and its own beach club. Apartments, penthouses and villas.

Dehesa de Campoamor

The most exclusive part of the coast, with a marina, quiet beaches and pine trees. Large villas with gardens and pools.

Cabo Roig

A small marina and a strip of restaurants above the sea, with coves on both sides. Villas and apartments close to the water.

Punta Prima

The northern end of Orihuela Costa, next to Torrevieja. Modern apartment complexes with pools near the sea.

Playa Flamenca and La Zenia

Sandy coves, the seafront path and the La Zenia Boulevard shopping centre. Apartments and townhouses for everyday life.

Villamartín

A golf resort a few minutes inland, with its own plaza of bars and restaurants. Apartments, duplexes and villas near the course.

What it costs to buy in Orihuela Costa

On top of the price you pay tax and fees. For a new apartment at €625,000, for example, that is €62,500 VAT and €8,750 stamp duty, plus notary, land registry and legal fees. Taxes on resale homes are different, so always ask us for the exact figure for the home you like.

Questions

Buying property in Orihuela Costa: questions and answers

How much does a property cost in Orihuela Costa?

Our apartments in Orihuela Costa start at €255,000 today. Most apartments we sell cost between €255,000 and €640,000, depending on the area and whether they are on a golf resort or near the beach. Villas start at about €500,000 in Villamartín and reach €3.6 million in Campoamor.

Which area of Orihuela Costa is best to buy in?

It depends on how you will use the home. Las Colinas and Villamartín are for golfers. Campoamor and Cabo Roig suit buyers who want a villa near the sea, and Punta Prima, Playa Flamenca and La Zenia suit buyers who want an apartment close to the beach and the shops.

Is Orihuela Costa good for living all year?

Yes. Many people live here all year, with shops, restaurants, health centres and international schools that stay open outside the summer, and a large international community.

How far is Orihuela Costa from the airport?

Alicante-Elche airport is about 45 minutes away by car, and Región de Murcia airport about 40 minutes.

What costs are there on top of the price?

For a new build you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty. For a resale home you pay 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million). Notary, land registry and legal fees add about 1.5–2.5%.

Can foreigners buy property in Orihuela Costa?

Yes. Anyone can buy property in Spain. You need a Spanish NIE tax number and usually a Spanish bank account, and we help you with both. See our guide to the NIE number.
