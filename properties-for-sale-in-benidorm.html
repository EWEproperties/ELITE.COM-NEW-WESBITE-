The city

Living in Benidorm

Benidorm is a seaside town in the north of the Costa Blanca with around 75,000 residents and visitors from all over Europe in every month of the year. Its skyline of tall towers is famous, and it is the reason why so many homes here have a view of the sea.

The town has two long sandy beaches. Levante is the lively one, with the promenade, cafés and Rincón de Loix at its end. Poniente is calmer and longer, with a modern promenade and newer apartment towers. Between them, the old town stands on a small headland with the Balcón del Mediterráneo viewpoint.

Benidorm is busy all year, not only in summer, so shops, restaurants and services stay open and demand from tenants does not stop. Alicante-Elche airport is about 45 minutes away by car, and the TRAM connects the town with Alicante and the villages along the coast.

Where to buy

The main areas of Benidorm

Levante

The best known beach, with the promenade, cafés and nightlife. Apartments in towers close to the sand, many with views along the bay.

Poniente

A longer and calmer beach with a modern promenade, popular with families and people who live here all year. Newer towers with sea views.

Old town

Narrow streets, tapas bars and the Balcón del Mediterráneo viewpoint on the headland between the two beaches. Smaller, older buildings.

Rincón de Loix

The far end of Levante beach, at the foot of the Sierra Helada natural park. Apartments with sea views and a lively holiday atmosphere.

Centre

The streets behind the beaches, with shops and services on foot. Modern apartment buildings, a few minutes' walk from the sea.

Finestrat and Sierra Cortina

The hills just behind Benidorm, next to the theme parks and a golf course. New apartments and villas with pools and views over the town and the sea.

Budget

What it costs to buy in Benidorm

On top of the price you pay tax and fees. For a new apartment at €507,000, for example, that is €50,700 VAT and €7,098 stamp duty, plus notary, land registry and legal fees. Taxes on resale homes are different, so always ask us for the exact figure for the home you like.

Questions

Buying property in Benidorm: questions and answers

How much does an apartment cost in Benidorm?

Our apartments in Benidorm start at €372,450 today. Most apartments with 2 bedrooms and sea views we sell cost between €500,000 and €870,000, depending on the floor, the view and the distance to the beach. A penthouse high up in a tower costs more than €1.2 million. In Finestrat, just behind Benidorm, new apartments start at about €289,000.

Levante or Poniente: which is better?

Levante is livelier, with more cafés, bars and holiday visitors. Poniente is calmer, with a longer beach and newer buildings, and suits people who want to live here for longer periods. Both have homes with sea views.

Is Benidorm a good place to rent out a property?

Benidorm is one of the busiest holiday towns in Spain and has visitors all year, so demand is high in every season. Holiday letting needs a tourist licence in the Valencian register, and many buildings and areas restrict it, so check before you buy. See our guide to the short-term rental rules.

How far is Benidorm from the airport?

Alicante-Elche Miguel Hernández airport is about 45 minutes by car. You can also reach Alicante by TRAM from Benidorm.

What costs are there on top of the price?

For a new build you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty. For a resale home you pay 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million). Notary, land registry and legal fees add about 1.5–2.5%.

Can foreigners buy property in Benidorm?

Yes. Anyone can buy property in Spain. You need a Spanish NIE tax number and usually a Spanish bank account, and we help you with both. See our guide to the NIE number.
