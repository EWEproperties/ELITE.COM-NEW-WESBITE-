The coast

Why buyers choose the Costa Cálida

The Mar Menor is a large salt-water lagoon separated from the Mediterranean by the strip of La Manga. Its water is calm and shallow, which makes it popular with families and for water sports, and towns such as Los Alcázares, San Pedro del Pinatar and Lo Pagán line its shore.

Inland, resorts such as Santa Rosalía, La Serena Golf and Roda offer new homes with communal pools and golf at prices that are often lower than on the Costa Blanca.

The Region of Murcia airport at Corvera is the closest, and Alicante-Elche airport is about an hour away by car.

Questions

Questions and answers

Is the Costa Cálida cheaper than the Costa Blanca?

Often, yes. Our homes on the Costa Cálida start at €151,900 today, and new apartments and villas near the Mar Menor and its golf resorts usually cost less than similar homes on the Costa Blanca.

What taxes do I pay when I buy in Murcia?

On a new build you pay 10% VAT (IVA) and 1.5% stamp duty (AJD). On a resale home you pay 7.75% transfer tax (ITP). Add about 1.5 to 2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer.

Which airport is closest?

The Region of Murcia airport at Corvera, about 30 to 40 minutes from the Mar Menor. Alicante-Elche airport is about an hour away and has more flights.
