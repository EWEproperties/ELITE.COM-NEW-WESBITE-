Location

Located in the province of Alicante in Spain, Calpe offers a stunning blend of natural beauty, historical sites and vibrant attractions. Located approximately 75 kilometres from Alicante Airport, Calpe provides convenient access for travellers from all over the world.

You are sure to be impressed by the magnificent Blue Flag beaches here. Calpe boasts some of the most beautiful sandy coastlines in the region, including the iconic Playa de la Fossa and Playa de la Arenal-Bol. Basking in the sun or enjoying water sports and activities that are an integral part of Calpe's coastal landscape.

Calpe is also adorned with historical monuments and cultural treasures. The distinctive Peñón de Ifach is the symbol of Calpe and offers breathtaking panoramic views. Hike to the top and admire the natural beauty that unfolds before your eyes.

The charming fishing port of Calpe is a must-see. Watch the fishermen bring in their daily catch, visit the fish market and enjoy fresh seafood in the numerous restaurants along the harbour. To learn about the local culture, visit the Museum of Archaeology and Ethnography to delve into the fascinating history of Calpe.

Explore Las Salinas Nature Park, a paradise for migratory birds such as flamingos and storks, or head to Serra Gelada Nature Park to see its rugged cliffs and captivating trails. For hiking enthusiasts, the Sierra de Bernia and Sierra de Oltà offer exciting trails and spectacular views. Immerse yourself in the lively atmosphere of Calpe's local markets, where you can discover fresh produce, handicrafts and traditions. Sample the local cuisine, renowned for its seafood, rice dishes and spicy tapas.
