Elite World Estates presents an exceptional apartment located in one of the most prestigious areas of the northern Costa Blanca – sunny Finestrat. The property is situated within a modern resort-style development designed for those seeking a high standard of living, comfort, and access to premium amenities surrounded by Mediterranean nature.

The apartment is located on the first floor of an elegant building and offers 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. The spacious interior of 88.4 m² has been designed in a modern open-plan style, providing a functional layout and an abundance of natural light. The heart of the property is a bright living room with dining area and kitchen measuring almost 35 m², seamlessly connected to the outdoor space through large panoramic windows.

One of the main highlights of the apartment is its impressive terraces with a total area of more than 47 m². Owners can enjoy a spacious covered terrace of 33.8 m², perfect for relaxing, dining outdoors, or entertaining family and friends. An additional open terrace of 13.4 m² provides the ideal space for sunbathing and enjoying over 300 days of sunshine per year.

The development offers outstanding communal areas designed to the standards of a luxury resort. Residents can enjoy two spectacular cascading swimming pools, relaxation areas, the Friends Pool Bar, a modern gym, sauna, wellness area, coworking space, social club, library, and children's playground. Beautifully landscaped gardens and recreational areas create the atmosphere of a private Mediterranean resort.

The apartment includes a parking space in the underground garage and a private storage room.

The location allows residents to enjoy everything the Costa Blanca has to offer. Within just a few minutes, you can reach Cala de Finestrat beach, Playa de Poniente, golf courses, La Marina Shopping Centre, numerous restaurants, and all the attractions of Benidorm.

Location

Finestrat is one of the most desirable locations on the northern Costa Blanca, situated just a few minutes from Benidorm and approximately 40 km from Alicante International Airport. The town offers a unique combination of mountain scenery, Mediterranean climate, and proximity to the sea.

The historic centre, located on the slopes of Puig Campana mountain, has preserved its traditional Spanish character with colourful houses and charming cobbled streets. Meanwhile, Cala de Finestrat offers a beautiful sandy beach, seafront promenade, restaurants, and all the amenities required for comfortable living and holidays.

The area provides quick access to golf courses, shopping centres, restaurants, and the attractions of Benidorm. Popular theme parks such as Terra Mítica, Terra Natura, Aqualandia, and Mundomar are also nearby.

Thanks to its peaceful atmosphere, beautiful surroundings, and excellent connections, Finestrat is an ideal location for both permanent living and a holiday home on the Costa Blanca.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala de Finestrat, about 4 km by road, around 7 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Balcó de Finestrat, about 3 km, around 5 minutes by car.
Private school: Col·legi Nuestra Señora de los Dolores, about 8 km, around 11 minutes by car.
International school: Elian's British School La Nucía, about 11 km, around 16 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Cala de Benidorm, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Comarcal de la Marina Baixa, about 4 km by road, around 10 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, about 3 km away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 61 km by road, around 45 minutes by car.
