Location

Estepona is conveniently located at a short distance from the Malaga airport, thanks to which it is easily available to travelers. The airport is about 80 kilometers away, which provides a smooth and comfortable journey for visitors.

Estepona has a beautiful old town with cobbled streets, whitewashed houses, intricately painted ceramic tiles (azulejos), flowered windows, patios and terraces. The local architecture seems more Andalusian than in other Costa del Sol.

If we go crazy with the streets of Estepona, we will discover a unique atmosphere that attracts thousands of tourists to this seaside town. Local coloring are drawings on the walls made with the hand of local artists (murals).

One of the unique curiosities we find there is the poetry trail - Ruta de la Poesia. 30 poems on beautiful, decorative boards, placed in the prettiest corners of the city - this is a treat for lovers of literature, especially Spanish. Among the poets honored by the creators of the route was the Polish Nobel Prize winner - Wisława Szymborska - this is a nice Polish accent.

A walk along the poetry trail is really a great opportunity to reach all charming places in Estepon. All the streets we go through are recently renovated and full of flowers. And some of the places are among the most picturesque in the city.

In addition, the city boasts a collection of breathtaking beaches that will delight the lovers of beach and sun seekers. Some of the best beaches in the area are Playa del Cristo, a covered cove with calm waters and a relaxing atmosphere, and Playa de la Council, a vast lane of golden sand offering a wide range of water sports. Playa del Saladillo is another fantastic option, known for its natural beauty and peaceful area.

The nearby Sierra Bermeja mountain range provides excellent conditions for hiking, allowing you to immerse in the delightful natural landscapes and admire the breathtaking panoramic views of the coast. Golf lovers will be delighted with numerous world -class golfing fields in the area where they can play against the background of lush green and azure sky.
