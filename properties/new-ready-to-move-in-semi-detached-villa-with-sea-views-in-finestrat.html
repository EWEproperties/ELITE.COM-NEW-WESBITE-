Location

Finestrat is a charming and picturesque town located in the province of Alicante, in the Valencian Community of Spain. This idyllic destination is known for its stunning mountain views, pristine beaches, and rich cultural heritage, making it the perfect location for a relaxing and memorable holiday.

Nestled on the slopes of the Puig Campana mountain, Finestrat offers breathtaking views of the mountains, coast, and Mediterranean Sea. The town is situated just 8 km from the bustling city of Benidorm and 40 km from Alicante and its international airport, making it easily accessible to visitors from all over the world.

Finestrat is a town with a typical Moorish feel, featuring colorfully painted houses and narrow cobbled streets that are perfect for exploring. The town is also home to Cala Finestrat, a seaside resort located just three kilometers from Benidorm. Here, visitors can enjoy all the necessary facilities, including a vast selection of restaurants, bars, and shops. La Cala beach is a clean strip of golden sand, ideal for children and safe for swimming. There are several lovely restaurants along the promenade, and an arts and crafts market in the afternoon during the summer months.

For those seeking adventure and fun, the area around Finestrat offers plenty of activities for families and couples alike. Nearby, visitors can enjoy theme parks like Terre Mitica and Terra Natura, the Aqualandia water park, and the Mundomar marine and exotic animal park, all of which offer fun for the whole family. If golf is your game, the nearby Real Faula Golf Club offers two excellent golf courses.
