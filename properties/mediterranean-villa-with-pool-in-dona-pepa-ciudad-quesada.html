We are pleased to present a new project of detached villas located in the picturesque town of Ciudad Quesada. The residences have been built for those seeking convenience, comfort and privacy surrounded by nature. Each of them has a swimming pool where you can relax with your loved ones for most of the year.

Your new Spanish home is an independent villa with a special design, characterised by a Mediterranean style combined with modern touches in the architecture. It is located in Doña Pepa - Ciudad Quesada, where all the necessary services are available, close to the beaches of Guardamar and with easy access to airports or cities such as Alicante or Torrevieja.

This property has spacious, high-quality interiors, including four double bedrooms and three full bathrooms, as well as a bright living area thanks to high ceilings and large windows.

Outside, you can enjoy the private solarium or cool off in the private pool before relaxing under the palm trees of the tropical garden. Located in the prestigious Doña Pepa - Ciudad Quesada urbanisation, this villa combines elegance and innovation, offering the ultimate in residential luxury.

Additional features such as central air conditioning or alarms enable a comfortable life without compromising on safety and comfort.

Location

Ciudad Quesada is located on the southern Costa Blanca, just 8 km north of the main town, Torrevieja. The neighbouring towns are Rojales and San Fulgencio. You can reach Ciudad Quesada directly from the nearby airport, where you will find a lot of connections. The city is only a 30-minute drive from Alicante airport, which is well connected to major cities in Europe. Come and experience the best of the southern Costa Blanca in this vibrant city.

Beaches

The city is just a stone's throw away from golden, sandy beaches, including El Moncayo en Guerdanar del Segura, and is surrounded by wonderful nature parks and picturesque landscapes.

Laguna La Mata

Nature and tranquillity lovers love nothing more than to grab their rucksack, put on their trainers or comfortable shoes and set off. In Ciudad Quesada, you can go hiking in amazing places. There are many hiking trails, but one of the most charming for nature lovers and birdwatchers is a tour of the La Mata lagoon.

Golf

Golf enthusiasts can enjoy the prestigious La Marquesa golf club, which offers breathtaking views of the surrounding area. Meanwhile, nature lovers can visit the nearby salt lakes of Torrevieja, which are home to many species of birds. For those who want to .water.

History

The town has a rich history dating back to the early 18th century and has grown into a vibrant community with a unique blend of Spanish, British and other European cultures. In Ciudad Quesada, you can experience the town's rural past in its charming old town, while the modern and cosmopolitan area around the town centre reflects its more recent development.

Beauty and relaxation ritual

Another unmissable plan in Ciudad Quesada is to go to the spa and let someone take care of your well-being for a morning, afternoon or all day. At the Hotel la Laguna spa, you can enjoy the ‘Circuit of the Senses’, which gives you access to a wellness area full of contrasts of heat and cold, a relaxation area and a saltwater jacuzzi, where you can work on all your senses through different areas: sight, smell, taste, touch and hearing. To relieve tension, you can enjoy a bamboo or chocolate massage or take advantage of the anti-stress therapy. You will leave here more relaxed than ever. The spa at the La Laguna hotel has an important advantage: you don't have to be a hotel guest to use its facilities.
