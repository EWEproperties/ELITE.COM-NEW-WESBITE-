Location

Pulpí, the first coastal town in Andalusia, situated on the border between Murcia and Almería, boasts one of the most picturesque and idyllic landscapes in Spain and offers a wealth of reasons to visit. The municipality, situated in the north-eastern part of the province, comprises three distinctly different areas: the coastal zone, an inland basin, and a small chain of hills separating them.

Pulpí Geode

In addition to spectacular beaches and coves, the area boasts a true natural gem which, since opening to the public in 2019, has become a major tourist attraction: the Geode. Located in Mina Rica, in Pilar de Jaravía, it is a gigantic geode approximately eight metres long and two metres high. It is the second largest in the world after the Crystal Cave of Chihuahua in Mexico and the largest of the geodes open to visitors.

Other places to visit

There is much more to see in Pulpí. The town of Pulpí is home to the Church of San Miguel, the second oldest building in the municipality, dating from 1801, and also the tallest, housing a spectacular altar. Also worth noting are the Benzal Picnic and Ethnographic Area and the Apartadero Viewpoint.

Also worth a visit is the Scenic Space, featuring the ‘Pintor Pedro Antonio Martínez’ room, which houses a collection of works by the influential Pulpileño painter, and the ‘Black Room’, the largest of its kind in Europe, housing a spectacular collection of 1,000 luminescent minerals.

Beaches

Almería also boasts a range of spectacular beaches and coves. The 13 beaches in Pulpí stretch along the coastline from north to south: Los Cocedores Beach, Las Palmeras beach, La Carolina beach, Invencible cove, Costa Tranquila cove, Cuartel cove, Tía Antonia cove, Rincón de los Nudos cove, Mar Rabiosa beach, Mar Serena beach, Calypso beach, La Entrevista beach and Los Nardos beach. Four of the five beaches further south have been awarded the Blue Flag for the quality of their facilities and the water, which is crystal clear and excellent across the board. These are Mar Serena, Mar Rabiosa, Calypso and Los Nardos.
