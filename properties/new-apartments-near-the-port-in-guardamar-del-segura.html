Location

Guardamar del Segura is a Mediterranean paradise, a beautiful town located in the province of Alicante, in the

Valencia Region of Spain. Situated right by the sea on the picturesque Costa Blanca, this Mediterranean gem is known for its beautiful beaches, rich and diverse nature and fascinating history. With over 1,800 years of history, Guardamar del Segura has witnessed the passing of civilisations that have left a lasting mark on this wonderful corner of the world. Discover the beauty of its beaches, experience its lush nature and learn about the traditions that have made Guardamar del Segura a truly unique place.

The town boasts a unique and unparalleled natural landscape: the Guardamar Dunes. Located south of the town, the dunes are a protected area that is home to a valuable natural diversity.

This special ecosystem is home to protected species such as the common lizard and the Greek tortoise, and its protection is crucial for the ecological balance of the region. Visitors can explore the dunes by following paths through the native vegetation, providing an opportunity to get in touch with nature.

The Alfonso XIII Park is also worth a visit. This urban park is an oasis of shade and greenery, making it an ideal place to relax or play sports.

The beaches of Guardamar del Segura are the main attraction for visitors who want to enjoy the warm Mediterranean sun. Along the eleven kilometres of coastline, there are several beaches that offer endless opportunities for enjoying the sea and sunbathing. Guardamar Beach, considered the symbol of the area, is famous for its fine golden sand and crystal-clear water. It has been awarded the prestigious Blue Flag, which guarantees high quality and safety for swimmers. La Roqueta Beach, located right next to the marina, is another popular option. Surrounded by impressive dunes, it offers a quieter and less crowded atmosphere for sun seekers who want to relax and admire nature.

The cuisine of Guardamar del Segura is a treat for the palate, where the flavours of the sea combine with the richness of the land. Fresh seafood dishes are a feast for the senses and reflect the region's fishing tradition. “Caldero” is one of the main dishes, a tasty fish soup with rice typical of the area. Local bars and pubs attract visitors with their relaxing and welcoming atmosphere, where they can sample delicious local cocktails and drinks.

Guardamar del Segura is also an ideal destination for lovers of active tourism and outdoor sports. The beaches and sea are the setting for many water activities, such as diving, sailing, kayaking and snorkelling. Hiking and cycling are popular in the region due to the beauty of the surrounding landscapes. Vía Verde del Segura is a former railway line converted into a cycle and walking route that runs through beautiful countryside and allows visitors to explore the area in a sustainable way

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja Babilònia, about 1.7 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP Reis Catòlics (about 600 m) and CEIP Dama de Guardamar (about 900 m).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centre de Salut de Guardamar, about 1.1 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Quirón/USP Hospital San Jaime, about 11 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Masymas, about 100 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 36 km by road, around 25 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 72 km, around 55 minutes by car.
