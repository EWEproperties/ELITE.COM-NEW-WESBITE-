For sale, an exceptional villa set in the green hills above Marbella, combining panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea, privacy and proximity to one of the most renowned destinations on the Costa del Sol. Its elevated position provides peaceful natural surroundings while keeping Marbella town centre and the coastline within easy reach.

The contemporary architecture has been carefully integrated into the surrounding landscape. Large windows fill the interiors with natural light and open the home towards the beautiful views. The spacious living area combines the lounge, dining room and modern kitchen in an open-plan layout with direct access to the terraces.

The residence has been designed to provide a comfortable separation between social and private areas. The bedrooms offer peaceful spaces for relaxation, while the contemporary interiors emphasise the elegant character of the property.

The outdoor areas form a natural extension of the home. Generous terraces provide plenty of space for relaxing, dining outdoors and spending time with family and friends. The surrounding greenery and hillside setting enhance privacy while allowing residents to enjoy the Mediterranean climate throughout the year.

Property owners have access to an extensive range of facilities within the private gated community.

At its heart is an exclusive residents' club featuring an indoor swimming pool, modern gym, jacuzzi and hammam. Residents can also enjoy yoga classes, a sea-view tennis court and dedicated areas for sport and relaxation.

For those working remotely, there is a coworking space with high-speed Wi-Fi, a coffee corner and outdoor working areas.

The community also features Olivos Garden, a shared outdoor area with barbecue facilities and a fireplace, ideal for social gatherings. A local Farmer's Market offers organic produce and regional Andalusian specialities.

Controlled access and 24-hour security provide a high level of privacy and peace of mind. Residents can also benefit from concierge services, including assistance with transport, restaurant reservations and other everyday requirements.

LOCATION

The hillside setting above Marbella offers tranquillity and contact with nature without sacrificing convenient access to the coast. A significant part of the development is dedicated to green areas, while the surrounding landscape provides excellent opportunities for walking, running and cycling.

Marbella town centre, the Old Town, Golden Mile, Puerto Banús and the Mediterranean beaches are all within easy reach. The surrounding area also offers renowned golf courses, restaurants, international schools and all the services required for comfortable year-round living.

An exceptional opportunity for those looking for a villa in Marbella combining panoramic sea views, privacy, natural surroundings and access to an extensive range of exclusive amenities.
