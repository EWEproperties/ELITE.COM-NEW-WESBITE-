Murcia Airport is just 25 km away, while the AP-7 Pista Mediterraneo motorway connects you to Alicante Airport in just 45 minutes. Live in a privileged location and indulge in the Mediterranean lifestyle you have long dreamed of!

Location

About 30 kilometers from the capital of Murcia, in the coastal part of the so-called Campo de Murcia, there is the district of Sucina. It is well connected to the capital Murcia, located 29 km from the city center, and the RM-1 provides a direct connection to San Javier Airport and the beaches of Mar Menor.

One of the most interesting places is La Manga del Mar Menor. You can relax here, enjoying the charms of the sea and the attractions of the marinas. You'll also find vast white beaches like San Pedro del Pinatar, semi-wild coves like those in Cartagena, interesting landscapes like the eroded lands of Mazarrón, and fishing towns like Águilas.

The region is also famous for various properties beneficial to health and beauty. Thanks to the very salty water and mud of the Mar Menor, numerous beaches here are considered a kind of open-air spa. Apart from them, the region boasts real health resorts of very important importance.

Sucina has also gained importance as a "residential tourism" destination due to the presence of several residential golf estates nearby, due to its flat landscape and easy access to the regional airport.

Sucina has a wide range of essential services, including a Cultural Centre, medical center and schools, bank, optician, supermarket, as well as a range of hotel services and shops. Local fiestas are held every October in honor of the Virgen del Rosario, and the city also celebrates Semana Santa (Easter) with processions.
