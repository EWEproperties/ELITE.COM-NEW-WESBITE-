Elite World Estates presents an exceptional collection of modern homes located in one of the most attractive areas of Costa Cálida. This exclusive development consists of 37 elegant quad-style properties situated directly next to a prestigious golf course and only approximately 1 km from the beaches of the Mar Menor lagoon.

The project has been designed for those seeking a comfortable home in sunny Spain that combines modern architecture, privacy, and an excellent location close to the sea and all amenities.

The properties offer a functional layout with 3 spacious bedrooms and 3 or 4 bathrooms. Bright interiors have been designed in a contemporary open-plan style, creating a spacious and comfortable living area. Large windows provide abundant natural light and seamlessly connect the interior with the terraces, garden, and outdoor relaxation area.

The homes are built on plots of approximately 177 m² and offer around 99 m² of built area distributed over two levels. Carefully designed architecture, high-quality materials, and a thoughtful layout make these properties ideal both as permanent residences and holiday homes.

Each villa features a private swimming pool, garden, spacious terraces, and private parking within the plot. An additional outdoor toilet next to the pool area enhances comfort when enjoying the outdoor spaces. Fully equipped bathrooms and electric shutters are included as standard.

One of the main advantages of this development is its location. The properties are situated directly beside a prestigious 18-hole golf course while being only about 1 km from the sandy beaches and promenade of the Mar Menor lagoon. It is the perfect setting for those who value an active lifestyle, nature, and proximity to the sea.

Location

Los Alcázares is a charming coastal town located on the shores of the Mar Menor lagoon in the Region of Murcia, on the sunny Costa Cálida. The town has attracted residents and investors from across Europe for many years thanks to its Mediterranean lifestyle, developed infrastructure, and attractive property prices compared to many resorts on the Costa Blanca and Costa del Sol.

The town offers more than 7 kilometres of sandy beaches and a beautiful promenade running along the Mar Menor. The calm, shallow waters provide ideal conditions for swimming, sailing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and various water sports. With more than 300 days of sunshine per year, residents can enjoy outdoor activities throughout the year.

Los Alcázares provides all the services required for comfortable living, including restaurants, cafés, shops, supermarkets, sports centres, schools, healthcare facilities, and recreational areas. The town retains its authentic Spanish character while offering all the conveniences expected by international residents and property owners.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of renowned golf courses such as La Serena Golf, Roda Golf, and Mar Menor Golf Resort. The historic city of Cartagena, famous for its Roman heritage, port, and cultural attractions, is also nearby.

From an investment perspective, Los Alcázares is currently one of the fastest-growing locations on Costa Cálida. Increasing demand from international buyers, ongoing residential developments, and a strong holiday rental market provide excellent potential for capital appreciation. Murcia International Airport is approximately 30 minutes away, while Alicante Airport can be reached in less than one hour.
