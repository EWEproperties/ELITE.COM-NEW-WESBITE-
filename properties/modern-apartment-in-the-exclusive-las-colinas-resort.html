Modern apartments for sale in the exceptional green surroundings of Las Colinas Golf & Country Club on the Costa Blanca. This boutique development consists of two elegant buildings harmoniously integrated into the natural landscape. The apartments offer 2 or 3 bedrooms, spacious terraces, while selected properties also feature private gardens or large solariums. From the terraces, residents can enjoy open views of the golf course and the surrounding Mediterranean landscape.

The interiors have been designed in a bright, contemporary style. The heart of each apartment is a spacious living room connected to the dining area and open-plan kitchen. Large windows provide plenty of natural light and create a seamless connection between the interior and the terrace.

The apartments feature a modern fitted kitchen with appliances including an induction hob, oven, microwave, refrigerator and dishwasher. Year-round comfort is ensured by ducted air conditioning with a heat pump, while the bathrooms are equipped with electric underfloor heating. The specification also includes built-in wardrobes, double glazing and electric blinds.

One of the main advantages of the development is its beautifully designed communal areas surrounded by Mediterranean greenery. Residents have access to two swimming pools, including one heated pool, allowing the outdoor leisure areas to be enjoyed for much of the year. Each apartment also includes a private parking space and storage room.

The project also incorporates solutions designed to improve comfort and energy efficiency, including an aerothermal system for hot water, photovoltaic panels for the communal areas, heat-recovery ventilation and smart controls for lighting and blinds. The buildings have an Energy Efficiency Certificate A.

Completion: May 2027.

Location

Las Colinas Golf & Country Club is one of the most distinctive locations in the southern Costa Blanca — a private, carefully designed resort set within an approximately 330-hectare valley, surrounded by hills and Mediterranean nature. Access is through an impressive palm-lined canyon, while controlled entry provides residents with privacy, peace and security.

It is an ideal location for those who want to combine a peaceful natural setting with access to high-quality facilities. The resort borders extensive protected natural areas, and its low-density architecture has been designed to blend harmoniously into the landscape. With more than 300 days of sunshine per year, the area is perfect for enjoying terraces, gardens and outdoor activities throughout most of the year.

Residents have access to a wide range of facilities, including a renowned golf course, restaurants, a sports club and fitness centre, tennis and padel courts, sports academies, a minimarket and scenic walking trails. The resort also offers 24-hour security, reinforcing its private and exclusive character.

Another major advantage is the proximity to the sea — the Mediterranean coast is only around 4.5 km away. Alicante Airport can be reached in approximately 50 minutes, while Murcia Airport is around 45 minutes away, making the property an excellent choice both as a second home in Spain and for regular holiday stays.

This is a location for those looking for more than just an apartment on the Costa Blanca — privacy, nature, space, security and a high standard of living, while still remaining close to beaches, services and the region’s main transport connections.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa Barranco Rubio Cala de Campoamor, about 10 km by road, around 16 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio de Educación Infantil y Primaria Número 20, about 6 km, around 15 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio San José de Calasanz, about 21 km, around 25 minutes by car.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 4 km, around 8 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Campoverde Centro de Salud, about 22 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 14 km by road, around 18 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Minimarket, about 200 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 50 km by road, around 35 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 73 km, around 55 minutes by car.
