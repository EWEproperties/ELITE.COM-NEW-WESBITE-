The residence offers approximately 679 m² of built area on a 1,000 m² plot. Every detail has been carefully considered to provide maximum comfort, energy efficiency, and security.

Location

Moraira is one of the most prestigious and desirable destinations on the northern Costa Blanca. Situated between Calpe and Jávea, this charming former fishing village has preserved its authentic Mediterranean character while offering an exclusive lifestyle, elegant residential areas, and an exceptional quality of life. It is a highly sought-after destination for those seeking privacy, tranquillity, and luxury properties overlooking the Mediterranean Sea.

Just a few minutes' drive away are the town's picturesque marina, beautiful sandy beaches, and idyllic coves such as Playa del Portet, Playa de l'Ampolla, and Cala L'Andragó, all renowned for their crystal-clear waters and excellent conditions for swimming, snorkelling, paddleboarding, sailing, and other water sports. Scenic coastal promenades and walking trails offer spectacular viewpoints overlooking the Mediterranean.

Moraira's town centre features an excellent selection of fine restaurants, cafés, boutiques, local shops, and traditional markets while maintaining its peaceful and exclusive atmosphere throughout the year. The surrounding area also offers renowned golf courses, international schools, medical centres, and all the essential amenities required for comfortable year-round living or unforgettable holiday stays.

Excellent connections to Alicante and Valencia airports, together with the proximity to prestigious towns such as Jávea, Benissa, and Calpe, make Moraira one of the most desirable locations on the Costa Blanca. The limited number of new developments, high-quality residential environment, and outstanding natural beauty ensure that purchasing a property here represents not only an exceptional lifestyle opportunity but also a secure long-term investment.
