Location

Torrevieja is a charming town that offers an array of local attractions and things to do. The Torrevieja Salt Lakes are a unique and natural phenomenon located just outside the town of Torrevieja. These lakes are part of a larger saltwater lagoon known as the La Mata-Torrevieja Lagoon and are an important ecological and environmental area. The salt lakes are a stunning sight to behold, with their pink waters caused by the microorganisms living in them. The lakes are surrounded by a unique landscape of salt flats, dunes, and wetlands, which provide a habitat for a diverse range of flora and fauna.

The Museo del Mar y de la Sal is a fascinating museum located in Torrevieja that is dedicated to the history of the town's maritime and salt industries. The museum is housed in a historic building that was once used as a salt storage facility, giving visitors a sense of the area's rich history and culture. The museum features a range of exhibits and displays that showcase the history of Torrevieja's salt industry, which has been an integral part of the town's economy for centuries. Visitors can learn about the process of salt production, the role of salt in local cuisine and culture, and the ways in which the industry has shaped the town's development.

La Mata beach is one of the most popular and picturesque beaches in the Torrevieja area, offering a vast array of amenities and activities for all ages. Located just a few minutes' drive from the town center, La Mata beach offers visitors a wide range of facilities, making it a perfect destination for families, couples, and solo travelers. The beach itself is long and wide, with golden sands that stretch for over two kilometers. The waters are crystal-clear and calm, making it an ideal spot for swimming, sunbathing, and relaxing. The beach is also equipped with showers, toilets, and changing rooms, making it easy for visitors to freshen up after a day in the sun. The beach is also wheelchair accessible, making it an inclusive destination for all.
