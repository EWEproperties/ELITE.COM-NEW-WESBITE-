New 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartments and 3-bedroom penthouses right next to La Serena Golf in Los Alcázares, about 1.5 km from the Mar Menor beaches and within walking distance of the town centre. Ground-floor homes have large terraces and gardens, first-floor homes a balcony terrace, and penthouses up to 108 m² of terraces and solarium. Prices start at €275,000 with a parking space and storage room, and the buyer pays no agency fee.

The home

The 2-bedroom apartments have 73 to 80 m² built, with an open-plan living room and kitchen, 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Ground-floor homes have 55 to 128 m² of terraces and garden; first-floor homes have a 15 to 16 m² terrace. The 3-bedroom penthouses have 94 to 95 m² built plus 94 to 108 m² of terraces and private solarium. Prices include electric shutters in the living room and bedrooms and fully finished bathrooms. Hot water comes from an aerothermal system, and air conditioning is pre-installed. An optional full-equipment pack adds furniture, automatic blinds, air-conditioning units and kitchen appliances.

Prices from (plus 10% VAT): ground floor €275,000, first floor €279,000, penthouse €455,000.

The development

The apartments are built in phases next to the golf course, with a large communal pool and landscaped gardens. Each home comes with a parking space and a storage room.

Planned completion: 2Q 2027 (last home in phase 2) and 2Q–3Q 2028 (phase 3).

The area

Los Alcázares is a seaside town on the Mar Menor, a calm, shallow salt lagoon with warm water and a long promenade, popular for sailing and paddleboarding.

  • Mar Menor beaches: about 1.5 km
  • Town centre, shops and restaurants: walking distance
  • Roda Golf and Mar Menor Golf: about 10 minutes by car
  • Murcia International Airport (Corvera): about 35 minutes by car
  • Alicante Airport: about 1 hour 30 minutes by car

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Punta Brava, about 5 km by road, around 9 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Grupo Escolar Los Alcázares (about 4 km) and I.E.S. ANTONIO MENÁRGUEZ COSTA (about 4 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Los Alcázares, about 5 km away. The nearest hospital is Centro Sociosanitario 'Cristo de los Mineros', about 14 km by road, around 15 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Dialprix, about 3 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 43 km by road, around 30 minutes by car.
