Elite World Estates presents a new and exceptional residential development located directly next to the prestigious La Serena Golf course in sunny Los Alcázares on the Costa Cálida. This unique place has been designed for those seeking harmony, modern comfort, and a Mediterranean lifestyle surrounded by greenery, lakes, and close proximity to the sea.

The modern complex impresses with its elegant architecture, panoramic golf course views, and thoughtfully designed living spaces. The development offers stylish 2- and 3-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms, created with a focus on natural light, spaciousness, and everyday comfort.

The spacious living room connected to a modern open-plan kitchen creates a bright and welcoming interior, perfect both for relaxation and spending time with family and friends. Large windows provide excellent natural light and a seamless connection between the indoor and outdoor living areas.

Ground-floor apartments feature private gardens and spacious terraces, ideal for relaxing outdoors. Apartments on the upper floors offer large balconies with beautiful views of the golf course and surrounding nature, while the exclusive penthouses include private rooftop solariums where you can enjoy the Spanish sunshine all year round.

Residents can enjoy an elegant communal swimming pool, beautifully landscaped green areas, and relaxing spaces designed for peaceful Mediterranean living. Each apartment also comes with a private parking space and a storage room.

The development is located in one of the most attractive areas of Los Alcázares — right next to the renowned golf course and only approximately 1.2–1.7 km from the beautiful sandy beaches of the Mar Menor lagoon. One of the greatest advantages of this location is the possibility to walk to the town center, where you will find restaurants, bars, shops, and all essential daily amenities.

Excellent transport connections provide quick access to the most important destinations in the region. Murcia International Airport is approximately 25 minutes away by car, while Alicante Airport can be reached in around 55 minutes.

This is an excellent opportunity both as a holiday apartment, a rental investment, or a comfortable year-round residence on the sunny Costa Cálida.

Location

Los Alcázares is a charming seaside town located in the Murcia region on the sunny Costa Cálida. The town is known for its pleasant climate, relaxed atmosphere, and direct access to the Mar Menor — the largest saltwater lagoon in Europe, famous for its warm, shallow waters and healthy microclimate throughout the year.

Thanks to excellent road connections with cities such as Cartagena, Murcia, and Alicante, as well as quick access to highways and airports, Los Alcázares is an ideal destination both for permanent living and holiday stays.

The town is famous for its wide sandy beaches stretching along the calm waters of the Mar Menor. It is a perfect place for families with children, water sports enthusiasts, and anyone looking to relax by the sea. Popular beaches such as Playa de Los Narejos and Playa de Las Salinas offer beautiful promenades, charming beach bars, and excellent conditions for sailing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and windsurfing.

Los Alcázares also stands out for its Mediterranean lifestyle and rich gastronomic offer. Numerous restaurants and bars serve fresh seafood, traditional paella, local tapas, and international cuisine. Evening walks along the promenade, cafés overlooking the sea, and the welcoming atmosphere make this town attractive to both residents and visitors all year round.

The town also offers a wide range of attractions and amenities, from boutiques and local shops to sports centers, marinas, and recreational areas. Active lifestyle enthusiasts will appreciate the cycling routes, water sports, and proximity to renowned golf courses such as La Serena Golf, Roda Golf, and Mar Menor Golf Resort.

This is a truly exceptional place where Mediterranean lifestyle, beautiful beaches, and modern infrastructure create the perfect environment for relaxation, investment, and living in Spain.
