Location

Manilva, surrounded by famous Andalusian tourist resorts, has its undeniable advantage that other towns on the Costa del Sol cannot boast of - vineyards. They are densely surrounded by the surrounding hills. Limited wine from Manilva is the quintessence of the richness of these lands and the showcase of the region.

It is located 97 kilometers from Malaga. The town is divided into two parts: a tourist resort by the beach and an urban part with beautiful, white tenement houses.

Manilva has retained the atmosphere and style of an old Spanish village. It is located near the Strait of Gibraltar, which makes it a geographically and culturally rich area. You can enjoy a quiet holiday here, away from the hustle and bustle of big cities. The nearest airport is 41 kilometers from Manilva.

The undeniable attraction of the town and its surroundings are the beaches - some of the best on the Costa del Sol. There are almost 8 kilometers of sandy areas available here. Some of the beaches are rocky and surrounded by coves, while others are wide and sandy. Rocky beaches are located in Punta Chullera, and sandy beaches are located next to Castillo, the port of Puerto del la Duquesa and the town of Sabinillas. In the waters of the surrounding beaches you can swim, dive, fish and enjoy water sports.

In addition to beaches and marinas, Manilva offers tourists an interesting historical and cultural heritage. So you'll find plenty of things to see and do in Manilva. In Manilva you can also see La Duquesa Castle, several churches and archaeological remains exhibited in the Municipal Archaeological Museum.
