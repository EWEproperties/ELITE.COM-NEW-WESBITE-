Location

Monforte del Cid is a municipality in the Valencian Community of Spain. It is located in the interior of Alicante province, in the Medio Vinalopó region.

Monforte del Cid in Alicante is the perfect town for those who enjoy golf in a natural setting with a mild climate, as well as for those seeking small towns rich in history, culture, heritage, and fantastic museums.

In Monforte del Cid, you can start by walking through the old town, which has preserved its medieval layout and houses from the 16th to 20th centuries, with facades decorated with typical Manises tiles depicting religious scenes.

Highlights include the town hall with its 16th-century arches and coat of arms, the Gothic Church of Nuestra Señora de las Nieves, and the hermitages of San Pascual and San Roque. You'll love strolling and admiring these buildings!

We recommend stopping at the Ibero, the Villa de Monforte del Cid History Museum, where you can admire Iberian-era bull sculptures and even a Roman tombstone. In fact, not far from Monforte del Cid and Novelda is Finca el Campet, where the remains of a large Roman villa have been discovered.

After exploring the city center, the next stop is Orito, a district of Monforte del Cid, a pilgrimage site, home to the 16th-century Church and Monastery of San Pascual and the Virgin of Orito, and the Cueva de Saint Pascual. You can stroll there and admire the incredible views. A fantastic pilgrimage to this site takes place in May!

For leisure, in addition to excellent golf courses, Monforte del Cid offers the opportunity to participate in the wonderful Moorish and Christian festivals held in December. Be sure to sample the excellent Vinalopó grapes in bags, aniseed and liqueurs, also protected by the Regulatory Council, as well as pasta and cakes.
