Elite World Estates presents a modern development located in the prestigious Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort complex in the Murcia region. This unique development is designed for those who dream of comfortable living in Spain, close to the sea, nature, and exceptional amenities.

The project offers 2- and 3-bedroom apartments, as well as elegant penthouses with spacious terraces and solariums. Terraces range from 23.4 m² to over 300 m², allowing residents to fully enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle in the fresh air.

Each property includes an underground parking space and a storage unit. The west-facing apartments offer bright and welcoming spaces, and the upper floors offer beautiful lake views.

The gated community offers residents excellent amenities, including two swimming pools, one of which is heated and open year-round, a children's playground, a barbecue and picnic area, bicycle parking, and laundry facilities.

Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort is one of the most unique complexes on the Costa Cálida. At its heart is a crystalline lagoon with white beaches, palm trees, and beach bars, where you can swim, kayak, or simply relax in a peaceful, holiday atmosphere.

The resort also features green spaces, walking and cycling paths, sports areas, restaurants, a gym, a spa, and numerous relaxation areas. It is the perfect place for both a vacation home and comfortable year-round living.

Location

Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort is located in the Murcia region, approximately 35 km from the city of Murcia and 19 km from La Manga del Mar Menor. The nearest airport, Murcia Regional International Airport, is just 22 km from the resort.

This unique destination on the Costa Cálida redefines the concept of a luxurious Mediterranean lifestyle. Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort is an exclusive five-star complex spanning approximately 700,000 m², designed with comfort, relaxation, and active recreation in mind for the entire family.

The resort offers over 120,000 m² of carefully landscaped green spaces, numerous sports and recreational areas, and over 16,000 Mediterranean trees and plants. The heart of the complex is an impressive, crystal-clear lake with beaches, ideal for swimming, paddleboarding, and kayaking. The entire area is fenced and monitored, with access control and 24-hour security, ensuring residents a sense of security and privacy.

Beach Club

The Beach Club is a unique place designed for relaxation, socializing, and spending time with family and friends. The club offers beautiful views of the lake and sandy beaches, spacious lounge terraces, and play areas for children and adults.

It's the perfect place for morning coffee, lunch, dinner, or an evening glass of wine in a Mediterranean atmosphere. The resort also hosts numerous attractions and activities, such as beach volleyball, kayaking, Adventure Golf, and pétanque tournaments.

Ecology and Recycling

Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort places great importance on ecology and sustainability. A waste separation system is in place throughout the complex, covering paper, glass, plastic, and other recyclable materials.

To protect the environment, the use of paper and single-use plastic is also being reduced, particularly in the Beach Club and sales offices.

Energy Efficiency

The entire resort is equipped with modern, low-energy LED lighting controlled by intelligent systems based on sundials. Energy efficiency is not only about saving money, but also about consciously protecting the environment.
