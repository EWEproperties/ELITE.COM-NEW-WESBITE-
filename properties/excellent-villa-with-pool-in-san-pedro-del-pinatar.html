Location

San Pedro del Pinatar is a small town in the province of Murcia, Spain, located on the shores of the saltwater lagoon Mar Menor. The town has a rich history dating back to the Roman Empire, and traces of Roman salt production facilities are still visible today.

The city has several beautiful beaches, including Playa de la Llana, Playa de Villananitos and Playa de la Torre Derribada. These beaches are ideal for water sports, sunbathing and relaxing. Playa de la Mota is a popular beach among locals, offering calm waters and excellent facilities.

San Pedro del Pinatar is also home to a fascinating natural park, the Salinas y Arenales de San Pedro del Pinatar Regional Park. This natural park offers a unique environment where a saltwater lagoon meets the Mediterranean Sea, creating a diverse ecosystem that supports a wide range of flora and fauna. Visitors can take a guided tour, observe birds and wildlife, and stroll along the park's trails.

There are several attractions in the city that are worth visiting, including the Fish Market, which offers a wide selection of fresh seafood caught on the same day, and the Museum of the Sea and Salt, which presents the city's rich history of salt production. Molino de Quintin is a historic windmill that has been restored and offers an insight into the city's agricultural past.

San Pedro del Pinatar also has many restaurants, cafés and bars where visitors can sample the local cuisine and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere. The town has a lively cultural scene, with several festivals throughout the year, including the San Pedro del Pinatar Carnival and the International Habaneras and Polyphony Festival.
