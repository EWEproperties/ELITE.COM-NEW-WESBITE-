This new development consists of 2 single-storey villas with a private 30 m² swimming pool and parking space on the plot.

These properties have a large garden and a rooftop solarium, allowing you to enjoy the sun all year round.

Each property has 4 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms. The properties have been designed in a modern style with an open-plan concept, including a fully equipped kitchen and living room with dining area.

The development is located in Roldán (Murcia), within the El Alba Mediterranean Resort. It is a gated community with security control, located on a 300,000 m² plot, surrounded by all amenities, sports facilities and close to several golf courses.

The properties will meet the highest standards and will be equipped with:

A 30 m² private swimming pool with a solar shower. Installation prepared for the installation of a heat pump.

Fully furnished kitchen equipped with oven, built-in microwave, integrated dishwasher, refrigerator and extractor hood.

Kitchen island.

LED lighting: inside the property (dining room, kitchen, corridors and bathrooms) and outside.

Wardrobes with drawers.

Fully equipped bathrooms with suspended washbasin cabinet and suspended toilet, mirror with lighting, rain shower and shower screens.

Installation prepared for duct air conditioning.

Summer kitchen with sliding doors in the solarium. Installation prepared for connecting a washing machine and refrigerator.

Solar panel.

Parking on the plot.

The estate will offer a range of amenities, including a dog park, volleyball court, padel court, bowling alley, gym, children's parks, green areas, barbecue areas and a cycle path connecting to the centre of Roldan.

Planned completion date: July 2026.

Location

Roldán is a charming village in the Murcia region of Spain, known for its peaceful rural atmosphere and beautiful natural surroundings. Located near the Mar Menor, Roldán offers easy access to beautiful beaches and golf courses. The village has several amenities, including shops, restaurants, bars and supermarkets. For sports enthusiasts, there are numerous facilities available, such as sports complexes and hiking trails. Roldán combines the best of peaceful rural life with easy access to urban amenities, making it an ideal location for both permanent residents and holidaymakers.
