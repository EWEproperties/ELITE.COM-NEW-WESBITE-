Location

La Mata nestled along the sun-kissed coastline of Spain's Costa Blanca, is a picturesque town that promises a blend of natural beauty, rich history, and modern amenities. Just a stone's throw away from the bustling city of Alicante, La Mata offers a serene escape for both residents and visitors.

One of the major advantages of La Mata is its proximity to Alicante Airport. Located just about 40 kilometers away, the journey between the airport and La Mata can be comfortably covered in under an hour. This makes La Mata an easily accessible destination for international travelers, looking for a tranquil beachside retreat.

La Mata boasts some of the best beaches in the region. The main beach, Playa de La Mata, stretches over 2 kilometers and is known for its golden sands and clear waters. It's a perfect spot for sunbathing, building sandcastles, or indulging in water sports. For those who prefer a quieter atmosphere, Cala del Mojón offers a more secluded beach experience, away from the crowds.

Beyond the beaches, the town has plenty to offer. The La Mata Natural Park is a haven for nature lovers. This protected wetland area is home to diverse flora and fauna, and the saltwater lagoons here are a sight to behold, especially during sunset. For those with a penchant for history, the Torre del Moro, a historic watchtower, offers panoramic views of the coastline and serves as a testament to the town's rich past, having once served as a lookout point against pirate invasions. Another architectural marvel is the Church of the Immaculate Conception, located in the heart of the town, which stands as a beautiful example of Spanish architecture and offers a peaceful place for reflection.

Food enthusiasts will not be disappointed either. The town is dotted with numerous restaurants and tapas bars offering local and international cuisines. A visit to La Mata is incomplete without trying the region's specialty dishes and wines. Additionally, the vibrant local culture comes alive at the weekly Wednesday market, where visitors can find everything from fresh produce to handmade crafts.

In conclusion, La Mata is more than just a beach town. It's a destination that offers a rich tapestry of experiences, from its pristine beaches to its historic landmarks. Whether you're a history buff, a nature enthusiast, or someone simply looking to relax by the beach, La Mata has something to offer for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala del Moro, about 1.8 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Ciudad del Mar, about 600 m.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 22 km, around 24 minutes by car.
International school: Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen, about 13 km, around 16 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Mata, about 2.3 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Quirón/USP Hospital San Jaime, about 3 km by road, around 5 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Aldi, about 400 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 47 km by road, around 35 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 62 km, around 45 minutes by car.
