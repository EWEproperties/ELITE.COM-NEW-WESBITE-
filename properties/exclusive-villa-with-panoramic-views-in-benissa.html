Location

The property is situated in one of the most prestigious areas of Benissa Costa on the northern Costa Blanca. This exclusive residential enclave is renowned for its luxury villas, spectacular Mediterranean views, and peaceful atmosphere, offering residents exceptional privacy and an outstanding quality of life. It is an ideal location for both year-round living and an exclusive holiday retreat.

Just a short distance away are some of the area's most beautiful coves and beaches, including La Fustera, Cala Baladrar, and Cala Advocat, all famous for their crystal-clear waters and unspoiled Mediterranean scenery. Nature lovers and outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the scenic Paseo Ecológico de Benissa, a coastal walking trail that connects some of the most picturesque stretches of the coastline.

Only a few minutes' drive away lies the charming town of Moraira, with its elegant marina, excellent restaurants, boutique shops, and attractive seaside promenade. The vibrant town of Calpe, home to the iconic Peñón de Ifach, beautiful sandy beaches, and a wide range of leisure and dining options, is equally easy to reach.

The surrounding area offers excellent infrastructure for comfortable year-round living. Supermarkets, shops, medical centres, international schools, sports facilities, and renowned golf courses, including Ifach Golf Club, are all within easy reach. Excellent connections to the AP-7 motorway provide convenient access to Alicante and Valencia airports, making this location particularly attractive for international buyers.
