Rojales offers the perfect combination of Mediterranean authenticity and top-class services: a town with real character, inhabited all year round, located just 10 minutes from the beaches of Guardamar del Segura, surrounded by four top-class golf courses (La Marquesa, La Finca, Vistabella and Las Colinas).

All essential services are within walking distance, including Mercadona and Consum supermarkets, pharmacies, a health centre, international schools, banks and a wide selection of restaurants and bars.

Location

Rojales is a picturesque town in the province of Valencia, in south-eastern Spain. Lemon and orange groves surround the town, and the Segura River flows through Rojales. The beaches of Guardamar del Segura are just 7 kilometres from Rojales. With its rich cultural heritage, picturesque landscapes and many attractions, Rojales is a must-visit destination.

Located just a 45-minute drive from Alicante and Murcia airports, Rojales offers convenient access for travellers seeking a wonderful holiday. Upon arrival, immerse yourself in the town's history by exploring the charming historic centre, where traditional architecture and vibrant flowers create an enchanting atmosphere.

Don't miss Cuevas del Rodeo, a remarkable cave complex converted into an art gallery. Admire the intricate murals and sculptures while appreciating the town's thriving artistic community. Nature lovers will enjoy exploring the stunning landscapes of the nearby Parque Natural de la Mata, perfect for walking and bird watching.

When it's time to relax, head to the beautiful Blue Flag beaches of Guardamar del Segura, just a 10-minute drive from the property. Dip your toes in the golden sand, soak up the Mediterranean sun and refresh yourself in the crystal-clear waters.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the presence of La Marquesa Golf and other prestigious courses nearby, offering a fantastic golfing experience in breathtaking surroundings.

After a day of sightseeing, treat yourself to the culinary delights of Rojales. In the many charming restaurants and cafés, you can enjoy traditional Spanish tapas, fresh seafood and local specialities, complemented by regional wines.

In short, Rojales offers a captivating combination of history, natural beauty, magnificent beaches, golfing opportunities and exquisite cuisine. Whether you are looking for a short break or considering making Rojales your permanent home, this charming town offers a truly unforgettable experience.
