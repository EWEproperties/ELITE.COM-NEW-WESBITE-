Location

The development is located in San Pedro del Pinatar, one of the most sought-after destinations on the Costa Cálida, just 600 metres from the sandy beaches of the Mar Menor. It is an ideal location both for year-round living and as a holiday rental investment, thanks to its proximity to the sea, excellent infrastructure, and numerous tourist attractions.

Within walking distance, you will find restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, shops, schools, medical centres, and a marina. The area is renowned for its beautiful seaside promenade, the therapeutic mud baths of the Mar Menor, and extensive walking and cycling routes. Golf enthusiasts and outdoor lovers will also appreciate the easy access to several golf courses, water sports facilities, and the Las Salinas y Arenales de San Pedro del Pinatar Natural Park.

Excellent transport connections provide quick access to Murcia International Airport (approximately 35 minutes) and Alicante International Airport (approximately 50 minutes), making this property an attractive choice for both holiday home buyers and investors.
