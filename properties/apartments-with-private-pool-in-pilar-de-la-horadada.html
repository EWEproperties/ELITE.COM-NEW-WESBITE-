Elite World Estates is proud to present an exclusive boutique development located in one of the most desirable areas of Pilar de la Horadada on the southern Costa Blanca. This is an excellent opportunity for buyers seeking a contemporary home in Spain that combines everyday comfort, high-quality finishes, and an outstanding location close to the Mediterranean beaches and all essential amenities. The intimate nature of the development ensures privacy and a peaceful atmosphere while providing easy access to restaurants, cafés, shops, supermarkets, schools, sports facilities, and recreational areas.

The development consists of just eight modern apartments thoughtfully designed to maximise functionality, comfort, and natural light. Buyers can choose between ground-floor apartments featuring a private garden and swimming pool or top-floor apartments offering a spacious private solarium with their own swimming pool. The carefully planned layouts and open-plan living areas create an inviting environment, ideal for both year-round living and relaxing holidays.

Each apartment features two bedrooms and two stylish contemporary bathrooms. The bright living and dining area flows seamlessly into a fully equipped open-plan kitchen, creating a spacious and practical living space. Large floor-to-ceiling windows allow abundant natural light to fill the interiors while connecting the indoor and outdoor living areas. The high specification includes a fully fitted kitchen with appliances, built-in wardrobes, electric shutters, underfloor heating in the bathrooms, pre-installation for ducted air conditioning, and a private parking space for every property.

The location allows residents to fully embrace the Mediterranean lifestyle. Within just a few minutes you can reach the beautiful sandy beaches of Torre de la Horadada and Mil Palmeras, the marina, the picturesque seaside promenade, and a wide selection of restaurants serving local cuisine. Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the extensive network of walking and cycling routes, nearby golf courses, and the excellent sports facilities available throughout the area. Excellent road connections provide easy access to Alicante and Murcia airports, making this property an ideal choice as a permanent residence, holiday home, or investment with excellent rental potential.

Location

The development is located in Pilar de la Horadada, one of the most attractive towns on the southern Costa Blanca, renowned for its peaceful atmosphere, excellent infrastructure, and close proximity to beautiful sandy beaches. Residents enjoy a wide range of restaurants, cafés, shops, supermarkets, schools, sports facilities, and leisure amenities, making it an ideal destination for both permanent living and holiday stays.

Just a short drive away are the stunning beaches of Torre de la Horadada and Mil Palmeras, known for their fine golden sand, crystal-clear waters, charming marina, scenic promenade, and numerous beachfront restaurants overlooking the Mediterranean Sea. These beaches have been awarded the prestigious Blue Flag for their outstanding water quality, safety, and environmental standards.

For those who enjoy an active lifestyle, the area offers extensive cycling and walking trails, a modern sports centre, and the renowned Lo Romero Golf course, located just a few minutes away. Excellent transport connections provide quick access to Alicante and Murcia airports, as well as popular destinations such as Torrevieja, Cartagena, and the city of Murcia, making this location highly attractive for both holiday homeowners and property investors.
