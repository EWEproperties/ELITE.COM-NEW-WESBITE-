Elite World Estates presents an exceptional residential development located in one of the most attractive areas of the Costa Cálida coastline – sunny Los Alcázares. This modern complex has been designed for those seeking comfort, beautiful views, and close proximity to both the sea and a golf course.

The development consists of 46 elegant apartments and 8 luxury villas situated directly next to the prestigious La Serena Golf course. The entire project has been carefully designed and west-facing, allowing residents to enjoy spectacular sunsets and uninterrupted views over the lush green fairways every day.

Buyers can choose from modern 2- or 3-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms. Ground-floor properties feature private gardens, middle-floor apartments offer spacious terraces, while penthouses include private rooftop solariums with panoramic views of the surrounding area.

The interiors have been designed in a contemporary Mediterranean style, focusing on functionality, comfort, and maximizing natural light. Large windows flood the interiors with sunlight and create a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living spaces.

Residents can enjoy attractive communal areas with a large swimming pool, landscaped gardens, and relaxation zones. Each property also includes a private parking space and storage room.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its location. The complex is situated just 1 km from the sandy beaches of the Mar Menor lagoon, reachable on foot in approximately 12–15 minutes. The area offers a wide selection of restaurants, cafés, shops, and sports facilities. Golf enthusiasts will appreciate direct access to La Serena Golf, while lovers of outdoor activities can enjoy various water sports on the calm waters of the Mar Menor.

Los Alcázares is one of the most popular towns on the Costa Cálida, famous for its more than 300 days of sunshine per year, beautiful seaside promenade, and excellent infrastructure. Murcia International Airport is approximately 25 minutes away by car, while Alicante Airport can be reached in less than one hour.

Location

The picturesque town of Los Alcázares, located in the beautiful Region of Murcia, offers a charming coastal lifestyle and countless attractions. Situated along the shores of the Mar Menor, Europe's largest saltwater lagoon, renowned for its crystal-clear waters and therapeutic properties, Los Alcázares provides an ideal setting for both holidays and year-round living.

The town enjoys excellent road connections with Cartagena, Murcia, Alicante, and the beaches of both Costa Cálida and Costa Blanca.

The area is famous for its beautiful beaches, stretching along the calm waters of the Mar Menor. From the lively Playa de Los Narejos to the more peaceful Playa de Las Salinas, there is a beach to suit every preference. Visitors can enjoy swimming, sailing, kayaking, windsurfing, and many other water sports. The shallow and calm waters make the area particularly attractive for families with children.

Los Alcázares also boasts a vibrant culinary scene, with numerous restaurants, bars, and cafés serving everything from traditional Spanish tapas to international cuisine. Fresh seafood, authentic paella, and refreshing drinks enjoyed at beachfront chiringuitos are part of the local lifestyle.

The town centre features charming streets lined with boutiques, shops, and artisan stores. Adventure seekers can enjoy paddleboarding, jet skiing, scuba diving, and other water-based activities.

Golf lovers will find themselves in paradise, as Los Alcázares is surrounded by world-class golf courses including La Serena Golf, Roda Golf, and Mar Menor Golf Resort, offering year-round golfing thanks to the Mediterranean climate.

For history and culture enthusiasts, the nearby city of Cartagena offers Roman ruins, beautiful architecture, and a rich maritime heritage.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Punta Brava, about 7 km by road, around 9 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: I.E.S. ANTONIO MENÁRGUEZ COSTA, about 1.4 km.
Private school: Colegio Nuestra Señora del Carmen, about 19 km, around 17 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Los Alcázares, about 3 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 13 km by road, around 13 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Dialprix, about 1.8 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 33 km by road, around 25 minutes by car.
