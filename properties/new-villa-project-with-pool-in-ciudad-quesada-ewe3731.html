Location

Ciudad Quesada is located on the southern Costa Blanca, just 8 km north of the vibrant city of Torrevieja. Nearby towns include Rojales and San Fulgencio. Ciudad Quesada is easily reached straight from the nearby airport, which offers numerous connections. The city is just a 30-minute drive from Alicante Airport, which provides excellent connections to major European cities. Come and discover the best that the southern Costa Blanca has to offer in this dynamic town.

This town is a short distance from beautiful sandy beaches such as El Moncayo in Guardamar del Segura, surrounded by picturesque natural parks and scenic landscapes.

For lovers of nature and tranquillity, there is nothing better than taking a rucksack, putting on sports or comfortable shoes and heading out for a hike. In Ciudad Quesada, you can go hiking to astonishing places. There are many hiking trails, but one of the most engaging experiences, especially for nature lovers and birdwatchers, is a visit to Laguna La Mata.

Golf enthusiasts can enjoy the prestigious La Marquesa Golf Club, which offers impressive views of the surrounding area. Meanwhile, those who appreciate nature can visit the nearby salt pans of Torrevieja, which provide a habitat for numerous bird species.

The town has a rich history dating back to the early 18th century and has become a vibrant community characterised by a unique blend of Spanish, British and other European cultures. In Ciudad Quesada, you can immerse yourself in the city's rural past by strolling through its charming old town, while the modern and cosmopolitan area around the city centre reflects its more contemporary development.

Another must-do in Ciudad Quesada is a visit to the spa, where you can entrust your wellbeing into the hands of professionals for a few hours, an afternoon or a whole day. At the spa of Hotel La Laguna, you can take advantage of the so-called ‘Sensory Circuit’, which gives you access to a wellness area with contrasting hot and cold sensations, a relaxation area and a salt jacuzzi, where you can stimulate all your senses through different zones dedicated to sight, smell, taste, touch and hearing. To relieve tension, you can opt for a bamboo or chocolate massage or take part in an anti-stress therapy. You will leave this place more relaxed than ever before. An added bonus of the spa at Hotel La Laguna is that you don't have to be a hotel guest to use its facilities.
