Location

On the Costa Blanca, in the northern part of the province of Alicante, there is a unique town. Jávea, also known as Xàbia, is a seaside town located on the Mediterranean coast. It is located on a wide bay and protected on two sides by stone promontories. It is very famous among tourists, because as much as half of the population of 35,000 are foreigners who have their residences here. Interestingly, the researchers determined that Jávea boasts the most favorable microclimate in the world after Rio de Janerio.

From the village of Jávea there are exceptional views of the surroundings - from here you can see, among others, the coast of Ibiza, which is about 90 kilometers away from it. You can also go to the popular Ibiza - ferries run regularly to it.

Mount Montgo, over 750 meters high, is the highest point in the area - it is a popular vantage point over the city and the surrounding islands of Portichol and Ambolo. It is worth taking the time to visit this place.

Lovers of relaxation will be able to relax on the popular beaches of Arenal, Sardinella, Granadella and Barraca. It is a great place to sunbathe, swim and dive in the crystal clear sea.

In the city center, which can charm with its historic tenement houses and churches, there are also atmospheric restaurants and shops, you can also use many service points and easily communicate in foreign languages. Noteworthy is also the seaside passage presenting the panorama of the Mediterranean Sea.

Also worth a visit is the fishing port, which offers fresh shrimp, squid, sardines and other treasures caught between Javea and Ibiza every day after fishing. In the nearby sailing club, you can admire impressive yachts and motor boats patiently waiting for a free moment or a summer holiday of their owners from all over Europe. Walking along the port promenade along the bay, we will pass the Gorgos River bed, dried up for most of the year, which is a natural border between the port district and the Montanar district.

In Montanar, the most extensive district of Javea, there are the most important beaches of the bay, both sandy and pebbly or rocky, and the famous chiringuitos beach bars located on their shores offering exotic drinks throughout the season. The buildings located in this district are mainly representative single-family houses in a traditional style - covered with tiles, with arcades, modernly furnished with glass and concrete, separated by a road from less frequented rocky beaches.
