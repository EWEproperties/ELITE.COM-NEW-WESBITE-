Elite World Estates is proud to present an exceptional residential development located directly on the Mediterranean coastline in the charming town of El Campello. This is one of the most prestigious and exclusive locations on the Costa Blanca, situated next to the natural Playa del Amerador beach, where residents can enjoy uninterrupted sea views, the soothing sound of the waves, and an authentic Mediterranean lifestyle every day.

This boutique development consists of only 30 exclusive semi-detached and terraced homes distributed across five elegant buildings with contemporary architecture. The project has been designed for those seeking luxury, privacy, and outstanding quality in a unique beachfront setting.

Each property offers a functional layout featuring 3 spacious bedrooms and bright, beautifully designed interiors. Large floor-to-ceiling windows maximize natural light and enhance the property's connection with the surrounding Mediterranean landscape.

The homes are available in several size options, ranging from approximately 199 m² to 343 m². Depending on the chosen model, owners can enjoy generous private gardens, additional bathrooms, private garages, and premium appliances from renowned brands.

Every home features a private garden, spacious terraces, and an impressive rooftop solarium prepared for the installation of a jacuzzi and barbecue area. It is the perfect place to relax, entertain family and friends, and enjoy spectacular views of the Mediterranean Sea.

The development boasts the highest Energy Efficiency Rating A, ensuring maximum comfort, lower operating costs, and modern sustainable solutions.

Residents will have access to exclusive resort-style communal facilities. At the heart of the complex is a stunning infinity pool with panoramic sea views and a dedicated swimming lane. The wellness area also includes a heated jacuzzi, a tranquil zen terrace overlooking the water, and an elegant relaxation zone with Balinese sunbeds.

Active residents will appreciate the modern pickleball court and outdoor gym. Everyday convenience is further enhanced by an intelligent RFID access system and modern smart parcel lockers.

One of the major advantages of this development is its excellent connectivity. A TRAM stop is located just next to the complex, providing fast and direct access to Alicante city centre. Alicante International Airport is only around 30 minutes away by car, making this location ideal for both permanent living and holiday retreats.

This is a unique opportunity for those dreaming of a luxury home by the sea in one of the most desirable locations on the Costa Blanca. The combination of a prime beachfront location, contemporary architecture, resort-style amenities, and direct access to the coastline also makes this an excellent investment with strong long-term growth potential.

LOCATION

El Campello is one of the most sought-after residential destinations in the northern Costa Blanca, located just minutes from Alicante city. Over the years, the town has attracted growing interest from both Spanish buyers and international investors, particularly from Northern Europe, who value its unique combination of Mediterranean lifestyle, excellent transport links, and high quality of life throughout the year.

One of El Campello's greatest attractions is its impressive coastline stretching for more than 23 kilometres, offering both wide sandy beaches and charming coves hidden between cliffs. Thanks to this, the town has preserved its authentic Mediterranean character while offering prestigious beachfront properties with direct access to the sea.

Unlike many seasonal tourist resorts, El Campello remains vibrant throughout the entire year. The town offers a wide range of restaurants, cafés, a marina, schools, medical centres, shops, and all the services required for comfortable everyday living. This year-round appeal creates strong and stable demand for both property sales and rentals.

Another key advantage is its excellent transport infrastructure. El Campello enjoys direct TRAM connections to Alicante, San Juan, Benidorm, and other coastal towns along the Costa Blanca. Alicante International Airport is only 25–30 minutes away by car, making the town an ideal destination for both permanent residence and holiday homes.

The growing interest among investors is also driven by the limited availability of new beachfront land. Areas such as Muchavista Beach, Playa Carrer La Mar, Amerador, and Coveta Fumà are particularly desirable due to the scarcity of new developments. As a result, exclusive frontline properties are among the most sought-after real estate opportunities in the entire Alicante region.

El Campello is the perfect choice for those seeking an exclusive seaside property that combines tranquillity, privacy, and breathtaking views with easy access to the city and international transport connections. It is a location that continues to attract increasing interest and remains one of the most promising investment destinations on the Costa Blanca.
