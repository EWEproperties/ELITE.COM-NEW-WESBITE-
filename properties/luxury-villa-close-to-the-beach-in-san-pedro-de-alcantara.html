Location

San Pedro de Alcántara is a district of the municipality of Marbella, in the province of Málaga. San Pedro de Alcántara and Marbella are 16 kilometres apart and the famous Puerto Banús is less than 9 kilometres from this picturesque town.

Restaurants

San Pedro also welcomes an increasing number of tourists who come to eat and socialise, thanks to the large variety of tapas bars, cafés and traditional restaurants in the old town and the growing selection of excellent tapas restaurants and eateries around the boulevard and Paseo Marítimo , offering everything from Spanish cuisine to Italian, French, Argentinian and Asian cuisine. San Pedro's beaches are surrounded by pleasant fish restaurants and beach clubs such as Bora Bora, Guayaba, El Ancón and Macao.

Beach

San Pedro de Alcántara beach is considered one of the quietest in the area. The dark sand and pebble beach has a unique line of eucalyptus trees that extends almost to the shore, and a large number of palm trees adorning the beautiful promenade.

Sports

In addition to paddle tennis and tennis clubs, San Pedro also has a municipal sports centre that offers gym classes, swimming pools and indoor sports. The football club attracts many young players and there are many excellent golf courses within a ten-minute drive, including Los Arqueros, El Higueral, La Zagaleta, La Quinta, Magna Marbella, Los Naranjos, Las Brisas, Aloha Golf, Dama de Noche, Guadalmina, Atalaya and El Campanario.

Among the palm trees, we can highlight an interesting sculpture, a life-size elephant, joyfully striding along the beach. As expected, this elephant has become one of the most popular features of the beach and one of the most photographed by visitors.

San Pedro de Alcántara beach is 1,400 metres long and has an average width of around 35 metres. A remarkable beach whose qualities have earned it the Blue Flag award for 24 years. Just as it has once again received the Q for tourist quality, clear symbols of the good condition of this beach.
