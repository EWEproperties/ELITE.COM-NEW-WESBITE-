Location

Strategically located La Nucia allows you to enjoy the countless attractions it offers. Just 10 kilometers from Benidorm, in the middle of a picturesque valley covered with fruit trees and pine forests, this inland town is located surrounded by the Sierra de Aitana mountain range.

Its old town consists of a network of white houses, headed by the parish church. It is also full of various corners offering privileged views of the surrounding area. Without losing its traditional character, La Nucia also has a significant residential area made up of elegant new developments.

The breathtaking beauty of the surroundings makes it the perfect setting for walking or cycling through picturesque areas such as Fuente de la Favara or Puig Campana mountain.

This town is famous throughout the region for a large second-hand goods market organized every Sunday on one of the main roads - Avenida Porvilla. The celebrations in honor of the Immaculate Conception in mid-August and the feast of San Rafael in November are also very popular.
