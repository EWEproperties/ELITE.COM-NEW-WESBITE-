Location

Pilar de la Horadada is a charming town located in the southern part of the province of Alicante, in the Community of Valencia, Spain. This town is famous for its picturesque landscapes, fascinating cultural heritage and crystal clear water, making it the perfect place for those seeking relaxation and tranquillity.

Pilar de la Horadada has everything you need to enjoy the sea, pine forests and the Spanish sun. Pilar de la Hordada's coastline is over 4 kilometres long and offers a wide variety of scenery. There are wide beaches with fine white sand, small stone ledges hanging over the water and small, intimate, hidden coves with golden sand.

The sea is usually calm here, the beaches are safe, and there are no steep slopes, so even the youngest children can bathe safely. Furthermore, the clear water and excellent visibility invite divers to observe the beauty of aquatic life.

The cleanliness of the beaches in Pilar de la Hordada has been awarded the blue flag of the European Union, which guarantees a healthy environment.

Inland, you can take various interesting trips near the towns of Sierra and Escalona. There are two signposted hiking trails along the 19-kilometre-long Rio Seco, which are definitely worth a visit. One of the trails is 4 kilometres long, while the other is 10 kilometres long and extends from the natural area of Pilar de Campoverde to the mouth of the river at Mil Palmeras beach.

There is also an 18-hole golf course in Lo Romero near the town of Pilar de la Horadada. It is a great place for both professionals and beginners.

Gastronomy has a privileged place in the culture of this beautiful city. Typical dishes of Pilar de la Hordada include potatoes with meat stew, meatballs, rabbit in garlic and Gachas Migas (a floury, garlicky pancake) as well as excellent rice dishes and soups. The desserts and pastries are also worth mentioning. The most popular are the meringue cake called ‘milhojas’ and the almond sweets called ‘cordiales’.
