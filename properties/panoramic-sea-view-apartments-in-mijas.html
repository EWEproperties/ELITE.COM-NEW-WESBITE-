Elite World Estates proudly presents an exclusive residential development located in the prestigious area of Cerrado del Águila in Mijas, one of the most desirable locations on the Costa del Sol. Surrounded by nature and overlooking the renowned golf course, this exceptional project combines contemporary architecture, premium amenities and breathtaking panoramic views, creating the perfect setting for a relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle.

The development consists of 71 premium apartments distributed across six elegant low-rise buildings, offering an intimate and peaceful residential environment. Every home has been carefully designed to maximise space, natural light and spectacular views, creating bright and sophisticated interiors that seamlessly connect with the surrounding landscape.

Spacious open-plan living areas, floor-to-ceiling windows and generous private terraces invite residents to enjoy the Mediterranean climate throughout the year. The contemporary architecture blends perfectly with the natural surroundings, while landscaped gardens and carefully designed outdoor spaces create a harmonious balance between modern living and nature.

Large windows frame breathtaking panoramic views stretching across the Mediterranean Sea and the rolling hills of Mijas, filling every room with natural light and enhancing the sense of openness and tranquillity. Every apartment has been designed to provide comfort, elegance and an exceptional indoor-outdoor living experience.

Residents will enjoy an outstanding selection of resort-style amenities created to enhance everyday life. Beautifully landscaped communal gardens, elegant swimming pools, wellness areas and modern social spaces provide the perfect environment for relaxation, recreation and wellbeing. Whether enjoying a refreshing swim, practising yoga in the gardens or spending time with family and friends, every detail has been thoughtfully designed to offer a truly exceptional lifestyle.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its privileged location. Situated next to Cerrado del Águila Golf & Resort, residents can enjoy immediate access to a scenic golf course, driving range and clubhouse, while tennis courts, padel courts, cycling routes and hiking trails are all just minutes away.

The beautiful beaches of Mijas Costa are only five minutes from the development, while the charming towns of La Cala de Mijas and Mijas Pueblo offer authentic Andalusian atmosphere, excellent restaurants, boutique shops and vibrant local culture. Fuengirola can be reached in approximately 7 minutes, Marbella and Málaga in around 20 minutes, and Málaga International Airport is only a 20-minute drive, ensuring excellent connectivity throughout the Costa del Sol.

Combining spectacular views, contemporary design, premium facilities and an outstanding location, this exclusive development offers a unique opportunity to own a modern apartment in one of the Costa del Sol's most attractive residential destinations. Whether you are looking for a permanent residence, a luxurious holiday home or an excellent investment opportunity, these exceptional apartments deliver the perfect balance of comfort, elegance and Mediterranean living.

Location

The development is located in the prestigious area of Casares Costa, between Estepona and Sotogrande, on the western Costa del Sol. This exceptional location combines a peaceful Mediterranean setting with excellent access to beautiful beaches, renowned golf courses, and all the amenities required for comfortable year-round living. The area's greatest highlight is the famous Crystal Lagoon – the first artificial lagoon in Europe featuring crystal-clear turquoise waters, a private beach, and a wide range of water activities, including kayaking, paddleboarding, and pedal boats.

Just a few minutes' drive away are the wide sandy beaches of Casares Costa and Estepona, the scenic seafront promenade, elegant beach clubs, and numerous restaurants serving traditional Andalusian and Mediterranean cuisine. Supermarkets, shops, schools, medical centres, and the marinas of Estepona and La Duquesa are all conveniently located nearby, ensuring that everyday amenities are always within easy reach.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of prestigious golf courses such as Finca Cortesín Golf Club, Doña Julia Golf Club, Estepona Golf, and Valle Romano Golf. The surrounding area also offers numerous walking and cycling routes along the coastline and through the picturesque Andalusian hills.

Excellent transport connections provide quick access to the Costa del Sol's most important destinations. Estepona town centre is approximately a 10-minute drive away, Marbella around 25 minutes, Sotogrande approximately 20 minutes, Gibraltar Airport around 35 minutes, and Málaga International Airport can be reached in approximately 60 minutes. This is an outstanding location both for those looking for a second home in Spain and for investors seeking a property with excellent rental potential in one of the most desirable areas of the Costa del Sol.
