Location and Lifestyle in Cumbre del Sol

Cumbre del Sol, an extravagant urbanisation in Benitachell, Alicante, Spain, is celebrated for its stunning vistas of the Mediterranean and majestic mountains. This area is a magnet for those desiring a lavish lifestyle in a peaceful setting.

Easily accessible, Cumbre del Sol is a short drive from Alicante-Elche Airport. The urbanisation boasts a plethora of amenities, including chic restaurants, cafes, and a shopping center. For sports enthusiasts, it offers a tennis and paddle club, a modern fitness center, and a swimming pool complex with both outdoor and indoor pools.

The enchanting Cala del Moraig beach, known for its clear waters and scenic cove, is perfect for water activities and hiking. The beach is well-equipped with facilities for a delightful day out.

For families, Cumbre del Sol is home to prestigious international schools like The Lady Elizabeth School, offering top-tier education and a range of extracurricular activities.

This luxury villa in Cumbre del Sol is not just a home; it's a statement of elite living, offering a unique blend of comfort, style, and exclusivity. It's an opportunity to own a piece of paradise in one of Spain's most sought-after locations.
