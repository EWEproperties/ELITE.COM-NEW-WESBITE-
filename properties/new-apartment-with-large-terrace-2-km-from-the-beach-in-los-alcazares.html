Location

The picturesque town of Los Alcázares, located in the charming region of Murcia in Spain, tempts with its seaside charm and a multitude of delightful attractions. Located along the magnificent Mar Menor, a saltwater lagoon famous for its crystal-clear water and healing properties, Los Alcázares offers an idyllic place for an unforgettable holiday or a peaceful getaway.

The area is connected by motorway to Cartagena, Murcia and Alicante as well as the other beaches of the Costa Cálida and Costa Blanca.

One of the town's greatest assets is its collection of beautiful beaches, each promising moments of bliss and relaxation. The golden sands stretch along the calm shores of the Mar Menor, creating a paradise for sun seekers and water lovers. From the lively and family-friendly Playa de Los Narejos to the more secluded and peaceful Playa de Las Salinas, there is a beach for everyone. Visitors can sunbathe, swim or enjoy a variety of water sports including sailing, kayaking and windsurfing. The shallow and calm waters of the Mar Menor make it an ideal place for families with children.

Los Alcázares boasts a lively culinary scene, offering a diverse range of restaurants, bars and cafés to tempt your taste buds. From traditional Spanish tapas to international cuisine, the town's restaurants cater to every palate. Savour fresh seafood made with local ingredients, enjoy authentic paella or have a refreshing drink at a chiringuito on the beach while taking in the stunning views. The warm and welcoming atmosphere of the city's eateries enhances the overall dining experience, making it a real treat for food lovers.

As well as beaches and eateries, Los Alcázares offers many attractions to keep visitors entertained. Explore the charming streets in the town centre, dotted with shops, boutiques and craft stores. Discover local handicrafts, souvenirs and unique treasures to commemorate your visit. For the adventurous, a variety of water sports centres offer opportunities for jet skiing, paddleboarding and diving, allowing you to immerse yourself in the depths of the Mar Menor's underwater wonders.

Golf enthusiasts will find themselves in paradise, as Los Alcázares is surrounded by a selection of world-class golf courses. You will discover famous golf courses such as La Serena Golf, Roda Golf and Mar Menor Golf Resort just a short distance away. Immerse yourself in lush green fairways, perfect your swing and enjoy the Mediterranean climate that allows you to play golf all year round.

To experience history and culture, we highly recommend a visit to the nearby city of Cartagena. Explore ancient Roman ruins, wander through charming streets full of colourful buildings and immerse yourself in the rich history of this historic port city.
