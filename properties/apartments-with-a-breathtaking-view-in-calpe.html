Introducing brand-new apartments in Calpe, nestled near the golden sands of la Fossa and El Arenal beaches. This represents a prime investment opportunity situated in a pivotal location - positioned between Calpe's finest beaches and the enchanting Las Salinas Natural Park. Conveniently adjacent to the renowned 5-star Diamante hotel, and just a stone's throw away from the vibrant city center, these apartments offer an unparalleled lifestyle experience.

Calpe, renowned as the cycling capital, beckons not only professionals and enthusiasts of the sport but also holidaymakers seeking year-round bliss on the Costa Blanca, thanks to its well-established infrastructure.

This apartments are housed within two impressive 10-story buildings, presenting units featuring 2 or 3 bedrooms and a minimum of 2 bathrooms. Each residence boasts a spacious terrace offering panoramic views of Calpe's breathtaking scenery.

But that's not all! The package includes a parking space in the garage and a storage room for your convenience.

Furthermore, residents will have access to a plethora of communal amenities, including a swimming pool catering to both adults and children, a rejuvenating solarium, paddle tennis courts, a luxurious gourmet room, lush green spaces, and a dedicated playground for the little ones.

Planned construction completion date: March 31, 2026 and July 31, 2026.

Location

Calpe exudes tranquility and charm on the Costa Blanca map, renowned for its pristine sandy beaches, crystalline waters, picturesque port, and the captivating natural park boasting a salt lake that occasionally blushes pink, serving as a sanctuary for rare flamingos. Outdoor enthusiasts will relish in activities such as scaling the iconic El Peñón de Ifach hill or leisurely strolling along Calpe's extensive coastline.

Notably, the drive to the airport is under an hour by car, while Calpe enjoys excellent public transport connections to Alicante.
