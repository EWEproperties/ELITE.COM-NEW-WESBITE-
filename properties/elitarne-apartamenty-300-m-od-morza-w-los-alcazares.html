Location

Los Alcázares is located in the Murcia region of Spain. The town boasts seaside charm and a wealth of delightful attractions. It lies along the magnificent Mar Menor, a saltwater lagoon renowned for its crystal-clear waters and medicinal properties.

The area is connected by highway to Cartagena, Murcia, and Alicante, as well as other beaches on the Costa Cálida and Costa Blanca.

One of the town's greatest assets is its collection of beautiful beaches, each promising moments of bliss and relaxation. Golden sands stretch along the tranquil shores of the Mar Menor, creating a paradise for sunseekers and water lovers. From the lively and family-friendly Playa de Los Narejos to the more secluded and tranquil Playa de Las Salinas, there's a beach for everyone. Visitors can sunbathe, swim, or enjoy a variety of water sports, including sailing, kayaking, and windsurfing.

Los Alcázares boasts a vibrant culinary scene, offering a diverse range of restaurants, bars, and cafés to tantalize your taste buds. From traditional Spanish tapas to international cuisine, the town's restaurants will satisfy every palate. Sample fresh seafood made with local ingredients, savor authentic paella, or sip a refreshing drink in a beachside chiringuito while admiring the stunning views.

Beyond its beaches and dining options, Los Alcázares offers plenty to keep visitors entertained. Explore the charming streets of the town center, dotted with shops, boutiques, and artisanal stores. Discover local handicrafts, souvenirs, and unique treasures to commemorate your visit. For those seeking adventure, a variety of water sports options offer jet skiing, paddleboarding, and scuba diving, allowing you to immerse yourself in the depths of the Mar Menor's underwater wonders.

Golf enthusiasts will be in paradise, as Los Alcázares is surrounded by a selection of world-class golf courses. Within a short distance, you'll discover renowned golf courses such as La Serena Golf, Roda Golf, and Mar Menor Golf Resort.
