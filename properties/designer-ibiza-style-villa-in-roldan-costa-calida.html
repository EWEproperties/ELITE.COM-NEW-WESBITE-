Discover this exclusive boutique development of just 8 detached villas, located in the charming town of Roldán in the Murcia region. This is a brand-new project where modern architecture meets a light, Mediterranean style inspired by the relaxed spirit of Ibiza.

Each villa is thoughtfully designed over two levels, offering the perfect blend of comfort, functionality, and effortless living.

The interiors impress with space and natural light – the open-plan living area seamlessly combines an elegant lounge, dining space, and a fully fitted modern kitchen. Large sliding glass doors lead directly to a private terrace, landscaped garden, and swimming pool, creating a natural flow between indoor and outdoor living.

The property features 3 spacious bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom, ensuring maximum privacy and comfort. Bedrooms on the first floor include private terraces – ideal for morning coffee or relaxing at sunset.

A standout feature is the generous rooftop solarium, offering the perfect space for relaxation, sunbathing, or entertaining in a true Mediterranean atmosphere.

Plots of approximately 240–250 m² provide both privacy and comfort, including a landscaped garden, private pool, outdoor shower, and on-plot parking.

Standard features include

  • Fitted wardrobes
  • LED lighting
  • Storage room
  • Pre-installed air conditioning

The location is another major advantage – the villas are set between green areas, close to local amenities, sports facilities, and renowned golf courses. The beaches of the Mediterranean Sea and Mar Menor are just 12–15 minutes away.

This is an excellent opportunity both as a second home in Spain and as a rental investment in the rapidly growing Costa Cálida region.

Completion date: From June 2026 to January 2027, depending on the selected villa

Location

Roldán is a charming village in the Murcia region of Spain, known for its peaceful rural atmosphere and beautiful natural surroundings. Located close to the Mar Menor, it offers easy access to stunning beaches and golf courses. The town provides a range of amenities, including shops, restaurants, bars, and supermarkets. Sports enthusiasts will also find plenty to enjoy, with various sports facilities and scenic walking routes available.

Roldán combines the best of tranquil countryside living with convenient access to urban amenities, making it an ideal location for both permanent residents and holidaymakers.

Frequently asked questions

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: IES Sabino Mora, about 500 m.
Private school: Colegio "San Agustín", about 20 km, around 21 minutes by car.
International school: El Limonar International School - Buenavista, about 27 km, around 27 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud El Jimenado, about 10 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 16 km by road, around 17 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Spar, about 3 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 15 km by road, around 17 minutes by car.
