Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional contemporary villa located in one of the most prestigious areas of Jávea on the northern Costa Blanca. Designed in a refined Mediterranean style with modern architectural elements, this elegant residence perfectly combines luxury, functionality and spectacular panoramic views of the valley and the Mediterranean Sea. Its privileged location offers a peaceful natural setting while providing quick access to Arenal Beach, renowned restaurants, golf courses, international schools and all essential amenities for comfortable year-round living.

The villa offers approximately 415 m² of built area and is set on a plot of around 1,000 m². This two-storey residence impresses with its contemporary architecture, expansive floor-to-ceiling windows and spacious terraces that seamlessly connect the indoor and outdoor living spaces. Bright, elegant interiors have been carefully designed to maximise natural light while providing exceptional comfort throughout the home.

The property features four spacious bedrooms and four modern bathrooms finished with high-quality materials. The heart of the home is a generous open-plan living and dining area connected to a designer fully fitted kitchen. Thanks to the large panoramic windows, almost every room enjoys breathtaking views of the sea, the valley and the surrounding Mediterranean landscape.

The outdoor area has been designed for relaxation and year-round enjoyment. Owners can enjoy a private swimming pool surrounded by spacious sun terraces, beautifully landscaped gardens with an automatic irrigation system and several inviting outdoor spaces perfect for relaxing or entertaining family and friends. The Mediterranean climate and stunning scenery create the ideal setting for embracing the Costa Blanca lifestyle every day.

This property is an outstanding choice for buyers seeking a modern villa in Spain that combines a prestigious location, timeless architecture, premium-quality finishes and exceptional panoramic views. Whether as a permanent residence, an exclusive holiday home or a high-potential investment on the Spanish property market, this villa represents a truly exceptional opportunity.

Location

The villa is located in the prestigious Monte Olimpo residential area of Jávea, one of the most desirable locations on the northern Costa Blanca. This peaceful and exclusive neighbourhood is surrounded by Mediterranean greenery and offers outstanding privacy, panoramic views of the valley, the Montgó mountains and the Mediterranean Sea, while remaining close to all the amenities required for comfortable year-round living.

The property is only around a five-minute drive from Playa del Arenal, Jávea's most famous sandy beach, and its vibrant promenade lined with restaurants, cafés, boutiques and Mediterranean dining venues. Supermarkets, renowned international schools, the town centre, the marina and the prestigious Jávea Golf Club are all within easy reach, making this an ideal location for both families and buyers looking for an exclusive holiday home.

Jávea has long been regarded as one of the most attractive places to live on the Costa Blanca. The town is famous for its beautiful coastline, charming historic centre, picturesque marina and numerous coves with crystal-clear waters. The nearby Montgó Natural Park, extensive hiking and cycling routes, a wide range of water sports and more than 300 days of sunshine each year make Jávea an exceptional destination for both permanent living and secure real estate investment.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja de l'Arenal, about 6 km by road, around 9 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Mediterrània, about 3 km, around 5 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Alfa & Omega, about 12 km, around 20 minutes by car.
International school: Den norske skole Costa Blanca, about 46 km, around 35 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centre de Salut de Xàbia, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital la Pedrera, about 16 km by road, around 24 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is masymas, about 4 km away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 103 km by road, around 75 minutes by car.
