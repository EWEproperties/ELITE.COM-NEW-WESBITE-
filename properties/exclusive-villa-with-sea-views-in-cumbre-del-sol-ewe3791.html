Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional luxury villa located in one of the most prestigious areas of the northern Costa Blanca – Cumbre del Sol, between Moraira and Jávea. This outstanding property has been designed for discerning buyers seeking a modern home in Spain that combines spectacular Mediterranean Sea views, timeless architecture, and the highest standards of construction and finishes.

The residence has been thoughtfully designed to blend luxury with its stunning natural surroundings. Its contemporary geometric architecture is enhanced by natural stone finishes, impressive floor-to-ceiling windows, and spacious terraces, allowing almost every room to enjoy breathtaking sea views. Bright, open-plan interiors create a seamless connection between the indoor living areas and the outdoor relaxation spaces.

The villa offers approximately 625 m² of built area on a generous 1,233 m² plot. Inside, there are three spacious en-suite bedrooms, an elegant open-plan living area with a panoramic lounge, a stylish dining room, and a designer kitchen equipped with premium appliances. Additional rooms provide the flexibility to create a private gym, home office, cinema room, or wellness area tailored to the owner's individual lifestyle.

One of the property's most impressive features is the spectacular outdoor area with an infinity swimming pool that appears to merge with the blue waters of the Mediterranean Sea. Expansive terraces, covered lounge areas, an outdoor dining space, and beautifully landscaped Mediterranean gardens create the perfect environment for relaxation, entertaining guests, and enjoying unforgettable sunrises.

The villa is equipped with state-of-the-art technology designed to maximise comfort and energy efficiency, including underfloor heating, an advanced aerothermal system, air conditioning, a smart home automation system, heat recovery ventilation, photovoltaic solar panels, and pre-installation for an electric vehicle charging station. A private lift connecting all levels and premium-quality materials throughout the property further emphasise its exclusive character.

Cumbre del Sol is one of the most sought-after locations on the Costa Blanca, offering exceptional privacy, dramatic cliffside scenery, and easy access to beautiful beaches, hidden coves, marinas, fine restaurants, and renowned golf courses. The charming coastal towns of Moraira, Jávea, and Calpe are just a short drive away, while the international airports of Alicante and Valencia provide convenient connections with the rest of Europe.

This remarkable villa is an outstanding opportunity both as a luxurious permanent residence and as an exclusive holiday home or prestigious investment on the Spanish property market. Its combination of contemporary architecture, panoramic sea views, premium finishes, and one of the Costa Blanca's most desirable locations makes it an exceptional property for even the most demanding buyers.

Location

The villa is situated in the prestigious area of Cumbre del Sol, between Moraira and Jávea on the northern Costa Blanca. This is one of the region's most exclusive residential locations, renowned for its spectacular cliffside landscapes, breathtaking Mediterranean Sea views, and peaceful surroundings. Thanks to its elevated position, residents enjoy exceptional privacy, close contact with nature, and one of the most beautiful coastlines in Spain.

Just a few minutes away lies the picturesque Cala Moraig, a stunning cove with crystal-clear waters that is considered one of Costa Blanca's hidden gems. The surrounding area also offers numerous scenic viewpoints, walking trails, and hiking routes that follow the dramatic Mediterranean coastline.

The elegant town of Moraira, with its sandy beaches, marina, boutique shops, and excellent Mediterranean restaurants, can be reached in approximately 15 minutes by car. The charming town of Jávea is equally close, offering a beautiful historic centre, a yacht marina, and the famous Arenal Beach with its lively promenade lined with cafés and restaurants. Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of several prestigious golf courses, while sailing, diving, paddleboarding, and other water sports are available nearby.

Everyday convenience is ensured by supermarkets, shops, an international school, medical centres, and a wide range of local services. Excellent road connections to Alicante and Valencia International Airports make travelling easy, making this location ideal for year-round living, an exclusive holiday retreat, or a high-value investment on the Costa Blanca.
