Elite World Estates presents an exceptional tourist apartment development located within the prestigious Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort in the Murcia region. This is an excellent opportunity for those seeking an investment property capable of generating short-term rental income while also providing a luxurious holiday home for personal use.

The project consists of modern apartments with 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, delivered on a turnkey basis. Each property is fully furnished and equipped, making it ready for immediate occupancy or rental from day one. The price includes furniture, household appliances, air conditioning, television, high-speed Wi-Fi, fully equipped kitchenware, bed linen, towels, and all essential everyday items.

The spacious interiors have been designed in a contemporary style with a strong focus on functionality and comfort. Large floor-to-ceiling windows provide abundant natural light, while private terraces allow residents to enjoy the Mediterranean climate throughout the year. Each apartment also includes a private parking space and a storage room.

The development operates under an aparthotel model, offering owners the possibility of entrusting rental management to a professional operator. The complex holds an official 4-key tourist license, ensuring full legal compliance for holiday rentals. On-site facilities include a reception area and comprehensive guest services such as check-in, housekeeping, and property promotion.

This exclusive gated and monitored complex consists of only 40 apartments distributed across five elegant buildings. Residents can enjoy not only two communal swimming pools but also full access to the extensive amenities of Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort.

The resort's main attraction is the spectacular Crystal Lagoon, covering 124,000 m² of crystal-clear water and surrounded by white sandy beaches, palm trees, and recreational areas. Residents also have access to the Beach Club, restaurants, gym, mini-golf, children's playgrounds, walking and cycling paths, and a variety of water sports such as kayaking and paddle boarding.

Its excellent location, just 4 km from the beaches of Mar Menor in Los Alcázares and close to some of the best golf courses on the Costa Cálida, makes this one of the most attractive tourist investment opportunities in southern Spain.

Location

Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort Murcia is located 35 km from Murcia city and 19 km from La Manga del Mar Menor. The nearest airport is Murcia International Airport, situated just 22 km away.

Mediterranean Living at Its Finest

Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort redefines the concept of luxury living by offering exceptional accommodation options for the entire family within a five-star resort covering approximately 700,000 square metres.

The resort features around 120,000 m² of landscaped areas with sports and leisure facilities, more than 16,000 native Mediterranean trees and plants, an impressive swimming lagoon, and a beach equipped for water sports, paddle boarding, and kayaking. The resort offers a high level of security with controlled access and on-site security services.

Beach Club

The Beach Club is the perfect place to relax, socialise, and enjoy meals within the resort. It offers spectacular views of the lagoon and beach, a relaxing terrace, and leisure areas for both adults and children. It is the ideal setting for a cold beer, lunch, dinner, or a glass of wine with friends.

The Beach Club also hosts many resort activities, including beach volleyball, kayaking, Adventure Golf, and pétanque tournaments.

Recycling

Santa Rosalía has implemented a comprehensive waste separation system for paper, cardboard, packaging, plastics, glass, and other recyclable materials. Small actions can make a big difference in protecting our planet. In our sales office, we use recyclable paper cups, minimise paper consumption, and actively promote recycling. This environmental policy is especially important within the Beach Club.

Energy Efficiency

The entire resort is equipped with energy-efficient LED lighting controlled by solar timers, ensuring low energy consumption. Energy efficiency is not only about reducing costs but also about protecting our planet for future generations.
