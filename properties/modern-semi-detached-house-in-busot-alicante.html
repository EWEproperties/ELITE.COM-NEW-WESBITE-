Location

Just 20 kilometers from the capital Alicante, the small and quiet town of Busot is an excellent alternative to enjoy nature and cultural visits. If you don't know it, it's very likely that this small town located on the slopes of the iconic Sierra del Cabeçó d'Or will surprise you.

In the city there are all the services necessary for everyday life, supermarkets, restaurants, banks, schools, etc. In 10 minutes you can drive to the municipalities of Mutxamel, Campello and San Juan. Next to this residential area there are the Municipal Sports Center, a supermarket and cafes, and just 5 minutes away, in the urban area, all city services.

Busot, whose etymological meaning is "a place in the forest", is located in an extremely picturesque natural landscape. The city has a very interesting tourist offer that cannot be ignored, including places and monuments such as:

Cuevas del Canelobre

The Canelobre Caves are Busot's main tourist attraction and are among the most impressive caves in the Valencian Community. These are caves with an area of over 80,000 m2, where you can find stalactites, stalagmites, jellyfish and columns in a karst cave, which are rare in our country. The tour leaves no one indifferent and is the first place you should discover when visiting Busot. The entrance to the caves is located at an altitude of 700 meters, on the western slope of the Sierra del Cabeçó d'Or.

Castillo de Busot

The Muslim-derived Busot Castle was of strategic importance in the area during the conquest of James I, as it became a border point between the kingdoms of Aragon and Castile. The castle was reinforced with the surrounding walls, and there are even remains of the first settlers' houses around it. Unfortunately, not much has survived, but the access is very well prepared and it is worth climbing the mountain, just to admire the views and try to imagine what the life of the soldiers guarding this watchtower would have been like.

Sierra del Cabeçó d'Or

Cabeçó d'Or is a spectacular mountain rising to a height of 1,209 meters, less than ten kilometers from the Mediterranean Sea. This mountain range is characterized by impressive ridges and limestone walls that have been Alicante's climbing school for decades.
