Location

Cumbre del Sol is the epitome of luxurious living in a tranquil and idyllic setting. Situated in the beautiful coastal town of Benitachell, this upscale urbanisation boasts stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea and surrounding mountains. Accessible by car or public transportation, the urbanisation is located just 90 kilometers away from Alicante-Elche Airport, making it a convenient and easily accessible destination for those seeking an elevated lifestyle.

Residents of Cumbre del Sol can enjoy a wide range of high-end amenities and facilities, including top-rated restaurants, cafes, and bars, as well as a shopping center that offers a variety of upscale products and services. The urbanisation also has a tennis and paddle club, a fitness center with state-of-the-art equipment, and a swimming pool complex with several outdoor pools and a heated indoor pool, perfect for year-round use.

For families with children, Cumbre del Sol is home to several international schools, including the prestigious Lady Elizabeth School. This private British school offers a high-quality education for students ranging from 1 to 18 years old and is known for its excellent academic standards and extracurricular activities.

The area surrounding Cumbre del Sol offers plenty of opportunities for leisure and recreation, with several natural parks, beaches, and golf courses just a short distance away. The local beach, Cala del Moraig, is a beautiful cove beach surrounded by cliffs and crystal-clear waters, perfect for swimming, snorkeling, and diving. The beach also offers all the necessary facilities, including a bar, restaurant, and showers, making it the ideal destination for a day out with family and friends.
