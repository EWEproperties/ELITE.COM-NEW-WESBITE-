Elite World Estates is proud to present an exceptional luxury villa located in the prestigious area of Campoamor on the southern Costa Blanca. This exclusive property is designed for the most discerning buyers seeking a premium home in Spain that combines contemporary architecture, outstanding craftsmanship, and complete privacy in one of the Mediterranean's most sought-after locations.

Offering approximately 585 m² of built area, the residence has been thoughtfully designed to create a seamless connection between the interior living spaces and the surrounding outdoor environment. Floor-to-ceiling windows flood the home with natural light while providing a smooth transition between the spacious living room, terraces, and landscaped garden.

The villa features five generous bedrooms and five elegant bathrooms. At the heart of the home is an impressive open-plan living area with a spacious lounge, dining area, and modern designer kitchen, creating the perfect setting for both everyday living and entertaining family and friends.

One of the property's most spectacular features is its stunning infinity swimming pool, which naturally extends the outdoor living area. Multiple terraces and a private rooftop solarium with an outdoor kitchen allow residents to enjoy the Mediterranean climate throughout the year while taking in panoramic views of the surrounding landscape and the sea.

The villa has been built using premium-quality materials and the latest construction technologies, ensuring exceptional comfort, energy efficiency, and security. An additional advantage is the possibility of personalising the finishes and interior details, allowing future owners to tailor the property to their individual preferences.

Situated in one of the most exclusive areas of the Costa Blanca, the villa is just minutes from the sandy beaches of Campoamor, prestigious golf courses, marinas, fine restaurants, and all essential amenities. Excellent connections to Alicante and Murcia airports make this an outstanding choice as a permanent residence, an exclusive holiday home, or a prestigious investment with strong long-term value.

Location

The property is situated in the prestigious residential area of Campoamor on the southern Costa Blanca, one of the most exclusive and highly desirable locations in the region. The area is renowned for its elegant villas, beautiful sandy beaches, and relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle, making it an ideal destination for both year-round living and luxury holidays.

Nearby, residents will find renowned golf courses, a modern marina, excellent restaurants serving local and international cuisine, cafés, supermarkets, and all essential services. Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the abundance of walking and cycling routes, water sports facilities, and year-round recreational opportunities.

Thanks to its excellent location, the beautiful beaches of Campoamor can be reached within just a few minutes, while the popular areas of Orihuela Costa, Cabo Roig, and La Zenia are also close by. La Zenia Boulevard, one of the largest shopping centres on the Costa Blanca, is only a short drive away. Convenient motorway access provides quick connections to both Alicante and Murcia International Airports.
