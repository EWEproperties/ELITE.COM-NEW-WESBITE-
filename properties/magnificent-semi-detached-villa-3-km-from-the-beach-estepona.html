Location

Estepona is a quiet town in the province of Malaga and one of the tourist resorts of the Costa del Sol. It is characterized by typical Andalusian low-rise buildings with narrow, patterned streets.

It is a paradise for artists and photographers.

The city is surrounded by the Sierra Bermeja Mountains, which dominate the entire landscape around Estepona.

It is one of the few places in the world where the mountain range is located just 10 km from the sea.

The beautiful views from the summit can be experienced by hiking along the designated trails.

One of the most interesting attractions in this picturesque town is the Orchidaria. The local orchid collection is the largest in Spain and is also considered one of the largest in Europe! Even if you're not a fan of botanical gardens, this place is worth seeing, if only for the array of colors and scents it offers. A true feast for the senses!

In Estepona, you'll find Playa de la Rada beach, which measures over 2,600 meters in length. There are many amenities nearby, including showers, umbrellas, toilets, pedal boats, playgrounds, and trampolines for children. For those who are hungry or not, there are seaside restaurants and bars (chiringuitos) serving local delicacies and drinks.
