Location

Calpe, located on the northern Costa Blanca, is a town that truly has it all. With its beautiful sandy beaches, crystal-clear water and lush vegetation, Calpe is a paradise for nature lovers. The town also boasts a rich cultural heritage, with charming streets, historical monuments and lively local markets.

Take a stroll along the promenade and enjoy the magnificent views of the Mediterranean Sea or visit the famous rock formation Peñon de Ifach, which rises from the sea like a natural fortress. For those who like outdoor activities, Calpe has a lot to offer, from hiking and cycling to water sports and fishing.

Calpe is quite well known for its quality fish restaurants, as well as for its incredible surroundings full of various vineyards hidden between the mountains.

Swimming in Calpe's turquoise waters, which are well known for their cleanliness, is amazing. There are 14 bays and beaches to choose from along the coastline, which runs for 13 km. On both sides of the majestic rock, there are two magnificent beaches, both with beautiful beaches and a good selection of cafés, bars and restaurants. La Fossa beach is located north of the Peñón de Ifach and is one of the most famous in Calpe, with a beautiful view of the magnificent Peñón de Ifach rock. This beach is about 950 metres long and has clear water and fine golden sand. It is a long, clean beach with lots of bars and cafés with Blue Flag status. It is backed by a wide promenade where you can sit and there is a children's playground on the beach.
