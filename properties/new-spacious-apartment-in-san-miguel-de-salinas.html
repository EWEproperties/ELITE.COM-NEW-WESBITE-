Location

San Miguel's central location means it is only a short drive to the lively Torrevieja, the beaches of Orihuela Costa or any of the golf courses in the area. The town has many amenities, such as restaurants, shops and international sports facilities. For a municipality of such limited size, the town has a very full calendar of festivals each year. The most famous of these is the fiesta in honour of the patron saint of San Miguel, from whom the town and church take their name. The most famous is the neighbourhood where you can still find dozens of cave houses. Most of them are now used as shops, art studios and even restaurants.

San Miguel de Salinas is located 75 metres above sea level. It is the highest town in this area of the southern Costa Blanca. Several places in San Miguel offer panoramic views of both the salt lakes of Torrevieja and the beaches of the Mediterranean Sea.

But it is not so much the slightly elevated location as the central location that has attracted many Europeans to buy a home here in recent decades. The relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere makes San Miguel an authentic Spanish town where life moves at a leisurely pace. The original inhabitants make up the vast majority of the population. Most home buyers have found their homes mainly in suburban developments such as El Galan, Blue Lagoon, Blue Hills and Las Filipinas.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala de lo Ferri, about 12 km by road, around 18 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio Público Gloria Fuertes, about 2.2 km.
Private school: Colegio San José de Calasanz, about 15 km, around 18 minutes by car.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 5 km, around 8 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud San Miguel de Salinas, about 2.2 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 9 km by road, around 11 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Eurosalinas, about 1.8 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 57 km by road, around 40 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 66 km, around 50 minutes by car.
