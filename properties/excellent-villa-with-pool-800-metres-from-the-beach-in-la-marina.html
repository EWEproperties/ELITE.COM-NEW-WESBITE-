Location

Located along the sun-drenched shores of the southern Costa Blanca, La Marina is a charming seaside district of the city of Elche that offers the perfect combination of relaxation and adventure. This picturesque area captivates visitors with its beautiful sandy beaches, vibrant local culture and warm Mediterranean climate. Discover the sandy Playa de La Marina beach with its crystal clear waters or take a leisurely stroll along the picturesque marina. Savour the local cuisine, fresh seafood and tapas at the beachfront restaurants. La Marina is conveniently located just a 30-minute drive from Alicante-Elche Airport, and there are regular bus services from nearby towns.

Known for its extensive fine sandy beaches and large pine forest, it is also famous for its tourist facilities, such as a luxury campsite, beach bars and leisure areas, all within the municipality of Elche. It is located between El Pinet and Les Pesqueres, near the N-332 road, about 10-13 km from the city of Elche and a short distance from Alicante airport.
