For sale: a modern villa located in Vera Playa on the Almería coast, just approximately 250 metres from the beach. The property offers 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, a private swimming pool and a spacious solarium, making it an excellent choice both as a comfortable holiday home and for year-round living.

The villa offers approximately 122 m² of built area and is situated on a private plot of around 227 m². Contemporary architecture, bright interiors and a functional layout have been designed with comfortable Mediterranean living in mind. Its south-east orientation provides excellent natural light throughout much of the day.

The heart of the home is a spacious open-plan living area combining the lounge, dining area and modern kitchen. Large glazed doors bring plenty of natural light into the interior and create a seamless connection with the outdoor spaces. The villa offers three comfortable bedrooms and three fully equipped bathrooms finished in a modern style.

One of the main highlights of the property is the approximately 35 m² solarium, providing an ideal space for relaxing, sunbathing or creating a private chill-out area. The design also includes a swimming pool next to the solarium area, allowing you to make the most of Almería’s sunny climate.

An additional option is the construction of an open-plan basement, which can be adapted to suit individual needs – as a gym, entertainment room, additional living space or storage area. According to the developer’s current offer, this option is available for €45,000 + VAT.

A private parking space is also provided within the plot.

Location

The villa is located in Vera Playa, a popular coastal area in the province of Almería. The sandy beach is only approximately 250 metres away, meaning the Mediterranean Sea can be reached within just a few minutes on foot.

Vera is known for its long coastline and wide sandy beaches, including El Playazo, Puerto Rey and Las Marinas-Bolaga. Restaurants, shops, recreational areas and other everyday services are available nearby, making the location suitable both for holidays and permanent residence.

This is an attractive option for buyers looking for a new villa with a private pool, generous outdoor space and a location very close to the sea on the Almería coast.
