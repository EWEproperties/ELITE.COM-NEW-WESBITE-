An exceptional residential development located in the picturesque hills of Istán, close to Marbella, surrounded by nature and offering beautiful views of the La Concepción reservoir, the mountains and the Costa del Sol coastline. The peaceful setting combines tranquillity with convenient access to Nueva Andalucía, Puerto Banús and the beaches of Marbella.

The intimate residential complex comprises 49 apartments with 2 and 3 bedrooms. The properties feature spacious terraces, while the penthouses also offer private rooftop solariums. The apartments provide approximately 98–110 m² of built area, with the largest terraces reaching approximately 96 m².

The interiors have been designed in a contemporary open-plan style, combining the living room, dining area and kitchen. Large windows provide plenty of natural light and direct access to the terraces. The kitchens come fully equipped, including Silestone worktops and Bosch appliances.

The properties also include ducted air conditioning with heating and cooling, an aerothermal system, built-in wardrobes and fully finished bathrooms. Each apartment comes with an underground parking space and a private storage room.

Residents can enjoy a communal swimming pool and extensive landscaped gardens that blend naturally with the surrounding environment.

One of the main advantages is the location – golf courses, international schools, shops and the amenities of Nueva Andalucía can be reached in approximately 10 minutes by car, while Puerto Banús and the Golden Mile are just a few kilometres away.

Estimated completion: October 2027.

Location

The development is situated in Istán, on the picturesque slopes of La Concha, surrounded by greenery and offering panoramic views of the La Concepción reservoir and the Marbella coastline. The location combines peace and proximity to nature with easy access to some of the most attractive destinations on the Costa del Sol.

Nueva Andalucía, international schools, renowned golf courses, shops and restaurants can be reached in approximately 10 minutes by car. Puerto Banús, the Golden Mile and the beaches of Marbella are only a few kilometres away, while Málaga Airport is approximately 60 km from the development.
