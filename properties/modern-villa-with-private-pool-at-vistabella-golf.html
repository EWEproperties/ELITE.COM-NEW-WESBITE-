Elite World Estates presents modern villas located in the prestigious Vistabella Golf area, one of the most attractive locations in the southern Costa Blanca. This is the perfect opportunity for those looking for a comfortable home surrounded by green landscapes, golf courses, and the Mediterranean lifestyle all year round.

The project consists of elegant properties with 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, designed in a contemporary style with large windows that fill the interiors with natural light. The open-plan living area seamlessly combines the living room, dining area, and kitchen, creating a functional space ideal for both everyday living and relaxation.

Each villa features a private garden, spacious terraces, and a private swimming pool, allowing residents to enjoy more than 300 sunny days per year. Selected properties also offer the option of a private rooftop solarium, providing an additional relaxation area with views of the surrounding landscape.

The standard specification includes a modern kitchen, fully equipped bathrooms, electric blinds in the bedrooms, fitted wardrobes, and high-quality finishes that ensure year-round comfort.

Expected completion date: September 2027.

Location

Vistabella Golf is one of the fastest-growing residential areas in southern Costa Blanca, situated between Los Montesinos and San Miguel de Salinas. It is the perfect location for those who wish to enjoy tranquillity, green surroundings, and a Mediterranean lifestyle while remaining close to the sea and the region’s main attractions.

At the heart of the area lies the prestigious 18-hole Vistabella Golf course, surrounded by modern residential developments, walking paths, and excellent infrastructure. Within the resort, residents can enjoy restaurants, cafés, a supermarket, a pharmacy, tennis courts, padel courts, and recreational areas, making everyday life comfortable and convenient.

Beach lovers will appreciate that the wide sandy beaches of Guardamar del Segura and Orihuela Costa can be reached in just 15–20 minutes. The vibrant city of Torrevieja, with its marina, promenades, shopping centres, restaurants, and lively atmosphere throughout the year, is also within easy reach.

Excellent road connections provide quick access to the entire Costa Blanca region, while Alicante International Airport is only around 40 minutes away by car. This makes the property ideal both as a holiday home and as a permanent residence.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Los Náufragos, about 18 km by road, around 19 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: IES Benejúzar, about 16 km, around 22 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio San José de Calasanz, about 11 km, around 14 minutes by car.
International school: Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen, about 14 km, around 16 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud de Benejúzar, about 15 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario Vega Baja, about 18 km by road, around 23 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Dicost Vistabella Supermarket, about 1.1 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 64 km by road, around 50 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 66 km, around 50 minutes by car.
