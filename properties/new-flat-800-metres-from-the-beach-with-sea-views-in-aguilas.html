Location

Águilas is a beautiful port city located on the Costa Cálida. It is 35 km from Lorca, 105 km from Murcia and 75 km from Cartagena. It is about a 2-hour drive from Alicante International Airport.

Its seafaring tradition is now combined with a wide range of tourist attractions, one of the main ones being its extensive coastline.

The town of Águilas has 28 kilometres of coastline with 35 beaches of different types of sand and thickness, located in different landscapes, from coves and unspoilt beaches in a natural environment to the urban beaches of the town of Águilas. The warm desert climate is present throughout the year with an average annual temperature of 22°, with mild temperatures in winter and very hot in summer.

Águilas is surrounded by the Sierra de la Carrasquilla, Lomo de Bas and Sierra de la Almenara mountain ranges, which are linked by a network of trails that converge in the Cabo Cope and Puntas de Calnegre nature parks. It is an ideal place for diving, water sports, hiking and golf.

In the centre of Águilas, it is worth visiting the beautiful gardens with centuries-old ficus trees in the Plaza de España, the town hall (19th century) and the parish church of San José, which houses a painting of the patron saint from the Salzillo school. At the top of the old quarter, on a promontory, stands the castle-fortress of San Juan de Águilas, built in 1579 for defensive purposes. At its foot is the port and the characteristic lighthouse, in operation since the mid-19th century, with black and white stripes.

Depending on the date you choose to visit, you can enjoy unique celebrations such as the Águilas Carnival, recognised as an event of international tourist interest. It usually takes place during the carnival season (February). You will be able to experience several days full of colour, joy, music, dance and fantasy.

With its varied Mediterranean cuisine based on seafood and garden produce, Águilas is the place for those palates that want to enjoy high-quality products. Among its standard products are red prawns, sea bream, sea bass, sea bream, tomatoes and capers.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa del Hornillo, about 600 m by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are IES Alfonso Escámez (about 2 km) and Colegio Carlos V (about 3 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Águilas Norte, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Policlínica Águilas, about 4 km by road, around 9 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Carrefour, about 1.2 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 73 km by road, around 55 minutes by car.
