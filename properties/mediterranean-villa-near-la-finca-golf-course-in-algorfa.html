Location

Algorfa, located in the Vega Baja del Segura region of Spain, is a town with a rich history dating back to Roman times. Its name comes from the Arabic word “Al-Gorfa”, which means “cave”, due to the numerous natural caves in the area. Today, Algorfa is a thriving town known for its citrus fruit and vegetable production and many attractions.

One of Algorfa's attractions is La Finca, a championship golf course set amid breathtaking natural scenery, offering magnificent views of the Vega Baja countryside. Designed for golfers of all levels, the course features top-of-the-line amenities such as a pro shop and restaurant.

Another must-see attraction is the Algorfa market, which takes place every Wednesday and offers a unique taste of local culture. Here, visitors can browse a wide range of goods, including fresh local produce, clothing and souvenirs. The market is also a great place to sample delicious dishes from the local, excellent cuisine.

Plaza de España is Algorfa's main square and a bustling hub of activity. It features a beautiful fountain, plenty of seating and a variety of restaurants and bars. It is the perfect place to soak up the local atmosphere, relax with a drink and admire the local culture and customs of the residents.
