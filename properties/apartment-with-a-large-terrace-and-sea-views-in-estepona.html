New apartments for sale in an exceptional area of Estepona on the Costa del Sol, surrounded by Mediterranean nature and offering beautiful sea views. The peaceful and green setting in the Selwo area provides privacy and tranquillity while offering convenient access to the beaches, Estepona town centre and some of the most renowned destinations on the western Costa del Sol.

The residential complex offers apartments and penthouses with 2, 3 or 4 bedrooms, designed to maximise space, natural light and the Mediterranean lifestyle. Large floor-to-ceiling windows connect the interiors with spacious terraces overlooking the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding landscape. Selected ground-floor apartments feature private gardens, while properties on the upper floors offer spacious private solariums.

The interiors combine contemporary design with functional layouts. Bright living rooms with open-plan kitchens and dining areas create spacious living spaces that flow naturally onto the terraces. Depending on the selected property, the apartments offer 2 or 3 bathrooms. Each property also includes a parking space in the underground garage and a storage room.

One of the main advantages of the complex is its extensive range of communal facilities, designed to create a private resort-style environment. Residents can enjoy an infinity pool with sea views, an outdoor swimming pool, children's pool, heated jacuzzi, spa, fully equipped gym and padel court. The development also features a coworking area, multifunctional room, relaxation areas and extensive landscaped gardens with walking paths.

Planned completion date: 30 October 2026.

Excellent location in Estepona

The development is situated in a peaceful area of Estepona, close to Selwo and surrounded by greenery and golf courses. Estepona town centre and the coastline are only a few minutes away by car. The location also provides convenient access to Cancelada, Benahavís, Puerto Banús and Marbella, which is approximately 18 km away.

The combination of sea views, extensive communal facilities and an excellent location makes these apartments an attractive choice both as a permanent residence in Estepona and as a second home on the Costa del Sol.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Guadalmansa, about 6 km by road, around 10 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio Público Cancelada (about 4 km) and The International School of Estepona (about 12 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud de San Pedro de Alcántara, about 12 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Hospiten de Estepona, about 3 km by road, around 7 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Tienda, about 4 km away.

How far is the airport?

Málaga Airport is about 78 km by road, around 60 minutes by car.
