Location

Jávea, also known as Xàbia, is located in the northern part of the Costa Blanca. Jávea has become a sought-after tourist destination, where more than half of the residents are international expats who have chosen this picturesque town as their permanent home. Jávea is renowned for its idyllic microclimate and is scientifically recognised as having one of the most favourable microclimates in the world, second only to Rio de Janeiro.

From Jávea there are breathtaking views reaching the coast of Ibiza, some 90 kilometres away. Convenient ferry services make the journey to Ibiza, a mecca for nightlife lovers and fun-seeking tourists.

Mount Montgo, rising to more than 750 metres, adorns the landscape as the highest point in the area, offering a vantage point to enjoy a panoramic view of the town and nearby islands, including Portichol and Ambolo. A visit to this spectacular site is an absolute must-see.

For those seeking relaxation, Jávea offers popular beaches such as Arenal, Sardinella, Granadella and Barraca. These sun-soaked shores provide the perfect backdrop for sunbathing, swimming and snorkelling in the crystal clear waters of the Mediterranean.

The historic centre of Jávea is a charming patchwork of stone buildings, historic churches and atmospheric restaurants and shops. Here you will find a range of services available in many languages, making it accessible to visitors from all over the world. A stroll along the waterfront will allow you to enjoy the panoramic views of the Mediterranean.
