Location

San Miguel de Salinas is located 75 metres above sea level. It is the highest town in this area of the southern Costa Blanca. Several places in San Miguel offer panoramic views of both the salt lakes of Torrevieja and the beaches of the Mediterranean Sea.

But it is not so much the slightly elevated location as the central location that has attracted many Europeans to buy a home here in recent decades. The relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere makes San Miguel an authentic Spanish town where life moves at a leisurely pace. The original inhabitants make up the vast majority of the population. Most home buyers have found their homes mainly in suburban developments such as El Galan, Blue Lagoon, Blue Hills and Las Filipinas.

San Miguel's central location means that it is only a short drive to the bustling Torrevieja, the beaches of Orihuela Costa or any of the golf courses in the area. The town has many amenities, such as restaurants, shops and international sports facilities. For a municipality of such limited size, the town has a very full calendar of festivals each year. The most famous of these is the fiesta in honour of the patron saint of San Miguel, from whom the town and church take their name. The most famous is the neighbourhood where you can still find dozens of cave houses. Most of them are now used as shops, art studios and even restaurants.
