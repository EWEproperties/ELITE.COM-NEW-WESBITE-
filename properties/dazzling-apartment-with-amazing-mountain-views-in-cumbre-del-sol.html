Location

Cumbre del Sol, a hidden gem along the stunning Costa Blanca in Spain where coastal beauty meets tranquility. Perched atop cliffs offering breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea, this picturesque residential area welcomes visitors with its awe-inspiring landscapes and laid-back charm.

The local beaches here are nothing short of paradise. Cala del Moraig boasts crystal-clear waters and dramatic cliffs, perfect for snorkelers and divers. Playa de Los Tiestos, a small sandy beach embraced by rugged cliffs, offers a peaceful escape for a leisurely day by the sea. For those seeking a hidden oasis, Cala Llebeig awaits at the end of a scenic hike, providing a tranquil and unspoiled setting.

Embrace the beauty of Cumbre del Sol through its various hiking trails like the "Ruta de los Acantilados" (Cliff Route) with captivating coastal views or the "Ruta del Faro" (Lighthouse Route) leading to the emblematic Cap d'Or lighthouse. For water enthusiasts, engage in kayaking, paddleboarding, and sailing in the calm waters of the nearby bays.

Experience the region's natural beauty on horseback with guided trails from the Cumbre del Sol Horse Riding Centre, offering a unique perspective of the area.

When it comes to dining, Cumbre del Sol offers a delightful culinary experience. Mirador del Mar presents Mediterranean cuisine with modern flair amidst mesmerizing sea views. Restaurante La Cumbre blends Spanish and international dishes on its terrace overlooking the sea. El Almendro charms visitors with traditional Spanish fare and delectable seafood.

Escape to Cumbre del Sol, where tranquility and coastal allure converge, providing you with a memorable experience of relaxation, adventure, and gastronomic delights. Immerse yourself in this coastal paradise and create cherished memories that will last a lifetime.
