A high quality home in a luxury resort!

Santa Rosalia Lake and Life Resort is a new gated complex offering a wide range of newly built villas and flats. This is where you will find your new Spanish home ready to move in ! In a prime location, just a stone's throw from the Mar Menor Golf Resort and a short drive from the sandy beaches and warm waters of the Mar Menor.

This luxury villa has two floors and features 3 elegant bedrooms, 3 fully equipped bathrooms and is fully furnished. Large and modern windows let in plenty of light and make the interiors of this development very cosy and bright. The colour scheme also plays an important role in this project. The best architects have focused on natural colours and a Mediterranean style to ensure that the residents enjoy harmony between the exterior and interior of the property.

The heart of the house, the spacious living area, is located on the ground floor. Here you will find a designer kitchen, which is fully equipped with everything you need to prepare delicious meals for you and your loved ones. The kitchen seamlessly connects to the living and dining area, creating a cosy and elegant living space, ideal for receiving guests or spending time with family and friends. On the same level you will also find 2 double bedrooms with private bathrooms and direct access to the garden.

The first floor, on the other hand, is a private sleeping area accessible only to the residents. The main, very comfortable and large bedroom is located here. All rooms have built-in wardrobes, associated and fully equipped bathrooms and direct access to the terraces.

The exterior part of the development also deserves a lot of attention because, mainly because of the sunny weather, we want to live or own a house for rent in Spain. . There will be enough space for children to play or to have dinners with friends at the barbecue. In addition, the development has a driveway for a car.

Combining comfortable living spaces, an open-plan layout and ready to move in immediately, the villa is ideal for holidays or year-round living.

Location

Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort Murcia is located 35 km from Murcia and 19 km from La Manga del Mar Menor. The nearest airport is the Murcia Region International Airport, 22 km away.

Mediterranean living at its best: Santa Rosalia Lake & Life Resort seeks to redefine the concept of luxury living by providing fantastic accommodation options for the whole family, in a five-star' complex with a total area of approximately 700,000 square metres.

Approximately 120,000 m² of landscaped grounds with various sports and leisure facilities, 16,000 native Mediterranean trees and plants, an impressive swimming lake and beach with facilities for water sports, paddle boarding and kayaking. Highly secure, it has access control and security services.

Beach Club

The Beach Club is the ideal place to meet for leisure activities or to enjoy a meal at the resort. The Santa Rosalia Beach Club offers spectacular views of the lake and beach, a terrace for relaxing and a play and relaxation area for children and adults. It is the ideal place for a good cold beer, lunch, dinner or a glass of wine with friends.

This is where some of the resort's activities are organised, including beach volleyball, kayaking, Adventure Golf and Pentanque tournaments.

Recycling

Santa Rosalía has a waste segregation system for collecting paper and cardboard, packaging and plastic, glass and other types of waste. Small things mean a lot when it comes to changing the world. In our commercial office, we use cardboard cups and make sure that we reduce our use of paper and recycle it. This policy is particularly important at the Beach Club.

Energy efficiency

LED lighting, controlled by solar clocks and low energy consumption, is installed throughout the resort. Energy efficiency is not just about saving money. It is an essential measure to help save the planet.
