For sale is a spacious, fully furnished villa located in Dehesa de Campoamor on Orihuela Costa, one of the most sought-after coastal locations on the southern Costa Blanca. The property is ready to move into and offers a total of 6 bedrooms, 5 bathrooms, a private swimming pool, garage, large terraces and a solarium.

The villa offers approximately 371 m² of built area and is set on a private plot of around 500 m². The interiors have been designed in a modern style, with plenty of natural light and a spacious living area connected to an open-plan kitchen. The property is sold with furniture, household appliances, lighting, decorations and curtains, allowing the new owners to enjoy the home practically immediately after purchase.

The main living area offers 4 spacious bedrooms and 3 bathrooms. An additional advantage is the large basement level, designed as a multifunctional space with the possibility of creating an additional living room and kitchen. This level also includes 2 further bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, making it ideal as an independent area for guests or family members.

Outside, there is a private swimming pool, garden and spacious terraces designed for relaxation and enjoying the Mediterranean climate. The property also features a solarium and private garage. The villa is equipped with air conditioning, heating, underfloor heating, a lift, laundry room and security entrance door.

Location

Dehesa de Campoamor is known for its beautiful coastline, green surroundings and excellent infrastructure. Nearby you will find the beaches of La Glea, Barranco Rubio and Aguamarina, as well as a marina, restaurants and facilities for water sports.

The beach and everyday services are located approximately 5 minutes by car from the property, while Alicante Airport is around 50 km away. This is an excellent location both for year-round living and as a comfortable holiday home on the Costa Blanca.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Aguamarina, about 1 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio de Educación Infantil y Primaria Número 20, about 5 km, around 10 minutes by car.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 10 km, around 17 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Pilar de Horadada Mil Palmeras Consultorio, about 5 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 9 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Consum, about 500 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 45 km by road, around 35 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 76 km, around 55 minutes by car.
