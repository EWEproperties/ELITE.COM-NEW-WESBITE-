Location

Vistabella Golf is a prestigious golf community located in the picturesque region of Orihuela, offering an unparalleled lifestyle for both golf and nature lovers. Located in the beautiful Costa Blanca region of Spain, the resort provides the perfect combination of modern amenities, natural surroundings and leisure opportunities.

The resort boasts a magnificent 18-hole golf course that challenges players of all skill levels. Designed to showcase the natural beauty of the region, the course features lush fairways, strategically placed bunkers and magnificent views of the surrounding countryside. Golfers can enjoy an unforgettable golfing experience while appreciating the Mediterranean climate and peaceful atmosphere.

For those who appreciate great beaches, the famous Playa de La Zenia is just a stone's throw away from Vistabella Golf Resort. This pristine beach, only a 20-minute drive from the resort, offers soft golden sand, crystal-clear water and a lively beach atmosphere. Whether you prefer sunbathing, swimming or water sports, Playa de La Zenia provides the perfect place to relax and have fun in the sun.

If you are looking for a lively city, the resort is conveniently located near the city of Torrevieja. With its lively marina, charming promenade and diverse range of restaurants and shops, Torrevieja offers a range of entertainment and dining options. Explore the local markets, savour fresh seafood or simply take a stroll along the coast to immerse yourself in the Mediterranean atmosphere.

Those interested in culture can take in nearby attractions such as the historic city of Murcia, known for its magnificent architecture and vibrant culture. The city's cathedral, museums and charming old town provide a captivating glimpse into Spain's rich heritage.

In terms of accessibility, Vistabella Golf Resort is approximately a 40-minute drive from Alicante Airport (ALC), which serves as the main international gateway to the region. Alicante Airport offers a wide range of domestic and international flights, making it easy for residents and visitors to reach the resort.

Whether you are looking for a round of golf, relaxation on the beach or cultural exploration, Vistabella Golf Resort and its surroundings offer a wealth of opportunities for a unique lifestyle.
