Location

Location-wise, Finestrat is a charming and picturesque town situated in the Alicante province, in the Valencian Community of Spain. It is a mere 8 km away from the popular and large city of Benidorm and 40 km from Alicante and its international airport. Renowned for its awe-inspiring mountain views, unspoiled beaches, and rich cultural heritage, Finestrat is the perfect place for anyone seeking a relaxing and unforgettable experience. There are a plethora of exciting activities to engage in while in Finestrat.

Located on the slopes of the Puig Campana mountain, Finestrat provides breathtaking views of the mountains, coast, and Mediterranean Sea. The town has a typical Moorish feel, with brightly colored houses and narrow cobbled streets. However, Cala Finestrat is also becoming increasingly popular, and its development has almost made it an extension of Benidorm.

Cala Finestrat is a seaside resort that is just three kilometers away from the bustling city of Benidorm. The resort has adequate amenities, an extensive selection of restaurants, bars, and shops. Everything you need is available in Benidorm, which is easily accessible by bus or taxi. La Cala beach is relatively quiet, as most visitors who prefer a more upbeat atmosphere go to the beaches surrounding Benidorm. The beach boasts a clean strip of golden sand, which is ideal for children and safe for swimming. Several lovely restaurants line the promenade, and during the summer months, there is an arts and crafts market in the afternoon.

Living in this enviable location offers residents an array of recreational amenities at their disposal. Aqua Natura, Terra Mítica, Terra Natura, Aqualandia, and Mundomar theme parks, as well as Sierra Cortina and Villaitana golf courses, are just a short drive away. Moreover, the location is ideal for various sports activities, such as hiking and climbing in Puig Campana or engaging in water sports.
