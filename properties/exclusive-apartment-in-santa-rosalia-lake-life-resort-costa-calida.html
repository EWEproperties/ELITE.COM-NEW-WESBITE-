Discover an exceptional lifestyle at Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort, one of the most prestigious residential resorts in the Murcia region. Located just 4 km from the sandy beaches of Los Alcázares and the Mar Menor, this exclusive gated community combines luxury living, nature, sports, and year-round sunshine on the beautiful Costa Cálida.

The resort covers approximately 700,000 m² of landscaped grounds and is centered around a spectacular 16,000 m² crystal-clear lagoon surrounded by white sandy beaches, palm trees, beach clubs, water sports facilities, walking and cycling paths, restaurants, and leisure areas. Santa Rosalía offers a unique Mediterranean lifestyle and is one of the most sought-after property destinations in southern Spain.

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is only 20 minutes away, while Alicante Airport can be reached in approximately 50 minutes.

The development offers modern 2 and 3-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms, designed to maximize comfort, natural light, and stunning views over the lagoon and resort gardens. The open-plan layout seamlessly connects the living and dining area with a fully equipped contemporary kitchen, creating a bright and spacious interior. Large sliding doors lead directly to generous terraces where you can enjoy the Mediterranean climate throughout the year.

The bedrooms have been designed as peaceful retreats, featuring fitted wardrobes and high-quality finishes. Elegant bathrooms with modern fittings and premium materials provide a sophisticated spa-inspired atmosphere.

Residents benefit not only from the incredible facilities of Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort but also from a beautiful communal swimming pool with sunbathing areas reserved exclusively for apartment owners. Each property includes a private parking space.

Location

Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort Murcia is located 35 km from Murcia and 19 km from La Manga del Mar Menor. The nearest airport is Murcia International Airport, 22 km away.

Mediterranean living at its best: Santa Rosalia Lake & Life Resort seeks to redefine the concept of luxury living, providing fantastic accommodation options for the whole family in a five-star complex with a total area of approximately 700,000 square meters.

Approximately 120,000 m2 of landscaped land with various sports and recreation centers, 16,000 native Mediterranean trees and plants, an impressive swimming lake and a beach with facilities for water sports, paddle boarding and kayaking. Highly secure, has access control and security services.

Beach Club

The Beach Club is the perfect place to meet to hang out or dine at the resort. Santa Rosalia Beach Club offers spectacular views of the lake and beach, a terrace for relaxing and a play and relaxation area for children and adults. It's the perfect place for a good cold beer, lunch, dinner or a glass of wine with friends.

This is where some of the resort's attractions are held, including beach volleyball, kayaking, Adventure Golf and Pentanque tournaments.

Recycling

Santa Rosalía has a waste separation system to collect paper and cardboard, packaging, plastic, glass and other types of waste. Little things mean a lot when it comes to changing the world. In our sales office we use cardboard cups and we make sure to reduce paper consumption and recycle it. This policy is especially important at the Beach Club.

Energy efficiency

LED lighting is installed throughout the facility, controlled by sundials and low energy consumption. Energy efficiency isn't just about saving money. This is an essential measure that will help save our planet.
