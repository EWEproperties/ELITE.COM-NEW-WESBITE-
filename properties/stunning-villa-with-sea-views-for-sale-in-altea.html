Location

Altea in Spain is considered one of the most beautiful cities. Beautiful beaches and promenades, whitewashed houses with colorful flowerpots, narrow cobbled streets, small handicraft shops, ice cream parlors and open-air restaurants are not all that this picturesque city has to offer.

Altea is located a 10-minute drive north of Benidorm (approximately 10 km), approximately 1 hour from Alicante and is one of the most memorable coastal towns on the Costa Blanca, retaining its unique Spanish charm despite increasing tourism and popularity. Altea is located directly by the sea, in the middle of a beautiful bay and is surrounded by hilly mountains. The name comes from the Moorish Althaya' which means health for all'.

The town center is a treasure trove of shops, art galleries, restaurants and small whitewashed buildings decorated with flowers - all waiting to be discovered on the winding and cobbled medieval streets.

The old town of Altea is preserved in a typically Spanish, or one could even say Andalusian, atmosphere. Whitewashed houses provide a blank canvas for Mediterranean flower beds. It's no wonder that Altea attracts artists and photographers from all over the world who want to experience the city's charm, incredible light and natural beauty. From cobbled streets, stunning views of the sea and mountains, to historic buildings.

Countless steps of narrow, charming streets lead from the coast to the Altea viewpoint. In the 16th century, the upper part of the old town of Altea was built to protect the city from attacks. The castle was built on what is now the Plaza de la Iglesia, and the upper part of the city was surrounded by a wall with three gates. Currently, only two of these gates exist, Vell Portal and Portal Nou.

The icon of the city is the famous Virgin de Consuelo church with a blue dome on the top - it is really worth seeing, and the view from the top of the bay of Altea with the mountains in the background is simply spectacular! The dome is a kind of symbol of the divinity of this city. Altea is a visually beautiful, atmospheric and incredibly charming city! It's hard to describe the mood here in words. The church was built in the 19th century with a magnificent interior with classic gold and floral motifs.

Altea's location at the foot of the 1,100-meter high Sierra Bernia, with its highest peak at 1,129 meters, and the 1,500-meter Sierra Aitana to the west, creates a magical atmosphere in the area. The mountain protects against cold winds from the interior. The mountainside lies like a green oasis all the way to the fertile lowlands and the sea. The river that flows through the city and thanks to which it is here is called Algar, which means healing river.

Altea and other towns in the north are among the greenest and most fertile areas of the entire Costa Blanca. Oranges and many exotic fruits are grown here.
