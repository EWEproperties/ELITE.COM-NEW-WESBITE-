Location

Calpe, a charming coastal town on the Mediterranean Sea, entices with its undeniable charm and abundance of attractions. Located in the province of Alicante in Spain, Calpe offers a delightful mix of natural beauty, historical sites and vibrant attractions. Located approximately 75 kilometres from Alicante Airport, Calpe provides convenient access for travellers wishing to explore its wonders.

Upon arrival, you will be delighted by the magnificent beaches, which have earned prestigious Q' certificates for quality and Blue Flags. Calpe boasts some of the most beautiful sandy coastlines in the region, including the iconic Playa de la Fossa and Playa de la Arenal-Bol. Immerse yourself in Mediterranean bliss, bask in the sun, or enjoy the water sports and activities that are an integral part of Calpe's coastal landscape.

Beyond its picturesque beaches, Calpe is adorned with historical landmarks and cultural treasures. The distinctive Peñón de Ifach, a massive limestone rock formation, is the symbol of Calpe and offers breathtaking panoramic views. Hike to the top and admire the natural beauty that unfolds before your eyes. The old town, with its charming narrow streets and historic architecture, invites you to stroll and discover its rich heritage.

Calpe's bustling fishing port is a must-see. Watch the fishermen bring in their daily catch, visit the fish market and enjoy fresh seafood in the numerous restaurants along the harbour. To learn about the local culture, visit the Museum of Archaeology and Ethnography to delve into the fascinating history of Calpe.

Nature lovers will find solace in the nature parks surrounding Calpe. Explore Las Salinas Nature Park, a paradise for migratory birds such as flamingos and storks, or head to Serra Gelada Nature Park to see its rugged cliffs and captivating trails. For hiking enthusiasts, the Sierra de Bernia and Sierra de Oltà offer exciting trails and magnificent views.

Immerse yourself in the lively atmosphere of Calpe's local markets, where you can discover fresh produce, handicrafts and traditions. Sample the local cuisine, renowned for its seafood, rice dishes and spicy tapas.
