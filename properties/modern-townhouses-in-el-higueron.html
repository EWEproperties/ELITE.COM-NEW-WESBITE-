Elite World Estates proudly presents an exclusive residential development of contemporary townhouses located in the prestigious area of El Higuerón, one of the most desirable and fast-growing locations on the Costa del Sol. Designed for buyers seeking a modern home in Spain, this exceptional development combines sustainable architecture, innovative construction, energy efficiency and premium lifestyle amenities in a peaceful natural setting.

The development comprises 40 elegant three-bedroom townhouses, created using an advanced industrialised construction system that ensures superior build quality, greater precision and improved energy performance. Every home has been thoughtfully designed to offer maximum comfort, contemporary style and long-term sustainability.

Bright open-plan interiors, generous living spaces and large windows allow natural light to flow throughout each home while enhancing thermal and acoustic comfort. Every residence incorporates energy-efficient technologies, including solar energy solutions, smart home systems and environmentally friendly materials that reduce energy consumption and support a more sustainable way of living.

Designed to encourage a healthy and active lifestyle, the beautifully landscaped communal areas offer an outstanding collection of resort-style amenities. Residents can enjoy a spectacular infinity swimming pool, a separate children's pool, an outdoor social club, a sauna, an urban vegetable garden, landscaped walking paths and dedicated wellness areas ideal for yoga, meditation and relaxation while surrounded by nature.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its exceptional location in El Higuerón, a prestigious residential area perfectly positioned between Fuengirola and Benalmádena. Residents enjoy easy access to beautiful beaches, renowned golf courses, international schools, restaurants, sports facilities and all essential daily services, while maintaining the privacy and tranquillity of an exclusive residential community.

The vibrant centre of Fuengirola, its marina and sandy beaches are only a short drive away, while Marbella and Málaga can be reached in approximately 20 minutes. Málaga International Airport is also conveniently located nearby, making the development an excellent choice for both permanent living and holiday homes.

Combining cutting-edge architecture, sustainable design, premium amenities and an outstanding location, this exclusive collection of townhouses offers a unique opportunity to enjoy a modern Mediterranean lifestyle while investing in one of the Costa del Sol's most promising residential destinations.

Estimated completion: Q4 2028.
