For sale, an exceptional Ibiza-style villa in Jávea, located in the prestigious and peaceful residential area of Monte Olimpo on the northern Costa Blanca. The property combines contemporary architecture with distinctive Mediterranean design, offering beautiful sea and mountain views, privacy and comfortable living throughout the year.

The villa is designed on a single level and offers 4 spacious bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms and an additional guest toilet. The heart of the home is a bright living room connected to the dining area and a modern open-plan kitchen. Large windows provide plenty of natural light and create a seamless connection between the interior living space, terrace and garden.

The outdoor area has been designed to make the most of the Mediterranean climate throughout much of the year. A large terrace, private swimming pool and Mediterranean garden create a comfortable outdoor living and relaxation area overlooking the surrounding landscape. The plot measures approximately 1,000 m², providing plenty of space and privacy.

The architecture of the property is inspired by the style of Ibiza, with white façades, natural materials, clean lines and subtle Mediterranean details giving the villa an elegant and timeless character. The property will be finished using high-quality materials and modern solutions designed to ensure everyday comfort.

Excellent location in Jávea

The property is situated in the sought-after Monte Olimpo area of Jávea, surrounded by greenery and tranquillity while remaining within easy reach of beaches and everyday amenities. The popular Playa del Arenal is approximately 7 minutes away by car, while restaurants, shops and supermarkets are also conveniently located nearby.

Jávea is one of the most desirable destinations on the northern Costa Blanca, renowned for its beautiful coves, sandy beaches, seafront restaurants and picturesque setting between the mountains and the Mediterranean Sea.

This is an excellent opportunity for anyone looking for a modern villa with a private pool and sea views in Jávea, whether as a permanent residence or an exclusive holiday home on the Costa Blanca.
