For sale is an exclusive 3-bedroom penthouse located in the green surroundings of Las Colinas Golf & Country Club on the Costa Blanca. The property forms part of a low-density residential development consisting of two elegant buildings that blend naturally into the surrounding landscape. One of its main highlights is the private solarium of more than 100 m², offering an impressive outdoor space for relaxing, sunbathing and entertaining, with open views over the golf course and Mediterranean surroundings.

The interior has been designed in a bright, contemporary style. The main living area combines a spacious lounge, dining area and open-plan kitchen. Large windows provide plenty of natural light and create a seamless connection with the terrace. The penthouse offers approximately 141–142 m² of built area, a terrace of around 39 m² and a private solarium of more than 100 m².

The modern kitchen is fitted and equipped with appliances including an induction hob, oven, microwave, refrigerator and dishwasher. Year-round comfort is ensured by ducted air conditioning with a heat pump, while the bathrooms feature electric underfloor heating. The specification also includes built-in wardrobes, double glazing and electric blinds.

Residents can enjoy beautifully landscaped communal areas surrounded by Mediterranean greenery, as well as two swimming pools, one of them heated. The penthouse also includes a private parking space and storage room.

The project also stands out for its high energy efficiency. It incorporates an aerothermal system for hot water, photovoltaic panels for the communal areas, heat-recovery ventilation and smart systems for controlling lighting and blinds. The buildings have an Energy Efficiency Certificate A.

Completion: May 2027.

Location

Las Colinas Golf & Country Club is one of the most distinctive locations in the southern Costa Blanca — a private, carefully designed resort set within an approximately 330-hectare valley, surrounded by hills and Mediterranean nature. Access is through an impressive palm-lined canyon, while controlled entry provides residents with privacy, peace and security.

It is an ideal location for those who want to combine a peaceful natural setting with access to high-quality facilities. The resort borders extensive protected natural areas, and its low-density architecture has been designed to blend harmoniously into the landscape. With more than 300 days of sunshine per year, the area is perfect for enjoying terraces, gardens and outdoor activities throughout most of the year.

Residents have access to a wide range of facilities, including a renowned golf course, restaurants, a sports club and fitness centre, tennis and padel courts, sports academies, a minimarket and scenic walking trails. The resort also offers 24-hour security, reinforcing its private and exclusive character.

Another major advantage is the proximity to the sea — the Mediterranean coast is only around 4.5 km away. Alicante Airport can be reached in approximately 50 minutes, while Murcia Airport is around 45 minutes away, making the property an excellent choice both as a second home in Spain and for regular holiday stays.

This is a location for those looking for more than just an apartment on the Costa Blanca — privacy, nature, space, security and a high standard of living, while still remaining close to beaches, services and the region’s main transport connections.
