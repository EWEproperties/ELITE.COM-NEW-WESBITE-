For sale: an exceptional villa located in the prestigious surroundings of Las Colinas on the Costa Blanca. The property stands out for its contemporary architecture inspired by the Mediterranean landscape, large windows and the use of natural materials. Light façades combined with stone and subtle earthy tones allow the building to blend harmoniously with the surrounding greenery. According to the specifications, the façade incorporates natural stone and lime-render finishes, while the interiors are designed in a light, neutral colour palette.

The heart of the home is a spacious open-plan living area comprising the lounge, dining room and modern kitchen. Large floor-to-ceiling windows fill the interiors with natural light and create a seamless connection with the generous terraces. Light colours are complemented by wooden elements and warm, natural textures, giving the interior a contemporary yet welcoming character.

The kitchen has been designed as an elegant part of the open-plan living space. The specifications include Egger cabinetry, a Living Ceramics Lithotech worktop and sink, as well as Franke fittings. The minimalist design perfectly complements the natural aesthetic of the property.

Great attention has also been paid to the bathrooms. The specification includes suspended Bernstein toilets, Sanycces fittings in brushed rose gold, Geberit systems and Marazzi finishes in the shower areas. Carefully selected materials create an elegant and cohesive interior.

Built-in wardrobes and interior joinery have been designed in light tones with decorative details and natural textures. The specification includes Natortex and Dauby elements as well as finishes in RAL 1013.

One of the main highlights of the property is its generous outdoor space. Large terraces form a natural extension of the living and dining areas, providing an ideal setting for relaxing, dining outdoors and enjoying the Mediterranean climate throughout much of the year. Thanks to the extensive glazing, the surrounding greenery and natural landscape become an integral part of the interior.

Location

The development is located within Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, in an exceptionally green and peaceful area of the southern Costa Blanca, between San Miguel de Salinas and Campoamor. It is a gated, carefully landscaped resort surrounded by Mediterranean nature, offering a golf course and a wide range of facilities for residents.

The location provides privacy and tranquillity while still allowing easy access by car to the beaches of Orihuela Costa, restaurants, shops and the coastal areas of Campoamor and Cabo Roig. It is an ideal setting for those who wish to live close to nature without giving up convenient access to services and the coast.
