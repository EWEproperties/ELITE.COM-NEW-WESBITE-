Location

Los Alcázares is a charming coastal town located in the Murcia region on the sunny Costa Cálida. The town is known for its pleasant climate, relaxed atmosphere, and direct access to the Mar Menor — the largest saltwater lagoon in Europe, famous for its warm, shallow waters and healthy microclimate all year round.

Thanks to excellent transport connections with cities such as Cartagena, Murcia, and Alicante, as well as quick access to motorways and airports, Los Alcázares is an ideal destination both for permanent living and holiday stays.

The town is famous for its wide sandy beaches stretching along the calm waters of the Mar Menor. It is a perfect place for families with children, water sports enthusiasts, and anyone looking to relax by the sea. Popular beaches such as Playa de Los Narejos and Playa de Las Salinas offer beautiful promenades, charming chiringuitos, and excellent conditions for sailing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and windsurfing.

Los Alcázares also offers a wonderful Mediterranean lifestyle and a rich gastronomic scene. Numerous restaurants and bars serve fresh seafood, traditional paella, local tapas, and international cuisine. Evening walks along the promenade, cafés overlooking the sea, and the welcoming atmosphere make this destination attractive throughout the entire year.

The town also provides a wide range of amenities and attractions — from boutiques and local shops to sports centers, marinas, and recreational areas. Active lifestyle lovers will appreciate the cycling routes, water sports, and proximity to renowned golf courses such as La Serena Golf, Roda Golf, and Mar Menor Golf Resort.

This is a truly special place where Mediterranean lifestyle, beautiful beaches, and modern infrastructure create ideal conditions for relaxation, investment, and living in Spain.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Punta Brava, about 5 km by road, around 8 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Grupo Escolar Los Alcázares (about 3 km) and I.E.S. ANTONIO MENÁRGUEZ COSTA (about 4 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Los Alcázares, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Centro Sociosanitario 'Cristo de los Mineros', about 13 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Dialprix, about 3 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 43 km by road, around 30 minutes by car.
