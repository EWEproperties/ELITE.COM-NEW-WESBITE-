La Quinta in Benahavís is one of the most luxurious urbanizations in all of Europe. This is a place where luxury construction abounds: clean lines, modern design and technology, maximum energy efficiency, integration with the landscape and the environment. These are just some of the features of real estate in Benahavis that make investing in this area increasingly popular. In Benahavís, luxury is more than a lifestyle, it is a haven in itself.

We are proud to present a new, exclusive project consisting of 18 individually designed villas located on the 200-hectare area of the prestigious La Quinta Golf Club. Surrounded by a wonderful area bordering a UNESCO biosphere reserve. These stunning and ultra-modern villas are located just 15 minutes from Marbella. The views of the Mediterranean Sea, the Strait of Gibraltar and the African coast are simply spectacular.

These designer houses will be very different from all investments previously built in this elite area. The 18 villas, as well as the furniture and accessories they contain, differ from each other. What they have in common, apart from their stunning views, is that they all feature the highest standard of design and feature a level of innovation and creativity rarely seen in residential development.

Home automation and general lighting are of the highest quality, making each villa a truly smart home. All villas are located on separate plots to ensure maximum privacy, with large terraces, large gardens, real infinity pools and spaces that can be used as entertainment rooms, gyms and home offices.

This concept is crucial in the design of any villa, where the living room and terrace seamlessly merge to create one spacious living space. In key parts of the villa, windows hide in the walls, providing unobstructed panoramic views of the idyllic nature.

All the amenities you could need are at your fingertips, including golf, water sports on the lake, a gourmet restaurant, bar and beach bar, exclusive spa, tennis courts and a selection of shops. These amenities, combined with the comprehensive facilities in each villa, mean owners will never have to leave the resort unless they want to.

Building today without jeopardizing tomorrow is the core value of the project, which is undergoing BREEAM certification. Design improvements resulting from this certification result in sustainable homes that improve the well-being of the people who live in them, help protect natural resources and make property investment more attractive.

Location

In Benahavís, nature, gastronomy, high-quality architecture and luxury coexist in perfect harmony. The commune has the most mountainous topography of the entire western Costa del Sol, many golf courses with carefully developed landscapes, a gastronomy that dances between the mountains and the sea and an architecture that simultaneously combines ancient Arabic features with the most modern ones on the market.

This white city is embedded in a landscape of rich flora and fauna both aesthetically, functionally and ecologically, allowing visitors to enjoy the three rivers that cross it: Guadaiza, Guadalmansa and Guadalmina. Read on to learn more about this white city characterized by clean air and modernity. From its height you can see 100 kilometers of the Spanish coast and even the north of the African continent.

Golf courses

The golf courses in Benahavis were designed by world-class golf course designers such as Robert Trent Jones, Severiano Ballesteros, and Jack Niclaus. In Benahavis, the golf season lasts all year round. The mild climate, great infrastructure, and delicious restaurants attract fans of this sport from all over the world. You can play in 12 clubs, including Los Flamingos Golf Club, La Quinta Golf & Country Club, La Zagaleta and Club de Golf Benahavís. The greenery of golf courses is an integral part of the Benahavis landscape. This is another reason why property investors in Spain choose Benahavis.

Restaurants

Benahavis is also famous for the best restaurants in Andalusia. We recommend visiting one of them: El Abanico, Bodegon Gallery, Rufino or Lemon Grass. El Abanico serves local and European cuisine, including vegetarian dishes. Similarly at Bodegon Gallery and Rufino. If you want Asian cuisine, it's best to visit Lemon Grass - the local Thai chef specializes in Asian Fusion, and the restaurant itself is called the best Asian cuisine on the Costa del Sol.

A place full of possibilities

It's impossible not to like Benahavis, it's a perfect place to relax, meet friends in restaurants and cafes or on the golf course. It is also a great base for hiking, cycling and swimming. Art lovers will also find something for themselves here: in the David Marschall gallery you can buy beautiful sculptures, inspired by nature, combined with ready-made objects - lathe chips, railway sleepers. The artist creates abstract sculptures, figures that are a combination of ceramics, glass and metal, wood and other materials.
