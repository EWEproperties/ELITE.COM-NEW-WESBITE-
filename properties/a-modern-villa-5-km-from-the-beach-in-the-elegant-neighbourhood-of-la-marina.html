Location

San Fulgencio is a town located in the south of the province of Alicante. It borders Daya Vieja, Dolores, Elche, Formentera del Segura, Guardamar del Segura and Rojales. It belongs to the Vega Baja region.

Although it is inland, it has numerous residential areas (La Marina, El Oasis, La Escuera, Bonavista, Las Pesqueras, etc.) that are located by the sea and are mostly inhabited by foreigners, giving it a cosmopolitan atmosphere.

Located in the middle of the Vega Baja of the Segura River, between fields of vegetables, cereals and orange trees, San Fulgencio is a town with a completely flat terrain, except for the north, where it is protected by the Sierra del Molar. It is only 2 km from the coast.

Although the municipality has traditionally been involved in agriculture, today its main activities are the service sector and tourism.

San Fulgencio is 15 km from Elche and 36 km from Alicante.

WHAT TO SEE

- San Fulgencio Church. A parish church built in the 18th century. Although it retains its Baroque style, it stands out for its austerity and simplicity. Its interior has a Greek cross plan, with a dome in the centre, with three naves, the narrowest and longest of which reaches the apse, where the main altar, tabernacle and image of the patron saint are located. Fulgencia.

- Archaeological Museum. Created in 1993, this museum houses a number of objects found in the municipality. It mainly collects Iberian and Greek ceramics.

LOCAL EVENTS

- Celebrations of San Fulgencio and San Antonio Abad. On 16 and 17 January, there are processions in honour of both saints, the blessing of animals and bread, and a traditional fraternal meal for all the town's inhabitants.

- Pilgrimage of San Isidro Labrador On the Sunday after 15 May, a pilgrimage is held in honour of the patron saint of farmers. The saint is transported by ox cart to Paraje El Oasis, where a mass is celebrated.

- Celebration of Our Lady of Peace. The summer festivities took place on the first weekend of August in the La Marina Urbanisation.

- Fiestas de la Virgen del Remedio In October, there are festivities in honour of the patron saint. Street decorations, parades, floral offerings to the Virgin and fireworks.
