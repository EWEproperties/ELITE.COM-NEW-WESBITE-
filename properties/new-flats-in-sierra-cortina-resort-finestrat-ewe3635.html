New apartments for sale in Finestrat on the Costa Blanca, located in the peaceful and green area of Sierra Cortina, just a short distance from Benidorm. The development stands out for its contemporary architecture, low-rise design and generous outdoor spaces, created to make the most of the sunny Mediterranean climate.

This is an excellent option for those looking for a peaceful setting away from the busier coastal areas, while still enjoying easy access to beaches, shops, restaurants and everyday amenities. The intimate character of the complex and its Mediterranean surroundings create an attractive environment for both year-round living and holidays on the Costa Blanca.

The complex offers apartments with 1, 2 or 3 bedrooms in a variety of layouts. Buyers can choose from ground-floor properties with private gardens, upper-floor apartments with spacious terraces and penthouses with large private solariums. Depending on the property, private gardens reach approximately 98 m², while solariums offer up to around 59 m² of additional outdoor space.

The interiors have been designed in a bright, contemporary style, with particular attention to functionality and natural light. The main living area features an open-plan layout combining the living room, dining area and modern kitchen. Large windows provide direct access to the terrace, creating a natural connection between the indoor and outdoor spaces.

The terraces are designed as an extension of the living area, providing plenty of room for outdoor dining and relaxation. Ground-floor apartments feature private gardens, offering additional space to enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle. The penthouses include private solariums, ideal for sunbathing, creating a chill-out area or simply relaxing outdoors.

A variety of layouts and sizes makes it possible to choose a property suited to different needs, from more compact apartments to spacious penthouses with generous outdoor areas. Available properties offer total areas ranging from approximately 64 m² to almost 200 m².

Estimated completion date: first quarter of 2028.

Location

Finestrat is one of the attractive residential locations on the northern Costa Blanca, combining a peaceful setting with convenient access to the coast and nearby Benidorm. Sierra Cortina offers a relaxed residential atmosphere while remaining within easy reach of all the amenities needed for everyday life.

The surrounding area offers shops, restaurants, shopping centres, sports and leisure facilities, as well as a wide range of attractions for the whole family. The sandy beaches and seafront promenades of Benidorm and Finestrat can also be reached within a short drive.

The location also provides convenient connections to other destinations along the Costa Blanca and to Alicante. This makes the property an attractive choice as a second home in Spain, a permanent residence or a base for regular stays on the Costa Blanca.
