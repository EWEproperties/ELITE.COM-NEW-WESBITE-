Location

Benijófar is a charming town located in the southern part of the Costa Blanca region, in the province of Alicante, Spain. Its excellent location makes it an ideal place for those seeking a peaceful environment without sacrificing the convenience and comfort of living in the heart of the city.

Benijófar is only a 20-minute drive from Alicante Airport, making it easily accessible for international travellers.

Benijófar is a short drive from some of the best beaches in the area, including Guardamar and La Mata. These beautiful beaches offer soft golden sand, clear blue water and plenty of amenities, including restaurants, bars and water sports.

In addition to its beautiful beaches, Benijófar offers a wide range of attractions and places to visit. Golf enthusiasts can play golf at one of the many golf courses in the area, including La Finca Golf and Villamartin Golf. Those who love nature can visit the nearby Laguna de La Mata Nature Park, which is home to many species of birds.

In the town itself, visitors can explore the historic centre with its charming narrow streets and beautiful market square. There are also several restaurants and bars where visitors can sample the local cuisine and get a taste of Spanish culture.
