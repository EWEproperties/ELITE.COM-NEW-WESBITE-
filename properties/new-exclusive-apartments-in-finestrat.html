Location

Finestrat is a town in the province of Alicante on the Costa Blanca. It is located just 8 km from the popular large town of Benidorm and 40 km from Alicante and its international airport. Known for its beautiful mountain views, pristine beaches and rich cultural heritage, Finestrat is the perfect place for anyone looking for a relaxing and unforgettable experience. There is no shortage of exciting things to do in Finestrat.

The town is located on the slopes of Puig Campana, offering magnificent views of the mountains, the coast and the Mediterranean Sea. The town has a typical Moorish atmosphere, with colourfully painted houses and narrow cobbled streets. On the other hand, Cala Finestrat is becoming increasingly popular, and its development has made it almost an extension of Benidorm itself.

Cala Finestrat

Cala Finestrat is a seaside resort located just three kilometres from the bustling town of Benidorm. The resort offers adequate facilities, a wide selection of restaurants, bars and shops. Everything you need is available in Benidorm, which is easily accessible by bus and taxi.

La Cala beach is quite quiet, as most of those seeking a more upbeat atmosphere head for the beaches surrounding Benidorm. The beach is a clean stretch of golden sand, ideal for children and safe for swimming. There are several charming restaurants along the promenade, and in the summer months there is an arts and crafts market in the afternoon.

The place is suitable for families and couples looking for a relaxing beach resort, yet close to many attractions and amenities. Nearby are the Terre Mitica and Terra Natura theme parks, the Aqualandia water park and the Mundomar marine and exotic animal park, which offer fun for the whole family.

If you prefer to spend your holiday on a round or two of golf, Finestrat will delight you with the nearby Real Faula golf club, which offers two excellent golf courses.

Both Finestrat and Cala Finestrat are quiet destinations compared to some of the neighbouring resorts. Both offer a selection of local bars and restaurants where you can enjoy good food and a relaxed atmosphere.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala de Finestrat, about 4 km by road, around 7 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Balcó de Finestrat, about 2.9 km.
Private school: Col·legi Nuestra Señora de los Dolores, about 8 km, around 11 minutes by car.
International school: Elian's British School La Nucía, about 11 km, around 16 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Cala de Benidorm, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Comarcal de la Marina Baixa, about 4 km by road, around 10 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

How far is the airport?
