In the heart of the luxurious Cumbre del Sol estate, in the Lirios district, there is this unique villa that offers its residents the highest standard of living and breathtaking sea views. This unique location offers a fantastic opportunity to live in luxury on the beautiful Costa Blanca and enjoy all the benefits this area has to offer.

The villa has as much as 740 square meters of living space, spread over three floors, with a total of 3 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms and a large basement allowing us to expand it to 4 bedrooms, or adapt the space to our preferences (gym, office, games area... .). Access to this spectacular villa is via the upper floor, with covered parking for two vehicles and a lift that connects the entire house. The design of the residence combines modern and Mediterranean architectural styles in a very elegant and functional way. Its façade, characterized by large geometric shapes created by diagonal lines, is dominated by white and natural stone in harmony with the beautiful surroundings.

On the ground floor we find the living area, where large open spaces and natural light are the protagonists of the day and where the boundaries between interior and exterior are blurred thanks to large panels of glass walls that also give access to the garden and pool area. The kitchen is equipped with modern appliances from leading brands, has a central island and plenty of storage space.

The sleeping area, consisting of two bedrooms, a suite with bathroom, dressing room and private terrace, and a shared bathroom, is located on the same floor. All 3 bedrooms have access to the terrace from which you can admire one of the best sea views on the Costa Blanca. Waking up to watch the sun rise over the horizon of the Mediterranean Sea is one of the many luxuries this wonderful villa has to offer.

Outside there is a covered area where you can enjoy socializing while being protected from the sun. A huge Mediterranean-style garden, topped with an infinity pool that blends with the blue of the sea, along with the mild climate of the Northern Costa Blanca, invites you to enjoy long days outdoors practically all year round. The location will allow you to enjoy a quiet and comfortable environment.

In addition, the villa has an underfloor heating system, as well as air conditioning with hot and cold air, domotic systems, ECOdan ....).

Location

Cumbre del Sol occupies 3.7 million square meters of land on the northern Costa Blanca in Spain. All aspects of its design and construction have been developed to provide maximum comfort and the best services to residents and guests, protecting the natural environment and integrating green areas. It is precisely located in the town of Benitachell, between Javea (20 minutes) and Moraira (15 minutes), in the north of the province of Alicante.

This luxurious district stretches over the seaside cliffs with the famous Morach beach and small coves. A paradise of pristine beaches awaits you here, each with its own unique charm. Cala del Moraig tempts with crystal clear waters and dramatic cliffs, a paradise for snorkelers and divers looking to discover underwater wonders. Playa de Los Tiestos, surrounded by rocky cliffs, provides a peaceful escape for those who long for peaceful days by the sea. Discover the hidden oasis of Cala Llebeig at the end of a scenic hike, a sanctuary of peace and untouched beauty.

Cumbre del Sol is perfect for nature lovers - it offers a network of hiking trails that invite you to immerse yourself in the rugged beauty of the region. The Ruta de los Acantilados' (cliff trail) reveals awe-inspiring views of the coast, leaving you in awe of nature's grandeur. Meanwhile, the Ruta del Faro' (Lighthouse Trail) leads to the iconic Cap d'Or lighthouse, a symbol of maritime heritage offering a sense of history and wonder.

Water lovers find their happiness among the calm waters of the nearby bays, indulging in kayaking, paddleboarding and sailing, adding an extra layer of charm to this seaside haven.

For an iconic perspective of the area, the Cumbre del Sol Equestrian Center offers enchanting horse riding trails, a delightful blend of nature and adventure that reveals the heart of this Mediterranean paradise.

Cumbre del Sol boasts a culinary scene as stunning as its surroundings, tempting visitors with a variety of dining options. At Mirador del Mar, Mediterranean cuisine meets modernity, and guests enjoy delicious dishes against the backdrop of mesmerizing sea views. Restaurante La Cumbre captivates with its combination of Spanish and international flavors, which can be enjoyed on the terrace overlooking the sea, which goes beyond an ordinary dinner. El Almendro delights with traditional Spanish dishes and tempting seafood, a real culinary delight for the demanding palate.
