New apartments for sale in Torrevieja, on the southern Costa Blanca, in a quiet residential area close to beaches, shops and local amenities. This boutique development comprises just 10 apartments with 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Residents will have access to a communal swimming pool, a relaxation area and parking spaces.

The apartments offer approximately 87 m² of total area, including 67 m² of living space and a share of the communal areas. Each apartment features a functional layout comprising 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a bright living room connected to the dining area and open-plan kitchen, and a private terrace. A penthouse with a spacious terrace has been designed on the top floor, providing additional outdoor space for relaxation.

The interiors will be finished using modern materials and solutions designed to ensure year-round comfort. Standard features include porcelain floor tiles, double-glazed windows, electric shutters, underfloor heating in the bathrooms and ducted air conditioning installation. Kitchens will be fitted with cabinetry, while bathrooms will include furniture, sanitary fittings and shower enclosures. Additional features include an aerothermal system, indoor and outdoor lighting, and photovoltaic panels supporting the electricity supply for communal areas. According to the developer, the building is expected to achieve an A energy efficiency rating.

The development will feature a communal swimming pool measuring approximately 45 m², surrounded by sunbathing areas, greenery and spaces for relaxation. The small number of apartments allows residents to enjoy the communal facilities without large numbers of neighbours. Each apartment includes a parking space.

The estimated construction completion period is approximately 18 months from signing the contract, according to the developer.

Location

The development is located on Calle Marcelina in Torrevieja, in a well-established part of the city with access to shops, supermarkets, restaurants, schools and everyday services. The popular Playa de los Locos and Playa del Cura beaches are nearby, along with the seafront promenade and Torrevieja town centre. The Habaneras shopping centre and Aquopolis water park are also within easy reach. This location offers convenient access to both city amenities and the attractions of a Mediterranean coastal resort throughout the year.
