Discover a collection of new apartments for sale in Mijas Costa, ideally located between La Cala de Mijas and Fuengirola and just a short walk from the Mediterranean Sea. Combining contemporary architecture, spacious outdoor areas and excellent communal facilities, the development offers an attractive setting for enjoying the Costa del Sol lifestyle close to beaches, golf courses, restaurants, shops and everyday services.

The private residential complex consists of 72 apartments with 2, 3 or 4 bedrooms, including ground-floor properties, apartments and penthouses. All homes have a south or southwest orientation, designed to maximise natural light and make the most of the Mediterranean climate, while many properties enjoy beautiful sea views.

Different property types provide options for a variety of lifestyles. Ground-floor apartments feature private gardens, while penthouses offer spacious terraces ideal for relaxing, dining outdoors and enjoying the Costa del Sol sunshine. Every property also includes at least one private parking space and a storage room.

The interiors combine contemporary design with practical layouts and quality finishes. Open living areas create a natural connection between the kitchen, dining and lounge spaces, while large terraces extend the living area outdoors and provide additional space for enjoying the Mediterranean climate.

The homes have also been designed with energy efficiency in mind. The building has an energy efficiency rating B and incorporates aerothermal technology for domestic hot water, enhanced thermal insulation, low-emissivity glazing and controlled mechanical ventilation.

The communal areas of these new apartments in Mijas Costa have been designed for relaxation, exercise and socialising. Landscaped gardens surround a communal swimming pool, creating a pleasant environment for enjoying sunny days without leaving the residential complex.

Residents also have access to a private gym and social-gourmet room, providing additional spaces for exercise, leisure and gatherings with family and friends.

Excellent location near the beach in Mijas

One of the main advantages of the development is its location between La Cala de Mijas and Fuengirola. According to the developer, the beach is only around a two-minute walk away, making it easy to enjoy the Mediterranean coast throughout the year.

The surrounding area offers restaurants, supermarkets, shopping centres, pharmacies and healthcare facilities. Golf enthusiasts will also find several courses nearby, including El Chaparral Golf Club, located less than two kilometres from the development.

Excellent connections via the A-7 and nearby AP-7 provide convenient access along the Costa del Sol. Málaga International Airport is less than 25 minutes away, while Marbella, Puerto Banús and Fuengirola are also easily accessible. Málaga María Zambrano railway station is approximately 40 minutes away by car.

Mijas Costa combines a relaxed coastal atmosphere with convenient access to some of the most popular destinations in southern Spain. Nearby La Cala de Mijas offers beaches, restaurants and local services, while the wider area is well known for its golf courses, Mediterranean coastline and excellent year-round climate.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa del Charcón - Playa Marina, about 7 km by road, around 9 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEP El Chaparral (about 4 km) and IES Torre Almenara (about 4 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Cala de Mijas, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Vithas Xanit International, about 24 km by road, around 25 minutes by car.

The nearest supermarket is Bazar - Supermarket Marina, about 900 m away.

How far is the airport?
