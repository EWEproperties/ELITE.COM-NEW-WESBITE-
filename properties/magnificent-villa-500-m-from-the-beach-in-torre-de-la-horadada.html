With five bedrooms, two of which have en-suite bathrooms and dressing rooms, a spacious living room with dining area and large terraces, the Gladiolo villa is the perfect place to live on the Spanish coast all year round. It offers maximum comfort, uses the highest quality materials and pays attention to the smallest details of the finish, offering an individual and luxurious design.

Just 8 km away is the famous Lo Romero golf course, and a short drive away is the largest shopping centre in the area, La Zenia Boulevard.

Location

Torre de la Horadada is a coastal district located in the municipality of Pilar de la Horadada, in the south of the province of Alicante. The resort is located in the south-east of Spain and borders Murcia (about 15 minutes away). Alicante is about a 40-minute drive away.

Pilar de la Horadada has everything you need to enjoy the sea, pine forests and Spanish sun.

The coastline of Pilar de la Hordada is over 4 kilometres long and offers a variety of scenery. There are wide beaches with fine white sand, small stone ledges hanging over the water and small, intimate, hidden coves with golden sand.

The sea is usually calm here, the beaches are safe, and there are no steep slopes, allowing even the smallest children to swim safely. In addition, the clear water and excellent visibility invite divers to observe the beauty of aquatic life.

The cleanliness of the beaches in Pilar de la Hordada has been recognised with the European Union's Blue Flag, which guarantees a healthy environment.

Inland, you can go on various interesting trips near the towns of Sierra and Escalona. There are two marked tourist routes along the Rio Seco riverbed, 19 kilometres long, which are definitely worth visiting. One route is 4 kilometres long, while the other is 10 kilometres long, stretching from the natural area of Pilar de Campoverde to the mouth of the Mil Palmeras beach.

Not far from the town of Pilar de la Horadada is also an 18-hole golf course in Lo Romero. Both professionals and beginners will find it suitable.

Gastronomy has a privileged place in the culture of this beautiful town. Typical dishes of Pilar de la Horadada include potatoes with meat stew, meatballs, rabbit in garlic and Gachas Migas (a flour and garlic pancake), as well as excellent rice dishes and soups. Sweets and cakes are also worth mentioning here, with confectionery such as meringue cake called milhojas and almond sweets called cordiales being the most popular.
