Location

Javea, also known as Xàbia, is a picturesque town located on the northern part of the Costa Blanca in the province of Alicante, Spain. With its idyllic location overlooking the Mediterranean Sea, Javea has become a popular tourist destination for visitors from all over the world.

Javea is conveniently located just an hour's drive from Alicante Airport, making it easy to access from major European cities. Additionally, Valencia Airport is just over an hour's drive away.

One of Javea's main attractions is its stunning beaches, which are among the most beautiful in the region. The Arenal beach is the most popular and well-known, with a wide stretch of golden sand, clear waters, and a variety of restaurants and bars to choose from. Other beaches worth visiting include Granadella, Cala Blanca, and Ambolo, all with crystal-clear waters and stunning natural scenery.

Aside from the beaches, Javea offers a range of activities for visitors to enjoy. The town is surrounded by natural parks and hiking trails, providing opportunities for outdoor enthusiasts to explore the area's stunning natural beauty. Visitors can also enjoy water sports such as kayaking, paddleboarding, and jet skiing, as well as scuba diving to discover the underwater world of Javea.

In terms of dining, Javea offers a wide range of culinary options, with restaurants ranging from traditional Spanish tapas bars to international cuisine. For seafood lovers, the town's fishing port offers some of the freshest seafood in the area, including shrimp, sardines, and squid. Some of the town's most popular restaurants include La Siesta, Masena, and La Fontana.

Javea is also home to several cultural attractions, including the Soler Blasco Archaeological and Ethnographic Museum and the Gothic-style Church of San Bartolomé, both located in the historic center of the town.
