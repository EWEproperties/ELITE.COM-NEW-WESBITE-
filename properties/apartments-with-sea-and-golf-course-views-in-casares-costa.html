Elite World Estates presents an exceptional residential development located in the prestigious area of Casares Costa on the Costa del Sol. This is an excellent opportunity for those looking for a modern apartment in Spain that combines high-quality finishes, a peaceful setting, proximity to the Mediterranean Sea, and outstanding lifestyle amenities.

This exclusive boutique development consists of 99 elegant apartments and penthouses with 2 or 3 bedrooms. Buyers can choose from spacious ground-floor apartments with large private gardens, stylish apartments with generous terraces, and luxurious penthouses featuring private rooftop solariums with panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea and the Sierra Bermeja mountains.

The interiors have been designed with a contemporary open-plan layout, seamlessly combining a spacious living room with an elegant Italian-style kitchen. Large floor-to-ceiling windows flood the interiors with natural light while creating a seamless connection between the living area and the outdoor terrace. Through the Exxacon Bespoke programme, homeowners also have the opportunity to personalise the finishes and adapt the property to their own preferences and lifestyle.

The development has been designed to provide maximum comfort and a modern way of living. Residents can enjoy a swimming pool for adults, a separate children's pool with spacious sunbathing areas, a sauna, a traditional hammam, a fully equipped gym, an outdoor fitness area, a professional golf simulator, an elegant residents' club with a gourmet kitchen, a coworking space, and a chill-out area with barbecue facilities.

One of the project's greatest advantages is its exceptional location. The apartments are situated in the peaceful area of Casares Costa, next to the prestigious Doña Julia Golf Course. The beautiful sandy beaches of the Mediterranean can be reached within just a few minutes, while excellent transport connections provide quick access to Estepona, Sotogrande, and Marbella. Restaurants, supermarkets, marinas, international schools, and a wide range of sports and leisure facilities are all located nearby.

Each apartment includes a private parking space and a storage room. Thanks to its high construction standards, contemporary architecture, extensive communal facilities, and prime location, this development is an excellent choice both as a holiday home in Spain and as an investment property with strong long-term appreciation potential.

Location – Apartments with Sea and Golf Course Views in Casares Costa

The development is located in the prestigious area of Casares Costa on the western Costa del Sol, one of the most desirable locations in Andalusia. This peaceful setting combines the beauty of the Mediterranean coastline with picturesque golf courses and mountain scenery, creating an ideal environment for both everyday living and relaxing holidays.

The development is just a few minutes from the wide sandy beaches of Casares Costa and its charming seaside promenade lined with restaurants, cafés, and beach clubs. The surrounding area is home to several renowned golf courses, including Doña Julia Golf, Finca Cortesín Golf, and Estepona Golf, making it a perfect destination for golf enthusiasts.

Residents also benefit from excellent local infrastructure, including supermarkets, restaurants, shops, international schools, medical centres, marinas, and numerous sports and leisure facilities.

With direct access to the A-7 motorway, the development offers excellent connections to the main destinations along the Costa del Sol:

  • Estepona – approximately 10 minutes
  • Sotogrande – approximately 15 minutes
  • Marbella – approximately 30 minutes
  • Gibraltar – approximately 35 minutes
  • Málaga International Airport – approximately 60 minutes
  • Gibraltar Airport – approximately 35 minutes

Casares Costa is highly regarded for its peaceful atmosphere, beautiful natural surroundings, and excellent investment potential. Its combination of beachside living, prestigious golf courses, and excellent transport links makes it one of the most promising locations on the western Costa del Sol.
