New apartments for sale in an attractive area of Estepona on the Costa del Sol, enjoying an elevated position with beautiful panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea, Gibraltar and, on clear days, the African coastline. The location combines peaceful, green surroundings with convenient access to the beaches, marina, Estepona town centre and all essential services.

The complex offers 2- and 3-bedroom apartments as well as spacious penthouses. The interiors have been designed with an emphasis on natural light, open-plan living and a seamless connection between the indoor areas and large terraces. Floor-to-ceiling windows open the homes to the surrounding landscape and allow residents to make the most of the Mediterranean climate. The penthouses also feature spacious private rooftop solariums.

The contemporary kitchens are fully equipped with high-quality cabinetry, quartz worktops and appliances. Large-format porcelain tiles are used throughout the apartments and continue onto the terraces, creating visual continuity between the indoor and outdoor spaces. Ducted air conditioning, an aerothermal system, double glazing and electric shutters provide additional comfort.

One of the highlights of the development is its extensive resort-style communal area. Residents will have access to an outdoor swimming pool surrounded by landscaped greenery, a wellness centre, gym, sauna, Turkish bath and beautifully designed gardens. The complex also includes a bar area and spaces dedicated to relaxation and socialising.

The development incorporates solutions designed to improve the energy efficiency of the buildings. An aerothermal system provides domestic hot water, photovoltaic panels contribute to the energy supply of the communal areas, and the underground garage is prepared for electric vehicle charging installations. Each property includes a parking space in the underground garage and a storage room.

Estimated completion: Q4 2027.

EXCELLENT LOCATION IN ESTEPONA

The development is situated in an elevated area of Estepona, offering open views of the sea and mountains while remaining close to the town centre. Beaches, the marina, restaurants, shops and everyday services are all within easy reach. The location also provides convenient access to Marbella, Puerto Banús and other popular destinations along the Costa del Sol.

Estepona is one of the most attractive coastal towns on the western Costa del Sol, renowned for its wide beaches, seafront promenade, picturesque old town, marina and excellent selection of restaurants. The combination of a relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle and modern infrastructure makes these properties an attractive option both for year-round living and as a second home in Spain.
