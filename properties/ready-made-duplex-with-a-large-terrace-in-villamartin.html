Location

Orihuela Costa is a coastal region located on the Costa Blanca in the province of Alicante. It lies close to the Murcia border, near La Manga and the beautiful Mar Menor.

It is the perfect destination for those looking for family beach holidays, with 16 kilometers of diverse coastline. It is also an excellent choice for golf lovers; in fact, Orihuela Costa is very well equipped for every need, offering numerous amenities, facilities, and leisure attractions.

The region includes several seaside resorts such as Punta Prima, Playa Flamenca, La Zenia, Cabo Roig, Villamartín, Campoamor, and Mil Palmeras.

The prestigious Villamartín golf development is located south of Alicante in Orihuela Costa. The complex resembles a charming Mediterranean village with apartments, townhouses, and villas, together with all the amenities you could wish for, including shops, cafés, bars, restaurants, and of course two outstanding 18-hole golf courses.

The seaside resort of Cabo Roig is located at the southern end of Costa Blanca. This charming town features several Blue Flag beaches and spectacular landscapes. The town itself, situated just 10 kilometers from Torrevieja, offers wide palm-lined avenues and stunning lush gardens overlooking the Mediterranean Sea, the La Manga peninsula, and Mar Menor.

The peaceful urbanization of Playa Flamenca is located near the lively city of Torrevieja on the Costa Blanca coastline. The area offers beautiful beaches, a golf course, and many leisure facilities such as water parks, tennis courts, and go-kart tracks.

Not far from Torrevieja lies the popular resort of La Zenia. The town has everything needed to enjoy the perfect holiday: Blue Flag beaches, golf courses, excellent amenities, many Spanish and international restaurants, and beautiful surroundings to explore. La Zenia is one of the most famous resorts on the Costa Blanca.

The local beaches around Punta Prima have been awarded numerous European Blue Flags and stretch from natural dunes backed by pine forests to long expanses of golden sandy beaches. Punta Prima is actually an urbanization rather than a town, consisting of apartments, townhouses, and villas located around 5 kilometers southwest of Torrevieja.
