Your new Spanish apartment is located in the picturesque town of Rojales, in a quiet residential area popular with tourists. This luxurious project is surrounded by extensive natural parks, prestigious golf courses and the best beaches, allowing you to enjoy the Mediterranean climate and life in close proximity to nature.

It is a private residential complex consisting of 135 houses with 2 or 3 bedrooms, designed in a minimalist way and maintaining high quality standards. Its modern architecture harmonises with large open spaces, nature and a swimming pool, offering a unique and peaceful atmosphere.

The apartments on the ground floor have private gardens, while the top floor apartments offer spacious roof terraces, perfect for sunbathing and enjoying the Spanish sun all day long.

The project is being built on a unique and exceptional plot of land, bordering the natural landscape of a salt lagoon, enjoying its biodiversity and exceptional environment, close to the best beaches of the Mediterranean, with all kinds of services and amenities. At the same time, thanks to the proximity of El Moncayo beach, about 15 minutes away by car, you will be able to go for a walk or ride a bike and enjoy the best climate on the Costa Blanca.

Location

Rojales is a picturesque town in the province of Valencia, in south-eastern Spain. Lemon and orange groves surround the town, and the river Segura flows through Rojales. The beaches of Guardamar del Segura are only 7 km from Rojales. With its rich cultural heritage, picturesque landscapes and many attractions, Rojales is a place you must visit.

Located just a 45-minute drive from both Alicante and Murcia airports, Rojales offers convenient access for travellers looking for a great holiday. Upon arrival, immerse yourself in the town's history by exploring the charming historic centre, where traditional architecture and vibrant flowers create a captivating atmosphere.

Don't miss the Cuevas del Rodeo, an extraordinary cave complex transformed into an art gallery. Admire the intricate wall paintings and sculptures, and appreciate the city's thriving artistic community. For nature lovers, the nearby Parque Natural de la Mata invites you to discover stunning landscapes, ideal for walks and bird watching.

When it's time to relax, head to the beautiful Blue Flag beaches of Guardamar del Segura, just a 10-minute drive from the property. Dip your feet in the golden sand, soak up the Mediterranean sun and cool off in the crystal-clear water.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the presence of La Marquesa Golf and other prestigious courses nearby, offering a fantastic golfing experience in breathtaking surroundings.

After a day of sightseeing, treat yourself to the culinary delights of Rojales. In the many charming restaurants and cafés, you can savour traditional Spanish tapas, fresh seafood and local specialities, complemented by regional wines.

All in all, Rojales offers a captivating combination of history, natural beauty, great beaches, golfing opportunities and delicious cuisine. Whether you are looking for a short getaway or considering making Rojales your permanent home, this charming town offers a truly unforgettable experience.
