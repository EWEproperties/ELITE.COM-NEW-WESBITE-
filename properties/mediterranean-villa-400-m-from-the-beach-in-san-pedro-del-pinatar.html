Location

San Pedro del Pinatar is a charming town located in the Murcia region of Spain, set along the shores of the saltwater lagoon of Mar Menor. The town boasts a rich history dating back to the Roman Empire, with remnants of ancient salt production still visible today.

The area offers several beautiful beaches, including Playa de la Llana, Playa de Villananitos, and Playa de la Torre Derribadaperfect for water sports, sunbathing, and relaxation. Playa de la Mota is particularly popular among locals, offering calm waters and excellent facilities.

Nature lovers will appreciate the nearby Regional Park of Salinas y Arenales de San Pedro del Pinatar, a unique environment where the saltwater lagoon meets the Mediterranean Sea. This protected area is home to a diverse ecosystem, ideal for birdwatching, nature walks, and guided tours.

The town also features several attractions worth visiting, including the Fish Market, offering fresh seafood caught daily, and the Sea and Salt Museum, which showcases the region's salt production heritage. Molino de Quintín, a restored windmill, provides insight into the area's agricultural past.

San Pedro del Pinatar offers a wide selection of restaurants, cafés, and bars where you can enjoy local cuisine in a relaxed atmosphere. The town also hosts a vibrant cultural calendar, including events such as the San Pedro Carnival and the International Festival of Habaneras and Polyphony.
