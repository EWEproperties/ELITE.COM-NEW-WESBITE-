Location and Surroundings

Benidorm is a city located on the east coast of Spain and is one of the most popular holiday destinations in the country. Known for its stunning beaches, clear waters, and vibrant nightlife, Benidorm attracts millions of tourists every year. Whether you're looking for a relaxing holiday, an action-packed adventure, or a combination of both, Benidorm has something to offer everyone.

Playa de Levante is one of Benidorm's two main beaches and is considered one of the best in the country. This vast, golden-sand beach is perfect for sunbathing, swimming, and soaking up the Mediterranean sun. You'll also find plenty of restaurants, bars, and shops along the promenade, making it the perfect place to spend a day or a night out.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala de Finestrat, about 1.9 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP el Murtal (about 1.4 km) and CEIP la Cala (about 1.5 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Cala de Benidorm, about 1.4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Clínica Benidorm, about 4 km by road, around 9 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, about 1.3 km away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 61 km by road, around 45 minutes by car.
