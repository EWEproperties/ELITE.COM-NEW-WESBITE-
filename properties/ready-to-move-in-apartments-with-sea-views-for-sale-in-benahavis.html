A new residential development located in a prestigious area of Benahavís, close to Marbella, surrounded by golf courses and Mediterranean nature. The elevated position of the complex offers beautiful panoramic views towards the coastline and the Mediterranean Sea, while providing a peaceful setting just a short drive from the beaches, Puerto Banús and Marbella.

The development offers modern 2 and 3-bedroom apartments, designed with an emphasis on space, natural light and comfort. Large windows open the interiors onto spacious terraces, creating a seamless connection between the living areas and the surroundings. Selected properties feature private gardens, while apartments on the upper floors make the most of the attractive sea views and surrounding landscape.

The interiors feature a bright, contemporary design. The open-plan living area combines the lounge, dining area and modern kitchen, while neutral colours and natural materials give the spaces an elegant Mediterranean character. The kitchens come with fitted cabinetry and appliances, while the bedrooms and bathrooms have been carefully designed to provide functionality and everyday comfort.

One of the main advantages of the development is its spacious terraces and private outdoor areas, allowing residents to enjoy the Costa del Sol climate for most of the year. Residents also have access to beautifully maintained communal areas with landscaped gardens and a swimming pool.

EXCELLENT LOCATION CLOSE TO MARBELLA AND GOLF COURSES

Benahavís is one of the most highly regarded locations on the Costa del Sol, known for its beautiful landscapes, high-quality properties and excellent gastronomic and golfing scene. Several renowned golf courses are located nearby, making this location particularly attractive for golf enthusiasts.

Puerto Banús, Marbella and the sandy beaches of the Costa del Sol can all be reached within a short drive. Its location between the mountains and the Mediterranean Sea combines a peaceful lifestyle with easy access to restaurants, shops, marinas, golf clubs and the full range of amenities available along the coast.

This is an attractive option both for those looking for an apartment for sale in Benahavís as a second home in Spain and for those seeking a property for year-round living in one of the most desirable areas of the Costa del Sol.
