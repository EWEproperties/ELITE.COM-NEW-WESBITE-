Location

El Campello is located in the heart of the Costa Blanca. It is only 10 km from the center of the picturesque city of Alicante and 30 km from modern Benidorm. Getting here is very easy as it is located just 15 km from L'Altet International Airport in Alicante.

The city is famous for its wonderful beaches, which stretch for over 23 kilometers along the Mediterranean coast. The main beach, known as Muchavista, is a wide and sandy beach with crystal clear waters, making it a popular destination for tourists and locals alike. Other popular beaches in the area include the secluded L'Amerador Beach and Cala del Morro Blanc.

In addition to its beaches, El Campello is also known for its rich history and cultural heritage. The city has been inhabited since ancient times and was once an important Roman port. Visitors can visit the remains of the Roman villa L'Almadrava, which was once used for salting fish and preserving agricultural products.

Alicante Golf

Alicante is famous for its numerous golf courses that attract golf enthusiasts from all over the world. Located just a few kilometers from the city center, Alicante Golf is one of the most popular golf courses in the region. Designed by Seve Ballesteros, this 18-hole course offers breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea and is known for its challenging layout.

Saint Barbara Castle

The city's rich history is evident in its many magnificent monuments, including St. Peter's Castle. Barbara. This historic fortress sits atop Mount Benacantil and offers breathtaking views of the city and the Mediterranean Sea. The castle is a testament to the many civilizations that have settled in Alicante over the centuries, including Romans, Moors and Christians.

Recreation

When it comes to recreation, Alicante is undoubtedly a favorite among tourists and locals. The Explanada de España promenade, located opposite the port, is a lively boulevard with terraces and sidewalk cafes. Here you can enjoy a cup of coffee or a delicious meal while admiring the wonderful views of the sea and the city.
