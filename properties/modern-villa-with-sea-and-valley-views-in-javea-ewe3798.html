Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional contemporary villa located in the prestigious Monte Olimpo residential area of Jávea, one of the most exclusive locations on the northern Costa Blanca. Designed for the most discerning buyers, this spectacular residence combines contemporary architecture, premium-quality finishes and breathtaking panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding valley. Nestled in a peaceful and private setting, the villa is just a few minutes from beautiful beaches, renowned restaurants, golf courses and all the amenities required for comfortable year-round living.

The property offers approximately 485 m² of built area and impresses with its elegant minimalist architecture featuring expansive floor-to-ceiling glazing that floods the interiors with natural light while framing spectacular views from almost every room. The harmonious combination of contemporary design and natural materials creates a timeless residence that blends perfectly with the Mediterranean landscape.

The upper floor comprises four spacious bedrooms, each with its own stylish en-suite bathroom, providing maximum privacy and comfort for both residents and guests. The main level has been designed as an open-plan living area featuring a sophisticated lounge, spacious dining area and a modern fully equipped designer kitchen. Large sliding glass doors seamlessly connect the interior with the terraces and garden, creating the perfect indoor-outdoor lifestyle typical of the Mediterranean.

The outdoor spaces have been designed for year-round enjoyment. Residents can relax by the impressive private swimming pool, unwind on the spacious sun terraces or enjoy the beautifully landscaped Mediterranean garden while taking in the panoramic views of the sea and valley. It is an ideal setting for both peaceful relaxation and entertaining family and friends.

Everyday comfort is further enhanced by a spacious double garage and the villa's prestigious location, providing quick access to beaches, marinas, restaurants, shops and renowned golf courses. Villa Atlantis is an outstanding opportunity for buyers seeking a modern luxury property in one of the most desirable areas of the Costa Blanca, whether as a permanent residence or an exclusive holiday home.

Location

The property is situated in one of the most prestigious residential areas of Jávea on the northern Costa Blanca. This peaceful hillside setting offers spectacular panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea, the lush valley and the impressive Montgó mountain. The exclusive residential environment provides privacy, tranquillity and security while remaining within easy reach of everything that makes Jávea one of Spain's most sought-after destinations.

Within just a few minutes' drive, residents can reach the sandy Arenal Beach, picturesque coves with crystal-clear waters and the lively seafront promenade lined with restaurants, cafés, boutiques and beach clubs. The nearby marina, prestigious golf courses, tennis clubs, international schools and numerous hiking and cycling routes through the Montgó Natural Park further enhance the exceptional lifestyle offered by this location.

Excellent local infrastructure makes this an ideal place for both permanent living and holiday escapes. Supermarkets, shops, medical centres and all essential services are available nearby, while Alicante and Valencia International Airports can both be reached in approximately 75 minutes by car, providing convenient connections to major European cities.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala dels Testos, about 8 km by road, around 16 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP Santa María Magdalena (about 4 km) and LAUDE The Lady Elizabeth School (about 5 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centre de Salut de Teulada, about 8 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital la Pedrera, about 20 km by road, around 30 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is masymas, about 2.6 km away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 101 km by road, around 75 minutes by car.
