Elite World Estates presents an exceptional development of modern detached villas located in the prestigious Bonalba Golf Resort in Mutxamel, on the northern Costa Blanca. This is an ideal opportunity for those seeking a comfortable home in a peaceful setting, close to the golf course, beaches, and the city of Alicante.

This exclusive project consists of 34 elegant single-level villas designed in a contemporary Mediterranean style. The layout has been carefully planned to provide maximum comfort and functionality.

Each villa offers approximately 144 m² of living space, featuring 3 spacious bedrooms and 3 modern bathrooms. The bright open-plan living area combines the lounge, dining room, and kitchen, creating a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living. Large windows flood the interiors with natural light and provide direct access to the terrace, garden, and private swimming pool.

The properties also feature a private rooftop solarium with beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea, the golf course, and the surrounding hills. Each plot includes a landscaped Mediterranean garden and private parking space.

For enhanced energy efficiency, all villas are equipped with solar panels, helping to reduce energy consumption and running costs.

The development is located next to the prestigious Bonalba Golf Course. The sandy beaches of El Campello are approximately 4 km away, while Alicante International Airport can be reached in around 25 minutes by car.

Bonalba Golf Resort is one of the most sought-after residential areas on the Costa Blanca, offering an excellent lifestyle with golf, nature, wellness facilities, restaurants, and easy access to Alicante and the Mediterranean coast.

Location: Bonalba Golf Resort – A Prestigious Setting in Mutxamel

Bonalba Golf Resort is one of the most sought-after and peaceful residential areas on the northern Costa Blanca, located in the town of Mutxamel, just a short drive from Alicante city centre and the Mediterranean coastline. The area is renowned for its beautiful landscapes, green surroundings, and the prestigious 18-hole Bonalba Golf Course, which forms the heart of the resort.

The location offers the perfect combination of a relaxed lifestyle, proximity to nature, and excellent infrastructure. Residents can enjoy nearby restaurants, cafés, shops, a spa centre, hotel facilities, and a wide range of leisure activities. Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the golf course, walking and cycling routes, as well as the beautiful beaches of the Costa Blanca.

One of the greatest advantages of Bonalba Golf Resort is its strategic location. The sandy beaches of El Campello and Playa de San Juan are only a few minutes away by car, while Alicante city centre and Alicante-Elche International Airport are easily accessible via excellent road connections.

Bonalba Golf Resort attracts those seeking a peaceful Mediterranean lifestyle, high-quality properties, and an exceptional environment for year-round living or holidays. It is an ideal destination for a second home in Spain, permanent residence, or a profitable rental investment.
