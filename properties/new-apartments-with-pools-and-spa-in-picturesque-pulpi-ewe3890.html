Modern apartments for sale in a peaceful area of Pulpí, close to San Juan de los Terreros on the Almería coast. The development has been designed in a Mediterranean style, with bright architecture, spacious terraces and extensive communal areas, providing comfort both for holiday stays and year-round living.

The development offers apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Ground-floor properties feature spacious terraces of approximately 24–27 m², while selected upper-floor apartments also offer a private solarium of approximately 62–86 m². The built areas of the available properties range from approximately 73 to 102 m², depending on the chosen layout. Each property includes a private parking space.

The heart of each apartment is a bright, open-plan living area combining the lounge, dining area and kitchen. The kitchens come with fitted cabinetry, a quartz worktop or similar, as well as appliances including a ceramic hob, extractor hood, oven, microwave, refrigerator, dishwasher and washing machine. The bedrooms feature built-in wardrobes with LED lighting, shelves and drawers.

The apartments also include aluminium windows with thermal break and double glazing, electric shutters and a reinforced entrance door. Pre-installation for ducted air conditioning is provided in the living room and bedrooms, together with an aerothermal hot water system. The solariums are equipped with hot and cold water connections for a shower and pre-installation for a future summer kitchen.

One of the main advantages of the development is its extensive communal facilities. Residents will have access to 3 outdoor swimming pools, an indoor spa area with a heated pool, a gym, landscaped gardens, a children’s playground and a multi-purpose room. The complex will also include pre-installation for electric vehicle charging.

The development is located in the San Juan de los Terreros area, known for its natural surroundings, peaceful atmosphere and beautiful Mediterranean beaches and coves. The location is particularly attractive for those looking for a property in a quieter part of Spain’s south-eastern coastline, with easy access to both the sea and recreational areas.

Estimated completion of the first phase: December 2028.

Location

The development is located in Pulpí, close to San Juan de los Terreros in the province of Almería, in an attractive setting combining the sea, mountains and Mediterranean nature.

It is an excellent location for those who want to enjoy peace and privacy while remaining within easy reach of beautiful beaches, secluded coves and local coastal amenities.

The area is characterised by its natural landscape, mild climate and relaxed holiday atmosphere for much of the year. It is an ideal location both for a second home in Spain and for a property intended for longer stays.

Another advantage is the proximity to golf courses and recreational areas, making the location attractive not only to beach lovers but also to those who enjoy an active lifestyle.
