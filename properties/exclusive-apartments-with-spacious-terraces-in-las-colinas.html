Modern 3-bedroom apartments for sale in the exceptional green surroundings of Las Colinas on the Costa Blanca. The low-density development has been harmoniously integrated into the natural landscape, offering residents privacy, tranquillity and generous outdoor spaces with open views of the surrounding nature.

The development consists of several low-rise buildings with contemporary architecture. Light façades, large glazed areas and spacious terraces give the complex an elegant Mediterranean character. At the heart of the development there is a communal swimming pool surrounded by landscaped gardens and relaxation areas.

The apartments offer 3 spacious bedrooms and have been designed for comfortable holiday stays as well as year-round living. Depending on the selected property, there are ground-floor apartments with very large terraces and private gardens, apartments on the upper floors, and properties on the highest levels with additional private outdoor spaces.

One of the main advantages of these apartments is the exceptionally generous terrace space, which in selected properties exceeds 130 m². Ground-floor apartments may also offer private gardens of up to approximately 129 m². This provides an ideal setting for relaxing, sunbathing, outdoor dining or creating your own Mediterranean-style leisure area.

The interiors have been designed with a modern, open-plan layout. The spacious living room, dining area and kitchen form the central part of the apartment, while large glazed doors provide direct access to the terrace. This allows plenty of natural light into the living area and creates a seamless connection between the interior and exterior spaces.

The 3-bedroom apartments offer built areas of up to approximately 150 m², providing plenty of space for families or buyers looking for a larger holiday property on the Costa Blanca. Selected apartments on the upper floors also feature additional private rooftop spaces.

Residents have access to a communal swimming pool and carefully landscaped green areas. The complex is surrounded by lush vegetation, preserving an intimate atmosphere and offering a strong connection with nature. The residential area benefits from 24-hour security, and owners can also make use of additional services available within the resort.

Location

The development is located within Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, in an exceptionally green and peaceful area of the southern Costa Blanca, between San Miguel de Salinas and Campoamor. It is a gated, carefully designed resort surrounded by Mediterranean nature, with a golf course and extensive facilities for residents.

The location offers privacy and tranquillity while still providing easy access by car to the beaches of Orihuela Costa, restaurants, shops and the coastal areas of Campoamor and Cabo Roig. It is an ideal location for those who want to live close to nature without giving up convenient access to services and the Mediterranean coast.
