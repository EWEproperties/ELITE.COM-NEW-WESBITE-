We are pleased to present a new villa project with a very elegant design, located within the La Serena Golf Resort in Los Alcázares.

The villas are laid out on one level and have a private roof terrace with ample space for sun loungers, a barbecue, a jacuzzi and, of course, beautiful views of the surrounding area. There is also the possibility of having a villa arranged on two levels, if desired, as the developer is flexible and can provide different villa models.

The villa has an open-plan living room, kitchen and dining room, three spacious bedrooms with fitted wardrobes and two or three bathrooms, depending on the type of villa chosen.

The villas are equipped with white goods, external and internal LED lighting, shower cubicles in the bathroom, ducted air conditioning and a private swimming pool.

The development is located on the La Serena golf course, a few minutes from the centre of the charming Spanish town of Los Alcázares and its beaches, where you can find all the necessary amenities, such as restaurants, pharmacies, supermarkets, shops and banks. Within a few minutes' drive, you can reach the Dos Mares shopping centre or the magnificent city of Cartagena.

Location

Los Alcázares is located in the Murcia region of Spain. The town tempts visitors with its seaside charm and a host of delightful attractions. Located along the magnificent Mar Menor, a saltwater lagoon famous for its crystal clear waters and healing properties, Los Alcázares offers an idyllic setting for an unforgettable holiday or a peaceful retreat.

The area is connected by motorway to Cartagena, Murcia and Alicante, as well as the other beaches of the Costa Cálida and Costa Blanca.

One of the town's greatest assets is its collection of beautiful beaches, each promising moments of bliss and relaxation. Golden sands stretch along the tranquil shores of the Mar Menor, creating a paradise for sun seekers and water lovers. From the lively and family-friendly Playa de Los Narejos to the more secluded and peaceful Playa de Las Salinas, there is a beach for everyone. Visitors can sunbathe, swim or enjoy a variety of water sports, including sailing, kayaking and windsurfing. The shallow and calm waters of the Mar Menor make it an ideal destination for families with children.

Los Alcázares boasts a vibrant culinary scene, offering a diverse range of restaurants, bars and cafés to tempt your taste buds. From traditional Spanish tapas to international cuisine, the town's restaurants cater to every palate. Sample fresh seafood made with local ingredients, savour authentic paella or enjoy a refreshing drink at a beachside chiringuito while taking in the stunning views. The warm and welcoming atmosphere of the town's eateries enhances the overall dining experience, making it a real treat for food lovers.

In addition to beaches and eateries, Los Alcázares offers many attractions to keep visitors entertained. Explore the charming streets of the town centre, dotted with shops, boutiques and craft stores. Discover local handicrafts, souvenirs and unique treasures to commemorate your visit. For the adventurous, a variety of water sports centres offer opportunities for water skiing, paddleboarding and diving, allowing you to immerse yourself in the underwater wonders of the Mar Menor.

Golf enthusiasts will find themselves in paradise, as Los Alcázares is surrounded by a selection of world-class golf courses. Within a short distance, you will discover famous golf courses such as La Serena Golf, Roda Golf and Mar Menor Golf Resort. Immerse yourself in lush green fairways, perfect your swing and enjoy the Mediterranean climate that allows you to play golf all year round.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Punta Brava, about 7 km by road, around 9 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are I.E.S. ANTONIO MENÁRGUEZ COSTA (about 1.8 km) and Grupo Escolar Los Alcázares (about 2.4 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Los Alcázares, about 3 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 13 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Dialprix, about 2.1 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 34 km by road, around 25 minutes by car.
