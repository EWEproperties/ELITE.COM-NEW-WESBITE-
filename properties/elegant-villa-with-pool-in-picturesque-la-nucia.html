Location

Strategically located, La Nucía allows residents to enjoy the peaceful Mediterranean lifestyle while offering quick access to the main attractions of northern Costa Blanca. The town is situated just around 10 kilometers from the vibrant city of Benidorm, between the sea and the majestic Sierra de Aitana mountain range. Surrounded by green valleys, citrus groves, and pine forests, the area creates a unique atmosphere ideal for relaxation, outdoor activities, and living close to nature.

The historic center of La Nucía charms visitors with traditional Spanish architecture. Narrow streets, whitewashed houses decorated with flowers, and picturesque squares give the town an authentic Mediterranean character. The parish church dominates the old town, while several viewpoints offer spectacular panoramas of the mountains, coastline, and nearby Costa Blanca towns. Despite preserving its traditional charm, La Nucía continues to grow dynamically and features modern residential areas with elegant villas, apartments, and new developments.

The town is highly popular among people looking for a quieter alternative to busy coastal resorts without giving up easy access to beaches, restaurants, golf courses, and shopping centers. Within just a short drive, you can reach the beaches of Benidorm, Altea, and Alfaz del Pi.

La Nucía is also famous for its outstanding sports and recreational infrastructure. The city has repeatedly been recognized as one of the best sports destinations in Spain thanks to the modern Ciudad Deportiva Camilo Cano complex, which hosts international sporting events, training camps, and tournaments.

Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the many walking and cycling routes through picturesque areas such as Fuente de la Favara and the impressive Puig Campana mountain. The region offers excellent opportunities for hiking, cycling, climbing, and enjoying nature throughout the entire year.

The town is also known for its local traditions and welcoming atmosphere. Every Sunday, a popular market takes place along Avenida Porvilla, where visitors can find local products, antiques, and second-hand treasures. Throughout the year, numerous fiestas and cultural events are organized, including the August celebrations in honor of the Immaculate Conception and the November festival of San Rafael, attracting both residents and visitors from across the region.
