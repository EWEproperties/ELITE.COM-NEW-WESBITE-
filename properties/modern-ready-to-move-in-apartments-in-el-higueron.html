Elite World Estates presents an exceptional residential development located in the prestigious area of El Higuerón in Fuengirola – one of the most sought-after destinations on the Costa del Sol. This exclusive development is an ideal choice for those looking for a modern apartment in Spain that combines premium finishes, panoramic Mediterranean Sea views, and an exceptional lifestyle in an exclusive setting.

This boutique development consists of just 18 ready-to-move-in apartments, designed with contemporary architecture that blends seamlessly into the surrounding natural landscape. Every residence has been carefully designed to maximize natural light and living space. Large floor-to-ceiling windows, spacious terraces, and high-quality finishes create bright, elegant interiors while providing a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living.

Residents can enjoy beautifully designed communal areas created with relaxation and well-being in mind, including landscaped gardens and peaceful outdoor spaces perfect for enjoying the Mediterranean climate throughout the year.

The development enjoys a prime location with easy access to a wide range of premium amenities. Renowned restaurants, sports facilities, fitness clubs, spa centres, golf courses, and the beautiful beaches of the Costa del Sol are all just a short drive away. Málaga International Airport can be reached in approximately 15 minutes, while the centres of Fuengirola, Benalmádena, and Málaga are easily accessible thanks to excellent transport connections.

This is the perfect opportunity for those seeking a permanent residence, a luxury holiday home, or a secure investment in one of Southern Spain's most prestigious locations.

Location

The development is located in the prestigious residential area of El Higuerón, between Fuengirola and Benalmádena, one of the Costa del Sol's most exclusive addresses. This sought-after location is renowned for its peaceful surroundings, panoramic Mediterranean Sea views, and excellent connectivity to the region's main attractions.

Within just a few minutes, residents can reach the sandy Carvajal Beach, a wide selection of restaurants, shops, prestigious golf courses, and the exclusive sports and wellness facilities of Higuerón Resort. The nearby Carvajal railway station provides direct connections to Málaga Airport and Málaga city centre, while quick access to the AP-7 motorway allows convenient travel along the entire Costa del Sol coastline.
