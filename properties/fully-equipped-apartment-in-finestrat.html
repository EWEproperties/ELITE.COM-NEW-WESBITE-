Location

Finestrat is a charming and picturesque town located in the province of Alicante, in the Valencian Community of Spain. This idyllic location is known for its stunning mountain views, pristine beaches and rich cultural heritage, making it the perfect place for a relaxing and memorable holiday.

Set on the slopes of Puig Campana, Hotel Finestrat offers breathtaking views of the mountains, the coast and the Mediterranean Sea. The city is located just 8 km from the bustling city of Benidorm and 40 km from Alicante and its international airport, making it easily accessible to tourists from all over the world.

Finestrat is a town with a typical Moorish character, with colorfully painted houses and narrow cobbled streets that are perfect for exploring. The city is also home to Cala Finestrat, a seaside resort just three kilometers from Benidorm. Here, visitors can enjoy all the necessary amenities, including a wide selection of restaurants, bars and shops. La Cala Beach is a clean strip of golden sand, perfect for children and safe for swimming. There are several lovely restaurants along the promenade and there is an arts and crafts market in the afternoon during the summer months.

For those seeking adventure and fun, the Finestrat area offers plenty of activities for families and couples alike. Nearby, guests can enjoy amusement parks such as Terre Mitica and Terra Natura, the Aqualandia water park and the Mundomar marine and exotic animal park, all of which provide entertainment for the whole family. If you play golf, the nearby Real Faula Golf Club offers two excellent golf courses.
