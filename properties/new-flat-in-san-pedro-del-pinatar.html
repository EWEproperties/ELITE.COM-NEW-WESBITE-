Location

San Pedro del Pinatar is a small town located in the province of Murcia, Spain, situated on the shores of the saltwater lagoon of the Mar Menor. The town has a rich history that dates back to the Roman Empire, and traces of Roman salt factories can still be seen today.

There are several beautiful beaches in the town, including Playa de la Llana, Playa de Villananitos and Playa de la Torre Derribada. These beaches are ideal for water sports, sunbathing and relaxation. Playa de la Mota is a beach popular with locals, offering calm waters and excellent facilities.

San Pedro del Pinatar is also home to a fascinating natural park, the Salinas y Arenales de San Pedro del Pinatar Regional Park. This natural park offers a unique environment where a salt water lagoon meets the Mediterranean Sea, creating a diverse ecosystem that supports a wide variety of flora and fauna. Visitors can go on guided tours, bird and wildlife watching and walk along the park trails.

The town has several attractions worth visiting, including the Fish Market, which offers a wide selection of fresh seafood caught the same day, and the Sea and Salt Museum, which showcases the town's rich history of salt production. Molino de Quintin is a historic windmill that has been restored and gives a glimpse into the town's agricultural past.

San Pedro del Pinatar is also home to a number of restaurants, cafés and bars where visitors can sample local cuisine and enjoy a relaxed atmosphere. The town has a vibrant cultural scene, with several festivals throughout the year, including the Carnival of San Pedro del Pinatar and the International Festival of Habaneras and Polyphony.
