Location

Benidorm is a city located on the east coast of Spain and is one of the most popular holiday destinations in the entire country. Known for its magnificent beaches, clear waters and lively nightlife, Benidorm attracts millions of tourists every year. Benidorm has the highest number of skyscrapers in Spain and is often referred to as the second New York. Whether you are looking for a place to relax or have fun, Benidorm has something to offer everyone.

El Balcón del Mediterráneo

Benidorm also has a particularly romantic side. We are talking about El Balcón del Mediterráneo, a viewpoint that captivates not only with its appearance but also with its location. Built on rocks, the castle separates two beaches, and its terrace offers an amazing view of the entire bay. In the past, this location was of strategic importance, allowing for the observation of potential threats on the horizon and the rapid organisation of defensive units. Today, El Balcón del Mediterráneo is one of the most popular tourist attractions in Benidorm and probably the most photographed place in this part of the Iberian Peninsula.

Beaches

Playa de Levante is one of Benidorm's two main beaches and is considered one of the best in the country. This vast beach with golden sand is ideal for sunbathing, swimming and basking in the Mediterranean sun. Along the promenade, you will also find many restaurants, bars and shops.

Playa de Poniente is Benidorm's largest beach: 3,000 metres of white, sandy shore, which is over 100 metres wide in some sections! Located south of the centre and awarded the Blue Flag, it is less crowded than Playa de Levante (the resort's other large beach) and is a favourite spot for locals. Playa de Poniente offers all amenities: parasols and sun loungers (available 11 months a year), free showers and toilets, children's playgrounds, lifeguard service, easy access for disabled people, and even a public library located directly on the beach.

Isla de Benidorm

As soon as you arrive in Benidorm and step onto the sandy beach, you will see an island rising out of the sea a few kilometres from the beach (exactly two nautical miles from the port of Benidorm). This is an island called Isla de Benidorm. The Spanish use another name for this island, Isla de los Periodistas. No one lives on the island, it has no permanent residents, but you can buy a trip to the island from Benidorm, where you can enjoy a seafood meal in a restaurant.
