Location

San Pedro del Pinatar is a small town located in the province of Murcia, Spain, on the shores of the Mar Menor saltwater lagoon. The town has a rich history dating back to the Roman Empire, and traces of Roman salt production facilities are still visible today.

The town has several beautiful beaches, including Playa de la Llana, Playa de Villananitos and Playa de la Torre Derribada. These beaches are ideal for water sports, sunbathing and relaxation. Playa de la Mota is a beach popular with locals, offering calm waters and excellent facilities.

San Pedro del Pinatar is also home to a fascinating nature park, the Salinas y Arenales de San Pedro del Pinatar Regional Park. This natural park offers a unique environment where a saltwater lagoon meets the Mediterranean Sea, creating a diverse ecosystem that supports a wide range of flora and fauna. Visitors can take a guided tour, observe birds and wildlife, and stroll along the park's trails.

The town has several attractions worth visiting, including the Fish Market, which offers a wide selection of fresh seafood caught that same day, and the Sea and Salt Museum, which showcases the town's rich history of salt production. Molino de Quintin is a historic windmill that has been restored and provides insight into the town's agricultural past.

San Pedro del Pinatar also has a number of restaurants, cafés and bars where visitors can sample the local cuisine and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere. The town has a vibrant cultural scene, with several festivals throughout the year, including the San Pedro del Pinatar Carnival and the International Habaneras and Polyphony Festival.
