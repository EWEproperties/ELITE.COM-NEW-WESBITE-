Location

Moraira is a unique town located on the northern Costa Blanca, offering wonderful beaches, natural beauty and rich history. Located just an hour's drive from Alicante Airport, Moraira is the perfect destination for those who want to experience the beauty of Spain's Mediterranean coast.

Moraira has since developed into a charming town that attracts tourists from all over the world. The city has a rich history, dating back to the 14th century. You can still see the remains of the city's Castillo de Moraira and the watchtower that protected the city from pirates in the past.

The main attraction of this intimate town is the Moraira Castle, which perches on a hill overlooking the city and offers breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea. The city marina is also a must-see, where you can admire luxury yachts and dine in one of the many restaurants along the waterfront.

Moraira is also known for its excellent golf courses that attract golfers from all over the world. The nearby Ifach Golf Course offers challenging and picturesque golf with stunning sea views.

The city has eight beaches, each of which has its own unique charm and beauty. El Portet is a secluded cove with crystal clear waters, perfect for swimming and diving. Playa del Moraig is a beautiful golden sand beach surrounded by cliffs, offering a calm and relaxing atmosphere. Another must-see beach in Moraira is Playa de l'Ampolla, a large and lively beach with plenty of restaurants and bars. Soft white sand and clear water make it an ideal destination for families and beachgoers of all ages.
