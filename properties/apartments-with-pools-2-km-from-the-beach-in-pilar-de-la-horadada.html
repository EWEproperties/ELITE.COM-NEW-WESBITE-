Location

Pilar de la Horadada is located in the southernmost corner of the Vega Baja region in the province of Alicante, on the border with Murcia. Its coastline stretches for over 4 kilometers and offers a mix of wide sandy beaches, rocky coastlines, and intimate coves with golden sand. Calm, shallow waters make it a safe destination for families with young children, and the crystal-clear sea encourages snorkelers to discover marine life. Many of the beaches have been awarded the prestigious European Union Blue Flag, guaranteeing both quality and cleanliness.

Nature lovers will delight in the inland landscapes, including the Sierra de Escalona and the famous Rio Seco trails. These marked trails, 4 km and 10 km long, lead from the Pilar de Campoverde Nature Reserve to Mil Palmeras Beach.

Golfers can enjoy the 18-hole Lo Romero golf course, which offers challenge and enjoyment for both experienced players and beginners.

The local cuisine also makes this area unique. Traditional dishes such as meat stews, garlic rabbit, gachas migas (garlic and flour pancakes), as well as rich rice dishes, soups, and desserts such as milhojas (meringue cake) and cordiales (almond sweets), reflect the authentic flavors of Spanish gastronomy.
