Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional luxury villa located in the prestigious area of Cumbre del Sol, between Moraira and Jávea on the northern Costa Blanca. This exclusive property has been designed for the most discerning buyers seeking a contemporary home in Spain that combines breathtaking Mediterranean Sea views, outstanding construction quality, and complete privacy.

The residence offers approximately 669 m² of built area and is set on a 997 m² plot. Designed over three levels, the villa blends harmoniously with the natural landscape, allowing spectacular panoramic sea views to be enjoyed from almost every room. Contemporary architecture, elegant lines, natural stone finishes, expansive floor-to-ceiling windows, and spacious terraces create a timeless residence that perfectly combines luxury with its surroundings.

The villa has been thoughtfully designed to provide the highest level of everyday comfort and relaxation. It features three spacious bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom, as well as an additional guest toilet. The impressive master suite serves as a private retreat, offering a stylish walk-in wardrobe, a luxurious bathroom, and direct access to a panoramic terrace overlooking the Mediterranean Sea.

At the heart of the home is a spectacular open-plan living area comprising a spacious lounge, elegant dining room, and a designer kitchen with a central island. Thanks to the floor-to-ceiling glazing, the interiors are flooded with natural light and seamlessly extend onto the terraces and outdoor living areas. The villa also includes a separate laundry room and practical utility areas.

The lower level offers a versatile space that can be customised as a private wellness and spa area, gym, home cinema, entertainment room, wine cellar, or guest apartment, allowing future owners to tailor the property to their individual lifestyle.

The outdoor area has been designed to make the most of the Mediterranean climate. The spectacular infinity swimming pool appears to merge seamlessly with the horizon, while expansive terraces, elegant lounge areas, a covered outdoor dining space, and a barbecue area create the perfect setting for relaxing and entertaining family and friends. Beautifully landscaped gardens complete this exclusive Mediterranean retreat.

The villa is equipped with state-of-the-art technology to ensure maximum comfort and excellent energy efficiency. Features include underfloor heating, aerothermal air conditioning, photovoltaic panels, a smart home automation system, mechanical ventilation, an integrated audio system, a security alarm, and pre-installation for an electric vehicle charging station.

The property is situated in one of the most prestigious areas of Cumbre del Sol, renowned for its spectacular Mediterranean views, exclusive luxury residences, and peaceful natural surroundings. Beautiful beaches and secluded coves, Moraira Marina, fine restaurants, international schools, and renowned golf courses are all within easy reach. The location also provides convenient access to both Alicante and Valencia International Airports.

This exceptional property is an outstanding opportunity for buyers seeking a contemporary luxury residence in Spain that combines timeless architecture, premium quality finishes, and breathtaking panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea.

Location

The development is located in the prestigious area of Cumbre del Sol, between Moraira and Jávea on the northern Costa Blanca. This is one of the region's most exclusive locations, renowned for its spectacular panoramic Mediterranean views, low-density residential environment, and exceptional harmony with the surrounding natural landscape. It perfectly combines luxury, privacy, and tranquillity with easy access to beaches, marinas, prestigious golf courses, and all the services required for comfortable year-round living.

Just a few minutes' drive away lies the picturesque Cala del Moraig, together with several other beautiful beaches and secluded coves considered among the finest on the northern Costa Blanca. The centres of Moraira and Jávea are also nearby, offering an excellent selection of restaurants, cafés, boutiques, marinas, and charming seaside promenades. Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to renowned golf courses, scenic coastal hiking trails, and a wide variety of water sports.

Families benefit from easy access to the prestigious Lady Elizabeth School, recognised as one of the leading international schools on the Costa Blanca. Supermarkets, medical centres, sports facilities, and all essential everyday services are also conveniently located nearby.

The property enjoys excellent transport connections, with Alicante International Airport approximately 1 hour and 15 minutes away and Valencia International Airport around 1 hour and 30 minutes by car. This outstanding location is ideal for year-round living, as a luxury holiday residence, or as a prestigious investment on the Spanish Mediterranean coast.
