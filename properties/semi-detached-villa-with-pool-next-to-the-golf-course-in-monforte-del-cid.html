Location

Monforte del Cid is a municipality in the Valencian Community of Spain. It is located in the interior of Alicante province, in the Medio Vinalopó region.

Monforte del Cid in Alicante is the perfect town for those who enjoy playing golf in a natural setting with a mild climate, as well as for those seeking small towns brimming with history, culture, and heritage, along with fantastic museums.

In Monforte del Cid, you can start by walking through the old town, which has preserved its medieval layout and houses from the 16th to 20th centuries, with facades decorated with typical Manises tiles depicting religious scenes.

Highlights include the town hall with its 16th-century arches and coat of arms, the Gothic Church of Our Lady of Nieves, and the San Pascual and San Roque hermitages. You'll definitely love walking and admiring these buildings!

We recommend stopping at the Ibero, the Villa de Monforte del Cid History Museum, where you can admire Iberian-era bull sculptures and even a Roman tombstone. In fact, near Monforte del Cid and Novelda is Finca el Campet, where the remains of a large Roman villa have been discovered.

After exploring the town center, your next stop is Orito, a district of Monforte del Cid that is a pilgrimage site and home to the 16th-century Church and Monastery of San Pascual and the Virgin of Orito, as well as the Cueva de Saint Pascual. You can stroll there and admire the incredible views. A fantastic pilgrimage to this site takes place in May!

For leisure, in addition to excellent golf courses, Monforte del Cid offers the opportunity to participate in the wonderful Moorish and Christian festivals held in December. Be sure to sample the excellent grapes in Vinalopó Designation of Origin bags, aniseed and liqueurs, also protected by the Regulatory Council, as well as pasta and cakes.
