Elite World Estates presents an exceptional residential development located within the prestigious Hacienda del Álamo Golf Resort in the Murcia region. This is an excellent opportunity for those seeking a comfortable apartment in a peaceful natural setting, with golf course views and access to a wide range of resort amenities.

This boutique development consists of modern three-storey buildings with only six apartments in each block, ensuring privacy, comfort, and an exclusive atmosphere for residents.

The project offers thoughtfully designed apartments with 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Bright interiors feature a modern open-plan layout that seamlessly combines the living room, dining area, and kitchen. Large windows provide abundant natural light while offering beautiful views of the surrounding greenery and golf course.

Ground-floor apartments feature a private garden and a spacious terrace of approximately 22 m². First-floor apartments offer large terraces of around 20 m², while penthouses stand out with two terraces and a private rooftop solarium of approximately 45 m², creating the perfect space for year-round outdoor living.

Residents can enjoy an elegant communal swimming pool, beautifully landscaped gardens, and relaxation areas. Each apartment includes a private parking space and a storage room.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its prime frontline location next to the renowned 18-hole Hacienda del Álamo Golf Course. Restaurants, a golf clubhouse, a hotel, and the resort's commercial and leisure facilities are all within easy reach.

The complex is located approximately 20 minutes from Murcia-Corvera International Airport and around 30 minutes from the beautiful beaches of Costa Cálida and the Mediterranean Sea.

This development is an ideal choice as a second home in Spain, a holiday property, or an attractive rental investment in one of the most sought-after golf destinations in the Murcia region.

Location

Fuente Álamo de Murcia is a charming Spanish town located in the heart of the Murcia region. It offers an authentic Mediterranean lifestyle, a relaxed atmosphere, and an excellent location close to the Costa Cálida coastline. Surrounded by picturesque hills, olive groves, and countryside landscapes, it is an ideal destination for those seeking sunshine, tranquillity, and a high quality of life throughout the year.

The town provides all essential services, including shops, restaurants, cafés, schools, healthcare facilities, sports centres, and everyday conveniences. Residents can enjoy peaceful surroundings without compromising on comfort and accessibility.

A major advantage of Fuente Álamo is its strategic location. Murcia-Corvera International Airport is only around 20 minutes away by car, while the sandy beaches of Costa Cálida and the Mediterranean Sea can be reached in approximately 25 minutes. Nearby attractions also include prestigious golf courses, natural parks, and the historic cities of Cartagena and Murcia, offering excellent cultural and gastronomic experiences.

With more than 300 days of sunshine per year, a pleasant climate, and a welcoming atmosphere, Fuente Álamo is becoming increasingly popular among both Spanish and international property buyers.
