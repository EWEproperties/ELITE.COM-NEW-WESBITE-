Location

Benalmádena is located just 20 km from Málaga and 40 km from Marbella. The city is loved by Spanish tourists. The high quality of services means that guests from all over the world moor at the local Puerto Marina, and the cosmopolitan atmosphere makes it hard to think about anything other than blissful relaxation and great entertainment.

Benalmádena can be divided into three main parts: Benalmádena Pueblo – the historic Andalusian area with characteristic white buildings, the modern residential Arroyo de la Miel, and the magnificent marina. Each of them is unique and worth attention.

1. Benalmádena Pueblo

The restored old town (pueblo) with narrow streets and two squares (Plaza de España and Plaza Andalucía). In the first one, there is the bronze La Niña fountain, which is a symbol of Benalmádena Pueblo.

2. Benalmádena Costa – the newest part of the city, located along the coast and directly by the sea. A significant part of its buildings consists of tourist facilities (hotels, dining venues, nightclubs, and beaches).

3. Arroyo de la Miel (“Honey Stream”) – originally a separate settlement located between the other two districts. It is the main residential area and the most commercially active.

Benalmádena is especially recommended for families with children, as it offers a large number of attractions also designed for the youngest visitors. Most of the most interesting ones are listed below:

Fairy-tale Columbus castle

Colomares Castle is not actually a castle but a monument dedicated to Columbus. Nevertheless, it is beautiful and one of the major attractions in Benalmádena. It was designed and built by Esteban Martín. Its construction lasted from 1987 to 1994. An interesting fact is that the castle houses the smallest church in the world, listed in the Guinness Book of Records. Its area is only 1.96 m².

STUPA

A living monument of an enlightened mind, a Buddhist symbol of peace and harmony in the world, and a place for learning, reflection, and meditation. It is also noteworthy because it is the largest stupa in Europe. Few people know that it was designed by the Polish architect Wojciech Kossowski. An additional attraction is the beautiful panoramic view from the hill.

Marina in Benalmádena

Puerto Marina is a great place for a family walk or dinner with wine. You can admire the moored boats and interesting architecture. The marina is full of numerous bars, shops, and cafés. It has twice been named the most beautiful marina in the world. It is also known as the “Andalusian Venice” due to its streets, narrow canals, and bridges. One of the attractions you can enjoy here is the aquarium. At Sea Life, you will find over 2,000 species of marine creatures, including turtles, sharks, and rays.

Tivoli World

An ideal option for spending time for both children and adults. Among fountains and gardens, you can enjoy not only over 30 attractions for the whole family but also a wide gastronomic offer. It is a classic-style amusement park with traditional attractions such as Ferris wheels, bumper cars, and a haunted house, as well as newer options for the more adventurous. The tower, from which you can experience a thrilling 60-meter drop, is undoubtedly one of the most spectacular attractions in the park.

Benalmádena cable car

The Benalmádena cable car allows you to see the whole city from a bird’s-eye view. The three-kilometer route leads to the top of Mount Calamorro in the Sierra de Mijas mountain range. Once there, you can admire a beautiful panorama of the Costa del Sol coastline, nearby Gibraltar, and, in good weather, even the coast of Africa. In addition to the stunning views, there are also optional attractions at the top, such as birds of prey shows, Andalusian horse training demonstrations, hiking trails, and cycling routes.

BEACHES

While in this beautiful city, it is definitely worth relaxing on one of Benalmádena’s beaches, and there are many to choose from: Arroyo Hondo, Carvajal, Bonita, Bil, La Morera, Arroyo de la Miel, Benalnatura, Fuente de la Salud, La Perla, Las Viborillas, Playa de las Yucas, Torrebermeja, Santa Ana, Tajo de la Soga, Torremuelle, Torrequebrada, and Torrevigía are options worth considering when planning your stay.
