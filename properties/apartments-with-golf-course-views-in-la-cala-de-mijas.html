A new residential development situated in the exceptional surroundings of La Cala de Mijas on the Costa del Sol, directly next to extensive golf courses and surrounded by greenery and mountain scenery.

The complex offers modern 2- and 3-bedroom apartments, designed with an emphasis on space, natural light and comfort. The available properties offer approximately 88 m² to 120 m² of built area, while spacious terraces provide the perfect setting to enjoy the Mediterranean climate and the surrounding views. Selected apartments also feature private solariums of up to approximately 66 m².

The interiors are finished in a bright, contemporary style. The kitchens come with fitted cabinetry and Balay appliances, while the bedrooms feature spacious built-in wardrobes. The bathrooms are equipped with high-quality sanitary ware and fittings, and large windows in the living areas provide plenty of natural light and convenient access to the terraces.

Each apartment features ducted air conditioning with heating and cooling, a mechanical ventilation system and energy-efficient LED lighting. The buildings are also equipped with solar panels that contribute to the energy supply of the residential complex.

One of the main advantages of the development is its extensive communal area with a large swimming pool and beautifully landscaped gardens. The buildings are equipped with lifts, while the apartments, depending on their type, include assigned outdoor parking spaces or spaces in an underground garage. Selected properties also include additional storage space.

Depending on the phase of the development, the expected completion dates are September and October 2026, as well as September 2027.

Location – golf, mountains and proximity to the Costa del Sol

The development is situated in a picturesque area of La Cala de Mijas, between Marbella and Fuengirola, surrounded by golf courses and the Sierra de Mijas mountains. Its elevated position provides wide panoramic views over the greenery, golf courses and mountains, while selected properties also enjoy views towards the Mediterranean Sea.

This location is particularly attractive for those who want to live in peaceful, green surroundings without giving up easy access to beaches, restaurants, shops and the coastal towns of the Costa del Sol. The beaches of La Cala de Mijas are approximately 7–8 km from the development, with the coast reachable by car in around 7–10 minutes.

The complex is located directly within an extensive golf resort offering three 18-hole golf courses and a 6-hole golf academy. The resort also provides a hotel, spa, restaurants, fitness centre, tennis courts and a variety of other sports and leisure facilities.

Its position between Fuengirola and Marbella also provides convenient access to the many attractions of the Costa del Sol. Marbella is approximately 20 minutes away by car, while Málaga-Costa del Sol International Airport can be reached in around 30 minutes. According to the developer's information, the airport is approximately 38.5 km away.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Calahonda - La Luna / Royal Beach, about 14 km by road, around 21 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio ECOS (about 3 km) and Colegio Alemán Juan Hoffmann (about 4 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Cala de Mijas, about 9 km away. The nearest hospital is Urgencias Hospital Costa del Sol, about 14 km by road, around 22 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is OpenCor, about 11 km away.

How far is the airport?

Málaga Airport is about 46 km by road, around 35 minutes by car.
