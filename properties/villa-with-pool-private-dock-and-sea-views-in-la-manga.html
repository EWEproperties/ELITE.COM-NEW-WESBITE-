For sale, a spacious villa located in an exceptional part of La Manga del Mar Menor, between the waters of the Mar Menor and the Mediterranean Sea. The property stands out for its spectacular views of the sea and surrounding canals, which can be enjoyed from the house, terraces and garden. An additional advantage is the private dock, providing direct access to the sea by boat. The beaches are just a few minutes from the property.

The villa offers 465.32 m² of built area and is set on a large 1,361 m² plot. Outside, there is a private swimming pool surrounded by relaxation areas and Mediterranean vegetation. Its waterfront location allows you to enjoy beautiful coastal scenery from many areas of the property.

The main living area comprises 3 spacious bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, a large living room with a fireplace, a separate kitchen, pantry and utility area. All bedrooms have built-in wardrobes.

The villa also features large covered terraces and a 72 m² solarium with a viewing area, offering particularly impressive panoramic views over the Mar Menor, the canals and the surrounding landscape.

On the lower level, there is an additional 239 m² basement and garage area, which can be used as a large garage, gym, games room, wine cellar or additional leisure space.

One of the property's greatest features is its private dock directly on the canal. The possibility of setting off by boat directly from your own property, combined with a private swimming pool and exceptional water views, makes this a particularly attractive home in one of Murcia's most distinctive coastal locations.

Location

The villa is located in an exceptional part of La Manga del Mar Menor in the Murcia region, on the narrow strip of land stretching between the Mediterranean Sea and the Mar Menor. This unique setting offers proximity to two seas, beautiful beaches and spectacular views over the water.

The property is situated directly on a canal, with its own private dock and access to the sea by boat. Natural islets, golf courses and a marina can be found nearby. The area offers a peaceful seaside atmosphere and excellent conditions for sailing, water sports and enjoying the Mediterranean lifestyle for most of the year.
