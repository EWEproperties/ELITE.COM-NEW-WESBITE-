We are pleased to present a new development in the charming area of Los Hidalgos, La Duquesa, Manilva, on the Costa del Sol. It is a great opportunity to live in luxury close to the popular town of Marbella, with everything this area has to offer.

The project comprises 38 stylish apartments and penthouses with large terraces and sea views. The homes have been designed for comfort and style. The open-plan kitchen and living room create a bright, spacious space, and large windows let in plenty of natural light and frame the views.

Choose your favourite: a ground-floor apartment with a private garden, or a penthouse with a sunny terrace and private pool. Whichever you choose, every home has breathtaking sea views.

Each property includes an underground parking space with a pre-installed electric car charger and a separate storage room.

Outside, residents can enjoy beautifully landscaped communal gardens with native plants and a large swimming pool, the perfect place to relax.

Location

Manilva, surrounded by well-known Andalusian resorts, has something other towns on the Costa del Sol cannot offer: vineyards, which cover the surrounding hills. Manilva's limited-production wine is a true symbol of the region.

The town lies 97 km from Málaga and has two parts: the tourist resort by the beach and the town itself, with its white historic houses. Manilva has kept the feel of a traditional Spanish village. Close to the Strait of Gibraltar, it is rich in geography and culture, and a place for a peaceful stay away from the bustle of big cities. The nearest airport is 41 km away.

The beaches are among the best on the Costa del Sol, with almost 8 km of coastline. Some are rocky with small coves, at Punta Chullera, while wide sandy beaches lie next to the Castillo, the port of Puerto de la Duquesa and the town of Sabinillas. You can swim, dive, fish and enjoy water sports.

Besides its beaches and marina, Manilva has an interesting history and culture, including the castle of La Duquesa, several churches and the archaeological finds in the Municipal Archaeological Museum.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de la Chullera, about 3 km by road, around 4 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP San Luis de Sabinillas (about 2.9 km) and Colegio Pablo Picasso (about 6 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud de Sabinillas, about 3 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital de Alta Resolución de Estepona, about 10 km by road, around 12 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Eroski, about 1.1 km away.

How far is the airport?

Málaga Airport is about 98 km by road, around 75 minutes by car.
