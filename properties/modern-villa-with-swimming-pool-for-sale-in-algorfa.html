The nearby La Finca golf course is located close to the Spanish town of Algorfa and a 15-minute drive from the beaches of Guardamar. The lively towns of Los Montesinos and San Miguel, which offer a wide range of amenities such as bars, shops and restaurants, are a short drive away.

Location

Algorfa, located in the Vega Baja del Segura region, has a rich history dating back to Roman times. The town's name comes from the Arabic word “Al-Gorfa”, meaning “cave”, as the area was known for its numerous natural caves. Today, Algorfa is known for its production of citrus fruits and vegetables.

Algorfa is home to many attractions that will appeal to visitors of all ages. Here are just a few of the highlights:

La Finca Golf Course

If you are a golf enthusiast, La Finca Golf Course is a must-visit. This championship golf course is set in beautiful natural surroundings, with stunning views of the Vega Baja countryside. The course is designed to challenge golfers of all levels, and there are plenty of amenities, including a pro shop and restaurant.

Algorfa Market

To get a taste of the local culture, be sure to visit the Algorfa Market, which takes place every Wednesday. Here you will find a wide selection of goods, including fresh local produce, clothing and souvenirs. It is the perfect place to buy a unique gift or sample the delicious local cuisine.

Plaza de España

Plaza de España is the main square in Algorf, which is a great place to soak up the local atmosphere. Here you will find a beautiful fountain, plenty of seating and many restaurants and bars. It is the perfect place to relax with a drink and admire the local culture.
