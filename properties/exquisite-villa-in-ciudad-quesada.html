Step into the world of luxury living with this stunning independent villa, where modern architecture meets the warm embrace of Mediterranean aesthetics. Nestled on a generous 655 m² plot, this home beckons you with its expansive garden and a private pool that promises delightful days under the sun. Our glorious weather, year-round, invites you to savor life outdoors right in your own villa.

The villa boasts four spacious bedrooms, three elegant bathrooms, a well-appointed kitchen, and a comfortable living area, all thoughtfully designed on a single floor for your convenience. As if that weren't enough, the property also features an expansive 130 m² solarium, providing you with panoramic views and endless possibilities.

Situated in the serene surroundings of Ciudad Quesada, this gem offers tranquility while ensuring you're never far from essential services. It's not just a house; it's an invitation to experience the very essence of luxurious living. Welcome home.

Location

Ciudad Quesada is situated in the southern part of the Costa Blanca, just 8 km north of the main city, Torrevieja. Nearby towns include Rojales and San Fulgencio. You can easily reach Ciudad Quesada from the nearby airport, which offers numerous connections. The city is only a 30-minute drive from Alicante Airport, which has excellent connections to major European cities. Come and explore the best of the southern Costa Blanca in this vibrant town.

Beaches

The town is a short distance from beautiful sandy beaches, such as El Moncayo in Guardamar del Segura, and it is surrounded by stunning natural parks and picturesque landscapes.

Laguna La Mata

Nature enthusiasts and peace seekers can't resist packing a backpack, putting on comfortable or sports shoes, and hitting the trails. In Ciudad Quesada, you can embark on captivating hikes through amazing places. There are many hiking routes, but one of the most enthralling for nature lovers and birdwatchers is a visit to La Mata Lagoon.

Golf

Golf enthusiasts can indulge in the renowned La Marquesa Golf Club, which offers breathtaking views of the surrounding area. Meanwhile, nature enthusiasts can explore the nearby salt lakes of Torrevieja, which are a habitat for numerous bird species. For those seeking water-based activities...

History

The town boasts a rich history dating back to the early 18th century and has evolved into a vibrant community with a unique blend of Spanish, British, and other European cultures. In Ciudad Quesada, you can experience the town's rural heritage in its charming old town, while the modern and cosmopolitan area around the city center reflects its recent development.

Pampering and Relaxation

Another must-do activity in Ciudad Quesada is to visit the spa and let someone take care of your well-being for the morning, afternoon, or the entire day. At the spa of Hotel La Laguna, you can indulge in the "Sensory Circuit," granting you access to a wellness zone with hot and cold contrasts, a relaxation area, and a saltwater jacuzzi, allowing you to engage all your senses through various areas: sight, smell, taste, touch, and hearing. To relieve stress, you can enjoy a bamboo or chocolate massage or partake in anti-stress therapy. You'll leave feeling more relaxed than ever before. The spa at Hotel La Laguna has a significant advantage: you don't need to be a hotel guest to use its facilities.
