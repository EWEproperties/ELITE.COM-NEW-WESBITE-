Location

Lo Pagan is part of San Pedro Del Pinatar, a coastal town on the Costa Calida. Its shores are bathed by both the Mar Menor and the Mediterranean Sea. Warm temperatures averaging 18 degrees throughout the year and over 315 days of natural light make it the perfect place to holiday and live. The Mar Menor has calm, warm waters, and the Mediterranean Sea is open and natural thanks to the Las Salinas de San Pedro del Pinatar nature reserve.

San Pedro del Pinatar has 14 kilometres of beaches scattered across two seas, the Mar Menor and the Mediterranean. Its strategic location on the coast and its environmental assets have made it a highly sought-after residential area.

In addition to its well-maintained beaches, port and complete commercial and service infrastructure, San Pedro del Pinatar has a unique natural area - the Las Salinas y Arenales de San Pedro del Pinatar protected landscape. It is a wetland area where you can find flamingos, seagulls, albatrosses, herons and owls; it also has shifting dunes with characteristic vegetation: sea lilies, rushes and reeds.

The municipality of San Pedro del Pinatar, in addition to the main town, consists of seventeen small towns, such as El Mojón, Las Salinas, Los Sáez and Lo Pagán. The latter area, Lo Pagán, occupies the most important place thanks to the great development of tourism.

The most famous beach in Lo Pagán is La Puntica, which offers picturesque amenities such as beach huts and a bathing area with access to the sea, as well as a wide range of leisure activities.
