Location

Moraira is a hidden gem that offers stunning beaches, natural beauty and a rich history. Located just an hour's drive from Alicante airport, Moraira is the perfect destination for those who want to experience the beauty of Spain's Mediterranean coast.

Calpe and Peñón de Ifach are just 9 kilometers southwest of the city of Moraira. Not much further is Xàbia-Jávea, about 11 kilometers to the north.

Perfectly developed tourist infrastructure, intimate atmosphere, amazing views and sunny weather attract not only tourists. Moraira is also an attractive location for investors looking for the best place to buy a holiday property or for tourist rental.

Moraira used to be a small fishing village which has since grown into a charming town that attracts visitors from all over the world. The city has a rich history dating back to the 14th century. You can still see the remains of the town's Castillo de Moraira and the watchtower that protected the town from pirates in the past.

One of the city's most famous attractions is the Moraira Castle, which is perched on a hill overlooking the city and offers breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea. The city's marina is also a must-visit, where you can admire luxury yachts and dine in one of the many restaurants along the waterfront.

Moraira is also known for its excellent golf courses that attract golfers from all over the world. The nearby Ifach Golf Course offers a challenging and scenic golf experience with stunning sea views.

Moraira is home to some of the most beautiful beaches on the Costa Blanca. The city has eight beaches, each of which has its own unique charm and beauty. El Portet is a secluded cove with crystal clear waters, ideal for swimming and snorkeling. Playa del Moraig is a beautiful golden sand beach surrounded by cliffs, offering a peaceful and relaxing atmosphere.

Another must-see beach in Moraira is Playa de l'Ampolla, a large and lively beach with plenty of restaurants and bars. Soft white sand and clear water make it an ideal destination for families and sunbathers of all ages.
