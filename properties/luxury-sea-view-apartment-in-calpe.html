Location

Calpe, an enchanting seaside town located on the Mediterranean Sea, entices with its undeniable charm and abundance of attractions. Located in the province of Alicante, Spain, Calpe offers a delightful blend of natural beauty, historic sites and vibrant attractions. Located approximately 75 kilometres from Alicante Airport, Calpe provides a convenient commute for travellers wishing to explore its wonders.

On arrival, you will be delighted by the stunning beaches, which have won prestigious Q' certificates for quality and the Blue Flag. Calpe boasts some of the most beautiful sandy coastlines in the region, including the iconic Playa de la Fossa and Playa de la Arenal-Bol. Immerse yourself in Mediterranean bliss, bask in the sun or enjoy the water sports and activities that are an integral part of Calpe's coastal landscape.

In addition to its picturesque beaches, Calpe is adorned with historical and cultural treasures. The distinctive Peñón de Ifach, a massive limestone rock formation, is a symbol of Calpe and offers breathtaking panoramic views. Take a hike to the top and admire the natural beauty unfolding before your eyes. The old town with its charming narrow streets and historic architecture invites you to stroll around and discover its rich heritage.

Calpe's bustling fishing port is a must-visit destination. Watch the fishermen bring in their daily catch, visit the fish market and enjoy fresh seafood in the many restaurants along the harbour. To learn about the local culture, visit the Museum of Archaeology and Ethnography to delve into Calpe's fascinating history.

Nature lovers will find solace in the nature parks surrounding Calpe. Explore Las Salinas Nature Park, a paradise for migratory birds such as flamingos and storks, or head to Serra Gelada Nature Park to see its rugged cliffs and enchanting trails. For hikers, the Sierra de Bernia and Sierra de Oltà offer exciting trails and stunning views.

Immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of Calpe's local markets, where you can discover fresh produce, handicrafts and traditions. Savour the local gastronomy, which is famous for its seafood, rice dishes and spicy tapas.
