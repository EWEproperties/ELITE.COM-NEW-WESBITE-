Location

Daya Nueva, in the province of Alicante, is a quiet town that stands out for its rural atmosphere and proximity to the coast. Although it is not a place of mass tourism, its charm lies in its authenticity and tranquillity. Tourism in Daya Nueva focuses on the rural surroundings and contact with nature, conducive to walking and outdoor activities. In addition, the proximity of the Costa Blanca beaches is an added bonus of this location. The lifestyle in Daya Nueva is peaceful and rooted in rural life. The residents enjoy the tranquillity and community life, valuing their connection to the land and local traditions. The cuisine is based on traditional regional dishes, with fresh produce and authentic flavours. Daya Nueva offers a peaceful and authentic atmosphere for those seeking a quiet life in a rural setting close to the coast.

The town itself has a church square, several restaurants and bars, and a street market where fresh produce is sold every Tuesday. The neighbouring towns of Almoradi, San Fulgencio, Dolores, Daya Vieja, Formentera del Segura and Rojales are a short drive away and offer a wide range of shops and leisure facilities, including restaurants, large supermarkets, golf courses, etc.

The village is located in a quiet area, yet close to the amenities of the town. Golf enthusiasts can take advantage of the nearby La Finca Golf Resort in Algorfa and La Marquesa Golf in Cuidad Quesada. The location is also ideal for travelling to Alicante Airport, which is approximately 35 km from the town.
