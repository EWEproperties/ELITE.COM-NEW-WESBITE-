Location

Located in the south of Alicante, Torrevieja is a beautiful city known for its sunny beaches, vibrant culture and nightlife. With its proximity to Elche airport, which is only 40 minutes away, Torrevieja is ideal for those looking for a quick and easy holiday

Beaches in Torrevieja

One of Torrevieja's main attractions is its stunning beaches. There are several beaches in the town with something for everyone. One of the most popular beaches is La Mata, which is famous for its fine sand and crystal clear waters. Other popular beaches include Los Locos, El Cura and Playa del Acequión, all of which are ideal for swimming, sunbathing and water sports.

In addition to the beaches, Torrevieja offers many attractions to keep visitors entertained. One of the most popular attractions is the Salt Lakes of Torrevieja, which is a natural park that is home to a wide variety of flora and fauna. Visitors can take a walk through the park and see the pink flamingos that inhabit the area.

Another must-see in Torrevieja is the promenade, which is the perfect place to take a leisurely stroll and enjoy the sea views. The promenade is dotted with restaurants, cafes and shops, making it the perfect place to relax and take in the local culture.

For history buffs, the Sea and Salt Museum is a great place to learn about the history of Torrevieja and the local salt industry. The museum has several exhibits that showcase the town's rich history and culture.

Nightlife in Torrevieja

When the sun goes down, Torrevieja comes alive with a vibrant nightlife. The town has several bars and nightclubs that offer a wide range of music and entertainment. One of the most popular clubs is Velice, which hosts world-renowned DJs and parties throughout the year. Whether you are looking for a quiet drink or an evening of dancing, Torrevieja has something to offer.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa del Acequión, about 800 m by road.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio Público El Acequión, about 600 m.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 21 km, around 23 minutes by car.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 14 km, around 16 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Patricio Pérez, about 800 m away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 4 km by road, around 7 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Tembo, about 600 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 58 km by road, around 45 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 66 km, around 50 minutes by car.
