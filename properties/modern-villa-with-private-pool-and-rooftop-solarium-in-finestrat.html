Location

Finestrat is located just 8 km from the popular city of Benidorm and approximately 40 km from Alicante and its international airport. Known for its breathtaking mountain views, beautiful beaches, and rich cultural heritage, Finestrat is the perfect destination for anyone seeking relaxation and unforgettable experiences.

Nestled on the slopes of Puig Campana mountain, Finestrat offers stunning views of the mountains, coastline, and Mediterranean Sea. The town retains a traditional Moorish charm, with colourful houses and narrow cobbled streets. Meanwhile, Cala Finestrat has become increasingly popular and has grown into a natural extension of Benidorm.

Cala Finestrat

Cala Finestrat is a charming seaside resort located just 3 km from bustling Benidorm. The area offers excellent amenities, a wide range of restaurants, bars, and shops. Everything you may need can also be found in Benidorm, which is easily accessible by bus or taxi.

La Cala Beach remains relatively peaceful, as many visitors seeking a livelier atmosphere head to the beaches of Benidorm. The beach features golden sand, calm waters, and safe swimming conditions, making it ideal for families with children. Along the promenade, you will find several attractive restaurants, while during the summer months an arts and crafts market takes place in the afternoons.

The area is ideal for families and couples looking for a relaxing beach destination while remaining close to a wide range of attractions and amenities. Nearby you will find Terra Mítica and Terra Natura theme parks, Aqualandia water park, and Mundomar marine and exotic animal park, providing entertainment for the whole family.

Golf enthusiasts will also appreciate the nearby Puig Campana Golf and Villaitana Golf courses, offering excellent facilities in a spectacular setting.

Both Finestrat and Cala Finestrat provide a more peaceful atmosphere than many neighbouring resorts while still offering a great selection of restaurants, bars, and leisure activities.
