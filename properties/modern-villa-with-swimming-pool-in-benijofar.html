Location

Welcome to Benijofar, a small town located on the southern Costa Blanca, in the province of Alicante, Spain. This charming town is known for its friendly locals, traditional Spanish architecture, and idyllic location surrounded by citrus groves and vegetable farms. In this article, we will take you on a journey through Benijofar and its surroundings, and show you why this hidden gem of the Costa Blanca deserves to be on your travel bucket list.

Location and History

Benijofar is located just a short drive away from the coastal towns of Guardamar del Segura and Torrevieja, and is only 30 minutes from the international airport of Alicante. The town has a rich history dating back to the Moorish era, and there are still traces of its past visible in the old town, with narrow streets, whitewashed houses, and a beautiful church.

Things to Do

Despite being a small town, Benijofar has plenty of things to do and see. One of the main attractions is the weekly street market, held every Tuesday morning, where you can buy local produce, clothing, and souvenirs. The town also has a number of bars and restaurants serving traditional Spanish cuisine, as well as international dishes.

If you're interested in outdoor activities, you can take a stroll along the Segura River, which runs through the town, or head to the nearby La Mata Natural Park for a hike or picnic. Golf enthusiasts will be delighted to know that there are several golf courses within a short drive from Benijofar, including the renowned La Finca Golf Resort.

Beaches

Of course, no trip to the Costa Blanca would be complete without a visit to the beach. Benijofar is just a short drive away from some of the best beaches in the area, including Guardamar Beach and La Mata Beach. These beautiful beaches offer soft golden sands, clear blue waters, and plenty of facilities, including restaurants, bars, and water sports.
