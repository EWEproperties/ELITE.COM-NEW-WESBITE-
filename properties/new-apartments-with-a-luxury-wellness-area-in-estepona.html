Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional residential development located on the prestigious New Golden Mile in Estepona. The development offers 1, 2, 3, and 4-bedroom apartments. Ground-floor residences feature private gardens, while the penthouses boast spacious terraces and private rooftop solariums with beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea, the surrounding mountains, or the landscaped gardens.

Residents enjoy access to an extensive range of resort-style amenities, including four outdoor swimming pools surrounded by tropical gardens, a modern 450 m² wellness and spa centre with a heated indoor pool, a fully equipped fitness centre, a private cinema room, a dedicated youth club, and a coworking space ideal for remote working.

The location on the New Golden Mile offers the perfect balance between tranquillity and convenience. Beautiful sandy beaches, renowned golf courses, restaurants, shops, marinas, and all everyday services are just a few minutes away. Location

Estepona enjoys an excellent location just a short distance from Málaga International Airport, making it easily accessible for both holidaymakers and permanent residents. The airport is approximately 80 kilometres away, ensuring a comfortable journey.

Estepona perfectly combines modern infrastructure with the authentic charm of Andalusia, making it one of the most desirable destinations on the Costa del Sol. Once a traditional fishing town, it has evolved into one of the region's most attractive places to live thanks to its exceptional quality of life, vibrant atmosphere, and wide range of leisure activities.

The area is home to numerous prestigious golf courses, earning the region the nickname "Costa del Golf." Surrounded by spectacular natural landscapes, Estepona also offers excellent opportunities for hiking, cycling, sailing, tennis, and many other outdoor activities throughout the year.

Today, Estepona is considered one of Spain's leading examples of sustainable urban development. Significant public investment since 2011 has transformed the town into a benchmark for quality of life, modern public services, environmental care, and tourism infrastructure.

The ambitious "Estepona, Garden of the Costa del Sol" project has revitalised the historic centre with thousands of colourful flower pots, pedestrian streets, beautifully landscaped public spaces, and improved commercial areas. Thanks to these initiatives, Estepona has become one of the fastest-growing municipalities in Andalusia in terms of employment, investment, and tourism.

The town boasts an impressive selection of beaches. Among the most popular are Playa del Cristo, a sheltered bay with calm waters ideal for families, and Playa de la Rada, a long stretch of golden sand offering excellent facilities and a wide range of water sports.

Nature lovers will appreciate the nearby Sierra Bermeja mountains, which provide fantastic hiking trails with breathtaking panoramic views of the Mediterranean coastline. Golf enthusiasts will find themselves surrounded by some of the finest golf courses in Southern Europe, all set within stunning natural scenery.
