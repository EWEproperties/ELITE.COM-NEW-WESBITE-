Location

Gran Alacant is a charming town located a short distance from the bustling city of Alicante in Spain. With its stunning beaches, natural beauty and wide range of activities and attractions, Gran Alacant is the perfect place to invest.

Beaches

One of Gran Alacant's attractions is the beautiful El Carabassi beach, which stretches over 2 kilometres with golden sand and crystal clear water. With its calm waters and gentle waves, it is the perfect place for swimming, sunbathing and water sports.

In addition to the beach, Gran Alacant offers a range of other activities and attractions to keep you entertained. The area is renowned for its natural beauty, with miles of walking and cycling trails winding through the nearby mountains and forests.

For nature lovers, the nearby Clot de Galvany Nature Reserve is a must-see destination. This protected area covers over 350 hectares of natural forests and wetlands, home to a variety of flora and fauna, including rare birds and wildlife.

If you're looking for a more cultural experience, be sure to visit the nearby city of Alicante, where you can explore landmarks such as St. Barbara's Castle and the Museum of Contemporary Art.

Located a short distance from Alicante-Elche Airport, Gran Alacant is easily accessible from anywhere in the world. Whether you are coming from Europe, North America or Asia, you will find that getting to Gran Alacant is quick, easy and hassle-free.
