For sale is a modern two-storey villa located in Ciudad Quesada on the Costa Blanca, just approximately 50 metres from La Marquesa Golf Course. The property is ready to move into and sold fully furnished, allowing the new owners to enjoy their home in Spain immediately after purchase.

The villa offers 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, with the living space conveniently distributed over two floors. Modern architecture, large windows and a bright open-plan living area provide plenty of natural light and create a comfortable space for everyday living and relaxation.

The property is set on a private plot of approximately 275 m². Outside, there is a private 3 × 6 m swimming pool, a spacious terrace with plenty of room for relaxation and a private parking space within the property.

An additional advantage is the private solarium, providing even more outdoor space for relaxing, sunbathing and enjoying the sunny Costa Blanca climate throughout most of the year.

The combination of contemporary design, complete furnishings, a private swimming pool and an excellent location close to the golf course makes this property an attractive option for both permanent living and as a holiday home in Spain.

Location

The villa is located in Ciudad Quesada, just approximately 50 metres from La Marquesa Golf Course. The surrounding area offers a wide selection of restaurants, bars, supermarkets, shops and all essential everyday services.

The sandy beaches of Guardamar del Segura are approximately 15 minutes away by car, while Alicante-Elche Airport can be reached in around 35 minutes. This convenient location allows you to enjoy peaceful surroundings while remaining within easy reach of the coast and some of the most popular destinations on the Costa Blanca.
