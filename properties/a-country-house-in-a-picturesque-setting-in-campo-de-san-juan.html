Location

Campo de San Juan, or simply San Juan, is a Murcian village in the municipality of Moratalla, located in the valley of the same name, between Moratalla, Archivel, San Bartolomé and Benízar.

It is 1,100 metres above sea level, which means that it snows regularly in the winter months and the temperatures are low in the summer months. There are restaurants, a supermarket, a sports centre and a sausage factory called ‘El Siglo’, as well as several country houses that welcome visitors all year round. The main crops are cereals and aromatic plants, as well as animal husbandry and meat and sausage production.

Campo de San Juan, in Moratalla, in the Region of Murcia, is at this time of year a real explosion of smells and colours typical of the summer season. Along with other wild aromatic plants such as thyme and rosemary, crops of lavender and lavandin adorn these highlands in the north-west of Murcia.

‘Little Provence’ manifests itself in the flowering, along with the hands of local essence artisans who cut the stems of these aromatic lavender plants to produce the base of many of the perfumes that we can find in the small bottles of large retail chains, adorned with celebrity photos and appropriate marketing, but whose base is grown in the environment of these mountainous fields, surrounded by a natural environment like few others in this region.
