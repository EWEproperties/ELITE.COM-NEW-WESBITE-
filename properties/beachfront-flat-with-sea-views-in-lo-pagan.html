Location

Lo Pagán is a major tourist resort in the municipality of San Pedro del Pinatar on the Costa Calida. The beaches of La Puntica and Villananitos, with fine sand and shallow waters, quality services and the existence of an outdoor mud therapy area are just some of the tourist attractions of this district.

At the end of the 19th century, Lo Pagán was an estate owned by Don Pedro Pagán y Ayuso, which was frequented by intellectuals and politicians for literary meetings on summer evenings.

Warm temperatures with an average of 18 degrees throughout the year and more than 315 days of natural light make it an ideal place to holiday and live. The Mar Menor has calm, warm waters and the Mediterranean Sea is open and natural thanks to the Las Salinas de San Pedro del Pinatar nature reserve.

San Pedro del Pinatar has 14 km of beaches scattered over two seas, the Minor Sea (Mar Menor) and the Mediterranean. Its strategic location on the coast and environmental advantages have made it a highly sought-after residential area.

In addition to its well-maintained beaches, harbour and full commercial and service infrastructure, San Pedro del Pinatar has a unique natural space - the Las Salinas y Arenales de San Pedro del Pinatar Protected Landscape Area. It is a wetland area where flamingos, gulls, albatrosses, herons and owls can be seen; it also has moving dunes, with characteristic vegetation: sea lilies, rushes and reeds.

The municipality of San Pedro del Pinatar, in addition to the main town, is made up of seventeen small towns, such as El Mojón, Las Salinas, Los Sáez and Lo Pagán. This last area, Lo Pagán, occupies the most important place thanks to the great development of tourism.

The most famous beach in Lo Pagán is La Puntica beach, which offers picturesque amenities such as booths and a swimming area with access to the sea, as well as a wide range of leisure activities.
