Elite World Estates proudly presents a modern residential development located in the thriving area of Las Lagunas de Mijas, one of the fastest-growing and best-connected locations on the Costa del Sol. The development comprises 116 contemporary one-bedroom loft-style apartments, thoughtfully designed to maximise space, natural light, and everyday comfort. Open-plan layouts create bright and versatile living areas that seamlessly connect the living room, kitchen and sleeping space, offering a modern lifestyle perfectly suited for permanent living, a holiday home or a profitable rental investment.

The interiors feature warm natural tones, carefully selected materials and elegant finishes that create a welcoming atmosphere. Large windows allow abundant daylight to fill every room, while private terraces extend the living space outdoors, providing the perfect place to relax and enjoy the pleasant Mediterranean climate throughout the year.

Residents can enjoy a carefully designed selection of communal facilities dedicated to relaxation and wellbeing. The development features a beautiful outdoor swimming pool, a fully equipped private gym, a pool bar, and a stylish chill-out area, creating the perfect environment to unwind, socialise and enjoy life under the Costa del Sol sunshine.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its exceptional location. Situated in the established residential area of Las Lagunas de Mijas, it offers immediate access to shopping centres, supermarkets, restaurants, healthcare facilities, schools and all essential daily services. Everything you need is within walking distance or just a short drive away, making everyday life exceptionally convenient.

The development is located only 2 km from the beach, while several renowned golf courses can be reached within just a few minutes. Excellent transport connections provide quick access to Fuengirola, Mijas, Marbella and Málaga, making this an ideal base for enjoying everything the Costa del Sol has to offer.

Thanks to its modern design, strategic location and strong rental demand, this development represents an excellent opportunity for both homeowners and investors. Whether you are looking for your first property in Spain, a stylish holiday apartment or a high-yield investment, these contemporary loft apartments offer exceptional value in one of the Costa del Sol's most promising residential areas.

Location

The development is located in Las Lagunas de Mijas, one of the fastest-growing areas of Mijas Costa, between Fuengirola and Marbella on the western Costa del Sol. This modern and well-connected location combines everyday convenience with easy access to beaches, renowned golf courses, and a full range of urban amenities. The dynamic growth of this part of the coast makes it an excellent choice both for year-round living and as a property investment.

The surrounding area offers numerous supermarkets, shopping centres, restaurants, cafés, schools, medical facilities, and sports venues. The sandy beaches of Fuengirola, its seaside promenade, and lively marina with a wide selection of restaurants and leisure facilities are just a few minutes' drive away. Direct access to the A-7 and AP-7 motorways ensures fast and convenient travel throughout the Costa del Sol.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of prestigious courses such as Cerrado del Águila Golf & Resort, Mijas Golf, Chaparral Golf Club, and La Cala Golf Resort. The area also features green spaces, walking and cycling trails, and a wide range of leisure facilities, making it ideal for an active lifestyle.

Another major advantage is the excellent connectivity to the region's key destinations. The beaches of Fuengirola are approximately 2 km away, Fuengirola town centre can be reached in around 10 minutes, Marbella is approximately 30 minutes by car, and Málaga International Airport is just 25–30 minutes from the development. It is an ideal location for those seeking a modern apartment with easy access to everything the Costa del Sol has to offer.
