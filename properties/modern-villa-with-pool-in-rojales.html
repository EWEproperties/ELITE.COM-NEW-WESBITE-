Location

Rojales is a charming town located in the province of Alicante, Spain. With a rich history, picturesque landscapes and a vibrant culture, Rojales offers a captivating blend of traditional Spanish charm and modern amenities.

One of Rojales' highlights is its picturesque location on the Segura River, which adds to the town's natural beauty. The surrounding area is dotted with magnificent citrus groves, offering a refreshing and fragrant backdrop. Rojales enjoys a Mediterranean climate with mild winters and hot summers, making it the perfect place for those looking for a sunny lifestyle.

The heart of Rojales is its historic centre, where winding narrow streets lead to charming squares adorned with traditional Spanish architecture. Explore the town's rich heritage by visiting the ancient church of San Pedro or the intriguing archaeological museum, which displays artefacts from Rojales' past.

Rojales is also famous for its impressive network of cave houses, known as Cuevas del Rodeo. These unique homes are built into the side of a hill, creating a fascinating underground community filled with art galleries, workshops and cultural events. Visitors can immerse themselves in the local art scene, admiring the vivid murals and taking in exhibitions by local artists.

For those looking for outdoor activities, Rojales offers plenty of options. Golf enthusiasts can indulge their passion at the famous La Marquesa golf course, which boasts stunning views and challenging waterways. The nearby Parque Natural de las Lagunas de La Mata y Torrevieja provides nature lovers with the opportunity to discover beautiful wetlands and observe a variety of bird species.

In terms of amenities, Rojales offers a range of services including supermarkets, restaurants serving both traditional and international cuisine, cosy cafés and leisure facilities. The town also hosts various cultural events and festivals throughout the year, providing an opportunity to celebrate local traditions and immerse yourself in the lively atmosphere.

La Marquesa Golf Course

La Marquesa Golf Club is located 58 km from Alicante in Rojales. It is located in the flat valley between the mountains of the Valencian Community and the layout of the round alternates between long and short holes. Players of all levels will enjoy playing at La Marquesa Golf Club. The course is surrounded by streams and lakes, providing wonderful views and a beautiful natural environment for playing at La Marquesa Golf Club. The varied and pleasant terrain is surrounded by pine and palm trees.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja del Camp, about 8 km by road, around 11 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen (about 2.4 km) and Colegio Público Poeta Miguel Hernández (about 4 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud de Benijófar, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Quirón/USP Hospital San Jaime, about 13 km by road, around 17 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is QuickSave, about 1.1 km away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 58 km by road, around 45 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 68 km, around 50 minutes by car.
