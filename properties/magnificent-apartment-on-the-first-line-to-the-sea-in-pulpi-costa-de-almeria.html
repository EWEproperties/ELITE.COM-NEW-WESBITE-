Location

Pulpí, the first coastal city in Andalusia, located on the border between Murcia and Almeria, constitutes one of the most picturesque and paradisiacal landscapes in the geography of Spain and offers many different reasons to visit it. The commune is located in the north-eastern part of the province and has three clearly differentiated areas on its territory: a coastal area, an inland basin and a small chain of mountain ranges separating them.

Pulpí Geode

In addition to its spectacular beaches and coves, it stands out as a true natural gem that has been transformed into a tourism engine since opening to the public in 2019: Geode. Located in Mina Rica, in Pilar de Jaravía, it is a gigantic geode about eight meters long and two meters high. It is the second largest in the world after the Crystal Cave of Chihuahua in Mexico and the largest geode that can be visited.

Other places to visit

There is much more to visit in Pulpí. In the town of Pulpí, the church of San Miguel stands out, the second oldest building in the commune, dating back to 1801, and at the same time the tallest, with a spectacular altar. Also noteworthy are the Benzal Picnic and Ethnographic Area and the Apartadero Viewpoint.

Also worth visiting is the Scenic Space with the "Pintor Pedro Antonio Martínez" room, which houses a collection of paintings by the influential painter Pulpileño, and the "Black Room", the largest of its kind in Europe, which houses a spectacular collection of 1,000 luminescent minerals.

Beaches

Almería also boasts a catalog of spectacular beaches and coves. 13 beaches in Pulpí, running along the coastline from north to south: Los Cocedores beach, Las Palmeras beach, La Carolina beach, Invencible bay, Costa Tranquila bay, Cuartel bay, Tía Antonia bay , Rincón de los Nudos bay, Mar Rabiosa beach, Mar Serena beach, Calypso beach, La Entrevista beach and Los Nardos beach. Four of the five beaches further south are those that have a blue flag due to the quality of services and the water, which on the other hand is crystal clear and all very good. These are Mar Serena, Mar Rabiosa, Calypso and Los Nardos.
