Location

Estepona is conveniently located a short distance from Malaga airport, making it easily accessible for travellers. The airport is about 80 kilometres away, ensuring a smooth and comfortable journey for visitors.

Estepona is a modern town with a good infrastructure and typical Andalusian charm. It is without doubt one of the most attractive spots on the Costa del Sol. The town has a maritime tradition and has become one of the most popular places on the Costa del Sol due to its high quality of life and wide range of leisure activities. The golf clubs here are proud of the name ‘Costa del Golf’, and the spectacular natural landscapes allow for a healthy lifestyle, including numerous outdoor sports.

There is no doubt that Estepona today is a benchmark for quality of life, modern tourist and leisure facilities, city care and provision of public services, thanks to the investment stimulus initiated by the Estepona City Council in 2011 to implement a sustainable city model, the central axis of which is the welfare of citizens and respect for the environment.

The commitment to the Estepona, Garden of the Costa del Sol project, which has yielded such good socio-economic results, has made it possible to renew the town, provide it with a unique tourist attraction and promote the economic recovery and revitalisation of commercial and retail facilities in this municipality of Malaga. The results are clear: Estepona is one of the municipalities that leads the way in job creation in Andalusia and public investment in the province of Malaga.

The town boasts a collection of breathtaking beaches that will delight beach lovers and sun seekers alike. Some of the best beaches in the area are Playa del Cristo, a sheltered cove with calm waters and a relaxed atmosphere, and Playa de la Rada, a wide stretch of golden sand offering a variety of water sports.

The nearby Sierra Bermeja mountain range is perfect for hiking, allowing you to immerse yourself in stunning natural landscapes and admire the breathtaking panoramic views of the coast. Golf enthusiasts will be delighted by the numerous world-class golf courses in the area, where they can play against a backdrop of lush greenery and azure skies.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Guadalobón, about 2.5 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio Diocesano Juan XXIII (about 1.7 km) and CEIP Ramón García (about 6 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Estepona, about 6 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital de Alta Resolución de Estepona, about 2.1 km by road.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Aldi, about 1 km away.

How far is the airport?

Málaga Airport is about 90 km by road, around 65 minutes by car.
