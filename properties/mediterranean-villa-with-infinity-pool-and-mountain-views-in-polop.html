Location

Located in the Marina Baja region of Alicante, about 15 kilometers from the coast, Polop de la Marina is a small town of less than 5,000 inhabitants. A few kilometers away from Benidorm, Altea or Castell de Guadalest, it boasts a beautiful historic center that you will immediately fall in love with.

The old town of Polop, located on a hill and developed at the foot of an Arab castle, is one of the most interesting and best preserved in the region. Its picturesque, colorful streets full of flowers and carved ornaments are an inspiration for many artists.

The most outstanding building in the historical case is the church of San Pedro Apóstol, in the upper part of the city, just before the path leading to the castle.

Although there are few remains of Polop Castle, it is a must to visit its ruins, located on top of the hill on which the old town is located. Undoubtedly, the path leading to the castle itself is one of the most interesting places to visit in Polop. Impeccably maintained, like the rest of the municipality, the successful surface and its zig-zag route offer us impressive views of the city and the surrounding area.

The colorful fountain - Fuente de los Chorros also deserves a lot of attention. It is decorated with ceramic mosaics with the coats of arms of various cities in the province of Alicante and has as many as 221 springs.

When it comes to hiking trails in Polop, there is one itinerary that stands out from the rest: climbing Mount Ponotx, known as 'Sleeping Lion'. A large limestone mass with vertical walls that rises to a height of 1,183 meters. This is a route with a high degree of difficulty, due to the positive difference of over a thousand meters, which is a route reserved for people with very good physical condition and used to this type of mountain routes.

Polop has a wide variety of restaurants. But if you want an unforgettable dining experience, you need to make a reservation at the Ca l'Àngels restaurant. Vicenta and Paco Teuler, together with other family members, run a restaurant where traditional cuisine reigns with local products (even from their own garden). An innovative wine list and artisanal desserts complete the excellent offer.
