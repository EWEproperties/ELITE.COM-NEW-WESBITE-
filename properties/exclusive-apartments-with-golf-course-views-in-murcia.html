Modern apartments for sale in an exceptional setting overlooking the golf course, designed for a comfortable lifestyle in the Mediterranean climate. The development stands out for its distinctive architecture with soft, curved lines, spacious terraces and beautifully designed communal areas with a swimming pool and landscaped gardens.

The development offers apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, available on different floors. Large windows fill the interiors with natural light and seamlessly connect the living areas with the terraces. Selected properties offer panoramic views of the golf course. Ground-floor apartments feature particularly generous outdoor spaces, while the penthouses also offer private solariums of over 90 m².

At the heart of each apartment is an open-plan living area combining the lounge, dining room and kitchen. The kitchen comes furnished and equipped with integrated appliances, including an oven, microwave, refrigerator, dishwasher and induction hob with integrated extractor. The properties also include air conditioning, an aerothermal system and high-quality porcelain flooring.

One of the main highlights of the development is its spacious terraces, designed as a natural extension of the living areas and perfect for enjoying the sunny Mediterranean climate throughout most of the year. The complex also features a large communal swimming pool surrounded by landscaped gardens, green areas and spaces designed for relaxation.

Each apartment includes a parking space and storage room in the price. The total areas of the available properties vary depending on the type and floor, ranging from approximately 99 m² to more than 245 m², including outdoor areas.

Completion is scheduled for the second quarter of 2028.

Location

The development is situated in the exceptional surroundings of El Valle Golf Resort in the Murcia region, where contemporary architecture blends beautifully with the greenery and open spaces of the golf course. It is an ideal location for those looking for sunshine, tranquillity and privacy while still enjoying convenient access to the city and the coast.

One of the greatest advantages of this location is its position directly beside the golf course. Extensive green areas create a peaceful environment for relaxation, while selected apartments enjoy panoramic golf views. The generous terraces make it possible to fully embrace the Mediterranean lifestyle, from morning coffee outdoors to relaxing evenings overlooking the surrounding landscape.

The location is particularly attractive to golf enthusiasts, but it is equally suitable for anyone looking for a second home in Spain away from the more densely developed coastal resorts. The peaceful resort setting offers a sense of space and connection with nature while providing an excellent base from which to enjoy the attractions of the Murcia region and the Costa Cálida.

The combination of a modern residential complex with a swimming pool, spacious terraces, green surroundings and a prime golf course location makes this an attractive option both for personal use and for those looking for a property in Spain within an appealing resort environment.

Frequently asked questions

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Nuestra Señora de las Maravillas, about 11 km, around 10 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio San Vicente Ferrer, about 19 km, around 19 minutes by car.
International school: El Limonar International School - Buenavista, about 14 km, around 15 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Consultorio de Los Martínez del Puerto, about 10 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Clínico Universitario Virgen de la Arrixaca, about 13 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Spar, about 17 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 11 km by road, around 11 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 96 km, around 70 minutes by car.
