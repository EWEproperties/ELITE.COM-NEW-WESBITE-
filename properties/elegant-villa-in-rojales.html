Location

Located on the southern coast of Spain, Costa Blanca, Rojales is a charming town with a mix of traditional Spanish architecture and modern amenities. The area is known for its beautiful mountain views and proximity to the salt lakes of Torrevieja, known for their healing properties. Rojales offers a range of amenities including supermarkets, shops, restaurants and bars, as well as sports facilities such as golf courses and tennis courts. Its proximity to the beaches of Guardamar del Segura and the historic city of Orihuela makes Rojales an attractive location for both holidaymakers and permanent residents.

Located just a 45-minute drive from Alicante and Murcia airports, Rojales offers convenient access for travellers looking for a great holiday. Upon arrival, immerse yourself in the town's history by exploring the charming historic centre, where traditional architecture and vibrant flowers create a captivating atmosphere.

Don't miss the Cuevas del Rodeo, an extraordinary cave complex transformed into an art gallery. Admire the intricate wall paintings and sculptures, and appreciate the city's thriving artistic community. For nature lovers, the nearby Parque Natural de la Mata invites you to discover stunning landscapes, ideal for walks and bird watching.

When it's time to relax, head to the beautiful Blue Flag beaches of Guardamar del Segura, just a 10-minute drive from the property. Dip your toes in the golden sand, soak up the Mediterranean sun and cool off in the crystal-clear water.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the presence of La Marquesa Golf and other prestigious courses nearby, offering a fantastic golfing experience in breathtaking surroundings.

After a day of sightseeing, treat yourself to the culinary delights of Rojales. In the many charming restaurants and cafés, you can savour traditional Spanish tapas, fresh seafood and local specialities, complemented by regional wines.

All in all, Rojales offers a captivating combination of history, natural beauty, great beaches, golfing opportunities and delicious cuisine. Whether you are looking for a short getaway or considering making Rojales your permanent home, this charming town offers a truly unforgettable experience.
