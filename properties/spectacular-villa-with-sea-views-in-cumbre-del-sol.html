We are proud to present this beautiful, modern and very luxurious villa with private pool, terraces, garage, garden and fabulous sea views in Cumbre del Sol. The property is located in a peaceful and quiet area, which guarantees comfortable living all year round. Additionally, not far from the villa there are shops, supermarkets, restaurants, pharmacies, bars and many others. The villa is designed and constructed by the best specialists and the highest quality materials, it will undoubtedly delight the most demanding customers.

All the advantages of this modern luxury villa can be found on a plot of over 1,300 square meters. This is a unique property with 3 bedrooms and 5 bathrooms and parking for up to 4 cars: 2 covered spaces and 2 in a closed garage.

The master bedroom is a real kingdom of rest and relaxation. It is equipped with an elegant, fully equipped bathroom and a large dressing room, and its private veranda allows you to admire the Mediterranean Sea at any time of the day. The remaining two bedrooms have a common terrace, a bathroom in each of them and additional space to locate a recreation room or office.

On the same floor there is a living area, i.e. a perfectly combined living room and kitchen connected via an island, making parties and meetings with friends a pure pleasure. Large glass windows overlook the terrace with an infinity pool and blur the boundary between the interior and exterior of the investment.

The exclusivity of this modern villa is concentrated on the ground floor, intended for recreation for the whole family. You will have a private gym with bathroom and sauna, as well as your own cinema, all on the veranda to rest and relax in the silence of the sea.

Location

Cumbre del Sol is located in the town of Benitachell, between Javea (20 minutes) and Moraira (15 minutes), in the north of the province of Alicante and covers 3.7 million square meters of land. All aspects of its design and construction have been developed to provide maximum comfort and the best services to residents and guests, protecting the natural environment and integrating green areas.

This prestigious residential area stretches over the seaside cliffs with the famous Morach beach and small coves. A paradise of pristine beaches awaits you here, each with its own unique charm. Cala del Moraig tempts with crystal clear waters and dramatic cliffs, a paradise for snorkelers and divers looking to discover underwater wonders. Playa de Los Tiestos, surrounded by rocky cliffs, provides a peaceful escape for those who long for peaceful days by the sea. Discover the hidden oasis of Cala Llebeig at the end of a scenic hike, a sanctuary of peace and untouched beauty.

Cumbre del Sol is perfect for nature lovers - it offers a network of hiking trails that invite you to immerse yourself in the rugged beauty of the region. The Ruta de los Acantilados' (cliff trail) reveals awe-inspiring views of the coast, leaving you in awe of nature's grandeur. Meanwhile, the Ruta del Faro' (Lighthouse Trail) leads to the iconic Cap d'Or lighthouse, a symbol of maritime heritage offering a sense of history and wonder. Water lovers, however, find their happiness among the calm waters of the nearby bays, engaging in kayaking, paddleboarding and sailing, adding an extra layer of charm to this seaside haven.

Cumbre del Sol also boasts a variety of dining options. At Mirador del Mar, Mediterranean cuisine meets modernity, and guests enjoy delicious dishes against the backdrop of mesmerizing sea views. Restaurante La Cumbre captivates with its combination of Spanish and international flavors, which can be enjoyed on the terrace overlooking the sea, which goes beyond an ordinary dinner. El Almendro delights with traditional Spanish dishes and tempting seafood, a real culinary delight for the demanding palate.
