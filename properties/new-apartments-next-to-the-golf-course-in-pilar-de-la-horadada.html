Elite World Estates proudly presents a new boutique residential development located in one of the greenest and most peaceful areas of Costa Blanca — right next to the prestigious Lo Romero Golf course in Pilar de la Horadada. This exceptional project has been designed for those seeking harmony, modern comfort, and an authentic Mediterranean lifestyle close to nature and the sea.

The modern residential complex consists of just 48 elegant bungalows with 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, distributed across intimate low-rise buildings with only 4 apartments per block. Buyers can choose between ground-floor apartments with spacious terraces or top-floor properties featuring a balcony and a large private solarium with beautiful views of the golf course, the Mediterranean Sea, and the Mar Menor lagoon.

The development impresses with its contemporary architecture, bright interiors, and high-quality finishes. Every home has been carefully designed to provide maximum comfort, functionality, and natural light throughout the day.

Stylish kitchens come fully equipped with high-quality furniture and a complete set of appliances, including a ceramic hob, oven, microwave, fridge, dishwasher, and washing machine. The apartments also feature pre-installation for ducted air conditioning, electric security shutters on ground floors, aerothermal water heating systems, and elegant black-finished fittings.

Residents will enjoy beautifully designed communal areas with a large swimming pool, a separate children’s pool, landscaped green areas, the Vista Park playground, and an outdoor fitness area equipped with bio-healthy exercise machines.

The residence is situated in a privileged location surrounded by pine trees and directly facing the renowned Lo Romero Golf course, famous for its spectacular 18-hole layout and iconic “golf island” hole. It is the perfect place both for golf enthusiasts and for anyone dreaming of a peaceful life in a Mediterranean setting.

Within just a few minutes, you can reach the beautiful beaches of Torre de la Horadada, known for their fine white sand and charming promenade filled with restaurants and local tapas bars. Shops, shopping centres, cycling routes, and all essential daily services are also nearby, making this an ideal location for year-round living.

This is an excellent opportunity both as a holiday home, a rental investment, or a permanent residence on the sunny Costa Blanca.

Location

The coastline of Pilar de la Horadada stretches for more than 4 kilometres and offers a wonderfully diverse landscape. Here you will find wide beaches with fine white sand, small rocky coves by the crystal-clear water, and intimate hidden bays with golden sand.

The sea in this area is calm and shallow, making the beaches especially safe for families and children. The transparent waters and excellent visibility also make this coastline a perfect destination for diving and discovering beautiful underwater life.

The beaches of Pilar de la Horadada have been awarded the prestigious Blue Flag of the European Union, guaranteeing high environmental and safety standards.

Inland, nature lovers can discover scenic routes in the nearby Sierra de Escalona area. The famous Río Seco riverbed offers beautiful hiking and cycling trails surrounded by Mediterranean landscapes. One of the routes is approximately 4 km long, while another extends for around 10 km through the Pilar de Campoverde area near Mil Palmeras beach.

Close to Pilar de la Horadada, you will also find the 18-hole Lo Romero Golf course, suitable both for experienced golfers and beginners looking to enjoy this prestigious sport.

Gastronomy also plays an important role in the local lifestyle. Traditional dishes of the region include meat stew with potatoes, meatballs, garlic rabbit, local flour-based specialities, delicious rice dishes, and traditional soups. The area is also famous for its pastries and desserts, such as meringue cakes, millefeuille, and almond sweets known as “cordiales”.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa del Puerto, about 8 km by road, around 13 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Mediterráneo, about 5 km, around 9 minutes by car.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 20 km, around 25 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Campoverde Centro de Salud, about 6 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 15 km by road, around 19 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Hiper Asia, about 4 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 40 km by road, around 30 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 87 km, around 65 minutes by car.
