Location

Gran Alacant is a charming town located a short distance from the bustling city of Alicante in Spain. With stunning beaches, natural beauty and a wide range of activities and attractions, Gran Alacant is the perfect place to invest.

Beaches

One of the attractions of Gran Alacant is the beautiful El Carabassi beach, which stretches over 2 km with golden sand and crystal clear waters. Thanks to its calm waters and gentle waves, it is an ideal place for swimming, sunbathing and water sports.

In addition to the beach, Gran Alacant offers a range of other activities and attractions to keep you entertained. The area is renowned for its natural beauty, with miles of hiking and biking trails winding through the nearby mountains and forests.

For nature lovers, the nearby Clot de Galvany Nature Reserve is a must-see destination. This protected area covers over 350 hectares of natural forests and wetlands, home to a wide variety of flora and fauna, including rare birds and wildlife.

For a more cultural experience, be sure to visit the nearby city of Alicante, where you can explore sights such as Castel Sant'Angelo. Barbara and the Museum of Contemporary Art.

Located a short distance from Alicante-Elche Airport, Gran Alacant is easily accessible from anywhere in the world. Whether you're coming from Europe, North America or Asia, you'll find that getting to Gran Alacant is quick, easy and hassle-free.
