Location

Discover the charm and beauty of Finestrat, a picturesque town located in the heart of the Valencian Community of Spain. With stunning views of the majestic Puig Campana mountain and the sparkling Mediterranean Sea, this town is the perfect destination for anyone seeking a relaxing and memorable experience.

Stroll along the narrow cobbled streets of the town, admiring the colorful painted houses that exude a typical Moorish feel. Explore the charming seaside resort of Cala Finestrat, just three kilometers from bustling Benidorm, and enjoy its clean, golden sandy beach, ideal for swimming and perfect for families.

Indulge in the many exciting attractions that surround the town, from the theme parks of Terre Mitica and Terra Natura to the Aqualandia water park and the Mundomar marine and exotic animal park. For golf enthusiasts, the nearby Real Faula Golf Club offers two excellent golf courses that are sure to impress.

Enjoy the local bars and restaurants, which offer a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere, perfect for savoring good food and creating unforgettable memories. With easy access to Benidorm by bus or taxi, everything you need is within reach, ensuring that your stay in Finestrat will be a truly unforgettable experience.

Whether you're a family seeking a peaceful beach resort or a couple looking for a relaxing getaway, Finestrat has something for everyone. Experience the beauty and charm of this stunning town for yourself and discover why it's one of the most sought-after destinations in Spain.
