Location

Algorfa, located in the Vega Baja del Segura region, has a rich history dating back to Roman times. The name of the city comes from the Arabic word 'Al-Gorfa', meaning 'cave', as the area was known for its numerous natural caves. Today, Algorfa is known for its production of citrus fruits and vegetables.

Algorfa is home to many attractions that will appeal to visitors of all ages. Here are just a few of the most important ones and:

La Finca Golf Course

If you're a golf fan, La Finca Golf Course is a must-visit place. This championship golf course is set in a beautiful natural setting with stunning views of the Vega Baja countryside. The course has been designed to challenge golfers of all levels and has many amenities including a professional shop and restaurant.

Algorfa Market

For a taste of the local culture, be sure to visit the Algorfa market, which takes place every Wednesday. Here you will find a wide selection of goods, including fresh, local produce, clothing and souvenirs. It is the perfect place to buy a unique gift or try the delicious local cuisine.

Plaza de Espana

Plaza de España is Algorfa's main square and is a great place to soak up the local atmosphere. Here you will find a beautiful fountain, plenty of seating and plenty of restaurants and bars. It's the perfect place to relax with a drink and enjoy the local culture.
