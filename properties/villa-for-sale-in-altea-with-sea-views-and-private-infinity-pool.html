Modern villa for sale in Sierra de Altea, one of the most prestigious residential areas of the northern Costa Blanca. Set between the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding mountains, the property combines contemporary architecture, spacious interiors and beautiful views in a peaceful natural setting.

The villa has been designed in a modern minimalist style, with clean architectural lines, natural materials and large floor-to-ceiling windows that fill the interiors with natural light. The open spaces create a seamless connection between the indoor and outdoor living areas while taking full advantage of the surrounding mountain and Mediterranean Sea views.

The property is distributed across three levels connected by both stairs and a private elevator.

The entrance is located on the upper floor, where there is a welcoming hall with direct access to a private garage for two cars.

The middle floor is dedicated to the sleeping area and offers three bedrooms. The master bedroom features a private bathroom, walk-in wardrobe and access to its own terrace. The two additional bedrooms also have en-suite bathrooms, spacious fitted wardrobes and access to a shared terrace.

The main living area is located on the ground floor. Large windows open the interior towards the terraces and outdoor areas, creating a bright and comfortable space for everyday living. The elegant open-plan kitchen connects naturally with the dining area and living room.

The kitchen is fully equipped with high-quality appliances, generous storage space and durable granite or quartz worktops. A large central island provides additional preparation space and creates a practical area for informal dining.

This level also includes a laundry room, an interior guest toilet, an additional exterior toilet and access to the elevator connecting all floors of the villa.

Outdoor living is one of the highlights of the property. The spacious landscaped plot has been designed to make the most of the Mediterranean climate and impressive surroundings. It includes a private infinity swimming pool, a finished garden with automatic irrigation, a relaxing seating area with pergola, space for sun loungers and a barbecue area.

Location – Sierra de Altea

The villa is located in Sierra de Altea, a highly regarded residential area above the coastal town of Altea. Its elevated position offers beautiful surroundings, mountain scenery and views towards the Mediterranean Sea while remaining within easy reach of the coast and local services.

Altea is considered one of the most picturesque towns on the Costa Blanca, known for its whitewashed houses, cobbled streets, art galleries, restaurants and Mediterranean atmosphere. Its historic old town is centred around the iconic church of Nuestra Señora del Consuelo, recognised by its characteristic blue dome and panoramic views across Altea Bay.

The town is surrounded by the Sierra de Bernia and Sierra Aitana mountains, creating a distinctive green landscape and a mild Mediterranean climate. Beaches, promenades, shops, restaurants and leisure facilities are all easily accessible.

Altea is located approximately 10 km north of Benidorm and around one hour from Alicante, making it an attractive location for both permanent residence and holidays on the Costa Blanca.
