Location

Located on the beautiful Costa Blanca in Spain, Torrevieja is a popular destination for both tourists and expats. The city has a lot to offer, from beautiful beaches and nature parks to lively nightclubs and cultural attractions. One of the most sought-after areas in Torrevieja is Los Balcones, a quiet and prestigious suburb that offers an exclusive lifestyle and easy access to some of the best amenities in the region.

Los Balcones is renowned for its beautiful villas and luxury apartments, as well as its quiet and peaceful atmosphere. The area is located a short distance from the centre of Torrevieja, making it the perfect place for those who want to enjoy the best of both worlds - the tranquillity of suburban life and the hustle and bustle of the city. The area is surrounded by beautiful nature parks, including the famous Torrevieja salt lakes, which are a protected nature reserve and wildlife sanctuary.

Torrevieja's beaches are among the most beautiful in the region. The area boasts some of the best beaches in the city, including Playa de los Locos, Playa del Cura and Playa de la Mata. These beaches are renowned for their crystal clear waters, fine sand and excellent facilities, making them popular with locals and tourists alike.

There are several golf courses in the area, including the prestigious Villamartin Golf Club, which is a short drive from the hotel. The nearby nature parks offer excellent walking and cycling trails, as well as opportunities for bird watching and nature photography.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala de lo Ferri, about 5 km by road, around 10 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio Público Romualdo Ballester, about 2.4 km.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 23 km, around 19 minutes by car.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 9 km, around 10 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Patricio Pérez, about 6 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 1.9 km by road.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, about 100 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 54 km by road, around 40 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 69 km, around 50 minutes by car.
