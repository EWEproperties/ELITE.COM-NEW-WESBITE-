A new development located directly beside the sandy beach in Vera Playa, on the Costa de Almería. The complex has been designed as a modern seaside resort, combining the comfort of a private property with an extensive range of leisure facilities available throughout the year.

The development comprises 156 apartments with 1 or 2 bedrooms and 44 independent 2-bedroom villas with private swimming pools. The entire complex is surrounded by beautifully landscaped gardens and recreational areas, while its beachfront location allows residents to fully enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle.

The apartments have been designed to maximise natural light and create a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living spaces. They feature spacious terraces, while selected ground-floor properties also offer a private garden and the option to install a private jacuzzi. Apartments on the top floor additionally benefit from a private rooftop solarium with a barbecue area.

Alternatively, the resort offers detached 2-bedroom villas, each with its own private plot, spacious terrace and private swimming pool, providing greater independence and generous private outdoor space.

The properties are offered fully furnished in a contemporary style, with a fully fitted kitchen including appliances, bed linen and kitchenware. The specifications also include central air conditioning, an aerothermal system for hot water production, large-format porcelain flooring, PVC windows with double glazing providing thermal and acoustic insulation, and built-in wardrobes.

Seaside resort with Beach Club

One of the development's greatest advantages is its extensive range of communal facilities. The private Beach Club overlooking the sea features two heated saltwater swimming pools, sunbathing areas and a premium clubhouse with a dining area. The resort also provides direct access to a designated private beach area.

Residents and guests will also have access to a gym, sauna, restaurant, Social Club, business centre, Kids Club and gaming area. One of the resort's communal swimming pools will be heated, allowing it to be enjoyed throughout the year.

Estimated completion: November 2028.

Excellent location in Vera Playa

The development is located in Vera Playa, in the province of Almería, directly on the coast and within easy reach of nearby towns and local amenities. Vera Water Park is approximately 1 km from the complex, Puerto Rey Yacht Club is around 5 minutes by car, Vera Plaza shopping centre is less than 8 minutes away, and the fishing port and marina of Garrucha are approximately 8 km away. Almería Airport can be reached in around 45 minutes.

The combination of a beachfront location, extensive resort facilities, contemporary architecture and fully equipped properties makes this an exceptional option for anyone looking for their own home by the sea in Spain.
