Location

This colorful town is located between the popular cities of Benidorm and El Campello, approximately a 30-minute drive from Alicante Airport.

Villajoyosa seems to literally hang from the rocky cliffs. Combined with its charming architecture, this creates an incredible impression of a picturesque coastline. Moreover, the town's name itself promises a pleasant time. Villajoyosa, or La Vila Joiosa, literally translates to "the cheerful town." Indeed, it certainly feels that way, with its colorful houses in the town center.

Urban legend has it that the buildings were painted in vibrant colors to help fishermen find their way home. Today, they undoubtedly attract crowds of tourists and inspire many photographers and artists.

In recent years, this charming town has become a desirable destination for those seeking a quiet second home. Villajoyosa has much to offer, from the wide sandy beach to the narrow, colorful streets of the historic center.

Villajoyosa has been associated with chocolate since the 19th century. It is here that the popular Spanish chocolate factory Valor is located. The Valor, Pérez, and Clavileño museums are must-sees. There, you'll learn everything about the chocolate-making process and its history in the area. Tours led by knowledgeable guides often include chocolate tastings.

During the summer season, Villajoyosa hosts a variety of cultural events. It's worth visiting in July, when the famous Moors and Christians Festival takes place. The main attraction is a reenactment of the 1538 battle, as well as concerts, plays, and shows.

Villajoyosa boasts 3 km of beaches with golden sands and views of the vast, clear sea. The most beautiful of these are the beaches of El Paraíso, Xarco, and la Caleta. Here, you'll find both larger tourist resorts and hidden, secret corners perfect for quiet relaxation. The seaside panorama, Mediterranean climate, and soft, warm sand create the perfect atmosphere for complete relaxation. Near the beaches, you'll find regional restaurants and cafés offering fresh, Mediterranean delicacies.
