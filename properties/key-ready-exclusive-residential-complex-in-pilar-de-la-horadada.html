Location

Pilar de la Horadada is a charming town located in the southern part of the Alicante province, in the Valencian Community of Spain. This town is renowned for its picturesque landscapes, fascinating cultural heritage, and crystal-clear waters, making it a perfect destination for those who seek relaxation and tranquility.

One of the main attractions in Pilar de la Horadada is its stunning beaches. Playa de las Higuericas is a popular local beach that stretches for over 2.5 kilometers and features golden sand, crystal-clear waters, and plenty of amenities, such as beach bars, restaurants, and showers. This beach is perfect for families with children, as it has a playground and is supervised by lifeguards.

In addition to the beaches, Pilar de la Horadada has plenty of cultural and historical landmarks worth visiting. The Casa de la Cultura is a cultural center that hosts a variety of exhibitions, workshops, and events throughout the year. The Iglesia de Nuestra Señora del Pilar is a stunning church that dates back to the 18th century and features a magnificent Baroque style.

For those who love the outdoors, there are plenty of options for hiking and cycling in Pilar de la Horadada. The "Del Pilar al Mar" route is a popular promenade that stretches for 3 kilometers and is perfect for a leisurely stroll or bike ride. There are also several nature reserves nearby, such as the Salinas de San Pedro del Pinatar Regional Park, which is home to a variety of bird species.

When it comes to food and drink, Pilar de la Horadada has a lot to offer. This town is known for its delicious seafood dishes, such as paella and arroz a banda. There are plenty of local bars and restaurants where you can enjoy these dishes, as well as a variety of other Spanish specialties.
