Location

Torre de la Horadada is located in the center of Alicante. The resort is located in southeastern Spain and on the border with Murcia (about 15 minutes away). Otherwise, Alicante is about a 40-minute drive away.

Pilar de la Horadada has everything you need to enjoy the charms of the sea, pine forests, and the Spanish sun.

The coastline of Pillar de la Horadada stretches over 4 kilometers and offers a wide, diverse landscape. You'll find wide beaches with fine white sand, a small, stone-walled canal above the water, and small, intimate, hidden coves with golden sand.

The sea is generally calm here, and the beaches are safe, with no steep slopes, allowing for safe access and swimming. Furthermore, the clear water and bright luminosity allow divers to see the beautiful aquatic life hidden within.

The cleanliness of the beaches at Pillar de la Horadada is recognized by the European Union's Blue Flag, guaranteeing a safe environment.

Inland, you can find various interesting hikes near the towns of Sierra and Escalona. The first two hiking trails, along the Rio Seco riverbed, are 19 kilometers long and well worth a visit. One is 4 kilometers long, while the other, 10 kilometers long, runs through the Pilar de Campoverde area, next to Mil Palmeras beach.

Near Pilar de la Horadada, there's also an 18-hole golf course in Lo Romero. Both professionals and beginners will enjoy the course.

Gastronomy has a privileged place in this beautiful town. Typical dishes at Pillar of the Horde include potatoes with meat stew, meatballs, rabbit in garlic, and oatmeal (a floury, garlic pancake), as well as excellent rice dishes and soups. Sweets and products used here, such as meringue cake, "millefeuille," and almond sweets called "cordiales," are the most popular.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de las Villas, about 3 km by road, around 6 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP María Moliner (about 300 m) and CEIP Martín Artigot (about 600 m).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is the local public health centre (Centro de Salud), about 500 m away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 12 km by road, around 12 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, about 700 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 39 km by road, around 30 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 82 km, around 60 minutes by car.
