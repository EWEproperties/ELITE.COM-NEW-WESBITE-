For sale, an exceptional 5-bedroom villa set in the hills above Marbella, within a prestigious gated residential community surrounded by Mediterranean nature. The residence combines classic, elegant architecture with contemporary comfort, creating a timeless home with beautiful views of the natural surroundings and the Mediterranean Sea.

The villa stands out for its spacious, bright interiors and harmonious layout. At the heart of the home is an elegant living room connected to the dining area and kitchen. Large windows provide abundant natural light and open the interiors towards the terraces and surrounding greenery.

The residence offers 5 spacious bedrooms, providing comfortable accommodation for both residents and guests. The master bedroom creates a private retreat, while the remaining rooms offer flexible space for family, guests or a home office.

Generous terraces form a natural extension of the interiors and provide several areas for relaxing and dining outdoors. The private garden and saltwater infinity pool*make it possible to fully enjoy the Costa del Sol climate, while the elevated position provides open views and a high level of privacy.

Additional space on the lower level can be adapted to the owner's individual lifestyle, with possibilities including a private gym, cinema room, entertainment area or wine cellar. A private lift connects the different levels of the villa.

OWNER'S CLUB AND EXCLUSIVE AMENITIES

Villa owners have access to the private Owner's Club, the wellness and leisure centre of the community. Residents can enjoy an indoor swimming pool, spa, sauna, hammam and modern gym with panoramic sea views.

The club also features a restaurant and bar, while the wider community facilities include padel courts, walking and running trails, fitness areas, a short-game golf practice area and landscaped gardens.

The experience is complemented by 24-hour security, controlled access and concierge services, providing additional comfort during every stay and assistance with transport, restaurant reservations, wellness services and property-related requirements.

LOCATION

The setting in the green hills above Marbella provides an excellent balance between tranquillity and privacy while remaining conveniently close to the coast. Marbella town centre, the Old Town, Golden Mile, Puerto Banús and the Mediterranean beaches*are all within easy reach.

The surrounding area also offers renowned golf courses, restaurants, international schools and all the services required for comfortable year-round living.

This elegant 5-bedroom villa for sale in Marbella is an outstanding choice for those looking for timeless architecture, generous private living spaces, sea views and the comfort of an exclusive resort lifestyle on the Costa del Sol.
