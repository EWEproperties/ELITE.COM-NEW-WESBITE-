For sale: a modern Model B villa, designed in an elegant Mediterranean style, featuring large windows and a living area that opens directly onto the private garden. The single-storey layout and carefully designed interiors provide comfort and convenience, making the property ideal both for holidays and year-round living.

The villa offers 171.93 m² of built area, with 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and an additional guest toilet. There is also a 31.23 m² basement, providing additional storage space or the possibility of adapting it to suit the owners’ needs.

At the heart of the home is a bright living room connected to the dining area and modern kitchen. Large floor-to-ceiling windows open onto the terrace, creating a seamless transition between the interior, garden and pool area.

One of the main advantages of Model B is its generous outdoor space. The available villas are situated on plots ranging from approximately 644 m² to 850 m², while the terraces offer between approximately 88 m² and 139 m², depending on the selected property. Each villa features a private swimming pool and spacious garden, creating an intimate outdoor area for relaxing and enjoying the Mediterranean climate.

The property comes with a fully equipped kitchen with appliances, premium porcelain flooring, ducted air conditioning with both cooling and heating, and underfloor heating in the bathrooms. The villa is also equipped with a comprehensive KNX smart home system.

Outside, there is also a BBQ area, while the swimming pool features a salt chlorination water treatment system. The property includes pre-installation for an electric vehicle charging point, a heat pump for the swimming pool and solar panels.

The estimated completion of the available villas is scheduled for the third quarter of 2027.

Location

Las Colinas Golf in Orihuela Costa is a renowned destination attracting golf enthusiasts and holidaymakers from around the world. Located on the south-eastern coast of Spain, this exceptional area combines a prestigious golf environment with easy access to beaches, leisure activities and numerous attractions.

Golf at Las Colinas

Golf enthusiasts will particularly appreciate the 18-hole championship golf course, designed by Cabell B. Robinson. The course offers beautifully maintained fairways, challenging bunkers and impressive views of the surrounding landscape, providing an enjoyable golfing experience in a peaceful natural setting.

Beaches near Las Colinas Golf

Some of the most popular beaches in the surrounding area include Playa Flamenca, La Zenia and Cabo Roig. The coastline is known for its clear Mediterranean waters, golden sandy beaches and excellent conditions for enjoying the Costa Blanca climate.

Activities around Las Colinas Golf

In addition to golf and the nearby beaches, the area offers plenty of opportunities for outdoor activities. Scenic routes make it ideal for hiking and cycling, while the coast provides access to water sports such as jet skiing, snorkelling and paddleboarding. Alicante, Murcia and Cartagena are also within reach for those wishing to discover the culture, history and gastronomy of south-eastern Spain.
