Location

La Nucía is one of the most desirable residential towns on the northern Costa Blanca, located just minutes from Benidorm, Altea, and the Mediterranean coastline. It is renowned for its outstanding quality of life, peaceful atmosphere, and excellent infrastructure, making it a popular destination for both Spanish residents and international buyers looking to relocate.

The town offers everything needed for comfortable everyday living, including supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, schools, medical centres, banks, and a wide range of local services. Thanks to its strategic location, residents can easily reach the sandy beaches of Benidorm, the charming old town of Altea, and the popular La Marina shopping centre within just a few minutes.

One of La Nucía's greatest highlights is the Ciudad Deportiva Camilo Cano, one of Europe's most prestigious sports complexes. Covering more than 400,000 m², it features football stadiums, tennis and padel courts, sports halls, swimming pools, gyms, running tracks, and the internationally recognised David Ferrer Tennis Academy. The complex regularly hosts international sporting events and professional training camps, further enhancing the town's reputation.

Nature lovers and outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the numerous hiking and cycling routes leading through the Sierra de Aitana mountains and the Serra Gelada Natural Park. With its Mediterranean climate and more than 300 sunny days each year, La Nucía offers an exceptional quality of life throughout the year.

The town also benefits from excellent transport connections, with quick access to the AP-7 motorway and the N-332 coastal road. Alicante International Airport is approximately a 45-minute drive away, providing convenient connections with major European cities.

La Nucía is the perfect destination for those looking to purchase a modern property on the Costa Blanca while enjoying tranquillity, safety, proximity to the sea, and an exceptional lifestyle. It is an ideal location both for permanent living and as a second home or a high-potential real estate investment in Spain.
