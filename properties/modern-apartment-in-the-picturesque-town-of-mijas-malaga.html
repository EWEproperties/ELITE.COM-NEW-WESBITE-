Location

Mijas is one of the largest towns in the province of Malaga, consisting of 3 different districts: Mijas Pueblo, Mijas Costa and Mijas la Cala. The most popular, attracting numerous tourists and fans of Andalusian white villages, is Mijas Pueblo.

It is a picturesque town located several kilometers from the coast, from the well-known town of Fuengirola. The nearest metropolis is Malaga, about 35 km away. The distance to Marbella is similar. Mijas is approximately half an hour's drive from Malaga Airport.

White houses located several kilometers from the coast, from a distance look as if "glued" to the mountain slope. The town tries to preserve the atmosphere of a traditional, white, Andalusian village.

Mijas la Cala offers many interesting pubs and restaurants where you can refresh yourself with a cool sangria or snack on tapas or eat typical Andalusian dishes such as gazpacho or dishes with seafood. However, La Cale de Mijas does not live only with Andalusian cuisine. You will also find here Greek and Chinese restaurants, as well as one Thai and several Indian restaurants.

One of the most popular places in La Cala de Mijas is Legends Bar La Cala. You can not only drink good drinks and eat something nice here, but above all you can spend a really fantastic evening here to the sounds of live music. It is an intimate place where thematic events are often held. The place has good reviews among tourists and it is worth coming here at least for a moment.

Mijas Pueblo is a multicultural town whose inhabitants are largely foreigners. The Andalusian town is also called the "kingdom of golf". Mijas Pueblo is famous primarily for its unusual taxis, whose functions are performed by donkeys. The idea was born in the 1960s, when tourists started accosting residents returning from work in the quarries, whose transport was donkeys. Their popularity was used to explore the city, so to this day it is one of the forms of "riding" and seeing the city.

In Mijas, it is worth visiting several observation decks, which offer a beautiful view of the coast and the town itself. One of the most famous is located in Avenida del Compas, right next to the sanctuary of the patron saint of Mijas. The second point with a panorama can be found in the Gardens on the Walls (Jardin de las Murallas).

Another unusual attraction of the town is the smallest chocolate factory in the world, Mayan Monkey Mijas Chocolate Factory at Plaza Virgen de la Peña 15. In addition to the production of chocolate from natural, local products, it is also famous for its workshops where you can produce your own chocolate.
