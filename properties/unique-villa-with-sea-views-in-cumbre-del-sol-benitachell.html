Make your dream come true and live in this modern, timeless villa with an optimal, perfectly designed layout, with the highest utility values and enjoy the Mediterranean climate offered by the Costa Blanca coast.

Location

Cumbre del Sol is located in the town of Benitachell, between Javea (20 minutes) and Moraira (15 minutes), in the north of the province of Alicante. This luxurious district stretches over the coastal cliffs with the famous Morach beach and small coves. Rising majestically atop cliffs, this idyllic residential area reveals breathtaking landscapes and a charming, laid-back charm that captivates all who venture here.

A paradise of pristine beaches awaits you, each exuding its own unique charm. Cala del Moraig tempts with crystal clear waters and dramatic cliffs, a paradise for snorkelers and divers who want to discover underwater wonders. Surrounded by rocky cliffs, Playa de Los Tiestos provides a peaceful escape for those longing for quiet days by the sea. Discover the hidden oasis of Cala Llebeig at the end of a scenic hike, a sanctuary of tranquility and untouched beauty.

For nature lovers, Cumbre del Sol offers a network of hiking trails that invite you to immerse yourself in the rugged beauty of the region. The ruta de los Acantilados' (cliff trail) reveals awe-inspiring views of the coast, leaving you in awe of the greatness of nature. Meanwhile, the Ruta del Faro' (Lighthouse Trail) leads to the iconic Cap d'Or Lighthouse, a symbol of maritime heritage offering a sense of history and wonder.

Water lovers find their happiness amidst the calm waters of the nearby bays, indulging in kayaking, paddleboarding and sailing, adding an extra layer of charm to this seaside haven.

For a distinctive perspective of the area, the Cumbre del Sol Equestrian Center offers enchanting horse riding trails, a delightful combination of nature and adventure that reveals the heart of this Mediterranean paradise.

Cumbre del Sol boasts a culinary scene as delightful as its surroundings, tempting guests with a variety of dining options. At Mirador del Mar, Mediterranean cuisine meets modernity, and guests enjoy delicious dishes against the backdrop of mesmerizing sea views. Restaurante La Cumbre captivates with a combination of Spanish and international flavors that can be enjoyed on the terrace overlooking the sea, which goes beyond a simple dinner. El Almendro delights with traditional Spanish dishes and tempting seafood, a real culinary delight for the discerning palate.
