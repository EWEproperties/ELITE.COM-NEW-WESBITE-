For sale: a new, spacious villa located in a peaceful and green area of San Miguel de Salinas on the Costa Blanca. The property offers 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, an additional guest toilet, a private swimming pool, solarium and private parking, providing a comfortable space both for year-round living and holiday stays.

The villa offers approximately 194 m² of built area and is situated on a large private plot of around 710 m². Contemporary architecture and an open-plan interior layout have been designed to create a sense of space and provide plenty of natural light.

The heart of the home is a spacious living area comprising a lounge, dining area and open-plan kitchen. The space has been designed with a comfortable Mediterranean lifestyle in mind, creating a natural connection between the interior, terrace, garden and private swimming pool.

The villa offers 3 comfortable bedrooms, each with access to its own bathroom, while an additional guest toilet enhances the functionality of the property. Residents also have access to a laundry room, terrace and private solarium, ideal for creating a relaxing outdoor area and enjoying the sunny Costa Blanca climate. The property also includes pre-installation for air conditioning and a security entrance door.

The large plot of approximately 710 m² provides plenty of space for outdoor living. The private swimming pool, garden and terraces create a comfortable setting for relaxing, spending time with family and friends or enjoying meals outdoors.

Estimated completion time: approximately 20 months from purchase.

Location

The property is located in one of the quieter and greener areas of San Miguel de Salinas, surrounded by Mediterranean nature and golf courses. The location offers privacy and tranquillity while remaining within easy reach of the full range of services available on the southern Costa Blanca.

The sandy beaches of Orihuela Costa are approximately 7 km from the property, and several popular golf courses can be found nearby. La Zenia Boulevard, restaurants, supermarkets, shops and the coastal towns of Orihuela Costa can also be reached within a short drive.

The location is particularly attractive for those looking for a modern villa in a peaceful setting, with a large private plot and convenient access to beaches, golf courses and everyday services.
