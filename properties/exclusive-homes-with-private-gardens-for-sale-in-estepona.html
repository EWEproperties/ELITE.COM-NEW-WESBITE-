For sale are new exclusive homes located in a prestigious area of Estepona on the Costa del Sol, in the peaceful residential neighbourhood of El Campanario, part of the popular New Golden Mile. This attractive location between Estepona and Marbella is surrounded by renowned golf courses and offers convenient access to beaches, restaurants, shops and all essential services.

The boutique complex offers spacious homes with 4 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms, designed in a contemporary style with an emphasis on comfort, natural light and a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living spaces. Contemporary architecture, large windows and carefully planned layouts create elegant interiors suitable for both year-round living and holidays on the Costa del Sol.

The heart of each home is a bright and spacious living area combining the lounge, dining room and modern kitchen. Large glass doors provide direct access to the terrace and private garden, creating a natural extension of the interior and allowing residents to enjoy the Mediterranean climate throughout most of the year.

The upper levels accommodate comfortable bedrooms and additional outdoor areas. Each property also features a private rooftop solarium, providing extra space for relaxing, sunbathing or spending time with family and friends.

Another advantage is the spacious basement level, which can be adapted to suit the individual needs of the owners. It can be transformed into a private gym, cinema room, home office, games room, additional leisure area or guest accommodation. Each home also includes underground parking for two cars.

The complex offers extensive communal areas designed to provide the atmosphere of a private resort. Residents can enjoy an infinity pool surrounded by greenery, landscaped gardens, a modern gym and spa area. The low-density development and carefully designed surroundings provide privacy and comfort while offering the amenities expected from a high-quality residential complex on the Costa del Sol.

Location

The properties are located in El Campanario, Estepona, one of the established residential areas of the New Golden Mile. The location is particularly attractive for golf enthusiasts, with El Campanario Golf, El Paraíso Golf and Guadalmina Golf nearby, as well as numerous other renowned golf courses throughout the region.

Restaurants, shops, supermarkets, sports clubs and everyday services are available in the surrounding area. The beaches of Guadalmina, El Saladillo and Guadalmansa are approximately 10 minutes away by car, making it easy to combine peaceful living surrounded by greenery with days by the Mediterranean Sea.

The location also provides convenient access to Estepona, San Pedro de Alcántara, Puerto Banús and Marbella, offering a wide selection of restaurants, beach clubs, shops, marinas and leisure facilities along this popular stretch of the Costa del Sol.

These properties are an attractive option for anyone looking for a new home for sale in Estepona combining a private garden, spacious terraces, rooftop solarium, excellent communal facilities and a location close to the sea and golf courses. The property is suitable as a permanent residence in Spain, a second home on the Costa del Sol or a holiday home close to Marbella.
