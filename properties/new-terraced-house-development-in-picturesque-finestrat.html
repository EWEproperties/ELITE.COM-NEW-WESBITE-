Location

Finestrat is a charming and picturesque town located in the province of Alicante, in the Valencian Community of Spain. It is only 8 kilometres from the popular large city of Benidorm and 40 kilometres from Alicante and its international airport. Known for its beautiful mountain views, pristine beaches and rich cultural heritage, Finestrat is the perfect destination for anyone looking for a relaxing and unforgettable experience. There is no shortage of exciting things to do in Finestrat.

Finestrat is located on the slopes of Puig Campana, offering magnificent views of the mountains, the coast and the Mediterranean Sea. The town has a typical Moorish atmosphere, with colourfully painted houses and narrow cobbled streets. On the other hand, Cala Finestrat is becoming increasingly popular, and its development has made it almost an extension of Benidorm itself.

Cala Finestrat

Cala Finestrat is a seaside resort located just three kilometres from bustling Benidorm. The resort offers adequate facilities, a large selection of restaurants, bars and shops. Everything you need is available in Benidorm, which is easily accessible by bus and taxi.

La Cala beach is quite quiet, as most people looking for a more lively atmosphere head to the beaches surrounding Benidorm. The beach is a clean stretch of golden sand, ideal for children and safe for swimming. There are several charming restaurants along the promenade, and in the summer months there is an arts and crafts market here in the afternoon.

The location is suitable for families and couples looking for a relaxing beach resort, yet close to many attractions and amenities.

Nearby are the Terre Mitica and Terra Natura theme parks, the Aqualandia water park, and the Mundomar marine and exotic animal park, which offer fun for the whole family.

If you prefer to spend your holiday playing a round or two of golf, Finestrat will be pleased with the nearby Real Faula Golf Club, which offers two excellent golf courses.

Both Finestrat and Cala Finestrat are quiet destinations compared to some of the neighbouring resorts. Both offer a selection of local bars and restaurants where you can enjoy good food and a relaxed atmosphere.
