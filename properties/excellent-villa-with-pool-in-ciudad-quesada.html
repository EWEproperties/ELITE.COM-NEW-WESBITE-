Located in the heart of Ciudad Quesada, this elegant, newly built villa strikes the perfect balance between modern design, high-quality construction and Mediterranean lifestyle. Designed with comfort and functionality in mind, this two-storey property is ideal for those seeking a sunny, peaceful environment with all amenities within easy reach. With its contemporary architecture, large windows and open spaces, the house combines light, spaciousness and aesthetics to create a cosy and sophisticated place for permanent residence, a second home or investment.

The villa is situated on a large plot, which provides privacy, space and plenty of outdoor areas. At the front, a private swimming pool invites you to cool off and relax on sunny days, surrounded by terraces ideal for sunbathing, reading or outdoor dining. Access to the property is provided by a driveway with sufficient space to comfortably park several cars. In addition, there is the possibility of further customising the property to suit individual needs by adding an additional basement, ideal for those who want extra space, e.g. for recreation, a gym, an office or storage.

The interior of the house is characterised by natural lighting and a layout designed with comfort in mind. The main floor features a spacious open-plan living room with a dining area, connected to a modern and functional kitchen. Large windows not only flood every corner with light, but also visually connect the interior with the outside environment. The house has three spacious and well-planned bedrooms, ideal for both families and guests. Two contemporary-style bathrooms, equipped with high-quality materials, complete the relaxation area, offering comfort and design in every detail.

Location

Ciudad Quesada is located on the southern Costa Blanca, just 8 km north of the main town, Torrevieja. Nearby towns include Rojales and San Fulgencio. You can reach Ciudad Quesada directly from the nearby airport, where you will find plenty of connections. The city is only a 30-minute drive from Alicante Airport, which is well connected to major cities in Europe. Come and discover the best of the southern Costa Blanca in this vibrant city.

Beaches

The city is a short distance from golden sandy beaches, including El Moncayo en Guerdanar del Segura, and is surrounded by magnificent nature parks and picturesque landscapes.

Laguna La Mata

Nature lovers and those seeking peace and quiet enjoy nothing more than grabbing their rucksack, putting on their trainers or comfortable shoes and setting off. In Ciudad Quesada, you can go on a walking tour of some amazing places. There are many hiking trails, but one of the most appealing for nature lovers and birdwatchers is a tour of the La Mata lagoon.

Golf

Golf enthusiasts can enjoy the prestigious La Marquesa Golf Club, which offers breathtaking views of the surrounding area. Meanwhile, nature lovers can visit the nearby salt lakes of Torrevieja, which are home to many species of birds. For those who want to enjoy water sports.

History

The city has a rich history dating back to the early 18th century and has become a vibrant community with a unique blend of Spanish, British and other European cultures. In Ciudad Quesada, you can experience the city's rural past in its charming old town, while the modern and cosmopolitan area around the city centre reflects its more recent development.

Beauty and relaxation ritual

Another unmissable plan in Ciudad Quesada is to visit a spa and let someone take care of your well-being for a morning, afternoon or whole day. At the Hotel la Laguna spa, you can enjoy the so-called “Circuit of the Senses”, which gives you access to a wellness area full of contrasts of heat and cold, a relaxation area and a saltwater jacuzzi, where you will work on all your senses through different areas: sight, smell, taste, touch and hearing. To release tension, you can enjoy a bamboo or chocolate massage or take advantage of anti-stress therapy. You will leave here more relaxed than ever before. The spa at the Hotel La Laguna has an important advantage: you do not have to be a hotel guest to enjoy its facilities.
