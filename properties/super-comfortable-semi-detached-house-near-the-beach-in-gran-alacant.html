Your new super comfortable home is located just 5 minutes from the amazing sandy beach of El Carabbassi. You are only 10 minutes from the nearby international airport and 15 minutes from the picturesque center of Alicante. Pine forests, beautiful landscapes and a healthy Mediterranean climate make it an ideal place to buy real estate for tourist purposes and for everyday life.

This newly built single-family house is designed in a Mediterranean style and blends perfectly with the surrounding natural environment. It is built in blocks of semi-detached bungalows, with parking on a paved surface of stamped concrete.

The great advantage of the property is the fact that it has three floors and a private solarium on the roof of the building. All rooms have been designed in a very thoughtful and functional way with attention to the smallest detail. the investment is very spacious, which allows comfortable living and receiving guests without losing privacy.

On the ground floor we will find a very bright and spacious living area. A fully equipped, semi-open kitchen connects with a dining room and a comfortable living room, creating a perfectly synchronized whole. In addition, on the same floor there is an elegant and fully equipped bathroom. The use of natural materials and colors plays an important role in this project, making the interiors, despite their modernity, very cozy and blend in perfectly with the natural surroundings.

The first floor was designed as a private night area available only to residents. You will find two comfortable bedrooms with built-in wardrobes and one fully equipped bathroom. The master bedroom has a private balcony with direct access to the sun terrace.

The private solarium is the perfect place to enjoy the sunny weather that the area offers most of the year. There will be enough space for an outdoor dining area and a lounge area with sun loungers. You will certainly spend a nice time here enjoying the Spanish atmosphere with your family and friends.

In addition to the wonderful sun terrace, your new home also includes a finished basement with an area of approximately 32 m². It is an ideal place to create an additional bedroom, study, gym or entertainment area. In addition, this intimate estate has a private swimming pool available only to residents.

The houses will be equipped with an independent solar energy system, consisting of photovoltaic panels with a battery for hot water intakes, placed on the roof. In addition, each has an internal patio and ground parking.

Location

Gran Alacant is a charming town located a short distance from the bustling city of Alicante in Spain. With stunning beaches, natural beauty and a wide range of activities and attractions, Gran Alacant is the perfect place to invest.

Beaches

One of the attractions of Gran Alacant is the beautiful El Carabassi beach, which stretches over 2 km with golden sand and crystal clear waters. Thanks to its calm waters and gentle waves, it is an ideal place for swimming, sunbathing and water sports.

In addition to the beach, Gran Alacant offers a range of other activities and attractions to keep you entertained. The area is renowned for its natural beauty, with miles of hiking and biking trails winding through the nearby mountains and forests.

For nature lovers, the nearby Clot de Galvany Nature Reserve is a must-see destination. This protected area covers over 350 hectares of natural forests and wetlands, home to a wide variety of flora and fauna, including rare birds and wildlife.

For a more cultural experience, be sure to visit the nearby city of Alicante, where you can explore sights such as Castel Sant'Angelo. Barbara and the Museum of Contemporary Art.

Located a short distance from Alicante-Elche Airport, Gran Alacant is easily accessible from anywhere in the world. Whether you're coming from Europe, North America or Asia, you'll find that getting to Gran Alacant is quick, easy and hassle-free.
