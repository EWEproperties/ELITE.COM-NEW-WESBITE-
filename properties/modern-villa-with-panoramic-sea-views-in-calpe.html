Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional contemporary villa located in the prestigious Empedrola II residential area of Calpe, on the northern Costa Blanca. This exclusive property has been designed for the most discerning buyers seeking a luxury home in Spain that combines spectacular Mediterranean Sea views, striking contemporary architecture and the highest standards of construction and finishing.

The residence offers approximately 751 m² of built area and is set on a 1,042 m² plot. This impressive three-level villa features elegant minimalist architecture, expansive floor-to-ceiling windows and a harmonious blend of modern design with the surrounding natural landscape. Thanks to its south-facing orientation, the interiors are flooded with natural light throughout the day, while almost every room enjoys breathtaking panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea, the iconic Peñón de Ifach and the skyline of Calpe.

The heart of the home is a spacious open-plan living and dining area seamlessly connected to a designer kitchen. Large sliding glass doors create a smooth transition between the interior and the expansive terraces, leading to a spectacular 10 x 3.3 m infinity swimming pool. This outstanding outdoor living area has been designed for relaxation, entertaining and enjoying the Mediterranean lifestyle all year round. More than 100 m² of terraces provide generous space for outdoor dining, sunbathing and taking in the magnificent scenery.

The villa features four spacious bedrooms, four elegant bathrooms and an additional guest toilet. Every bedroom has been thoughtfully designed to maximise comfort, privacy and natural light while offering beautiful panoramic views. The luxurious master suite benefits from an en-suite bathroom and direct access to the terrace.

The property stands out for its premium-quality finishes and cutting-edge technology, ensuring exceptional comfort and energy efficiency. Features include underfloor heating, air conditioning, high-quality aluminium joinery, modern glazing, a smart home automation system and advanced energy-saving solutions, resulting in an excellent Energy Rating A.

The exterior includes a private garage of approximately 52 m², additional parking spaces and beautifully landscaped Mediterranean gardens that perfectly complement the villa's contemporary architecture while providing complete privacy and an exclusive atmosphere.

Location

Empedrola II is one of Calpe's most prestigious and peaceful residential areas, renowned for its panoramic sea views, natural surroundings and exclusive luxury villas. It offers the perfect balance between privacy and convenient access to all the amenities and attractions the town has to offer.

Within just a few minutes' drive, residents can reach the sandy beaches of Playa Arenal-Bol and Playa de la Fossa, the picturesque marina, a wide selection of restaurants, cafés, boutiques and supermarkets. Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to hiking trails leading to the famous Peñón de Ifach Natural Park, sailing clubs, sports facilities and several renowned golf courses nearby. Excellent connections to the AP-7 motorway, as well as Alicante and Valencia airports, make this property an ideal choice as a luxury permanent residence, an exclusive holiday home or a prestigious investment opportunity on the Spanish property market.
