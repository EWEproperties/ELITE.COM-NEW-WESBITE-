Location

Jávea (Xàbia) is one of the most exclusive and picturesque destinations on the northern Costa Blanca, renowned for its dramatic coastline, crystal-clear Mediterranean waters, and exceptional microclimate, considered one of the healthiest in Europe. The town perfectly blends traditional Spanish charm with modern infrastructure, offering an outstanding quality of life throughout the year.

The surrounding area is home to some of the Costa Blanca's most beautiful beaches and coves, including Playa del Arenal, Cala Granadella, and Cala Portitxol. Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the nearby Montgó Natural Park, scenic hiking and cycling trails, marinas, water sports facilities, and prestigious golf courses.

Residents enjoy easy access to fine dining restaurants, stylish cafés, boutique shops, international schools, medical centres, and all essential everyday services. Excellent connections to Alicante and Valencia airports make Jávea an ideal destination for both permanent living and luxury holiday escapes.

This prestigious location is highly sought after by those who value privacy, security, and an exceptional Mediterranean lifestyle surrounded by nature, while remaining close to every convenience.
