New homes in Calanova, Mijas, on the Costa del Sol, offering four spacious bedrooms plus an additional flexible room. Located close to Calanova Golf and within easy reach of La Cala de Mijas, these contemporary multi-level properties combine modern interiors, generous outdoor areas and access to a gated residential community with a swimming pool, spa, gym and landscaped gardens. Many homes enjoy open views towards the Mediterranean Sea and surrounding mountains.

Each property is arranged over several levels and has been designed to provide privacy, space and flexibility. The layout includes four bedrooms together with an additional multipurpose room that can be adapted as a home office, private gym, cinema room, games room or other living space. Two private parking spaces and generous storage areas are included with each home. Selected properties also feature private solariums with panoramic views, while a private swimming pool is available as an option depending on the chosen property.

The interiors focus on natural light, comfort and a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living. Large-format porcelain flooring extends across the ground floor, terraces and solariums, creating a consistent contemporary finish. Upper-floor bedrooms feature laminate wood flooring with acoustic underlay. Bathrooms are equipped with electric underfloor heating, quality ceramic finishes, resin shower trays, thermostatic shower columns, glass screens, wall-mounted vanity units with integrated LED mirrors and electric towel warmers.

The kitchens are fully fitted with contemporary cabinetry, porcelain worktops and matching splashbacks. Integrated Bosch appliances include a fridge-freezer, dishwasher, induction hob, extractor hood, oven and microwave. A separate tiled laundry room with washing machine provides additional practical space. Each home is also equipped with an individual aerothermal system for hot water and climate control, supported by thermal and acoustic insulation and other energy-efficient installations. Selected homes include photovoltaic panels.

The fully gated community offers extensive communal facilities for everyday comfort and wellbeing. Landscaped gardens with native planting surround a saltwater swimming pool, while residents also have access to a Wellness & Spa area and a fully equipped gym. Additional features include a security lodge, colour video-entry system, digital parcel area and lifts adapted for reduced mobility.

Location

The properties are located in Calanova, Mijas, one of the established residential areas of the Costa del Sol in Málaga province. The setting is especially attractive for buyers looking for a property near golf, with Calanova Golf and several other courses close by. At the same time, the beaches, restaurants and leisure facilities of La Cala de Mijas are within easy reach, allowing residents to enjoy both a quieter residential environment and convenient access to the coast.

Calanova also benefits from good road connections. Access to the AP-7 motorway provides convenient links towards Fuengirola, Marbella and Málaga Airport, making travel along the Costa del Sol straightforward. The location can therefore work well for permanent living, a holiday home or a spacious second residence in southern Spain.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Calahonda - Riviera, about 1.8 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP Jardín Botánico (about 3 km) and IES Cala De Mijas (about 6 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Cala de Mijas, about 5 km away. The nearest hospital is Urgencias Hospital Costa del Sol, about 13 km by road, around 15 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Supermercado Miraflores, about 1 km away.

How far is the airport?

Málaga Airport is about 40 km by road, around 30 minutes by car.
