Location

The popular area of Punta Prima in Spain is located on the border of Torrevieja and Orihuela Costa. Punta Prima is very popular with tourists all year round and has a large population of permanent residents of all European nationalities, which means that shops and services are open all year round, regardless of the season.

For shopping, you will find a selection of well-known supermarkets and mini-markets where you can find all the essentials. The Punta Marina shopping centre has a supermarket, pharmacy, clothing shops, card shop, hairdressers, electrical shop, vets, tattoo parlour and many bars and restaurants.

Beaches

Punta Prima has two beaches and a small cove on its shores, La Playa Rocio del Mar, Cala Piteras and Punta Prima beach, the latter with a blue flag. A Blue Flag beach means that the beach is safe, the water quality is good, lifeguards are present during the high season, accessibility is good and toilets are available. The magnificent Punta Prima beach is 234 m long and 28 m wide. The beach is sandy with many rocky coves to explore.

There are also many fans of Cala Piteras, where one of Punta Prima's iconic establishments, Restaurante Nautilus, is located.

Restaurants

The road that connects the central part of Punta Prima with the beach is about a 10-minute walk away. At the end of this road is the famous Nautilus restaurant. This restaurant specialises in Mediterranean and Spanish cuisine. The food is beautifully presented and the location on the beachfront of Punta Prima is stunning. A great place to celebrate a special occasion.

RESTAURANTE PUNTA PRIMA on the southern coast of the Costa Blanca has been delighting locals and tourists for over 25 years with its exquisite approach to traditional Spanish and Western Mediterranean cuisine. It is a popular spot due to its magnificent large terrace, which offers impressive views of the main beach of Punta Prima and the Mediterranean Sea.

The central part of Punta Prima also boasts a huge variety of bars and restaurants to suit all tastes, from McDonalds and fish and chips to tapas and Japanese cuisine. The traditional Chinese restaurant Dinastia' is very popular and offers delicious Asian cuisine.

In the evening, there is a wonderful atmosphere and all the bars and restaurants are lit up.

Golf

This area of the southern Costa Blanca is home to many championship and award-winning golf courses. The closest to Punta Prima, just a 10-minute drive away, are Villamartin Golf, Las Ramblas Golf, Campoamor Golf and the famous Las Colinas Golf and Country Club. All have 18 holes and all have restaurants where you can enjoy a cold drink and a bite to eat after a round of golf.

A little further away are Romero Golf, Vistabella Golf, La Finca Golf, La Marquesa Golf and the famous La Manga Golf Club. Whether you are an avid golfer or a beginner, you are sure to find a golf course to suit your skills.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala Piteras, about 300 m by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio Público Romualdo Ballester (about 2 km) and Colegio Público Amanecer (about 2.1 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Patricio Pérez, about 6 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 4 km by road, around 7 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Novasia, about 800 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 51 km by road, around 40 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 74 km, around 55 minutes by car.
