New apartments for sale in Casares Costa on the Costa del Sol, offering 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms, spacious terraces and access to a private residential complex with landscaped gardens and communal swimming pools. The properties are located close to the coast, golf courses and everyday services, less than 15 minutes by car from Estepona and around 30 minutes from Marbella.

The development consists of 64 apartments distributed across 4 residential buildings, with two communal swimming pools serving the different sections of the complex. The buildings are arranged over a ground floor and two upper levels, creating a low-rise residential environment surrounded by landscaped areas. Each property includes a private parking space and storage room, while vehicle access to the underground garages is positioned around the perimeter so that the interior communal areas remain free from surface traffic.

Different property types are available to suit a range of requirements. Ground-floor apartments offer spacious terraces and, depending on the chosen unit, private garden areas. First-floor apartments provide generous outdoor spaces, while the upper-floor homes and penthouses feature particularly large terraces with open views towards the Mediterranean Sea and surrounding mountains.

The interiors have been designed around bright, functional living spaces. The main living and dining area forms the centre of the home and connects naturally with the terrace, while the kitchen is integrated into the living space. The kitchen is fully equipped and includes an induction hob, extractor hood, oven, microwave with grill, integrated washer-dryer, fridge-freezer and dishwasher. Built-in wardrobes are also included in the bedrooms.

Additional specifications include thermal and acoustic double glazing, aerothermal hot-water production and ducted air conditioning providing both heating and cooling. The homes also include video intercom systems and pre-installation for motorised shutters, while the residential buildings have photovoltaic installations serving the communal areas. The properties have an A energy rating according to the project specifications.

The communal areas include landscaped gardens, pedestrian paths and swimming pools with relaxation areas. The development is fully enclosed and designed to provide a private residential environment, while underground parking and storage areas help keep the exterior spaces focused on gardens and pedestrian use.

Location

The apartments are located in Casares Costa, Málaga province, on the western Costa del Sol, close to the New Golden Mile. Estepona is less than 15 minutes away by car, Marbella can be reached in approximately 30 minutes, and Málaga International Airport is less than 1 hour away. The surrounding area offers supermarkets, beaches, marinas, hospitals and a wide selection of golf courses.

Nearby beaches include Sabinillas, La Galera, Bahía Dorada and Arroyo Vaquero, while the marinas of Estepona and La Duquesa are also within easy reach. The area is particularly attractive for golfers, with several courses close to the development, including Finca Cortesín Golf Club, Doña Julia Golf, Casares Costa Golf, Estepona Golf, Azata Golf and Valle Romano.
