Location

Estepona is a quiet town in the province of Málaga and one of the tourist resorts of the Costa del Sol. They are characterized by typical Andalusian, low-rise buildings with narrow and patterned streets.

It is a paradise for artists and photographers.

The city is surrounded by the Sierra Bermeja mountains that dominate the entire landscape around Estepona. It is one of the few places in the world where the mountain range is located only 10 km from the sea. You can see how beautiful the views are from the top by setting off on marked hiking trails.

One of the most interesting attractions in this picturesque city is the orchidarium. The local orchid collection is the largest in Spain and is also among the largest in Europe! Even if you are not a fan of botanical gardens, this place is worth seeing if only because of the riot of colors and smells spreading here. A real feast for the senses!

In Estepona, there is the Playa de la Rada beach, which is over 2,600 meters long. We have many amenities in the area, such as: showers, umbrellas, toilets, pedal boats, playgrounds and trampolines for children. For smaller and bigger hunger, seaside restaurants and bars (Spanish: chiringuitos) serving local delicacies and drinks are recommended.
