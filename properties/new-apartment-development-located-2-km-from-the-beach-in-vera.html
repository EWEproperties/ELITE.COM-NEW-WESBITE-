Discover with us a new project of magnificent apartments located only 2 km from the beach. The flats have been designed with attention to every detail in a Mediterranean style and are perfectly located in the heart of Vera Playa

The development is located in Vera Playa (Almería), in an exclusive, luxury complex surrounded by green areas and amenities, parks and sports facilities, just a 20-minute walk or 2 km from the beach.

The residential complex consists of 60 flats and 18 bungalows in phase 1.

The flats have 2 or 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, distributed in 5 modern blocks equipped with 2 communal swimming pools and an additional heated indoor pool.

Each property has an underground parking space and a storage room. All floors have spacious terraces, and the apartments on the top floor have a private solarium with impressive sea views, ideal for enjoying the sun all year round.

The bungalows also offer 2 or 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, a parking space and storage room in the communal garage, as well as a communal swimming pool.

The ground floor flats have a terrace, while those on the first floor have a solarium and summer kitchen, ensuring maximum comfort in a prime location. All homes have been designed in a contemporary style and based on the concept of open spaces, combining a fully equipped kitchen and living room with a dining area.

Location

Vera, located on the Mediterranean coast of Almeria and 92 km from Almeria, has everything to become your dream summer holiday destination or a place to live all year round. It is a family-friendly place where you can get away from the routine and discover all the possibilities the municipality has to offer for an unforgettable summer full of unique and unforgettable experiences.

A paradise with five kilometres of beaches

Those looking for a holiday to relax, sunbathe and swim at the beach have a variety of areas with fine sand and crystal-clear waters at their disposal. This year, Vera is also celebrating the renewal of the Q for Quality award for Las Marinas-Bolaga beach, a large area covering 1,350 square metres with an average width of 200 metres, along which there is an attractive promenade; as well as the Blue Flag for El Playazo, a beach known worldwide for offering a large area for naturism. They are joined by the beaches of Puerto Rey and Cala Marqués. All of them offer the possibility of having a drink in bars, beach bars or exclusive beach clubs.

Nature, leisure and outdoor sports

Vera is also a place that offers the opportunity to enjoy nature in its surroundings, and in particular to get to know the ecosystem of two wetlands, Salar de los Canos, next to El Playazo, which covers more than 57 hectares and is a refuge and feeding lagoon especially for water birds; and the Puerto Rey lagoon, next to the beach of the same name, where many birds migrating between Africa and Europe rest and nest.

In addition to enjoying nature, Vera offers many opportunities for various outdoor sports, from golf, on a course where you can even play croquet, to water activities such as windsurfing, paddle surfing, kitesurfing, kayaking, catamaran sailing or sailing, to rugby, beach volleyball and tennis, among others, hiking.

Heritage, culture and gastronomy

This Mediterranean municipality has more than two millennia of history, and one of the remnants of its heritage is the Cerro del Espíritu Santo (CES), the ancient Nasrid city of Barya, declared a cultural asset. Other characteristic places that are also worth visiting are the Plaza Mayor of Vera, where the unique church of Nuestra Señora de la Encarnación, with its Mudejar-Andalusian structure, is located; the Fuente de los Cuatro Caños, of Arab origin; or the Plaza de Toros, considered the oldest bullring in Almería.

The history of the municipality is also reflected in its gastronomy, through aromas, colours and flavours that reveal the Hispano-Arabic heritage of Al-Andalus, which still survives in spices, dishes and the most avant-garde recipes, such as ajo colorado or gurullos with rabbit.

A family trip to remember

Fun on the beach, practising all kinds of sports, spending the day at the water park, enjoying local products and, in short, enjoying special moments with the family in a pleasant climate, Vera is what makes the municipality a destination for travelling with children, not only in summer but also at any time of the year.

Vera has been awarded the Family Tourism Seal by the National Federation of Large Families, a distinction that honours the most child-friendly destinations for leisure, services and the ideal tourist offer for family holidays.

A place to stay and live

In addition to all the possibilities that Vera offers for spending a few days that will remain in your memory forever, it also offers a quality of life that encourages you to stay and live there. This option is already appreciated by many people who spend long periods of time or stay to live there thanks to the advantages offered by teleworking and the comforts of the destination.

The municipality is also one of those with the highest per capita income in the province and shares with the capital of Almeria a good climate, landscapes and services that have distinguished the city of Almeria as the happiest in Spain, according to the Spain Happy Index 2024 survey.

Added to all this is the good nature of the local population, whose human value makes tourists feel welcome, thus contributing to the fact that the stories they experience in Vera will remain in their memory forever.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa El Playazo, about 2.1 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio Valdeserra (about 2.3 km) and Colegio de Educación Infantil y Primaria (about 5 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest hospital is Hospital La Inmaculada, about 32 km by road, around 25 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Cositas Supermercado y Cafeteria, about 100 m away.

How far is the airport?

Almería Airport is about 83 km by road, around 60 minutes by car.
