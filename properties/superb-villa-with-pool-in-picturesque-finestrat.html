Location

Finestrat, a charming town located in the Costa Blanca region of Spain, offers the perfect combination of natural beauty, cultural attractions and recreational activities. Located a short distance from the coast, Finestrat boasts magnificent beaches, a rich history and easy access to the nearby airport.

One of Finestrat's main attractions is its beautiful beaches. The town is conveniently located near several sandy shores, including the popular and picturesque Playa de Poniente and Playa de Levante in neighbouring Benidorm. These beaches offer pristine turquoise waters, soft golden sand and excellent facilities, making them ideal for sunbathers, swimmers and water sports enthusiasts.

Beyond its beaches, Finestrat has much to offer in terms of cultural and historical attractions. The historic town centre, with its narrow, winding streets, traditional Spanish architecture and charming squares, is a real pleasure to explore. Be sure to visit the iconic Church of San Bartolomé, a 14th-century church that bears witness to the town's rich heritage.

For the adventurous, a visit to the nearby Puig Campana mountain is a must. This impressive peak is a paradise for hikers and climbers, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding landscape. The trek to the summit is an exhilarating experience and provides an opportunity to commune with nature.

If you are a fan of amusement parks and entertainment, you are in luck. Finestrat is conveniently located near Terra Mítica, one of the largest amusement parks in Spain. This exciting attraction offers a variety of thrilling rides, live shows and themed areas inspired by ancient civilisations.

When it comes to amenities, Finestrat offers a wide range of shops, restaurants, and bars that cater to the needs of both residents and tourists. The town's numerous eateries allow you to savour traditional Spanish cuisine, sample fresh seafood, or enjoy international dishes.

In terms of transport, Finestrat benefits from its proximity to Alicante-Elche Airport, which is approximately 45 kilometres away. This international airport provides excellent connections to major cities in Europe and beyond, allowing travellers to reach Finestrat with ease.
