Location

Benidorm is a city located on the east coast of Spain and is one of the most popular holiday destinations in the entire country. Known for its stunning beaches, clear waters and vibrant nightlife, Benidorm attracts millions of tourists every year. Benidorm is the city with the most skyscrapers in Spain and is often called the second New York. Whether you're looking for a place to relax or have fun, Benidorm has something to offer everyone.

El Balcón del Mediterráneo

Benidorm also has a particularly romantic side. We are talking about El Balcón del Mediterráneo, a viewpoint that captivates not only with its appearance, but also with its location. The castle built on the rocks separates the two beaches, and its terrace offers an amazing view of the entire bay. This location used to be of strategic importance - it allowed for the observation of possible threats appearing on the horizon and the rapid organization of defense units. Today, El Balcón del Mediterráneo is one of the most popular tourist attractions in Benidorm and probably the most photographed place in this part of the Iberian Peninsula.

Beaches

Playa de Levante is one of Benidorm's two main beaches and is considered one of the best in the country. This vast beach of golden sand is perfect for sunbathing, swimming and soaking up the Mediterranean sun. You will also find many restaurants, bars and shops along the promenade.

Playa de Poniente is the largest beach in Benidorm: 3,000 meters of white sandy shore, which in some sections is over 100 meters wide! Located south of the center and awarded the Blue Flag, it is less crowded than Playa de Levante (the resort's other large beach) and is a favorite among locals. Playa de Poniente offers all services: umbrellas and deckchairs (available 11 months a year), free showers and toilets, playgrounds for children, lifeguards, easier access for disabled people, and even a public library, located directly on the beach.

Isla de Benidorm

As soon as you arrive in Benidorm and step onto the sandy beach, you will see an islet sticking out of the sea a few kilometers from the beach (exactly two nautical miles from the port of Benidorm). This is an island called Isla de Benidorm. The Spanish use another name for this island, Isla de los Periodistas. There are no people living on the island, it has no permanent inhabitants, although from Benidorm you can buy a trip to the islet, where you can eat a seafood meal in a restaurant.
