Discover an exclusive collection of new apartments with sea views in El Chaparral, Mijas, located in an attractive residential area of the Costa del Sol, just 700 metres from the beach. Combining contemporary architecture, spacious terraces and excellent communal facilities, the complex offers a comfortable Mediterranean lifestyle within easy reach of La Cala de Mijas, Fuengirola, Marbella and some of the area's most popular golf courses.

The boutique development consists of only 36 apartments, providing residents with greater privacy and a peaceful atmosphere compared with larger residential resorts. Thanks to its elevated position, the complex enjoys beautiful views towards the Mediterranean Sea, including from some of the lower-level properties. Construction has already been completed, with delivery planned for autumn 2026.

The apartments have been designed around natural light, open spaces and outdoor living. Large windows connect the interiors with generous private terraces, allowing the living areas to benefit from the surrounding views and Mediterranean sunshine. The terraces provide plenty of space for outdoor dining, relaxing or simply enjoying the sea views throughout the year.

Inside, the homes feature contemporary finishes and practical layouts created for comfortable everyday living. The kitchens are fitted with quality appliances and a washing machine, while the apartments also include fitted wardrobes and fully equipped bathrooms. Neutral finishes and large glazed areas create bright interiors that can easily be adapted to different styles of furnishing.

One of the highlights of these new apartments in Mijas Costa is the range of communal facilities available within the private residential complex. Residents can enjoy swimming pools for adults and children surrounded by landscaped areas, providing an inviting place to relax during the warmer months.

The development also includes a gym with panoramic views, allowing residents to exercise while overlooking the surrounding landscape. Additional leisure facilities include mini golf, a pétanque area and a children's playground, creating spaces suitable for different ages and lifestyles. Access to the complex is controlled through a secure gated entrance, adding privacy and comfort for residents.

EXCELLENT LOCATION 700 METRES FROM THE BEACH

The development enjoys an excellent location in El Chaparral, Mijas Costa, combining proximity to the Mediterranean with convenient access to everyday services, leisure facilities and neighbouring coastal towns. The beach is approximately 700 metres away, making it possible to reach the coast on foot and enjoy the seaside lifestyle without relying on the car for every trip.

Golf enthusiasts will also appreciate the proximity of El Chaparral Golf Club, located around five minutes away. La Cala de Mijas, with its beaches, restaurants, shops and traditional coastal atmosphere, can also be reached in approximately five minutes, while Fuengirola is around 10 minutes away.

For shopping and everyday conveniences, Miramar Shopping Centre is approximately six minutes from the development. Marbella is around 25 minutes away, while Málaga Airport can be reached in approximately 22 minutes, providing convenient connections for international homeowners and visitors travelling to the Costa del Sol.

Mijas Costa is particularly attractive for those looking to combine a relaxed coastal setting with easy access to the main destinations of the Costa del Sol. Beaches, golf courses, restaurants, shopping facilities and established coastal towns are all within easy reach, while the residential surroundings of El Chaparral offer a quieter setting away from the busiest tourist areas.
