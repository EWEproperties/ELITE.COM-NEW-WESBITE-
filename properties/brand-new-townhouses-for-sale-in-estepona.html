Elite World Estates proudly presents an exclusive collection of contemporary townhouses located in one of the most desirable areas of Estepona on the Costa del Sol. Designed for buyers seeking a spacious and elegant home in Spain, these exceptional residences combine modern architecture, premium finishes, generous outdoor spaces, and an outstanding location just minutes from the Mediterranean Sea.

Part of an exclusive residential development comprising only 87 residences, these elegant 4-bedroom townhouses have been thoughtfully designed to offer privacy, comfort, and a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living. With over 160 m² of interior living space, they provide an ideal setting for families, holiday homeowners, or investors looking for a premium property on the Costa del Sol.

Each townhouse features bright open-plan living areas, expansive floor-to-ceiling windows, and carefully designed interiors that maximize natural light throughout the day. High-quality materials, contemporary finishes, and functional layouts create a sophisticated yet welcoming atmosphere that perfectly suits modern Mediterranean living.

One of the standout features of these homes is the generous outdoor space. Private terraces, landscaped gardens, and an impressive rooftop solarium provide multiple areas for relaxing, dining outdoors, entertaining guests, or simply enjoying the sunshine and panoramic surroundings. Thanks to the predominantly south, southeast, and southwest orientation, the homes benefit from abundant natural light and spectacular sunsets throughout the year.

The spacious bedrooms have been designed as peaceful retreats, featuring built-in wardrobes and large windows that create bright, airy interiors. The master suite offers an elegant and comfortable space where residents can fully embrace the relaxed lifestyle of southern Spain.

Residents also enjoy beautifully landscaped communal gardens, an elegant outdoor swimming pool, and inviting relaxation areas designed to make the most of the Costa del Sol's exceptional climate. Every detail of the development has been carefully planned to create a private and tranquil environment while maintaining a strong connection with nature.

Each townhouse includes a private underground parking space and a storage room, with direct access from the secure two-level underground garage, ensuring convenience, privacy, and peace of mind.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its exceptional location in Estepona, one of the most sought-after destinations on the Costa del Sol. Beautiful sandy beaches, prestigious golf courses, restaurants, boutiques, marinas, international schools, and all daily amenities are just a short drive away. Marbella is approximately 20 minutes away, while Málaga International Airport can be reached in less than an hour, making these townhouses an outstanding choice for permanent living, a second home, or a long-term investment.

These exclusive townhouses offer the perfect combination of contemporary design, spacious interiors, premium quality, and an exceptional Mediterranean lifestyle in one of southern Spain's most desirable coastal locations.

Location

The development is located in one of the most sought-after areas of Estepona, just a few minutes from the town centre and the Costa del Sol coastline. This exceptional location perfectly combines the Mediterranean lifestyle with the convenience of everyday living. The area offers a peaceful residential setting while providing quick access to beaches, the marina, renowned golf courses, and all the essential amenities required for comfortable year-round living. The dynamic growth of western Estepona also makes it an attractive investment destination.

Residents are just a short walk from the wide sandy La Rada Beach and the lively seaside promenade lined with restaurants, cafés, and beach clubs. The immediate surroundings offer supermarkets, shops, schools, medical centres, sports facilities, and Estepona Marina with its excellent selection of restaurants, leisure venues, and nautical activities. Estepona is also renowned for its charming old town, beautiful parks, and more than 20 kilometres of Mediterranean coastline with stunning sandy beaches.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to prestigious golf courses such as Valle Romano Golf, Estepona Golf, Azata Golf, and Finca Cortesín. Excellent connections to the A-7 and AP-7 motorways provide easy access to Marbella, Puerto Banús, Sotogrande, and the international airports of Málaga and Gibraltar, making this location ideal as a permanent residence, a holiday home, or a buy-to-let investment.
