Location

Benalmádena is one of the most beautiful and popular destinations on the Costa del Sol, and an easy city to visit that surprises visitors with everything it has to offer. It is located only 20 km from Malaga and 40 km from Marbella. This city is loved by Spanish tourists. Benalmádena offers us its wonderful port, its monuments, not forgetting good gastronomy, a charismatic, typically Andalusian old town, festivals, unique places, all in the company of a privileged, typically Mediterranean climate.

1. Puerto Marina and Plaza Solimar give Benalmádena a wide range of opportunities where we can have fun, drink coffee, walk or go to the club.

2. Plaza Sol y Mar has recently been rebuilt and has a shopping center where we can go shopping.

3. In addition, Benalmádena has numerous tourist attractions such as Sealife in Benalmádena or the Tivoli World amusement park in Arroyo de la Miel.

4. In Benamádena you will also find a wide range of hotels, among which we highlight the Torrequebrada hotel with a golf course, casino, restaurants and shows.

5. Benalmádena Costa is a family tourist town where we can enjoy the beach and Benalmádena Pueblo, which has retained the charm of an Andalusian town to this day and is located in the upper part, with a beautiful view of the entire coast.

Benalmádena can be divided into three main parts. It will be: Benalmádena Pueblo, the ancient Andalusian part with characteristic white buildings, the modern and residential Arroyo de la Miel, and a wonderful port. Each of them is unique and worth attention.

1. Benalmádena Pueblo

A restored old town (pueblo) with narrow streets and two squares (Plaza de España and Plaza Andalucia). The first one features the La Niña bronze fountain, which is the symbol of Benalmádena Pueblo

2. Benalmádena Costa, the newest part of the city, located along the coast and directly adjacent to the sea. A significant part of its buildings are tourist facilities (hotels, catering outlets, discos and the beach).

3. Arroyo de la Miel (Honey-flowing stream), originally a separate settlement, located between the other two districts. It is the main residential district and the most commercially active part of the city.
