Location

Algorfa, located in the Vega Baja del Segura region, boasts a rich history dating back to Roman times. The town's name originates from the Arabic word “Al-Gorfa,” meaning “cave,” as the area was once known for its numerous natural caves. Today, Algorfa is renowned for its citrus fruit and vegetable production.

The town offers a variety of attractions suitable for visitors of all ages.

La Finca Golf Course

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the renowned La Finca Golf Course. This championship course is set in beautiful natural surroundings and offers stunning views of the Vega Baja countryside. Designed to challenge players of all skill levels, it also features excellent facilities including a pro shop and restaurant.

Algorfa Weekly Market

To experience the local culture, be sure to visit the Algorfa market held every Wednesday. Here you will find a wide selection of fresh produce, clothing, local products, and souvenirs. It is the perfect place to discover authentic Spanish life and cuisine.

Plaza de España

Plaza de España is the heart of Algorfa and an ideal place to enjoy the local atmosphere. The square features a beautiful fountain, plenty of seating areas, and a variety of restaurants and bars. It is the perfect spot to relax with a drink and watch daily life unfold.
