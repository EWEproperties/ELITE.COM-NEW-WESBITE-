Location

Located on the beautiful Costa Blanca in Spain, Torrevieja is accessible to both locals and foreigners. The city offers numerous attractions, from beautiful beaches and nature parks to vibrant nightlife and cultural attractions. One of the most sought-after areas in Torrevieja is Los Balcones, a quiet and prestigious suburb offering an exclusive lifestyle and easy access to the best of the region.

Los Balcones boasts elegant villas and luxury apartments, along with a quiet and peaceful atmosphere. This area is located a short distance from Torrevieja town center, making it an ideal destination for those who enjoy the best of both worlds—a peaceful suburban lifestyle with the hustle and bustle of the city. The area is surrounded by beautiful natural parks, including the protected Torrevieja Salt Lakes, which are designated as a nature reserve and wildlife sanctuary.

The beaches of Torrevieja lead the way to the region's best beaches. The area boasts some of the city's best beaches, including Playa de los Locos, Playa del Cura, and Playa de la Mata. These beaches are renowned for their crystal-clear waters, fine sand, and excellent golfing practices, making them popular with locals and non-local residents alike.

Several golf courses are located within the area, including the prestigious Villamartin Golf Club, which is a short drive away. Nearby nature parks offer excellent hiking and biking trails, as well as opportunities for birdwatching and nature photography.
