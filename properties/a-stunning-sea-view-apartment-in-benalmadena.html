Location

Benalmádena is located just 20 km from Malaga and 40 km from Marbella. This town is beloved by Spanish tourists. The high quality of services attracts guests from all over the world to its Puerto Marina port, and the cosmopolitan atmosphere makes it hard to think of anything other than blissful relaxation and great fun.

Benalmádena can be divided into three main areas: Benalmádena Pueblo, the ancient Andalusian part with its characteristic white buildings; the modern and residential Arroyo de la Miel; and the magnificent port. Each is unique and worth seeing.

1. Benalmádena Pueblo

A restored old town (pueblo) with narrow streets and two squares (Plaza de España and Plaza Andalucia). The first one features the bronze fountain of La Niña, the symbol of Benalmádena Pueblo.

2. Benalmádena Costa, the newest part of the city, located along the coast and directly adjacent to the sea. A significant portion of its development consists of tourist facilities (hotels, restaurants, discos, and the beach).

3. Arroyo de la Miel (Honey Stream), originally a separate settlement, located between the other two districts. It is the main residential area and the most commercially active part of the city.

Benalmádena is especially recommended for families with children, as it offers a wealth of attractions, including those for children. You can find most of the most interesting ones below:

The fairy-tale Colomares Castle

Colomares Castle is not actually a castle, but a monument to Columbus. Nevertheless, it is beautiful and one of the major attractions in Benalmádena. It was designed and built by Esteban Marin Martin. Its construction lasted from 1987 to 1994. Interestingly, the castle houses the smallest church in the world, listed in the Guinness Book of Records. Its area is a mere 1.96 square meters.

STUPA

A living monument to the enlightened mind, a Buddhist monument to peace and harmony in the world, and a place for study, reflection, and meditation. This place is particularly interesting, also because it is the largest stupa in Europe. Few people know that it was designed by Polish architect Wojciech Kossowski. An additional attraction is the beautiful panorama from the hill.

Port of Benalmádena

Puerto Marina is a good place for a family stroll or a wine dinner. You can admire the ships moored here and the interesting architecture. The port is full of bars, shops, and cafes. This place has twice been named the most beautiful port in the world. It's also been nicknamed the "Venice of Andalusia" due to its narrow streets, narrow canals, and bridges. One of the attractions you can count on here is the aquarium. At Sea Life, you'll find over 2,000 species of marine life, including turtles, sharks, and rays.

Tivoli World

An ideal activity for both children and adults. Among the fountains and gardens, you can enjoy not only over 30 attractions for the whole family but also a wide selection of food. This is an old-school amusement park, with classic attractions such as ferris wheels, bumper cars, and a mansion of terror, as well as new offerings for the more daring. The tower, from which you can experience a dizzying fall from a height of 60 meters, is undoubtedly one of the most spectacular attractions in the park.

Benalmádena Cable Car

The Benalmádena Cable Car offers a bird's-eye view of the entire city. The three-kilometer route leads to the summit of Calamorro in the Sierra de Mijas mountain range. Upon arrival, you can admire the beautiful panorama of the Costa del Sol, nearby Gibraltar, and, weather permitting, the African coast. Besides the magnificent scenery at the top of Calomorro, there are several other optional attractions, such as bird of prey shows, Andalusian horse training demonstrations, trekking trails, and cycling routes.

BEACHES

While visiting this beautiful town, it's definitely worth relaxing on one of Benalmádena's beaches, and there's a wide selection to choose from: Arroyo Hondo, Carvajal, Bonita, Bil, La Morera, Arroyo de la Miel, Benalnatura, Fuente de la Salud, La Perla, Las Viborillas, Playa de las Yucas, Torrebermeja, Santa Ana, Tajo de la Soga, Torremuelle, Torrequebrada, and Torrevigía are all options worth exploring when planning your stay here.
