Location

Calpe, a charming seaside town located on the Mediterranean Sea, tempts with its undeniable charm and lots of attractions. Located in the province of Alicante in Spain, Calpe offers a stunning mix of natural beauty, historic sites and vibrant attractions. Located approximately 75 kilometers from Alicante airport, Calpe provides convenient access for travelers wishing to discover its wonders.

Upon arrival, you'll be blown away by the stunning beaches that have earned the prestigious Q' certificates for quality and the Blue Flag. Calpe boasts some of the most beautiful sandy shores in the region, including the iconic Playa de la Fossa and Playa de la Arenal-Bol. Immerse yourself in Mediterranean bliss, bask in the sun or enjoy the water sports and activities that are an integral part of Calpe's coastal landscape.

In addition to its picturesque beaches, Calpe is adorned with monuments and cultural treasures. The distinctive Peñón de Ifach, a massive limestone rock formation, is the symbol of Calpe and offers breathtaking panoramic views. Hike to the top and marvel at the natural beauty unfolding before your eyes. The old town with its charming narrow streets and historic architecture invites you to stroll and discover its rich heritage.

The bustling fishing port of Calpe is a must-visit. Watch the fishermen bring in their daily catch, visit the fish market, and enjoy fresh seafood at the numerous restaurants along the harbour. For local culture, visit the Museum of Archeology and Ethnography to delve into Calpe's fascinating history.

Nature lovers will find solace in the nature parks surrounding Calpe. Explore Las Salinas Natural Park, a paradise for migratory birds such as flamingos and storks, or head to the Serra Gelada Natural Park to see its rugged cliffs and enchanting trails. For hikers, the Sierra de Bernia and the Sierra de Oltà offer exciting routes and stunning views.

Immerse yourself in the lively atmosphere of Calpe's local markets, where you can discover fresh produce, handicrafts and traditions. Taste the local gastronomy, famous for its seafood, rice dishes, and spicy tapas.
