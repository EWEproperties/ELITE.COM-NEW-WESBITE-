Architectural perfection is ensured by an intimate complex that provides security, privacy and panoramic sea views.

The properties have been designed in a modern style and open concept, consisting of a fully equipped kitchen and living room with dining room, bedrooms with built-in wardrobes, elegant bathrooms and spacious terraces. This elite residential complex offers amazing common spaces with extensive green areas. Here you will find playgrounds, a large swimming pool, a jacuzzi, a barbecue area and a work area. In addition, residents can enjoy a fully equipped gym, enabling both cardio and strength exercises with efficient ventilation and beautiful views of the garden.

Location

Cabo Roig is a beautiful urbanization located on the shores of the Mediterranean Sea in the south of the Costa Blanca, belonging to the Orihuela Costa and being one of the most exclusive areas of the Costa Blanca. As its name suggests, Cabo Roig is a strip of land extending into the sea and therefore surrounded by beaches, all marked with a blue flag. The name Cabo Roig' is translated from the Valencian word Cabo Rojo', given by the red rock that forms this cape.

One of the main characteristics of Cabo Roig is the lack of tall buildings, most of the properties in this area are villas with large plots a very short distance from the coast, along with the famous white sand beaches and a wide range of all kinds of activities related to the sea and proximity to the La Shopping Center Zenia Boulevard and the Campoamor and Villamartín golf courses make this city a real paradise and a privileged place to spend holidays, long periods of time or even settle permanently.

A beautiful promenade allows you to walk along the entire shoreline and connects with neighboring buildings. A famous monument is the historic tower of Cabo Roig, built in the 16th century as a defense against pirates. Walk or bike the quiet streets and avenues of Cabo Roig, lined with large palm trees, while observing the well-kept gardens and beautiful single-family homes that can be seen on every side, enjoying the sea breeze, making this area a true oasis of peace.

Golf enthusiasts will delight in the numerous golf courses available in the area, including Villamartin Golf Course, Las Colinas Golf & Country Club and Campoamor Golf Club. These courses offer world-class facilities and stunning views, providing an unforgettable golfing experience.

Frequently asked questions
