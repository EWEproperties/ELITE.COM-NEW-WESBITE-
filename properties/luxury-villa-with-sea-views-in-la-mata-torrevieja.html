Location

La Mata is a charming coastal town located near Torrevieja, on the sunny Costa Blanca. With its stunning beaches, vibrant culture, and world-class amenities, La Mata is the perfect destination for anyone looking to experience the best of Spain.

The local beach in La Mata is a long stretch of golden sand, offering plenty of space for sunbathing and swimming. There are also plenty of water sports available, including jet skiing, windsurfing, and sailing, making it a popular spot for adventure seekers.

When it comes to things to do, La Mata has plenty to offer. You can explore the local shops and markets, sampling fresh produce and handmade goods. The town also has a rich cultural heritage, with historic landmarks and museums showcasing its unique history and traditions.

If you're looking for a bite to eat, La Mata has some of the best restaurants in the region. From traditional Spanish cuisine to international flavors, there is something for everyone. Be sure to try the local seafood, which is caught fresh daily and served up in a variety of delicious dishes.

La Mata is conveniently located just a short drive from Alicante Airport, making it easily accessible from anywhere in Europe. With its stunning beaches, vibrant culture, and world-class amenities, La Mata is the perfect destination for anyone looking to experience the best of the Spanish coast.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de La Mata, about 1.2 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Ciudad del Mar, about 4 km, around 6 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 22 km, around 24 minutes by car.
International school: Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen, about 12 km, around 18 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Mata, about 1.2 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Quirón/USP Hospital San Jaime, about 3 km by road, around 5 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Unide - Maxcoop, about 400 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 44 km by road, around 35 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 65 km, around 50 minutes by car.
