Elite World Estates presents an exceptional collection of modern detached villas located within the prestigious Hacienda del Álamo Golf Resort in the Murcia region. This is an ideal opportunity for those seeking a comfortable home surrounded by nature, golf courses, and the Mediterranean lifestyle all year round.

This exclusive boutique development consists of only a limited number of elegant single-storey villas designed with comfort, functionality, and contemporary living in mind. Buyers can choose between 2 or 3-bedroom layouts, each featuring 2 stylish bathrooms. The bright interiors offer an open-plan design combining a spacious living room, dining area, and modern kitchen. Large floor-to-ceiling windows flood the interiors with natural light and seamlessly connect the living space with the terrace and garden.

Each villa is situated on a private, fenced plot ranging from approximately 300 m² to over 550 m². Standard features include a private swimming pool, landscaped garden, and covered parking space. An additional highlight is the spacious rooftop solarium of approximately 55 m², offering beautiful views of the golf course and surrounding mountains.

Hacienda del Álamo Golf Resort is one of the most complete golf resorts in southeastern Spain. Residents enjoy access to restaurants, bars, a hotel with SPA facilities, a golf club, tennis courts, sports facilities, and 24-hour security. Murcia International Airport is approximately 20 minutes away by car, while the wide sandy beaches of Costa Cálida can be reached in around 25 minutes.

Location

Fuente Álamo de Murcia is a charming Spanish town located in the Murcia region, known for its peaceful Mediterranean lifestyle, authentic local character, and excellent connections to the Costa Cálida coastline. Surrounded by picturesque mountains and agricultural landscapes, it offers ideal conditions for year-round living.

The town provides all essential services for everyday life, including shops, restaurants, schools, medical centres, and sports facilities. Thanks to its strategic location, residents can enjoy both the tranquillity of a traditional Spanish town and easy access to the region’s main attractions.

Fuente Álamo is located approximately 20 minutes from Murcia International Airport and around 25 minutes from the beautiful sandy beaches of Costa Cálida on the Mediterranean Sea. Another advantage is its proximity to prestigious golf courses, natural parks, and historic cities such as Cartagena and Murcia.

It is an excellent location for those looking for a peaceful place to live, a second home in Spain, or an attractive investment property in a region enjoying more than 300 sunny days per year.

Frequently asked questions

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Nueva Escuela, about 6 km, around 14 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio "San Agustín", about 6 km, around 14 minutes by car.
International school: El Limonar International School - Buenavista, about 45 km, around 35 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is the local public health centre (Centro de Salud), about 8 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Clínico Universitario Virgen de la Arrixaca, about 45 km by road, around 35 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, about 6 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 20 km by road, around 27 minutes by car.
