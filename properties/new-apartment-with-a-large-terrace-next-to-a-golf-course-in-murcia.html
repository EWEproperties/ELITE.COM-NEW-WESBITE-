For sale, a modern apartment with 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, located in an exceptional setting next to the golf course. The property has been designed for a comfortable Mediterranean lifestyle, combining spacious interiors, generous outdoor areas and contemporary architecture with distinctive soft, curved lines.

The apartment offers a bright and functional layout. Large windows provide plenty of natural light and create a seamless connection between the interior and the terrace. Three bedrooms provide comfortable space for family and guests, while two modern bathrooms add convenience for everyday living.

At the heart of the apartment is an open-plan living area combining the lounge, dining room and kitchen. The kitchen comes furnished and equipped with integrated appliances, including an oven, microwave, refrigerator, dishwasher and induction hob with integrated extractor. The property also includes air conditioning, an aerothermal system and high-quality porcelain flooring.

One of the main highlights of the property is its spacious terrace, which creates a natural extension of the living room and provides the perfect place to enjoy the Mediterranean climate and the beautiful golf course surroundings. Depending on the location within the development, there are also ground-floor properties with extensive outdoor areas and penthouses with private solariums. The available 3-bedroom apartments offer a variety of sizes and outdoor spaces.

The residential complex features a large communal swimming pool surrounded by landscaped gardens and green areas, creating an attractive setting for relaxation and enjoying the sunny climate.

A parking space and storage room are included in the price of the apartment.

Completion is scheduled for the second quarter of 2028.

Location

The property is situated in the exceptional surroundings of El Valle Golf Resort in the Murcia region, where contemporary architecture blends harmoniously with the greenery and open spaces of the golf course. It is an ideal location for those looking for sunshine, tranquillity and privacy while still enjoying convenient access to the city and the coast.

One of the greatest advantages of the location is its position directly next to the golf course. Extensive green areas create a peaceful setting for relaxation, while selected apartments offer panoramic golf views. The generous terraces allow residents to make the most of the Mediterranean lifestyle, from morning coffee outdoors to relaxing evenings overlooking the surrounding landscape.

The location is particularly attractive to golf enthusiasts, but it is equally suitable for those looking for a second home in Spain away from the more densely developed coastal resorts. The peaceful resort setting provides a sense of space and connection with nature while offering a convenient base for exploring the Murcia region and the Costa Cálida.

The combination of 3 bedrooms, a spacious terrace, modern specifications, a communal swimming pool and an excellent golf course location makes this property an attractive choice both as a holiday home and for year-round living.

Frequently asked questions

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Nuestra Señora de las Maravillas, about 11 km, around 10 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio San Vicente Ferrer, about 19 km, around 19 minutes by car.
International school: El Limonar International School - Buenavista, about 14 km, around 15 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Consultorio de Los Martínez del Puerto, about 10 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Clínico Universitario Virgen de la Arrixaca, about 13 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Spar, about 17 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 11 km by road, around 11 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 96 km, around 70 minutes by car.
