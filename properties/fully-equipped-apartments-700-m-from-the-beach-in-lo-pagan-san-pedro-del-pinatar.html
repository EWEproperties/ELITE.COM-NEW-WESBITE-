Location

Lo Pagan is part of San Pedro del Pinatar, a coastal town on the Costa Calida. Its shores are bathed by both the Mar Menor and the Mediterranean Sea. Warm temperatures, averaging 18 degrees Celsius year-round, and over 315 days of natural sunlight make it an ideal place for both holidays and living. The Mar Menor has calm, warm waters, and the Mediterranean Sea is open and natural thanks to the Las Salinas de San Pedro del Pinatar Nature Reserve.

San Pedro del Pinatar boasts 14 km of beaches scattered along two seas: the Mar Menor and the Mediterranean Sea. Its strategic coastal location and environmental advantages have made it a highly sought-after residential area.

In addition to its well-maintained beaches, port, and comprehensive commercial and service infrastructure, San Pedro del Pinatar boasts a unique natural area – the Las Salinas y Arenales de San Pedro del Pinatar Protected Landscape Area. This wetland is home to flamingos, gulls, albatrosses, herons, and owls. It also boasts shifting sand dunes with distinctive vegetation, including sea lilies, bulrushes, and reeds.

The municipality of San Pedro del Pinatar, in addition to the main town, consists of seventeen small towns, including El Mojón, Las Salinas, Los Sáez, and Lo Pagán. The latter, Lo Pagán, is the most important area due to its rapid development in tourism.

The most famous beach in Lo Pagán is La Puntica Beach, which offers picturesque amenities such as huts and a swimming area with access to the sea, as well as a wide range of recreational activities.
