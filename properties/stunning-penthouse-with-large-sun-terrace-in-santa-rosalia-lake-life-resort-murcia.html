Treat yourself to a life of luxury and stay in this exclusive Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort project, which covers 700,000 m2 with 24-hour security, a wonderful lake with surrounding beaches, water sports, golf and tropical recreational areas. Discover with us this new project that offers apartments for sale in the luxurious Santa Rosalía Lake and Life Resort in the Region of Murcia, surrounded by excellent golf courses and just 10 minutes from the sandy beaches of the Menor Sea.

Take a walk, eat a meal, sunbathe or relax outdoors. Santa Rosalía Lake and Life Resort is an intimate, cozy place where you can leave the hustle and bustle of the world behind. With excellent facilities for both permanent residents and holidaymakers, it is a whole new world, just 30 km from Murcia airport and 95 km from Alicante airport.

Each apartment was designed by the renowned architect Zorg Arquitectos. The apartments are divided into the ground floor with a garden, the first and second floors with a terrace and the third floor with a spacious private terrace. There are two apartments on each floor, and each level is accessible by elevator. From the apartments you can admire panoramic views of the swimming pool, public areas and, depending on the floor, the beautiful surroundings and the charming Lake Santa Rosalía. It's up to you to choose which of these modern apartments you will call your new home.

The developer offers apartments with 2 or 3 comfortable bedrooms and 2 fully equipped bathrooms. Open spaces, well-thought-out room layout, bright and natural colors and attention to the smallest detail make each of these elite properties meet the expectations of even the most demanding customers.

Make yourself comfortable in your elegant and fully equipped kitchen, perfectly combined with the dining room and the cozy living room. Here you will find high-quality household appliances as well as cabinets and drawers offering plenty of space for storing kitchen utensils. You can enjoy a spacious and comfortable space perfect for cooking, eating and relaxing with friends and family.

Comfortable bedrooms have been designed to provide their residents with a peaceful break from the hustle and bustle of everyday life. Each bedroom has a built-in wardrobe, providing plenty of space for all your clothes and accessories. Wardrobes are designed to be functional and stylish, with elements that will add elegance to your space.

In addition, this exclusive residential residence has a well-developed common area with a wonderful, modern swimming pool, a social club and green areas. All apartments have underground parking and a storage room.

Location

Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort Murcia is located 35 km from Murcia and 19 km from La Manga del Mar Menor. The nearest airport is Murcia International Airport, 22 km away.

Mediterranean living at its best: Santa Rosalia Lake & Life Resort seeks to redefine the concept of luxury living, providing fantastic accommodation options for the whole family in a five-star complex with a total area of approximately 700,000 square meters.

Approximately 120,000 m2 of landscaped land with various sports and recreation centers, 16,000 native Mediterranean trees and plants, an impressive swimming lake and a beach with facilities for water sports, paddle boarding and kayaking. Highly secure, has access control and security services.

Beach Club

The Beach Club is the perfect place to meet to hang out or dine at the resort. Santa Rosalia Beach Club offers spectacular views of the lake and beach, a terrace for relaxing and a play and relaxation area for children and adults. It's the perfect place for a good cold beer, lunch, dinner or a glass of wine with friends.

This is where some of the resort's attractions are held, including beach volleyball, kayaking, Adventure Golf and Pentanque tournaments.

Recycling

Santa Rosalía has a waste separation system to collect paper and cardboard, packaging, plastic, glass and other types of waste. Little things mean a lot when it comes to changing the world. In our sales office we use cardboard cups and we make sure to reduce paper consumption and recycle it. This policy is especially important at the Beach Club.

Energy efficiency

LED lighting is installed throughout the facility, controlled by sundials and low energy consumption. Energy efficiency isn't just about saving money. This is an essential measure that will help save our planet.
