For sale: a modern villa located in Vera Playa on the Almería coast, just approximately 200 metres from a sandy beach. The property offers 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a private swimming pool, a spacious solarium and a private plot, making it an excellent choice both as a holiday home and for year-round living.

The villa is designed on one level and offers approximately 90 m² of built area. It sits on a private plot of around 244 m². The bright Mediterranean-style architecture is complemented by natural materials and large glazed areas, providing plenty of natural light and creating a pleasant connection between the interior and outdoor spaces.

The heart of the home is an open-plan living area combining the lounge, dining area and modern kitchen. The kitchen is fitted with cabinetry and a Silestone-style worktop, while additional LED lighting enhances the contemporary character of the interior. The villa offers three comfortable bedrooms and two bathrooms.

Outside, there is a private swimming pool measuring approximately 6 × 3 m, equipped with lighting and a decorative waterfall feature. The terrace and garden create a private relaxation area, ideal for enjoying the sunny climate of southern Spain throughout most of the year.

Another major advantage of the property is the private rooftop solarium, which can be arranged as a chill-out area, sunbathing space or summer lounge. Pre-installation is also included for a jacuzzi and outdoor kitchen. A private parking space is provided within the plot.

Location

The villa is located in Vera Playa, a popular coastal area in the province of Almería, just approximately 200 m from the beach and the Mediterranean Sea. The area is known for its long sandy beaches, peaceful atmosphere and excellent climate. Restaurants, bars, shops and other everyday services are located within easy reach.

The location allows you to enjoy the tranquillity of Vera’s coastal area while offering convenient access to nearby towns and attractions along the Almería coastline. It is an attractive option for buyers looking for a modern home with a private pool within a short walk of the beach.
