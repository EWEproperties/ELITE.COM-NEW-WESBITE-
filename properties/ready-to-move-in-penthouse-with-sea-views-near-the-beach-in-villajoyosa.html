Location

Villajoyosa is a hidden pearl on Spain's Costa Blanca.

This colorful fishing village is located between the popular town of Benidorm and El Campello, approximately 30 minutes' drive from Alicante Airport.

Villajoyosa seems to literally hang on the rocky seaside cliffs. Combined with the charming architecture, it gives an amazing impression of a picturesque coast. What's more, the name of the town itself promises a nice time. Villajoyosa or La Vila Joiosa literally means a joyful town. This is indeed the impression it gives, with its colorful houses in the city center.

Urban legend has it that the buildings were painted in bold colors to help fishermen find their way home. Today they certainly attract crowds of tourists and are an inspiration for many photographers and artists.

In recent years, this charming town has become a desirable place for people looking for a second home in a quiet area.

Villajoyosa has a lot to offer, from the wide sandy beach to the narrow and colorful streets of the historic center.

Villajoyosa has been associated with chocolate since the 19th century. This is where the popular Spanish chocolate factory Valor is located. The Valor, Pérez and Clavileño museums are must-sees. There you will learn everything about the chocolate production process and its history in this area. Trips led by knowledgeable guides are most often combined with chocolate tasting.

During the holiday season, Villajoyosa hosts various cultural events. It is worth visiting the village in July, when the famous Moors and Christians festival takes place here. The main attraction is the reconstruction of the battle from 1538, as well as concerts, performances and shows.

Villajoyosa contains 3 km of beaches with golden sand and views of the endless clear sea. The most beautiful of them are playa El Paraíso, Xarco and la Caleta. Here you will find both larger tourist resorts and hidden, mysterious corners perfect for peaceful relaxation. The seaside panorama, Mediterranean climate and soft, hot sand perfectly create an atmosphere of complete relaxation. Near the beaches you will find regional restaurants and cafes offering fresh Mediterranean delicacies.

Villajoyosa delights tourists with its idyllic, cheerful atmosphere and colorful houses. There is no shortage of attractions for people looking for experiences and interesting history and culture. Just walking along the seaside provides stunning views and impressions that are hard to forget.
