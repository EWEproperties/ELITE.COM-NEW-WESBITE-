Location

Ciudad Quesada is located in the south of the Costa Blanca, just 8 km north of the main town, Torrevieja. The nearby towns are Rojales and San Fulgencio. You can get to Ciudad Quesada directly from the nearby airport, where you will find quite a few connections. The city is only a 30-minute drive from Alicante Airport, which is well connected to major cities in Europe. Come and experience the best of the southern Costa Blanca in this vibrant city.

Beaches

The city is within easy walking distance of golden, sandy beaches, including El Moncayo en Guerdanar del Segura, and is surrounded by magnificent nature parks and picturesque landscapes.

La Mata Lagoon

Nature lovers and lovers of tranquillity like nothing more than to grab a rucksack, put on sports or comfortable shoes and head out. In Ciudad Quesada, you can go hiking in amazing places. There are many hiking trails, but one of the most engaging for nature lovers and those who enjoy birdwatching is a visit to the La Mata lagoon.

Golf

Golf enthusiasts can enjoy the prestigious La Marquesa Golf Club, which offers breathtaking views of the surrounding area. Meanwhile, nature lovers can visit the nearby salt lakes of Torrevieja, home to many species of birds. For those who want to .water.

History

The town has a rich history dating back to the early 18th century and has become a vibrant community with a unique mix of Spanish, British and other European cultures. In Ciudad Quesada you can experience the city's rural past in its charming old town, while the modern and cosmopolitan area around the city centre reflects its more recent development.

A ritual of beauty and relaxation

Another plan not to miss in Ciudad Quesada is to head to the spa and let someone take care of your wellbeing for the morning, afternoon or whole day. At the spa of Hotel la Laguna, you can enjoy the so-called ‘Circuit of the Senses’, through which you will gain access to a wellness area full of contrasts of heat and cold, a relaxation area and a jacuzzi with salt water, where you will work on all your senses through different areas: sight, smell, taste, touch and hearing. To relieve tension, you can enjoy a massage with bamboo or chocolate or take advantage of anti-stress therapy. You'll leave feeling as relaxed as ever. The Spa at Hotel La Laguna has an important advantage: you don't have to be a hotel guest to use its facilities.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja del Montcaio, about 8 km by road, around 13 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio Público Poeta Miguel Hernández, about 3 km, around 8 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 12 km, around 14 minutes by car.
International school: Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen, about 1.7 km.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud de Rojales, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Quirón/USP Hospital San Jaime, about 13 km by road, around 18 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is QuickSave, about 1.5 km away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 58 km by road, around 45 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 68 km, around 50 minutes by car.
