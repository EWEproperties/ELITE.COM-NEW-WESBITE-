Location

Nestled amidst the rolling hills of the Vega Baja region, Guardamar del Segura stands as a beacon of natural beauty and historical significance. Easily accessible via the N-332 and the AP-7 autopista, it's a mere stone's throw away from renowned golf courses like La Marquesa and La Finca. And with Alicante International Airport just 25 minutes away, you're always connected to the world.

Discover Playa Los Viveros

Tucked away in Guardamar del Segura, Playa Los Viveros is a serene escape. Stretching over 1,300 meters and flanked by the region's signature nurseries, this beach is a testament to Guardamar's agricultural heritage. But it's not just about the sands; nearby lies the Rábita Califal, a relic from the Andalusian era. The beach's Blue Flag status is a nod to its pristine environment and commitment to safety.

What truly sets Playa Los Viveros apart is its untouched natural beauty. Set within a protected zone, the beach is a haven of lush greenery, characteristic of the region. The gentle shoals and calm waves make it perfect for a relaxed day out. And the journey to the beach? A tranquil walk through pine forests, with wooden pathways easing the descent onto the sandy shores.

But Guardamar is more than just beaches. Dive into a rich tapestry of history, from Phoenician to Roman eras. Explore the vibrant gastronomy, a blend of traditional flavors and modern twists. For the thrill-seekers, Puerto Marina de las Dunas offers a plethora of water sports, from sailing to windsurfing.

In essence, Guardamar del Segura is the Costa Blanca - South's crown jewel. A place where nature's tranquility meets modern amenities, offering a life that's truly the best of both worlds.
