Location

Mil Palmeras is a seaside neighbourhood located in the municipality of Pilar de la Horadada, in the south of the province of Alicante. The resort is located in the south-east of Spain and borders Murcia (about 15 minutes away). Alicante, on the other hand, is approximately 40 minutes' drive away. Mil Palmeras literally means ‘a thousand palm trees’.

Pilar de la Horadada has everything you need to enjoy the delights of the sea, pine forests and the Spanish sun. The coastline of Pilar de la Hordada is more than 4 kilometres long along very diverse scenery. You will encounter wide beaches with fine white sand, small stone wall shelves suspended above the water and small and intimate hidden coves with golden sand.

The sea here is generally calm, the beaches are safe and there are no steep slopes, allowing even the smallest children to swim safely. In addition, the clear water and excellent visibility invites divers to observe the beauty of the aquatic life.

The cleanliness of the beaches in Pilar de la Hordada has been awarded the distinction of a European Union Blue Flag, which provides guarantees of a healthy environment.

Inland, various interesting excursions can be made near the towns of Sierra and Escalona. There are two signposted hiking trails, along the Rio Seco riverbed with a length of 19 kilometres, definitely worth a visit. One route is 4 kilometres long, while the other is 10 kilometres stretching from the natural area of Pilar de Campoverde to the mouth of Mil Palmeras beach.

Not far from the town of Pilar de la Horadada is also the 18-hole golf course at Lo Romero. Professionals in the field as well as novice players will find a good match there.

Gastronomy has a privileged place in the culture of this beautiful town. Among the typical dishes of Pilar de la Hordada are potatoes with meat stew, meatballs, rabbit in garlic and Gachas Migas (a floury, garlicky pancake) as well as great rice dishes and soups. Sweets and cakes also deserve a mention here, confectionery such as the meringue cake called ‘’milhojas‘’ or the almond sweets called ‘’cordiales‘’ being the most popular.
