Elite World Estates presents an exceptional collection of modern villas located within one of the most exclusive residential resorts in Spain – Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort in the Murcia region. This is an ideal opportunity for those seeking a luxury home in a secure environment, close to the sea and surrounded by outstanding leisure facilities.

The development consists of 18 elegant detached villas designed with comfort, privacy, and contemporary living in mind. Each property offers approximately 142 m² of living space, 3 spacious bedrooms, and 3 bathrooms. The functional layout includes one bedroom and one bathroom on the ground floor, ensuring maximum convenience for everyday living.

Large floor-to-ceiling windows flood the interiors with natural light, creating a seamless connection between the indoor and outdoor living areas. Each villa features a private swimming pool, landscaped garden, spacious terraces, private parking on the plot, and an impressive rooftop solarium that can be enjoyed throughout the year.

An additional highlight of the property is the fully finished 68 m² basement with natural daylight, which can be used as a home cinema, gym, office, or additional leisure area.

Residents enjoy access to all the amenities of Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort – a unique residential complex covering approximately 700,000 m². At the heart of the resort lies the spectacular Crystal Lagoons lake with crystal-clear waters and white sandy beaches. Residents can also enjoy a Beach Club with restaurant, sports facilities, cycling and walking paths, children's playgrounds, relaxation areas, and numerous recreational activities for the whole family.

The resort is located just 4 km from the beaches of the Mar Menor in Los Alcázares and approximately 30 km from Murcia International Airport, providing excellent connectivity and convenient travel throughout the year.

Location

Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort is one of the most prestigious residential resorts in the Murcia region, located just 4 km from the beaches of the Mar Menor, 35 km from the city of Murcia, and 22 km from Murcia International Airport. It is a unique destination designed for those seeking luxury, security, and an exceptional Mediterranean lifestyle.

The resort extends across approximately 700,000 m² and features a stunning crystal-clear lagoon with sandy beaches, extensive green areas, walking and cycling trails, and numerous sports and leisure facilities. Residents can enjoy kayaking, paddleboarding, sports courts, and a variety of family-friendly attractions. The entire resort is gated and benefits from 24-hour security.

Beach Club

At the heart of the resort is the exclusive Beach Club, featuring a restaurant, panoramic terraces, and relaxation areas for both children and adults. It is the perfect place to meet family and friends, enjoy lunch or dinner, or relax with a glass of wine overlooking the lagoon.

Sustainability

Santa Rosalía is also committed to sustainability and energy efficiency. The resort features a waste separation and recycling system, while energy-efficient LED lighting helps reduce energy consumption and protect the environment.
