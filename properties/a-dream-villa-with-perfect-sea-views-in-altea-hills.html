Location

Altea in Spain is considered one of the most beautiful towns. Beautiful beaches and promenades, whitewashed houses with colourful pots, narrow cobbled streets, small craft shops, ice cream parlours and outdoor restaurants are not all that this picturesque town has to offer.

Altea is a 10-minute drive north of Benidorm (about 10 km), about 1 hour from Alicante and is one of the most memorable seaside towns on the Costa Blanca, retaining its unmistakable Spanish charm despite growing tourism and popularity. Altea is located directly on the sea, in the middle of a beautiful bay and is surrounded by hilly mountains. The name comes from the Moorish Althaya‘, meaning health for all’.

The town centre is a treasure trove of shops, art galleries, restaurants and small whitewashed buildings decorated with flowers - all waiting to be discovered on the winding and cobbled medieval streets.

The old town of Altea is preserved in a typically Spanish, one might say Andalusian, atmosphere. The whitewashed houses provide a blank canvas for the Mediterranean flower beds. No wonder Altea attracts artists and photographers from all over the world who want to experience the city's charm, incredible light and natural beauty. From the cobbled streets, stunning views of the sea and mountains, to the historic buildings.

Countless steps of narrow, charming streets lead from the coast to Altea's viewpoint. In the 16th century, the upper part of the old town of Altea was built to protect the town from attacks. The castle was built on the current Plaza de la Iglesia and the upper part of the town was surrounded by a wall with three gates. Today, only two of these gates exist - Vell Portal and Portal Nou.

The icon of the town is the famous Virgin de Consuelo church with its blue dome on top - it is truly remarkable, and the view from the top over Altea bay with the mountains in the background is simply spectacular!

Altea's location at the foot of the 1100-metre-high Sierra Bernia mountain, with its highest peak of 1129 metres, and the 1500 Sierra Aitana to the west, creates a magical' climate in the area. The mountain protects from cold winds from inland. The mountainside lies like a green oasis down to the fertile lowlands and the sea. The river that flows through the town and thanks to which it is located here is called Algar, which means healing river.

Altea and other towns in the north are among the greenest and most fertile areas of the entire Costa Blanca. Oranges and many exotic fruits are grown here.
