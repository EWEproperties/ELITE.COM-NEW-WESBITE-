Location

Picturesque Algorfa, located in the Vega Baja del Segura region, has a rich history dating back to Roman times. The town's name comes from the Arabic word Al-Gorfa‘, meaning cave’, as the area was known for its numerous natural caves. Today, Algorfa is known for its citrus fruit and vegetable production.

Algorfa is home to many attractions that will appeal to residents of all ages. Here are just a few of the most important ones and:

La Finca golf course

If you are a fan of golf, La Finca Golf Course is a must-visit destination. This championship golf course is set in a beautiful natural setting, with stunning views of the Vega Baja countryside. The course has been designed to challenge golfers of all levels, and there are many facilities including a pro shop and restaurant.

Algorfa Market

For a taste of the local culture, be sure to visit the Algorfa Market, which takes place every Wednesday. Here you will find a wide selection of goods including fresh local produce, clothing and souvenirs. This is the perfect place to buy a unique gift or sample the delicious local cuisine.

Plaza de España

Plaza de España is the main square in Algorfa and is the perfect place to soak up the local atmosphere. Here you'll find a beautiful fountain, plenty of places to sit and many restaurants and bars. It's the perfect place to relax with a drink and enjoy the local culture.
