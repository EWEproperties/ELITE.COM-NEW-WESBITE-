This modern residential complex is located in the city of Torrevieja in a privileged natural environment, very close to the Natural Park of Lagunas de La Mata and Laguna Rosa de Torrevieja, as well as the natural area of Lo Albentosa, ideal for cycling and hiking routes. A 10-minute drive separates you from your new home to the beautiful La Mata beach with the Molino del Agua park and Cabo Cervera beach. Be one of the first to visit this unique project with us and book your dream apartment in sunny Spain.

This perfectly located residence offers quality living close to all the services you need. It is located next to the International Auditorium of Torrevieja and the Quirón Hospital. Road connections are excellent: direct access to the N-332 road, a few minutes from the A-7 motorway and 40 minutes by car from Alicante-Elche airport along with the AVE high-speed train in Alicante. In the area you will find a diverse range of services: Habaneras shopping center, sports center, restaurants, shops, schools, institutes and even the largest market in the area, which takes place every Friday. It is an ideal location both for everyday life and for a holiday stay.

The residential complex will be very extensive and you will find modern apartments, bungalows, semi-detached houses and independent villas. All developments will have access to private common areas with many amenities. You can choose from investments with 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms and 2 and 3 bathrooms. The apartments and bungalows on the ground floor will have a garden, and those on the top floor will have a rooftop solarium, all very bright and with large spaces. Additionally, the villas have a large garden with a private swimming pool. All houses will have outdoor and/or underground parking with or without storage space, depending on type. Most houses face southeast and southwest, with some bungalows facing northwest. Everything designed so that you can enjoy the proximity of the azure sea and beautiful pink and green lagoons.

This wonderful residence will have over 15,200 m2 of common space, perfect for a healthy lifestyle. Here you will find 2 swimming pools for adults, 2 swimming pools for children, 2 heated jacuzzis, a children's playground, petanque courts and landscaped green areas. Every day you will be able to enjoy the beauty of Mediterranean gardens with a wide range of aromatic plants, palm trees, lakes, fountains and paths.

Location

Torrevieja, located about 40 kilometers from Alicante airport, is a city that never ceases to amaze, being a very desirable destination for many residents and foreigners. Misto is located on the Costa Blanca, in the province of Alicante and is very well connected to the rest of the country.

One of the reasons why it is worth living or relaxing in Torrevieja is the modernity of this city. This is mainly because it is a city in the development phase, due to its location and the large number of people who visit and live there. It is therefore a modern city, equipped with everything necessary to welcome tourists and residents. Moreover, multiculturalism reigns here, and you can hear different languages on the streets.

Torrevieja is famous for its beautiful beaches, where golden sand meets crystal clear water. Some of the best beaches in the area include La Mata Beach, Playa del Cura and Los Locos Beach. Soak up the sun, take a refreshing swim in the Mediterranean Sea or engage in a variety of water sports. The beautiful promenades along these beaches are perfect for leisurely strolls, full of charming cafes, restaurants and shops.

Beyond the beaches, Torrevieja offers a wealth of activities and attractions to suit all interests. Immerse yourself in local culture and history by visiting the Torrevieja Museum, where you can view fascinating exhibits showcasing the city's heritage. Take a leisurely boat trip from Marina de Torrevieja and admire the stunning coastline from a different perspective.

Golf enthusiasts will delight in the abundance of unique golf courses in the area. Some of the best golf courses near Torrevieja include Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, Villamartin Golf Club and La Marquesa Golf. Enjoy challenging fairways, scenic landscapes and world-class facilities while indulging in your favorite pastime.

When it comes to dining, Torrevieja offers a diverse culinary scene. Discover a wide range of restaurants, from traditional Spanish tapas bars to international cuisine, to suit every palate and preference. Don't forget to taste the fresh seafood dishes, a local specialty not to be missed.

For shopping enthusiasts, Habaneras and Ozone Leisure Center shopping centers are popular. These modern complexes are home to a variety of shops, boutiques and entertainment venues, providing a great shopping experience.

Torrevieja also boasts a nightlife, with numerous bars, clubs and live music venues. Whether you're looking for a relaxing evening with friends or an exciting night of dancing, Torrevieja's nightlife has something for everyone.
