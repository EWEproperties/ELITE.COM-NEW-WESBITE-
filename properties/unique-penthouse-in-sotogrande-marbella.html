Nestled in a unique natural setting, the 2, 3 and 4-bedroom residences are exceptional in scale. The scheme exudes nature and provides an outstanding quality of life.

Three façades and large floor-to-ceiling windows make for light-flooded homes throughout.

The spacious living room—which is two-storey in the case of duplex penthouses—is the core of the

property and, along with the terraces and gardens, succeeds in capturing the natural environment and

bringing the outside in.

Offering superb proportions, the residences range from 200 sq m for a two-bedroom to 330 sq m for a

four-bedroom. For their part, the three-bedroom penthouses are exceptional in scale and atmosphere

and start at 270 sq m.

The apartments are designed to create a unique living experience.

The generous sized terraces— ranging from 40 sq m to 260 sq m—and expansive private

gardens of up to 155 sq m that create a constant and ever-present dialogue between the interior and exterior spaces

Residents will benefit from two outdoor swimming pools, with separate swimming and leisure areas and an impressive waterfall in between. Also available to residents will be a large beach area to enjoy and take delight in Sotogrande’s year-round sunshine.

An imposing and welcoming 700 sq m lobby, which perfectly reflects the spirit of the scheme,

houses the reception and a fully-equipped coworking area of generous proportions,

which is an alternative to the spacious residences for welcoming guests,

working or studying.
