Location

Finestrat is a charming town in the province of Alicante, located on Spain’s renowned Costa Blanca. It lies just 8 km from the vibrant city of Benidorm and approximately 40 km from Alicante International Airport. Known for its breathtaking mountain scenery, pristine beaches, and rich cultural heritage, Finestrat is an ideal destination for those seeking relaxation and unforgettable experiences.

The town is situated on the slopes of the majestic Puig Campana mountain, offering spectacular views of the mountains, coastline, and Mediterranean Sea. The historic center preserves a traditional Moorish character, with colorful houses and narrow cobbled streets. Meanwhile, Cala Finestrat has developed into a highly sought-after coastal area that is often considered a natural extension of Benidorm.

Cala Finestrat

Cala Finestrat is a charming seaside resort located just three kilometres from bustling Benidorm. The area offers a wide range of amenities, including restaurants, bars, shops, and essential services. Everything else you may need can be found in nearby Benidorm, which is easily accessible by bus or taxi.

La Cala Beach is generally quieter than the beaches of Benidorm, making it an ideal destination for families and those seeking a more relaxed atmosphere. The beach features a beautiful stretch of golden sand, safe swimming conditions, and crystal-clear waters. Along the promenade, visitors can enjoy a selection of charming restaurants, while during the summer months an artisan market takes place in the afternoons.

The area is perfect for families and couples looking for a relaxing beach destination while remaining close to numerous attractions and amenities. Nearby attractions include the theme parks Terra Mítica and Terra Natura, the Aqualandia water park, and the marine and exotic animal park Mundomar, offering entertainment for all ages.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the nearby Villaitana Golf and Puig Campana Golf courses, providing excellent facilities and spectacular surroundings.

Both Finestrat and Cala Finestrat offer a peaceful environment compared to some of the more lively neighbouring resorts. Visitors and residents can enjoy a wide selection of local bars and restaurants serving excellent cuisine in a relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere.
