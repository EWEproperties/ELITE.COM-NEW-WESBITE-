We present a new project of 6 villas in avant-garde style perfectly located in the first line of the El Campanario golf course, on the New Golden Mile in Estepona.

The villas are located in the exclusive neighbourhood of "El Campanario", a few metres from the clubhouse with an exquisite restaurant, tennis and paddle courts, spa, gym, swimming pool and golf course. Just a few minutes' drive from your new home you can find: Puerto Banús, Estepona or Benahavís. In the area you will find the best international schools, restaurants and 1 km from the beaches of Puro Beach or Salduna. Málaga airport is only a 30-minute drive away.

Each house has been designed by top architects, creating a modern and contemporary Mediterranean architecture with lots of light, space and abundant geometry. Located on a plot of 471 m2, it offers its residents a life of the highest standard.

The villas consist of a ground floor, a first floor and a bright basement with a large cinema room, a gym, a sauna and a spa area with access to a Japanese patio with a waterfall pool for swimming and cooling off.

On the main floor there is an unspectacular entrance hall, a kitchen open to the living room from which you can access the pool, covered veranda and edgeless pool. Bedroom and bathroom. Outside there is covered parking.

Upstairs there are two bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms, the master with dressing room, both with access to the terrace.

In the basement there is a bedroom, gym, spa, bathroom, equipment room, large media room and patio with Japanese garden.

The villa is equipped with hot and cold air conditioning, underfloor heating on the main floor, floor-to-ceiling windows, floating wooden staircase, fully equipped kitchen, suspended fireplace, gym, sauna and whirlpool.

There is an outdoor seating area on the spacious terrace by the pool. An outdoor area with a magnificent garden, plenty of space for sun loungers, patio furniture and a wonderful view of the golf course. It will be straightforwardly the perfect place for you and your loved ones to relax and enjoy the Mediterranean climate that the sunny Costa Blanca offers.

Location

Picturesque Estepona is a hidden gem located along the magnificent Costa del Sol. With an idyllic Mediterranean landscape, Estepona offers a wonderful combination of picturesque beaches, rich cultural heritage and a vibrant atmosphere. Let's take a look at what this town has to offer.

Estepona is conveniently located a short distance from Malaga Airport, making it easily accessible for travellers. The airport is approximately 80 kilometres away, ensuring a smooth and comfortable journey for visitors.

Estepona boasts a collection of breathtaking beaches that will delight beach lovers and sun seekers. Some of the best beaches in the area are Playa del Cristo, a sheltered cove with calm waters and a relaxing atmosphere, and Playa de la Rada, a vast strip of golden sand offering a wide range of water sports. Playa del Saladillo is another fantastic option, known for its natural beauty and tranquil surroundings.

Estepona is also home to many attractions and activities. Be sure to visit the historic Old Town, known as Casco Antiguo. Wander through the charming cobbled streets decorated with colourful pots and explore the city's rich cultural heritage. Admire the traditional Andalusian architecture, visit the Church of San Luis and enjoy the vibrant atmosphere of the bustling squares and lively markets.

Estepona also offers a variety of outdoor activities for nature lovers. The nearby Sierra Bermeja mountain range provides excellent hiking, allowing you to immerse yourself in stunning natural landscapes and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the coast. Golf enthusiasts will be delighted with the numerous world-class courses in the area, where they can play against a backdrop of lush greens and azure skies.

When it comes to restaurants and entertainment, Estepona boasts a vibrant culinary scene. Explore the charming streets with traditional tapas bars and seafood restaurants, where you can enjoy the fresh and flavoursome cuisine of the region. The marina area, Puerto Deportivo, is a popular dining destination and offers a wide selection of restaurants and bars overlooking the yachts and sparkling Mediterranean Sea.
