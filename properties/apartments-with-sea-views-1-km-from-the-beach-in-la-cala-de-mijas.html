An exceptional residential development located in La Cala de Mijas on the Costa del Sol, in an attractive setting that combines proximity to the coast with an elevated position offering beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea. Contemporary architecture, spacious terraces and extensive communal areas create a comfortable environment for both permanent living and holidays in Spain.

The complex offers 1, 2 and 3-bedroom apartments with a variety of sizes and layouts. Available properties range from approximately 68 m² to over 124 m², while terraces, depending on the apartment, can exceed 120 m². Large windows provide plenty of natural light and create a seamless connection between the living areas and outdoor spaces.

One of the development’s main highlights is its spacious terraces with sea views, designed as an extension of the living area. They provide an ideal setting for relaxing, dining outdoors and enjoying the sunny Costa del Sol climate for most of the year.

The kitchens are delivered fully fitted with cabinetry, compact quartz worktops and Bosch appliances or similar, including a hob, oven, refrigerator, dishwasher, extractor hood and washing machine. The apartments are also equipped with ducted air conditioning with a heat pump, an aerothermal system for domestic hot water and mechanical ventilation.

Another major advantage is the extensive range of communal facilities. Residents will have access to an adult swimming pool with saltwater chlorination, a fully equipped gym, spa, coworking area, social room and restrooms. This allows residents to enjoy facilities for sport, relaxation and work without leaving the residential complex.

The buildings are equipped with lifts suitable for people with reduced mobility. Depending on the property type, underground parking spaces and storage rooms are also available, while the garage includes pre-installation for electric vehicle charging.

An additional benefit is the A energy efficiency rating, achieved through thermal insulation, energy-efficient solutions and the use of renewable energy for domestic hot water.

Estimated completion date: Q4 2028.

Location

The development is situated in an attractive, elevated area of La Cala de Mijas on the Costa del Sol, combining peaceful surroundings and Mediterranean Sea views with convenient access to the beaches and a wide range of everyday services. Shops, supermarkets, restaurants, shopping centres, medical facilities and numerous golf courses can all be found nearby. According to the developer, many of these amenities can be reached within approximately 7 minutes on foot.

The beach is located less than 2 km away, while convenient access to the A-7 and AP-7 provides excellent connections along the Costa del Sol. Fuengirola can be reached in approximately 8 minutes by car, the historic centre of Marbella in around 15 minutes, and Puerto Banús and Málaga-Costa del Sol International Airport in approximately 20 minutes. Málaga María Zambrano high-speed AVE train station is around 30 minutes away by car.
