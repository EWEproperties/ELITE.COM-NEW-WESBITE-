Location

Benijófar is a charming Spanish town located in the heart of the southern Costa Blanca. In recent years, it has become one of the most desirable locations for both permanent living and holiday home ownership. The town combines authentic Mediterranean charm with excellent infrastructure and close proximity to the region’s main attractions.

The beautiful sandy beaches of Guardamar del Segura and La Mata are only a short drive away, while Alicante International Airport can be reached in approximately 30 minutes. Benijófar has preserved its traditional Spanish character while offering a wide selection of restaurants, cafés, shops, and services necessary for comfortable year-round living.

The town is surrounded by picturesque citrus groves and green landscapes, while the Segura River flows nearby, providing excellent opportunities for walking, cycling, and outdoor activities. Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to renowned golf courses such as La Finca Golf Resort and La Marquesa Golf.

Another advantage is the traditional weekly street market, where residents and visitors can purchase fresh local products and experience authentic Spanish culture. Benijófar is the perfect destination for those seeking tranquility, a high quality of life, and easy access to beaches, golf courses, and all the attractions of the Costa Blanca.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja de La Roqueta, about 12 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio Público San Jaime, about 300 m.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 8 km, around 10 minutes by car.
International school: Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen, about 3 km, around 6 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud de Benijófar, about 400 m away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Quirón/USP Hospital San Jaime, about 16 km by road, around 19 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Bazar Chino, about 500 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 54 km by road, around 40 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 68 km, around 50 minutes by car.
