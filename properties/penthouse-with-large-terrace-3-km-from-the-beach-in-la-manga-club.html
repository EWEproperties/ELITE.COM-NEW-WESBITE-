Elite World Estates presents an exceptional 3-bedroom penthouse located in the prestigious La Manga Club in the Murcia region. The property stands out for its impressive outdoor space, including a spacious terrace and a large private rooftop solarium, ideal for relaxing and enjoying the Mediterranean climate throughout the year.

The apartment offers a bright and functional interior with 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. At the heart of the property is a spacious living and dining area with an open-plan kitchen. Large windows provide plenty of natural light and create a seamless connection between the living area and the terrace.

The penthouses offer approximately 117–120 m² of built area, terraces of around 22 m² and impressive private solariums measuring approximately 117–123 m². This generous rooftop space offers plenty of possibilities, from creating a comfortable lounge and sunbathing area to an outdoor dining space for enjoying meals with family and friends.

An additional advantage is the pre-installation for a jacuzzi/spa on the private solarium, allowing the rooftop area to be transformed into an exclusive relaxation space. The solariums will also feature artificial grass in designated areas according to the project design.

Residents will have access to a communal swimming pool for adults and children, landscaped green areas and a pétanque area. The property also includes a private parking space with pre-installation for electric vehicle charging.

The penthouse will be delivered to a high standard. The kitchen will be fitted with modern cabinetry and appliances, including a ceramic hob, oven, microwave and extractor hood, while the worktop will be finished in compact quartz such as Silestone or a similar material.

The specifications also include ducted hot and cold air conditioning, an aerothermal system, electric shutters, fitted wardrobes and fully finished bathrooms with shower screens. Apartments with private solariums will also be equipped with photovoltaic panels.

The estimated completion date is February 2028.

The property is located in the heart of La Manga Club, one of the most prestigious sports and leisure resorts in the Murcia region. Surrounded by mountains, green areas and golf courses, it offers a peaceful atmosphere and an exceptional setting for enjoying the Mediterranean lifestyle throughout the year.

La Manga Club offers extensive sports and leisure facilities, including three 18-hole golf courses, tennis and padel facilities and professional sports facilities. The resort also offers restaurants, shops, hotel facilities and wellness services.

Another major advantage of the location is its proximity to both the Mediterranean Sea and the Mar Menor, providing easy access to beautiful beaches and attractive coastal towns. It is an excellent location both for holidays and for comfortable year-round living.
