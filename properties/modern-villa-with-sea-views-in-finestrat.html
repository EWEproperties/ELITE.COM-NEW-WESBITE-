Location

Finestrat, located in the province of Alicante, in the Valencian Community of Spain, is a charming and picturesque town that offers an unforgettable holiday experience. Known for its stunning mountain views, pristine beaches, and rich cultural heritage, Finestrat is the perfect destination for anyone seeking a relaxing and memorable place.

Finestrat is situated just 8 km from the popular and bustling city of Benidorm and 40 km from Alicante and its international airport. The town is nestled on the slopes of the Puig Campana mountain, offering stunning views of the mountains, coast, and Mediterranean Sea. Finestrat has a typical Moorish feel, with colorfully painted houses and narrow cobbled streets.

Cala Finestrat

Cala Finestrat is a seaside resort located just three kilometers from bustling Benidorm. The resort offers all the necessary facilities, including a vast selection of restaurants, bars, and shops. La Cala beach is a clean strip of golden sand, ideal for children and safe for swimming. There are several lovely restaurants along the promenade, and an arts and crafts market in the afternoon during the summer months.

Activities

The area around Finestrat offers plenty of activities for families and couples alike. Nearby, you will find theme parks like Terre Mitica and Terra Natura, the Aqualandia water park, and the Mundomar marine and exotic animal park, all of which offer fun for the whole family. If you prefer to spend your holiday golfing, the nearby Real Faula Golf Club offers two excellent golf courses.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala de Finestrat, about 5 km by road, around 8 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP Balcó de Finestrat (about 700 m) and CEIP el Murtal (about 5 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Cala de Benidorm, about 6 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Comarcal de la Marina Baixa, about 6 km by road, around 12 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, about 4 km away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 62 km by road, around 45 minutes by car.
