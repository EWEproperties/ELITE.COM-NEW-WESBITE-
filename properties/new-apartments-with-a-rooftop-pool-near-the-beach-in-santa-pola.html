New apartments for sale in Santa Pola on the Costa Blanca, approximately 800 metres from the beach and 900 metres from the port. The development is located in a well-established part of the town, surrounded by supermarkets, restaurants, public transport and all essential everyday services.

The residential complex consists of 48 apartments with 1, 2 or 3 bedrooms, distributed across two sections of the building. Properties are available on the ground floor and on the first, second and third floors, all with private balconies or terraces. The top floor features penthouses with exceptionally spacious terraces, offering more than 120 m² of outdoor space in selected units.

One of the main highlights of the development is the communal swimming pool located on the rooftop, surrounded by a solarium area and landscaped spaces. It is an ideal place to relax, sunbathe and enjoy the Mediterranean climate throughout much of the year. The development also includes two underground levels with parking spaces and storage rooms.

Parking spaces and storage rooms can be purchased separately. According to the current price list, parking spaces start from approximately €15,000, while storage rooms start from approximately €6,000.

The apartments have been designed in a bright, contemporary style. The specifications include large-format porcelain flooring, aluminium exterior carpentry with thermal break, double glazing, reinforced entrance doors and practical fitted wardrobes in the bedrooms.

The kitchens will be fitted with cabinetry, a COMPAC-type or similar worktop, sink and extractor hood, as well as pre-installation for household appliances. The apartments also include pre-installation for ducted air conditioning, an aerothermal system for hot water and an individual photovoltaic solution designed to help reduce energy consumption.

Estimated completion: September 2028.

Location

The location is very convenient both for year-round living and as a holiday property. The development is situated in a well-established area of Santa Pola, allowing residents to enjoy the town’s infrastructure without needing to rely on a car every day. The beach is approximately 800 m away and Santa Pola port around 900 m away, placing the promenade, marina and town centre within easy walking distance.

Santa Pola is a lively coastal town throughout the year and remains active outside the summer season. It offers a full range of services including shops, supermarkets, restaurants, schools, healthcare facilities and public transport. The town is known for its wide sandy beaches, long seafront promenade, port and pleasant Mediterranean climate.

Another important advantage is the proximity to everyday services. The bus station is approx

Imately 50 m from the building, the health centre around 400 m away, while supermarkets, cafés and restaurants can be found in the immediate surroundings.

Santa Pola also enjoys excellent connections with Alicante. Alicante-Elche Airport is approximately 15 km away, while Alicante city centre is around 20 km away. This makes the location particularly convenient for people who travel frequently between Spain and other European countries.

The combination of proximity to the sea, wide beaches, urban infrastructure and easy access to Alicante makes this an attractive location for year-round living, a holiday apartment or seasonal stays.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja de Llevant, about 1.3 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP Ramón Cuesta (about 500 m) and CEIP José Garnero (about 600 m).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Santa Pola, about 600 m away. The nearest hospital is Cruz Roja, about 10 km by road, around 17 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is ALDI, about 600 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 20 km by road, around 39 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 97 km, around 70 minutes by car.
