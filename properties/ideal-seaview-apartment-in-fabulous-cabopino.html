Location

The Cabopino resort is located on the Costa del Sol in Málaga, between Marbella (14 km west) and Fuengirola (14 km east). The Cabopino area in Marbella brings together everything you could wish for. To the north, pine forests and golf courses such as Cabopino golf. To the south, the protected natural area of Dunas de Artola, which ends at Cabopino beach, a beautiful and isolated stretch of golden sand about 1,200 metres long, next to which is the marina of the same name.

Cabopino beach

This is the first beach in the municipality which, being a little further away from the centre, maintains a moderate occupancy rate, retaining a different atmosphere to the other beaches in Marbella. In addition, the beach has become very popular in recent years as it has a very wide sandy area and exceptionally clear water. In addition, Cabopino beach has many services to help you have a comfortable and fun time. You will find showers and toilets, bars, beach bars and restaurants that offer a wide selection of local and international cuisine. You will also have the opportunity to rent sun loungers and umbrellas. You'll also see various places where you can hire pedal boats, boats and jet skis. You can even go on organised excursions to see dolphins.

Cabopino marina

The history of the Cabopino marina began in 1985, when its construction began. Opened in 1987, the marina has grown to 550 berths and is home to many restaurants, bars, flats and penthouses. It has a total of 85 outlets, including excellent restaurants, bars and brasseries, as well as gift shops, jewellery, clothing and much more. There is plenty to do in this marina, with a golf course and diving lessons nearby. There is even an animal sanctuary where you can meet rescued animals up close!

The dunes at Artoli

The dunes at Artoli are a protected natural park, so no building is allowed on them. As a result, they have remained relatively untouched and rare species can thrive. You can take long walks along the trails that run through the area, or you can get off the road and let your imagination run wild. One of the best ways to see the area is by bike, although there are not many trails if you want to get away from other people. You can also go horse riding in this area.
