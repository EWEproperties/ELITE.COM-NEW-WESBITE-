For sale: a modern 3-bedroom apartment located in a new residential development in a peaceful area of Mijas on the Costa del Sol, between La Cala de Mijas and Fuengirola. The elevated position of the complex and its green surroundings provide beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea and the mountains. The beach is approximately 4 km from the development, as is the nearest golf course, while Málaga Airport is around 28 km away.

The apartment has been designed with space, natural light and comfortable living in mind. It offers 3 bedrooms, a bright living area with an open-plan kitchen and a spacious terrace, which creates a natural extension of the interior. Large windows provide plenty of natural light and open the living area towards the surrounding landscape.

The southeast and southwest orientation of the buildings allows residents to enjoy the sun for much of the day. The outdoor space is ideal for relaxing, dining outdoors and making the most of the mild Costa del Sol climate. The development also offers 3-bedroom penthouses with large private solariums.

The interiors are finished in a bright, contemporary style. The open-plan kitchen is fully equipped and includes a Silestone worktop and Bosch or similar appliances, including an oven, microwave, induction hob, extractor hood, integrated refrigerator, dishwasher and washing machine. The apartment also features built-in wardrobes and fully finished bathrooms with shower screens.

The property will be equipped with hot and cold air conditioning, an aerothermal system for domestic hot water, mechanical ventilation, energy-efficient LED lighting and fibre-optic installation. Double glazing and electric shutters in the bedrooms are also included. The buildings have been designed with high energy efficiency in mind, with an expected energy rating of A.

Residents will have access to a communal swimming pool with a sunbathing area and landscaped gardens. The complex will also be equipped with lifts. A parking space is included with the apartment, and the parking spaces are prepared for the possible installation of electric vehicle charging points.

3-bedroom apartments in the second phase are currently available from €455,000 + VAT, with completion scheduled for October 2027.

Location – Mijas, Costa del Sol

The location between La Cala de Mijas and Fuengirola combines peaceful surroundings with easy access to the beaches and all the amenities of the Costa del Sol. Nearby you will find golf courses, recreational areas, Miramar Shopping Centre and numerous restaurants and leisure facilities.

La Cala de Mijas, Fuengirola and Marbella offer a wide selection of restaurants, shops and attractions, while excellent road connections make it easy to explore the Costa del Sol. Málaga and its international airport can be reached in approximately 30 minutes.
