Location

The city of Marbella is the symbol of the Costa del Sol. The resort, synonymous with prestige and luxury, attracts the attention of investors and tourists from around the world, and its inseparable element are spectacular properties worth even over PLN 100 million!

Marbella, thanks to the actions taken by influential people like Ricardo Soriano or Prince Alfonso of Hohenlohe-Langenburg, has become the favorite holiday destination of the cream of society from Spain and Europe.

Today, politicians, athletes, musicians and screen stars have their properties here, and Marbella itself attracts crowds of tourists from around the world. This is confirmed by e.g. results of the plebiscite organized by the popular Belgian travel portal, which awarded Marbella the title of Best European Destinations 2022'.

Currently, the most prestigious and thus the most desirable locations include:

Golden Mile - The Golden Mile is one of the most beautiful and luxurious avenues in Spain, and is also a symbol of Marbella. Legendary hotels and stunning properties are located here, including a scaled-down replica of the White House, built by the late King of Saudi Arabia. The Golden Mile is an avenue of over 6 kilometers long, lined with classic and modern villas surrounded by lush vegetation. It is an ideal place to invest capital, especially in times of rampant inflation.

La Zagaleta - you can't pass by this complex indifferently. It is situated in an extraordinary scenery - by the lake, between the sea and the mountains. On a huge area that could accommodate up to 4,000. real estate, there are only 400 residences, inhabited, among others, by by people on the front pages of newspapers. The excellent security of La Zagaleta, however, guarantees them full discretion and the comfort of a peaceful holiday at home on the Costa del Sol.

Puerto Banús - one of the most luxurious marinas in Spain, located between Marbella and San Pedro Alcantara. People who have managed to buy a property in Puerto Banús are undoubtedly the lucky ones! Here we will find designer boutiques from around the world, the best restaurants, spectacular yachts and a Salvador Dali sculpture!

When visiting this luxurious resort, you must visit the Parque de Alameda - they are said to be the green lungs of the city. Dating back to the 18th century, Alameda Park occupies an area of 20,000 m2, which is 20% of the area of the entire city center. Alameda Park is a place rich in various species of plants. The ones that attract the most attention are the spreading Ficus Pandurata and Pinus Pinae.

Nature lovers will also be happy to visit the Bonsai Museum.

It is the first museum in Spain dedicated to bonsai trees. It was founded in 1992, and its collection includes the most valuable bonsai specimens in Europe, some of which are 200, 300 and even 400 years old! A visit to the Museo del Bonsai is a great opportunity to calm down and commune among the most beautiful works created by nature. In addition to the permanent exhibition, the museum also organizes educational workshops to better understand the art of caring for bonsai trees.

Regardless of how you like to spend your free time, Marbella will certainly meet your expectations. There are 26 km of beaches waiting for sunbathers. For gourmets of good cuisine - great restaurants, prestigious restaurants offering cuisine from around the world. Those who like active holidays can try their hand at windsurfing, sailing or golf. Regarding the latter, it is worth mentioning that around the city there are unparalleled golf courses of excellent quality, also adapted to play at night (they are specially illuminated for this purpose).

If you like mountain climbing, you cannot miss the La Concha mountain rising majestically above the city. It measures 1,200 meters above sea level, and the best trail to reach it can be found in the town of Istán, located on the other side of the mountain. The trip is not the easiest, but all possible difficulties are compensated by the amazing view that awaits us at the top! It is hard to disagree with the statement that this is the most beautiful panorama of the city of Marbella. However, for even greater mountain challenges, the Sierra Nevada mountain range is recommended.

The most popular and, according to many people, also the best beaches in Marbela are: Playa de Guadalmina, Playa de Lindavista, Playa de San Pedro de Alcántara, Cortijo Blanco, Playa de Nueva Andalucía, Rio Verde - Playa de Puerto Banús, Playa de El Ancón, Nagüeles , Fontanilla, Playa del Faro.
