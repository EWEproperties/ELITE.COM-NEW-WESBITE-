If you dream of a Mediterranean life in a great location, near the prestigious city of Marbella, you have just found an investment that meets your expectations. Admire the azure of the Mediterranean Sea from the terrace of your future luxury apartment and watch the beautiful sunrises and sunsets. Allow yourself the luxury of living in close proximity to nature without giving up all amenities and extensive infrastructure.

These new elite apartments are located near the beach, between Playa Arroro Vaquero, 2.4 km away, and Playa Seghers, 3.4 km away. The center of Estepona is only five kilometers away. Your new home is in a privileged position, very close to shops and restaurants and just a five minute drive (4 km) from Estepona Marina. It has a simple access route to the A7 motorway, which provides easy access to the ports of Sotogrande and Puerto Banus (Marbella). It also has two international airports less than an hour away; Malaga and Gibraltar airports are 50 and 45 minutes away respectively.

Your future home is one of 84 apartments in a gated community in Urbanizacion la Gaspara in Estepona. All apartments are very spacious and bright. They have a living area from 110.35 to 152.75 m2, and spacious terraces from 29 to 80 m2. They have two to four bedrooms and two to three bathrooms. Thanks to the large glazing, there will be a lot of light and a sense of space in the apartments, and the residents will feel close to the seaside nature.

During the construction of luxury apartments, the creators of the project took care to use materials and measures that cause only low CO2 emissions. For example, hot water is produced using aerothermal energy.

The open-plan living area is designed to enjoy the sun and Mediterranean surroundings all day long. We will find here an open, fully equipped kitchen combined with a dining room and a comfortable living room. We have direct access to a large terrace, ideal for relaxing and admiring the seaside views. It is also possible to buy a storage room and a parking space.

The private common area has been designed so that you can fully enjoy life in close proximity to nature without giving up luxury. Here you will find a swimming pool, deckchairs, a gym with fitness equipment and a playground for children. Certainly, everyone will find here the perfect space for rest, fun or relaxation in the company of loved ones.

Location

Estepona is a quiet town in the province of Málaga and one of the tourist resorts of the Costa del Sol. They are characterized by typical Andalusian, low-rise buildings with narrow and patterned streets.

It is a paradise for artists and photographers.

The city is surrounded by the Sierra Bermeja mountains that dominate the entire landscape around Estepona.

It is one of the few places in the world where the mountain range is located only 10 km from the sea.

You can see how beautiful the views are from the top by setting off along the marked trails

for hiking.

One of the most interesting attractions in this picturesque city is the orchidarium. Orchid collection here

is the largest in Spain and is also among the largest in Europe! Even if you are not a fan of botanical gardens, this place is worth seeing if only because of the riot of colors and smells spreading here. A real feast for the senses!

In Estepona, there is the Playa de la Rada beach, which is over 2,600 meters long. We have many amenities in the area, such as: showers, umbrellas, toilets, pedal boats, playgrounds and trampolines for children. For smaller and bigger hunger, seaside restaurants and bars (Spanish: chiringuitos) serving local delicacies and drinks are recommended.
