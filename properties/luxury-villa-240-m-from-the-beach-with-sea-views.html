Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional luxury villa located in the prestigious La Fustera area of Benissa on the northern Costa Blanca. This exclusive property has been designed for the most discerning buyers seeking a contemporary home in Spain that combines breathtaking Mediterranean Sea views, outstanding construction quality, and an exceptional location just a few steps from the beach.

The residence offers approximately 621 m² of built area and is set on a spacious 1,250 m² plot. This impressive three-storey villa features contemporary architecture, elegant lines, natural materials, and spectacular floor-to-ceiling windows that flood the interiors with natural light while framing panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea and the coastline of Moraira.

The main floor features a spacious open-plan living and dining area alongside a modern kitchen fully equipped with Siemens appliances. The living space flows seamlessly onto a large covered terrace leading to a spectacular infinity swimming pool and an elegant outdoor lounge area complete with a summer kitchen and barbecue area. This level also includes two comfortable bedrooms, one of which benefits from an en-suite bathroom, as well as a guest toilet.

The upper floor has been dedicated to the private sleeping quarters and comprises two spacious bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom and direct access to panoramic terraces where you can enjoy unforgettable Mediterranean sunrises.

The lower level has been designed as an exclusive wellness and entertainment area. It includes an indoor swimming pool, sauna, fully equipped gym, home cinema, games room, laundry room, and an additional guest toilet. This outstanding leisure area offers the comfort and luxury of a five-star resort throughout the year.

The villa has been finished using the highest quality materials and state-of-the-art technology. Features include Technal aluminium windows with safety glazing, underfloor heating powered by an aerothermal heat pump, ducted air conditioning for both heating and cooling, photovoltaic solar panels, LED lighting, electric shutters, a pre-installed alarm and surveillance system, and an automatic entrance gate. The beautifully landscaped Mediterranean garden perfectly complements the elegant architecture of this exceptional residence.

This property is an outstanding opportunity for buyers seeking a luxury residence on the Costa Blanca, an exclusive holiday home, or a prestigious investment in one of the most desirable locations on the Spanish Mediterranean coast. Its privileged location just 240 metres from La Fustera Beach, spectacular panoramic sea views, and premium specifications make it one of the finest luxury properties currently available in this part of the Costa Blanca.

Location

The villa is located in the prestigious area of La Fustera, Benissa, on the northern Costa Blanca, between Moraira and Calpe. This is one of the region's most exclusive residential areas, renowned for its breathtaking Mediterranean views, elegant luxury villas, and peaceful surroundings. The location perfectly combines an authentic Mediterranean lifestyle with easy access to beaches, renowned restaurants, marinas, and all the services required for comfortable year-round living.

The beautiful Blue Flag beach of La Fustera is just 240 metres away. This charming sandy and pebly beach is known for its crystal-clear waters and excellent facilities. A picturesque coastal ecological path runs along the shoreline towards Calpe, offering spectacular views of dramatic cliffs, hidden coves, and the Mediterranean Sea. Restaurants, cafés, a supermarket, and everyday amenities are all within easy reach.

The elegant seaside town of Moraira, with its marina, boutique shops, and excellent Mediterranean restaurants, is only a few minutes away by car. Likewise, the vibrant town of Calpe offers beautiful sandy beaches, a lively fishing port, and the iconic Peñón de Ifach Natural Park. Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of the prestigious Ifach Golf Club, while lovers of water sports can enjoy sailing, diving, paddleboarding, and many other outdoor activities.

The nearby AP-7 motorway and N-332 coastal road provide excellent connections to both Alicante and Valencia International Airports, each approximately one hour away by car. This exceptional location is ideal both for permanent residence and as a luxury holiday home or premium investment property on the Costa Blanca.
