Discover the latest real estate opportunity in Torrevieja—an exclusive residential complex situated on one of the city's most prominent avenues. This prime location offers residents unparalleled access, with just a 300-meter walk to some of the finest beaches and proximity to a wide range of essential services. Enjoy the convenience of nearby restaurants, cafes, supermarkets, and cultural centers, all within an easy 8-minute stroll to the central bus station and the heart of Torrevieja.

This modern residential complex in Torrevieja features 56 thoughtfully designed homes, offering a range of 1, 2, and 3-bedroom options with 2 bathrooms. The property includes a variety of layouts, such as duplexes and penthouses with spacious solariums, providing large terraces and stunning panoramic sea views. Ground-floor units boast private terraces with direct access to the communal areas, perfect for seamless indoor-outdoor living.

Constructed with the highest quality materials, each home is designed for low maintenance and sustainability, adhering strictly to environmental standards and the latest building regulations. The architecture emphasizes uniform quality throughout, ensuring that every residence, regardless of its position in the complex, offers a luxurious living experience.

A standout feature of the complex is a large structural opening facing one of Torrevieja’s main avenues, allowing natural light and sea breezes to flow through the communal spaces. This design element also serves as a grand communal balcony, ideal for enjoying the year-round sunshine typical of the Levantine coast.

Each home benefits from a dual orientation, ensuring excellent cross ventilation and ample natural light, with most units facing east and south to maximize sunlight throughout the day.

The complex is accessible from two streets and is equipped with two elevators that service both the residences and the underground garage. Residents can enjoy a beautifully designed communal pool with a waterfall, surrounded by lush vegetation that creates a tranquil, natural environment. Additional amenities include a communal toilet, a storage room for pool furniture during the winter months, and a community room designed for meetings or individual work in a bright, relaxed atmosphere.

For added convenience, optional underground parking is available, offering 57 car spaces, 2 motorcycle spaces, and 3 versatile spaces equipped with pre-installed charging points for electric vehicles.

This residential complex combines innovative design with practical features, making it an ideal choice for those seeking a modern, comfortable, and eco-friendly living environment in one of Torrevieja’s most desirable locations.
