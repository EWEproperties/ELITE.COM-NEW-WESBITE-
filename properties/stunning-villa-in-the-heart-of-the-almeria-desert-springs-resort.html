Introducing a new project located in the heart of the Almeria Desert Springs Resort, which offers a unique combination of luxury, natural beauty and adventure. Visit these new luxury villas with us, set in the mesmerising desert landscape of the Almanzora Valley, the resort is the ultimate destination for those seeking an extraordinary experience in one of Europe's sunniest regions.

With an award-winning golf course, world-class amenities and an exclusive selection of unique properties. Conveniently located a short distance from the Mediterranean coast, it offers the ideal balance between the tranquillity and privacy of an exclusive resort and easy access to the vibrant culture and breathtaking beaches of the Costa de Almería.

The properties offer magnificent views of the desert and mountains, harmonising with the natural surroundings while providing all the modern comforts and amenities one could wish for.

Located in a secluded and secure residential enclave, this modern villa is part of a housing complex where villas are carefully arranged around individually designed gardens and shared swimming pools. This design fosters an atmosphere of privacy, tranquillity and refined elegance.

This contemporary detached villa with three bedrooms and two bathrooms seamlessly combines luxury, comfort and Mediterranean charm, all on one level for easy living. The spacious ground floor layout has been carefully designed to maximise natural light and enhance open-plan living. The master bedroom has direct access to a private terrace, creating a secluded place to relax.

There is the option to build a private swimming pool.

The spacious living and dining room seamlessly flow onto the spacious terraces with pergola, as well as additional open terraces - an idyllic place for al fresco dining, relaxation and entertainment.

Built on a large plot of between 380 m² and 425 m², Buenaventura I enjoys a privileged position, offering a peaceful and private environment surrounded by the majestic Almanzora mountain range – a truly inspiring backdrop for a sophisticated and tranquil lifestyle.

Location

Desert Springs Resort and Golf Club is Europe's only international award-winning luxury family resort and desert golf course, located in Cuevas del Almanzora and Vera, in the province of Almeria, Andalusia.

You don't have to fly to Florida or the Canary Islands to enjoy the winter sun, it's right here in Almanzora!

Almanzora enjoys a true desert climate. Sunny, warm and dry in winter, hot in summer inland, but cooler on the coast and on the plateau where the Desert Springs Resort is located. It is a fact that Almanzora enjoys the warmest, sunniest and driest winter in Spain and Europe. Sun-drenched family holidays in Spain as well as golf weekends are available all year round, including a tan from September to May!

We are only a two and a half hour flight from Northern Europe and a 50-minute drive from Almeria International Airport. The international airports of Murcia and Alicante are also an affordable and easy option. A unique desert climate, stunning surroundings, sandy beaches, fantastic cuisine, countless attractions and places to visit, and the best resort in Spain with accommodation in apartments, duplexes and villas.

There is something for everyone here... relaxation, world-class golf, family fun, beaches, water sports, outdoor living, sports facilities, quality of life and the true Spanish way of life. The unhurried pace of daily life, festivities, sociability and emphasis on outdoor living create a relaxing atmosphere.
