This is an outstanding opportunity for those looking for a modern apartment in Spain that combines high-quality construction, a peaceful setting, and beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea and surrounding mountains. An additional advantage is the highly competitive price, rarely found in such a prestigious location.

The development comprises 123 contemporary apartments with 1, 2, and 3-bedroom layouts, designed according to the latest architectural trends. Built on gently elevated terrain, the complex offers excellent natural light, enhanced privacy, and stunning panoramic views of the Mediterranean coastline and the surrounding mountains.

The spacious interiors feature an open-plan layout that seamlessly connects the living room, dining area, and kitchen. Large floor-to-ceiling windows maximize natural light, while every apartment benefits from a generous south-facing terrace, allowing residents to enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle all year round. Selected ground-floor apartments include private gardens, while the penthouses feature private rooftop solariums with the option to install a jacuzzi or a small private pool.

Thanks to its elevated position, the development enjoys exceptional sea and mountain views. The most spectacular panoramas can be enjoyed from the penthouses and their private rooftop terraces, creating the perfect space for relaxation and outdoor living.

Residents will have access to beautifully designed communal areas, including a large swimming pool, landscaped Mediterranean gardens, relaxation zones, and a children's playground. Each apartment also comes with a private parking space or garage.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its excellent location. The complex is situated just 4–5 km from the beautiful beaches of El Albir, which can be reached in approximately 10 minutes by car. Restaurants, shops, international schools, golf courses, marinas, and a wide range of leisure facilities are all within easy reach, making it an ideal destination for both permanent living and holiday escapes.

This is an exceptional opportunity for buyers seeking a modern property in one of the most prestigious areas of Costa Blanca. The combination of high-quality construction, an outstanding location, panoramic views, and competitive pricing makes this development an excellent choice for both homebuyers and investors looking for strong long-term value.

Location

The development is located in Alfaz del Pi, one of the greenest and most desirable towns on the northern Costa Blanca. This charming Mediterranean town has long attracted residents looking for a peaceful lifestyle, offering an excellent year-round climate, beautiful natural surroundings, and a full range of everyday amenities. It is an ideal destination for both permanent relocation and property investment.

The complex is only 4–5 km from the sandy beaches of El Albir, which can be reached in approximately 10 minutes by car. The picturesque town of Altea, famous for its charming old town, whitewashed streets, excellent restaurants, and scenic seafront promenade, is also just a short drive away. The vibrant city of Benidorm, with its shopping centres, golf courses, marinas, entertainment, and beaches, is easily accessible within minutes.

Residents will benefit from excellent local infrastructure, including supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, international schools, medical centres, sports facilities, and numerous walking and cycling routes. Nature lovers will appreciate the proximity of the Sierra Helada Natural Park, offering spectacular hiking trails with breathtaking panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea.

The development also enjoys excellent transport connections via the AP-7 motorway and the N-332 coastal road, providing easy access to the entire Costa Blanca. Alicante International Airport is approximately 45–50 minutes away by car, offering regular flights to major cities across Europe throughout the year.

The combination of a peaceful Mediterranean setting, close proximity to the beach, and excellent infrastructure makes Alfaz del Pi one of the most sought-after locations on the Costa Blanca, perfect for year-round living, a holiday home, or a secure property investment.
