New apartments for sale in a peaceful area of San Miguel de Salinas on the Costa Blanca. The residential complex offers modern 2-bedroom properties, with the possibility of creating a 3rd bedroom, as well as attractive communal areas with a swimming pool, spa area, children’s playground and landscaped gardens.

Buyers can choose between ground-floor apartments with a private garden and upper-floor properties with a spacious private solarium. Ground-floor apartments feature gardens finished with artificial grass, while the upper-floor homes offer a large private rooftop space, ideal for relaxing and enjoying the Mediterranean climate. Each property also includes a parking space within the residential complex.

The interiors have been designed in a modern and functional style. The kitchen is fitted with contemporary cabinetry with a choice of finishes and a quartz worktop. The apartments also feature built-in wardrobes with sliding doors, fully lined interiors and drawers.

For added comfort, the properties are equipped with ducted inverter air conditioning, an aerothermal system for domestic hot water, LED lighting and modern aluminium exterior carpentry with thermal insulation and Climalit-type glazing.

The bathrooms include bathroom furniture, mirrors and shower screens. In the 3-bedroom properties, the price also includes a kitchen appliance package with a fridge-freezer, oven, dishwasher, ceramic hob, extractor hood and microwave.

One of the main advantages of the development is its well-maintained communal area with a swimming pool, spa, children’s area and extensive landscaped gardens with palm trees and Mediterranean vegetation.

Currently available are, among others, upper-floor apartments of approximately 73.5 m² with a terrace/solarium of around 67.5 m², as well as a ground-floor apartment with a private garden of approximately 59 m².

The earliest completion dates are scheduled for December 2026, with further properties due for completion in April 2027.

Location

San Miguel de Salinas is a peaceful town located in the south of the Costa Blanca, surrounded by Mediterranean scenery and within a short distance of the Orihuela Costa coastline. It is a good choice for those looking for a quieter place to live while still enjoying easy access to beaches, golf courses and larger towns in the region.

The centre of San Miguel de Salinas offers shops, supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, pharmacies, banks and other everyday services. The area is well connected with Torrevieja and popular coastal destinations on Orihuela Costa, including Punta Prima, Playa Flamenca, La Zenia and Campoamor.

Golf enthusiasts will find several well-known courses nearby, including Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, Campoamor Golf and Las Ramblas Golf. Zenia Boulevard, one of the largest shopping centres in the southern Costa Blanca, is also within easy reach.

The nearest beaches of Orihuela Costa offer wide sandy stretches, picturesque coves and promenades lined with restaurants and bars. The location also provides convenient access to Torrevieja, known for its seafront promenade, marina and the pink lagoons of the Lagunas de La Mata y Torrevieja Natural Park.

San Miguel de Salinas is therefore an attractive option both for year-round living and as a holiday base on the Costa Blanca — away from the busiest resorts, yet still close to the sea, services, golf and the main attractions of the region.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala Mosca, about 16 km by road, around 18 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are British School (about 1.8 km) and Colegio Público Gloria Fuertes (about 4 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud San Miguel de Salinas, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 12 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, about 6 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 51 km by road, around 40 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 70 km, around 55 minutes by car.
