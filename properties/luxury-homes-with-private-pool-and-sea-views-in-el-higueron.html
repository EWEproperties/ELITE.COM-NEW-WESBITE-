Elite World Estates is proud to present an exceptional residential development located in the prestigious area of El Higuerón, Fuengirola – one of the most sought-after destinations on the Costa del Sol. This exclusive project is an excellent choice for those seeking a modern home in Spain that combines premium finishes, breathtaking Mediterranean Sea views, and the comfort of living in a private gated community.

The development comprises only 25 exclusive townhouses, thoughtfully designed to offer maximum privacy while blending harmoniously with the surrounding natural landscape. The terraced layout of the development ensures that every home enjoys panoramic sea views and an abundance of natural light throughout the day.

Buyers can choose between elegant 3- and 4-bedroom homes, designed with spacious open-plan interiors. Depending on the selected property, each home features either a private garden with its own swimming pool or an impressive rooftop solarium with a private infinity pool, offering spectacular panoramic views of the Costa del Sol coastline.

The interiors have been designed with comfort and elegance in mind, featuring high ceilings, floor-to-ceiling windows, and functional layouts that maximise both space and natural light. The contemporary kitchen seamlessly connects to the bright living and dining area, which opens onto generous terraces, creating the perfect setting for relaxing or entertaining family and friends.

Every home is finished to an exceptional standard using premium-quality materials and includes underfloor heating throughout, an energy-efficient aerothermal air conditioning system, a Smart Home automation system, and a fully fitted designer kitchen equipped with high-end appliances.

Residents also benefit from exclusive communal facilities, including a swimming pool with a sun terrace, a fully equipped gym, a wellness area, coworking spaces, and beautifully landscaped gardens. An additional advantage is the close proximity to the renowned Higuerón Resort, offering a luxury spa, sports club, fine dining restaurants, and a wide range of premium lifestyle services.

The development enjoys an excellent location with quick access to sandy beaches, golf courses, international schools, restaurants, shops, and Málaga International Airport. It is an outstanding opportunity both for those looking to relocate permanently to the Costa del Sol and for investors seeking a prestigious property with excellent long-term value.

Location

The development is located in the prestigious El Higuerón area, on the border of Fuengirola and Benalmádena, one of the most exclusive residential locations on the Costa del Sol. This privileged setting is renowned for its peaceful surroundings, stunning Mediterranean Sea views, and excellent infrastructure, allowing residents to enjoy an exceptional lifestyle all year round.

Within just a few minutes, you can reach beautiful sandy beaches, the seafront promenade, a wide selection of restaurants, cafés, and beach clubs. The area also offers renowned golf courses, international schools, shopping centres, private medical facilities, and the prestigious Higuerón Resort, featuring a luxury spa, sports club, tennis and padel courts, as well as award-winning restaurants, including a Michelin-starred dining experience.

Excellent transport connections provide quick access to the centres of Fuengirola, Benalmádena, Marbella, and Málaga. Málaga International Airport is approximately 15 minutes away by car, while the nearby train station offers convenient connections to the airport and the main destinations along the Costa del Sol.

El Higuerón is the perfect location for those looking for a luxury property in a peaceful setting without compromising on easy access to beaches, leisure facilities, and all essential amenities for everyday living.
