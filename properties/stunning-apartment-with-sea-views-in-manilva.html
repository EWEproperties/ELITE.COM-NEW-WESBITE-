Location

Manilva is a picturesque town located on the Costa del Sol, 97 kilometres from Malaga. The town is divided into two parts: a tourist beach resort and an urban area with beautiful white townhouses. This beautiful town is surrounded by impressive vineyards, where mainly the moscatel grape variety is grown. This variety is mainly used to produce sweet wine, which is the signature drink during the Feria de Malaga. The Manilva grapes are also used to produce delicious sultanas, which are dried in autumn.

Manilva has retained the atmosphere and style of an old Spanish village. It is situated close to the Strait of Gibraltar, making it a geographically and culturally rich area. Here you can experience a peaceful holiday, away from the hustle and bustle of the big cities. The nearest airport is 41 kilometres from the town of Manilva.

The undeniable attraction of the town and its surroundings are the beaches - some of the best on the Costa del Sol. Almost 8 kilometres of sandy areas are available here. Some beaches are rocky and are surrounded by coves, while others are wide and sandy. The rocky beaches are located in Punta Chullera and the sandy beaches are located next to Castillo, the port of Puerto del la Duquesa and the town of Sabinillas. You can swim, snorkel, fish and enjoy water sports in the waters of the surrounding beaches.

In addition to the beaches and marinas, Manilva offers tourists an interesting historical and cultural heritage. So you will find many things to see and do in Manilva. In Manilva, you can also see the castle of La Duquesa, several churches and archaeological remains on display at the Municipal Archaeological Museum.

Frequently asked questions
