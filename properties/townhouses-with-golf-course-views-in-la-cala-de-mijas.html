A new residential development located in the exceptional surroundings of La Cala Golf Resort in Mijas on the Costa del Sol, between the mountains and the Mediterranean coast. Its peaceful setting, surrounded by greenery and extensive golf courses, offers beautiful panoramic views and an ideal environment for both holidays and year-round living. The centre of La Cala de Mijas and its beaches can be reached in approximately 7 minutes by car, while Málaga Airport is around 30 minutes away.

The complex offers modern 3-bedroom townhouses, designed in a bright, contemporary style. The spacious open-plan living area combines the lounge, dining area and kitchen. Large sliding glass doors provide direct access to the terrace, creating a natural connection between the interior, outdoor spaces and surrounding landscape. The homes also feature 2 full bathrooms, including one en-suite, as well as an additional guest toilet.

One of the main highlights of the properties is the large terraces with panoramic views over the golf course, valley and surrounding hills. Sizes and layouts vary depending on the selected property, allowing buyers to choose a home that best suits their needs. Each townhouse also has a private garage at basement level with direct internal access to the property.

The kitchens are fully equipped with Silestone worktops and appliances, including an oven, microwave, induction hob, extractor fan and washing machine, as well as an integrated refrigerator and dishwasher. The bedrooms feature fitted wardrobes, while large sliding windows in the living room provide plenty of natural light and open the interiors towards the views.

The homes are equipped with ducted hot and cold air conditioning, an aerothermal system for domestic hot water, mechanical ventilation and LED lighting. High standards of insulation and energy efficiency contribute to an A energy efficiency rating.

Residents have access to a gated communal area with a swimming pool, sunbathing area and landscaped gardens. Another major advantage is the extensive range of facilities available within the resort, including three golf courses, a golf academy, a hotel with spa, restaurants and sports facilities such as tennis, padel and squash courts.

The first phase is scheduled for completion in November 2026, while the following phase is expected to be completed in August 2027.

The development is located within La Cala Golf Resort in Mijas, in one of the greenest and most peaceful areas of the Costa del Sol. Surrounded by extensive golf courses, rolling hills and Mediterranean nature, the setting offers beautiful panoramic views as well as a sense of space and privacy.

Despite its peaceful surroundings, the properties are only a short drive from the coast. La Cala de Mijas and its sandy beaches can be reached in approximately 7 minutes by car. The town offers a wide selection of restaurants, shops, cafés and a pleasant seafront promenade.

Another major advantage of the location is the resort's excellent infrastructure, including three golf courses, a golf academy, a hotel with spa, restaurants and sports facilities, including tennis, padel and squash courts.
