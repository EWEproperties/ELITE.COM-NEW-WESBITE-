Elite World Estates presents an exceptional residential development located in the prestigious Altos de Los Monteros area of Marbella – one of the most exclusive locations on the Costa del Sol. This is an outstanding opportunity for buyers seeking a modern apartment in Spain that combines premium-quality finishes, spectacular Mediterranean Sea views, and the comfort of living surrounded by nature, just a few minutes from Marbella's city centre and beautiful beaches. This exclusive residential complex offers elegant apartments and duplex penthouses with 2, 3 and 4 bedrooms.

The spacious interiors have been designed according to the latest architectural trends, featuring large floor-to-ceiling windows that provide abundant natural light and create a seamless connection between the living area and the generous terraces. Most properties enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea and the green hills surrounding Marbella. The contemporary architecture blends harmoniously with the natural landscape, while high-quality construction and energy-efficient solutions ensure year-round comfort. Functional layouts and carefully selected premium finishing materials have been designed to satisfy even the most demanding buyers.

Residents can enjoy an impressive range of resort-style amenities, including an outdoor swimming pool with a sun terrace, a heated indoor pool, a modern fitness centre, a luxurious spa and wellness area, an elegant pool bar, coworking spaces and beautifully landscaped Mediterranean gardens, creating the perfect environment for relaxation throughout the year. The location perfectly combines tranquillity with easy access to everything the Costa del Sol has to offer. Marbella's sandy beaches, renowned restaurants, luxury boutiques, prestigious golf courses and the famous Puerto Banús marina are all approximately 10 minutes away. Excellent access to the AP-7 motorway also provides quick connections to Málaga International Airport and the rest of the Costa del Sol.

Location

The city of Marbella is a seaside resort synonymous with prestige and luxury. You will easily notice this both when visiting the luxurious marina of Puerto Banús and when going to the boutiques of global brands such as Gucci, Luis Vuitton or Prada. This elite city has been attracting the attention of investors and tourists from all over the world for many years, and its inseparable element are spectacular estates worth over PLN 100 million.

Currently, the most prestigious and therefore most desirable locations include:

Golden Mile The Golden Mile is one of the most beautiful and luxurious avenues in Spain, which is also the symbol of Marbella. Here there are legendary hotels and dazzling properties, including: a scaled-down replica of the White House, built by the late king of Saudi Arabia. The Golden Mile is an over 6-kilometer long avenue lined with classic and modern villas surrounded by lush vegetation. It is an ideal place to invest capital, especially in times of rampant inflation.

La Zagaleta You can't ignore this complex. It is located in an extraordinary setting by the lake, between the sea and the mountains. On a huge area that could accommodate up to 4,000 people. real estate, there are only 400 residences, inhabited, among others, by by people from the front pages of newspapers. However, La Zagaleta's excellent security guarantees full discretion and the comfort of a peaceful holiday at home on the Costa del Sol.

Puerto Banús one of the most luxurious marinas in Spain, located between Marbella and San Pedro Alcantara. People who managed to buy a property in Puerto Banús are undoubtedly real lucky people! Here you will find designer boutiques from all over the world, the best restaurants, spectacular yachts and a sculpture by Salvador Dalí!

Nature fans will also love visiting the Bonsai Museum.

It is the first museum in Spain dedicated to bonsai trees. It was founded in 1992, and its collection includes the most valuable bonsai specimens in Europe, some of which are 200, 300 or even 400 years old! A visit to the Museo del Bonsai is a great opportunity to relax and commune among the most beautiful works created by nature. In addition to the permanent exhibition, the museum also organizes educational workshops to better understand the art of caring for bonsai trees.

No matter how you like to spend your free time, Marbella will certainly meet your expectations. There are 26 km of beaches for beachgoers. For lovers of good cuisine, there are wonderful restaurants, prestigious restaurants offering cuisine from all over the world. Those who like active recreation can try their hand at windsurfing, sailing or golf. Regarding the latter, it is worth mentioning that around the city there are unrivaled, excellent quality golf courses, also suitable for playing at night (they are specially illuminated for this purpose).

The most popular, and according to many people also the best, beaches in Marbelia are: Playa de Guadalmina, Playa de Lindavista, Playa de San Pedro de Alcántara, Cortijo Blanco, Playa de Nueva Andalucía, Rio Verde Playa de Puerto Banús, Playa de El Ancón, Nagüeles, Fontanilla and Playa del Faro.
