This ready-to-move-in villa stands majestically between the sea and the mountains of La Nucia, close to the lively town of Benidorm.

Its modern, clean architecture, simple lines and high-quality finishes catch the eye and promise an unparalleled living experience.

The living room is a showcase of elegance and comfort, with impressive panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea through huge windows that filter natural light and sea breezes, creating a bright and cosy atmosphere that invites you to relax.

It features a study and four complete suites that guarantee privacy and sea views, two of which have their own terraces. The modern design and minimalist furniture in light colours create an oasis of tranquillity for luxurious and comfortable living.

At the heart of the villa is a large spa, a sanctuary dedicated to well-being, with a heated Jacuzzi-style pool, hydromassage and Finnish sauna, complemented by a wine cellar and a lift with stops on each level, combining luxury with convenience.

No less impressive is the outdoor area with an infinity pool and barbecue area, which is sure to become the centre of outdoor gatherings, where you can enjoy the atmosphere and sunset in the spacious garden.

Sustainability is a fundamental pillar of this villa, whose façade faces south and whose 19 photovoltaic panels ensure an energy rating of ‘A’, reflecting a commitment to environmental protection.

With all the amenities designed for the 21st century, including advanced home automation, underfloor heating and air conditioning, this villa is not just a home, but an expression of a conscious and luxurious lifestyle on the Costa Blanca.

Location

Strategically located, La Nucia allows you to enjoy the countless attractions it has to offer. Just 10 kilometres from Benidorm, in the middle of a picturesque valley covered with fruit trees and pine forests, this inland town is surrounded by the Sierra de Aitana mountain range.

Its old town consists of a network of white houses, with the parish church at its centre. It is also full of various corners offering privileged views of the surrounding area. Without losing its traditional character, La Nucia also has an important residential area made up of elegant new housing estates.

The breathtaking beauty of the surroundings makes it the perfect setting for walks or bike rides through picturesque areas such as Fuente de la Favara and Puig Campana mountain.

The town is famous throughout the region for its large flea market, held every Sunday on one of the main roads, Avenida Porvilla. The celebrations in honour of the Immaculate Conception in mid-August and the feast of San Rafael in November are also very popular.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja de Cap Blanc, about 10 km by road, around 16 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Col·legi d'Educació Infantil i Primària Sant Rafael (about 700 m) and IES la Nucia (about 700 m).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is the local public health centre (Centro de Salud), about 600 m away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Clínica Benidorm, about 9 km by road, around 15 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Spar, about 500 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 69 km by road, around 50 minutes by car.
