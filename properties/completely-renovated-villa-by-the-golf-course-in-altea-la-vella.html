Elite World Estates proudly presents this exceptional villa located in the prestigious area of Altea la Vella on the northern Costa Blanca. This elegant residence is an outstanding choice for buyers seeking a spacious home in Spain that combines a peaceful setting, beautiful Mediterranean Sea views, and direct proximity to a renowned golf course. The location offers privacy while providing easy access to beaches, Altea town centre, restaurants, marinas, and all the amenities required for comfortable year-round living.

The villa offers approximately 324 m² of built area and is set on a 1,200 m² private plot. This two-storey residence features a practical layout, expansive floor-to-ceiling windows, and bright interiors that maximise natural light while framing the surrounding landscapes. From many areas of the home, residents can enjoy panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea, the Sierra de Bernia mountains, and the charming old town of Altea.

The spacious living area features a cosy fireplace and elegant oak flooring, seamlessly connecting to the outdoor terrace and relaxation areas. The open-plan kitchen with a central island flows into the dining area and overlooks the landscaped garden and the golf course, creating an ideal setting for both everyday living and entertaining. An enclosed glazed terrace provides additional living space that can be enjoyed throughout the year.

The property offers four comfortable bedrooms, three bathrooms, and a guest toilet. Every room has been thoughtfully designed to provide comfort, functionality, generous living space, and an abundance of natural light.

Outside, the villa boasts a private swimming pool surrounded by sunny terraces, an outdoor summer kitchen with a barbecue area, and a beautifully landscaped garden equipped with an automatic irrigation system. One of the property's most distinctive features is its direct access to the practice facilities of the nearby golf course, making it an exceptional choice for golf enthusiasts.

Built in 2000, the villa underwent an extensive renovation in 2021, significantly enhancing both its comfort and energy efficiency. The renovation included the complete refurbishment of all bathrooms, two modern kitchens, elegant wooden flooring throughout the house, an enlarged garage, and the construction of a new swimming pool with upgraded pool equipment. Additional improvements include photovoltaic solar panels, a water filtration and water softening system, automatic garden irrigation, central heating, air conditioning, fibre-optic internet, and an alarm system. The property also benefits from a closed garage for two vehicles and additional storage space.

This is an outstanding opportunity for buyers looking for an elegant villa with sea views, premium features, and an exceptional location in one of Costa Blanca's most prestigious residential areas.

Location

The villa is situated in the prestigious residential area of Altea la Vella, one of the most desirable locations on the northern Costa Blanca. This exclusive setting combines Mediterranean charm, a peaceful atmosphere, and spectacular views of the sea and the Sierra de Bernia mountains. The neighbourhood is renowned for its elegant villas, lush green surroundings, and immediate proximity to the prestigious Don Cayo Golf Club, making it an ideal destination for golf lovers as well as those seeking privacy and an exceptional quality of life.

Within just a few minutes, you can reach the charming centre of Altea la Vella with its restaurants, cafés, shops, and local services. The picturesque old town of Altea, famous for its whitewashed streets, boutique shops, art galleries, and beautiful seafront promenade, is also only a short drive away. The surrounding area offers beautiful beaches and coves, marinas, scenic walking and cycling routes, and the stunning Serra Gelada Natural Park.

Excellent access to the AP-7 motorway allows quick connections to Calpe, Benidorm, and Alicante, while Alicante International Airport is approximately 45-50 minutes away by car. This is the perfect location for those wishing to enjoy the tranquillity of an exclusive residential community while remaining close to the sea, everyday amenities, and the many attractions of the northern Costa Blanca.
