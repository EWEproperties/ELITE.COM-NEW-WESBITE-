Location

San Javier is a coastal town with a lively shopping centre at its heart and a beautiful church square surrounded by bars and restaurants. It is located on the northern corner of the Mediterranean coast known as Costa Calida Murcia. San Javier is famous for its golden sandy beaches, warm climate and wide range of tourist attractions, including water sports, restaurants, bars and shops. In addition, the town has a rich history and culture, which is reflected in its historic buildings and monuments, such as the Church of San Francisco Javier and the Town Museum.

Getting there

From Murcia International Airport, you can reach the town of San Javier via the RM 19 motorway (35 km), and the journey takes about 25 minutes by car. From Alicante International Airport, you can reach it via the AR-7 motorway (87 km) - 56 minutes by car.

Golf

Costa Calida is a mecca for golf lovers. There are many high-class golf courses here. The Nicklaus complex includes six courses designed by Nicklaus Design, including La Torre, El Valle, Alhama and Mar Menor. Mar Menor is a more difficult golf course with three holes, surrounded by large lakes. La Torre is a local favourite, as it is easier and more forgiving to play. Valle Golf Resort is located in a natural valley with spectacular views of the surrounding mountains. It is a course more suited to intelligent, strategic players than those with a powerful swing.

Alhama is considered by many to be the most difficult of the Nicklaus Complex courses. It is a long, spectacular course, considered one of the best in Spain.

Beaches

San Javier has dozens of beaches in the area where you can spend every free moment. The beaches are mainly located in the Santiago de La Ribera and La Manga del Mar Menor areas, i.e. the Mar Menor spit. One of the beaches of La Manga del Mar Menor is Playa Las Antillas.

Another interesting beach in the area is Playa de Hita, which is quite unusual because it is a protected area considered to be the habitat and breeding ground of many valuable species of migratory birds. Most beaches have a good standard of facilities, with the possibility of renting water and beach equipment, as well as taking advantage of the offer of more than one restaurant or beach bar. Toilets and showers are standard at the beaches. The other beaches in the San Javier area are Playa El Castillico and Banco del Tabal, both of which have been awarded blue flags.

Protected areas

There are many beautiful protected areas in the San Javier area. La Mayor o del Barón and Isla Perdiquera are two islands of volcanic origin, which are also habitats for protected birds.

Other islands that are protected areas are La Isla Grosa and El Islote del Farallón, also of volcanic origin, which are mainly home to Roman gulls, but also other birds. All these places are accessible to tourists, but they are not as popular as standard beaches, so perhaps this is a good idea for spending your holiday in San Javier?

Isla Grosa is the perfect place for scuba divers, who come here from all over Spain.
