We present a luxurious residential residence that belongs to the most exclusive group of villas in the entire Las Colinas Golf Resort. All properties in this elite area have been designed to the highest standards of quality, exclusivity and elegance, both in the villas and in the common areas. The houses are integrated with the trees of the Mediterranean forest through spectacular garden terraces and large windows that connect the interior of the house with its natural environment.

This luxurious project presents modern architecture, with simple lines, whose life opens to the outside, providing spaciousness and functionality in each of the rooms of the house. It offers an area of 145 m2, spread over two floors. The property has 3 impressive bedrooms, 3 elegant bathrooms and a guest toilet. The developer gives you the opportunity to personalize the investment through a wide selection of materials and colors.

On the ground floor of this luxurious residence there is a bright living room with an open space for the kitchen and dining room, a large bedroom with a built-in wardrobe and an adjacent bathroom and guest toilet. The living area of this villa is a true masterpiece, boasting a contemporary design that seamlessly combines elegance and comfort. The open kitchen is fully equipped with high-end appliances and a stunning kitchen island that creates an atmosphere of sophistication and comfort, making it the perfect place for those who love cooking and entertaining guests.

On the first floor there are two further bedrooms with built-in wardrobes and two bathrooms, one of which is attached to the master bedroom. Both bedrooms have access to a large terrace of 45 m2. All bedrooms are finished with the utmost attention to detail, equipped with built-in wardrobes with modules, guaranteeing maximum comfort for each guest.

The villa is equipped with ducted air conditioning and Air Zone system, underfloor heating in the bathrooms, household appliances, LED lighting package, full KNX automation, pre-installed pool heating system.

The luxury of living in harmony with nature and access to services with high added value combined with privacy, quality and customer service make Las Colinas Golf & Country Club a place where life takes on a completely new meaning.

Location

Las Colinas Golf & Country Club is located on the Spanish Mediterranean coast, south of Alicante. A unique natural environment, carefully preserved, with exclusive houses integrated into nature with over 200,000 m2 of natural area that makes you feel like you are in another world.

The heart of Las Colinas is a championship golf course, awarded the title of "Best Golf Course in Spain" five times in the World Golf Awards competition and recognized as one of the 100 best golf courses in Europe by Golf World magazine.

Las Colinas Golf & Country Club offers accommodation, sports and recreation, including: Las Colinas Sports & Health Club, a racket club, two restaurants and a Beach Club by the beach.

The resort's unique atmosphere, unique natural environment and range of services are designed to meet the needs of its residents and guests, while promoting a healthy, Mediterranean lifestyle. Las Colinas Sports and Health Club offers a full range of services, including a gym for a day of exercise, a wonderful swimming pool and a wellness center where you can relax your mind and body.

Featuring tennis, table tennis, soccer and basketball, and a scenic trail winding through the Mediterranean forest, perfect for exploring by mountain bike or on foot, the Racquet Club offers visitors and residents a variety of sporting opportunities.

The resort's four restaurants offer a wide range of gourmet dishes, from traditional Italian cuisine at il Palco to international cuisine with Mediterranean elements at unik and Japanese, Mexican and Peruvian cuisine at ,umawa. A few minutes from Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, on the first line of La Glea beach, there is WOW Beach, a paradise for lovers of sun, sand and sea, with the best Mediterranean cuisine.
