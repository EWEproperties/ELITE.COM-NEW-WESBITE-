Location

Vera Playa is a lively coastal town offering a wide selection of restaurants, bars and shops. Its wide, sandy beaches offer plenty of opportunities for relaxation and water sports.

  • Almeria Airport: ± 1 hour by car
  • Murcia Airport: ± 1 hour and 20 minutes by car
  • A paradise with five kilometres of beaches

Those looking for a holiday to relax, sunbathe and swim at the beach have a variety of areas with fine sand and crystal clear water at their disposal. This year, Vera is also celebrating the renewal of the Q for quality for Las Marinas-Bolaga beach, a large area covering 1,350 square metres with an average width of 200 metres, along which there is an attractive promenade; as well as the blue flag for El Playazo, a beach known worldwide for offering a large area for naturism. They are joined by the beaches of Puerto Rey and Cala Marqués. All of them offer the possibility of having a drink in bars, beach bars or exclusive beach clubs.

Nature, leisure and outdoor sports

Vera is also a place that offers the opportunity to enjoy nature in its surroundings, and in particular to get to know the ecosystem of two wetlands, Salar de los Canos, next to El Playazo, which covers more than 57 hectares and is a refuge and feeding lagoon especially for water birds; and the Puerto Rey lagoon, next to the beach of the same name, where many birds migrating between Africa and Europe rest and nest.

In addition to enjoying nature, Vera offers many opportunities for various outdoor sports, from golf, on a course where you can even play croquet, to water activities such as windsurfing, paddle surfing, kitesurfing, kayaking, catamaran sailing or sailing, to rugby, beach volleyball and tennis, among others, hiking.

Heritage, culture and gastronomy

This Mediterranean municipality has more than two millennia of history, and one of the remnants of its heritage is the Cerro del Espíritu Santo (CES), the ancient Nasrid city of Barya, declared a cultural asset. Other characteristic places that are also worth visiting are the Plaza Mayor of Vera, where the unique church of Nuestra Señora de la Encarnación, with its Mudejar-Andalusian structure, is located; the Fuente de los Cuatro Caños, of Arab origin; or the Plaza de Toros, considered the oldest bullring in Almería.

The history of the municipality is also reflected in its gastronomy, through aromas, colours and flavours that reveal the Hispano-Arabic heritage of Al-Andalus, which still survives in spices, dishes and the most avant-garde recipes, such as ajo colorado or gurullos with rabbit.
