Location

Finestrat is a charming and picturesque town located in the province of Alicante, in the Valencian Community of Spain. It is only 8 km from the popular, large city of Benidorm and 40 km from Alicante and its international airport. Known for its beautiful mountain views, unspoilt beaches and rich cultural heritage, Finestrat is the ideal place for anyone looking for a relaxing and unforgettable experience. There is no shortage of exciting things to do in Finestrat.

Finestrat is located on the slope of the Puig Campana mountain, from where you have a wonderful view of the mountains, the coast and the Mediterranean Sea. The town has a typical Moorish flair with colourfully painted houses and narrow cobblestone streets. On the other hand, Cala Finestrat is becoming increasingly popular and its development has made it almost an extension of Benidorm itself.

Cala Finestrat

Cala Finestrat is a beach resort located just three kilometres from the busy Benidorm. The resort offers adequate facilities, a large selection of restaurants, bars and shops. Everything you need is available in Benidorm, which can be easily reached by bus and taxi.

La Cala beach is fairly quiet, as most people looking for a more upbeat atmosphere head for the beaches surrounding Benidorm. The beach is a clean stretch of golden sand, ideal for children and safe for swimming. There are several charming restaurants along the promenade, and in the summer months, an art and craft market is held here in the afternoons.

The place is suitable for families and couples looking for a relaxing beach resort, yet close to many attractions and amenities.

Nearby are the theme parks of Terra Mitica and Terra Natura, the Aqualandia water park and the Mundomar marine and exotic animal park, which offer fun for the whole family.

However, if you prefer to spend your holiday on one or two rounds of golf, Finestrat will be happy with the nearby Real Faula Golf Club, which offers two excellent golf courses.

Both Finestrat and Cala Finestrat are peaceful destinations compared to some of the neighbouring resorts. Both offer a selection of local bars and restaurants where you can enjoy good food and a relaxed atmosphere.
