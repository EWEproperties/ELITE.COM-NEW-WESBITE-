Location

Jávea is a town located on the Mediterranean coast of Spain, in the province of Alicante, in the Valencian Community. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, crystal clear waters and mild climate, making it a popular tourist destination. Javea is one of the most amazing places on the Costa Blanca and is often referred to as the ‘Jewel of the Costa Blanca’. Javea has a lot to offer tourists, with a wide range of activities and attractions.

Javea's beaches are among the best in the region, if not in all of Spain, and there are also several private and exotic beaches where you can relax in peace and quiet.

There are also many caves to explore in the area, and Jávea is a popular destination for water sports such as kayaking, paddleboarding, snorkelling and surfing.

Visitors can enjoy breathtaking views from the village of Jávea, which offers panoramic views of the Ibiza coastline, some 90 kilometres away. A visit to the highest point in the area, Mount Montgo, which is more than 750 metres high, is a must for tourists wanting a bird's-eye view of the town and the surrounding islands of Portichol and Ambolo.

Beach lovers can enjoy the sun and sea at popular beaches such as Arenal, Sardinella, Granadella and Barraca. In addition to swimming, sunbathing and diving in the crystal clear waters, the centre of Jávea offers visitors the opportunity to explore the charming historic townhouses, churches, atmospheric restaurants and shops, as well as the many service outlets where various foreign languages can be easily learned. Don't miss the picturesque coastal walkway, which offers a magnificent panorama of the Mediterranean.

A must-see is the town's fishing harbour, which offers fresh seafood such as prawns, squid and sardines, caught daily between Jávea and Ibiza. The nearby sailing club is another popular venue, showcasing impressive yachts and powerboats owned by enthusiasts from all over Europe.

In the sprawling Montanar district, visitors can enjoy sandy, pebbly or rocky beaches, as well as the famous chiringuitos beach bars offering exotic drinks throughout the season. The district is dotted with stately single-family homes in the traditional style, tiled and fitted with arcades, glass and concrete. The area is separated by a road from the less frequented rocky beaches.

The nearest schools are CEIP Mediterrània (about 2 km) and FPA de Xàbia (about 2.1 km).

The nearest health centre is Centre de Salut de Xàbia, about 2.8 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital la Pedrera, about 14 km by road, around 24 minutes by car.
