Location

Dolores is a small town in the southern part of the province of Vega Baja, about 50 kilometres from Alicante. It is easily accessible via the AP7 motorway and the road from Elche. If you are travelling by plane, the international airport of Alicante is only 25 minutes away from Dolores.

The open and hospitable residents, high quality of life, pleasant and healthy climate as well as the proximity to the sea (about 10 kilometres) are certainly the great advantages of the town of Dolores. All this makes this region one of the top places to live in the Mediterranean.

The proximity of the El Hondo nature reserve offers a wonderful space for relaxation and the joy of many walking paths in the company of various birds that live in the reserve (which is their refuge).

Beaches

Guardamar del Segura Beach: Located about 15 minutes by car from Dolores, Guardamar del Segura Beach is a vast beach with golden sand and crystal clear water. It has a wide range of services, such as beach bars, restaurants and playgrounds for children.

La Marina Beach: Located south of Guardamar del Segura, La Marina Beach is another nearby option. It is a quieter and less crowded beach, surrounded by natural dunes and pine forests. Here you can enjoy a more natural and relaxing environment.

Golf courses

La Marquesa Golf: This golf course is located in the nearby town of Rojales, about a 10-minute drive from Dolores. It is an 18-hole course designed for players of all levels. It offers panoramic views of the surrounding area and services such as equipment rental, golf lessons and a restaurant.

La Finca Golf & Spa Resort: Located in Algorfa, about 20 minutes' drive from Dolores, this 18-hole golf course offers a magnificent location with views of the mountainous landscape. In addition to the golf course, the resort also has a spa, a hotel and several restaurants.
