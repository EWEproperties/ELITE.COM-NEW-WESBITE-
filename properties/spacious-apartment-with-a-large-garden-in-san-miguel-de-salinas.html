A new residential development in San Miguel de Salinas, where one of the main highlights is the exceptionally spacious terraces, private gardens and generous outdoor areas. The distinctive cascading architecture of the building has been designed so that the terraces become a natural extension of the apartments, allowing residents to enjoy the Mediterranean climate throughout most of the year.

The development offers apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, available from the ground floor up to the 7th floor. The apartments have built areas ranging from approximately 65 m² to 80 m², but it is the outdoor spaces that truly distinguish this project.

The ground-floor apartments feature large terraces and private gardens. Selected properties offer gardens of more than 100 m², complemented by spacious terraces. For example, one of the 3-bedroom apartments features a 59.98 m² terrace and a 101.76 m² private garden, providing an impressive amount of space for outdoor living.

The apartments on the upper floors are equally attractive. Selected corner properties feature impressive terraces exceeding 100 m², with some reaching approximately 150 m². These generous outdoor areas provide plenty of space to create a private lounge area, an outdoor dining space or a comfortable place for sunbathing and relaxation.

The interiors have been designed in a modern and functional style. Large windows and direct access to the terraces naturally connect the indoor living areas with the outdoor spaces. Different orientations are available, allowing buyers to choose a property that best suits their individual preferences.

Another important advantage of the development is its carefully designed communal areas. At the heart of the complex is a swimming pool surrounded by landscaped green areas and spaces for relaxation. The project also includes a recreational area and a dedicated space for children, creating a welcoming environment for the whole family.

The apartments also include parking spaces.

This development is particularly attractive for buyers looking not only for a new apartment, but also for an exceptionally large private outdoor space. The spacious terraces and gardens make these properties ideal for both year-round living and holidays in Spain.

The development is scheduled for completion in September 2028.

Location

The development is located in San Miguel de Salinas, in the southern part of the Costa Blanca, in a peaceful area where residents can enjoy the Mediterranean climate while still having convenient access to everyday services.

Shops, supermarkets, restaurants, bars and other amenities can be found within easy reach. By car, you can quickly reach the popular beaches of Orihuela Costa, as well as nearby towns such as Torrevieja and Pilar de la Horadada. The area is also particularly attractive to golf enthusiasts, with several renowned golf courses located in this part of the Costa Blanca.

Good road connections make it easy to travel throughout the region and provide convenient access to Alicante-Elche Airport. The location is suitable for both holidays and year-round living.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa Cala La Mosca, about 11 km by road, around 12 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio Público Gloria Fuertes (about 300 m) and Montessori San Miguel (about 1.9 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud San Miguel de Salinas, about 200 m away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 8 km by road, around 10 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Masymas, about 300 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 56 km by road, around 40 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 67 km, around 50 minutes by car.
