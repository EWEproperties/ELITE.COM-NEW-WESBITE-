Location

Fuengirola is one of the most vibrant and popular destinations on the Costa del Sol, located just 25 km from Malaga International Airport. Once a small fishing village, it has evolved into a lively coastal town that combines authentic Andalusian charm with modern infrastructure.

The town offers a wide selection of restaurants, tapas bars, cafés, and boutiques, making it an ideal place for both relaxation and everyday living.

Beaches of Fuengirola

With over 7 kilometres of coastline, Fuengirola is one of the best places to enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle.

Some of the most popular beaches include

Playa del Castillo – ideal for water sports thanks to its calm waters

Playa de Los Boliches – a long sandy beach, perfect for families

Santa Amalia – a wide beach with crystal-clear water, popular among both locals and visitors

Playa de la Morera – a more peaceful, natural setting away from busy areas

Old Town & Andalusian Atmosphere

The heart of the town is Plaza de la Constitución, a charming square that has preserved its traditional character and serves as a hub of local life. It is especially beautiful during the festive season and summer events.

The district of Los Boliches is also worth visiting, known for its friendly atmosphere and beautiful beach. Walking through the narrow streets of the old town is a wonderful way to discover local restaurants, hidden corners, and the authentic spirit of southern Spain.
