Elite World Estates presents a modern development in the coastal area of Lo Pagán, on the Costa Cálida. It is an excellent opportunity for anyone looking for a comfortable apartment in Spain that combines contemporary architecture, high-quality finishes and a prime location, just a few minutes' walk from the Mar Menor beaches and all everyday services.

The complex offers carefully designed apartments with a spacious living and dining room, an open-plan kitchen, one or two bedrooms and one or two elegant bathrooms. Residents can enjoy an attractive communal area with a rooftop pool and solarium, with views over the surroundings and sun all year round. The building stands out for its contemporary architecture, elegant finishes and high-quality materials that suit the coastal character of the area.

Location

The development is in San Pedro del Pinatar, one of the most attractive towns on the Costa Cálida, just 600 metres from the sandy beaches of the Mar Menor. It is an ideal location both for living all year round and for buying a home to let as a holiday rental, thanks to its location, full infrastructure and wide range of leisure.

Nearby you will find restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, shops, schools, medical centres and a marina. The town is known for its beautiful seafront promenade, the famous therapeutic mud of the Mar Menor and many walking and cycling routes. Sports lovers will also enjoy the nearby golf courses, water sports and the Las Salinas y Arenales de San Pedro del Pinatar Regional Park.

Excellent road links take you to Murcia International Airport in about 35 minutes and to Alicante Airport in about 50 minutes, making this property a great choice both as a second home and as an investment with strong rental potential.
