Elite World Estates presents a new residential development located in one of the most sought-after areas of the Costa Blanca – Cala de Finestrat. This exceptional project has been designed for those who value modern comfort, proximity to the sea and the Mediterranean lifestyle. Its prime location makes it an ideal opportunity both for buyers looking for their own home in Spain and for investors seeking a property with excellent rental potential.

The modern complex consists of 52 carefully designed apartments, each offering 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Spacious interiors of approximately 80 m² have been thoughtfully planned to maximise comfort and functionality. Large windows provide plenty of natural light, while spacious covered terraces create the perfect space to relax and enjoy the Mediterranean surroundings.

Residents can enjoy excellent communal facilities, including two large swimming pools, beautifully landscaped gardens and an outdoor fitness area. The entire development has been designed to promote a comfortable and active lifestyle throughout the year.

One of the greatest advantages of this project is its outstanding location. Beautiful beaches in Cala de Finestrat and Benidorm, numerous restaurants, shops, shopping centres, golf courses and all essential services are located nearby. Excellent connections to Alicante Airport and the main attractions of the Costa Blanca further enhance the appeal of this development.

Thanks to its exceptional location, high-quality construction and extensive leisure facilities, this project represents an excellent investment opportunity and the perfect place to enjoy life by the Mediterranean Sea.

LOCATION – Villajoyosa / Finestrat

This new development is located in the stunning coastal area of Cala Villajoyosa. The privileged location lies between the charming towns of Villajoyosa and Finestrat, as well as the vibrant city of Benidorm, offering the perfect balance between tranquillity and convenience.

Residents are only 400 metres from shopping centres, restaurants and a gym, ensuring that all everyday amenities are within easy reach.

Beaches and Attractions

The beautiful Cala de Finestrat beach is located just 1 km away, offering golden sand and crystal-clear waters. It is the ideal destination for those who enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle and relaxing days by the sea.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of the prestigious Puig Campana Golf course, located only 6 km away, where they can enjoy excellent facilities and spectacular views.

Accessibility

Alicante International Airport can be reached in approximately 40 minutes by car, providing excellent connections to the rest of Europe and beyond.

Tourist Attractions

The area offers a wide range of attractions, from the picturesque old town of Villajoyosa to the vibrant nightlife of Benidorm. Popular attractions such as Mundomar, Terra Mítica and Aqualandia are also nearby, making this an ideal location for both permanent living and holiday stays.
