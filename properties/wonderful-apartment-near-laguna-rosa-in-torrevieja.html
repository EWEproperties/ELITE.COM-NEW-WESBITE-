Elite World Estates presents a new project of a unique residential complex located in the popular city of Torrevieja. We invite you on a trip to this wonderful place where your new Spanish house is being built!

The estate is being built in a privileged natural environment, very close to the Natural Park of Lagunas de La Mata and Laguna Rosa de Torrevieja, as well as the natural area of Lo Albentosa, ideal for cycling and hiking routes. A 10-minute drive separates you from your new home to the beautiful La Mata beach with the Molino del Agua park and Cabo Cervera beach. Be one of the first to visit this unique project with us and book your dream apartment in sunny Spain.

It is an ideal location both for everyday life and for a holiday stay. The residence offers high-quality living close to all the necessary services. It is located next to the International Auditorium of Torrevieja and the Quirón Hospital. Road connections are excellent: direct access to the N-332 road, a few minutes from the A-7 motorway and 40 minutes by car from Alicante-Elche airport along with the AVE high-speed train in Alicante. In the area you will find a diverse range of services: Habaneras shopping center, sports center, restaurants, shops, schools, institutes and even the largest market in the area, which takes place every Friday.

The residence will be very extensive and you will find modern apartments, bungalows, semi-detached houses and independent villas. All developments will have access to private common areas with many amenities. You can choose from investments with 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms and 2 and 3 bathrooms. The apartments and bungalows on the ground floor will have a garden, and those on the top floor will have a rooftop solarium, all very bright and with large spaces. Additionally, the villas have a large garden with a private swimming pool. All houses will have outdoor and/or underground parking with or without storage space, depending on type. Most houses face southeast and southwest, with some bungalows facing northwest. Everything designed so that you can enjoy the proximity of the azure sea and beautiful pink and green lagoons.

In this new residential complex you will feel like you are in the best hotel. This wonderful residence will have over 15,200 m² of common space, perfect for a healthy lifestyle. Here you will find 2 swimming pools for adults, 2 swimming pools for children, 2 heated jacuzzis, a children's playground, petanque courts and landscaped green areas. Every day you will be able to enjoy the beauty of Mediterranean gardens with a wide range of aromatic plants, palm trees, lakes, fountains and paths.

Location

The bustling city of Torrevieja, located on the picturesque Costa Blanca in Spain. Thanks to its convenient location and countless attractions, Torrevieja offers unforgettable experiences for both residents and visitors. Located approximately 40 kilometers from Alicante airport, Torrevieja provides easy access for international travelers. Just a short distance away, you can embark on a journey or welcome guests with ultimate comfort.

The city is famous for its beautiful beaches, some of the best beaches in the area are La Mata Beach, Playa del Cura and Los Locos Beach. Soak up the sun, take a refreshing swim in the Mediterranean Sea or engage in a variety of water sports. The beautiful promenades along these beaches are perfect for leisurely strolls, full of charming cafes, restaurants and shops.

Learn about its culture and history by visiting the Torrevieja Museum, where you can see fascinating exhibits showcasing the city's heritage. Another interesting form of entertainment is a boat trip from Marina de Torrevieja, during which you can admire the wonderful coast from a different perspective.

Golf enthusiasts will delight in the abundance of unique golf courses in the area. Some of the best golf courses near Torrevieja include Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, Villamartin Golf Club and La Marquesa Golf. Enjoy challenging fairways, scenic landscapes and world-class facilities while indulging in your favorite pastime.

Torrevieja also offers a diverse culinary offer. Discover a wide range of restaurants, from traditional Spanish tapas bars to international cuisine, to suit every palate and preference. Don't forget to taste the fresh seafood dishes, a local specialty not to be missed.

For shopping enthusiasts, Habaneras and Ozone Leisure Center shopping centers are popular. These modern complexes are home to a variety of shops, boutiques and entertainment venues, providing a great shopping experience.

Torrevieja also boasts a nightlife, with numerous bars, clubs and live music venues. Whether you're looking for a relaxing evening with friends or an exciting night of dancing, Torrevieja's nightlife has something for everyone.
