This true work of architecture is located in the Mediterranean village of Cumbre del So, a small paradise in one of the most characteristic areas on the Costa Blanca. This area combines peaceful relaxation surrounded by nature, privacy with the possibility of cultural plans offered by the large neighbouring towns.

This luxury villa is located in a privileged area with impressive views of the sea. The house is perfectly integrated into the site, embracing it and creating several levels, allowing for maximum privacy. Open terraces with an infinity pool surround the villa and provide panoramic views of the sea and mountains.

Contemporary style blends with Mediterranean tradition to create a harmonious and balanced space that encourages relaxation and enjoyment of the senses. The main garden with Mediterranean vegetation and aromatic plants connects you to nature.

This unfussy villa is divided into two levels. On the upper floor is the sleeping area, where two bedrooms share a bathroom, while the master bedroom has a spacious private bathroom and dressing room. From the bedrooms you can enjoy breathtaking views of the sea and mountains, creating a sense of peace and tranquillity that encourages relaxation.

On the ground floor, there is a living area that opens up to the outside, creating a sense of continuity and connection with the surroundings. This space consists of an entrance hall, a toilet and a tandem formed by a large living-dining room and a kitchen that are perfectly integrated. The two modules converge in an outdoor area with a terrace and pool as a connecting point.

Designed in open modules arranged in an L-shape, the villa encompasses the main garden, in which the impressive edgeless pool, which connects to the sea, stands out. An open-plan space that invites you to enjoy the sun and the Mediterranean climate, but oriented in such a way as to achieve maximum privacy.

This unique villa was created to enjoy life to the fullest and create unforgettable memories in a dream setting. A villa that invites you to dream of a life full of luxury, comfort and beauty, in a space where architecture and nature come together in perfect symphony.

Location

Cumbre del Sol is located in the town of Benitachell, between Javea (20 minutes) and Moraira (15 minutes), in the north of the province of Alicante. This luxurious district stretches over the coastal cliffs with the famous Morach beach and small coves. This idyllic residential area reveals breathtaking landscapes and a charming, laid-back charm that captivates all who venture here.

A paradise of pristine beaches awaits you, each exuding its own unique charm. Cala del Moraig tempts with crystal clear waters and dramatic cliffs, a paradise for snorkelers and divers who want to discover underwater wonders. Surrounded by rocky cliffs, Playa de Los Tiestos provides a peaceful escape for those longing for quiet days by the sea. Discover the hidden oasis of Cala Llebeig at the end of a scenic hike, a sanctuary of tranquility and untouched beauty.

For nature lovers, Cumbre del Sol offers a network of hiking trails that invite you to immerse yourself in the rugged beauty of the region. The ruta de los Acantilados' (cliff trail) reveals awe-inspiring views of the coast, leaving you in awe of the greatness of nature. Meanwhile, the Ruta del Faro' (Lighthouse Trail) leads to the iconic Cap d'Or Lighthouse, a symbol of maritime heritage offering a sense of history and wonder.

Water lovers find their happiness amidst the calm waters of the nearby bays, indulging in kayaking, paddleboarding and sailing, adding an extra layer of charm to this seaside haven.

For a distinctive perspective of the area, the Cumbre del Sol Equestrian Center offers enchanting horse riding trails, a delightful combination of nature and adventure that reveals the heart of this Mediterranean paradise.

Cumbre del Sol boasts a culinary scene as delightful as its surroundings, tempting guests with a variety of dining options. At Mirador del Mar, Mediterranean cuisine meets modernity, and guests enjoy delicious dishes against the backdrop of mesmerizing sea views. Restaurante La Cumbre captivates with a combination of Spanish and international flavors that can be enjoyed on the terrace overlooking the sea, which goes beyond a simple dinner. El Almendro delights with traditional Spanish dishes and tempting seafood, a real culinary delight for the discerning palate.

Frequently asked questions

The nearest beach is Cala del Moraig, about 1.4 km by road.
