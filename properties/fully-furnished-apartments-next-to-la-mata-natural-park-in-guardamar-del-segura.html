Elite World Estates presents a modern residential development located in the picturesque area of El Raso, Guardamar del Segura. This is an excellent opportunity for anyone looking for a contemporary apartment in Spain that combines high-quality finishes, outstanding resort-style amenities, and a prime location next to the La Mata and Torrevieja Natural Park, just a few minutes from the beautiful sandy beaches of the Mediterranean Sea.

The development consists of 110 elegant 2 and 3-bedroom apartments distributed across modern five-storey buildings. Buyers can choose from ground-floor apartments with private gardens, spacious middle-floor apartments with large terraces, and exclusive penthouses with private rooftop solariums. The interiors have been designed with a contemporary open-plan layout, offering maximum comfort, abundant natural light, and premium-quality finishes.

One of the project's greatest advantages is its exceptional standard of equipment. All apartments will be delivered fully furnished and completely equipped, allowing owners to move in immediately upon completion. The price includes furniture, a fully fitted kitchen with appliances, kitchenware, air conditioning, LED lighting, electric shutters, fully finished bathrooms, a private parking space, and a storage room. This makes the development an excellent choice both as a holiday home in Spain and as a ready-to-rent investment property.

Residents will enjoy an extensive range of resort-style communal facilities, including a large swimming pool, beautifully landscaped gardens, a modern spa with jacuzzi, an indoor gym, a children's playground, a mini-golf course, and a pétanque court. The carefully planned architecture and thoughtfully designed communal areas provide the perfect environment for relaxation and year-round living.

El Raso is a peaceful and green residential area of Guardamar del Segura that is highly sought after by both homeowners and investors. The surrounding area offers restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, shops, cycling and walking trails, as well as several renowned golf courses. The proximity to the La Mata Natural Park and the stunning beaches of Guardamar makes this an ideal destination for permanent living, holiday escapes, or property investment on the Costa Blanca. The development also benefits from excellent access to Alicante International Airport.

Estimated completion date: September 2027.

Location

The development is located in the prestigious residential area of El Raso, in Guardamar del Segura, one of the most desirable locations on the southern Costa Blanca. This peaceful, green setting offers privacy and tranquillity while providing easy access to beaches, restaurants, shops, and a wide range of local amenities.

The immediate proximity to the La Mata and Torrevieja Natural Park makes this an ideal location for nature lovers. The area offers scenic walking and cycling routes, together with spectacular views of the salt lakes, home to flamingos and many other bird species.

The beautiful sandy beaches of Guardamar del Segura, considered among the finest on the Costa Blanca, are only a few minutes away by car. Nearby you will also find supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, pharmacies, shopping centres, and prestigious golf courses, ensuring that everything you need is within easy reach throughout the year.

Excellent connections to the N-332 coastal road and the AP-7 motorway provide convenient access to the region's main destinations, including Alicante International Airport.
