Modern villa for sale in Calpe on the Costa Blanca, offering panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea, the surrounding mountains and the city. Set in a natural environment surrounded by pine forests, the property combines contemporary architecture, modern technology and generous indoor and outdoor living spaces.

The villa sits on a private plot of approximately 637 m² and is distributed across three floors, connected by both internal stairs and a private elevator. The house has been designed with comfort, functionality and energy efficiency in mind and includes modern home automation solutions.

The architecture is clean and contemporary, with minimalist lines and large floor-to-ceiling windows that allow plenty of natural light into the interiors. The open design creates a strong connection between the living spaces, terraces and surrounding garden.

The villa offers 4 spacious bedrooms, each with its own fully equipped en-suite bathroom, providing privacy and comfort for residents and guests.

At the heart of the property is a modern designer kitchen, fully equipped and integrated with the open-plan dining and living area. This spacious social zone has been designed for both everyday living and entertaining, with large windows opening towards the outdoor areas.

The villa is equipped with underfloor heating and modern air conditioning, ensuring comfortable temperatures throughout the year. A private garage is located on the ground floor, with direct access to the upper levels via the elevator.

The outdoor areas are one of the main highlights of the property. The Mediterranean-style garden extends across three levels and blends naturally with the surrounding landscape. Spacious terraces provide several areas for relaxing, dining or enjoying the views.

The centrepiece is the private infinity swimming pool measuring approximately 12 x 3 m, positioned to take full advantage of the panoramic views towards the sea and the mountains of Calpe.

The first floor opens almost completely towards the garden, creating a seamless transition between the interior, sun terrace and landscaped areas. This layout allows the property to make full use of Calpe’s Mediterranean climate and natural surroundings.

LOCATION – CALPE, COSTA BLANCA

Calpe is one of the best-known coastal towns in the northern Costa Blanca, combining sandy beaches, clear Mediterranean waters, natural landscapes and a wide range of local services.

One of the town’s most distinctive landmarks is the Peñón de Ifach, an impressive rock formation rising directly from the Mediterranean and surrounded by protected natural areas. The town also offers a long promenade, restaurants, cafés, shops, local markets and numerous opportunities for outdoor activities.

Calpe has approximately 13 km of coastline with 14 beaches and coves. Among the best known is Playa de la Fossa, located north of the Peñón de Ifach. This sandy beach extends for approximately 950 metres and is bordered by a promenade with restaurants, cafés and leisure areas.

The area is also known for its seafood restaurants, Mediterranean cuisine and surrounding landscapes of mountains and vineyards.

With its combination of beaches, natural scenery, services and year-round Mediterranean climate, Calpe is an attractive location both for permanent residence and as a holiday home on the Costa Blanca.
