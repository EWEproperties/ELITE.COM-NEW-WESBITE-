Modern bungalows for sale in San Pedro del Pinatar, in the Murcia region, in a peaceful area close to Lo Pagán, the salt lagoons and the beaches of the Mar Menor. This intimate residential development consists of only 12 properties with 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, designed for comfortable holidays as well as year-round living in the Mediterranean climate.

Buyers can choose between ground-floor properties with private outdoor space and upper-floor homes with a balcony and a large private solarium of approximately 56 m². This makes it possible to select a property that best suits your lifestyle – with a garden ideal for relaxing or a spacious rooftop terrace offering plenty of room for sunbathing, unwinding and creating a private chill-out area.

Each bungalow offers a bright living area with lounge, dining space and open-plan kitchen, 2 comfortable bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Contemporary architecture, a functional layout and large glazed areas provide plenty of natural light and create a pleasant connection between the interior and outdoor spaces.

The properties will be delivered with high-quality finishes. The standard specification includes a fully fitted kitchen with appliances, equipped bathrooms with furniture and shower screens, built-in wardrobes, LED lighting and central air conditioning with heat pump and Air Zone system. Depending on the selected property, there is also the possibility of adding a private swimming pool.

Each property includes a private parking space.

Planned completion of the development: 2028.

Location

San Pedro del Pinatar is a popular coastal town located between the Mediterranean Sea and the Mar Menor. The area is known for the Salinas y Arenales de San Pedro Natural Park, its salt lagoons, promenades and wide range of leisure opportunities.

Nearby there are shops, restaurants, supermarkets, schools, sports facilities and all services needed for everyday living. The coastal area of Lo Pagán offers a promenade, marina, restaurants and sandy beaches along the Mar Menor. The sea is approximately 1.7 km away.

San Pedro del Pinatar is a small town in the Murcia region of Spain, located on the shores of the saltwater lagoon of the Mar Menor. The town has a rich history dating back to the Roman Empire, and traces of Roman salt-production facilities can still be found today.
