Location

Águilas is a beautiful port town located on the Costa Cálida. It is located 35 km from Lorca, 105 km from Murcia and 75 km from Cartagena. Approximately 2 hours by car from the international airport in Alicante.

Its sailing tradition is now combined with a wide tourist offer, one of the main attractions of which is the extensive coast.

The city of Águilas has 28 kilometers of sea coast, on which there are 35 beaches of different types of sand and thickness, located in different landscapes, from coves and pristine beaches in a natural environment to the municipal beaches of the city of Águilas. The warm desert climate occurs all year round with an annual average of 22°, with mild winter temperatures and very hot summer temperatures.

Águilas is surrounded by the Sierra de la Carrasquilla, Lomo de Bas and Sierra de la Almenara, which combine into a network of trails converging in parts of the Cabo Cope and Puntas de Calnegre natural parks. It is an ideal place for diving, water sports, hiking and golf.

In the center of Águilas, it is worth visiting the beautiful gardens with centuries-old ficus trees in the Plaza de España, the town hall (19th century) and the parish church of San José, which houses the image of the patron saint, which comes from the Salzillo school. At the top of the old area on the headland stands the castle-fortress of San Juan de Águilas, built in 1579 for defensive purposes. At its feet there is a port and a characteristic lighthouse, operating since the mid-19th century, with black and white stripes.

Depending on the date you decide to visit, you can enjoy unique celebrations such as the Águilas Carnival, recognized as an international tourist interest. It usually falls during the Carnival season (February). You will be able to experience several days full of colors, joy, music, dance and fantasy.

With a diverse Mediterranean cuisine based on seafood and garden products, Águilas is a place for those palates who want to enjoy high-quality products. Its standard products include red shrimp, mullet, sea bass, sea bream, tomato and caper.
