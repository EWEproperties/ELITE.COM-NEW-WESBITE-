Elite World Estates proudly presents an exclusive boutique residential development located in the peaceful area of Buenas Noches, one of the hidden gems of Estepona on the Costa del Sol. Situated just a short walk from the Mediterranean Sea, this modern development combines elegant architecture, high-quality finishes and exceptional sea views, creating the perfect setting for a relaxed coastal lifestyle.

The development comprises only 24 exclusive apartments, offering spacious 2 and 3-bedroom residences designed to maximise natural light, comfort and functionality. Every home has been carefully planned to provide generous indoor and outdoor living spaces, allowing residents to fully embrace the Mediterranean way of life.

Buyers can choose from elegant ground-floor apartments with private gardens, stylish mid-floor residences featuring large sea-view terraces, and spectacular penthouses with expansive terraces and private rooftop solariums. Floor-to-ceiling windows create bright and airy interiors while seamlessly connecting the living spaces with the surrounding landscape and allowing the Mediterranean breeze to become part of everyday living.

Residents can enjoy beautifully landscaped communal gardens, a large outdoor swimming pool and a fully equipped fitness centre, creating the perfect environment for relaxation, wellbeing and an active Mediterranean lifestyle.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its privileged location in Buenas Noches, a quiet residential area ideally positioned between Estepona and Casares. Just 550 metres from the beach, residents can easily enjoy the beautiful coastline, crystal-clear waters and relaxed atmosphere of this charming part of the Costa del Sol.

The nearby beaches of Playa de la Galera and Bahía Dorada offer ideal conditions for swimming, paddleboarding and other water sports, while numerous renowned golf courses can be reached within just a few minutes by car. The vibrant marina and historic centre of Estepona are also nearby, offering a fantastic selection of restaurants, boutiques, cafés and leisure activities. Málaga International Airport can be reached in approximately one hour, ensuring excellent connections throughout Europe.

Combining a boutique atmosphere, elegant contemporary design, exceptional amenities and an unbeatable location just moments from the Mediterranean Sea, this exclusive development represents an outstanding opportunity to own a stylish home in one of the Costa del Sol's most desirable coastal destinations.

Location

The development is located in the prestigious area of Buenas Noches, between Estepona and Casares, on the western Costa del Sol. This peaceful coastal setting is highly regarded for its stunning Mediterranean Sea views, low-density residential atmosphere, and excellent connections to the region's most popular destinations. It perfectly combines privacy and tranquillity with easy access to beaches, golf courses, and all the amenities required for comfortable year-round living.

Just a few minutes' walk from the development are the beautiful sandy beaches of the Costa del Sol, where residents can enjoy the seaside promenade, restaurants serving Mediterranean cuisine, and a variety of stylish beach clubs. Supermarkets, shops, schools, medical centres, and Estepona Marina—with its excellent selection of restaurants, cafés, and water sports facilities—are all within easy reach.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to some of the Costa del Sol's most prestigious golf courses, including Valle Romano Golf, Estepona Golf, and Finca Cortesín Golf, one of Europe's finest luxury golf resorts. Marbella, Puerto Banús, Sotogrande, and Gibraltar are all just a short drive away, making this an ideal location for both permanent living and a luxury holiday home or investment property.

Another major advantage is the excellent transport connections. Gibraltar Airport can be reached in approximately 35–40 minutes, while Málaga International Airport is only about one hour away by car.
