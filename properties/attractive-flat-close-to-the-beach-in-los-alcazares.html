Location

Los Alcázares, is located in the Murcia region of Spain. The town entices with its seaside charm and a host of delightful attractions. Situated along the magnificent Mar Menor, a saltwater lagoon that is renowned for its crystal clear water and medicinal properties, Los Alcázares offers an idyllic location for an unforgettable holiday or a quiet break.

The area is connected by motorway to Cartagena, Murcia and Alicante, as well as the rest of the Costa Cálida and Costa Blanca beaches.

One of the town's greatest assets is its collection of beautiful beaches, each promising moments of bliss and relaxation. Golden sands stretch along the calm shores of the Mar Menor, creating a paradise for sun seekers and water lovers alike. From the lively and family-friendly Playa de Los Narejos beach to the more secluded and tranquil Playa de Las Salinas, you'll find a beach for everyone. Visitors can sunbathe, swim or enjoy a variety of water sports, including sailing, kayaking and windsurfing. The shallow and calm waters of the Mar Menor make it an ideal place for families with children.

Los Alcázares boasts a vibrant culinary scene, offering a diverse range of restaurants, bars and cafes to tempt your taste buds. From traditional Spanish tapas to international dishes, the city's restaurants will satisfy any palate. Enjoy fresh seafood made from local ingredients, savour an authentic paella or sip a refreshing drink at a beachside chiringuito while taking in the magnificent views. The warm and welcoming atmosphere of the city's eateries enhances the overall dining experience, making it a real delight for food lovers.

In addition to the beaches and eateries, Los Alcázares offers many attractions to keep visitors entertained. Browse the charming streets in the town centre, dotted with shops, boutiques and artisan shops. Discover local handicrafts, souvenirs and unique treasures to commemorate your visit. For the adventure-seekers, a variety of water sports centres offer opportunities for water skiing, paddleboarding and scuba diving, allowing you to plumb the depths of the Mar Menor's underwater wonders.

Golf lovers will find themselves in paradise, as Los Alcázares is surrounded by a selection of world-class golf courses. Within a short distance, you will discover famous golf courses such as La Serena Golf, Roda Golf and Mar Menor Golf Resort. Immerse yourself in the lush green fairways, perfect your swing and enjoy a Mediterranean climate that allows you to play golf all year round.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Punta Brava, about 5 km by road, around 8 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: I.E.S. ANTONIO MENÁRGUEZ COSTA, about 1.9 km.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 34 km by road, around 25 minutes by car.
