Location

Torrevieja is a Spanish town located 40 km from Alicante, famous for its pink lake. The pink lake is part of the Las Lagunas de La Mata y Torrevieja Natural Park, where, in addition to its unusual colour, the flamingos that live there are protected. The lake is the largest in Europe and one of the few of its kind in the world, and owes its colour to the crustaceans that live there, which also make the flamingos pink.

The city is located on the Costa Blanca, in the province of Alicante, and is very well connected to the rest of the country.

One of the reasons why it is worth living or holidaying in Torrevieja is the modernity of this city. This is mainly because it is a city in the process of development, due to its location and the large number of people who visit and live there. It is therefore a modern city, equipped with everything necessary to welcome tourists and residents. What is more, multiculturalism reigns here, and you can hear different languages spoken on the streets.

Torrevieja is also famous for its beautiful beaches, where golden sand meets crystal clear water. Some of the best beaches in the area are La Mata Beach, Playa del Cura and Los Locos Beach. The beautiful promenades along these beaches are ideal for leisurely walks, full of charming cafés, restaurants and shops.

Golf enthusiasts will be delighted by the abundance of exceptional golf courses in the area. Some of the best golf courses near Torrevieja include Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, Villamartin Golf Club, and La Marquesa Golf. Enjoy challenging water courses, picturesque landscapes, and world-class facilities while indulging in your favourite pastime.

When it comes to dining, Torrevieja offers a diverse culinary scene. Discover a wide range of restaurants, from traditional Spanish tapas bars to international cuisine, catering to every palate and preference. Be sure to sample the fresh seafood, a local speciality that is not to be missed.

For shopping enthusiasts, the Habaneras and Ozone Leisure Centre shopping centres are popular. These modern complexes feature a variety of shops, boutiques and entertainment venues, providing a wonderful shopping experience.

Torrevieja also boasts a lively nightlife, with numerous bars, clubs and live music venues. Whether you are looking for a relaxing evening with friends or an exciting night of dancing, Torrevieja's nightlife has something for everyone.
