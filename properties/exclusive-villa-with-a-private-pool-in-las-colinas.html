For sale, a modern villa located in the exceptional green surroundings of Las Colinas on the Costa Blanca. The property has been designed in an elegant, contemporary style, with large glazed areas, a bright colour palette and natural materials that create a calm and harmonious living environment.

The villa offers 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and an additional guest toilet. Depending on the selected option, the built area is approximately 165–172 m², while the private plots range from approximately 644 to 868 m².

The heart of the home is a spacious, open-plan living area comprising the lounge, dining area and modern kitchen. Large windows provide direct access to the terrace and garden, bringing plenty of natural light into the interiors and creating a seamless connection between the indoor and outdoor spaces.

The private plot includes a swimming pool, garden and spacious terraces, providing a comfortable setting for relaxing, dining outdoors and enjoying the Mediterranean climate for most of the year.

The property is situated in a peaceful, carefully landscaped environment surrounded by greenery. It is an excellent choice both for year-round living and as a comfortable holiday home on the Costa Blanca.

Location

The development is located within Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, in an exceptionally green and peaceful area of the southern Costa Blanca, between San Miguel de Salinas and Campoamor. It is a gated, carefully maintained resort surrounded by Mediterranean nature, with a golf course and an extensive range of facilities for residents.

The location offers privacy and tranquillity, while still allowing easy access by car to the beaches of Orihuela Costa, restaurants, shops and the coastal areas of Campoamor and Cabo Roig. It is an ideal location for those who want to live close to nature without giving up convenient access to services and the coast.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa Barranco Rubio Cala de Campoamor, about 12 km by road, around 21 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are British School (about 6 km) and Colegio de Educación Infantil y Primaria Número 20 (about 8 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Campoverde Centro de Salud, about 24 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 16 km by road, around 23 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Minimarket, about 2.7 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 52 km by road, around 40 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 75 km, around 55 minutes by car.
