Modern apartments for sale in the exceptional green surroundings of Las Colinas on the Costa Blanca. The low-density development has been carefully integrated into the natural landscape, offering residents privacy, tranquillity and spacious outdoor areas with open views over the surrounding countryside.

The development consists of several low-rise buildings with contemporary architecture. Light façades, large windows and generous terraces give the complex an elegant Mediterranean character. At the heart of the development there is a communal swimming pool surrounded by landscaped gardens and relaxation areas.

The properties include apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms, designed to provide comfort both for holidays and year-round living. The selection includes ground-floor apartments with exceptionally large terraces and, in some cases, private gardens, as well as apartments on the upper floors and properties on the top levels with additional private outdoor spaces.

One of the main advantages of this development is the generous terrace space. Depending on the selected property, terraces range from approximately 36 m² to more than 130 m². Some ground-floor apartments also offer private gardens of up to approximately 129 m², providing plenty of space for relaxing, sunbathing, outdoor dining or creating your own Mediterranean-style leisure area.

The interiors have been designed with a modern, open-plan layout. The living room, dining area and kitchen form the central part of the apartment, while large glazed doors provide direct access to the terrace. This allows plenty of natural light into the living areas and creates a seamless connection between the interior and outdoor spaces.

The 3-bedroom apartments offer built areas of up to approximately 150 m², while the more compact 2-bedroom options provide around 105–107 m² of built space. Selected apartments on the upper floors also benefit from additional private rooftop terraces.

Residents have access to a communal swimming pool and carefully landscaped green areas. The complex is surrounded by lush vegetation, giving it an intimate atmosphere and a strong connection with nature. The residential area benefits from 24-hour security, while owners can also take advantage of additional services available within the resort.

Location

The development is located within Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, in an exceptionally green and peaceful area of the southern Costa Blanca, between San Miguel de Salinas and Campoamor. It is a gated, carefully designed resort surrounded by Mediterranean nature, featuring a golf course and a wide range of facilities for residents.

The location offers privacy and tranquillity while still allowing easy access by car to the beaches of Orihuela Costa, restaurants, shops and the coastal areas of Campoamor and Cabo Roig. It is an excellent choice for those who want to live close to nature without giving up convenient access to services and the Mediterranean coast.
