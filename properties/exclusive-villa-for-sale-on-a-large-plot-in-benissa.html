Elite World Estates presents an exceptional villa located in the peaceful area of Serrallonga in Benissa, set on an impressive plot of more than 11,600 m². The design is inspired by the traditional Mediterranean architecture characteristic of this part of the Costa Blanca, combining natural materials and classic elements with modern comfort.

The villa offers 3 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, with 255 m² of built area. An additional 169 m² of outdoor and auxiliary areas brings the total area to approximately 423 m². The property sits on a private plot of 11,672 m², providing plenty of space and a high level of privacy.

At the heart of the home is a spacious living and dining area with an open-plan kitchen. Large glazed doors and windows fill the interiors with natural light and create a direct connection to the covered terrace, garden and private swimming pool. The layout has been carefully designed to seamlessly connect the indoor living spaces with the outdoor areas.

The architecture of the villa is distinguished by whitewashed façades, traditional tiled roofs and natural wood elements. The interiors feature warm tones, neutral finishes and exposed wooden ceiling beams, enhancing the property's authentic Mediterranean character.

Large terraces surrounding the house and swimming pool provide several separate areas for relaxing, sunbathing and outdoor dining. The extensive plot and surrounding Mediterranean vegetation create a peaceful setting with a wonderful sense of space and privacy.

Excellent location in Benissa

The villa is located in Serrallonga, a peaceful and green area of Benissa that offers privacy and space while remaining within easy reach of the town centre and the Costa Blanca coastline.

Benissa town centre is approximately 1.5 km from the property, while the nearest supermarket is around 2 km away. The Mediterranean coastline and beaches are approximately 9 km away. The location also provides convenient access to popular coastal towns such as Moraira and Calpe.

This is an ideal location for those looking to enjoy the tranquillity and natural surroundings of a large private plot while remaining within easy reach of restaurants, shops, services and the beautiful beaches of the northern Costa Blanca.
