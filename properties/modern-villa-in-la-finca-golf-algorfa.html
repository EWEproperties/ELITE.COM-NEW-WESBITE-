Location

Algorfa located in the Vega Baja del Segura region, has a rich history that dates back to Roman times. The town's name is derived from the Arabic word 'Al-Gorfa,' meaning 'cave,' as the area was known for its numerous natural caves. Today, Algorfa is known for its production of citrus fruits and vegetables.

Algorfa is home to numerous attractions that will appeal to visitors of all ages. Here are just a few of the highlights:

La Finca Golf Course

If you're a fan of golf, then La Finca Golf Course is a must-visit destination. This championship golf course is situated in a beautiful natural setting, with stunning views of the Vega Baja countryside. The course has been designed to provide a challenge for golfers of all levels, and there are plenty of facilities, including a pro shop and a restaurant.

Algorfa Market

For a taste of the local culture, be sure to visit the Algorfa Market, which takes place every Wednesday. Here, you'll find a wide range of goods, including fresh produce, clothing, and souvenirs. It's the perfect place to pick up a unique gift or sample some of the delicious local cuisine.

Plaza de España

The Plaza de España is the main square in Algorfa, and it's a great place to soak up the local atmosphere. Here, you'll find a beautiful fountain, plenty of seating, and a range of restaurants and bars. It's the perfect place to relax with a drink and watch the world go by.
