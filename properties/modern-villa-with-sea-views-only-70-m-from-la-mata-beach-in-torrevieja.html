Location

Torrelamata (officially La Mata) is a district of Torrevieja, in the province of Alicante, in the Valencian Community. It is bordered to the north by Guardamar del Segura and to the west by the Lagunas de La Mata and Torrevieja Natural Park.

This city boasts an extraordinary, healthy microclimate, whose healthy properties come from, among others, from the nearby large salt deposits located in two salty lagoons located in the very center of the city.

La Mata is a privileged area less than 50 meters from the wonderful Playa de la Mata, over 15 km long, and the Lagunas de La Mata Natural Park.

It is an amazing adventure to see these two lakes: pink and green, which are surrounded by natural parks with many species of birds, including flamingos. Laguna de Torrevieja owes its pink color to the high salinity of the water, the crustaceans living here and algae that produce carotenoids. The view is very impressive, no wonder it is one of the biggest tourist attractions of this place.

The second lagoon (La Laguna de la Mata) is green. The park's interpretive center is located here. We can see unusual plants here, such as Aleppo pine, orchid, thyme, seness, saffron and many others.

Also noteworthy are the vineyards where the local de la Mata wine is produced.

The climate in Torrevieja definitely encourages sunbathing and relaxing on one of the many local sandy beaches. There's a lot to choose from, so it's hard to decide which beach to choose for your holiday. Well, one of the most popular is Playa de la Mata, approximately 2.5 km long. This beautiful sandy beach is marked with the Blue Flag and is accessible to people with disabilities. People who like to spend time actively on the beach can play on one of several volleyball courts.
