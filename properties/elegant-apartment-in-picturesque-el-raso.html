Location

Guardamar del Segura is a charming seaside town located on the Costa Blanca. Thanks to its rich infrastructure, from the panoramic promenade to various shopping districts, it is an ideal place for people looking for both relaxation on the beach and the opportunity to use a variety of services. El Raso, located near Guardamar del Segura, is a residential area known for its quiet surroundings. El Raso also stands out for its variety of local amenities, offering residents access to restaurants, shops and other services, creating the perfect place to live comfortably and relax in the Spanish sun.

Los Viveros is a beautiful and secluded beach located in Guardamar del Segura in the province of Alicante in the Valencian Community. It is approximately 1,300 meters long and 45 meters wide. This beach takes its name from the proximity of the nurseries in the area, which characterize the region due to the different crops that grow there. In addition, Rábita Califal, an Andalusian archaeological treasure, is nearby. This beach also has a blue flag, which is an international recognition awarded to beaches that meet the highest standards of environmental quality and safety.

The natural environment of Playa Los Viveros is unique because it is located in a protected area of great importance to the local community. Moreover, the area is characterized by lush vegetation, which is a characteristic feature of this region. The beach is located on a large sandbar, with shallow water and no big waves. You can reach it by paths through a pine forest, which has several wooden piers that allow you to go down to the sand, leveling the unevenness of the natural dunes.

In Guardamar del Segura we have not only beaches, but also a wide tourist offer such as gastronomy, culture and history with archaeological, Phoenician, Arab and Roman sites. Water sports enthusiasts can also enjoy all kinds of water sports in Puerto Marina de las Dunas'

(sailing, windsurfing, water skiing, kayaking, motorboat, etc.)

All this together makes it one of the best places to live on the Costa Blanca - the south, where you can live surrounded by nature without giving up extensive infrastructure.
