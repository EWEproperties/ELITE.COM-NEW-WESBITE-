If you are looking for a new flat in a prime location in the centre of Alicante, you should definitely consider this offer!

Invest in this new development in Alicante and enjoy your dream Spanish flat in a lively neighbourhood with relatively new buildings, wide streets and tree-lined avenues.

This is an exclusive residential complex consisting of 55 new flats, located on Bulevar del Pla, one of the most developing areas of Alicante. It offers modern and bright flats with high-quality finishes, spacious rooms and functional design.

It has complete communal areas: swimming pools, gardens with Wi-Fi, a terrace, children's playgrounds and spaces for socialising. Each home has a storage room, a garage and, in the case of the penthouses, a fully equipped private solarium.

This is an opportunity to live in style, comfort and quality in an excellent location.

Although the development overlooks three streets, the main façade is located on a picturesque avenue in the heart of the neighbourhood and one of the city's main thoroughfares, consisting of a wide avenue that connects to other parts of the city and provides access to some of the most important roads, such as the A7 motorway.

It is on this important transport artery that one of the city's main public transport systems, the TRAM Metropolitano de Alicante, runs. There are two stops just a few metres away, which, together with the city buses, provide convenient and environmentally friendly access in a matter of minutes to the city centre, the university, shopping centres, the beach...

In terms of proximity to hospitals, we are undoubtedly in the closest neighbourhood, just 500 metres from the Hospital General, 700 metres from the Vistahermosa centre and only 1 km from Quirón Salud.

This new project is also a fantastic option if you want to live close to educational establishments, as there is a wide range of nurseries, primary and secondary schools, both public and private, in the immediate vicinity.

The commercial and service offer in the area is so rich that it will satisfy everyone's expectations, thanks to small local shops, restaurants and nearby supermarkets. In any case, using the TRAM, you can reach the city's two main shopping centres, C.C. Gran Vía and C.C. Plaza Mar, in just a few minutes.

Location

Alicante is a beautiful city on the south-east coast of Spain and is the perfect place for those looking for an unforgettable experience. With its warm climate, magnificent beaches and rich history, it is no wonder that Alicante is one of the most popular tourist destinations in Spain. The calm waters of the Mediterranean Sea in the city are ideal for water sports and sunbathing, making it a favourite spot for both locals and tourists.

Golf

Alicante is home to some of the most beautiful golf courses in the region, making it a popular destination for golf enthusiasts. Alicante Golf is one of the most famous golf courses, offering 18 challenging holes designed by the legendary Seve Ballesteros. The course offers magnificent views of the Mediterranean Sea and is a true paradise for golf lovers.

History

Alicante's rich history is evident in its magnificent monuments, such as St. Barbara's Castle. This historic fortress, perched atop Mount Benacantil, offers breathtaking views of the city and the sea. The castle's walls and towers have stood for centuries, and the site bears witness to the many civilisations that have settled in Alicante over the years, including the Romans, Moors and Christians.

Beaches

Of course, no trip to Alicante is complete without a visit to its beautiful beaches. The city's warm climate and crystal-clear waters make it the perfect place for water sports and sunbathing. Playa del Postiguet is one of the most popular beaches in the region, located in the heart of Alicante. The beach is a favourite spot for both tourists and locals, offering stunning views of the city and the Mediterranean Sea.

The Promenade

The Explanada de España promenade is another must-see in Alicante. Located opposite the port, this lively boulevard is lined with terraces and pavement cafés, making it the perfect place for a cup of coffee or a delicious meal. The promenade is a popular meeting place for locals and tourists alike and the perfect place to enjoy magnificent views of the sea and the city.
