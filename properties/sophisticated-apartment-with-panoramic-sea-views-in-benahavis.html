Take advantage of the unique opportunity to invest in a unique property in a highly desirable location in Benahavis. It's a beautiful mountain town just a few miles from Marbella, known for its luxury properties, golf courses and top restaurants. It is a desirable address for those who want to invest in real estate in Spain, like to play golf or are looking for the delight of the palate.

This new residential project is located directly on the hills of Benahavis and offers its residents undeniably some of the best panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea.

In the area you can visit golf courses, eat in one of the many restaurants or go for a walk in nature. If you want to swim in the Mediterranean, Playa de Guadalmina is the closest. It can be reached in 15 to 20 minutes by car. Not far from your future home is Puerto Banus in Marbella, which can be reached by car in about 20-25 minutes. In the area, you can shop, dine or admire the yachts. Estepona is also worth a visit. The city can be reached by car in about 25 to 30 minutes.

Apartments with an area of 144 to 185 m². will have, among others spacious terraces with a wonderful view of the Mediterranean Sea. The kitchens of each house are fully equipped with designer furniture and low-energy household appliances. Some apartments will also have their own swimming pool and garden. All with amazing panoramic sea views, you'll enjoy a fantastic sunset here overlooking Africa and Gibraltar.

Location

Benahavís is a mountain town in the province of Málaga, 7 km from the coast. It lies between Marbella, Estepona and Ronda, on the West Coast of the Costa del Sol. It is popular for its wide range of restaurants and as a white city with a typical Arab street layout. Within Benahavis there is one of the most exclusive estates in Spain - La Zagaleta. According to data from the Tax Office, in 2018 Benahavís was the first municipality in the province of Málaga in terms of gross income and the fourth in Andalusia, thanks to La Zagaleta.

Golf courses

The golf courses in Benahavis have been designed by world class golf course designers such as Robert Trent Jones or Severiano Ballesteros or Jack Niclaus. In Benahavis, the golf season lasts all year round. The mild climate, great infrastructure, excellent restaurants attract fans of this sport from all over the world. You can play in 12 clubs, including Los Flamingos Golf Club, La Quinta Golf & Country Club, La Zagaleta and Club de Golf Benahavís. The greenery of the golf courses is an inseparable element of the Benahavis landscape. This is another reason why property investors in Spain choose Benahavis.

Restaurants

Benahavis is also famous for the best restaurants in Andalusia. We recommend visiting one of them: El Abanico, Bodegon Gallery, Rufino or Lemon Grass. El Abanico serves local and European cuisine, including vegetarian dishes. Same with Bodegon Gallery and Rufino. If you feel like Asian cuisine, it's best to visit Lemon Grass - the local Thai chef specializes in Asian Fusion, and the restaurant itself is called the best Asian cuisine on the Costa del Sol.

A place full of possibilities

It is impossible not to like Benahavis, it is a perfect place to relax, meet friends in restaurants and cafes or on the golf course. It is also a great base for hiking, cycling and swimming. Art lovers will also find something for themselves here: in David Marschall's gallery you can buy beautiful sculptures inspired by nature, combined with ready-made objects - turning chips, railway sleepers. The artist creates abstract sculptures, figures that are a combination of ceramics, glass and metal, wood and other materials.
