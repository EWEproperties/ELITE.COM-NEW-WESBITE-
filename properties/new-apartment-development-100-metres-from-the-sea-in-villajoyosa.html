Location

Villajoyosa is a charming Mediterranean seaside town located between Alicante and Benidorm, about a 30-minute drive from Alicante Airport. Villajoyosa, or La Vila Joiosa, literally means ‘joyful town’. Indeed, it gives that impression with its colourful houses in the town centre. This traditional fishing village is considered one of the most beautiful in the province of Alicante.

Urban legend has it that the buildings were painted in bright colours to help fishermen find their way home. Today, they certainly attract crowds of tourists and are an inspiration for many photographers and artists.

Just take a stroll through the picturesque streets to realise that Villajoyosa is full of joy (as its name suggests) and charm. There are many reasons why this municipality is the perfect place to spend an unforgettable holiday: good weather all year round, idyllic beaches and coves, history and culture everywhere, and an environment that encourages relaxation.

Villajoyosa has been associated with chocolate since the 19th century. It is home to the popular Spanish chocolate factory Valor. The Valor, Pérez and Clavileño museums are must-see attractions. There you will learn everything about the chocolate-making process and its history in the area. Tours led by knowledgeable guides are usually combined with chocolate tasting.

Villajoyosa has 3 km of golden sandy beaches with views of the endless clear sea. The most beautiful ones are Playa El Paraíso, Xarco and La Caleta. Here you will find both larger tourist resorts and hidden, mysterious corners perfect for peaceful relaxation. The coastal panorama, Mediterranean climate and soft, hot sand are ideal for complete relaxation. Near the beaches, you will find regional restaurants and cafés offering fresh Mediterranean delicacies.
