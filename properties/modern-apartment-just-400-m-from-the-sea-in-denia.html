New apartments for sale located just 400 metres from the beach in picturesque Dénia on the Costa Blanca. This contemporary residential development has been designed with comfort, proximity to nature and the Mediterranean lifestyle in mind.

Its excellent location, just a few steps from a park and a beautiful beach, allows residents to enjoy peaceful surroundings while remaining close to shops, restaurants, recreational areas and all essential amenities. Dénia town centre and the marina are also within easy reach.

The development stands out for its contemporary architecture, designed to blend harmoniously with the natural surroundings, as well as its environmentally friendly solutions. These include rainwater collection and reuse systems, photovoltaic panels, gardens with low-maintenance native vegetation and charging points for electric vehicles.

The development offers apartments with 1, 2 or 3 bedrooms, including ground-floor properties with private gardens, apartments with spacious terraces and penthouses with private solariums. Well-designed layouts, large windows and open-plan interiors provide plenty of natural light and a wonderful sense of space.

The open-plan living area combines a comfortable lounge, dining area and a fitted kitchen with selected appliances included. Large terraces form a natural extension of the interior and provide an ideal setting for relaxing, dining outdoors and enjoying the sunny Costa Blanca climate.

One of the main advantages of the development is its extensive communal areas surrounded by Mediterranean greenery. Residents will have access to several swimming pools, a private spa with sauna, yoga and fitness areas, sensory gardens, vegetable gardens, social clubs, picnic areas, bicycle workshops and dedicated pet-care areas. There will also be spaces for storing bicycles and water sports equipment.

Location

Dénia is a popular coastal town on the Costa Blanca, situated between Alicante and Valencia. Both international airports can be reached by car in approximately one hour.

The town is renowned for its beautiful beaches, historic centre, impressive castle, marina and excellent gastronomy. The Montgó mountain rises above Dénia and is surrounded by a protected natural park offering numerous walking and cycling routes.

Dénia boasts approximately 15 km of coastline. Las Marinas offers long sandy beaches ideal for relaxing and enjoying water sports, while Las Rotas is known for its rocky coves and excellent conditions for diving and snorkelling.

Another major advantage of Dénia is its port, which provides regular ferry connections to Ibiza, Mallorca and Formentera.

The combination of proximity to the beach, natural surroundings, excellent infrastructure and extensive communal facilities makes this development an excellent choice both as a second home in Spain and for year-round living.
