Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional contemporary villa located in one of the most prestigious areas of the northern Costa Blanca – Cumbre del Sol. This impressive residence has been designed for discerning buyers seeking an exclusive property in Spain that combines timeless architecture, breathtaking Mediterranean Sea views, and the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship.

The villa's contemporary design blends seamlessly into the Mediterranean landscape, featuring natural stone finishes, expansive floor-to-ceiling windows, and elegant architectural details. Every room has been carefully designed to maximise natural light while offering uninterrupted panoramic sea views.

The property offers approximately 542 m² of built area on a 932 m² plot. The interior features four spacious bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom. The luxurious master suite also includes a walk-in wardrobe and a private terrace overlooking the Mediterranean Sea.

The heart of the home is an impressive open-plan living area where the lounge, dining room, and designer kitchen come together in one elegant and harmonious space. Large sliding glass doors create a seamless transition between the interior and the outdoor terraces, blurring the boundaries between indoor and outdoor living. The kitchen is equipped with premium appliances, a central island, and bespoke cabinetry, offering both style and practicality.

The outdoor areas have been designed to embrace the Mediterranean lifestyle. Expansive terraces provide multiple spaces for relaxing, entertaining, dining al fresco, and enjoying spectacular sunsets. The stunning infinity swimming pool appears to merge with the sea, creating the atmosphere of a private luxury resort.

The property also includes a private garage, utility rooms, and additional technical spaces. Year-round comfort is ensured by energy-efficient solutions, air conditioning, underfloor heating, a smart home automation system, and premium-quality finishes throughout.

Location

The villa is situated in the prestigious residential area of Cumbre del Sol, between Moraira and Jávea, on the northern Costa Blanca. This exclusive location is renowned for its dramatic cliffs, panoramic Mediterranean views, and peaceful, private surroundings.

Nearby, residents can enjoy beautiful beaches and coves, including Cala del Moraig, scenic walking trails, spectacular viewpoints, restaurants, shops, the prestigious Lady Elizabeth School, and all the amenities required for comfortable year-round living. The charming centres of Moraira and Jávea, with their marinas, boutiques, and excellent restaurants, are only a short drive away.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to renowned golf courses such as Club de Golf Jávea and La Sella Golf. Excellent connections to both Alicante and Valencia airports make this property an outstanding choice as a permanent residence, holiday home, or secure investment on the Costa Blanca.
