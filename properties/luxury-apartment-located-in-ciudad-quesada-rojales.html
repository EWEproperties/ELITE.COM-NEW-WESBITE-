With several models to choose from, buyers can select between a ground floor with a plot or a first floor with a solarium and elevator. All the units come with three bedrooms, two to three-and-a-half bathrooms (including one en-suite), an open-plan kitchen, living room and terrace. For those who desire a pool, there is an option for both.

Inside, the apartments are designed with open space living rooms featuring big windows that provide plenty of natural light and breathtaking views. This combination of design elements creates a warm and welcoming atmosphere, perfect for relaxation and entertaining.

With three bedrooms in each unit, there is plenty of space for you and your guests to relax and unwind. Residents of Oceanic Luxury Apartments can also enjoy an array of amenities, such as an indoor and outdoor pool, gardens, GYM, barbecue, sauna, and hammocks. The qualities of these apartments are similar to those of the residential Oceanic, setting a high standard for luxury living in the region.

Location

Ciudad Quesada, located on the southern Costa Blanca, is a popular year-round destination for sun-seekers. With a Mediterranean climate, the town is just a short drive from golden sandy beaches, including El Moncayo en Guerdanar del Segura, and is surrounded by stunning natural parks and picturesque landscapes.

Golf enthusiasts can enjoy the prestigious La Marquesa Golf Club, with breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape. Meanwhile, nature lovers can visit the nearby salt lakes of Torrevieja, home to a diverse range of bird species. For those looking to hit the beach, the stunning beaches of Guardamar del Segura, La Mata, and Torrevieja offer a variety of water sports.

The town has a rich history dating back to the early 18th century and has grown into a vibrant community with a unique blend of Spanish, British, and other European cultures. In Ciudad Quesada, you can experience the town's rural past in its charming old town, while the modern and cosmopolitan area around the town center reflects its more recent development.

Ciudad Quesada is just a 30-minute drive from Alicante Airport, which is well-connected to major cities in Europe. Come and experience the best of the southern Costa Blanca in this vibrant town.
