Location

Benahavís has the most mountainous topography of the entire western Costa del Sol, many golf courses with meticulously landscaped gardens, a gastronomy that dances between the mountains and the sea and an architecture that simultaneously combines ancient Arab features with the most modern on the market.

This white city is integrated into a landscape of rich flora and fauna both aesthetically, functionally and ecologically, allowing visitors to enjoy the three rivers that cross it: the Guadaiza, Guadalmansa and Guadalmina. Read on to find out more about this white city characterised by clean air and modernity. From its heights, you can see 100 kilometres of Spanish coastline and even the north of the African continent.

Golf courses

The golf courses in Benahavis, were designed by world-class golf course designers such as Robert Trent Jones, or Severiano Ballesteros, or Jack Niclaus. At Benahavis, the golf season lasts all year round. The mild climate, great infrastructure and excellent restaurants attract lovers of the sport from all corners of the world. You can play in 12 clubs, including Los Flamingos Golf Club, La Quinta Golf & Country Club, La Zagaleta or the Club de Golf Benahavís. The green of the golf courses is an integral part of the Benahavis landscape. This is another reason why property investors in Spain choose Benahavis.

Restaurants

Benahavis is also famous for the best restaurants in Andalusia. We recommend taking a look at one of them: El Abanico, Bodegon Gallery, Rufino or Lemon Grass. El Abanico serves local and European cuisine, including vegetarian dishes. Likewise at Bodegon Gallery and Rufino. If you fancy Asian cuisine, the best place to look is Lemon Grass - the Thai chef there specialises in Asian Fusion, and the restaurant itself is described as the best Asian cuisine on the Costa del Sol.

A place full of possibilities

Benahavis is impossible not to like, it's a great place to relax, meet friends in the restaurants and cafes or on the golf course. It is also a great base for hiking, cycling and swimming. Art lovers will also find something for themselves here: in the David Marschall gallery, you can buy beautiful sculptures, inspired by nature, combined with readymade objects - turning chips, railway sleepers. The artist creates abstract sculptures, figures that are a combination of ceramics, glass and metal, wood and other materials.
