Elite World Estates proudly presents a modern residential development located just on the second line from Las Higuericas Beach in Torre de la Horadada, one of the most sought-after seaside destinations on the southern Costa Blanca. This is an excellent opportunity for those looking for a brand-new home close to the sea, combining contemporary architecture, high-quality construction and an exceptional location that allows you to enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle all year round.

This boutique residential complex offers a selection of modern apartments, townhouses and semi-detached villas, all thoughtfully designed for comfortable everyday living. Bright, functional interiors blend seamlessly with spacious terraces, creating the perfect extension of the living area and an ideal space to relax outdoors. Each property includes a private parking space, while residents can enjoy a beautiful communal swimming pool surrounded by landscaped gardens.

One of the development's greatest advantages is its outstanding location. Las Higuericas Beach is just a short walk away, making morning swims, relaxing beach days and seaside walks part of everyday life. The surrounding area offers a wide range of restaurants serving fresh Mediterranean cuisine, cafés, shops, a marina and the picturesque promenade stretching along the coast of Torre de la Horadada. The nearby town of Pilar de la Horadada provides all essential amenities, including supermarkets, schools, medical centres, sports facilities and a full range of services for comfortable year-round living.

Excellent connections to Alicante and Murcia airports, together with the proximity to prestigious golf courses such as Lo Romero Golf, make this development an outstanding choice as a holiday home, permanent residence or investment property with excellent rental potential. Contemporary design, premium finishes and an exceptional location just moments from the Mediterranean Sea create the perfect setting to enjoy everything the Costa Blanca has to offer.

Location

The development is situated in one of the most desirable areas of Torre de la Horadada, approximately 250 metres from Las Higuericas Beach. It is an exceptional location for those seeking proximity to the sea, a peaceful environment and a comfortable Mediterranean lifestyle throughout the year. Within just a few minutes on foot, you can reach the sandy beach with crystal-clear waters, the seaside promenade, and a variety of restaurants and cafés overlooking the Mediterranean.

Supermarkets, shops, pharmacies, a medical centre, schools and sports facilities are all conveniently located nearby, ensuring that every essential service is within easy reach. The centre of Pilar de la Horadada is only a few minutes away by car, while golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of the renowned Lo Romero Golf course, located approximately 10 minutes from the development.

Excellent access to the AP-7 motorway and the N-332 coastal road provides quick connections to Alicante and Murcia airports, as well as to nearby destinations such as San Pedro del Pinatar, Orihuela Costa and Torrevieja. This prime location makes the development an ideal choice for a holiday apartment, a permanent residence or a property with excellent investment potential.
