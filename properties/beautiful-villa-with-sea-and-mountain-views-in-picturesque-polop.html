Location

On the beautiful Costa Blanca, in the interior of the province of Alicante, there are charming little towns that are often overshadowed by other coastal or more well-known towns. A clear example is Polop de la Marina, located a few kilometres from Benidorm, Altea or Castell de Guadalest, with a beautiful historic centre that will make you fall in love at first sight.

Located in the Marina Baja region of Alicante, about 15 kilometres from the coast, Polop de la Marina is a small town with less than 5,000 inhabitants.

Polop's old town, located on a hill and developed at the foot of the Arab castle, is one of the most interesting and best-preserved in the province. Its picturesque, colourful streets full of flowers and carved ornaments are an inspiration for many artists.

The most outstanding building in historical terms is the church of San Pedro Apóstol, in the upper part of the town, just before the path leading to the castle.

Although few remains of Polop Castle have survived, it is well worth visiting the ruins, located at the top of the hill on which the old town is situated. The path leading to the castle is undoubtedly one of the most interesting places to visit in Polop. Impeccably maintained, like the rest of the municipality, the successful surface and its zigzag route offer impressive views of the city and the surrounding area.

The colourful fountain, Fuente de los Chorros, is also worth a visit. It is decorated with ceramic mosaics depicting the coats of arms of various towns in the province of Alicante and has 221 jets.

When it comes to hiking trails in Polop, one route stands out from the rest: the ascent of Mount Ponotx, known as the Sleeping Lion. A large limestone mass with vertical walls that rises to a height of 1,183 metres. It is a very difficult route, due to the positive difference of over a thousand metres, and is reserved for people with very good physical condition and who are used to this type of mountain route.

There are many different restaurants in Polop. But if you want to experience an unforgettable culinary experience, you should make a reservation at the restaurant Ca l'Àngels. Vicent and Paco Teuler run the restaurant together with other family members. Traditional cuisine with local products (even from their own garden) is the order of the day. An innovative wine list and homemade desserts round off the excellent offer.
