For sale, a modern villa located in a peaceful area of La Manga del Mar Menor, just 300 metres from Playa Honda beach. The property offers 4 bedrooms, spacious interiors, a private swimming pool, large terraces and a private solarium, making it an excellent choice both as a permanent residence and as a holiday home on the Costa Cálida.

The villa has been designed with comfortable Mediterranean living in mind. The main level features a bright and spacious living and dining area together with an open-plan kitchen with an island or breakfast bar. Large glazed doors open directly onto the terrace, garden and private pool area, creating a natural connection between the interior and outdoor spaces. This level also includes one bedroom and a bathroom.

Upstairs, there is a master bedroom, two additional bedrooms and two bathrooms. The terraces provide further outdoor space for relaxing and enjoying the sunny climate throughout most of the year.

The property also includes a spacious basement that can be adapted to suit the owner’s needs, for example as an additional lounge, games room, gym, home cinema or guest area. A private rooftop solarium completes the property, offering an ideal space for sunbathing, relaxing and enjoying views of the surrounding area, palm trees and nearby mountains.

The villa is set on a private plot starting from approximately 440 m², providing plenty of space

for outdoor living. The private pool, terraces and garden create a comfortable area for spending time with family and friends.

Location

One of the main advantages of this property is its location just 300 m from Playa Honda, a peaceful sandy beach on the Mar Menor. The proximity to the sea means you can walk to the beach in just a few minutes and enjoy swimming, seaside walks and water sports throughout the year.

La Manga del Mar Menor is one of the most distinctive coastal areas in the Murcia region, located between the Mar Menor and the Mediterranean Sea. The area offers a wide range of restaurants, shops, marinas, sailing clubs and leisure facilities. The Mar Menor is particularly popular for sailing, windsurfing, kayaking and other water sports.

Cabo de Palos, with its popular marina, restaurants and picturesque coastline, is also within easy reach. The historic city of Cartagena can be reached by car in approximately 25–30 minutes.

An attractive option for anyone looking for a modern villa with a private pool, generous outdoor space and the opportunity to live just 300 metres from the beach.
