Location

Alicante is a beautiful city on the south-east coast of Spain and is the ideal destination for those looking for an unforgettable experience. With its warm climate, magnificent beaches and rich history, it is no wonder that Alicante is one of the most popular tourist destinations in Spain. The city's tranquil Mediterranean waters provide the perfect setting for water sports and beachcombing, making it a favourite with locals and tourists alike.

Golf

Alicante is home to some of the most beautiful golf courses in the region, making it a popular destination for golf lovers. Alicante Golf is one of the most famous courses, offering 18 challenging holes designed by the legendary Seve Ballesteros. The course offers magnificent views of the Mediterranean Sea and is a true paradise for golf lovers.

History

Alicante's rich history is evident in its magnificent monuments, such as the Castle of St Barbara. This historic fortress, rising from the top of Benacantil mountain, offers breathtaking views of the city and the sea. The castle's walls and towers have stood for centuries, and the site bears witness to the many civilisations that have settled in Alicante over the years, including Romans, Moors and Christians.

Beaches

Of course, no trip to Alicante is complete without a visit to its beautiful beaches. The city's warm climate and crystal clear waters make it the ideal place for water sports and beachcombing. Playa del Postiguet is one of the most popular beaches in the region, located in the heart of Alicante. The beach is a favourite with tourists and locals alike, offering stunning views of the city and the Mediterranean Sea.

Promenada

The Explanada de España Promenade is another must-visit place in Alicante. Located opposite the harbour, this lively boulevard is surrounded by terraces and pavement cafés, providing the perfect place to enjoy a cup of coffee or a delicious meal. The promenade is a popular meeting place for locals and tourists and an ideal place to enjoy the magnificent views of the sea and the city.
