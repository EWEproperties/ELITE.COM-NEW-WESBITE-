Modern 2 bedroom penthouse for sale in the newest key ready phase of Alcazaba Lagoon. Beautiful new construction home ready to move in and fully furnished. Large open plan living kitchen room with large floor to ceiling windows around the entire property. Corner penthouse with huge wraparound terrace accessible from all rooms in the property. 2 bedrooms, master ensuite and guest bedroom with guest bathroom.

Modern design property with 2 parking spaces, golf buggy space and large storage room. Lift access from the underground parking takes you to the property floor level 2. The resort is home to Europe’s first crystal water lagoon for water sports where residents can also enjoy 2 restaurants, swimming pools, a gymnasium, bar, reception, lounge, spa facilities and tropical gardens. The restaurant is open all year round for breakfast, lunch and dinner.
