Elite World Estates presents an exclusive boutique development of modern bungalow-style apartments located in the prestigious residential area of Los Balcones in Torrevieja, on the southern Costa Blanca. This is an excellent opportunity for buyers looking for a contemporary property in Spain that combines high-quality finishes, a peaceful setting, and a prime location just minutes from the Mediterranean beaches and the famous Pink Lake (Laguna Rosa).

This private gated community features stylish apartments with functional layouts, offering 2 or 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Buyers can choose between ground-floor apartments with private gardens or top-floor residences with spacious terraces and private rooftop solariums, providing the perfect space to relax and enjoy the Mediterranean climate all year round.

The interiors have been designed with a modern open-plan concept, featuring bright living and dining areas connected to fully equipped kitchens. Large floor-to-ceiling windows flood the interiors with natural light, while premium-quality materials and carefully selected finishes create an elegant and timeless living environment.

Each apartment is delivered to a high standard and includes a fully fitted kitchen equipped with household appliances (refrigerator, dishwasher, oven and hob), fully finished bathrooms with underfloor heating, electric shutters, built-in wardrobes in the bedrooms, and energy-efficient windows.

Residents can enjoy a large communal swimming pool with a dedicated children's area, beautifully landscaped gardens, and attractive relaxation spaces. Each apartment also includes a private parking space within the gated community.

One of the development's greatest advantages is its exceptional location. Los Balcones is one of Torrevieja's most sought-after residential areas, offering a peaceful atmosphere while providing easy access to all everyday amenities. Restaurants, supermarkets, shops, medical centres, and leisure facilities are all nearby. The sandy beaches of Torrevieja and Orihuela Costa are approximately 5 km away, while golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of prestigious golf courses, including Las Colinas Golf & Country Club.

Whether you are looking for a permanent residence, a stylish holiday home, or an attractive rental investment, this development offers an outstanding opportunity in one of the most desirable areas of the southern Costa Blanca.

Estimated completion: May or December 2027.

Location

The development is situated in the prestigious residential area of Los Balcones in Torrevieja, one of the most desirable locations on the southern Costa Blanca. The neighbourhood is renowned for its peaceful atmosphere, low-density residential environment, and close proximity to the famous Pink Lake (Laguna Rosa) and the Lagunas de La Mata y Torrevieja Natural Park, creating a unique microclimate that promotes health and well-being throughout the year.

Residents benefit from excellent local infrastructure, with supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, pharmacies, medical centres, schools, and the Habaneras Shopping Centre all within easy reach. Torrevieja town centre, the marina, and the beautiful sandy beaches of the Costa Blanca can be reached within just a few minutes by car.

For outdoor enthusiasts, the area offers numerous walking and cycling routes, sports facilities, and several renowned golf courses, including Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, Villamartín Golf, Campoamor Golf, and Las Ramblas Golf.

Excellent transport connections via the AP-7 motorway and the national road network provide quick access to Alicante and Murcia airports, making this location ideal both for year-round living and as a holiday home or rental investment.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala de lo Ferri, about 6 km by road, around 11 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio Público Romualdo Ballester, about 2.9 km.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 23 km, around 19 minutes by car.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 9 km, around 11 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud San Miguel de Salinas, about 6 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 2.5 km by road.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, about 800 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 53 km by road, around 40 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 69 km, around 50 minutes by car.
