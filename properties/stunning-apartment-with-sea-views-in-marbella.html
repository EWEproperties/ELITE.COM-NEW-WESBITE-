Location

Marbella is one of the most exclusive cities in the Mediterranean and the pride of the Costa del Sol. Twenty-eight kilometers of coastline, four marinas, fourteen golf courses and a privileged climate make it incomparable to other cities in this region.

A visit to Marbella is a unique experience. From shopping in luxury boutiques to dining in fine restaurants, partying at beach clubs and practicing every type of sport, the possibilities are endless. Although if relaxation is the most important thing, the city has numerous health and beauty centers and a wide and exclusive hotel offer in three main centers: Marbella, San Pedro de Alcántara and Nueva Andalucía-Puerto Banús.

Marbella is not only about luxury and wealth; it also offers green areas to explore. Alameda Park, dating back to the 18th century, covers much of the city center and boasts a variety of plant species, including the impressive Ficus Pandurata and Pinus Pinae. The Bonsai Museum, the first museum in Spain dedicated to bonsai, showcases valuable specimens that are up to 400 years old, providing a tranquil experience amidst the beauty of nature.

Lovers of good food will be delighted by a wide range of excellent restaurants offering cuisine from all over the world. Adventure seekers can try windsurfing, sailing or golf on the best courses, some of which are floodlit for night fun. For hikers, Mount La Concha provides an inspiring challenge, rewarding climbers with breathtaking views of Marbella. Additionally, the Sierra Nevada mountain range offers even greater mountain adventures.

The most popular, and according to many people also the best, beaches in Marbelia are: Playa de Guadalmina, Playa de Lindavista, Playa de San Pedro de Alcántara, Cortijo Blanco, Playa de Nueva Andalucía, Rio Verde Playa de Puerto Banús, Playa de El Ancón, Nagüeles, Fontanilla and Playa del Faro.
