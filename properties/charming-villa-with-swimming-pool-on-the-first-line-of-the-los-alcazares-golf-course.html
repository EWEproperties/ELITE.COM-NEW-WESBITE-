The ideal location of this intimate estate allows a short walk to the city center and the beach. Location

The picturesque town of Los Alcázares, located in the charming region of Murcia in Spain, tempts with its seaside charm and plenty of delightful attractions. Situated along the magnificent Mar Menor, a salt water lagoon famous for its crystal clear waters and healing properties, Los Alcázares offers an idyllic setting for an unforgettable holiday or a quiet getaway.

The area is connected by highway to Cartagena, Murcia and Alicante and the rest of the Costa Cálida and Costa Blanca beaches.

One of the city's greatest assets is its collection of beautiful beaches, each of which promises moments of bliss and relaxation. Golden sands stretch along the serene shores of the Mar Menor, creating a paradise for sun seekers and water lovers. From the lively and family-friendly Playa de Los Narejos to the more secluded and quiet Playa de Las Salinas, there's a beach for everyone. Visitors can sunbathe, swim or engage in a variety of water sports, including sailing, kayaking and windsurfing. The shallow and calm waters of the Mar Menor make it an ideal destination for families with children.

Los Alcázares boasts a vibrant food scene, offering a diverse range of restaurants, bars and cafes to tempt your taste buds. From traditional Spanish tapas to international cuisine, the city's restaurants cater to every palate. Taste fresh seafood from local ingredients, enjoy an authentic paella, or enjoy a refreshing drink at a beachside chiringuito while enjoying the stunning views. The warm and welcoming atmosphere of urban eateries enhances the overall dining experience, making it a real delight for food lovers.

In addition to beaches and dining options, Los Alcázares offers many activities to keep visitors entertained. Explore the charming streets of the city center, dotted with shops, boutiques and craft shops. Discover local handicrafts, souvenirs and unique treasures to commemorate your visit. For the adventurous, a variety of watersports resorts offer jet skiing, paddleboarding and scuba diving, allowing you to immerse yourself in the depths of the Mar Menor's underwater wonders.

Golf enthusiasts will find themselves in paradise as Los Alcázares is surrounded by a selection of world-class golf courses. Within a short distance you will discover famous golf courses such as La Serena Golf, Roda Golf and Mar Menor Golf Resort. Immerse yourself in the lush green fairways, perfect your swing and enjoy the Mediterranean climate that allows you to play golf all year round.

For history and culture, a visit to the nearby city of Cartagena is highly recommended. Explore ancient Roman ruins, wander the charming streets lined with colorful buildings, and immerse yourself in the rich history of this historic port city.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Punta Brava, about 5 km by road, around 8 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: I.E.S. ANTONIO MENÁRGUEZ COSTA, about 3 km, around 6 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Nuestra Señora del Carmen, about 17 km, around 17 minutes by car.
International school: Agave International School, about 25 km, around 22 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Los Alcázares, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 13 km by road, around 16 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Dialprix, about 2.7 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 43 km by road, around 30 minutes by car.
