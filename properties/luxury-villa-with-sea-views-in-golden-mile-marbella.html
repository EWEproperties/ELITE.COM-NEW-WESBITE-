With the mountains at the back and the sea in front, forming part of one of the best gated communities with 24-hour security measures in Marbella. It is one of the most secure and private residential complexes in Marbella.

The villas were designed by renowned architects Salvador Cejudo and Carlos Morales, with architecture designed to be an ode to elegance and mark the beginning of a new contemporary era. They have worked meticulously to create a new, fresh and different concept, in keeping with Marbella's exclusive market. This impressive project consists of five fabulously spacious homes with panoramic sea and mountain views.

Location

Marbella is synonymous with prestige and luxury for all. It attracts the attention of investors and tourists from all over the world, with spectacular estates worth up to more than 100 million! Politicians, sportsmen, musicians or screen stars have their properties here, and Marbella itself attracts crowds of tourists from all over the world. This is confirmed, among other things, by the results of a poll organised by a popular Belgian travel portal, which awarded Marbella the title of Best European Destinations 2022'.

Among the most prestigious, and therefore most desirable, locations are:

‘Golden Mile - The Golden Mile’ is one of the most beautiful and luxurious avenues in Spain and is also a symbol of Marbella. Here you will find legendary hotels and dazzling real estate, which includes a scaled-down replica of the White House, built by the late King of Saudi Arabia. The Golden Mile is a more than 6km-long avenue lined with classic and modern villas surrounded by lush vegetation. It is an ideal place to invest capital, especially in these times of rampant inflation.

La Zagaleta - this complex is impossible to pass by indifferently. It is located in an extraordinary setting - on a lake, between the sea and the mountains. There are only 400 residences on a huge site that could accommodate up to 4,000 properties, inhabited by, among others, people from the front pages of newspapers. However, La Zagaleta's excellent security guarantees them complete discretion and the comfort of a peaceful holiday at home on the Costa del Sol.

Puerto Banús - one of the most luxurious marinas in Spain, located between Marbella and San Pedro Alcantara. Those who manage to purchase a property in Puerto Banús are undoubtedly the lucky ones! Here you will find designer boutiques from all over the world, top restaurants, spectacular yachts and a Salvador Dali sculpture!

When visiting this luxury resort, be sure to visit the Parque de Alameda- said to be the green lungs of the city. Dating back to the 18th century, the Alameda Park covers an area of 20,000 m², which is 20% of the entire city centre. The Alameda Park is a place abundant in different plant species. The ones that attract the most attention are the spreading Ficus Pandurata and the Pinus Pinae.

No matter how you like to spend your free time, Marbella is sure to meet your expectations. For beachgoers, 26 km of beaches await. For gourmets of good cuisine - excellent restaurants , prestigious restaurants offering cuisine from all over the world. Those who like an active holiday can try their hand at windsurfing, sailing or golf. As far as the latter is concerned, it is worth mentioning that there are unrivalled, excellent quality golf courses spread around the city, which are also suitable for playing at night (they are specially illuminated for this purpose).

The most popular, and according to many, also the best beaches in Marbella are: Playa de Guadalmina, Playa de Lindavista, Playa de San Pedro de Alcántara, Cortijo Blanco, Playa de Nueva Andalucía, Rio Verde - Playa de Puerto Banús, Playa de El Ancón, Nagüeles, Fontanilla and Playa del Faro.

If you enjoy mountain climbing, then your attention should not miss the La Concha mountain rising majestically above the city. It measures 1,200 metres above sea level, and the best trail to climb it can be found in the town of Istán, located on the other side of the mountain. The hike is not the easiest, but any difficulties are compensated for by the incredible view that awaits you at the top! It is hard not to agree with the statement that this is the most beautiful panorama of the city of Marbella. On the other hand, for an even greater mountain challenge, the Sierra Nevada mountain range is recommended.
