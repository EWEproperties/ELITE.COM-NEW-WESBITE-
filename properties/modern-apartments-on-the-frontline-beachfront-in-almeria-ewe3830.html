Elite World Estates presents an exceptional new development located on the seafront in one of the most prestigious areas of Almerimar, on the Costa de Almería. This is an excellent opportunity for anyone looking for a modern apartment in Spain that combines a Mediterranean lifestyle, direct beach access, and outstanding leisure facilities.

The development offers spacious 1, 2, 3, and 4-bedroom apartments, thoughtfully designed to maximize comfort and natural light. Every home features a generous private terrace, while selected ground-floor apartments include private gardens. The exclusive penthouses boast spectacular terraces with panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea.

Residents can enjoy an extensive range of premium communal amenities, including three swimming pools, beautifully landscaped gardens, a fully equipped gym, a coworking space, a pool bar, and a concierge service. The gated community also benefits from controlled access and video surveillance, ensuring privacy and security.

The apartments are finished to a high standard and include a fully fitted kitchen with integrated appliances, ducted air conditioning with heating and cooling, underfloor heating in the bathrooms, an energy-efficient aerothermal system for domestic hot water, photovoltaic panels, and LED lighting. Each apartment comes with a private underground parking space and a storage room.

Located directly on the beachfront promenade and a wide sandy beach, the development is surrounded by restaurants, cafés, shops, a marina, a 27-hole golf course, and all the amenities required for comfortable year-round living.

Whether you are looking for a holiday home, a second residence by the sea, or a profitable investment property in one of Spain's sunniest regions, this development offers an exceptional lifestyle with more than 320 days of sunshine per year.

Location

The development is situated in the prestigious area of Almerimar, directly on the beachfront and adjacent to a wide sandy beach and the seafront promenade on the Costa de Almería. It is the perfect destination for those seeking tranquillity, comfort, and an active Mediterranean lifestyle. With more than 320 sunny days each year, residents can enjoy the region's exceptional climate throughout every season.

The surrounding area offers an excellent selection of restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, shops, a marina, and the renowned 27-hole Almerimar Golf Course. Thanks to its well-developed infrastructure, all essential services are within easy walking distance.

The location also provides convenient access to the city of Almería and Almería Airport. Granada, Málaga, and the Sierra Nevada mountains can all be reached within approximately two hours by car, while Alicante Airport is around a 3-hour and 15-minute drive away. This outstanding location makes the development an excellent choice for both holiday living and permanent residence.

Frequently asked questions
