Elite World Estates presents an exclusive collection of only 8 luxury detached villas located within the prestigious LA Sunset development in Los Alcázares. Positioned directly on the frontline of La Serena Golf, these properties offer maximum privacy, contemporary design, and spectacular views over the golf course and stunning sunsets.

Designed for the most demanding buyers, each villa features 3 spacious bedrooms and 2 or 3 elegant bathrooms. The master bedroom includes an en-suite bathroom and a private dressing room, ensuring maximum comfort and functionality.

The villas enjoy a west-facing orientation, allowing residents to admire panoramic golf views and beautiful sunsets every day. Large floor-to-ceiling windows and innovative hide-and-slide patio doors seamlessly connect the indoor living area with the terrace, garden, and private pool.

Each property includes a private swimming pool surrounded by landscaped gardens, modern terraces, and privacy walls. An additional highlight is the spacious rooftop solarium offering panoramic views of the golf course, the surrounding area, and the Mar Menor lagoon.

The high-quality specification includes a fully fitted premium kitchen with Silestone quartz worktops and integrated appliances. Year-round comfort is ensured by a fully installed ducted air-conditioning system with heating and cooling functions.

The development is located approximately 1 kilometre from the beaches of the Mar Menor and close to restaurants, shops, leisure facilities, and golf amenities. It is an excellent choice as a luxury holiday home, permanent residence, or rental investment.

Location

The charming coastal town of Los Alcázares, located in the Murcia region of Spain, offers a unique Mediterranean lifestyle on the shores of the Mar Menor, Europe's largest saltwater lagoon. The area is famous for its crystal-clear waters, mild climate, and excellent conditions for water sports.

Los Alcázares enjoys excellent motorway connections to Cartagena, Murcia, Alicante, and the rest of the Costa Cálida and Costa Blanca coastline.

The town is renowned for its beautiful sandy beaches, lively promenade, restaurants, cafés, and vibrant atmosphere throughout the year. Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of renowned golf courses including La Serena Golf, Roda Golf, and Mar Menor Golf Resort.
