A new residential development located just 400 metres from the beach in Dénia, one of the most popular coastal towns on the Costa Blanca. The location allows you to enjoy the Mediterranean Sea and peaceful surroundings while remaining within easy reach of the town centre, marina, restaurants, shops and all essential everyday services. Alicante and Valencia airports can both be reached by car in approximately one hour.

The residential complex has been designed with a strong focus on comfort, green spaces and environmentally friendly solutions. The project includes rainwater collection and reuse systems, photovoltaic panels, vegetation adapted to the local climate and charging points for electric vehicles.

The development offers apartments with 1, 2 or 3 bedrooms. Buyers can choose between ground-floor properties with private gardens, apartments with spacious terraces and penthouses with large private solariums, providing additional outdoor space to enjoy the Spanish sunshine.

The interiors are bright, modern and functional. The open-plan living area combines the lounge, dining area and a fitted kitchen equipped with selected appliances. Large windows provide plenty of natural light and open directly onto the terrace, creating an additional living space ideal for relaxing or dining outdoors.

One of the main highlights of the development is its extensive communal areas surrounded by Mediterranean vegetation. Residents will be able to enjoy several swimming pools, a private spa with sauna, areas for yoga and exercise, sensory gardens, vegetable gardens and social clubs. The complex will also feature picnic areas, bicycle workshops, pet care areas and dedicated storage space for bicycles and water sports equipment.

Estimated completion: end of 2028.

Location

Dénia is a popular seaside town located on the northern Costa Blanca, between Alicante and Valencia. It combines Mediterranean charm with excellent infrastructure, beautiful beaches, a marina, a historic town centre and a wide selection of restaurants.

The town offers approximately 15 km of varied coastline. The Las Marinas area is known for its long sandy beaches, while Las Rotas attracts visitors with its small coves and rocky coastline, ideal for snorkelling and diving.

Overlooking Dénia is the impressive Montgó mountain, surrounded by a protected natural park with numerous walking and cycling routes. Another major advantage is the town's port, which offers regular ferry connections to Ibiza, Mallorca and Formentera.

Its location just 400 metres from the beach, extensive leisure facilities and proximity to local amenities make this development an attractive option as a second home in Spain, a holiday apartment or a permanent residence.
