Location

San Javier is a coastal town with a lively shopping centre in its heart and a beautiful church square surrounded by bars and restaurants. It is located on the northern corner of the Mediterranean coast known as Costa Calida Murcia. San Javier is famous for its golden sandy beaches, warm climate and wide range of tourist attractions, including water sports, restaurants, bars and shops. In addition, the town has a rich history and culture, which is reflected in its historic buildings and monuments, such as the Church of San Francisco Javier and the Town Museum.

How to get there

From Murcia International Airport, you can reach the town of San Javier via the RM 19 motorway (35 km), and the journey takes about 25 minutes by car. From Alicante International Airport, you can reach the town via the AR-7 motorway (87 km) - 56 minutes by car.

Golf

Costa Calida is a mecca for golf lovers. There are many high-class golf courses here. The Nicklaus complex includes six courses designed by Nicklaus Design, including La Torre, El Valle, Alhama and Mar Menor. Mar Menor is a more difficult golf course with three holes, surrounded by large lakes. La Torre is a favourite among locals, as it is easier and more forgiving to play. Valle Golf Resort is located in a natural valley with spectacular views of the surrounding mountains. It is a course more suited to intelligent players with a strategy than players with a strong swing.

Alhama is considered by many to be the most difficult course in the Nicklaus Complex. It is a long, spectacular course, considered one of the best in Spain.

Beaches

San Javier has dozens of beaches in the area where you can spend your free time. The beaches are mainly located in the Santiago de La Ribera and La Manga del Mar Menor areas, i.e. the Mar Menor spit. One of the beaches of La Manga del Mar Menor is Playa Las Antillas.

Another interesting beach in the area is Playa de Hita, which is quite unusual as it is a protected area considered to be the habitat and breeding ground of many valuable migratory bird species. Most beaches are well equipped, with water and beach equipment available for hire, as well as a number of restaurants and beach bars. Toilets and showers are standard on the beaches. Other beaches in the San Javier area include Playa El Castillico and Banco del Tabal, both of which have been awarded blue flags.

Protected areas

There are many beautiful protected areas in the vicinity of San Javier. La Mayor o del Barón and Isla Perdiquera are two volcanic islands that are also home to protected bird species.

Other protected islands include La Isla Grosa and El Islote del Farallón, also of volcanic origin, which are home mainly to Roman gulls, but also other birds. All these places are accessible to tourists, but they are not as popular as the standard beaches, so perhaps they are a good idea for spending your holiday in San Javier?

Isla Grosa is an ideal place for scuba divers who come here from all over Spain.
