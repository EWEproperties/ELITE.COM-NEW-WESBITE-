Nestled within the serene neighborhood of the Cumbre del Sol, this premium Villa offers an exceptional living experience. Situated between the coastal towns of Jávea and Moraira on the enchanting North Costa Blanca, this villa is a modern masterpiece that seamlessly blends premium quality with contemporary style.

This remarkable villa features three bedrooms and three bathrooms, providing ample space for both relaxation and privacy. With a generous 425 square meters of living space and a sprawling 805 square meter plot, this property offers room to breathe and grow. Villa boasts a private and fenced plot, a thoughtfully landscaped garden, a refreshing swimming pool, convenient parking, a professionally designed interior lighting scheme, under-floor heating, ducted air conditioning, a fully equipped kitchen with appliances, a favorable south-eastern orientation, an energy rating of A, and breathtaking sea views.

A collection of modern villas in this consolidated area, where neighbors are already relishing their properties and enjoying stunning sea views. This exclusive location offers a peaceful and natural environment while providing easy access to the conveniences of Cumbre del Sol, Benitatxell, Moraira, and Jávea. It's a unique opportunity to live in a tranquil residential setting while being close to vibrant tourist and residential hubs.
