Location

Algorfa is a charming village located in the Vega Baja region of southern Costa Blanca. Surrounded by citrus groves and green landscapes, it has preserved its traditional Spanish character while offering all the amenities needed for everyday living.

The town offers shops, restaurants, cafés, sports facilities and a weekly local market, which remains an important part of community life. Thanks to its peaceful atmosphere and authentic character, Algorfa is popular both among Spanish residents and international buyers looking for a second home on the Costa Blanca.

A major attraction of the area is the nearby La Finca Golf Resort, offering an excellent golf course, restaurants and leisure facilities. The sandy beaches of Guardamar del Segura can be reached within minutes, while Alicante Airport is approximately 40 minutes away by car.

It is an ideal location for those seeking a peaceful Mediterranean lifestyle close to nature, while still enjoying easy access to the coast and the region's main attractions.
