Are you looking for a private villa with a pool at an attractive price, but also in an elegant neighbourhood? Visit this new project with us, located in a quiet area in Roldán in the Murcia region of Spain. This house offers a unique combination of luxury, style and affordability, making it a unique choice for those looking for a new home in the sun.

This is the third phase of this larger project. It includes a diverse selection of detached villas with 2 or 3 bedrooms, each with a private swimming pool equipped with a built-in seat (4.80 × 2.70 metres), as well as a pre-installation for a heat pump and a solar shower.

These magnificent villas also offer private gardens, car parks and solariums. Located on the outskirts of Roldan in the charming village of Los Cachimanes, often referred to as Campo de Cartagena, the development enjoys a sun-drenched atmosphere amidst orchards and is close to numerous amenities and golf courses. The highly recommended beaches of the Mar Menor are only 19 km away, Murcia Airport 18 km and Alicante Airport around 103 km.

These villas have been designed with an open-plan living/dining/kitchen area. On the ground floor, there are 2 bedrooms with 2 bathrooms. Outside, there is a veranda and a pool ranging from 17 to 20 m2, a 45 m2 solarium and a garden with high-quality grey non-slip porcelain stoneware combined with artificial grass in the pedestrian entrance area.

The kitchen is fully equipped with an oven, microwave, refrigerator, integrated dishwasher, extractor fan and a comfortable kitchen island. The bedrooms are equipped with padded wardrobes with drawers, and the bathrooms are fully equipped with wall-mounted toilets, shower screens, dressing tables, mirrors and lights. Additional features include LED lighting both inside and out, a summer kitchen on the solarium with a sink and refrigerator socket, as well as private parking on the plot.

The residential area will offer a range of amenities including a dog park, volleyball court, paddle tennis court, bowling alley, gym, children's parks, green areas, barbecue areas and a cycle path connecting to the centre of Roldan.

Planned completion date: November 2026, depending on the model

Location

Roldán is a charming village in the Murcia region of Spain, known for its peaceful rural atmosphere and beautiful natural surroundings. Located near the Mar Menor, Roldán offers easy access to beautiful beaches and golf courses. The town has several amenities including shops, restaurants, bars and supermarkets. For sports enthusiasts, there are numerous facilities such as sports complexes and hiking trails. Roldán combines the best of quiet country life with easy access to urban amenities, making it an ideal location for both permanent residents and holidaymakers.
