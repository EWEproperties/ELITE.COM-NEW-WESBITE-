Location

Nestled in the heart of the Costa Blanca, Sierra Cortina is a spectacular residential area located in the charming town of Finestrat, Spain. Boasting an enviable location with breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding mountains, Sierra Cortina is a truly exceptional destination that offers the ultimate in luxury living.

Finestrat is a picturesque town just 8 km from the popular, large city of Benidorm and 40 km from Alicante and its international airport. Known for its stunning mountain views, pristine beaches and rich cultural heritage, Finestrat is the perfect destination for anyone looking for a relaxing and memorable experience. There is no shortage of exciting things to do in Finestrat.

Beaches

The area is renowned for its stunning beaches, including the beautiful Levante Beach and Poniente Beach, both of which belong to the neighboring town of Benidorm. Benidorm is a popular destination known for its impressive skyline of skyscrapers and high-rise buildings, as well as its vibrant nightlife and wide range of entertainment options.

Places to Visit

In addition to the beautiful beaches, Sierra Cortina and the surrounding area is home to several other attractions that are well worth a visit. The Terra Mitica theme park is a popular destination for families and thrill-seekers, offering a range of exhilarating rides, shows, and attractions. The park is also home to several restaurants, cafes, and shops, making it a great place to spend a day out.

For those interested in history and culture, the town of Finestrat is home to several historic landmarks, including the Castell de Finestrat, a medieval castle that dates back to the 12th century, and the beautiful Santa Maria church, which features stunning gothic architecture.

Nearby Towns

The area surrounding Sierra Cortina is also home to several charming towns and villages that are well worth a visit. The nearby town of Villajoyosa is known for its beautiful multi-colored houses and its delicious chocolate industry, while the town of Altea is renowned for its picturesque old town, stunning beaches, and vibrant cultural scene. The city of Alicante, which is only a short drive away, is a bustling metropolis that offers a wide range of cultural, entertainment, and shopping opportunities, including the stunning Santa Barbara Castle, the Mercado Central, and the Explanada de España.
