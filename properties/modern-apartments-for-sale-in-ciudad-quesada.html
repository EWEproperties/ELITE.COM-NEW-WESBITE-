Discover a brand-new private residential complex in the heart of Ciudad Quesada, one of the most desirable areas on the southern Costa Blanca. This modern development combines contemporary Mediterranean design, comfort, privacy and an excellent location close to beaches, golf courses and all everyday amenities.

It is the perfect place for those looking to enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle in a peaceful yet highly developed area of Spain. The beautiful sandy beaches of Guardamar del Segura and La Mata are less than 10 minutes away by car, while Alicante Airport can be reached in approximately 35 minutes.

All essential services are within walking distance, including restaurants, supermarkets, cafés, pharmacies, shops and leisure facilities. Whether you are searching for a holiday home in Spain, a permanent residence under the sun or a smart property investment on the Costa Blanca, this modern development offers exceptional value and quality of life.

The residential complex is located in the prestigious area of Doña Pepa in Ciudad Quesada — an elegant urbanisation known for its wide avenues, green surroundings and beautiful views towards the Natural Park and the famous salt lakes of Torrevieja.

This charming area perfectly combines tranquillity with excellent infrastructure, making it ideal both for holidays and year-round living.

Location

Ciudad Quesada is located in the south of the Costa Blanca, just 8 km from the vibrant coastal city of Torrevieja. The nearby towns of Rojales and San Fulgencio offer additional services, restaurants and traditional Spanish charm.

The area benefits from excellent transport connections and easy access to Alicante International Airport, which offers direct flights to most major European cities.

Beautiful Beaches Nearby

One of the biggest advantages of Ciudad Quesada is its proximity to some of the best beaches on the Costa Blanca. Within a short drive you can reach the golden sandy beaches of Guardamar del Segura, La Mata and El Moncayo, famous for their crystal-clear waters, beach promenades and relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere.

Nature and Outdoor Lifestyle

Nature lovers will appreciate the nearby Natural Park of La Mata and Torrevieja lagoons. The area offers beautiful walking and cycling routes surrounded by unique landscapes and rich birdlife. It is an ideal place for hiking, outdoor sports and enjoying the sunny climate all year round.

Golf and Leisure

Golf enthusiasts can enjoy the renowned La Marquesa Golf Course, located just minutes away from the development. Several other prestigious golf courses can also be found nearby, making this region one of the best golf destinations in Spain.

The area also offers

  • Tennis and paddle courts
  • Wellness centres and spas
  • Water sports
  • Shopping centres
  • International restaurants and beach clubs

Wellness and Relaxation

Ciudad Quesada is also known for its relaxed lifestyle and wellness facilities. One of the highlights is the Spa at Hotel La Laguna, where visitors can enjoy thermal circuits, saltwater jacuzzis, massages and wellness treatments designed to help you fully disconnect and recharge.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja del Camp, about 9 km by road, around 13 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen (about 2.8 km) and Escuela Infantil Municipal Beniófar (about 4 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud de Benijófar, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Quirón/USP Hospital San Jaime, about 12 km by road, around 15 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is ALDI, about 700 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 58 km by road, around 45 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 68 km, around 50 minutes by car.
