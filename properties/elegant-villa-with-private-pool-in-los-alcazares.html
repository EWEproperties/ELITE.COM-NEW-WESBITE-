Location

Los Alcázares is a charming coastal town located in the beautiful Murcia region of Spain. Situated along the stunning Mar Menor lagoon, famous for its crystal-clear waters and therapeutic properties, Los Alcázares offers the perfect destination for unforgettable holidays or peaceful year-round living.

The area is well connected by motorway to Cartagena, Murcia, and Alicante, as well as the beaches of both Costa Cálida and Costa Blanca.

One of the town’s greatest attractions is its collection of beautiful beaches. Golden sands stretch along the calm shores of the Mar Menor, creating a paradise for sun lovers and water sports enthusiasts. From the lively and family-friendly Playa de Los Narejos to the quieter Playa de Las Salinas, there is a beach for everyone.

Visitors can enjoy swimming, sunbathing, sailing, kayaking, paddleboarding, and windsurfing. The shallow and calm waters of the Mar Menor make it especially ideal for families with children.

Los Alcázares also boasts a vibrant culinary scene with a wide selection of restaurants, bars, and cafés. From traditional Spanish tapas to international cuisine, the town offers something for every taste. Enjoy fresh seafood, authentic paella, or a refreshing drink at a beachfront chiringuito while admiring spectacular sea views.

Beyond its beaches and gastronomy, Los Alcázares offers plenty of entertainment and leisure activities. Explore the charming streets filled with boutiques, artisan shops, and local markets, or discover unique souvenirs and handcrafted products.

For golf lovers, the area is a true paradise. Nearby you will find prestigious golf courses such as:

La Serena Golf

Roda Golf

Mar Menor Golf Resort

The Mediterranean climate allows for golfing all year round.

For those interested in history and culture, a visit to nearby Cartagena is highly recommended. Discover ancient Roman ruins, colourful streets, and the fascinating heritage of this historic Mediterranean port city.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Punta Brava, about 5 km by road, around 7 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: I.E.S. ANTONIO MENÁRGUEZ COSTA, about 2.9 km.
Private school: Colegio Nuestra Señora del Carmen, about 16 km, around 16 minutes by car.
International school: Agave International School, about 24 km, around 21 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Los Alcázares, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 13 km by road, around 15 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Dialprix, about 2.3 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 43 km by road, around 30 minutes by car.
