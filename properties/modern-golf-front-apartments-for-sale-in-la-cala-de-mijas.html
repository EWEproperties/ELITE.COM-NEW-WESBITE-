Location – La Cala de Mijas

La Cala de Mijas is one of the most sought-after destinations on the Costa del Sol, renowned for its unique combination of Mediterranean charm, excellent infrastructure, and relaxed atmosphere. Once a traditional fishing village, it has evolved into an elegant seaside resort while preserving its authentic Andalusian character.

The town offers wide sandy beaches, a picturesque seafront promenade, an excellent selection of restaurants serving both local and international cuisine, cafés, boutiques, and all the services required for comfortable year-round living. The area is also home to prestigious international schools, medical centres, supermarkets, and modern sports facilities.

La Cala de Mijas is a true paradise for golf enthusiasts. Several prestigious golf courses, including Calanova Golf Club, are located nearby, along with many other renowned golf clubs across Costa del Sol, making the region one of Europe's premier golfing destinations.

Its strategic location provides excellent connections to Marbella, Fuengirola, and Málaga. Málaga International Airport is approximately a 30-minute drive away, offering convenient flights to major cities throughout Europe.

The combination of beautiful beaches, world-class golf courses, outstanding infrastructure, and an exceptional quality of life makes La Cala de Mijas an ideal place for permanent residence, a holiday home, or a profitable real estate investment.
