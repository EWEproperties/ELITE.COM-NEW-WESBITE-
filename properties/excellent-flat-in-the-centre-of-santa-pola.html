Location

Santa Pola is a seaside town located in the southern part of the Costa Blanca. It is only 19 km from the beautiful city of Alicante and only 14 km from El Altet Alicante airport, where you can easily reach it by public transport, both from the airport and from the centre of Alicante.

The city is famous for its beautiful beaches, monuments and diverse culture, making it a popular tourist destination for people from all over the world. It is the ideal place to spend a family holiday or a seaside getaway for two, where you can relax and enjoy the local seafood, the atmosphere and the impressive natural environment, which includes salt marshes with flamingos and sand dunes.

Beaches

Santa Pola is a real paradise for beachgoers. Here you will find long stretches of fine white sand, varied by more intimate, picturesque coves. Many of these beaches are close to the town centre, so you don't need to take any transport, you can walk from your flat in Santa Pola.

A beach worth going to is Playa Levante, awarded the Blue Flag. Here you can relax with all the amenities of a beach that is considered comfortable and well-equipped. Another place where we recommend spending a sunny day is Playa de Varadero, located in the more urbanised eastern part of Santa Pola. These are two ideal places for a family break in the sun, on the warm sand enjoying the sea breeze of the Costa Blanca.

If you prefer quieter, less crowded places with a more homely feel, then Playa de la Ermita, Playa de la Gola, or the coves of Calas del Cuartel and Calas de L'Aljub should be on your list.

Water sports

Santa Pola is a great place for water sports enthusiasts. The beaches here offer ideal conditions for this type of sport. Both the weather conditions and the infrastructure of the beaches are favourable for windsurfing and kitesurfing. It is in Santa Pola that the world windsurfing championships are held.

The best beaches for windsurfing in Santa Pola are Gran Playa, Playa Lisa, Playa Tamarit and Playa de la Gola. What's more, near these beaches you will find clubs and sports centres where you can rent equipment and sign up for windsurfing or kitesurfing classes. Other suggestions for water sports in Santa Pola include sailing, paddle boarding, kayaking, scuba diving and snorkelling.

Salinas de Santa Pola

In the western part of Santa Pola, the Salinas de Santa Pola (salt pans of Santa Pola) have been protected as a nature reserve and this is the zone where the numerous wild birds in the area find refuge. In the reserve there is the Museo de la Sal (Salt Museum) where you can learn all about the salt extraction process and its history. Huge ‘icebergs’ of salt remind us that we are in Santa Pola, the salt kingdom.

Salinas is the perfect place to relax and spend a day with the children in nature. There are several walking trails leading through the park, allowing you to observe the unique, extremely interesting and diverse flora and fauna. The undisputed stars of the salt reserve are the resident flamingos. These elegant birds, with their pink plumage, look spectacular.

Isla Tabarca

Isla Tabarca (Tabarca Island) is the closest from Santa Pola, the two places being only 9 kilometres apart. Isla Tabarca is the smallest island in Spain. It is inhabited all year round.

The story of Tabarca reads like an adventure novel starring pirates. It was the highwaymen, the sea wolves who sought refuge on the island for hundreds of years. There are several transport companies in Santa Pola that will take you to the island. The journey takes no longer than 15 minutes! Don't forget to bring your snorkelling gear, as the pebbly beaches and picturesque little coves make for an ideal place to practice this type of sport.
