New apartments for sale in Punta Prima, Orihuela Costa, approximately 2 km from the sea and the beach. This modern residential complex consists of 56 properties arranged around a spacious communal area with a swimming pool, landscaped gardens and areas designed for relaxation.

The development offers apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, available on different floors. Ground-floor properties offer private outdoor space, while homes on the upper floors and penthouses provide additional terrace areas and, depending on the selected model, a private solarium. According to the current project specification, ground-floor and middle-floor apartments mainly offer 3 bedrooms, while penthouses are available with 2 or 3 bedrooms.

The interiors have been designed in a modern and functional style. Kitchens will be delivered furnished, and the apartments will also include built-in wardrobes and electric shutters. Each property comes with a parking space in the underground garage. Kitchen appliances are not included in the standard specification.

The heart of the development is a spacious communal leisure area with a swimming pool and landscaped green areas. The residential complex has been designed so that most of the properties surround the central recreational area, providing residents with easy access to the pool and communal spaces.

Expected completion: end of 2028.

Location

Punta Prima is one of the popular coastal areas of Orihuela Costa, located between Torrevieja and Playa Flamenca. The development is situated in the La Ciñuelica area, close to shops, supermarkets, restaurants and other everyday services. A supermarket is located approximately 0.3 km from this part of the development.

The beaches of Punta Prima are approximately 2 km from the property.

Playa Flamenca, La Zenia and the Zenia Boulevard shopping centre are also located nearby. The area also provides convenient access to several golf courses on Orihuela Costa and the N-332 road, offering easy connections to Torrevieja and other destinations along the southern Costa Blanca.
