Elite World Estates presents a modern, detached villa located directly on the prestigious golf course in sunny Los Alcázares on the Costa Cálida. The property will be ready for possession by the end of the year, and the developer offers the option of fully furnished and equipped, making the villa ready for immediate occupancy or rental.

The villa is designed on one level and offers approximately 105 m² of modern living space. Residents have access to three comfortable bedrooms, two elegant bathrooms, and a spacious living room with an open-plan kitchen and large windows that provide ample natural light and beautiful views of the golf course.

The property sits on a plot of approximately 260 m² and features a private pool, garden, large terrace, and a private rooftop solarium—the perfect place to relax and enjoy the Spanish sun year-round.

A major advantage of this location is its direct access to the golf course and proximity to the Mar Menor beaches, restaurants, shops, and all the essential amenities of everyday life. Murcia Airport is approximately 25 minutes' drive away, and Alicante Airport is approximately 55 minutes away.

This is an ideal option for a luxury holiday home, a rental investment, or a comfortable place to live year-round in Spain.

Location

Los Alcázares is a charming seaside town located in the Murcia region, on the sunny Costa Cálida. The town delights with its unique climate, tranquil atmosphere, and direct access to the Mar Menor, the largest saltwater lagoon in Europe, known for its warm, shallow waters and favorable year-round microclimate.

Thanks to excellent transport links to cities such as Cartagena, Murcia, and Alicante, as well as quick access to highways and airports, Los Alcázares is an ideal place for both year-round living and a relaxing holiday.

The town is renowned for its wide, sandy beaches stretching along the calm waters of the Mar Menor. It's an ideal destination for families with children, water sports enthusiasts, and those seeking relaxation by the sea. Popular beaches such as Playa de Los Narejos and Playa de Las Salinas offer beautiful promenades, atmospheric chiringuitos, and excellent conditions for sailing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and windsurfing.

Los Alcázares also delights with its Mediterranean lifestyle and rich culinary offerings. Numerous restaurants and bars offer fresh seafood, traditional paella, local tapas, and international cuisine. Evening strolls along the promenade, cafés overlooking the sea, and a friendly atmosphere make this place a year-round attraction for locals and tourists.

The city also offers a wide range of attractions and amenities, from boutiques and local shops to sports centers, marinas, and recreational areas. Active lifestyle enthusiasts will appreciate the numerous cycling trails, water sports, and proximity to renowned golf courses such as La Serena Golf, Roda Golf, and Mar Menor Golf Resort.

This is a unique place where the Mediterranean lifestyle, beautiful beaches, and modern infrastructure create ideal conditions for relaxation, investment, and life in Spain.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Punta Brava, about 5 km by road, around 9 minutes by car.
