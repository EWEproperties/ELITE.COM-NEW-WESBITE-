Location

San Miguel de Salinas is located 75 metres above sea level. It is the highest town in this area of the southern Costa Blanca. Several locations in San Miguel offer panoramic views of both the salt lakes of Torrevieja and the beaches of the Mediterranean.

But it is not so much the slightly elevated location as the central location that has prompted many Europeans to buy a home here in recent decades. The relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere makes San Miguel an authentic Spanish town where life moves slowly. The original inhabitants are the vast majority here. Most home buyers have found a home mainly in suburban urbanisations such as El Galan, Blue Lagoon, Blue Hills and Las Filipinas.

San Miguel's central location means it is only a short car ride to bustling Torrevieja, the beaches of the Orihuela Costa or any of the golf courses in the area. The town has many amenities such as restaurants, shops and international sports facilities. For a municipality of such limited size, the town has a very full calendar of festivals each year. The most famous of these is the fiesta in honour of the patron saint San Miguel, after whom the town and church are named. The most famous is the district where dozens of cave houses can still be found. Most of them are now used as shops, art workshops and even restaurants.
