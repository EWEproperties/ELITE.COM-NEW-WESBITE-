Location

Located approximately 45 km south of Alicante, Torrevieja is renowned for its exceptional microclimate, considered one of the healthiest in Europe. This unique environment is largely created by the area's extensive salt lakes and two remarkable lagoons—Laguna de Torrevieja and Laguna de La Mata—situated in the heart of the city. One lagoon is famous for its striking pink waters, while the other is known for its green hues. Together they form a protected natural park and provide a habitat for numerous bird species, including flamingos. The distinctive pink colour of Laguna de Torrevieja is created by its high salt concentration combined with naturally occurring algae and microorganisms.

La Mata Lagoon, with its green waters, is home to an interpretation centre and an abundance of native Mediterranean flora, including Aleppo pine forests and wild orchids. The surrounding area is also known for its vineyards, where the traditional local Vino de La Mata is produced.

Torrevieja is a paradise for beach lovers. From the 2.5-kilometre Blue Flag Playa de La Mata to the lively Playa del Cura and Playa de los Locos, there is a beach for every lifestyle. The city's natural rock pools offer a unique swimming experience, complemented by iconic sculptures such as the Sea Man and Beautiful Lola. Cala Ferris and Cala Piteras are equally impressive, offering crystal-clear waters, palm-lined coves, and excellent snorkelling opportunities.

A stroll along the Vista Alegre Promenade reveals the vibrant atmosphere of Torrevieja, with its wide selection of restaurants, cafés, bars, boutiques, and marina. Families can enjoy attractions such as Aquopolis Water Park and nearby amusement parks, while the city also comes alive during the summer with open-air beach clubs and nightlife.

Sports enthusiasts will appreciate Torrevieja's outstanding facilities, including football, tennis, handball, rugby, swimming, sailing, and numerous water sports. Once a small coastal settlement centred around a historic watchtower—"Torre Vieja", meaning "Old Tower"—the town has grown into one of the Costa Blanca's most vibrant and desirable destinations.

With more than 300 days of sunshine each year, Torrevieja is far more than a holiday destination—it is an ideal place to enjoy a relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle throughout the year.
