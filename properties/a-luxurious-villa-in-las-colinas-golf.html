For sale, an exceptional ready-to-move-in villa featuring modern, organic architecture, set on a spacious **1,254 m² plot**. The property stands out for its distinctive design, curved lines, large windows and beautifully landscaped garden, seamlessly integrating the house with its Mediterranean surroundings. The villa is ready for immediate occupancy and is offered with a package of indoor and outdoor furniture.

The property offers **332 m² of built area** and features **3 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms**. An additional **124 m² basement** provides further space. The different levels of the villa are connected by an impressive spiral staircase and a **private lift with three stops**.

At the heart of the villa is a spacious open-plan living area combining the lounge, dining room and modern kitchen. Large-format glazing allows the interior to open almost completely onto the terrace and pool area, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor living space. The kitchen features FENIX cabinetry, a worktop with integrated hob and sink, a Bora extraction system and Bosch appliances.

Outside, the property offers an impressive **120 m² of terraces**, a beautifully landscaped garden and a **40 m² private swimming pool** with an irregular, organic shape. The pool features LED lighting and a saltwater chlorination system. The garden is equipped with irrigation and lighting systems, while the landscaping has been carefully designed to blend naturally with the surrounding environment.

The villa offers a high level of technology and comfort, including **underfloor heating throughout the property, hot and cold air conditioning, geothermal energy, photovoltaic panels and a smart home automation system**. Energy- and water-saving solutions have also been incorporated, including the recovery and reuse of greywater and rainwater.

The property also includes a **72 m² covered parking area**. This is an excellent choice for those looking for a ready-to-move-in villa with distinctive architecture, a large private plot, swimming pool and advanced modern technology.

Location

The villa is located within **Las Colinas Golf & Country Club**, one of the most prestigious and green residential areas on the southern Costa Blanca. The surroundings are characterised by low-density development, Mediterranean vegetation and a high level of privacy, making it an ideal location for both year-round living and holidays.

The resort is home to a renowned golf course, restaurants and leisure facilities. The beaches of **Campoamor and Mil Palmeras** are also within easy reach, while Orihuela Costa, with its wide range of shops, restaurants and services, can be reached within a short drive. **Alicante Airport is approximately 45–50 minutes away**, while Murcia Airport can be reached in around 40 minutes.

The **Racquet Club**, offering tennis, table tennis, football and basketball, together with a scenic trail winding through the Mediterranean forest, provides residents and guests with numerous opportunities for outdoor activities, whether on foot or by mountain bike.

The resort's four restaurants offer a varied gastronomic selection, ranging from traditional Italian cuisine at **il Palco**, to international dishes with a Mediterranean influence at **unik**, as well as Japanese, Mexican and Peruvian cuisine at **umawa**. Just a few minutes from Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, directly on La Glea Beach, is **WOW Beach**, an ideal place to enjoy the sun, sea and Mediterranean cuisine.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?
