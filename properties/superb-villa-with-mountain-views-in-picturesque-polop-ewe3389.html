Location

On the beautiful Costa Blanca, in the hinterland of the province of Alicante, you will find charming towns that are often overshadowed by other seaside or more famous ones. A clear example is Polop de la Marina, located a few kilometres from Benidorm, Altea or Castell de Guadalest, with a beautiful historic centre that you will immediately fall in love with.

Located in the Marina Baja region of Alicante, about 15 kilometres from the coast, Polop de la Marina is a small town of less than 5,000 inhabitants.

The old town of Polop, perched on a hill and developed at the foot of a castle of Arab origin, is one of the most interesting and best preserved in the province. Its picturesque, colourful streets full of flowers and carved ornaments are the inspiration for many artists.

The most outstanding building in the historical case is the church of San Pedro Apóstol, in the upper part of the town, just before the path leading to the castle.

Although there are few remains of Polop Castle, a trip to its ruins, located at the top of the hill on which the old town is located, is a must. Without a doubt, the path leading to the castle itself is one of the most interesting points to visit in Polop. Impeccably groomed, like the rest of the community, the successful pavement and its zigzagging route offer us impressive views of the town and the surrounding area.

The colourful fountain - Fuente de los Chorros - also deserves much attention. It is decorated with ceramic mosaics with the coats of arms of the various towns in the province of Alicante and has as many as 221 springs.

If we are talking about hiking trails in Polop, there is one route that stands out: the climb up Ponotx mountain known as the Sleeping Lion'. A large limestone mass with vertical walls that rises to a height of 1183 metres. It is a route with a high degree of difficulty, due to the positive difference of more than a thousand metres, being a route reserved for those in very good physical condition and accustomed to this type of mountain route.

There is a wide variety of restaurants in Polop. But if you want an unforgettable culinary experience, you must make a reservation at the Ca l'Àngels restaurant. Vicenta and Paco Teuler, together with other family members, run a restaurant where traditional cuisine with local products (even from their own garden) reigns supreme. An innovative wine list and artisanal desserts crown the excellent offer.
