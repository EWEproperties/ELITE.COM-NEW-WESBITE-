Elite World Estates presents an exceptional residential development located in the prestigious area of La Cala de Finestrat – one of the most sought-after locations on the Costa Blanca. This unique setting perfectly combines the tranquillity of a Mediterranean resort with the proximity of vibrant Benidorm, offering residents comfort, excellent infrastructure, and quick access to the sea.

The modern complex consists of just 47 elegant apartments with 1, 2, and 3 bedrooms. Contemporary architecture, spacious terraces, and large windows fill every home with natural light, allowing residents to enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle all year round.

The apartments have been designed with comfort and functionality in mind. The open-plan living area seamlessly connects the lounge, dining room, and modern kitchen, creating the perfect space for both everyday living and entertaining family and friends. The high standard of finishing is enhanced by premium materials, most of which come from the renowned Porcelanosa brand, known for its quality, durability, and timeless design.

Each property includes an underground parking space and a private storage room, providing additional convenience and practicality.

The development offers impressive communal areas inspired by luxury resort living. Residents will enjoy outdoor swimming pools, a heated year-round pool, indoor and outdoor gyms, a large jacuzzi, sauna, Turkish bath, multi-purpose leisure area, pétanque court, and beautifully landscaped green areas designed for relaxation and wellbeing.

One of the project's greatest advantages is its location. La Cala de Finestrat beach is only approximately 850 metres away, allowing residents to reach the sea with a pleasant walk. Restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, schools, and the La Marina Shopping Centre are all located nearby.

Another major benefit is the proximity to Benidorm – one of the most famous destinations on the Spanish coast. Within just a few minutes, residents can reach its sandy beaches, marina, golf courses, restaurants, shopping centres, and year-round attractions. This allows homeowners to enjoy both the peaceful atmosphere of La Cala de Finestrat and the vibrant lifestyle offered by Benidorm.

This development is an excellent opportunity for those seeking a modern apartment near the sea in a rapidly growing area that combines quality of life with strong investment potential on the Costa Blanca.

Completion is scheduled between December 2027 and May 2028.

Location

La Cala de Finestrat is one of the most desirable locations in the northern Costa Blanca, situated between the charming town of Finestrat and the famous city of Benidorm. It offers the perfect balance between a relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle and access to all services and attractions.

The heart of the area is the beautiful sandy beach of La Cala de Finestrat, known as one of the most family-friendly and peaceful beaches in the region. Crystal-clear waters, gentle access to the sea, and a pleasant promenade lined with restaurants and cafés make it an ideal place to enjoy life by the Mediterranean.

The proximity to Benidorm is one of the area's strongest advantages. Within minutes, residents can enjoy the famous Levante and Poniente beaches, marina, shopping centres, restaurants, and a wide range of entertainment options available throughout the year.

The area also offers prestigious golf courses, modern sports facilities, cycling and walking routes, and numerous green spaces. Shopping enthusiasts will appreciate the nearby La Marina Shopping Centre with its wide selection of stores, restaurants, and services.

La Cala de Finestrat benefits from excellent transport connections throughout the Costa Blanca. Alicante International Airport is approximately 40 minutes away, while the AP-7 motorway provides quick access along the Mediterranean coast.

The combination of sea views, modern infrastructure, year-round sunshine, and proximity to Benidorm makes La Cala de Finestrat one of the most attractive places to live and invest on the Costa Blanca.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala Racó del Conill, about 4 km by road, around 9 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP el Murtal, about 3 km, around 5 minutes by car.
Private school: Col·legi Nuestra Señora de los Dolores, about 8 km, around 11 minutes by car.
International school: Lope de Vega International School, about 13 km, around 15 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Cala de Benidorm, about 3 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Comarcal de la Marina Baixa, about 4 km by road, around 7 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is ALDI, about 2 km away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 60 km by road, around 45 minutes by car.
