For sale: a new, spacious villa located in a peaceful and green area of San Miguel de Salinas on the Costa Blanca. The property offers 4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, a private swimming pool, solarium and garage, creating a comfortable space both for year-round living and holiday stays.

The villa offers approximately 197 m² of built area and is situated on a private plot of around 500 m². Contemporary architecture, large windows and a south-facing orientation provide plenty of natural light and allow you to enjoy the sun for most of the day.

The heart of the home is a spacious living area with a lounge, dining space and modern kitchen. Large glazed doors create a seamless connection between the interior, terrace, garden and private pool. The outdoor areas have been designed with the Mediterranean lifestyle in mind, offering space for a chill-out area, barbecue zone or outdoor dining.

The villa includes 4 comfortable bedrooms and 3 bathrooms. An additional advantage is the private solarium, ideal for relaxing, enjoying the views and making the most of the sunny Costa Blanca climate. The bathrooms feature underfloor heating, and the property is also prepared for the construction of an additional basement of approximately 134 m², offering the possibility to create extra leisure rooms, a gym, home cinema or guest accommodation.

Estimated completion time: approximately 20 months from purchase.

Location

The property is situated in one of the quieter and greener areas of San Miguel de Salinas, surrounded by Mediterranean nature and offering open views of the surrounding area. The sandy beaches of Orihuela Costa are approximately 7–8 km away, while several renowned golf courses are located nearby, including Las Colinas Golf & Country Club and other popular golf clubs in the southern Costa Blanca.

La Zenia Boulevard, restaurants, supermarkets and the full range of services available in Orihuela Costa can also be reached within a short drive. The location combines a peaceful natural setting with easy access to beaches, golf courses and coastal towns.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala Mosca, about 15 km by road, around 17 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio Público Gloria Fuertes, about 4 km, around 5 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio San José de Calasanz, about 18 km, around 20 minutes by car.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 1.3 km.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud San Miguel de Salinas, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 11 km by road, around 13 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Minimarket, about 4 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 50 km by road, around 40 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 70 km, around 50 minutes by car.
