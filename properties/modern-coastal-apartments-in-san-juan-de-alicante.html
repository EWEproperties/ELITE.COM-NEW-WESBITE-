Location

San Juan is a picturesque coastal town situated in the province of Alicante, nestled on the Costa Blanca. It is conveniently located just 7 km from Alicante and 3 km from El Campello. Known for its stunning fine-sand beaches, vibrant streets, lush parks, and atmospheric restaurants and pubs, San Juan attracts visitors throughout the year.

One of the area’s highlights is the renowned San Juan Beach, considered one of the most beautiful beaches in the Alicante province. Located near the fishing village of El Campello on Cape Cabo de las Huertas, this 7-kilometer stretch of golden sand is accompanied by a charming promenade lined with pubs and restaurants. The beach caters to both those who enjoy relaxing on the sand and water sports enthusiasts, with several sports centers nearby offering equipment rentals.

San Juan is also adjacent to the Alicante Golf course, and its beach consistently earns the Blue Flag certification for its cleanliness and safety. The beach is just 25 km from Alicante International Airport, making it a convenient destination for Spanish families seeking a weekend escape.

Legend has it that in ancient times, a beautiful Moorish queen would bathe in the cove among the rocks at this very beach—a story that adds a layer of intrigue and allure for visitors.

San Juan is also famous for its vibrant celebrations, particularly the Fiesta de San Juan, one of Spain’s most anticipated festivals. Celebrated from June 19-24, this festival features dance parties, parades, fireworks displays, and traditional food tastings, with fire playing a central symbolic role.

The festivities kick off on June 19 with the appearance of colorful sculptures made from paper, cardboard, or wood, representing each district of Alicante. The city comes alive with dancing, singing, and celebrations that continue through the night. The festival also includes traditional bullfights at the Alicante Korrida, and the culinary delights of the season, such as brevas and cocas—savory cakes filled with tuna, onions, and nuts.

The highlight of the celebration is the Midsummer Night from June 23 to 24, when the sculptures are set ablaze in a spectacular display. Each district prepares its own sculpture, often depicting figures from the world of culture or politics. Residents also dress in traditional folk costumes, adding to the festive atmosphere and making the Fiesta de San Juan a truly unforgettable experience.
