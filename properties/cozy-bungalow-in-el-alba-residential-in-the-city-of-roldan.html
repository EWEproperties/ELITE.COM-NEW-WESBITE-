A new phase of an intimate residential development located within El Alba Mediterranean Resort in Roldán, in the Murcia region. This is an attractive option for those looking for a modern property in peaceful surroundings, with extensive leisure facilities and easy access to shops, restaurants, golf courses and the Mar Menor coastline.

Phase II offers bungalows with 2 or 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Buyers can choose between ground-floor properties with spacious terraces and top-floor apartments with a terrace and a large private solarium of over 50 m². The properties offer approximately 67–83 m² of living space, with current prices starting from €268,900. Completion of this phase is scheduled for December 2027.

The interiors have been designed in a bright, contemporary style. The kitchen comes fully fitted in white combined with wood-effect finishes and includes appliances such as a refrigerator, oven, microwave, extractor hood and dishwasher. The bedrooms feature built-in wardrobes, fully finished inside and fitted with drawers, while the bathrooms include vanity units with washbasins, illuminated mirrors, shower screens and rain showers.

The standard specification also includes LED lighting, a video intercom, pre-installation for ducted air conditioning in the living room and bedrooms, as well as a photovoltaic system. Each property also includes a parking space.

The top-floor bungalows are particularly attractive. In addition to a terrace, they feature a private solarium of approximately 50.55 m², providing plenty of space to relax and enjoy the Mediterranean climate. The solarium also includes a summer kitchen with a sink and worktop, as well as water and electricity connections.

Residential complex and leisure facilities

One of the main advantages of this development is its attractive setting. Residents can enjoy a communal swimming pool surrounded by palm trees and relaxation areas, a children's pool area, landscaped green spaces, a sports area and a children's playground. The complex already features beautifully landscaped leisure areas with a tropical feel, plenty of greenery and spaces designed for relaxation.

El Alba Mediterranean Resort also features the La Palapa leisure area, offering a restaurant and café, a large lagoon-style swimming pool and sports facilities, including padel courts. This means residents can enjoy a wide range of amenities without having to leave the resort.

Location

Roldán is a peaceful town belonging to the municipality of Torre Pacheco. The area is particularly attractive for those looking for more space, recreational facilities and easy access to golf courses while remaining within convenient driving distance of the coast.

Shops, supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, a pharmacy and essential everyday services are located approximately 1 km from the resort. The beaches of the Mar Menor are approximately 15 km away, while Murcia-Corvera International Airport is around 18 km away.

This development is an interesting option both as a holiday property and for year-round living, particularly for those who value peaceful surroundings, generous outdoor spaces and access to extensive leisure facilities.

Frequently asked questions

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP El Alba (about 300 m) and Escuela infantil Colorines Roldán (about 1.9 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud El Jimenado, about 10 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 19 km by road, around 22 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Spar, about 10 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 16 km by road, around 20 minutes by car.
