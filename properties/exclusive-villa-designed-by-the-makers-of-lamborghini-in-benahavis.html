Location

Benahavís is a mountain town in the province of Málaga, located 7 km from the coast. It lies between Marbella, Estepona and Ronda, on the West Coast of the Costa del Sol. It is popular for its wide range of restaurants and as a white town with a typical Arab street grid layout. Within Benahavis is one of the most exclusive estates in Spain - La Zagaleta. According to data from the Tax Authority in 2018. Benahavís was the first municipality in the province of Málaga in terms of gross income and the fourth in Andalusia, thanks precisely to La Zagaleta.

Benahavis is a great place to relax, meet friends in restaurants and cafés or on the golf course. It is also a great base for hiking, cycling and swimming. Art lovers will also find something for themselves here: in the David Marschall gallery, you can buy beautiful sculptures, inspired by nature, combined with readymade objects - turning chips, railway sleepers. The artist creates abstract sculptures, figures that are a combination of ceramics, glass and metal, wood and other materials.
