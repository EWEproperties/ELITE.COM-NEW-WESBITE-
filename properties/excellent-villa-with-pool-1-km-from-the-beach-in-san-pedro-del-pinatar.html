Location

San Pedro del Pinatar is a charming town located in the Murcia region of Spain, set along the shores of the saltwater lagoon of Mar Menor. The town boasts a rich history dating back to Roman times, with remnants of ancient salt production still visible today.

The area offers several beautiful beaches, including Playa de la Llana, Playa de Villananitos, and Playa de la Torre Derribada—perfect for water sports, sunbathing, and relaxation. Playa de la Mota is especially popular among locals, offering calm waters and excellent facilities.

San Pedro del Pinatar is also home to the stunning Regional Park of Salinas y Arenales. This natural reserve provides a unique environment where the saltwater lagoon meets the Mediterranean Sea, creating a diverse ecosystem rich in flora and fauna. Visitors can enjoy guided tours, birdwatching, wildlife observation, and scenic walks along the park’s trails.

The town features several interesting attractions, including the Fish Market, where you can find a wide selection of fresh seafood caught daily, and the Sea and Salt Museum, showcasing the town’s long-standing salt production heritage. Molino de Quintín, a restored historic windmill, offers insight into the area’s agricultural past.

San Pedro del Pinatar also boasts a wide range of restaurants, cafés, and bars where visitors can enjoy local cuisine in a relaxed atmosphere. The town has a vibrant cultural scene, hosting various festivals throughout the year, including the San Pedro Carnival and the International Festival of Habaneras and Polyphony.
