Location

Alicante is a beautiful city located on the south-eastern coast of Spain and is the perfect destination for those seeking unforgettable experiences. With its warm climate, stunning beaches, and rich history, it is no surprise that Alicante is one of the most popular destinations in Spain. The calm waters of the Mediterranean Sea make it an ideal place for water sports and beach life, loved by both residents and visitors alike.

Golf

Alicante is home to some of the most beautiful golf courses in the region, making it a popular destination for golf lovers. Alicante Golf is one of the best-known golf courses, offering 18 challenging holes designed by the legendary Seve Ballesteros. The course boasts spectacular views of the Mediterranean Sea and is a true paradise for golf enthusiasts.

History

Alicante’s rich history is reflected in its remarkable landmarks, such as Santa Bárbara Castle. This historic fortress, perched on top of Mount Benacantil, offers breathtaking views of the city and the sea. The castle’s walls and towers have stood for centuries and bear witness to the many civilizations that settled in Alicante over the years, including the Romans, Moors, and Christians.

Beaches

Of course, no visit to Alicante is complete without enjoying its beautiful beaches. The city’s warm climate and crystal-clear waters make it the perfect place for beach life and water sports. Playa del Postiguet is one of the most popular beaches in the area, located right in the heart of Alicante. It is loved by both tourists and locals and offers stunning views of the city and the Mediterranean Sea.

Promenade

The Explanada de España promenade is another must-visit spot in Alicante. This lively boulevard, located opposite the port, is lined with terraces and charming cafés, making it the perfect place to enjoy a coffee or a delicious meal. The promenade is a favorite meeting point for locals and visitors and an ideal place to admire the beautiful sea and city views.
