Location

Orihuela Costa is a coastal region located on the Costa Blanca in the province of Alicante. It is close to the border with Murcia, close to La Manga and the beautiful Mar Menor. Despite its name, it is actually about 20 kilometers from the main resort of Orihuela.

Orihuela Costa is the perfect place for those looking for a family beach holiday, with 16 kilometers of varied coastline. It's also a great choice for a golf holiday; in fact, Orihuela Costa is well equipped for all needs, with many amenities, facilities and leisure activities.

The region includes several seaside resorts, including Punta Prima, Playa Flamenca, La Zenia, Cabo Roig, Villamartin, Campoamor and Mil Palmeras.

The seaside resort of Cabo Roig is located on the southern tip of the Costa Blanca in Orihuela Costa. This charming town has several Blue Flag beaches and spectacular mountain scenery. The town itself, just 10 kilometers from Torrevieja, has wide, palm-lined avenues and delightful lush gardens, overlooking the Mediterranean Sea, the La Manga Peninsula and the Mar Menor.

The tranquil urbanization of the Playa Flamenca resort is close to the lively town of Torrevieja on the Costa Blanca. The area has great beaches, a golf course and many recreational facilities such as water parks, tennis, go-karting and tennis.

Again, not far from Torrevieja is the popular resort of La Zenia. The city has everything you need to enjoy a perfect holiday: blue flag beaches, a golf course, good amenities, many Spanish and international restaurants, and a beautiful area to explore. La Zenia is one of the most famous resorts on the Costa Blanca.

The local beaches around Punta Prima have been awarded 67 European Blue Flags and range from natural dunes with pine trees in the background to long stretches of golden sandy beaches. Punta Prima is actually an urbanization, not a city; consists of apartments, townhouses and villas, located about 5 kilometers southwest of Torrevieja.

Campoamor is the perfect location for those who want to appreciate an element of true Spanish culture. Stunning scenery, green forests and beautiful beaches make Campoamor a prime tourist destination on the Orihuela Costa.

The prestigious Villamartin golf development is located south of Alicante on the Orihuela Costa. The complex gives the impression of a charming Mediterranean village with apartments, townhouses and villas and all the amenities you could wish for including shops, cafes, bars and restaurants and of course two 18-hole golf courses. beaches nearby that are never too crowded.

Mil Palmeras is the southernmost resort on the Orihuela Costa. It is an attractive and popular seaside resort that boasts beautiful sandy beaches, rocky coves and bays, and plenty of water sports and beach facilities.

Orihuela Costa is a great choice for a golf holiday, with three great golf courses in the region - Villamartin, Campoamor and Las Ramblas.
