Apartment for sale overlooking the Font del Llop Golf Resort in Monforte del Cid.

Whether you are passionate about golf or not, the new apartments at Font del Llop Golf Resort are a wonderful place to live. Well-kept common spaces surrounded by picturesque mountain landscapes, luxurious amenities and an excellent location in a safe and elite urbanization. These stunning apartments are located within the Font del Llop Golf Resort and offer stunning views of the golf course and mountains.

This new residential estate has a truly excellent location. Elche is 10 minutes away by car, Aspe 5 minutes, Alicante center 20 minutes, the beaches also 20 minutes and Alicante airport only 15 minutes. Live in an ideal place where everything is close enough, and at the same time far enough to be in an oasis of peace and quiet, surrounded by nature.

The project consists of modern apartments and duplex houses with 2 or 3 bedrooms divided into 4 blocks. They share 2 communal swimming pools and Mediterranean gardens lush with native species, and each house has an underground parking space and a storage room included in the price. The top floor houses are 3 bedroom maisonettes and the remaining apartments are 2 bedroom apartments. The houses are arranged staggered, so they do not stand on top of each other like in a traditional apartment and have only one neighbor on one side.

The investments are built of high-quality materials and equipped with an aerothermal system that manages hot and cold air conditioning through ducts, underfloor heating and hot water. The apartments are bioclimatic because they use natural resources and avoid high conventional energy consumption. All properties are designed and equipped to meet high energy efficiency requirements. They are also equipped with full bathrooms and kitchens (including household appliances).

Font del Llop Golf Course is a beautiful, green setting with lots of native plants and trees and plenty of places to walk and cycle while enjoying nature. The club has a wonderful Mediterranean restaurant with a terrace with spectacular views, serving delicious, typical local dishes. After the construction of the investment is completed, a number of services will be created in the estate, such as shops, a supermarket, a gym and a sports zone.

Location

Monforte del Cid owes its "name" to the Sierra del Cid mountain range, where legend associates this geographical feature with the figure of a medieval knight. It is a commune in the Valencian Community in Spain. It is located deep in the province of Alicante, in the Medio Vinalopó region.

Monforte del Cid in Alicante is the perfect city for those who enjoy playing golf in a natural environment with a mild climate, as well as for those who are looking for small places full of history, culture and heritage and fantastic museums.

In Monforte del Cid you can start by walking through the old town, which has retained its medieval layout and houses from the 16th to the 20th centuries, with facades decorated with typical Manises tiles with religious scenes.

The most important attractions include the town hall with 16th-century arches and a coat of arms and the Gothic church of Nuestra Señora de las Nieves, as well as the hermitages of San Pascual and San Roque. You will definitely love walking around and seeing these buildings!

Of course, we recommend stopping in Ibero, the Villa de Monforte del Cid History Museum, where you can admire bull sculptures from the Iberian era and even a Roman tombstone. In fact, near Monforte del Cid and Novelda there is Finca el Campet, where the remains of a large Roman villa were found.

After visiting the city center, your next stop is Orito, the pilgrimage district of Monforte del Cid, home to the 16th century church and convent of San Pascual and the Virgin of Orito, as well as the Cueva de Saint Pascual. You can walk there and admire the amazing views of the place. There is a fantastic pilgrimage to this place in May!

In terms of relaxation, in addition to the excellent golf courses, in Monforte del Cid you will have the opportunity to join the wonderful Moorish and Christian festivals taking place in December. Be sure to try the excellent bagged grapes with the Vinalopó designation of origin, anise and liqueurs, also protected by the Regulatory Board, as well as pastas and cakes.
