The last two 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom homes in this phase at Santa Rosalía Lake & Life Resort, a gated resort around a swimming lagoon in Torre-Pacheco, about 4 km from the Mar Menor beaches of Los Alcázares. Choose a ground-floor home with 103 m² built, a 23 m² terrace and an 80 m² private garden (€384,900), or a top-floor penthouse with 113 m² built and a 61 m² terrace (€499,900). Both come with a parking space and storage room in the basement, and the buyer pays no agency fee.

The home

An open-plan living room and kitchen opens onto the terrace. The 3 bedrooms have fitted wardrobes, and the 2 bathrooms have walk-in showers with glass screens. The kitchen is white with a Silestone worktop and appliances, and floors are 80x80 cm porcelain tiles. Other details: aluminium windows with thermal break and double glazing, an armoured front door, a video intercom and hot water from a heat pump. Air conditioning, underfloor heating in the bathrooms and wood-effect floors are available as extras.

The development

The homes are in low blocks of eight apartments around a communal pool and gardens, with a gated entrance. Owners can also use the resort's swimming lagoon with its sandy beach, the beach club and the sports areas.

The area

Santa Rosalía lies between Torre-Pacheco and Los Alcázares, a short drive from the Mar Menor.

  • Mar Menor beaches, Los Alcázares: about 4 km
  • Shops and supermarket: about 4 km
  • Murcia International Airport (Corvera): about 30 minutes by car
  • Alicante Airport: about 1 hour 30 minutes by car

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Punta Brava, about 12 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: I.E.S. ANTONIO MENÁRGUEZ COSTA, about 5 km, around 8 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Nuestra Señora del Carmen, about 20 km, around 19 minutes by car.
International school: Agave International School, about 20 km, around 22 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Los Alcázares, about 7 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 15 km by road, around 15 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is La Tienda de Los Olmos, about 4 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 30 km by road, around 25 minutes by car.
