For sale: a modern villa located in a prestigious area of Ciudad Quesada, in the second line of the La Marquesa Golf Course. The property combines contemporary architecture, spacious and bright interiors, and generous outdoor areas, creating a comfortable home for both year-round living and holidays on the Costa Blanca.

The villa offers 3 spacious bedrooms and 4 bathrooms. The layout has been carefully designed to provide maximum comfort and privacy. The open-plan living area, with a lounge, dining space and modern kitchen, creates a bright and welcoming interior, perfect for everyday living as well as spending time with family and friends.

Large windows allow plenty of natural light to enter the interiors and provide direct access to the terraces and garden. This creates a seamless connection between the indoor and outdoor spaces, allowing you to make the most of the Mediterranean climate throughout most of the year.

The different levels of the property are connected by a private lift, providing additional comfort and convenience. The villa also features a dedicated private wellness area with a sauna and gym – an ideal space for relaxation and exercise without leaving home.

The property is situated on a private plot of approximately 393 m². The outdoor area is centred around a private swimming pool surrounded by a spacious terrace, where you can create a comfortable relaxation area with sun loungers and space for outdoor dining. Contemporary architecture and carefully designed outdoor areas give the property an elegant and cohesive character.

Location

The villa is located in Ciudad Quesada, just a few steps from the popular La Marquesa Golf Course. This peaceful and well-established area of the Costa Blanca is particularly appreciated by those looking for privacy, recreational facilities and easy access to everyday amenities.

Restaurants, bars, shops, supermarkets and other services can be found nearby. Golf enthusiasts can enjoy the La Marquesa Golf Course practically on their doorstep.

The sandy beaches of Guardamar del Segura are approximately 10–15 minutes away by car. The convenient location also provides easy access to Torrevieja, other popular coastal towns on the Costa Blanca and Alicante-Elche Airport.
