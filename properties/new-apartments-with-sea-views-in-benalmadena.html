Discover an exclusive collection of new apartments for sale in Benalmádena, ideally positioned close to the Mediterranean Sea and offering spectacular sea views. This boutique residential complex on the Costa del Sol consists of only 28 contemporary homes, combining modern architecture, spacious terraces and excellent communal facilities with a privileged location just a few minutes’ walk from the beach. Thanks to the elevated position of the complex and the south-facing orientation of the homes, residents can enjoy abundant natural light and impressive unobstructed views towards the Mediterranean.

The properties feature 3 bedrooms, comfortable living spaces and large terraces designed as a natural extension of the interior. Open-plan living and dining areas create bright and welcoming spaces, while large windows connect the interiors with the terraces and surrounding Mediterranean landscape. Each home has been carefully designed to maximise space, functionality and natural light.

Selected properties also offer private gardens, while the penthouse-style homes are arranged over two levels and include additional outdoor spaces and private solariums. The generous terraces provide an ideal setting for outdoor dining, relaxing and enjoying the sea views throughout the year.

One of the highlights of this new development in Benalmádena is its impressive selection of communal facilities. Residents will have access to an outdoor swimming pool surrounded by landscaped gardens and relaxation areas, as well as a heated indoor swimming pool that can be enjoyed throughout the year.

The complex also includes a fully equipped gym, extensive green areas and a modern social and coworking space, providing comfortable areas for both leisure and remote working. Private parking spaces and storage rooms provide additional convenience for residents.

EXCELLENT LOCATION CLOSE TO THE BEACH IN BENALMÁDENA

The development enjoys a privileged location in Benalmádena, surrounded by greenery and positioned close to Torremuelle Beach. A landscaped pathway provides direct pedestrian access towards the coast, allowing residents to reach the beach in approximately three minutes on foot.

The area offers an excellent combination of tranquillity and convenient access to everyday services, restaurants, international schools, sports facilities and golf courses. Torrequebrada Golf Course and Puerto Marina are only a short drive away, while the nearby Torremuelle train station provides convenient connections along the Costa del Sol.

The location also provides excellent connections to some of the most popular destinations on the Costa del Sol. Puerto Marina is approximately 7 minutes away, Málaga around 20 minutes and Marbella approximately 25 minutes, while Málaga-Costa del Sol International Airport can be reached in approximately 28 minutes.

These new apartments for sale in Benalmádena are an excellent choice for buyers looking for a permanent residence, a second home on the Costa del Sol or a high-quality property close to the Mediterranean Sea, combining sea views, contemporary architecture, extensive communal facilities and easy access to the beach.
