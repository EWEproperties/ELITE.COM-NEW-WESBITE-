Location

Villajoyosa is a hidden pearl on the Spanish Costa Blanca.

This colourful fishing village is located between the popular town of Benidorm and El Campello, about a 30-minute drive from Alicante airport.

Villajoyosa seems to literally hang on rocky, coastal cliffs. Combined with the charming architecture, this gives an amazing impression of a picturesque coastline. What's more, the very name of the town promises a nice time. Villajoyosa or La Vila Joiosa literally translates to joyful town. Indeed, it also gives this impression, with its colourful houses in the city centre.

Urban legend has it that the buildings were painted in vivid colours to help fishermen find their way home. Today, they certainly attract crowds of tourists and are an inspiration for many photographers and artists.

In recent years, this charming town has become a desirable destination for those looking for a second home in a quiet area.

Villajoyosa has a lot to offer, from the wide sandy beach to the narrow and colourful streets of the historic centre.

Villajoyosa has been associated with chocolate since the nineteenth century. This is where the popular Spanish chocolate factory Valor is located. The Valor, Pérez and Clavileño museums are must-see on your sightseeing list. There you will learn all about the chocolate production process and its history in the area. Tours led by knowledgeable guides are most often combined with chocolate tasting.

During the holiday season, various cultural events take place in Villajoyosa. It is worth visiting the village in July, when the famous Moorish and Christian festival takes place here. The main attraction is the recreation of the battle of 1538, as well as concerts, performances and shows.

Villajoyosa contains 3 km of beaches with golden sand and views of the vastness of the transparent sea. The most beautiful of them are playa El Paraíso, Xarco and lah Caleta. You will find here both larger tourist resorts and hidden, mysterious corners perfect for peaceful relaxation. The seaside panorama, Mediterranean climate and soft, hot sand are ideal for an atmosphere of complete relaxation. Near the beaches, you will find regional restaurants and cafes offering fresh Mediterranean delicacies.

Villajoyosa delights tourists with its idyllic, cheerful atmosphere and colourful houses. There is no shortage of attractions for those seeking excitement and those interested in history and culture. Just a walk along the seashore provides stunning views and impressions that are hard to forget.
