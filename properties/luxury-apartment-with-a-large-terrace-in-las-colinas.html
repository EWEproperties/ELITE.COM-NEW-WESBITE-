Modern apartments for sale, located in the exceptional green surroundings of Las Colinas Golf & Country Club on the Costa Blanca. The development has been designed to blend harmoniously with the natural landscape, offering residents privacy, tranquillity and a high level of comfort in one of the most highly regarded locations in the region.

The apartments offer 3 bedrooms, a spacious living area and a large terrace that forms a natural extension of the living room. Available properties offer approximately 141–145 m² of built area and terraces of around 28 m². Depending on the selected apartment, the layout includes 2 bathrooms, while some options also feature an additional guest toilet.

The interiors have been designed in a bright, contemporary style. The heart of each apartment is a spacious living room combined with a dining area and an open-plan kitchen. Large glazed doors provide plenty of natural light and lead directly onto the terrace, creating a seamless connection between the indoor and outdoor spaces.

The modern architecture of the building is characterised by light-coloured façades, large windows and distinctive curved terrace lines. Mediterranean vegetation surrounding the development further enhances the peaceful and elegant atmosphere of the complex.

Residents will have access to a communal swimming pool surrounded by landscaped gardens and relaxation areas, providing the perfect setting to enjoy the Costa Blanca climate for most of the year. The development is surrounded by nature and also offers 24-hour security.

The Golf & Country Club is an extensive, carefully planned resort surrounded by Mediterranean scenery. The location is ideal for year-round living, as a second home in Spain or as a property for holiday stays.

This is an excellent option for those looking for a spacious 3-bedroom apartment with a large terrace, set in peaceful and green surroundings on the Costa Blanca.

The planned completion date for the complex is December 2027.

Location

The property is located within Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, in the municipality of Orihuela, in the province of Alicante on the Costa Blanca. The resort is situated along the road connecting Campoamor and San Miguel de Salinas, in a peaceful and green setting away from the densely developed coastal areas.

Las Colinas is a carefully planned residential resort surrounded by Mediterranean nature, offering residents access to a wide range of facilities and services as well as 24-hour security.

The location is ideal both for those looking for a peaceful place for year-round living and for those searching for a second home on the Costa Blanca.
