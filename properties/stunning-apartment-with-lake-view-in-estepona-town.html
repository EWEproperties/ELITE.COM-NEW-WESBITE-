Location

Estepona combines the rich cultural heritage and traditional atmosphere of an old fishing village with a cosmopolitan atmosphere. There is no shortage of golf courses nearby, with 10 in the area. In addition, Estepona has more than 20 km of beaches with many bars, restaurants and water sports.

The town is surrounded by the Sierra Bermeja mountains, which dominate the entire landscape around Estepona.

This is one of the few places in the world where the mountain range is only 10 km from the sea.

You can see how beautiful the views are from the summit by setting out on designated hiking trails.

One of the most interesting attractions in this picturesque town is the orchidarium. The orchid collection here

is the largest in Spain and one of the largest in Europe! Even if you are not a fan of botanical gardens, this place is worth a visit, if only for the feast of colours and scents. A real feast for the senses!

In Estepona, there is the Playa de la Rada beach, which measures over 2,600 metres in length. There are plenty of facilities in the area, such as showers, umbrellas, toilets, pedal boats, playgrounds or trampolines for children. For lesser and greater hunger, the seaside restaurants and bars (Spanish: chiringuitos) serving local delicacies and drinks are recommended.
