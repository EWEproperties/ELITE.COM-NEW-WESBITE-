Discover a collection of spacious new apartments with large terraces and beautiful sea views, located in an exceptional area of Estepona on the Costa del Sol. The development stands out for its generous living spaces, extensive outdoor areas and views towards the Mediterranean Sea. Its peaceful and green setting in the Selwo area offers privacy and tranquillity while providing convenient access to the beaches, Estepona town centre and some of the most popular destinations on the western Costa del Sol.

The complex offers spacious 2-, 3- and 4-bedroom apartments and penthouses, designed with a strong focus on space, natural light and comfortable Mediterranean living. One of the most distinctive features of the properties is their generously sized terraces, which become a natural extension of the living areas and provide plenty of space for outdoor dining, relaxing and enjoying the Mediterranean climate throughout most of the year.

Large floor-to-ceiling windows fill the interiors with natural light and open the living spaces onto terraces overlooking the Mediterranean Sea and surrounding landscape. Selected ground-floor apartments also feature private gardens, while the penthouses offer extensive terraces and private solariums, providing even more outdoor space and panoramic surroundings.

The interiors combine contemporary design with generous proportions and functional layouts. Large open-plan living areas with integrated kitchens and dining spaces connect seamlessly with the terraces, creating a bright and spacious environment. Depending on the selected property, the apartments include two or three bathrooms. Each home also comes with a parking space in the underground garage and a private storage room.

Another major advantage of the development is its extensive selection of communal facilities, designed to create the atmosphere of a private resort. Residents will have access to an infinity pool overlooking the sea, a summer swimming pool, children's pool, heated jacuzzi, spa, fully equipped gym and padel court. The complex also includes a coworking area, multifunctional room, relaxation areas and extensive landscaped gardens with walking paths.

Expected completion date: 30 October 2026.

EXCELLENT LOCATION IN ESTEPONA

The development is situated in a peaceful part of Estepona, close to Selwo and surrounded by greenery and golf courses. Estepona town centre and the Mediterranean coastline are only a short drive away. The location also provides convenient access to Cancelada, Benahavís and Puerto Banús, while Marbella is approximately 18 km away.

The combination of spacious interiors, exceptionally large terraces, sea views and resort-style facilities makes these apartments an attractive choice for both year-round living in Estepona and as a comfortable second home on the Costa del Sol.
