Elite World Estates proudly presents an exclusive boutique residential development located in one of the most desirable areas of Mijas Costa, just minutes from the charming village of La Cala de Mijas on the Costa del Sol. Surrounded by prestigious golf courses and overlooking the Mediterranean Sea, this exceptional development combines contemporary architecture, elegant interiors and premium lifestyle amenities, creating the perfect setting for modern coastal living.

The development comprises 54 exclusive residences offering 1, 2 and 3-bedroom apartments, thoughtfully designed to maximise natural light, spaciousness and spectacular sea views. Every home features an open-plan layout that seamlessly connects the living, dining and kitchen areas, creating bright, contemporary interiors perfectly suited to everyday living and entertaining.

Large floor-to-ceiling windows and generous private terraces blur the boundaries between indoor and outdoor spaces, allowing residents to fully embrace the Mediterranean lifestyle throughout the year. Ground-floor residences benefit from private gardens, while selected apartments include spacious solariums with breathtaking panoramic sea views, providing the perfect space for relaxing, dining outdoors or enjoying spectacular sunrises and sunsets over the Mediterranean.

Designed with comfort and quality in mind, the apartments feature premium finishes, elegant open-plan kitchens and carefully selected materials that create sophisticated yet welcoming living spaces. Spacious bedrooms, including luxurious master suites with en-suite bathrooms in selected layouts, provide a peaceful retreat where comfort and privacy take centre stage.

Residents can enjoy an outstanding collection of resort-style amenities designed to enhance everyday wellbeing. Beautifully landscaped gardens, elegant outdoor swimming pools, a heated indoor pool, a fully equipped fitness centre and stylish chill-out areas create an exclusive environment where relaxation and an active lifestyle go hand in hand.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its outstanding location in Mijas Costa. Situated just minutes from La Cala de Mijas, residents enjoy quick access to beautiful beaches, renowned golf courses including Calanova Golf, La Cala Golf Resort, Chaparral Golf Club and Miraflores Golf, as well as excellent restaurants, beach clubs, cafés and everyday amenities. Excellent road connections also provide easy access to Fuengirola in approximately 10 minutes, Marbella in 20 minutes, and Málaga International Airport in around 25 minutes, making the development an excellent choice for both permanent living and investment.

Combining breathtaking sea views, contemporary architecture, premium amenities and one of the Costa del Sol's most desirable locations, this exclusive residential development offers a unique opportunity to own a stylish apartment where comfort, elegance and the Mediterranean lifestyle come together in perfect harmony.

Location

The development is situated in the prestigious area of Mijas Costa, in the peaceful neighbourhood of Torrenueva, between Fuengirola and Marbella. It is one of the most sought-after locations on the Costa del Sol, offering the perfect combination of a Mediterranean lifestyle, beautiful beaches, and all the amenities needed for comfortable year-round living.

The wide sandy beaches of the Mediterranean Sea and the picturesque seaside promenade, lined with restaurants, cafés, and beach clubs, are just 500 metres away. The surrounding area also offers supermarkets, shops, schools, medical centres, and a wide range of sports facilities, ensuring that all everyday services are within easy reach.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to renowned golf courses such as La Cala Golf Resort, Calanova Golf Club, and Miraflores Golf, while those who enjoy an active lifestyle can take advantage of numerous walking trails, cycling routes, and a variety of water sports.

Excellent transport connections provide quick access to Marbella, Fuengirola, the exclusive marina of Puerto Banús, and Málaga International Airport, which is approximately a 25-minute drive away. This outstanding location is ideal both for those looking for a second home in Spain and for investors seeking an attractive rental property.
