Elite World Estates proudly presents an exclusive new residential development located in one of the most desirable areas of Estepona on the Costa del Sol. This exceptional collection of contemporary apartments and townhouses has been designed for buyers seeking a modern property in Spain that combines elegant architecture, premium finishes, generous outdoor living, and an outstanding location close to the Mediterranean Sea.

The development consists of just 87 exclusive residences, including stylish 2 and 3-bedroom apartments and elegant 4-bedroom townhouses. Every home has been thoughtfully designed to maximize natural light, comfort, and functionality while creating a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor spaces.

The apartments offer spacious interiors ranging from 77 m² to 155 m², complemented by large private terraces of up to 55 m², perfect for enjoying the Mediterranean climate throughout the year. The townhouses feature more than 160 m² of living space, generous private outdoor areas, and an impressive rooftop solarium—ideal for relaxing, entertaining, or taking in the panoramic surroundings.

Thanks to their predominantly south, southeast, and southwest orientation, most residences enjoy abundant sunshine throughout the day, open views, and spectacular sunsets that create the perfect setting for everyday living.

Designed with a focus on quality and wellbeing, every home features contemporary open-plan layouts, expansive windows, premium materials, and carefully selected finishes. Spacious bedrooms with built-in wardrobes provide a peaceful retreat, while large windows flood the interiors with natural light, creating bright and inviting living spaces.

Residents will enjoy beautifully landscaped communal gardens, a stunning outdoor swimming pool, and tranquil relaxation areas that encourage a healthy Mediterranean lifestyle. Whether spending time by the pool, enjoying the sunshine on your terrace, or simply appreciating the peaceful surroundings, every detail has been created to offer comfort and serenity.

Each property also includes a private underground parking space and a storage room, with direct lift access from the two-level underground garage, providing both convenience and security.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its exceptional location. Situated in the heart of Estepona, it offers the perfect balance between coastal living and everyday convenience. The vibrant town centre, beautiful sandy beaches, renowned golf courses, restaurants, boutiques, marinas, and all essential amenities are just minutes away. Marbella can be reached in approximately 20 minutes, while Málaga International Airport is less than an hour away, making the development an excellent choice for both permanent living and investment.

This is a unique opportunity to own a contemporary home on the Costa del Sol, where sophisticated design, exceptional quality, and the relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle come together in one outstanding address.

Location

The development is situated in one of Estepona's most sought-after areas, just a few minutes from the town centre, the marina, and the Costa del Sol coastline. It is an ideal location for those who wish to enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle every day without compromising on the convenience of urban amenities. The peaceful residential setting offers privacy and tranquillity while providing quick access to beaches, restaurants, shops, schools, and all the essential services required for comfortable year-round living.

Just a short walk away is the wide sandy La Rada Beach and the vibrant seafront promenade lined with restaurants, cafés, and beach clubs. Nearby residents will also find Estepona Marina, the charming Old Town with its colourful flower-filled streets, supermarkets, shopping centres, international schools, medical facilities, and a wide range of sports and leisure amenities. Thanks to the excellent infrastructure, all daily conveniences are within easy reach.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to renowned golf courses such as Valle Romano Golf, Estepona Golf, Azata Golf, and Finca Cortesín, recognised as one of Europe's finest golf resorts. Excellent connections to the A-7 and AP-7 motorways provide quick access to Marbella, Puerto Banús, Sotogrande, and the international airports of Málaga and Gibraltar, making this location an excellent choice both for permanent living and as a property investment with strong rental potential.
