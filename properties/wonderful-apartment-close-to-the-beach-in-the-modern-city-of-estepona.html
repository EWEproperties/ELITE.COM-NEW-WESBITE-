Location

The quaint town of Estepona is a hidden gem located along the stunning Costa del Sol. With its idyllic Mediterranean landscape, Estepona offers a wonderful combination of picturesque beaches, rich cultural heritage and a lively atmosphere. Let's see what this city has to offer.

Estepona is conveniently located a short distance from Malaga Airport, making it easily accessible for travellers. The airport is approximately 80 kilometers away, ensuring a smooth and comfortable journey for visitors.

Estepona boasts a collection of breathtaking beaches that will delight beach lovers and sun seekers alike. Some of the best beaches in the area include Playa del Cristo, a sheltered cove with calm waters and a relaxing atmosphere, and Playa de la Rada, a vast stretch of golden sand offering a wide range of water sports. Playa del Saladillo is another fantastic option, known for its natural beauty and tranquility.

In addition to its beautiful beaches, Estepona is also home to many attractions and activities. A must-visit is the historic Old Town, known as Casco Antiguo. Wander the quaint cobbled streets decorated with colorful flower pots and learn about the city's rich cultural heritage. Admire the traditional Andalusian architecture, visit the Church of San Luis, and soak up the bustling atmosphere of bustling squares and bustling markets.

For art enthusiasts, the Estepona Museum of Contemporary Art is a hidden gem, home to an impressive collection of modern and contemporary works. The Orchidarium of Estepona is another unique attraction, offering a mesmerizing display of colorful orchids and tropical flora.

Estepona also offers a variety of outdoor activities for nature lovers. The nearby Sierra Bermeja mountain range provides excellent hiking opportunities, allowing you to immerse yourself in stunning natural landscapes and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the coast. Golf enthusiasts will love the numerous world-class golf courses in the area, where they can play against the backdrop of lush greenery and azure skies.

When it comes to dining and entertainment, Estepona boasts a vibrant food scene. Explore the charming streets lined with traditional tapas bars and seafood restaurants where you can enjoy the fresh and flavorful cuisine of the region. The marina area, Puerto Deportivo, is a popular dining spot and offers a wide range of restaurants and bars overlooking the yachts and the sparkling Mediterranean Sea.

There is always something to see and do in Estepona. Whether you're strolling along the promenade, practicing water sports, exploring historic streets or simply relaxing on pristine beaches, Estepona promises an unforgettable experience for guests of all ages.

Welcome to Estepona, where an enchanting combination of natural beauty, cultural heritage and seaside charm awaits to captivate your senses and create treasured memories!
