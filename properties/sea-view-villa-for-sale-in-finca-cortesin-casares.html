New 3-bedroom villa for sale in Casares with sea views, private outdoor space and a prime location near Finca Cortesín. Discover the property.

New villa for sale in Casares, located in Casares del Sol on the Costa del Sol, just 1.2 km from the beach and next to the prestigious Finca Cortesín golf area. The property offers 3 bedrooms, all with en-suite bathrooms, approximately 185–197 m² of built area and a private plot ranging from around 196 to 381 m², depending on the selected villa.

The villas form part of an exclusive residential development of just 55 attached homes. Contemporary architecture, floor-to-ceiling windows and carefully designed interiors create bright living spaces that connect naturally with the terraces and surrounding Mediterranean landscape.

Each home has been designed with an emphasis on natural light and comfortable everyday living. The main living area combines the lounge, dining space and an open-plan kitchen with a central island, creating a modern social area for family life and entertaining.

Large floor-to-ceiling windows open the interior towards the terraces and surrounding views. The homes enjoy views towards the Mediterranean Sea, golf course and mountains, while the master bedroom provides direct access to a private terrace.

All three bedrooms feature en-suite bathrooms. The bathrooms are finished in a contemporary style with porcelain surfaces, walk-in shower areas, glass screens, integrated washbasins and backlit mirrors. A separate guest toilet and laundry room provide additional practicality.

The property also includes private parking or garage space, while the architectural plans and project visuals show landscaped exterior areas designed for enjoying the Costa del Sol climate.

One of the strongest advantages of this sea view villa in Casares is its location. The residential area is positioned alongside hole 14 of Finca Cortesín Golf Course with a south-southeast orientation and views across the golf course, sea and surrounding mountains.

The beach is approximately 1.2 km away, while shops and everyday services are located less than 1 km from the development. Estepona can be reached in around 5 minutes, Sotogrande in 15 minutes and Puerto Banús in approximately 20 minutes. Gibraltar Airport is around 45 minutes away and Málaga Airport approximately 50 minutes by car.

This villa for sale in Casares near Finca Cortesín is an attractive option for buyers looking for a modern Costa del Sol property combining sea views, contemporary architecture, golf surroundings and convenient access to beaches, Estepona and Sotogrande.
