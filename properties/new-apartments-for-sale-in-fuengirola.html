Discover an exclusive collection of new apartments for sale in Fuengirola, ideally located between Fuengirola and Mijas on the Costa del Sol. This contemporary residential complex combines distinctive architecture, spacious terraces and high-quality interiors with extensive resort-style facilities, all within easy reach of the beach and everyday services.

The development comprises 233 residences, offering a wide selection of layouts from 1-bedroom apartments to spacious 2 and 3-bedroom homes and impressive penthouses. Selected properties also include flexible multifunctional spaces. The homes have been designed to maximise natural light and create a seamless connection between the interiors and generous outdoor areas.

Spacious terraces are a key feature of the development, providing plenty of room for outdoor dining and relaxation. Depending on the property, outdoor areas may include private gardens, chill-out zones, outdoor kitchens or private pools, creating the perfect setting to enjoy the Mediterranean climate throughout the year.

Residents can enjoy an impressive range of communal facilities, including three swimming pools, a spa, fully equipped gym, coworking and meeting areas, as well as a versatile social and events space. These amenities create a comfortable resort-style environment suitable for permanent living, holidays and remote working.

The development also incorporates energy-efficient solutions and holds an A energy rating, with features including high-performance glazing, efficient systems, photovoltaic solar panels and a heat pump for domestic hot water.

Excellent location between Fuengirola and Mijas

The development enjoys a strategic location between Fuengirola and Mijas, providing easy access to beaches, restaurants, shops, golf courses and the main attractions of the Costa del Sol.

The beach and commuter train station are approximately 5 minutes away, while Málaga-Costa del Sol Airport can be reached in around 20 minutes. Marbella and Málaga are both approximately 25 minutes away, providing excellent connections along the coast.

These new apartments for sale in Fuengirola are an attractive choice for a permanent residence or second home on the Costa del Sol, combining contemporary design, generous outdoor spaces, extensive communal facilities and an excellent location close to the Mediterranean.
