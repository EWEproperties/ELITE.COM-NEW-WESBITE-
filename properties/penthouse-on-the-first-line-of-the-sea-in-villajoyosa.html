Location

Villajoyosa is a hidden pearl on the Spanish Costa Blanca.

This colorful fishing village is located between the popular city of Benidorm and El Campello, approximately 30 minutes' drive from Alicante Airport.

Villajoyosa seems to literally hang on the rocky coastal cliffs. Combined with the charming architecture, it gives an amazing impression of the picturesque coast. What's more, the very name of the town promises a nice time. Villajoyosa or La Vila Joiosa literally means a joyful town. Indeed, it also seems so, with its colorful houses in the city center.

An urban legend has it that the buildings were painted in bright colors to help fishermen find their way home. Today, they certainly attract crowds of tourists and are an inspiration for many photographers and artists.

In recent years, this charming town has become a desirable place for people looking for a second home in a quiet area.

Villajoyosa has a lot to offer, from the wide sandy beach to the narrow and colorful streets of the historic center.

Villajoyosa has been associated with chocolate since the 19th century. It is here that the popular Spanish chocolate factory Valor is located. The Valor, Pérez and Clavileño museums are must-sees. There you will learn everything about the chocolate production process and its history in the area. Tours led by knowledgeable guides are usually combined with chocolate tasting.

Various cultural events take place in Villajoyosa during the holiday season. It is worth visiting the village in July, when the famous festival of Moors and Christians takes place here. The main attraction is the reenactment of the 1538 battle, as well as concerts, performances and shows.

Villajoyosa contains 3 km of beaches with golden sand and a view of the vastness of the transparent sea. The most beautiful of them are playa El Paraíso, Xarco and la Caleta. You will find both larger tourist resorts and hidden, secret corners perfect for peaceful relaxation. The seaside panorama, Mediterranean climate and soft, hot sand are ideal for an atmosphere of complete relaxation. Near the beaches you will find regional restaurants and cafes offering fresh Mediterranean delicacies.

Villajoyosa delights tourists with its idyllic, cheerful atmosphere and colorful houses. There is no shortage of attractions for people thirsty for excitement and interesting history and culture. Just a walk along the seashore provides stunning views and impressions that are hard to forget.
