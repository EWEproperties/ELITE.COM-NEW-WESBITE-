Location

Guardamar del Segura is a charming seaside town located on the Costa Blanca. With its rich infrastructure, from the panoramic promenade to the diverse shopping districts, it is the perfect place for those seeking both relaxation on the beach and access to a variety of services. El Raso, located near Guardamar del Segura, is a residential neighborhood known for its peaceful surroundings. El Raso also stands out for its variety of local amenities, offering residents access to restaurants, shops, and other services, creating the perfect place to live comfortably and relax in the Spanish sun.

Los Viveros is a beautiful and secluded beach located in Guardamar del Segura in the province of Alicante in the Valencian Community. It is approximately 1,300 meters long and 45 meters wide. This beach takes its name from the proximity of nurseries in the area, which characterize the region due to the various crops that grow there. In addition, the Rábita Califal, an Andalusian archaeological treasure, is located nearby. This beach also has a blue flag, which is an international recognition awarded to beaches that meet the highest standards of environmental quality and safety.

The natural environment of Playa Los Viveros is unique because it is located in a protected area of great importance to the local community. In addition, the area is characterized by lush vegetation, which is a hallmark of this region. The beach is located on a large sandbar, with shallow water and no large waves. It can be reached by paths through a pine forest, which has several wooden walkways that allow you to descend onto the sand, overcoming the unevenness of the natural dunes.

In Guardamar del Segura, we not only have beaches, but also a wide range of tourist attractions, such as gastronomy, culture, and history with Phoenician, Arab, and Roman archaeological sites. Water sports enthusiasts can also enjoy all kinds of water sports in Puerto Marina de las Dunas

(sailing, windsurfing, water skiing, kayaking, motorboating, etc.).

All this together makes it one of the best places to live on the Costa Blanca South, where you can live surrounded by nature without giving up extensive infrastructure.
