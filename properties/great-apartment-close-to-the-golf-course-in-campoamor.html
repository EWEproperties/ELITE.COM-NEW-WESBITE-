Location

Orihuela Costa is a coastal region located on the Costa Blanca in the province of Alicante. It is close to the border with Murcia, near La Manga and the beautiful Mar Menor. Despite its name, it is actually about 20 kilometres from the main resort of Orihuela.

It is the ideal place for those looking for a family beach holiday, with 16 kilometres of varied coastline. It is also an excellent choice for a golfing holiday; in fact, Orihuela Costa is well equipped for all needs, with many amenities, facilities and leisure activities.

The region includes several coastal resorts, including Punta Prima, Playa Flamenca, La Zenia, Cabo Roig, Villamartin, Campoamor and Mil Palmeras.

The coastal resort of Cabo Roig is located at the southern end of the Costa Blanca in Orihuela Costa. This charming town has several Blue Flag beaches and spectacular mountain scenery. The town itself, just 10 kilometres from Torrevieja, has wide palm-lined avenues and delightful, lush gardens, overlooking the Mediterranean Sea, the La Manga peninsula and the Mar Menor.

The quiet urbanisation of Playa Flamenca is located near the lively town of Torrevieja on the Costa Blanca. The area has wonderful beaches, a golf course and many leisure facilities such as water parks, tennis, go-karting and tennis.

Again, not far from Torrevieja is the popular holiday resort of La Zenia. The town has everything you need to enjoy the perfect holiday: blue flag beaches, a golf course, good amenities, many Spanish and international restaurants and a beautiful surrounding area to explore. La Zenia is one of the most famous holiday resorts on the Costa Blanca.

The local beaches around Punta Prima have been awarded 67 European Blue Flags and stretch from natural sand dunes with a pine forest backdrop to long stretches of golden, sandy beaches. Punta Prima is actually an urbanisation rather than a town; it consists of apartments, townhouses and villas, located about 5 kilometres southwest of Torrevieja.

Campoamor is the perfect location for people who want to appreciate the true Spanish culture. Stunning scenery, green forests and beautiful beaches make Campoamor a major tourist destination on the Orihuela Costa.

The prestigious Villamartin golf development is located south of Alicante on the Orihuela Costa. The complex has the feel of a charming Mediterranean village with apartments, townhouses and villas and all the amenities you could wish for including shops, cafes, bars and restaurants and of course two 18-hole golf courses. Nearby beaches which are never too crowded.

Mil Palmeras is the southernmost resort on the Orihuela Costa. It is an attractive and popular seaside resort that boasts beautiful sandy beaches, rocky coves and bays, and a wealth of water sports and beach facilities.

Orihuela Costa is an excellent choice for a golfing holiday, with three great golf courses in the region - Villamartin, Campoamor and Las Ramblas.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Aguamarina, about 6 km by road, around 9 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio de Educación Infantil y Primaria Número 20, about 3 km, around 7 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio San José de Calasanz, about 20 km, around 25 minutes by car.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 7 km, around 11 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud San Miguel de Salinas, about 6 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 9 km by road, around 17 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, about 1.2 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 50 km by road, around 35 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 72 km, around 55 minutes by car.
