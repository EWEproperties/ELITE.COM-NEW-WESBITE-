Location

San Pedro del Pinatar is a small town in the province of Murcia, Spain, nestled on the shores of the saltwater lagoon, the Mar Menor. The town has a rich history dating back to the Roman Empire, and traces of Roman saltworks are still visible today.

The town boasts several beautiful beaches, including Playa de la Llana, Playa de Villananitos, and Playa de la Torre Derribada. These beaches are perfect for water sports, sunbathing, and relaxation. Playa de la Mota is a beach popular with locals, offering calm waters and excellent amenities.

San Pedro del Pinatar is also home to a fascinating natural park, the San Pedro del Pinatar Regional Park of Salinas y Arenales. This natural park offers a unique environment where a saltwater lagoon meets the Mediterranean Sea, creating a diverse ecosystem that supports a wide variety of flora and fauna. Visitors can take guided tours, observe birds and wildlife, and walk the park's trails.

The town has several attractions worth visiting, including the Fish Market, which offers a wide selection of fresh seafood caught the same day, and the Sea and Salt Museum, which showcases the town's rich history of salt production. The Quintin Mill is a historic windmill that has been restored and offers a glimpse into the town's agricultural past.

San Pedro del Pinatar also boasts numerous restaurants, cafés, and bars where visitors can sample local cuisine and enjoy the relaxing atmosphere. The city has a vibrant cultural scene, with several festivals throughout the year, including the San Pedro del Pinatar Carnival and the International Festival of Habaneras and Polyphony.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa Las Charcas, about 100 m by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio Público Nuestra Señora del Loreto (about 2.7 km) and IES Manuel Tarraga Escribano (about 4 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud San Pedro del Pinatar, about 2.7 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 10 km by road, around 12 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Charter, about 700 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 37 km by road, around 30 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 87 km, around 65 minutes by car.
