Elite World Estates presents a modern residential development of elegant detached villas located in one of the most attractive areas of the southern Costa Blanca – Pilar de la Horadada. This is an excellent opportunity for buyers looking for a comfortable home in Spain that combines contemporary architecture, high-quality construction, and a convenient location close to beautiful beaches and all essential amenities.

The development features spacious, thoughtfully designed villas with bright open-plan interiors, large floor-to-ceiling windows, and private gardens, allowing residents to fully embrace the Mediterranean lifestyle. Every home has been designed with comfort and functionality in mind, offering privacy, modern design, and high-quality finishes.

One of the project's greatest advantages is its excellent location. The sandy Playa de las Higuericas is only approximately 3–3.5 km away, just 5–7 minutes by car or 10–12 minutes by bicycle. A dedicated pedestrian and cycling path running alongside the town's Rambla provides a pleasant and safe route to the Mediterranean coast, while walking to the beach takes approximately 35–40 minutes.

Located in the heart of Pilar de la Horadada, the development offers easy access to supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, schools, sports facilities, and all the services required for comfortable year-round living. It is an ideal choice both as a permanent residence and as a holiday home or rental investment.

The contemporary architecture, functional layouts, and proximity to the Mediterranean Sea make this development an excellent option for those seeking a high standard of living on the Spanish coast.

Estimated construction time: approximately 8 months.

Location

The development is situated in Pilar de la Horadada, one of the most desirable towns on the southern Costa Blanca, offering excellent conditions for both permanent living and holiday stays. Residents benefit from immediate access to supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, schools, sports centres, and a wide range of leisure facilities.

The beautiful sandy Playa de las Higuericas is located approximately 3–3.5 km away, which means just 5–7 minutes by car or around 10–12 minutes by bicycle. A dedicated pedestrian and cycling path running along the town's Rambla offers a comfortable and scenic route to the beach, while walking takes approximately 35–40 minutes.

Another important advantage is the proximity to renowned golf courses, shopping centres, and the international airports of Alicante and Murcia. This location perfectly combines the peaceful atmosphere of a Mediterranean town with quick access to the coast and all everyday conveniences.
