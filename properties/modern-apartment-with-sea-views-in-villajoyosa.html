Location

This colourful town is located between the popular city of Benidorm and El Campello, about a 30-minute drive from Alicante Airport.

Villajoyosa seems to literally hang on the rocky coastal cliffs. Combined with its charming architecture, this gives an incredible impression of a picturesque coastline. What's more, the very name of the town promises a pleasant time. Villajoyosa or La Vila Joiosa literally means ‘joyful town’. Indeed, it gives that impression with its colourful houses in the town centre.

Urban legend has it that the buildings were painted in bright colours to help fishermen find their way home. Today, they certainly attract crowds of tourists and are an inspiration for many photographers and artists.

In recent years, this charming town has become a desirable location for people looking for a second home in a quiet area. Villajoyosa has a lot to offer, from a wide sandy beach to the narrow and colourful streets of the historic centre.

Villajoyosa has been associated with chocolate since the 19th century. It is home to the popular Spanish chocolate factory Valor. The Valor, Pérez and Clavileño museums are must-see attractions. There you will learn everything about the chocolate production process and its history in the area. Tours led by knowledgeable guides are usually combined with chocolate tasting.

During the holiday season, Villajoyosa hosts a variety of cultural events. It is worth visiting the village in July, when the famous Moors and Christians festival takes place here. The main attraction is a re-enactment of the battle of 1538, as well as concerts, performances and shows.

Villajoyosa has 3 km of golden sandy beaches with views of the endless clear sea. The most beautiful of these are Playa El Paraíso, Xarco and La Caleta. Here you will find both larger tourist resorts and hidden, mysterious corners perfect for peaceful relaxation. The coastal panorama, Mediterranean climate and soft, hot sand are ideal for complete relaxation. Near the beaches, you will find regional restaurants and cafés offering fresh Mediterranean delicacies.

Villajoyosa delights tourists with its idyllic, cheerful atmosphere and colourful houses. There is no shortage of attractions for those seeking excitement and those interested in history and culture. Just walking along the seashore provides breathtaking views and experiences that are hard to forget.

Frequently asked questions

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP Poble Nou (about 300 m) and IES Marina Baixa (about 400 m).

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Almàssera de Tonda, about 1.8 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Comarcal de la Marina Baixa, about 6 km by road, around 10 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Supermercado Eurospar, about 300 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 56 km by road, around 40 minutes by car.
