Location

Benissa is located between Valencia and Alicante. Manises Airport in Valencia is an hour's drive away, and L'Altet Airport in Alicante is 50 minutes away. Both airports are major

international airports served by many airlines.

Benissa is a small town located in the province of Alicante. The town has won the hearts of many tourists thanks to its incredible views - the sea and mountains meet here, making Benissa a unique place in this part of the Iberian Peninsula.

It is a place that inspires above all with its very well-preserved medieval architecture. While walking around the town, you will discover unique buildings and elegant palaces, see the traditional Lonja (trade exchange), the Cultural Centre and the Franciscan monastery. In the town centre, there is also the Riberero monument, dedicated to the inhabitants who emigrated to Ribera Valenciana to harvest rice.

Around Benissa, there are wonderful hiking trails that reveal breathtaking panoramas of the Mediterranean Sea. In addition, amusement parks and many sports facilities are also within easy reach. Concerts and exhibitions await culture lovers. If you want to experience a typical Spanish fiesta, this place will not disappoint you either. Local festivals are held here in honour of the patron saint Purísima Xiqueta, and in June the Moors and Christians festival is celebrated.

Benissa has four kilometres of coastline with some of the most beautiful bays on the Costa Blanca. The crystal clear waters are ideal for water sports such as diving, sailing, windsurfing and kayaking.

Playa la Fustera is by far the most famous and popular sandy beach in Benissa. The beach has been awarded the Blue Flag, which aims to promote environmental protection in coastal towns, bathing areas and marinas. The beach is ideal for family holidays: it is very easy to get to, both by car and by bus, and there is parking available, as well as numerous showers and toilets. An additional advantage of the beach is the constant supervision of lifeguards during the season from 15 June to 15 September and two restaurants.

Another well-known beach in this coastal town is Cala Baladrar. The picturesque bay offers unearthly views of Calpe and Peñon de Ilfach, while being easily accessible. The water is calm because the beach is sheltered from strong winds. Cala Baladrar is famous for its shallow waters and rich marine fauna, making it the perfect place for diving enthusiasts. It is a small Mediterranean paradise - during the season, there is a beach bar where you can have lunch, drink a cocktail and listen to live music.

Benissa is the perfect place if you want to escape the hustle and bustle of coastal towns but are still looking for an elegant villa or exclusive apartment near the sea. The views of the Mediterranean Sea in this town speak for themselves and are truly breathtaking.
