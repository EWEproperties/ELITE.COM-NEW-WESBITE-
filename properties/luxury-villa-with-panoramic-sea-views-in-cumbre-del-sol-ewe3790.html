Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional luxury villa located in one of the most prestigious areas of the northern Costa Blanca. This exclusive property has been designed for the most discerning buyers seeking a contemporary home in Spain that combines breathtaking Mediterranean Sea views, outstanding construction quality, and complete privacy.

The residence has been thoughtfully designed to create a harmonious connection between modern architecture and its natural surroundings. Elegant contemporary lines, natural stone finishes, floor-to-ceiling windows, and expansive terraces allow natural light to flood the interiors while framing spectacular panoramic sea views throughout the day.

The villa offers approximately 588 m² of built area on an 856 m² plot. Its well-designed layout features three spacious bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms, five elegant bathrooms, an impressive open-plan living and dining area, and a modern kitchen with a central island. The master suite has been designed as a private retreat, complete with a walk-in wardrobe, a luxurious bathroom, and direct access to a panoramic terrace overlooking the Mediterranean Sea.

The outdoor area is centered around a spectacular infinity swimming pool that seamlessly blends with the horizon. Spacious terraces and elegant lounge areas provide the perfect setting for relaxing, outdoor dining, and enjoying unforgettable sunsets all year round.

An additional highlight of the property is the generous lower level, which can be customized to suit the owner's lifestyle. This versatile space is ideal for creating a private gym, wellness area with spa and sauna, home cinema, wine cellar, or a separate guest apartment.

The villa is equipped with state-of-the-art features designed to maximize comfort and energy efficiency, including underfloor heating, air conditioning, an aerothermal system, photovoltaic solar panels, premium-quality windows, and high-end finishing materials throughout.

This outstanding property is an excellent choice for buyers seeking a luxury home in Spain that combines spectacular sea views, contemporary architecture, and an exclusive location on the Costa Blanca. It is perfectly suited as a permanent residence, an elegant holiday home, or a secure investment in one of Europe's most desirable real estate markets.

Location

The development is located in one of the most prestigious areas of the northern Costa Blanca, between Moraira and Jávea. This exceptional location perfectly combines breathtaking Mediterranean Sea views with peace, privacy, and convenient access to all the amenities required for comfortable year-round living. The area is renowned for its exclusive luxury villas, contemporary architecture, and stunning Mediterranean landscape featuring dramatic cliffs and picturesque coves.

Within just a few minutes' drive, residents can reach beautiful sandy beaches and charming coves with crystal-clear waters, ideal for swimming, diving, and a wide range of water sports. The surrounding area also offers elegant marinas, fine dining restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, boutiques, international schools, and medical facilities, ensuring everyday comfort and convenience.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to some of the region's finest golf courses, including Club de Golf Jávea and La Sella Golf Resort. Nature lovers can enjoy numerous hiking and cycling routes through Granadella Natural Park and along the spectacular Costa Blanca coastline, making this an ideal destination for outdoor activities throughout the year.

The property enjoys excellent connections to the area's main destinations. Moraira town centre is approximately 10 minutes away, Jávea around 20 minutes, and Denia about 30 minutes by car. Alicante International Airport can be reached in approximately 1 hour and 15 minutes, while Valencia Airport is around 1 hour and 30 minutes away. This prestigious location offers an exceptional Mediterranean lifestyle while providing strong long-term investment potential in one of Spain's most sought-after real estate markets.
