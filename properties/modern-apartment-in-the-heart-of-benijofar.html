Location

Welcome to Benijófar, a charming town located on the southern Costa Blanca in the province of Alicante. Known for its friendly atmosphere, traditional Spanish architecture, and picturesque surroundings of citrus groves and farmland, Benijófar is one of the hidden gems of the Costa Blanca.

The town is situated just a short drive from the coastal towns of Guardamar del Segura and Torrevieja, and only 30 minutes from Alicante International Airport. Its rich history dates back to Moorish times, and traces of its past can still be seen in the old town with its narrow streets, whitewashed houses, and beautiful church.

Despite its small size, Benijófar offers a wide range of amenities, including restaurants, cafés, shops, and a traditional weekly market held every Tuesday morning.

Nature lovers can enjoy walks along the Segura River or visit the nearby La Mata Natural Park. Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to several renowned golf courses, including La Finca Golf Resort.

The beautiful beaches of Guardamar and La Mata are only a short drive away, offering golden sand, crystal-clear waters, and a variety of leisure facilities.
