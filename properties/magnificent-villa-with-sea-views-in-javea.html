Location

Jávea is a beautiful seaside town located in the province of Alicante in Spain. It is located on the easternmost point of the Iberian Peninsula and is bordered by the Mediterranean Sea on three sides. Jávea is famous for its beautiful beaches, crystal clear waters and idyllic Mediterranean climate.

One of the most popular beaches in Jávea is Arenal Beach. It is a wide sandy beach that stretches for over a kilometer. The beach is ideal for families with children as it is gently sloping and has a playground. It also has many restaurants, cafes and bars along the promenade, providing plenty of food and drink options.

Another popular beach in Jávea is Granadella beach, which is a small and secluded beach located at the foot of a pine-covered hill. It is considered one of the most beautiful beaches in the region and is ideal for snorkelling and scuba diving due to its clear and calm waters. There are also several hiking trails in the surrounding hills, offering breathtaking views of the coast.

Cala Blanca Beach is another beautiful beach in Jávea, famous for its crystal clear waters and pristine white sand. It is located in a small cove and is surrounded by pine trees, which gives a calm and peaceful atmosphere. The beach is perfect for swimming, sunbathing and enjoying the wonderful views of the Mediterranean Sea.

In addition to beautiful beaches, Jávea also has a charming old town, full of history and culture. The town has narrow cobbled streets, whitewashed houses and a picturesque harbour. There are also several sights such as the Church of San Bartolome and the Soler Blasco Archaeological and Ethnographic Museum that are definitely worth a visit.
