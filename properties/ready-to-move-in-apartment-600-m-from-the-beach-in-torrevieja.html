The apartment has been designed with comfortable Mediterranean living in mind. The functional layout includes a bright living area with a lounge and kitchen, a comfortable bedroom and a bathroom.

Thanks to this, the apartment is ready for quick occupancy and can be an attractive option both as a holiday home and for year-round living.

The location provides easy access to all the amenities of Torrevieja. This is an excellent option for those looking for a new apartment close to the sea and all services in Torrevieja.

Location

Torrevieja is one of the best-known towns on the Costa Blanca, appreciated for its exceptional Mediterranean climate, beautiful beaches and comfortable year-round lifestyle. The town is located approximately 40 minutes from Alicante International Airport, providing quick and convenient access for both residents and holiday visitors.

Torrevieja is famous for its wide sandy beaches and picturesque promenades lined with restaurants, cafés and shops. Popular beaches in the area include La Mata, Playa del Cura and Los Locos, ideal for relaxing, walking and enjoying water sports. Another distinctive symbol of the town is the famous Pink Lagoon, which contributes to a unique microclimate considered one of the healthiest in Europe.

The town offers excellent infrastructure for everyday living, including shopping centres, international schools, private clinics, numerous supermarkets and a wide variety of restaurants. Renowned golf courses, marinas and recreational areas can also be found nearby, making Torrevieja an excellent place both for holidays and permanent residence.

It is an ideal location for those looking for a property in sunny Spain, whether as a holiday home, rental investment or a place to enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle all year round.
