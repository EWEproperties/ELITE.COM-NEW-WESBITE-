Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de La Alcaidesa, about 3 km by road, around 5 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Instituto de Educación Secundaria Sierra Almenara, about 9 km, around 11 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Juan Pablo II y San Pedro, about 13 km, around 17 minutes by car.
International school: Sotogrande International School San Roque Campus, about 4 km, around 6 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Juan Batanero, about 8 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de La Línea de la Concepción, about 11 km by road, around 16 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Ruiz Galán, about 3 km away.

How far is the airport?

Málaga Airport is about 117 km by road, around 90 minutes by car.
