An exceptional collection of contemporary villas set in the hills above Marbella, surrounded by protected Mediterranean nature and enjoying expansive views of the Mediterranean Sea. This intimate gated development comprises just 30 private residences, combining contemporary architecture, generous living spaces and privacy with access to five-star resort-style amenities.

This is an outstanding choice for those looking for an exclusive property on the Costa del Sol, close to Marbella town centre and the coastline, yet away from the bustle of the city.

The villas offer more than 800 m² of built area and are available with 4 or 5 spacious bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom. The individual villa designs vary in layout and style, allowing owners to choose a residence that best suits their personal preferences and lifestyle.

At the heart of each villa is an impressive living area, where the lounge, dining room and kitchen come together in a bright, open-plan space filled with natural light. Large floor-to-ceiling windows open directly onto the terraces and garden, creating a seamless connection between the interiors and the surrounding landscape.

Each residence features a private lift connecting all floors, while the additional space on the lower level can be adapted to suit the owner's individual lifestyle and requirements.

The outdoor areas have been designed as a natural extension of the home. Spacious terraces, a private garden and a saltwater infinity pool provide the perfect setting for relaxing and enjoying the Mediterranean climate throughout most of the year. The elevated position offers sweeping views across the valley, the natural surroundings and the Mediterranean Sea. The architecture has been carefully integrated into the landscape, providing a high level of privacy while maximising natural light, space and the surrounding views.

One of the development's greatest advantages is the exceptional range of facilities available exclusively to residents.

Owners have access to an exclusive Owner's Club featuring an indoor swimming pool, spa, sauna and hammam, as well as a modern gym with panoramic sea views, a restaurant, bar and dedicated spaces for relaxation and socialising.

The complex also offers padel courts, walking and running trails, a fitness area, a short-game golf practice area, landscaped green spaces and organic gardens. The experience is complemented by 24-hour controlled access and security, together with concierge services that can include transport arrangements, restaurant reservations, private chefs, wellness services, housekeeping and preparation of the property before the owners' arrival.

LOCATION

The privileged hillside setting above Marbella combines tranquillity, nature and privacy with convenient access to some of the most renowned destinations on the Costa del Sol.

Marbella town centre, the Old Town, the Golden Mile, Puerto Banús and the Mediterranean beaches are all within easy reach. The surrounding area also offers prestigious golf courses, excellent restaurants, international schools and a full range of services, making the location ideal for both holiday stays and year-round living.

The combination of a substantial private residence, panoramic views, beautiful natural surroundings and exclusive resort-style services makes these villas one of the most attractive premium property opportunities in Marbella.
