This new residential development in Guardamar del Segura is an excellent choice for those dreaming of owning an apartment in Spain, close to the sea, with easy access to everyday amenities and outdoor spaces to enjoy throughout the year. The modern building will feature 41 apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms, spacious terraces and, in the case of ground-floor properties, private gardens.

The development stands out for its contemporary architecture, large windows and thoughtfully designed interiors. Each apartment offers a bright living room connected to an open-plan kitchen and dining area, comfortable bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. The terraces are a natural extension of the living space, providing the perfect setting for morning coffee, outdoor dining or simply relaxing in the Mediterranean climate.

The development also includes penthouses with 3 bedrooms and large terraces, offering even more private outdoor space for relaxation.

The apartments will be finished using modern materials in light, timeless colours. Kitchens will feature fitted cabinetry with porcelain worktops, while bathrooms will include walk-in showers with glass screens, vanity units and elegant fittings. Built-in wardrobes are planned for the bedrooms, while additional comfort will be provided by electric shutters, an aerothermal water heating system and pre-installation for ducted air conditioning.

Residents will have access to a communal swimming pool surrounded by a relaxation area. The residential complex will be gated and equipped with a video entry system. Each apartment also includes an underground parking space in the property price.

The 2-bedroom apartments offer usable living areas ranging from 71.7 to 79.8 m², while the 3-bedroom penthouses provide between 100.2 and 104.9 m² of usable living space.

Expected completion date: June 2028.

Location

The development is located in Guardamar del Segura, a popular coastal town on the southern Costa Blanca, known for its wide sandy beaches, natural dunes and extensive green areas.

Its location near the Segura River offers a peaceful setting without sacrificing the convenience of city living. Supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, pharmacies, shops and everyday services can all be found nearby. The centre of Guardamar also offers local markets, a promenade, parks and plenty of places to enjoy a walk.

Guardamar del Segura is a place where you can enjoy the Spanish coastline all year round, not just during the summer months. Its pleasant climate, proximity to the sea and year-round amenities make these apartments an excellent choice as a second home in Spain or a permanent residence.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja Babilònia, about 2 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP Dama de Guardamar (about 700 m) and CEIP Reis Catòlics (about 900 m).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centre de Salut de Guardamar, about 1.4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Quirón/USP Hospital San Jaime, about 12 km by road, around 15 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Masymas, about 400 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 37 km by road, around 30 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 73 km, around 55 minutes by car.
