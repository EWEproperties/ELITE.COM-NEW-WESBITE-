Location

Finestrat is a charming and picturesque town located in the province of Alicante, in the Community of Valencia, Spain. It is located just 8 kilometres from the popular large city of Benidorm and 40 kilometres from Alicante and its international airport. Known for its stunning mountain views, unspoilt beaches and rich cultural heritage, Finestrat is the ideal destination for anyone looking for a relaxing and unforgettable experience. There is no shortage of exciting things to do in Finestrat.

Finestrat is located on the slope of the Puig Campana mountain, offering magnificent views of the mountains, the coast and the Mediterranean Sea. The town has a typical Moorish atmosphere, with colourfully painted houses and narrow cobbled streets. Cala Finestrat, on the other hand, is becoming increasingly popular and its development has almost made it an extension of Benidorm itself.

Cala Finestrat

Cala Finestrat is a seaside resort located just three kilometres from busy Benidorm. The resort offers adequate facilities, a large selection of restaurants, bars and shops. Everything you need is available in Benidorm, which is easily accessible by bus and taxi.

La Cala beach is fairly quiet, as most of those craving a more upbeat atmosphere head for the beaches surrounding Benidorm. The beach is a clean stretch of golden sand, ideal for children and safe for swimming. There are several charming restaurants along the promenade and during the summer months there is an arts and crafts market in the afternoon.

The location is suitable for families and couples looking for a relaxing beach resort, yet close to many attractions and amenities.

The Terre Mitica and Terra Natura theme parks, Aqualandia water park and Mundomar marine and exotic animal park are all nearby, offering fun for the whole family.

Meanwhile, if you prefer to spend your holiday enjoying a round or two of golf, Finestrat will be pleased with the nearby Real Faula Golf Club, which offers two excellent courses.

Both Finestrat and Cala Finestrat are quiet destinations compared to some of the neighbouring resorts. Both offer a selection of local bars and restaurants where you can enjoy good food and a relaxed atmosphere.
