The home

The townhouse is set over several floors. The living room and kitchen share one level and the three bedrooms are on another, so the day space never disturbs the night space. There are three bathrooms. Large windows bring in plenty of light and open onto terraces, where you can eat outdoors for most of the year. The architecture is simple and modern, and the car stays out of sight in the underground garage, with two spaces assigned to each home.

The development

The development has 56 townhouses with 3 or 4 bedrooms. Residents share a swimming pool, a gym and a social club for meeting neighbours, family and friends, so a swim or a workout doesn't mean leaving the complex. Completion is planned for the first quarter of 2028. The homes work equally well for living all year round, for holidays or for long winter stays.

The area

Mutxamel is a quiet town in the province of Alicante, just inland from the coast. The development is in L'Alluser, a residential area with schools, supermarkets, shops and sports facilities nearby, about 2 km from the centre of Mutxamel. The nearest beach is Muchavista, about 8 km by car, and the long sandy Playa de San Juan is about 10 km away. Golfers have Bonalba Golf in Mutxamel itself and Alicante Golf near Playa de San Juan. Alicante city centre is about 20 minutes by car and Alicante-Elche airport about 25 minutes.
