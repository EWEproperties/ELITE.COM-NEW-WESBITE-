About this property

New Townhouses for Sale in Mutxamel, Alicante.

Elite World Estates presents an attractive residential development of contemporary townhouses located in Mutxamel, on the northern Costa Blanca. Designed for those seeking a spacious and comfortable home in a peaceful residential setting, this development combines modern architecture, functional layouts, and excellent access to local amenities and the Mediterranean coastline.

The residential complex consists of 56 modern townhouses with 3 and 4 bedrooms, offering a choice of layouts to suit different lifestyles. The properties are designed across several floors, with a clear separation between the living and sleeping areas, creating practical and comfortable spaces for everyday living.

Each home features a bright and spacious living area, a functional kitchen, comfortable bedrooms, and terraces that provide a natural connection between indoor and outdoor spaces. Contemporary architectural lines and carefully planned interiors make the most of the available space and natural light.

All properties include two parking spaces in the underground garage, providing convenience and secure parking for residents.

The residential complex also offers a range of communal facilities designed to enhance everyday comfort and leisure. Residents can enjoy a communal swimming pool, a gym, and a social club, creating opportunities for relaxation, exercise, and spending time with family and friends without leaving the development.

Located in the L'Alluser area of Mutxamel, the development benefits from a peaceful residential environment while remaining close to the services needed for everyday life. Schools, supermarkets, shops, sports facilities, and other amenities are available nearby.

The beaches of Muchavista and San Juan are within easy reach, while convenient road connections provide access to Alicante, the wider Costa Blanca, and Alicante-Elche International Airport.

This development is an excellent option for those looking for a modern family home on the Costa Blanca, whether for permanent residence, a holiday home, or a Mediterranean lifestyle with more space and comfort.

Mutxamel, Alicante | A Comfortable Mediterranean Lifestyle

Mutxamel is a well-connected residential town in the province of Alicante, on Spain's northern Costa Blanca. It offers a pleasant combination of a quieter residential atmosphere, everyday amenities, and convenient access to the coast and the city of Alicante.

The L'Alluser area provides a residential setting with schools, supermarkets, shops, sports facilities, and other essential services nearby. This makes the location suitable for families and those who want to enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle while having practical amenities within easy reach.

One of the main advantages of the location is its proximity to the beaches of Muchavista and San Juan, two well-known coastal destinations where residents can enjoy sandy beaches and the Mediterranean climate.

Thanks to its road connections, Mutxamel also provides convenient access to Alicante and other destinations along the Costa Blanca, as well as Alicante-Elche International Airport.

The combination of modern homes, communal leisure facilities, nearby services, and access to the coast makes this development an appealing choice for permanent living or a second home in Spain.
