Location

Guardamar del Segura is a Mediterranean paradise , a beautiful town located in the province of Alicante, in the Region of

Valencia in Spain. Situated right by the sea on the picturesque Costa Blanca, this Mediterranean gem is known for its beautiful beaches, rich, diverse wildlife and fascinating history. Over 1,800 years old, Guardamar del Segura has witnessed the passing of civilisations that have left a lasting mark on this wonderful corner of the world. Discover the beauty of its beaches, experience the lush countryside and learn about the traditions that have made Guardamar del Segura a truly unparalleled destination.

The town boasts a unique and unparalleled natural landscape: The Guardamar Dunes. Located to the south of the town, the dunes are a protected area that is home to a valuable natural diversity.

This particular ecosystem is home to protected species such as the common short-toed lizard and the Greek tortoise, and its conservation is crucial to the ecological balance of the region. Visitors can explore the dunes by following paths through the native vegetation, providing an opportunity to get in touch with nature.

The Alfonso XIII Park is also worth a visit. This urban park is an oasis of shade and greenery, providing the ideal place to relax or enjoy sports.

The beaches of Guardamar del Segura are the main attraction for visitors who want to enjoy the warm Mediterranean sun. Along eleven kilometres of coastline, there are several beaches that offer endless opportunities for enjoying the sea and sunbathing. Guardamar beach, considered the symbol of the area, is famous for its fine golden sand and crystal clear waters. It has the prestigious Blue Flag, which guarantees a high standard of quality and safety for bathers. La Roqueta beach, located right next to the marina, is another popular choice. With impressive dunes surrounding it, it offers a quieter and less crowded atmosphere for sun seekers who want to relax and enjoy nature

The cuisine of Guardamar del Segura is a feast for the palate, where the flavours of the sea combine with the richness of the land. Fresh seafood dishes are a feast for the senses and reflect the fishing tradition of the region. 'Caldero' is one of the main dishes, a tasty fish soup with rice typical of the area. Local bars and pubs entice visitors through a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere, where they can sample delicious local cocktails and drinks.

Guardamar del Segura is also the ideal place for lovers of active tourism and outdoor sports. The beaches and sea are home to many water activities such as diving, sailing, kayaking and snorkelling. Hiking and cycling are popular in the region due to the beauty of the surrounding countryside. The Vía Verde del Segura is a former railway line converted into a route for cyclists and pedestrians that runs through beautiful countryside and allows visitors to explore the area in a sustainable way
