Elite World Estates presents a new residential development located in the prestigious La Manga Club in the Murcia region. This is an exceptional opportunity for those looking for a property in Spain that combines peaceful surroundings, a Mediterranean climate and access to extensive sports and leisure facilities.

This exclusive residential complex offers 3-bedroom apartments, designed to provide space, natural light and everyday comfort. The interiors feature a spacious living and dining area with an open-plan kitchen, three bedrooms and two bathrooms.

The apartments offer built areas ranging from approximately 102 m² to 120 m², while the private gardens and solariums of selected properties exceed 117 m².

Ground-floor apartments feature spacious terraces and private gardens, while properties on the upper floor offer terraces and large private rooftop solariums, providing additional outdoor space to relax and enjoy the Mediterranean climate.

Residents will have access to a communal swimming pool for adults and children, landscaped gardens and a pétanque area. Each property also includes a private parking space with pre-installation for electric vehicle charging.

The apartments will be delivered to a high standard. The kitchens will be fitted with modern cabinetry and appliances, including a ceramic hob, oven, microwave and extractor hood, while the worktops will be finished in compact quartz such as Silestone or a similar material.

The specifications also include ducted hot and cold air conditioning, an aerothermal system, electric shutters, fitted wardrobes and fully finished bathrooms with shower screens. The buildings have been designed to achieve an A energy efficiency rating.

An additional advantage is the pre-installation for a jacuzzi/spa in designated areas of the private ground-floor gardens and rooftop solariums.

The estimated completion date is February 2028.

The development is located in the heart of La Manga Club, one of the most prestigious sports and leisure resorts in the Murcia region. Surrounded by mountains, green areas and golf courses, it offers a peaceful setting and excellent conditions for enjoying the Mediterranean lifestyle throughout the year.

La Manga Club provides an extensive range of sports and leisure facilities, including three 18-hole golf courses, tennis and padel facilities and professional sports facilities. Residents also have convenient access to restaurants, shops, hotel facilities and wellness services.

Another major advantage of the location is its proximity to both the Mediterranean Sea and the Mar Menor, providing easy access to beautiful beaches and coastal towns. It is an excellent location for both holidays and comfortable year-round living.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala del Barco, about 5 km by road, around 15 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio Público San Ginés de la Jara, about 10 km, around 14 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Nuestra Señora del Carmen, about 14 km, around 15 minutes by car.
International school: Agave International School, about 25 km, around 29 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud La Unión, about 16 km away. The nearest hospital is Centro Sociosanitario 'Cristo de los Mineros', about 17 km by road, around 19 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Dia, about 2.5 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 50 km by road, around 35 minutes by car.
