Location

Pilar de la Horadada has everything you need to enjoy the sea, pine forests and Spanish sun. The coastline of Pilar de la Hordada is over 4 kilometers long along a very diverse scenery. There you will find wide beaches with fine white sand, small stone wall ledges suspended over the water and small and intimate hidden coves with golden sand.

The resort is located in the south-east of Spain and borders with Murcia (about 15 minutes away). Alicante is approximately a 40-minute drive from here.

The sea here is usually calm, the beaches are safe, there are no steep slopes, which allows even the smallest children to swim safely. Moreover, clear water and great visibility invite divers to observe the beauty of aquatic life.

The cleanliness of the beaches in Pilar de la Hordada has been awarded the blue flag of the European Union, which guarantees a healthy environment.

Inland, you can make various interesting trips near the towns of Sierra and Escalona. There are two marked tourist routes along the Rio Seco riverbed, 19 kilometers long, definitely worth a visit. One of the routes is 4 kilometers long, while the other is 10 kilometers long, stretching from the natural area of Pilar de Campoverde to the mouth of Mil Palmeras beach.

There is also an 18-hole golf course in Lo Romero near the town of Pilar de la Horadada. Both professionals in this field and beginners will find a good place there.

Gastronomy has a privileged place in the culture of this beautiful city. Typical dishes of Pilar de la Hordada include potatoes with meat stew, meatballs, rabbit in garlic and Gachas Migas (flour, garlic pancake), as well as great rice dishes and soups. Sweets and cakes also deserve recognition here, confectionery such as meringue cake called 'milhojas' or almond sweets called 'cordiales' are the most popular.
