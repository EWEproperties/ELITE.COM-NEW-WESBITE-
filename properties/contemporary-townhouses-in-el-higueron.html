Elite World Estates proudly presents an exclusive collection of contemporary townhouses located in the prestigious area of El Higuerón, perfectly positioned between Málaga and Marbella on the Costa del Sol. Designed for buyers seeking privacy, comfort and a genuine Mediterranean lifestyle, this exceptional development combines elegant architecture, spacious interiors and a privileged natural setting just minutes from the beach.

The development comprises 52 stylish three-bedroom townhouses, thoughtfully designed to provide generous living spaces, modern functionality and a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living. Every home has been created to maximise natural light, allowing open-plan interiors to flow effortlessly onto spacious terraces and private gardens, creating bright, welcoming spaces that can be enjoyed throughout the year.

The homes feature spacious open-concept living and dining areas, fully equipped contemporary kitchens and carefully designed interiors that prioritise comfort and practicality. In addition to the main living areas, each residence includes a versatile multi-purpose room and a private basement with additional storage space, offering flexibility to adapt to every stage of family life.

Expansive terraces become a natural extension of the home, providing the perfect setting for outdoor dining, relaxing in the sunshine or enjoying peaceful evenings overlooking the surrounding landscape. Every detail has been carefully considered to create a harmonious balance between contemporary design, functionality and everyday comfort.

Residents benefit from an outstanding location in one of the Costa del Sol's most sought-after residential areas. El Higuerón offers a unique combination of tranquillity and excellent connectivity, with beautiful beaches, prestigious golf courses, sports facilities, restaurants, shopping areas and everyday amenities all within easy reach.

The development enjoys excellent transport connections, with Málaga International Airport, the commuter train station and the main motorway all just a short drive away. The vibrant centres of Fuengirola, Benalmádena, Marbella and Málaga are easily accessible, making this an ideal location for both permanent living and holiday homes.

Combining contemporary architecture, spacious homes, a privileged location and the relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle, this exclusive development offers a unique opportunity to own a modern townhouse in one of the most prestigious areas of the Costa del Sol.

Location

The development is located in the prestigious area of El Higuerón, between Fuengirola and Benalmádena, in one of the most exclusive locations on the western Costa del Sol. This exceptional setting perfectly combines the tranquility of a Mediterranean environment with excellent transport connections and quick access to beaches, luxury amenities, and all the services required for comfortable year-round living. El Higuerón has long been regarded as one of the region's most desirable residential areas, offering high-quality developments, security, and an exceptional lifestyle surrounded by greenery and the Mediterranean Sea.

Just a few minutes' walk away is the sandy Carvajal Beach and its lively seafront promenade, lined with restaurants, cafés, and beach clubs. The nearby Carvajal train station provides direct connections to Fuengirola town centre, Málaga-Costa del Sol Airport, and the historic centre of Málaga. Thanks to its convenient location, residents can enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle without relying on a car for everyday activities.

The surrounding area offers supermarkets, restaurants, international schools, medical centres, and the prestigious Higuerón Resort, featuring a luxury spa, wellness centre, state-of-the-art sports club, padel courts, and excellent dining options. Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to renowned golf courses such as Torrequebrada Golf, Cerrado del Águila Golf, and Mijas Golf, while outdoor lovers can enjoy numerous walking and cycling routes along the coastline and through the surrounding hills.

Excellent connectivity is one of the area's greatest advantages. Fuengirola town centre is approximately 10 minutes away, Benalmádena around 10 minutes, Marbella approximately 25 minutes, while Málaga International Airport can be reached in just 15–20 minutes. Easy access to the AP-7 motorway and the Cercanías commuter rail network makes El Higuerón an ideal destination for both permanent living and property investment with strong growth potential.
