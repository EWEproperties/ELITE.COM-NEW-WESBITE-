Location

Guardamar del Segura is a charming and popular seaside town on the southern Costa Blanca, combining a relaxing seaside lifestyle with stunning nature and an authentic Spanish atmosphere. The town is renowned for its miles of beautiful, sandy beaches, considered among the best in the area, surrounded by protected dunes and pine forests, which give Guardamar a unique, green character. Life here revolves around the sea, nature, and a pleasant climate with approximately 320 days of sunshine a year, making it attractive year-round.

The town center is vibrant and welcoming, with squares, cafés, restaurants, and local shops where locals and tourists mingle daily. A popular weekly market attracts tourists from across the region and adds to the town's dynamism. At the same time, Guardamar maintains a more leisurely pace than larger coastal towns, making it an ideal destination for those seeking a balance of activity and relaxation. The seaside promenade and numerous walking and cycling paths allow you to enjoy the outdoors from morning to night.

Guardamar del Segura also offers excellent services and excellent transport links. Schools, health centers, sports facilities, and a wide range of leisure activities are available, and the proximity of golf courses, nature reserves, and the Segura River offers numerous opportunities for active recreation. Alicante Airport is approximately 30 minutes away, making the city easily accessible for both residents and tourists. With its combination of nature, beach, services, and authentic charm, Guardamar del Segura is a place where quality of life is a priority – a city to enjoy, to miss, and to call home.
