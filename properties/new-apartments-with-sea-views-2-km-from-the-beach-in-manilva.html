A new residential development located in a peaceful area of Manilva on the Costa del Sol, in the exclusive Bahía de las Rocas area, close to Sotogrande. The elevated position of the complex provides beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding landscape, while the location offers tranquillity, nature and easy access to beaches, golf courses and nearby coastal towns.

The complex offers modern apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. The properties have been designed with space, natural light and comfortable living in mind. Large windows connect the living room with a spacious terrace, creating an open and bright living area and allowing residents to fully enjoy the Mediterranean climate. The apartments have southeast or southwest orientation, and many of them offer sea views.

The 2-bedroom apartments offer approximately 82–84 m² of built area, while the 3-bedroom properties provide around 104 m². One of the main features of the homes is their spacious terraces of approximately 31–35 m². Properties with private gardens are also available, as well as penthouses with large private solariums.

The interiors feature contemporary design and carefully selected materials. The kitchens are fully fitted and equipped with Bosch appliances, including an oven, microwave, induction hob, extractor hood, refrigerator, dishwasher and washing machine. The apartments also feature ducted air conditioning with both cooling and heating, as well as an aerothermal system for domestic hot water.

Residents can enjoy communal swimming pools, landscaped gardens and shared recreational areas, creating an ideal environment for relaxation throughout the year. Each apartment also includes an underground parking space and storage room, with pre-installation for an electric vehicle charging point.

The estimated completion date of the development is November 2027.

Location

The development is located in Bahía de las Rocas in Manilva, in an attractive area of the Costa del Sol between the Manilva coastline and Sotogrande. The surrounding area offers numerous beaches, marinas and renowned golf courses. Thanks to its elevated position, the complex also enjoys panoramic views towards Gibraltar and the North African coastline.

  • Beach – approximately 2 km
  • Golf course – approximately 10 km
  • Gibraltar Airport – approximately 30 km
  • Sotogrande – a few minutes by car
  • Estepona – easily accessible via the A-7

Retaining the charm of a traditional Spanish village, Manilva offers a peaceful setting close to the Strait of Gibraltar, with a rich geographical and cultural landscape. With almost 8 kilometres of sandy beaches, including rocky coves and long stretches of coastline, there are plenty of opportunities for swimming, diving, fishing and water sports.

In addition to its beautiful beaches and marinas, Manilva offers a variety of historical and cultural attractions, including La Duquesa Castle, several churches and archaeological exhibits at the Municipal Archaeological Museum.

Outdoor activities, shopping and a wide selection of restaurants can also be enjoyed throughout the municipality, making Manilva an attractive destination for both permanent residents and visitors.
