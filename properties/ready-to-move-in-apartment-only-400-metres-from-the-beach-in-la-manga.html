Location

La Manga or La Manga del Mar Menor is a peninsula in Spain between the Mediterranean Sea and the Mar Menor near Murcia. La Manga is located near the city of Cartagena. It is 22 km long and approx. 100 m wide. Here you can find everything you would expect from a holiday in this country: sunny and sandy beaches, blue sea, amazing views and tasty food.

It is the perfect holiday destination, with the Mar Menor on one side, which is technically a bay, and the Mediterranean Sea on the other.

La Manga, which means ‘sleeve’ in Spanish, is located on the Costa Calida in the Murcia region. It is 24 kilometres long and less than 40-50 metres wide at its narrowest point. It is a bit like the Spanish Hel Peninsula, but with a warmer and saltier sea and less lush vegetation.

Thanks to the peninsula's location, it is already warm and the sea is warm in June. It is no surprise that the region is popular with water sports enthusiasts such as windsurfers and kitesurfers as well as snorkellers and divers.

Some of the most popular beaches on both the Mediterranean and the Atlantic are, for example, Cala del Pino, Playa Mistral, Playa del Barco Perdido, Playa del Pudrimel, Mahalo Beach, Cala Reona, Playa la Gola, Playa del Pudrimel, Playa del Estacio, Playa de Cavanna, Playa del Banco del Tabal, Playa de Puerto Bello, and Playa Paraíso.

On the other hand, diving enthusiasts should visit Isla Grossa, El Fallon Cabo de Palos, and the Isla Hormigas Marine Reserve. The latter is one of the best diving spots in all of Spain.

There is something for everyone here. Fans of historic architecture can visit the Faro Cabo de Palos lighthouse or the nearby city of Cartagena with its Roman buildings.

The La Manga peninsula can be reached by flying to San Javier airport in Murcia, but there are no direct flights from the UK yet.

The closest airport for flights from the UK is El Altet in Alicante. The journey to La Manga from Alicante takes about 1.5 hours.
