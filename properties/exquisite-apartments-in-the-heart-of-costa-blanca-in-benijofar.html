Location

Discover Benijofar: A Charming Town in the Heart of Costa Blanca

Benijofar is a hidden gem nestled in the heart of Costa Blanca. This picturesque town offers a delightful blend of natural beauty, rich cultural heritage, and a vibrant lifestyle. Located in close proximity to the airport, coastal attractions, and a plethora of activities, Benijofar is the perfect destination for those seeking an idyllic getaway.

Proximity to Airports and Coastline

Benijofar enjoys an enviable location that offers easy access to major airports and stunning beaches. The town is conveniently situated just a short drive away from Alicante Airport ( 25 minuts), making it a convenient choice for both domestic and international travelers. Within a short distance, you'll find the pristine beaches of the Costa Blanca coastline, where you can bask in the sun, take a refreshing dip in the Mediterranean Sea, or enjoy various water sports activities.

Tranquil Charm and Authentic Atmosphere

Benijofar embraces a relaxed and welcoming ambiance, providing a serene escape from the hustle and bustle of larger cities. The town's narrow streets are adorned with traditional Spanish houses, blooming bougainvillea, and charming local shops. As you stroll through the quaint town center, you'll be captivated by the authentic atmosphere and the warm hospitality of the locals.

Things to Do and Places to Visit

Roqueta Park: Explore the natural beauty of Benijofar at Roqueta Park, a verdant oasis offering lush green spaces, walking trails, and picnic areas. Take a leisurely stroll, have a family picnic, or simply relax amidst nature's tranquility.

La Marquesa Golf Course: Golf enthusiasts will find themselves in paradise at La Marquesa Golf Course. This scenic 18-hole course offers breathtaking views of the surrounding landscapes, providing a challenging yet enjoyable experience for players of all skill levels.

Cultural Center: Casa de Cultura: Immerse yourself in the local culture at the Casa de Cultura. This cultural center hosts a variety of events, including art exhibitions, concerts, theater performances, and workshops, showcasing the vibrant arts scene of Benijofar.

Weekly Market: Indulge in the vibrant flavors and colors of the region at the weekly market. Held every Tuesday, the market offers a wide array of fresh local produce, handmade crafts, clothing, and souvenirs. Immerse yourself in the lively atmosphere as you explore the stalls and discover unique treasures.

Day Trip to Guardamar del Segura: Just a short drive from Benijofar, you'll find the coastal town of Guardamar del Segura. With its stunning blue-flag beaches, sand dunes, and a picturesque marina, Guardamar del Segura is the perfect place to unwind, soak up the sun, and enjoy the vibrant beachfront promenad.
