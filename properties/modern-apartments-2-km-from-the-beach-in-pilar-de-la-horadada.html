New 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartments in a gated development of 90 homes in Pilar de la Horadada, about 2 km from the beaches of Torre de la Horadada and Mil Palmeras. Choose a ground-floor home with a private garden, a first-floor home with a balcony, or a top-floor home with a private rooftop solarium. Prices start at €250,000 with a parking space in the underground garage, and the buyer pays no agency fee.

The home

Every home has about 54 m² of usable living space: an open-plan living room and kitchen, 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Ground-floor homes have a private garden of 103 to 219 m². First-floor homes have a balcony. Top-floor homes have a private solarium of 51 to 64 m², with water, electricity and a TV point, prepared for a jacuzzi and an outdoor kitchen.

The kitchen has upper and lower cabinets, a quartz worktop and a stainless-steel extractor hood. Bedrooms have fitted wardrobes, and the bathrooms have porcelain tiles, anti-slip shower trays and screens. Other details include double glazing, electric blinds, aerothermal hot water, and ducts ready for air conditioning.

Prices from: first floor €250,000, ground floor with garden €270,000, top floor with solarium €330,000 (plus VAT).

The development

The development is built in two phases around landscaped communal gardens, with a large communal pool and a children's pool. Every home comes with one parking space in the underground garage. It suits buyers looking for a holiday home, a home for the whole year, or a property to rent out.

Planned completion date: May 2028 (Phase I) and October 2028 (Phase II).

The area

Pilar de la Horadada is the most southern town of the Costa Blanca, on the border with the Murcia region. Shops, cafés, restaurants and everyday services are a short walk away.

  • Torre de la Horadada and Mil Palmeras beaches: about 2 km
  • Lo Romero Golf (18 holes): about 5 km
  • Zenia Boulevard shopping centre: about 15 minutes by car
  • Murcia International Airport (Corvera): about 35 minutes by car
  • Alicante Airport: about 45 minutes by car

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa del Puerto, about 2.9 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP Martín Artigot (about 1.4 km) and CEIP María Moliner (about 1.5 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is the local public health centre (Centro de Salud), about 1.9 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 13 km by road, around 13 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Manper, about 900 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 40 km by road, around 30 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 80 km, around 60 minutes by car.
