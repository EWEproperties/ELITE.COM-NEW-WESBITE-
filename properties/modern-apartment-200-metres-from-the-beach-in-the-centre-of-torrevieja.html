Location

Torrevieja, a town located about 45 km from Alicante, is famous for its unique, healthy microclimate. This is largely attributed to the large salt deposits and two remarkable lagoons, Laguna de Torrevieja and La Laguna de la Mata, in the heart of the town. These lagoons, one pink and the other green, are not only a visual wonder but also a habitat for various species of birds, including flamingos. The pink hue of Laguna de Torrevieja, caused by high salinity, crustaceans and algae, makes it a unique attraction.

La Laguna de la Mata, with its green waters, is home to a park interpretation centre and a variety of unique flora, including Aleppo pines and orchids. Also noteworthy are the vineyards in the area, which produce the local wine de la Mata.

Torrevieja's climate is ideal for sunbathers. From the 2.5-kilometre-long, Blue Flag-awarded Playa de la Mata to the accessible and lively beaches of Playa de los Locos and Playa del Cura, there is a beach for everyone here. In the city centre, natural pools among the rocky coastline offer a unique swimming experience, enhanced by nearby statues such as the Man of the Sea and the Beautiful Lola. Cala Ferris and Cala Piteras, with their stunning views, palm trees and snorkelling opportunities, are not to be missed.

Strolling along the Vista Alegre promenade, you'll immerse yourself in the lively atmosphere of the city with its abundance of bars, restaurants, cafés and shops. When it comes to entertainment, Torrevieja boasts the Aquopolis water park and nearby amusement park, as well as summer open-air discos.

Sports enthusiasts will find Torrevieja a paradise, with facilities for football, handball, tennis, rugby, swimming and more. The city, which once had only an observation tower (“old tower” or “torre vieja”), has been transformed into an architectural marvel.

With over 300 days of sunshine a year, Torrevieja is more than just a holiday destination; it is an enticing place for a longer stay or a new home.
