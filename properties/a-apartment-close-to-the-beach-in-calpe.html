Location

Calpe, a captivating coastal town nestled on the Mediterranean, beckons with its undeniable charm and an array of attractions. Located in the province of Alicante, Spain, Calpe offers a delightful blend of natural beauty, historical landmarks, and vibrant activities. Situated approximately 75 kilometers from Alicante Airport, Calpe provides convenient access for travelers seeking to explore its wonders.

Upon arrival, you'll find yourself captivated by the stunning beaches that have earned the prestigious "Q" for Quality and the Blue Flag certifications. Calpe boasts some of the finest sandy shores in the region, including the iconic Playa de la Fossa and Playa de la Arenal-Bol. Immerse yourself in the Mediterranean bliss, bask in the sun, or indulge in water sports and activities that are an integral part of Calpe's coastal landscape.

Beyond its picturesque beaches, Calpe is adorned with historical landmarks and cultural treasures. The emblematic Peñón de Ifach, a massive limestone rock formation, stands as a symbol of Calpe and offers breathtaking panoramic views. Take a hike to the summit and marvel at the natural beauty that unfolds before your eyes. The old town, with its charming narrow streets and historic architecture, invites you to wander and explore its rich heritage.

Calpe's bustling fishing port is a must-visit destination. Watch the fishermen bring in their daily catch, visit the fish market, and savor fresh seafood delicacies in the numerous restaurants that line the harbor. For a taste of local culture, visit the Museum of Archaeology and Ethnography to delve into Calpe's fascinating history.

Nature enthusiasts will find solace in the natural parks that surround Calpe. Explore the Las Salinas Natural Park, a haven for migratory birds such as flamingos and storks, or venture into the Serra Gelada Natural Park to witness its rugged cliffs and captivating trails. For hiking enthusiasts, the Sierra de Bernia and Sierra de Oltà offer exhilarating routes and stunning vistas.

Indulge in the vibrant atmosphere of Calpe's local markets, where you can discover fresh produce, handicrafts, and traditional products. Sample the local gastronomy, renowned for its seafood, rice dishes, and savory tapas.

Beyond the confines of your residence, immerse yourself in the awe-inspiring beauty of Calpe. Located in close proximity to the Mediterranean coast, you'll have the privilege of embracing the gentle caress of the Mediterranean breeze, feeling the fine sand beneath your feet, and admiring the crystal-clear waters at a moment's notice. Calpe's beaches have been certified with the prestigious "Q" for Quality and the Blue Flag, attesting to their exceptional standards. Furthermore, the remarkable microclimate, rich flora, and diverse fauna further enhance the allure of this coastal paradise. Water sports and activities are seamlessly integrated into the landscape, offering endless adventures and opportunities for exploration.

Nature lovers will find solace in the proximity to natural parks, where you can immerse yourself in the wonders of the surroundings. From the enchanting Natural Park of Las Salinas, where migratory birds such as flamingos, coots, Audouin's gulls, and storks grace the skies, to the panoramic views offered from the highest point of the Peñón de Ifach. Prepare to be mesmerized by the vistas that stretch before you, encompassing the Serra Gelada Natural Park, the protected landscape of Puig Campana, the majestic Sierra de Aitana, and even glimpses of the distant island of Ibiza on the clearest of days.
