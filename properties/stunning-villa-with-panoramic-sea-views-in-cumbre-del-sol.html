This luxurious villa with spectacular sea views is a dream come true for all those who want a Mediterranean residence in the best possible location on the Costa Blanca. It is located in an elite residential complex in Cumbre del Sol. It is a prestigious residential area, completely safe and private, where luxury residences blend perfectly with the mountain landscapes and the blue of the Mediterranean Sea in the background.

Property characterized by modern architecture oriented to open space, towards the blue of the sea. Thus, you can enjoy amazing views from anywhere. The investment has been designed with the thought of sharing common moments and celebrating in a Mediterranean style.

The entire living area is located on the first floor for extra comfort. The night zone consists of three bedrooms bathed in natural light, which retain a large dose of privacy in relation to the day zone. All rooms are spacious and bright and have their own bathroom. The master bedroom has a dressing room, a large terrace and a bathroom with a bathtub, where you can relax while watching the sea. The cube that houses the terrace of the master bedroom, which extends outwards and overlooks the sea, stands out.

The living room, dining room and kitchen are connected to the outdoors thanks to the use of glass walls that favor a permanent visual connection with the sea. The living area is the perfect place for your guests, its spacious lounge includes a dining area that can accommodate a large family.

The terrace with an area of over 70m² is a real treasure of this house. It has an infinity swimming pool in the central point from which we can admire the majestic views and relax all year round. The house is complemented by an additional space with two verandas and a garage.

The geometric shapes that outline the external structure are covered with glass and slabs of natural stone in gray, which contrasts with the white of the façade. Modernity perfectly harmonizes with the natural surroundings creating a delightful whole worthy of admiration.

Location

Situated along Spain's stunning Costa Blanca, Cumbre del Sol is a hidden gem where the beauty of the coast meets tranquility. Rising majestically atop cliffs, this picturesque residential neighborhood offers inspiring landscapes and a Spanish charm that captivates all who visit.

The beaches here are a real paradise, each with its own charm. Cala del Moraig boasts crystal clear waters and cliffs, inviting snorkelers and divers to discover the underwater wonders. Surrounded by rocky cliffs, Playa de Los Tiestos provides a peaceful escape for those looking for a quiet day by the sea. A hidden oasis, Cala Llebeig is at the end of a scenic hike, offering a peaceful and unspoilt setting for perfect relaxation.

For those who love discovering the wonders of nature, Cumbre del Sol offers a network of hiking trails to immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the region. The Ruta de los Acantilados' (cliff trail) offers captivating coastal views that will leave you breathless, while the Ruta del Faro' (lighthouse trail) leads to the iconic Cap d'Or lighthouse, a symbol of maritime heritage. Water lovers can enjoy canoeing, paddle boarding and sailing in the calm waters of the nearby bays, adding to the charm of this seaside haven.

For a unique perspective of the area, the horse riding trails from the Cumbre del Sol Equestrian Center offer an enchanting experience that combines nature and adventure.

Cumbre del Sol also boasts a great culinary setting with plenty of dining options waiting for you. At Mirador del Mar, Mediterranean cuisine is served in a modern style, all set against the backdrop of mesmerizing sea views. Restaurante La Cumbre tempts with a mix of Spanish and international dishes, which can be enjoyed on the terrace overlooking the sea. El Almendro enchants visitors with its offer of traditional Spanish dishes and delicious seafood, a real feast for the taste buds.
