Experience the luxurious lifestyle that awaits you at this residencial complex in Playa Albir, l'Alfas del Pi.

Discover this brand new project of modern apartments located in Playa Albir, l'Alfas del Pi which offers a remarkable environment for permanent residence or holiday-making. This residencial complex occupies a fully consolidated area, situated on a 30,000 square meter plot in the prime location of Albir.

Apartment boasts a consolidated location with exceptional gardens and top-tier amenities, all of which are just a stone's throw from the shoreline and the natural surroundings. The distinguishing feature of your future apartment is their capacious ground floor gardens and extensive terraces, ranging from 20 to over 130 square meters, that provide stunning views of the sea and Sierra Helada Natural Park.

Apartments come with an open plan living and kitchen area, which is a spacious and comfortable area designed for relaxation and entertainment. The natural light that streams in through the large windows illuminates the room and highlights the modern and sophisticated design. The open-plan kitchen is fully equipped with top-of-the-line appliances and offers ample storage space for all your cooking needs. The layout is functional and modern, allowing you to cook and entertain at the same time.

You can choose between apartments with one, two, three, or four bedrooms, which are spacious and elegantly designed with comfort and relaxation in mind. Large windows provide natural light and allow for stunning views of the surrounding area. The bathrooms are sleek and contemporary, featuring high-quality finishes and fixtures.

This complex features numerous ornamental fountains and several swimming pools, including a heated indoor pool, located at various levels surrounding the buildings within the complex. In close proximity to the outdoor pools, the complex features a Pool Bar and Sun Deck, equipped with luxurious sun loungers, umbrellas, and stylish furniture for sunbathing in comfort.

Urbanization also includes a fruit and vegetable grove that pays homage to the traditional agricultural roots of Albir. The land will be exploited agriculturally through terrace plantations, allowing visitors to experience the area's agricultural heritage first-hand.

Tennis and Paddle courts will be thoughtfully incorporated into the landscaping, harmoniously blending in with the natural surroundings. The development will also provide co-working facilities and specific amenities, allowing guests to work remotely in complete comfort and convenience.

Complex boasts an e-parking facility that offers charging points for electric and hybrid vehicles on demand. There is also a bike parking and maintenance area, as well as differentiated washing areas for both vehicles and bikes. The complex features a heated indoor and outdoor swimming pool, tennis and paddle court, and a fully equipped gym and sauna for the exclusive use of residents. Other amenities include a multipurpose community room with a Gourmet and wine cellar room, a cinema, a nautical equipment storage room, and heated waste disposal rooms. The concierge and parcel reception service is air-conditioned, and there is also a coworking and reading room available for guests.

Location

Albir is a popular coastal town in Alicante, Spain, that's ideal for tourists seeking warm Mediterranean weather, stunning scenery, and a laid-back way of life. With the Sierra Helada Natural Park to the north and the Mediterranean Sea to the south, the town is surrounded by breathtaking natural beauty.

The nearest international airport to Albir is Alicante Airport, which is approximately 60 kilometers away. Visitors can easily access the town via rental cars, taxis, or public buses.

Albir's beaches are some of the best in the Costa Blanca region, with Racó de l'Albir Beach in l'Alfàs del Pi offering an exceptional view of the Mediterranean Sea. The beach is located next to the famous Paseo de las Estrellas and hosts an annual film festival. During the summer months, a bustling craft market adds vibrancy to the area, and visitors can enjoy refreshments at the various dining options available on this urban beach.

Albir has a variety of attractions for visitors, including a lively Sunday market, art galleries, and museums, such as the Museo al Aire Libre. The Sierra Helada mountain range provides a stunning natural backdrop to the town and separates it from its larger neighbor, Benidorm. The range is part of a natural park that's highly protected and spans over 5,600 hectares.

Albir is renowned for its food and wine culture, with a variety of restaurants and bars serving traditional Spanish cuisine and locally produced wines. Whether you're looking for a romantic dinner for two or a lively night out with friends, Albir has something to offer everyone.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja de l'Albir, about 400 m by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are CEIP Racó de l'Albir (about 1.1 km) and Costa Blanca International College (about 3 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centre de Salut, about 6 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Clínica Benidorm, about 8 km by road, around 13 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Consum, about 300 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 69 km by road, around 50 minutes by car.
