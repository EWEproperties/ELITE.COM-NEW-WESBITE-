Location

Calpe, a charming coastal town on the Mediterranean Sea, will captivate you with its undeniable charm and many attractions. Located in the province of Alicante in Spain, Calpe offers a delightful mix of natural beauty, historical monuments and lively attractions. Located about 75 kilometres from Alicante Airport, Calpe provides convenient access for travellers eager to discover its wonders.

Upon arrival, you will be amazed by the magnificent beaches, which have won prestigious Q' certificates for quality and the Blue Flag. Calpe boasts some of the most beautiful sandy shores in the region, including the iconic Playa de la Fossa and Playa de la Arenal-Bol. Immerse yourself in Mediterranean bliss, bask in the sun or enjoy the water sports and activities that are an integral part of Calpe's seaside landscape.

In addition to its picturesque beaches, Calpe is also home to historical monuments and cultural treasures. The distinctive Peñón de Ifach, a massive limestone rock formation, is the symbol of Calpe and offers breathtaking panoramic views. Hike to the top and admire the natural beauty that unfolds before your eyes. The old town with its charming narrow streets and historic architecture invites you to stroll and discover its rich heritage.

The lively fishing port of Calpe is a must-see. Watch the fishermen bring in their daily catch, visit the fish market and savour fresh seafood in the numerous restaurants along the harbour. To get to know the local culture, visit the Museum of Archaeology and Ethnography to delve into Calpe's fascinating history.

Nature lovers will find solace in the nature parks surrounding Calpe. Explore the Las Salinas nature park, a paradise for migratory birds such as flamingos and storks, or head to the Serra Gelada nature park to see its rugged cliffs and enchanting trails. For hiking enthusiasts, the Sierra de Bernia and Sierra de Oltà offer exciting routes and magnificent views.

Immerse yourself in the lively atmosphere of Calpe's local markets, where you can discover fresh products, handicrafts and traditions. Enjoy the local cuisine, which is famous for its seafood, rice dishes and spicy tapas.
