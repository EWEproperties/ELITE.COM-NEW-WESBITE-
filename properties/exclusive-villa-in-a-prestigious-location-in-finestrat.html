Location

Finestrat, a charming town located in the Costa Blanca region of Spain, offers a perfect blend of natural beauty, cultural attractions, and recreational activities. Situated just a short distance from the coastline, Finestrat boasts stunning beaches, a rich history, and easy access to the nearby airport.

One of the main highlights of Finestrat is its beautiful beaches. The town is conveniently located near several sandy shores, including the popular and picturesque Playa de Poniente and Playa de Levante in neighboring Benidorm. These beaches offer pristine turquoise waters, soft golden sands, and excellent facilities, making them ideal for sunbathing, swimming, and water sports enthusiasts.

Beyond the beaches, Finestrat has much to offer in terms of cultural and historical attractions. The historical center of the town is a delight to explore, with its narrow, winding streets, traditional Spanish architecture, and charming squares. Be sure to visit the iconic Church of San Bartolomé, a 14th-century church that stands as a testament to the town's rich heritage.

For those seeking adventure, a visit to the nearby Puig Campana mountain is a must. This impressive peak is a paradise for hikers and climbers, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding landscape. Embarking on a trek to the summit is a thrilling experience and provides an opportunity to connect with nature.

If you're a fan of theme parks and entertainment, you're in luck. Finestrat is conveniently located near Terra Mítica, one of Spain's largest theme parks. This exciting attraction offers a variety of thrilling rides, live shows, and themed areas inspired by ancient civilizations.

In terms of amenities, Finestrat has a wide range of shops, restaurants, and bars that cater to both locals and tourists. You can indulge in traditional Spanish cuisine, sample fresh seafood, or savor international dishes at the numerous dining establishments in the town.

Regarding transportation, Finestrat benefits from its proximity to Alicante-Elche Airport, which is approximately 45 kilometers away. This international airport provides excellent connectivity to major cities in Europe and beyond, making it convenient for travelers to reach Finestrat.

Frequently asked questions
