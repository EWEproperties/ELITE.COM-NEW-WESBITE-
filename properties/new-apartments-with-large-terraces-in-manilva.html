New apartments for sale in Manilva on the Costa del Sol, offering 1 bedroom, generous terraces and access to a gated residential complex with a swimming pool, padel court, outdoor gym and children's playground. Set in a quiet residential area close to the Mediterranean coast, the development is just a short drive from Puerto de la Duquesa, while Estepona and Sotogrande are also within easy reach.

The complex consists of 4 residential buildings with a total of 88 apartments. Each block has four floors and an underground parking level, with one dedicated parking space allocated to every apartment. The available layouts include ground-floor homes with private garden areas, first- and second-floor apartments with terraces, and penthouses with particularly spacious outdoor areas

According to the available plans, the properties offer total built areas from approximately 59.85 m² to 141.09 m², depending on the unit. Terrace sizes vary significantly between the different layouts, from approximately 20.63 m² up to 102.08 m², making outdoor living one of the strongest features of these apartments. Ground-floor homes include garden areas, while the penthouse layouts provide especially large terraces.

Each apartment includes 1 bedroom and 1 bathroom, together with a living area and kitchen. The layouts have been designed to make practical use of the available space while connecting the interiors naturally with the terraces and outdoor areas. Large openings shown in the plans and visualisations create a bright, contemporary feel and extend the living space towards the exterior.

The apartments are delivered turnkey with fully fitted bathrooms, built-in wardrobes and a fully equipped kitchen. The kitchen is integrated into the living area, creating an open and functional space suited to everyday living. Some elements, equipment and finishes shown in the illustrative kitchen images are optional and are not necessarily included in the standard specification, so the exact equipment should be confirmed for the selected apartment.

The communal areas have been designed to provide residents with several options for relaxation and outdoor activity. The development includes a landscaped outdoor swimming pool, a padel court, an outdoor gym and a children's playground, all integrated into the green areas surrounding the residential buildings. The combination of gardens, recreational facilities and low-rise architecture creates a comfortable residential environment focused on outdoor living.

Location

The apartments are situated in Manilva, Málaga province, on the western side of the Costa del Sol. The development occupies a quieter residential setting close to the coast, while still providing convenient access to beaches, marinas, restaurants and neighbouring coastal towns. The brochure describes the beaches as being a short drive away, with natural coastal landscapes and sandy areas nearby.

Puerto de la Duquesa is approximately 4 minutes away, providing access to its marina and local amenities. Estepona and Sotogrande are approximately 15 minutes away, while Puerto Banús is around 30 minutes and Marbella around 40 minutes. Tarifa can be reached in approximately 55 minutes.

For international travel, Gibraltar Airport is approximately 38 minutes away, while Málaga Airport is around 1 hour 20 minutes by car according to the development information. This location makes Manilva particularly practical for buyers who want to combine a quieter residential environment with access to the main destinations of the western Costa
