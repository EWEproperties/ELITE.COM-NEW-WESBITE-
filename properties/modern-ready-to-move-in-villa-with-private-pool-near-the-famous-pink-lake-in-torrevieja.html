Location

Torrevieja is one of the most recognised destinations on the Costa Blanca, appreciated for its exceptional Mediterranean climate, beautiful beaches, and comfortable year-round lifestyle. The city is located approximately 40 minutes from Alicante International Airport, providing quick and convenient access for both residents and holidaymakers visiting Spain.

Torrevieja is famous for its wide sandy beaches and picturesque promenades filled with restaurants, cafés, and shops. Popular nearby beaches include La Mata, Playa del Cura, and Los Locos Beach, perfect for relaxing, walking, and enjoying water sports. One of the city’s most iconic landmarks is the famous Pink Lagoon, which creates a unique microclimate considered one of the healthiest in Europe.

The city offers excellent infrastructure for everyday living, including shopping centres, international schools, private clinics, supermarkets, and a wide variety of restaurants. Nearby you will also find renowned golf courses, marinas, and recreational areas, making Torrevieja an ideal place both for holidays and permanent living.

This is the perfect location for anyone looking for a property in sunny Spain — whether as a holiday home, rental investment, or permanent residence in the Mediterranean lifestyle.
