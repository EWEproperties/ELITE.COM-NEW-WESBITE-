Elite World Estates presents modern investment apartments located in Santiago de la Ribera, the coastal district of the municipality of San Javier, in the Region of Murcia. Situated just 900 metres from the beaches of the Mar Menor, only a few minutes' walk from the promenade, marina, restaurants and all essential amenities, this development is an excellent choice for buyers seeking a property with outstanding investment potential.

The development offers contemporary apartments designed with comfort and functionality in mind. Each property features a bright open-plan living and dining area with a modern kitchen, three bedrooms, two elegant bathrooms, and spacious terraces where you can enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle all year round. The penthouses also benefit from private rooftop solariums, providing the perfect space to relax and unwind. Every apartment includes an outdoor parking space, with the option to purchase a storage room and an underground parking space.

Residents can enjoy beautifully landscaped communal areas with two swimming pools and green gardens, creating the perfect environment for holidays and relaxation. The boutique character of the development, combined with its contemporary architecture, offers an ideal setting for enjoying life on the Spanish coast.

One of the project's greatest advantages is its tourist rental licence, allowing owners to legally rent out their apartment on a short-term basis. Owners can either manage the rentals themselves or appoint a professional management company, while still enjoying the flexibility of using the property for their own holidays throughout the year.

The proximity to the beaches of the Mar Menor, sports facilities, renowned golf courses, shopping centres, and the international airports of Murcia and Alicante makes this development an attractive opportunity for both investors and those looking for their own holiday home in Spain.

Location

The development is located in Santiago de la Ribera, a charming seaside district of the municipality of San Javier, on the shores of the unique Mar Menor, Europe's largest saltwater lagoon. This picturesque destination is renowned for its authentic Spanish atmosphere, relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle and beautiful palm-lined promenade stretching along sandy beaches, making it the perfect setting for leisurely walks, cycling or enjoying spectacular sunsets.

Santiago de la Ribera has long been a favourite destination for both Spanish residents and international property owners. The area offers excellent infrastructure, with everything needed for comfortable everyday living just a short walk away. Residents can enjoy a wide selection of restaurants serving fresh seafood and traditional Mediterranean cuisine, charming tapas bars, cafés, supermarkets, pharmacies, banks and local markets offering fresh regional produce.

The nearby marina and extensive range of water sports make this an ideal destination for sailing, paddleboarding, kayaking and windsurfing enthusiasts. Several prestigious golf courses, cycling routes and recreational areas are also located nearby, making the area equally attractive for active lifestyles and those seeking peace and relaxation.

Another major advantage is the excellent transport connections. Historic Cartagena, the city of Murcia and both Murcia International and Alicante airports are all within easy driving distance, ensuring convenient travel throughout the year. Combining a pleasant Mediterranean climate, beautiful beaches, excellent gastronomy and comprehensive local amenities, Santiago de la Ribera is an outstanding location for both permanent living and a holiday home investment.
