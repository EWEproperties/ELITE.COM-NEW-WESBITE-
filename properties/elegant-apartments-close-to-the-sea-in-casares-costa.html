Elite World Estates presents a new residential development located in Casares Costa, on the Costa del Sol, surrounded by golf courses and Mediterranean nature, just a few minutes from the sea. It is an attractive option for those looking for a modern apartment in Spain, whether as a permanent residence, holiday home or investment property.

This low-density residential complex comprises 99 apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms, including ground-floor properties with private gardens and penthouses with spacious terraces and private solariums. Contemporary architecture, large windows and generous outdoor spaces provide plenty of natural light and allow residents to fully enjoy the Mediterranean climate.

One of the main advantages of the development is its extensive communal areas designed for relaxation, sport and everyday comfort. The location is particularly attractive for golf enthusiasts, with renowned golf courses nearby, including Casares Costa Golf and Finca Cortesín.

The apartments are offered with a high standard of finishes. The specifications include a fully fitted Italian-style kitchen equipped with Bosch appliances or similar, fitted wardrobes, ducted air conditioning, an aerothermal system for hot water, and high-quality double-glazed windows providing excellent thermal and acoustic insulation.

The development offers convenient access to some of the most popular destinations on the Costa del Sol. Estepona is approximately 10 minutes away, Sotogrande 21 minutes, Puerto Banús 27 minutes, Marbella 35 minutes, and Málaga Airport approximately 60 minutes by car. The prestigious Finca Cortesín resort is just a few minutes from the development.

This is an excellent choice for those looking to combine proximity to the sea, golf courses, modern living and the peaceful surroundings of Casares Costa, while enjoying easy access to Estepona, Marbella and the many attractions of the Costa del Sol.

Estimated completion: Q3 2029.
