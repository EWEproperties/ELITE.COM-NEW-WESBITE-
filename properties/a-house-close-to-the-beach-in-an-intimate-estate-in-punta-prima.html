Location

The popular area of Punta Prima in Spain is located on the border between Torrevieja and Orihuela Costa. Punta Prima is very popular with tourists all year round and has a large population of permanent residents of all European nationalities, which means that shops and services are open all year round, whatever the season.

To do your shopping, you will find a selection of many well-known supermarkets and mini markets, where you will find all your essentials. In the shopping center "Punta Marina" there is a supermarket, a pharmacy, clothing stores, a card shop, hairdressers, an electrical shop, vets, a tattoo parlor and many bars and restaurants.

Beaches

Punta Prima has two beaches and a small cove on its shores, La Playa Rocio del Mar, Cala Piteras and Punta Prima beach, the latter with a blue flag. A Blue Flag beach means the beach is safe, the water quality is good, lifeguards are present during high season, accessibility is good and toilets are available. The magnificent Punta Prima beach is 234 m long and 28 m wide. The beach is sandy with many rocky coves to explore.

There are also many followers of "Cala Piteras", where one of Punta Prima's emblematic establishments, "Restaurante Nautilus", is located.

Restaurants

The road that connects the central part of Punta Prima to the beach is about a 10-minute walk away. At the end of this road is the famous Nautilus restaurant. This restaurant specializes in Mediterranean and Spanish dishes. The food is beautifully presented and the location on Punta Prima beach is stunning. A great place to celebrate a special occasion.

RESTAURANTE PUNTA PRIMA on the southern Costa Blanca has been delighting locals and tourists for over 25 years with its exquisite approach to traditional Spanish and Western Mediterranean cuisine. It is a popular place because of its wonderful large terrace, which offers an impressive view of the main beach of Punta Prima and the Mediterranean Sea.

The central area of Punta Prima also boasts a huge variety of bars and restaurants to suit every taste, from McDonalds, fish and chips to tapas and Japanese cuisine. The traditional Chinese restaurant "Dinastia" is very popular and offers delicious Asian cuisine.

In the evening there is a lovely atmosphere and all the bars and restaurants are illuminated.

Golf

This area of the southern Costa Blanca is home to many championship and award-winning golf courses. The closest to Punta Prima, just 10 minutes by car, are Villamartin Golf, Las Ramblas Golf, Campoamor Golf and the famous Las Colinas Golf and Country Club. All have 18 holes and all have restaurants so you can enjoy a cold drink and a bite to eat after a round of golf.

A little further is Romero Golf, Vistabella Golf, La Finca Golf, La Marquesa Golf and the famous La Manga Golf Club. If you are an avid golfer or a novice, you are sure to find a golf course here that suits your skills.
