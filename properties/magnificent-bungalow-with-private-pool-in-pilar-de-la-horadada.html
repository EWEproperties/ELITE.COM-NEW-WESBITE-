Location

Pilar de la Horadada is a charming town located in the southern part of the province of Alicante, in the Valencian Community of Spain. The town is famous for its picturesque landscapes, fascinating cultural heritage and crystal-clear waters, making it an ideal destination for those seeking relaxation and tranquillity.

One of the main attractions of Pilar de la Horadada are its magnificent beaches. Playa de las Higuericas is a popular local beach that stretches for over 2.5 kilometres and offers golden sand, crystal clear water and many amenities such as beach bars, restaurants and showers. This beach is ideal for families with children as it has a playground and is supervised by lifeguards.

In addition to its beaches, Pilar de la Horadada offers many cultural and historical attractions that are worth visiting. Casa de la Cultura is a cultural centre that hosts a variety of exhibitions, workshops and events throughout the year. Iglesia de Nuestra Señora del Pilar is a magnificent church dating back to the 18th century and characterised by its magnificent Baroque style.

For those who love nature, Pilar de la Horadada offers many opportunities for hiking and cycling. The “Del Pilar al Mar” route is a popular promenade that stretches for 3 kilometres and is ideal for a leisurely walk or bike ride. There are also several nature reserves nearby, such as the Salinas de San Pedro del Pinatar Regional Park, which is home to many species of birds.

When it comes to food and drink, Pilar de la Horadada has a lot to offer. The town is famous for its delicious seafood dishes, such as paella and arroz a banda. There are many local bars and restaurants where you can sample these dishes, as well as many other Spanish specialities.
