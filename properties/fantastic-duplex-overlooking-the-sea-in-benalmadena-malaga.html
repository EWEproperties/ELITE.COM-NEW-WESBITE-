Location

Benalmádena is just 20 km from Málaga and 40 km from Marbela. This city is loved by Spanish tourists. The high quality of services means that guests from all over the world moor to the port of Puerto Marina located here, and the cosmopolitan atmosphere makes it hard to think about anything else than just blissful rest and great fun.

Benalmádena can be divided into three main parts. These will be: Benalmádena Pueblo, the old Andalusian part with characteristic white buildings, the modern and residential Arroyo de la Miel and the wonderful port. Each of them is unique and worth attention.

1.Benalmádena Pueblo

A restored old town (pueblo) with narrow streets and two squares (Plaza de España and Plaza Andalucia). On the first is the La Niña bronze fountain, which is the symbol of Benalmádena Pueblo

2.Benalmádena Costa, the newest part of the city, located along the coast and directly adjacent to the sea. A significant part of its buildings are tourist facilities (hotels, restaurants, discos and the beach).

3. Arroyo de la Miel (Honey Stream) originally a separate settlement, located between the other two districts. It is the main residential area and the most commercially active part of the city.

Benalmádena is especially recommended for families with children, because it abounds in a large number of attractions prepared also for the youngest. Most of the most interesting ones can be found below:

COLUMBA'S FAIRY-TALE CASTLE

Colomares Castle is not really a castle, but a monument to Columbus. Nevertheless, it is beautiful and is one of the major attractions in Benalmádena. It was designed and built by Esteban Marin Martin. Its construction lasted from 1987 to 1994. An interesting fact is that in the castle there is the smallest church in the world, entered in the Guinness Book of Records. Its area is only 1.96 m2.

STUP

A living monument to the enlightened mind, a Buddhist monument to peace and harmony in the world and a place to study, reflect and meditate. A very interesting place, also due to the fact that it is the largest Stupa in Europe. Few people also know that it was designed by the Polish architect Wojciech Kossowski. An additional attraction is the beautiful panorama stretching from the hill.

BENALMADEN PORT

Puerto Marina is a good place for a family walk or dinner with wine. You can admire moored ships and interesting architecture here. The port is full of bars, shops and cafes. This place was twice hailed as the most beautiful port in the world. It has also been called the Andalusian Venice', due to its narrow streets, narrow canals and bridges. One of the attractions you can count on here is the aquarium. In Sea Life, you will find over 2,000 species of sea creatures, including: turtles, sharks and stingrays.

TIVOLI WORLD

An ideal option to spend time for both children and adults. Among the fountains and gardens, you can enjoy not only more than 30 attractions for the whole family, but also a rich gastronomic offer. It's an old-fashioned amusement park with classic attractions like Ferris Wheels, Bumper Cars and the Terror Mansion, as well as new offerings for the more adventurous. The tower, from which you can experience a stunning fall from a height of 60 meters, is undoubtedly one of the most spectacular attractions in the park.

TEREFERICO BENALMADENA - QUEUE

Teleférico Benalmádena is a cable car that will give you a bird's eye view of the whole city. The three-kilometer route leads to the peak of Calamorro in the Sierra de Mijas mountain range. Upon arrival, we can admire the beautiful panorama of the Costa del Sol, nearby Gibraltar, and in good weather, also the coast of Africa. In addition to the magnificent landscapes at the top of Calomorro, there are several other optional attractions waiting for us, such as: bird of prey shows, Andalusian horse training shows, trekking trails and bicycle trails.

BEACHES

Being in this beautiful city, it is certainly worth relaxing on one of the beaches of Benalmádena, and the choice is quite large: Arroyo Hondo, Carvajal, Bonita, Bil Bil, La Morera, Arroyo de la Miel, Benalnatura, Fuente de la Salud, La Perla, Las Viborillas , Playa de las Yucas, Torrebermeja, Santa Ana, Tajo de la Soga, Torremuelle, Torrequebrada y Torrevigía are options that are definitely worth taking when planning a stay in this place.
