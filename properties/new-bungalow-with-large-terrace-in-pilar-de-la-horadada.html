Location

Pilar de la Horadada is located in the southernmost corner of the Vega Baja region in the province of Alicante, on the border with Murcia. Its coastline stretches for over 4 kilometres and offers a mix of wide sandy beaches, rocky shores and secluded coves with golden sand. The calm, shallow waters make it a safe place for families with young children, while the crystal-clear sea encourages diving enthusiasts to explore the marine life. Many of the beaches have been awarded the prestigious European Union Blue Flag, guaranteeing both quality and cleanliness.

Nature lovers will be delighted by the inland landscapes, including the Sierra de Escalona and the famous Rio Seco trails. These marked trails, 4 km and 10 km long, lead from the nature reserve in Pilar de Campoverde to Mil Palmeras beach.

Golfers can enjoy the 18-hole golf course at Lo Romero, which offers challenges and enjoyment for both experienced players and beginners.

The local cuisine also makes this area unique. Traditional dishes such as meat stews, garlic rabbit, gachas migas (garlic and flour fritters), as well as rich rice dishes, soups and desserts such as milhojas (meringue pastry) and cordiales (almond sweets) reflect the authentic flavours of Spanish cuisine.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de las Villas, about 4 km by road, around 7 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are IES Thiar (about 400 m) and CEIP Mediterráneo (about 1.3 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is the local public health centre (Centro de Salud), about 1.1 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 11 km by road, around 12 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Aldi, about 1.3 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 38 km by road, around 30 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 83 km, around 60 minutes by car.
