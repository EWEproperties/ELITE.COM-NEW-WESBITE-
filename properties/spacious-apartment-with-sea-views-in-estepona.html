Introducing the next phase of a new project in Estepona, which consists of modern apartments and penthouses with large terraces and spectacular views of the Mediterranean Sea. All of them are located a short distance from the beach (15 minutes walk) and the most exclusive golf courses in the area. Furthermore, it is close to exclusive shopping areas and close to Puerto Banús - 25 minutes by car and 20 minutes from Marbella city center. Malaga and Gibraltar airports are approximately 45-50 minutes away by car.

This new project will feature 28 modern apartments and 7 penthouses with 2 to 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Apartments and penthouses ranging in size from 89.40 to 137.43 m2 have an open-plan kitchen, living room and terraces and are distributed over three buildings with various amenities. There are three different types of apartments to choose from (type 1, type 2, type 3), which differ in location in the building, number of square meters and size of terraces. 7 penthouses (type 4) also have a private swimming pool with an area of 17 to 19 square meters.

A characteristic feature of the project is the use of special sliding doors that can be opened particularly wide and provide maximum light flow. Your living room is integrated with the terrace: Technal sliding doors consist of 4 panels, 3 of which can be opened completely to the sides, giving you an opening of 75%.

All properties have 2 parking spaces in the underground garage and a private storage room for bicycles and other items.

In your new home you can look at the sea from the south and the mountains from the north. You'll also benefit from amenities such as swimming pools, a gym and spa on-site, as well as a great location: it won't take long to get from your apartment or penthouse to the beach.

Planned construction completion date: end of 2024.

Location

Estepona, one of the most attractive corners of the Costa del Sol, convinces with its modernity, good infrastructure and typical Andalusia charm. A city with maritime traditions, which, due to the quality of life and a wide range of leisure activities, has become one of the most popular places on the Costa del Sol. The golf clubs located here are proud to be called Costa del Golf, and the spectacular natural landscapes allow you to lead a healthy lifestyle, including practicing numerous outdoor sports.

Estepona is conveniently located a short distance from Malaga Airport, making it easily accessible to travelers. The airport is approximately 80 kilometers away, ensuring a smooth and comfortable journey for visitors.

There is no doubt that Estepona is today a benchmark for quality of life, modern tourism and recreation facilities, city care and the provision of public services, thanks to the investment incentives initiated by the Estepona City Council in 2011 to implement a sustainable city model with the well-being of the citizen as its central axis and respect for the environment.

The involvement in the Estepona project, the Costa del Sol' Garden, which has achieved such good socio-economic results, has made it possible to regenerate the city, provide it with a unique tourist attraction and support the economic recovery and revitalization of the commercial and commercial tourist life of this municipality in Malaga. The results are clear: Estepona is among the municipalities that lead in job creation in Andalusia and public investment in the province of Malaga.

The city boasts a collection of breathtaking beaches that will delight beach lovers and sun seekers alike. Some of the area's best beaches include Playa del Cristo, a sheltered cove with calm waters and a relaxing atmosphere, and Playa de la Rada, a vast stretch of golden sand offering a wide range of water sports.

The nearby Sierra Bermeja mountain range provides excellent hiking opportunities, allowing you to immerse yourself in stunning natural landscapes and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the coast. Golf enthusiasts will delight in the numerous world-class golf courses in the area where they can play against the backdrop of lush greenery and azure skies.
