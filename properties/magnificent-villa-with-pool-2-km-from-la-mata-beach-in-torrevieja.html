Location

The popular town of Torrevieja is located about 40 minutes ( 45 km ) from one of the region's largest cities, Alicante. The town boasts an extraordinary, healthy microclimate, whose healthy properties derive, among other things, from the surrounding large salt deposits located in two salt lagoons located right in the middle of the town.

It is an amazing adventure to see these two lakes, pink and green, which are surrounded by natural parks with many species of birds including flamingos. Laguna de Torrevieja owes its pink colour to the high salinity of the water, the crustaceans that live here and the algae that produce carotenoids. The view is very impressive and it is no wonder that it is one of the biggest tourist attractions of the place.

The second lagoon (La Laguna de la Mata) is green in colour. The park's interpretive centre is located here. Here we can see unusual vegetation, i.e. Aleppo pine, orchid, thyme, carob, zatrwiana and many others.

Also notable here are the vineyards where the local wine de la Mata is produced.

One of the most popular beaches is Playa de la Mata, which is approximately 2.5 km long. This beautiful sandy beach has a Blue Flag and is accessible to people with disabilities. Those who like to be active on the beach can play on one of the several volleyball courts.

On the north side of the town, there are two beaches Playa de los Locos and Playa del Cura. Both are equipped with full infrastructure and Blue Flag designated.

It is worth arranging a walk along the elegant promenade (Vista Alegre). Here you can get a great feel for the atmosphere of the city and relax while admiring the beautiful views. Along the promenade you will find a huge number of bars, restaurants, cafés, ice cream parlours and shops selling souvenirs and handicrafts. For those seeking excitement, Torrevieja offers the Aquapark ‘Aquopolis’ and an amusement park close to the promenade. During the summer season, the open-air summer discos are also very popular, with thousands of people enjoying themselves.
