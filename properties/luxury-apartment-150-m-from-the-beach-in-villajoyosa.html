Location

Villajoyosa is a small, very intimate town located in the northern part of the Costa Blanca. This colorful town is located between the popular town of Benidorm and El Campello, approximately 30 minutes' drive from Alicante Airport.

According to urban legend, the buildings were painted in bright colors to help fishermen find their way home. Today, they attract crowds of tourists and inspire many photographers and artists.

In recent years, Villajoyosa has become a desirable place for people looking for a second home in a quiet area. It has a lot to offer, from its wide sandy beach to the narrow and colorful streets of the historic center.

Villajoyosa has been associated with chocolate since the 19th century. The popular Spanish chocolate factory, Valor, is located here. The Valor, Pérez, and Clavileño museums are a must-see, where visitors can learn everything about the chocolate production process and its history in the area. Tours, led by knowledgeable guides, are usually combined with chocolate tasting.

Various cultural events take place in Villajoyosa during the holiday season. It is worth visiting the town in July, when the famous festival of Moors and Christians takes place. The main attraction is the reenactment of the 1538 battle, as well as concerts, performances, and shows.
