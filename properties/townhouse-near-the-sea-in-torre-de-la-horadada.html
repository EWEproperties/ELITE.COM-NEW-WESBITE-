Location

Torre de la Horadada is a seaside district located in the municipality of Pilar de la Horadada, in the south of the province of Alicante. The resort is located in the south-eastern part of Spain and borders Murcia (about 15 minutes away). Alicante, on the other hand, is about a 40-minute drive away.

Pilar de la Horadada has everything you need to enjoy the sea, pine forests and the Spanish sun.

The coastline of Pilar de la Hordada is over 4 kilometers long along a very diverse scenery. We will meet there wide beaches with fine white sand, small stone wall ledges suspended above the water and small and intimate, hidden coves with golden sand.

The sea is usually calm here, the beaches are safe, there are no steep slopes, which allows even the smallest children to swim safely. In addition, the clear water and great visibility invite divers to observe the beauty of aquatic life.

The cleanliness of the beaches in Pilar de la Hordada has been awarded the Blue Flag of the European Union, which guarantees a healthy environment.

Inland, you can make various interesting excursions near the cities of Sierra and Escalona. There are two marked hiking trails along the Rio Seco riverbed, 19 kilometers long, definitely worth a visit. One of the routes is 4 kilometers long, while the other is 10 kilometers long, stretching from the natural terrain of Pilar de Campoverde to the mouth of Mil Palmeras beach.

Not far from the town of Pilar de la Horadada, there is also an 18-hole golf course in Lo Romero. Both professionals in this field and beginners will find themselves well there.

Gastronomy has a privileged place in the culture of this beautiful city. Typical Pilar de la Hordada dishes include potatoes with meat stew, meatballs, rabbit in garlic and Gachas Migas (a floury garlic pancake) as well as great rice dishes and soups. Sweets and cakes also deserve a mention here, confectionery products such as meringue cake called ''milhojas'' or almond sweets called ''cordiales'' are the most popular.
