Explore with us this elegant new project located in a quiet area in Roldán in the Murcia region of Spain. This home offers a unique combination of luxury, style and affordability, making it an exceptional choice for those looking for a new home in the sun.

Phase 1 of this project is now complete. The final completion will include four phases, three for residential space and one for shops.

Phase 2 includes a diverse selection of detached 2 or 3 bedroom villas, each with a private swimming pool equipped with a built-in seat (4.80 × 2.70 metres), as well as pre-installation of a heat pump and solar shower.

These stunning villas also offer private gardens, parking and solariums. Located on the outskirts of Roldan in the charming village of Los Cachimanes, often referred to as the Campo de Cartagena, the development enjoys a sun bathed atmosphere amidst orchards and is close to numerous amenities and golf courses. The highly recommended Mar Menor beaches are just 19 km away, while Murcia airport is 18 km away and Alicante airport is approximately 103 km away.

These 123 m2 villas with 3 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, are designed with an open plan living/dining/kitchen area. There are 2 bedrooms with 2 bathrooms on the ground floor and a master bedroom with en-suite bathroom on the ground floor. Outside on the plot of 200 to 224 m2 there is a veranda of 10 m2, a swimming pool of 13 m2, a solarium of 37 m2 and a garden with top quality grey non-slip porcelain stoneware combined with artificial grass in the pedestrian entrance area.

The kitchen is fully equipped with oven, microwave, fridge, integrated dishwasher, extractor fan and convenient work island. The bedrooms are fitted with padded wardrobes with drawers and the bathrooms are fully equipped with suspended toilets, shower screens, dressing tables, mirrors and lights. Additional features include LED lighting both indoors and outdoors, a summer solarium kitchen with sink and fridge outlet, and private parking on the plot.

The development will offer a range of amenities including a dog park, volleyball court, padel court, bowling alley, gym, children's parks, green spaces, barbecue areas and a cycle path connecting to Roldan town centre.

Planned completion date: December 2024 and February 2025, depending on the model

Location

Roldán is a charming village in the Murcia region of Spain, known for its peaceful rural atmosphere and beautiful natural surroundings. Located close to the Mar Menor, Roldán offers easy access to beautiful beaches and golf courses. There are several amenities in the village, including shops, restaurants, bars and supermarkets. For sports enthusiasts, there are numerous facilities such as sports complexes and hiking trails. Roldán combines the best of peaceful rural living with easy access to urban amenities, making it an ideal location for both permanent residents and holidaymakers.
