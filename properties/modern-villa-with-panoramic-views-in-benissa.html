This is a truly outstanding opportunity for buyers seeking a premium contemporary villa in Benissa. Location

The property is situated in the prestigious Montemar area of Benissa, one of the most exclusive residential locations on the northern Costa Blanca. This peaceful, green neighbourhood is renowned for its spectacular Mediterranean Sea views, low-density development, and close proximity to some of the region's most beautiful beaches and coves. The location offers the perfect balance of privacy and convenient access to all services and amenities required for comfortable year-round living.

Within just a few minutes, residents can reach picturesque beaches and coves such as Cala Baladrar, Cala Advocat, and Playa de La Fustera. The charming towns of Moraira and Calpe are also nearby, offering a wide selection of restaurants, cafés, boutiques, marinas, and leisure facilities. Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to prestigious golf courses, scenic hiking and cycling routes, and a variety of water sports clubs.

Alicante International Airport is approximately one hour away by car, while Valencia Airport can be reached in around one hour and twenty minutes. Excellent access to the AP-7 motorway provides convenient connections to the main destinations along the Costa Blanca, making this property an ideal choice both for permanent living and as a luxurious Mediterranean holiday home.
