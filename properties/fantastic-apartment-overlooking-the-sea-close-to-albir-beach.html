The main advantage of the investment are spacious ground-floor gardens and extensive terraces with an area of 20 to over 130 m2, from which there are stunning views of the sea and the Sierra Helada Natural Park.

The properties consist of an open living and kitchen area, which is a spacious and comfortable space for relaxation and entertainment. You can choose from apartments with one, two, three or four bedrooms. Each of them is very cozy, spacious and designed with the comfort and relaxation of its residents in mind. The bathrooms are elegant and modern, with high-quality finishes and fittings.

Other facilities include a multi-purpose lounge with a gourmet room and wine cellar, a cinema, sailing equipment storage and air-conditioned waste disposal rooms. Concierge service and parcel collection are additional advantages of the investment.

Location

Albir is a popular seaside town in Alicante, Spain, ideal for travelers looking for warm Mediterranean weather, stunning scenery and a laid-back lifestyle. With the Sierra Helada Natural Park to the north and the Mediterranean Sea to the south, the city is surrounded by breathtaking natural beauty.

The nearest international airport to Albir is Alicante Airport, approximately 60 kilometers away. Visitors can easily get to the city by rental cars, taxis or public buses.

Albir's beaches are among the best in the Costa Blanca, and Racó de l'Albir Beach in l'Alfàs del Pi offers a unique view of the Mediterranean Sea. The beach is located next to the famous Paseo de las Estrellas and hosts an annual film festival. During the summer months, a lively artisanal market brings the area to life, and guests can enjoy refreshments at the various dining options available on this urban beach.

Albir has many attractions for visitors, including a lively Sunday market, art galleries and museums such as the Museo al Aire Libre. The Sierra Helada mountain range provides the city with a stunning natural backdrop and separates it from its larger neighbour, Benidorm. The range is part of a nature park that is heavily protected and covers over 5,600 hectares.

Albir is famous for its food and wine culture, with many restaurants and bars serving traditional Spanish cuisine and local wines. Whether you're looking for a romantic dinner for two or a lively evening with friends, Albir has something for everyone.
