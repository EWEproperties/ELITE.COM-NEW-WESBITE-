Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional contemporary villa located in one of the most desirable areas of Jávea on the northern Costa Blanca. This elegant residence has been designed for those seeking a luxury home in Spain that combines contemporary architecture, outstanding craftsmanship and a peaceful Mediterranean setting. Its prime location provides quick access to Arenal Beach, international schools, restaurants, shops and all the amenities required for comfortable year-round living.

The property offers approximately 237 m² of built area and is set on a plot of approximately 1,000 m². Its contemporary architectural design, enhanced by expansive floor-to-ceiling windows, blends seamlessly with the Mediterranean landscape. Panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea, the majestic Montgó Mountain and the iconic Cabo de San Antonio lighthouse can be enjoyed from the terraces and several interior spaces. Every detail has been carefully designed to maximise comfort, privacy and natural light throughout the home.

The villa features four spacious bedrooms, each with its own elegant en-suite bathroom, ensuring maximum comfort and privacy for both residents and guests. The heart of the home is an impressive open-plan living area with a spacious lounge, dining room and a designer kitchen equipped with premium BOSCH appliances. Large panoramic windows create a seamless connection between the interior and the generous outdoor terraces, perfectly embracing the Mediterranean lifestyle.

Outside, an elegant infinity pool is surrounded by beautifully landscaped Mediterranean gardens. Expansive terraces provide the ideal setting for entertaining family and friends, relaxing in the sunshine or enjoying spectacular sunsets overlooking the coastline. The property also includes a spacious double garage and a practical utility room, adding convenience and functionality to everyday living.

Villa Aurea will be completed to the exceptional quality standards for which Miralbo is renowned and delivered fully finished on a turnkey basis, ready to move into from day one. It represents an outstanding opportunity as a permanent residence, an exclusive holiday home or a prestigious investment in one of Spain's most sought-after luxury property markets.

For many years, Jávea has been regarded as one of the finest places to live on the Costa Blanca. Crystal-clear beaches, picturesque coves, marinas, renowned golf courses and an outstanding selection of restaurants and leisure facilities make it an ideal destination for those seeking a relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle as well as investors looking for long-term value appreciation. Villa Aurea perfectly embodies this exclusive way of life, offering luxury, privacy and timeless architecture in one of the region's most desirable locations.

Location

The development is situated in one of the most prestigious areas of Jávea, one of the most exclusive and picturesque resorts on the northern Costa Blanca. This exceptional destination perfectly combines Mediterranean charm, spectacular natural scenery and excellent infrastructure, offering ideal conditions for both year-round living and unforgettable holidays. Its peaceful surroundings and close proximity to Arenal Beach provide privacy while ensuring convenient access to all everyday amenities.

Just a few minutes' drive away lies the beautiful sandy Playa del Arenal, with its lively promenade lined with restaurants, cafés, boutiques and Mediterranean dining venues. Nearby are the stunning coves of Granadella and Portitxol, widely recognised as two of the most beautiful locations for swimming, snorkelling and diving on the Costa Blanca. Outdoor enthusiasts will also appreciate the numerous hiking and cycling trails through the Montgó Natural Park and along the spectacular coastline with its dramatic cliffs and breathtaking viewpoints.

Jávea offers all the services required for comfortable year-round living. Supermarkets, shopping centres, medical facilities, international schools, sports clubs, marinas and prestigious golf courses are all within easy reach. Excellent road connections provide convenient access to both Alicante and Valencia, while their international airports can be reached in approximately one hour by car.

The combination of spectacular beaches, crystal-clear Mediterranean waters, an exceptional range of leisure activities and an outstanding quality of life has made Jávea one of the most desirable destinations on the Spanish coast. It is the perfect choice for those looking for a luxury residence, an exclusive holiday home or a secure investment on the Costa Blanca.
