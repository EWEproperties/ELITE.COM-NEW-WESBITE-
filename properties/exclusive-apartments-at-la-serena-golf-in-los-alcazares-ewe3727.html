Location

Los Alcázares is a unique seaside town located on the sunny Costa Cálida, in the Murcia region. The town delights with its Mediterranean climate, tranquil atmosphere, and direct access to the Mar Menor—the largest saltwater lagoon in Europe, renowned for its warm, shallow waters and favorable year-round microclimate.

Thanks to its excellent location, Los Alcázares offers quick access to Cartagena, Murcia, and Alicante, as well as convenient connections to the airports and the region's main transport routes. It is an ideal place for both everyday life and a holiday under the Spanish sun.

The town is renowned for its wide, sandy beaches and picturesque promenade along the calm waters of the Mar Menor. Beaches such as Playa de Los Narejos and Playa de Las Salinas attract lovers of relaxation, water sports, and family vacations. Excellent conditions for sailing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and windsurfing make the area popular year-round.

Los Alcázares also offers a wide range of dining and recreational opportunities. Atmospheric restaurants, seaside cafés, and traditional Spanish chiringuitos create a unique atmosphere conducive to a relaxed, Mediterranean lifestyle. Local restaurants offer fresh seafood, tapas, and regional Spanish specialties.

The city boasts a well-developed infrastructure, including shops, sports centers, marinas, schools, and numerous recreational areas. Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of renowned golf courses such as La Serena Golf, Roda Golf, and Mar Menor Golf Resort.

Los Alcázares is a place that perfectly combines seaside relaxation, an active lifestyle, and the comfort of everyday life, creating excellent conditions for both investment and residence on the Spanish coast.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Punta Brava, about 5 km by road, around 8 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Grupo Escolar Los Alcázares (about 3 km) and I.E.S. ANTONIO MENÁRGUEZ COSTA (about 4 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Los Alcázares, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Centro Sociosanitario 'Cristo de los Mineros', about 13 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Dialprix, about 2.9 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 42 km by road, around 30 minutes by car.
