Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Cala de lo Ferri, about 5 km by road, around 10 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio Público Romualdo Ballester (about 2.4 km) and Colegio Público Amanecer (about 3 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Patricio Pérez, about 6 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 1.9 km by road.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Mercadona, about 100 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 54 km by road, around 40 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 69 km, around 50 minutes by car.
