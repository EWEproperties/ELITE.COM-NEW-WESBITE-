Elite World Estates proudly presents a brand-new residential development featuring luxury apartments located just 100 metres from the sandy beach in one of the most prestigious coastal destinations on the southern Costa Blanca – Torre de la Horadada. This exceptional development is the perfect choice for buyers seeking a property that combines Mediterranean living with outstanding investment potential.

The development is currently available during the pre-sale phase, offering buyers the opportunity to secure the best prices and choose from the finest apartments before construction is completed. It is the ideal time for investors looking to maximise the future value of their investment.

This exclusive boutique complex consists of 48 modern two-bedroom, two-bathroom apartments, designed to the highest contemporary standards. Buyers can choose from spacious ground-floor apartments with private gardens, stylish residences with large terraces, or luxurious penthouses featuring private rooftop solariums complete with a jacuzzi, pergola, outdoor summer kitchen and relaxation area. Selected properties also enjoy beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea.

All apartments are delivered fully furnished and ready to move into or rent out immediately. The purchase price includes high-quality furniture, home décor, kitchen appliances, air conditioning, underfloor heating in the bathrooms, a utility room, an underground parking space and a private storage room.

Residents will enjoy a wide range of premium resort-style facilities within this secure gated community. The development offers beautifully landscaped Mediterranean gardens, outdoor swimming pools including a heated pool, a modern spa and a fully equipped fitness centre, creating the perfect environment for year-round relaxation and wellbeing.

One of the project's greatest advantages is its tourist-oriented concept. Every apartment comes with a tourist rental licence, allowing owners to legally rent the property on a short-term basis. In addition, the developer offers a professional rental management service, enabling owners to generate attractive rental income with minimal involvement.

Located in the charming seaside town of Torre de la Horadada, the development offers direct access to beautiful sandy beaches as well as a wide selection of restaurants, cafés, a marina, walking and cycling paths, and renowned golf courses. Excellent transport connections to both Alicante and Murcia airports make this an ideal destination for holidays, permanent living and property investment alike.

Location

Torre de la Horadada is one of the most prestigious and fastest-growing coastal destinations on the southern Costa Blanca. Situated within the municipality of Pilar de la Horadada, close to the Murcia border, it has become increasingly popular among both holidaymakers and property investors seeking strong capital appreciation and excellent rental potential.

The area is renowned for its wide Blue Flag beaches, crystal-clear waters and Mediterranean climate with more than 300 days of sunshine each year. These outstanding conditions extend the tourist season well beyond the summer months, creating consistently high demand for holiday rentals throughout the year.

Residents benefit from an excellent range of amenities, including restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, a marina, walking and cycling routes, and all essential services required for comfortable year-round living. Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of the prestigious Lo Romero Golf Course, located just a few minutes away.

The development also enjoys excellent transport links. Murcia International Airport is approximately 30 minutes away, while Alicante International Airport can be reached in around 45 minutes. The nearby AP-7 motorway provides quick and convenient access to Orihuela Costa, Torrevieja, Cartagena and Alicante.

Whether you are looking for a stylish holiday home by the sea or a high-performing investment property with excellent rental potential and long-term capital growth, Torre de la Horadada is an outstanding choice.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de las Villas, about 100 m by road.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Martín Artigot, about 2.8 km.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 15 km, around 17 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is the local public health centre (Centro de Salud), about 3 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitario Los Arcos del Mar Menor, about 13 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Manper, about 2.7 km away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 40 km by road, around 30 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 82 km, around 60 minutes by car.
