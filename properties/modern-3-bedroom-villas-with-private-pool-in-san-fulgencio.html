Elite World Estates presents an exclusive boutique development of modern detached villas located in the town of San Fulgencio on the southern Costa Blanca. This is an excellent opportunity for those looking for a contemporary home in Spain that combines functional architecture, high-quality finishes, and a convenient location close to beaches, golf courses, and everyday amenities.

The development consists of just seven exclusive villas, each offering 3 bedrooms and either 2 or 3 bathrooms. Every home has been carefully designed to provide maximum comfort, featuring bright open-plan living areas, large floor-to-ceiling windows, and a seamless connection between the living room, terrace, and private garden, creating a spacious and light-filled living environment.

Each villa includes a private landscaped garden with a mosaic-finished swimming pool, a spacious terrace, and the option to add a private rooftop solarium. The homes offer built areas ranging from approximately 116 to 152 m², while plot sizes start from around 180 m², providing plenty of outdoor space to enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle all year round.

The villas are finished to a high standard and include aluminium double-glazed windows, electric shutters, LED lighting throughout, a video intercom system, and pre-installation for ducted air conditioning. The modern kitchen comes fully fitted with integrated appliances, including an oven, ceramic hob, extractor hood, fridge-freezer, and dishwasher.

The development is situated in a quiet residential area of San Fulgencio, within walking distance of supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, pharmacies, and other everyday services. The sandy beaches of La Marina are just a few minutes away by car, while several renowned golf courses can also be found nearby. Alicante International Airport is approximately a 30-minute drive away.

This development is an excellent choice for buyers looking for a modern, energy-efficient villa with a private pool at an attractive price, whether as a holiday home, a permanent residence, or an investment property with strong long-term potential.

Estimated completion date: September / December 2026

Location

The development is located in a peaceful residential area of San Fulgencio on the southern Costa Blanca, offering comfortable everyday living with easy access to the region's main attractions. Supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, pharmacies, and other essential services are all within walking distance, making daily life convenient without the need for long journeys.

The beautiful sandy beaches of La Marina and Guardamar del Segura are located approximately 4 to 7 km away. Nature lovers will appreciate the nearby natural parks, walking and cycling routes, while golf enthusiasts can enjoy several prestigious golf courses, including La Marquesa Golf in Ciudad Quesada, just around 6 km from the development.

The location also offers excellent transport connections throughout the Costa Blanca. Torrevieja can be reached in approximately 20 minutes by car, while Alicante International Airport is around a 30-minute drive away. Easy access to the N-332 coastal road and the AP-7 motorway allows convenient travel along the Mediterranean coastline.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja de les Pesqueres - el Rebollo, about 6 km by road, around 12 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio de Educación Infantil y Primaria José María Manresa, about 3 km, around 5 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 11 km, around 13 minutes by car.
International school: Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen, about 10 km, around 16 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is the local public health centre (Centro de Salud), about 1.2 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital del Vinalopó, about 16 km by road, around 19 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is SuperValu, about 1.1 km away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 34 km by road, around 25 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 76 km, around 55 minutes by car.
