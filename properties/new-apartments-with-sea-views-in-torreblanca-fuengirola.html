Discover an exclusive collection of new apartments for sale in Torreblanca, Fuengirola, enjoying an elevated position with beautiful views towards the Mediterranean Sea. This boutique residential complex offers just 35 homes, combining contemporary architecture, spacious terraces and bright interiors with a peaceful setting close to the beaches and amenities of the Costa del Sol.

The development offers a selection of 1, 2 and 3-bedroom apartments, together with an exclusive penthouse. Open-plan layouts have been designed to maximise space and natural light, while large windows connect the living areas with generous sea-facing terraces. Elegant finishes, built-in wardrobes and air conditioning provide comfort throughout the year.

The kitchens are designed as part of the main living space and come equipped with premium appliances. Spacious terraces include both covered and open areas, creating comfortable spaces for outdoor dining, entertaining or simply relaxing while enjoying the Mediterranean surroundings.

Residents have access to attractive communal facilities including a swimming pool with sun deck, landscaped gardens and a fully equipped indoor gym. The gated residential setting has been designed to provide privacy and tranquillity while remaining well connected to the surrounding area. Private underground parking and storage are also included among the development's features.

Excellent location in Torreblanca, Fuengirola

The apartments are located in Torreblanca del Sol, a popular residential area of Fuengirola offering easy access to the coast and everyday services. Shops and essential amenities can be found nearby, while the beaches, promenade and restaurants of Fuengirola are within easy reach.

Los Boliches and the centre of Fuengirola are just a short drive away. Málaga-Costa del Sol Airport is approximately 19 minutes away, Málaga city centre around 28 minutes and Marbella approximately 36 minutes by car.

These new apartments with sea views for sale in Fuengirola are an attractive choice for buyers looking for a permanent residence, holiday home or property on the Costa del Sol, combining generous terraces, Mediterranean views, modern communal facilities and a convenient location close to the sea.
