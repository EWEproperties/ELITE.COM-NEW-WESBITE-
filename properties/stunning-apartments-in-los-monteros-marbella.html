Elite World Estates presents an exceptional residential development located in the prestigious Altos de Los Monteros area of Marbella – one of the most exclusive locations on the Costa del Sol. This is an outstanding opportunity for buyers seeking a modern apartment in Spain that combines premium finishes, spectacular Mediterranean Sea views, and a peaceful lifestyle surrounded by nature, just minutes from Marbella's city centre and beautiful beaches.

This exclusive residential complex offers elegant apartments and duplex penthouses with 2, 3 and 4 bedrooms. The spacious interiors have been designed according to the latest architectural trends, featuring large floor-to-ceiling windows that flood the living spaces with natural light while creating a seamless connection between the open-plan living areas and the generous terraces. Most properties enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea and the green hills surrounding Marbella.

The contemporary architecture blends perfectly with the natural landscape, while high-quality construction and energy-efficient solutions ensure maximum comfort throughout the year. Functional layouts, premium materials and carefully selected finishes have been designed to satisfy even the most demanding buyers.

Residents can enjoy an impressive range of resort-style amenities, including an outdoor swimming pool with a sun terrace, a heated indoor pool, a fully equipped fitness centre, a luxurious spa and wellness area, an elegant pool bar, modern coworking spaces, and beautifully landscaped Mediterranean gardens that create the perfect environment for relaxation all year round.

The location offers the perfect balance between tranquillity and easy access to everything the Costa del Sol has to offer. Marbella's sandy beaches, renowned restaurants, luxury boutiques, prestigious golf courses and the famous Puerto Banús marina are all approximately 10 minutes away. Excellent access to the AP-7 motorway also provides quick connections to Málaga International Airport and the rest of the Costa del Sol.

Whether you are looking for a luxurious holiday home, a comfortable permanent residence or a high-potential investment property, this development represents an outstanding opportunity. Its prestigious location, contemporary architecture, panoramic sea views and exceptional resort facilities make it one of the most attractive residential developments on the Costa del Sol.

Location

The development is situated in the prestigious residential area of Altos de Los Monteros, in the eastern part of Marbella, one of the most sought-after and exclusive destinations on the Costa del Sol. This privileged setting perfectly combines privacy, tranquillity and natural surroundings with easy access to Marbella's vibrant lifestyle.

Thanks to its elevated position, residents can enjoy spectacular panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea, the Costa del Sol coastline and the surrounding mountains. The area is renowned for its elegant residential developments, luxury villas and peaceful atmosphere, making it an ideal location for both permanent living and holiday escapes.

Within approximately 10 minutes, you can reach Marbella's beautiful sandy beaches, exclusive beach clubs, fine dining restaurants, luxury boutiques and all essential daily services. The surrounding area also offers several prestigious golf courses, international schools, private healthcare facilities and shopping centres.

Excellent access to the AP-7 motorway allows quick travel to Marbella's city centre, the exclusive Puerto Banús marina, the charming Old Town and Málaga International Airport, which is approximately a 40-minute drive away. It is a location that perfectly combines the Mediterranean lifestyle, modern comfort and outstanding investment potential in one of Southern Spain's most desirable destinations.
