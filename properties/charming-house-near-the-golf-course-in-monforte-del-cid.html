Location

Monforte del Cid is a municipality well known and visited throughout the province for its grapes, golf courses and pilgrimages to the sanctuary of San Pascual, as well as for the anise liqueur from which the popular drink ‘paloma’ is made. Located on the plains of the middle course of the Vinalopó river, it is a town with a typically Arab profile with a labyrinth of concentric streets surrounding the old mosque, now a parish church.

Monforte del Cid owes its name to the Sierra del Cid mountain range, where legend connects this geographical feature with the figure of a medieval knight. It is a municipality in the Valencian Community in Spain. It is located in the interior of the province of Alicante, in the Medio Vinalopó region.

Monforte del Cid in Alicante is the ideal city for those who like to play golf in a natural environment with a mild climate, as well as for those who are looking for small places full of history, culture and heritage, and fantastic museums.

Monforte del Cid has two 18-hole, par 72 golf courses in close proximity to each other, making it the perfect place to practise your favourite sport.

Alenda Golf

The Alenda golf course was designed and built by Roland Favrat, respecting as much as possible the original orography and vegetation of the estate, avoiding aggressive earthworks during its construction. The course was completed in October 1999. It covers an area of 49 hectares and 6,257 metres in length and also has an excellent practice area.

As for leisure, in addition to excellent golf courses, in Monforte del Cid you will have the opportunity to join the wonderful Moors and Christians festivals held in December. Be sure to try the excellent grapes in sacks with the Vinalopó designation of origin, aniseed and liqueurs, also protected by the Regulatory Council, as well as pastas and cakes.

Frequently asked questions

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: IES Las Norias, about 800 m.
Private school: Colegio Santa María Magdalena, about 5 km, around 7 minutes by car.
International school: Magno International School, about 8 km, around 7 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centre de Salut d'Agost, about 12 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital General Universitari d'Elx, about 19 km by road, around 15 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Consum, about 400 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 32 km by road, around 25 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 98 km, around 75 minutes by car.
