Location

Casares Costa is a small stretch of coast forming part of the commune of Casares in the province of Malaga in Andalusia. The beachfront area is ideal for a family beach holiday, set in a fairly peaceful setting whilst still enjoying local amenities and within easy reach of Estepona, Sotogrande and Gibraltar.

Casares is divided into two urban areas: Casares Costa, which is a residential strip on the beach, a shopping center with bars and restaurants, and the old historic village of Casares, located a few kilometers in the mountains at an altitude of 430 meters above sea level.

Places to visit

Casares Costa is well situated for exploring the western end of the Costa del Sol, from Marbella and Estepona, through to Sotogrande, Gibraltar and the province of Cadiz. The area offers many facilities and amenities for those who want to take part in recreational activities such as water sports, jeep safaris, inland excursions, trekking or simply sit back and relax at the beach bar and taste sardines al espeto (sardines on spit). and a glass of wine or sangria.

Golf

Casares Costa is a great place for a golf holiday or for those who want to take part in a round or two while away. There are many world-class golf courses nearby, including La Duquesa Golf & Country Club, Estepona Golf, Valderrama (Sotogrande) and Alcaidesa Links Golf in San Roque.

Beaches

The golden sand beaches stretch from Estepona to Casares to Sotogrande and Gibraltar and are never too crowded. You can walk to the beach from anywhere in Casares Costa, and there are great chiringuitos (beach restaurants) along the way.

Climate

Casares Costa enjoys a subtropical Mediterranean climate, with hot summers and mild winters. The cooling breeze from the sea makes it possible to cope with the heat in summer, the average temperature is 32°C in summer and 15-16°C in the winter months.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Play Costa Natura (Nudist), about 7 km by road, around 10 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP San Luis de Sabinillas, about 5 km, around 8 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Diocesano Juan XXIII, about 7 km, around 10 minutes by car.
International school: Sotogrande International School, about 22 km, around 25 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud de Sabinillas, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital de Alta Resolución de Estepona, about 7 km by road, around 11 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Supermarket, about 5 km away.

How far is the airport?

Málaga Airport is about 95 km by road, around 70 minutes by car.
