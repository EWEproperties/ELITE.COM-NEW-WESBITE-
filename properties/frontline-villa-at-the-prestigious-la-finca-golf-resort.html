Elite World Estates presents an exceptional collection of modern villas located within the prestigious La Finca Golf Resort in Algorfa, on the southern Costa Blanca. This is the perfect opportunity for those seeking privacy, high-quality finishes, and a peaceful Mediterranean lifestyle surrounded by nature and a renowned golf course.

The villas have been designed in a contemporary and elegant style and are built on independent plots. Their single-level layout provides maximum comfort for everyday living, while large floor-to-ceiling windows seamlessly connect the interior spaces with the garden, terraces, and private swimming pool.

The properties offer 3 comfortable bedrooms and 2 or 3 bathrooms, depending on the selected model. The master bedroom features a private walk-in wardrobe and a stylish en-suite bathroom. The open-plan living area combines the lounge, dining room, and modern kitchen, creating a bright and functional space ideal for both everyday living and relaxation.

Each villa includes a private swimming pool, beautifully landscaped Mediterranean-style gardens, spacious terraces, and outdoor relaxation areas. Selected models also feature a private rooftop solarium and a large basement that can be customized as a gym, home cinema, additional bedrooms, or an entertainment area.

The villas are finished to a high standard using premium-quality materials and modern technologies, including ducted air conditioning, underfloor heating in the bathrooms, a smart home system, and elegant interior finishes throughout.

Located within La Finca Golf Resort, residents enjoy access to a full range of resort facilities, including restaurants, a hotel, spa, sports centre, tennis courts, padel courts, and everyday services. The centre of Algorfa is just a few minutes away, while the sandy beaches of Guardamar del Segura and Moncayo can be reached within approximately 15 minutes by car. Alicante International Airport is located around 56 km away.

This is a unique opportunity for those looking for a luxury villa in a peaceful setting, close to golf courses, nature, beautiful beaches, and all the amenities of the Costa Blanca.

Location – Algorfa and La Finca Golf Resort

Algorfa is a charming town located on the southern Costa Blanca, highly regarded for its peaceful atmosphere, excellent quality of life, and well-developed infrastructure. It is an ideal destination for both permanent living and property investment, whether as a holiday home or a rental investment.

The area's greatest attraction is the prestigious La Finca Golf Resort, one of the most renowned golf resorts on the Costa Blanca. In addition to its outstanding golf course, the resort offers a hotel, restaurants, wellness facilities, sports amenities, and a wide range of services available throughout the year.

Residents can enjoy a relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle away from the crowds of the larger coastal resorts while remaining close to all essential amenities. Supermarkets, schools, shopping centres, medical facilities, and recreational areas are all located nearby.

Another major advantage of the location is its proximity to the coast. The beautiful sandy beaches of Guardamar del Segura and La Mata are approximately 15 minutes away by car, while Alicante International Airport can be reached in around 35 minutes.

Thanks to the growing interest in real estate within the Vega Baja region, Algorfa also offers excellent investment potential. It is a location increasingly chosen by both international investors and people looking for a comfortable year-round residence in sunny Spain.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja del Camp, about 16 km by road, around 23 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: IES Benejúzar, about 10 km, around 16 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 8 km, around 11 minutes by car.
International school: Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen, about 8 km, around 12 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is the local public health centre (Consultorio Médico), about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario Vega Baja, about 11 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Alldays, about 4 km away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 57 km by road, around 45 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 65 km, around 50 minutes by car.
