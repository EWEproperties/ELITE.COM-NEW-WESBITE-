Elite World Estates proudly presents a modern residential development located in the prestigious New Golden Mile area of Estepona. This is an excellent opportunity for those looking for a stylish apartment on the Costa del Sol that combines high-quality construction, contemporary architecture, and a prime location close to beaches, golf courses, and all essential amenities.

This exclusive gated community comprises just 50 elegant apartments and penthouses with 2 or 3 bedrooms, distributed across four low-rise buildings. Designed with comfort and functionality in mind, the development features spacious interiors, practical layouts, and large floor-to-ceiling windows that flood the homes with natural Mediterranean light.

The development offers a variety of property types, including ground-floor apartments with private gardens, spacious apartments with generous terraces, as well as luxurious penthouses and duplex residences providing even more outdoor living space. Property sizes range from approximately 114 m² to 160 m², making this an ideal choice for families, holiday home buyers, and investors alike.

Residents can enjoy a carefully designed selection of communal facilities, including a large swimming pool with a sun terrace, a fully equipped fitness centre, an elegant residents' lounge, and secure bicycle storage. The gated community offers privacy, security, and a comfortable lifestyle throughout the year.

Situated in one of the most desirable locations on the Costa del Sol, the development is surrounded by sandy beaches, prestigious golf courses, restaurants, shops, and a wide range of leisure facilities. Excellent access to the A-7 motorway allows quick connections to Marbella, Puerto Banús, Sotogrande, and Málaga International Airport.

Thanks to its south and southwest orientation, the apartments enjoy abundant sunshine throughout most of the day. The penthouses and upper-floor residences boast spectacular panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea and the Costa del Sol coastline. Ground-floor and lower-level apartments overlook beautifully landscaped gardens, the communal swimming pool, and lush Mediterranean greenery, creating a peaceful and relaxing environment.

Location

Estepona – A Prestigious Destination on the Costa del Sol

Estepona is one of the most sought-after locations for purchasing property on the Costa del Sol. Combining authentic Andalusian charm with beautiful beaches and modern infrastructure, it is an ideal place for permanent living, a holiday home, or a profitable investment.

Its picturesque Old Town, colourful flower-filled streets, elegant marina, excellent restaurants, cafés, and boutique shops create a unique atmosphere enjoyed by both residents and visitors throughout the year. More than 20 kilometres of sandy beaches, a lively seafront promenade, and stylish beach clubs provide the perfect Mediterranean lifestyle.

Estepona is also renowned for its outstanding golf facilities. Numerous prestigious golf courses are located nearby, making it one of the top destinations in southern Spain for golf enthusiasts. At the same time, the nearby Sierra Bermeja mountains offer excellent opportunities for hiking, cycling, and outdoor activities.

One of Estepona's greatest advantages is its excellent connectivity. Puerto Banús is only around 15 minutes away, Marbella can be reached in approximately 20 minutes, and Málaga International Airport is less than an hour's drive. The area also offers international schools, shopping centres, private healthcare facilities, and all the services needed for comfortable year-round living.

Over the past few years, Estepona has experienced remarkable growth, attracting both international investors and buyers searching for a luxury home in Spain. Consistent property value appreciation, strong rental demand, and ongoing infrastructure improvements make purchasing an apartment in Estepona not only a lifestyle choice but also a secure long-term investment.
