Location

Cabo Roig is a beautiful urbanization located on the shores of the Mediterranean Sea in the south of the Costa Blanca, belonging to Orihuela Costa and being one of the most exclusive areas of the Costa Blanca. As its name suggests, Cabo Roig is a strip of land extending into the sea and therefore surrounded by beaches, all marked with a blue flag. The name "Cabo Roig" is translated from the Valencian word "Cabo Rojo", given by the red rock that forms this cape.

One of the main characteristics of Cabo Roig is the lack of tall buildings, most of the properties in this area are villas with large plots a very short distance from the coast, along with the famous white sand beaches and a wide range of all kinds of activities related to the sea and proximity to the La Shopping Center Zenia Boulevard and the Campoamor and Villamartín golf courses make this city a real paradise and a privileged place to spend holidays, long periods of time or even settle permanently.

The beautiful and romantic promenade allows you to walk along the entire coastline and connects with neighboring buildings. In the future, it is planned to reach Torrevieja and Torre de la Horadada. A famous monument is the historic tower of Cabo Roig, built in the 16th century as a defense against pirates. Next to this tower are the offices of Inmobiliaria Mevilla.

Stroll on foot or by bike through the quiet streets and avenues of Cabo Roig, lined with large palm trees, while observing the well-kept gardens and beautiful single-family houses that can be seen on every side, enjoying the sea breeze, making this area a true oasis of peace and a true delight for the senses.

Golf enthusiasts will delight in the numerous golf courses available in the area, including Villamartin Golf Course, Las Colinas Golf & Country Club and Campoamor Golf Club. These courses offer world-class facilities and stunning views, providing an unforgettable golfing experience.
