Location

Located on the east coast of Spain in the province of Alicante, Jávea is a beautiful town nestled between the mountains and the sea. This charming town has a rich history dating back to prehistoric times, with evidence of settlement from the Palaeolithic and Neolithic eras. Jávea is approximately 100 km from the nearest Alicante-Elche Airport.

Jávea is famous for its beautiful beaches with crystal clear waters and pristine sand. Some of the best beaches in the area are Arenal Beach, Granadella Beach and Cala Blanca Beach. Arenal Beach is the most popular, with a wide range of facilities such as restaurants, bars and water sports. Granadella Beach is a quieter, more secluded option, ideal for those seeking a quieter retreat. Cala Blanca Beach is a hidden gem that can only be reached by foot or boat, providing a secluded and peaceful place to relax.

In addition to the beaches, Jávea offers a wide range of outdoor activities including hiking, cycling and water sports such as scuba diving, paddleboarding and kayaking. There are also several golf courses in the town, which are ideal for golf enthusiasts.

For those interested in history and culture, Jávea has a rich heritage worth exploring. The Old Town, also known as El Pueblo', is a maze of narrow streets and historic buildings, offering a glimpse into the town's rich past. Be sure to visit the Gothic church of San Bartolomé, which dates back to the 14th century. The Soler Blasco Archaeological and Ethnographic Museum provides a fascinating insight into the history and culture of Jávea.
