We present a new project of modern apartments with an ideal location in picturesque Estepona. Investments are being built near Teatro Auditorium Felipe VI. You can also walk to Parque del Calvario, the center of Estepona, the old town and in 25 minutes to la Rada beach. The excellent location also allows you to enjoy unobstructed views of the sea, mountains or city center. Thanks to this, you have ideal conditions for renting the apartment to guests, as well as for using it yourself.

Your new apartment is also close to many golf courses, including Estepona Golf, Finca Cortesin, Casares Golf and Valle Romano Golf, offering golf fans a range of leisure activities. If you want to go on a trip to Benahavís or Marbella, you need 25 or 30 minutes by car respectively. Malaga and Gibraltar airports are approximately 45-50 minutes away by car.

This modern residential complex offers luxurious apartments from one to four bedrooms and living space from 50 to 120 m2. The apartments are designed to ensure spatial fluidity between the living room and the kitchen, with direct access to a large terrace with beautiful views of the mountains or sea. The price includes a garage and a storage room.

The exclusive common area available only to residents has been designed so that you can fully enjoy life in close proximity to nature without sacrificing luxury. This unique, protected residential complex has two swimming pools with chlorinated brine, a gym with a sauna where you can enjoy a healthy life, a co-working room that will bring your work office closer than ever, and a social club. The residential buildings are surrounded by beautiful Mediterranean gardens.

Planned construction completion date: Q1 2025.

Location

Estepona convinces with its modernity, good infrastructure and typical Andalusia charm. This is undoubtedly one of the most attractive corners of the Costa del Sol. A city with maritime traditions, which, due to the quality of life and a wide range of leisure activities, has become one of the most popular places on the Costa del Sol. The golf clubs located here are proud to be called Costa del Golf, and the spectacular natural landscapes allow you to lead a healthy lifestyle, including practicing numerous outdoor sports.

Estepona is conveniently located a short distance from Malaga Airport, making it easily accessible to travelers. The airport is approximately 80 kilometers away, ensuring a smooth and comfortable journey for visitors.

There is no doubt that Estepona is today a benchmark for quality of life, modern tourism and recreation facilities, city care and the provision of public services, thanks to the investment incentives initiated by the Estepona City Council in 2011 to implement a sustainable city model with the well-being of the citizen as its central axis and respect for the environment.

The involvement in the Estepona project, the Costa del Sol' Garden, which has achieved such good socio-economic results, has made it possible to regenerate the city, provide it with a unique tourist attraction and support the economic recovery and revitalization of the commercial and commercial tourist life of this municipality in Malaga. The results are clear: Estepona is among the municipalities that lead in job creation in Andalusia and public investment in the province of Malaga.

The city boasts a collection of breathtaking beaches that will delight beach lovers and sun seekers alike. Some of the area's best beaches include Playa del Cristo, a sheltered cove with calm waters and a relaxing atmosphere, and Playa de la Rada, a vast stretch of golden sand offering a wide range of water sports.

The nearby Sierra Bermeja mountain range provides excellent hiking opportunities, allowing you to immerse yourself in stunning natural landscapes and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the coast. Golf enthusiasts will delight in the numerous world-class golf courses in the area where they can play against the backdrop of lush greenery and azure skies.
