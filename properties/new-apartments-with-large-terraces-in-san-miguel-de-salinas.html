A new residential development offering modern apartments with spacious terraces and attractive communal areas. The distinctive cascading architecture of the building has been designed to provide residents with plenty of outdoor space and natural light.

The development offers apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, available on different floors, from the ground floor up to the 7th floor. The apartments have built areas ranging from approximately 65 m² to 80 m², with large terraces providing a natural extension of the living space. Selected corner apartments feature exceptionally spacious terraces of more than 100 m².

The apartments have been designed in a modern and functional style. Bright interiors, large windows and direct access to the terrace create a comfortable living environment suitable for both year-round living and holidays. Depending on the property selected, different orientations are available, including southwest, southeast and northwest.

One of the main advantages of the development is its carefully designed communal areas. At the heart of the complex is a swimming pool surrounded by landscaped green areas and spaces for relaxation. The project also includes a recreational area and a dedicated space for children, creating a welcoming environment for the whole family.

Parking spaces are also included with the apartments.

This is an attractive option for those looking for a new apartment in Spain with a large terrace, swimming pool and modern communal areas, whether for personal use or as a holiday property.

The development is scheduled for completion in September 2028.

Location

The development is located in San Miguel de Salinas, in the southern part of the Costa Blanca, in a peaceful area where residents can enjoy the Mediterranean climate while still having convenient access to everyday services.

Shops, supermarkets, restaurants, bars and other amenities can be found within easy reach. By car, you can quickly reach the popular beaches of Orihuela Costa, as well as nearby towns such as Torrevieja and Pilar de la Horadada. The area is also particularly attractive to golf enthusiasts, with several renowned golf courses located in this part of the Costa Blanca.

Good road connections make it easy to travel throughout the region and provide convenient access to Alicante-Elche Airport. The location is ideal both for holidays and year-round living.
