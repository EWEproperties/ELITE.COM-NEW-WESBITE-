Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional contemporary villa located in one of the most prestigious areas of Moraira on the northern Costa Blanca. This exclusive property has been designed for the most discerning buyers seeking a luxury home in Spain that combines contemporary architecture, panoramic Mediterranean Sea views and a prime location just minutes from the beach and the town centre. The villa offers premium-quality finishes, spacious interiors and outstanding comfort tailored to a modern lifestyle.

Built on a plot of approximately 913 m², the residence offers around 350 m² of built area. The two-storey design impresses with its minimalist architecture, elegant lines and floor-to-ceiling glazing that fills the interiors with natural light while seamlessly connecting the home with its beautiful surroundings. The harmonious combination of contemporary design and functionality makes this property an ideal choice both as a luxurious permanent residence and an exclusive holiday home.

The heart of the villa is a spacious open-plan living room connected to an elegant dining area and a modern kitchen featuring a central island and premium Siemens appliances. The bright living area opens directly onto generous terraces, creating the perfect setting for relaxing, entertaining family and friends or simply enjoying the breathtaking Mediterranean views. Every detail has been carefully designed to create a seamless transition between the indoor and outdoor living spaces.

The villa offers four comfortable bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom, ensuring maximum privacy and comfort for all residents. The impressive master suite features a spacious walk-in wardrobe and a private terrace overlooking the sea. Additional highlights include a private cinema room, a laundry room and a guest toilet, further enhancing the home's practicality and luxurious appeal.

Outside, the property boasts a spectacular infinity swimming pool measuring approximately 11.5 × 4 metres, expansive sun terraces, a barbecue area and beautifully landscaped low-maintenance gardens. It is the perfect place to relax, sunbathe and enjoy the Mediterranean climate throughout the year. A private garage together with additional parking spaces provides convenience and security for both owners and guests.

The villa is equipped with state-of-the-art technology, including underfloor heating powered by a heat pump, ducted air conditioning, premium Technal aluminium joinery with safety glazing, electric shutters, an alarm system, video intercom, LED lighting and pre-installation for photovoltaic solar panels. High-quality materials and equipment from renowned manufacturers guarantee exceptional comfort and long-lasting value.

Moraira has long been regarded as one of the most exclusive destinations on the Costa Blanca. Its charming marina, sandy beaches, excellent restaurants, stylish boutiques and renowned golf courses create an exceptional environment for both permanent living and unforgettable holidays. The villa's privileged location just a few minutes from the town centre and the coastline allows residents to enjoy the very best of the Mediterranean lifestyle while maintaining complete privacy and tranquillity.

Location

The development is situated in one of the most prestigious areas of Moraira on the northern Costa Blanca. This exceptional location perfectly combines a peaceful Mediterranean setting with close proximity to beautiful beaches, an elegant marina and all the services required for comfortable year-round living. Moraira is widely recognised as one of the region's most exclusive destinations, appreciated for its charming atmosphere, high-quality residential developments and relaxed coastal lifestyle.

Just a few minutes away are the sandy Playa de l'Ampolla beach and the picturesque coves of El Portet and Platgetes, where residents can enjoy crystal-clear waters, waterfront restaurants and a wide range of water sports. The marina of Moraira is also nearby, offering an excellent selection of cafés, restaurants, boutiques and vibrant year-round social life.

The area benefits from excellent infrastructure, with supermarkets, shops, schools, medical centres, pharmacies, banks and sports facilities all within easy reach. Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity of prestigious courses such as Club de Golf Ifach in Benissa and La Sella Golf Resort in Dénia, while nature lovers can explore numerous walking and cycling routes along the coastline and surrounding hills.

Excellent road connections provide quick access to nearby destinations including Jávea, Benissa, Calpe and Altea. Alicante International Airport can be reached in approximately one hour by car, while Valencia Airport is around one hour and thirty minutes away, ensuring convenient connections with major European cities.

The combination of spectacular Mediterranean Sea views, a prestigious location and an outstanding range of leisure facilities makes Moraira an exceptional place to live all year round or to purchase an exclusive holiday or investment property.
