Discover an exclusive collection of modern apartments for sale in Estepona, enjoying an elevated position next to Valle Romano Golf & Resort and spectacular views towards Estepona Bay, the Mediterranean landscape and the surrounding golf course. Contemporary architecture, spacious terraces and excellent communal facilities create an attractive setting for enjoying life on the Costa del Sol.

The private residential complex comprises 144 apartments with 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms, distributed across six elegant low-rise buildings designed to blend naturally into the surrounding landscape. The south-facing orientation allows the homes to benefit from abundant natural light throughout the day, while large windows frame the open views and create bright, welcoming interiors.

The apartments feature modern, functional layouts designed to maximise space and comfort. Open-plan living areas connect naturally with the kitchens, creating spacious interiors ideal for everyday living. The kitchens are generously sized and fully equipped with energy-efficient appliances, combining contemporary design with practicality. Soft, natural tones throughout the interiors create an elegant and relaxing atmosphere.

Every property features a spacious private terrace with glass balustrades designed to preserve the open views. Ground-floor apartments also benefit from private outdoor areas, while the penthouses stand out for their impressive panoramic views of the sea and surrounding landscape. The generous terraces provide an ideal setting for outdoor dining, relaxing in the sun and enjoying Estepona's Mediterranean climate.

The communal areas have been carefully designed to provide spaces for relaxation, exercise and socialising. Residents can enjoy a communal swimming pool, indoor gym and gourmet lounge, complemented by extensive landscaped gardens planted with native Mediterranean species.

Privacy and security are also an important part of the development. The residential complex is fully gated and monitored from a dedicated security post, creating a peaceful and private environment for residents.

Excellent location next to golf in Estepona

The development is located in Estepona on the Costa del Sol, next to Valle Romano Golf & Resort and within easy reach of beaches, the marina, restaurants, shops and everyday services.

Estepona town centre is approximately 9.3 km away, while Estepona Marina is around 8 km from the development. Golf enthusiasts have an excellent selection of courses nearby, including Azata Golf Club at approximately 2.8 km, Estepona Golf at 6 km and Doña Julia Golf at 9.8 km. Sotogrande Marina is approximately 24 km away.

Estepona combines traditional Andalusian character with a contemporary Mediterranean lifestyle. Its renovated old town, pedestrian streets, beaches and marina are complemented by restaurants, shopping facilities, sports centres, schools and healthcare services, making the area suitable for both holidays and year-round living.

Direct access to the A-7 and AP-7 provides convenient connections along the Costa del Sol. According to the developer, both Málaga and Gibraltar airports can be reached in approximately 45 minutes.

With sea and golf views, spacious terraces, contemporary interiors and excellent communal facilities, these new apartments for sale in Estepona are an attractive choice for a permanent residence or second home on the Costa del Sol.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Guadalobón, about 6 km by road, around 10 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: IES Monterroso, about 10 km, around 15 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio Diocesano Juan XXIII, about 4 km, around 9 minutes by car.
International school: The International School of Estepona, about 26 km, around 29 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Estepona, about 9 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital de Alta Resolución de Estepona, about 4 km by road, around 8 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Aldi, about 4 km away.

How far is the airport?

Málaga Airport is about 93 km by road, around 70 minutes by car.
