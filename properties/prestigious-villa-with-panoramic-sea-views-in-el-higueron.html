For sale, an exceptional new villa located in the prestigious area of El Higuerón on the Costa del Sol, enjoying a peaceful, elevated position with panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding mountains. Contemporary architecture, spacious interiors and an extensive wellness area create a property designed for comfort, privacy and year-round Mediterranean living.

The villa offers approximately 399 m² of built area together with impressive outdoor spaces. The open-plan living area combines a spacious lounge, dining area and modern kitchen, creating a bright and functional interior. Large floor-to-ceiling windows provide abundant natural light and open the home onto the terraces and garden, while showcasing beautiful views of the sea and mountains.

The bedrooms have been designed as comfortable and private spaces for relaxation, while the bathrooms are finished with high-quality materials, including elegant porcelain stoneware. Carefully selected finishes and modern features emphasise the contemporary character of the property.

One of the villa’s main highlights is its private outdoor area. The beautifully designed garden surrounds an impressive saltwater swimming pool with a dedicated relaxation area. Residents can also enjoy spacious covered and open terraces, a barbecue area and an atmospheric fire pit, perfect for relaxing in the evening or spending time with family and friends.

The property also features an extensive private wellness area, including a gym, jacuzzi, sauna and Turkish bath, providing dedicated spaces for exercise and relaxation without leaving home.

The villa also includes a private garage, laundry room and practical utility and storage areas. The combination of a functional layout, spacious terraces, private swimming pool and modern amenities makes this property an excellent choice both as a year-round residence and an exclusive holiday home on the Costa del Sol.

Excellent location in El Higuerón

The villa is located in El Higuerón, one of the most sought-after areas of the coastline between Benalmádena and Fuengirola. Its elevated position provides beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea and mountains, while beaches, restaurants, shops, sports clubs and everyday services are within easy reach.

The area also provides convenient access to golf courses, international schools and the popular coastal destinations of the Costa del Sol. Málaga Airport is approximately 15 minutes away by car, while Fuengirola, Benalmádena and other well-known destinations along the coast are easily accessible thanks to excellent road connections.

This is an excellent opportunity for those looking for a new, spacious villa with a private swimming pool, spa area and panoramic sea views in one of the most attractive locations on the Costa del Sol.
