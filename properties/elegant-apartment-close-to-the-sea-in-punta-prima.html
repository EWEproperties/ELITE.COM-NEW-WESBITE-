Location

Punta Prima, in the Orihuela Costa, is one of the most sought-after areas due to its excellent location and services. It is located on the border of Torrevieja and Orihuela Costa. Punta Prima is very popular with tourists all year round and has a large population of permanent residents of all European nationalities, meaning that shops and services are open all year round, regardless of the season.

To do your shopping, you will find a selection of many well-known supermarkets and minimarkets where you can find all your essentials. The Punta Marina' shopping centre has a supermarket, pharmacy, clothing shops, card shop, hairdressers, electrical shop, vets, tattoo parlour and many bars and restaurants.

Beaches

Punta Prima has two beaches and a small cove on its shores, La Playa Rocio del Mar, Cala Piteras and Punta Prima beach, the latter with a Blue Flag. A Blue Flag beach means that the beach is safe, the water quality is good, lifeguards are present in high season, accessibility is good and toilets are available. The magnificent Punta Prima beach is 234m long and 28m wide. The beach is sandy with many rocky coves to explore.

There are also many devotees of Cala Piteras‘, where one of Punta Prima's emblematic establishments, Restaurante Nautilus’, is located.

Restaurants

The road that connects the central part of Punta Prima to the beach is about a 10-minute walk away. At the end of this road is the famous Nautilus' restaurant. This restaurant specialises in Mediterranean and Spanish cuisine. The food is beautifully served and the location on Punta Prima beach is stunning. A great place to celebrate a special occasion.

RESTAURANTE PUNTA PRIMA on the southern Costa Blanca has been delighting locals and tourists for over 25 years with its exquisite approach to traditional Spanish and Western Mediterranean cuisine. It is popular for its magnificent large terrace, which offers an impressive view of Punta Prima's main beach and the Mediterranean Sea.

The central part of Punta Prima also boasts a huge variety of bars and restaurants to suit every taste, from McDonalds and fish and chips to tapas and Japanese dishes. The traditional Chinese restaurant Dinastia' is very popular and offers delicious Asian cuisine.

There is a wonderful atmosphere in the evening and all the bars and restaurants are illuminated.

Golf

This area of the southern Costa Blanca is home to many championship and award-winning golf courses. The closest to Punta Prima, just a 10-minute drive, are Villamartin Golf, Las Ramblas Golf, Campoamor Golf and the famous Las Colinas Golf and Country Club. All have 18 holes and all have restaurants so you can enjoy a cold drink and a snack after a round of golf.

A little further afield is Romero Golf, Vistabella Golf, La Finca Golf, La Marquesa Golf and the famous La Manga Golf Club. If you are a keen golfer or a novice, you are sure to find a golf course to suit your skills.
