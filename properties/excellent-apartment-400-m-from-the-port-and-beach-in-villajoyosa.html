Location

Villajoyosa is a picturesque Spanish town nestled between the mountains and the sea on the beautiful Costa Blanca. This colourful town is located between the popular town of Benidorm and El Campello, about a 30-minute drive from Alicante airport.

Villajoyosa seems to literally hang on rocky coastal cliffs. Combined with its charming architecture, this gives an amazing impression of a picturesque coastline. What's more, the name of the town itself promises a pleasant stay. Villajoyosa or La Vila Joiosa literally means ‘joyful town’. And that is exactly what it feels like, with its colourful houses in the town centre.

A local legend says that the buildings were painted in bright colours to help fishermen find their way home. Today, they certainly attract crowds of tourists and are an inspiration for many photographers and artists.

In recent years, this charming town has become a desirable place for people looking for a second home in a quiet area.

Villajoyosa has a lot to offer, from a wide sandy beach to the narrow and colourful streets of the historic centre.

Villajoyosa has been associated with chocolate since the 19th century. It is home to the popular Spanish chocolate factory Valor. The Valor, Pérez and Clavileño museums are must-see attractions. There you will learn everything about the chocolate production process and its history in this area. Tours led by knowledgeable guides are usually combined with chocolate tasting.

During the holiday season, Villajoyosa hosts a variety of cultural events. It is worth visiting the village in July, when the famous Moors and Christians festival takes place here. The main attraction is a re-enactment of the battle of 1538, as well as concerts, performances and shows.

Villajoyosa has 3 km of golden sandy beaches with views of the endless clear sea. The most beautiful ones are Playa El Paraíso, Xarco and La Caleta. You will find both larger tourist resorts and hidden, mysterious corners perfect for peaceful relaxation. The coastal panorama, Mediterranean climate and soft, hot sand are perfect for complete relaxation. Near the beaches, you will find regional restaurants and cafés offering fresh Mediterranean delicacies.

Villajoyosa delights tourists with its idyllic, cheerful atmosphere and colourful houses. There is no shortage of attractions for those thirsty for excitement and those interested in history and culture. Even a simple walk along the seashore provides breathtaking views and unforgettable experiences.
