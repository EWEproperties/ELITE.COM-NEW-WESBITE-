Location – Estepona

Estepona is one of the most charming towns on the Costa del Sol, situated between the Mediterranean Sea and the Sierra Bermeja mountains. It has preserved its authentic Andalusian character, featuring whitewashed houses, flower-filled streets, and picturesque old-town squares.

The town boasts more than 20 kilometres of coastline with wide sandy beaches and a modern marina full of restaurants, cafés, and leisure facilities. One of Estepona’s most famous attractions is its impressive Orchidarium, home to the largest collection of orchids in Spain and one of the largest in Europe.

Nature lovers and outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the nearby Sierra Bermeja mountains, offering numerous hiking trails and breathtaking views of the Costa del Sol. With over 320 days of sunshine per year, Estepona is an ideal place to live, relax, and invest in property.
