For sale, an exceptional contemporary villa set in the hills above Marbella, within a prestigious gated residential community surrounded by Mediterranean nature. The property combines contemporary architecture, privacy and panoramic views across the valley and Mediterranean Sea with access to exclusive five-star resort-style amenities.

The villa offers 5 spacious bedrooms and 7 bathrooms, providing comfortable accommodation for both family and guests. The open-plan living area combines the lounge, dining room and modern kitchen, while large windows fill the interiors with natural light and create a seamless connection with the terraces.

The spacious master bedroom has been designed as a private retreat, with its own terrace and open views of the surrounding landscape.

The outdoor areas form an integral part of the home. Generous terraces lead to a private saltwater infinity pool and relaxation area, creating an ideal setting for outdoor dining and spending time with family and friends.

Additional space on the lower level can be adapted to the owner's individual requirements and used as a private gym, cinema room, games room or wine cellar. A private lift provides convenient access between the different levels.

One of the property's greatest advantages is access to the private **Owner's Club**, created exclusively for residents. Facilities include an indoor swimming pool, spa, sauna, hammam and modern gym with panoramic sea views.

The club also features a restaurant and bar, providing an exclusive place to relax and socialise without leaving the community. Residents can also enjoy padel courts, walking and running trails, a fitness area, a short-game golf practice area and organic gardens.

The experience is complemented by **24-hour security, controlled access and concierge services**, which can include transport arrangements, restaurant reservations, private chefs, wellness services, housekeeping and preparation of the property before the owners' arrival.

LOCATION

The hillside setting above Marbella provides tranquillity, natural surroundings and expansive views while remaining conveniently close to the coast. Marbella town centre, the Old Town, Golden Mile, Puerto Banús and the Mediterranean beaches*are all within easy reach.

The surrounding area also offers renowned golf courses, restaurants, international schools and all the services required for comfortable year-round living.

This **5-bedroom villa for sale in Marbella** combines the space and privacy of a private residence with the comfort and services of a five-star resort, offering an exceptional lifestyle in one of the most desirable locations on the Costa del Sol.
