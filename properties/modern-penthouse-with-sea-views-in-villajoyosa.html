Location

Villajoyosa is a charming Mediterranean coastal town, located between Alicante and Benidorm, about 30 minutes drive from Alicante airport. Villajoyosa or La Vila Joiosa literally translates as a happy town. Indeed, this is also the impression it gives, with its colourful houses in the town centre. This traditional fishing village is considered one of the most beautiful in the province of Alicante.

Urban legend has it that the buildings were painted in distinctive colours to help fishermen find their way home. Today, they certainly attract a multitude of tourists and are the inspiration for many photographers and artists.

You only have to stroll through the picturesque streets to realise that Villajoyosa is full of joy (as its name suggests) and charm. There are many reasons why this municipality is the ideal place to spend an unforgettable holiday: good weather all year round, paradisiacal beaches and coves, history and culture everywhere and an overall environment that encourages relaxation.

Villajoyosa has been associated with chocolate since the 19th century. It is here that the popular Spanish chocolate factory Valor is located. The Valor, Pérez and Clavileño museums are a must on your sightseeing list. There you will learn all about the chocolate-making process and its history in the area. Tours led by knowledgeable guides are usually combined with a chocolate tasting.

Villajoyosa contains 3 km of beaches with golden sand and a view of the endless clear sea. The most beautiful of these are playa El Paraíso, Xarco and la Caleta. Here you will find both the larger tourist resorts and hidden, secret corners perfect for quiet relaxation. The coastal panorama, Mediterranean climate and soft, hot sand are perfect for an atmosphere of total relaxation. Near the beaches you will find regional restaurants and cafés offering fresh Mediterranean delicacies.
