Location

Torrevieja, a city nestled about 45 km from Alicante, is famed for its unique, healthy microclimate. This is largely attributed to the large salt deposits and two remarkable lagoons, Laguna de Torrevieja and La Laguna de la Mata, right in the city's heart. These lagoons, one pink and the other green, are not only a visual marvel but also a habitat for diverse bird species, including flamingos. The pink hue of Laguna de Torrevieja, caused by high salinity, crustaceans, and algae, makes it a standout attraction.

La Laguna de la Mata, with its green waters, is home to the park's interpretative center and a variety of unique flora, including Aleppo pine and orchids. The area's vineyards are also noteworthy, producing the local wine de la Mata.

Torrevieja's climate is perfect for beach lovers. From the 2.5 km long, Blue Flag-awarded Playa de la Mata to the accessible and vibrant beaches of Playa de los Locos and Playa del Cura, there's a beach for every preference. In the city center, the natural pools amidst the rocky coast offer a unique swimming experience, enhanced by nearby statues like the Man of the Sea and Beautiful Lola. Cala Ferris and Cala Piteras, with their stunning views, palm trees, and opportunities for snorkeling, are not to be missed.

Strolling along the Vista Alegre promenade, you'll immerse yourself in the city's lively atmosphere, with a plethora of bars, restaurants, cafes, and shops. For entertainment, Torrevieja boasts the Aquopolis water park and a nearby amusement park, plus open-air summer discos.

Sports enthusiasts will find Torrevieja a haven, with facilities for football, handball, tennis, rugby, swimming, and more. The city, once home to only an observation tower (the 'old tower' or 'torre vieja'), has transformed into an architectural wonder.

With over 300 sunny days a year, Torrevieja is more than a vacation spot; it's a tempting destination for an extended stay or a new home.
