Location

Estepona impresses with its modernity, good infrastructure and typical Andalusian charm. It is undoubtedly one of the most attractive corners of the Costa del Sol. A city with maritime traditions that has become one of the most popular destinations on the Costa del Sol due to its quality of life and wide range of leisure activities. The golf clubs located here boast the name Costa del Golf' and the spectacular natural landscapes allow for a healthy lifestyle, including numerous outdoor sports.

Estepona is conveniently located a short distance from Malaga Airport, making it easily accessible to travellers. The airport is approximately 80 kilometres away, ensuring a smooth and comfortable journey for visitors.

There is no doubt that Estepona is today a benchmark for quality of life, modern tourist and leisure facilities, urban care and public service provision, thanks to the investment impetus launched by Estepona City Council in 2011 to implement a sustainable city model with citizen wellbeing and respect for the environment as its central axis.

The involvement in the Estepona project, ‘The Garden of the Costa del Sol’, which has yielded such good socio-economic results, has made it possible to renew the city, provide it with a unique tourist attraction and support the economic recovery and revitalisation of the commercial and retail facilities of the tourist life of this municipality in Malaga. The results are clear: Estepona is one of the municipalities at the forefront of job creation in Andalusia and public investment in the province of Málaga.

The town boasts a collection of breathtaking beaches that will delight beach lovers and sun seekers. Some of the best beaches in the area are Playa del Cristo, a sheltered cove with calm waters and a relaxing atmosphere, and Playa de la Rada, a vast stretch of golden sand offering a wide range of water sports.

The nearby Sierra Bermeja mountain range provides excellent conditions for hiking, allowing you to immerse yourself in stunning natural landscapes and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the coast. Golf enthusiasts will be delighted with the numerous world-class courses in the area, where they can play against a backdrop of lush greens and azure skies.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Play Costa Natura (Nudist), about 2.8 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

The nearest schools are Colegio Diocesano Juan XXIII (about 3 km) and CEIP San Luis de Sabinillas (about 5 km).

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud de Sabinillas, about 4 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital de Alta Resolución de Estepona, about 3 km by road, around 5 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Supermarket, about 300 m away.

How far is the airport?

Málaga Airport is about 91 km by road, around 70 minutes by car.
