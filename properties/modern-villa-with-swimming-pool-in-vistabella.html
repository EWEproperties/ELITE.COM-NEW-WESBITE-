Location

Vistabella Golf is a prestigious golf community nestled in the picturesque region of Orihuela, offering an unparalleled lifestyle for golf enthusiasts and nature lovers alike. Located in the beautiful Costa Blanca region of Spain, this resort provides a perfect blend of modern amenities, natural surroundings, and leisure opportunities.

The resort boasts a magnificent 18-hole golf course that challenges players of all skill levels. Designed to showcase the region's natural beauty, the course features lush fairways, strategically placed bunkers, and stunning views of the surrounding landscapes. Golfers can enjoy a memorable golfing experience while appreciating the Mediterranean climate and serene ambiance.

For those who appreciate stunning beaches, the renowned Playa de La Zenia is a short distance away from Vistabella Golf Resort. Just a 20-minute drive from the resort, this pristine beach offers soft golden sands, crystal-clear waters, and a vibrant beachfront atmosphere. Whether you prefer sunbathing, swimming, or engaging in water sports, Playa de La Zenia provides the perfect setting for relaxation and fun in the sun.

If you're looking for a vibrant city experience, the resort is conveniently situated near the city of Torrevieja. With its bustling marina, charming promenade, and diverse range of restaurants and shops, Torrevieja offers an array of entertainment and dining options. Explore the local markets, indulge in fresh seafood, or simply take a leisurely stroll along the coast to soak in the Mediterranean atmosphere.

For those interested in cultural experiences, nearby attractions include the historic city of Murcia, known for its stunning architecture and vibrant culture. The city's cathedral, museums, and charming old town provide a captivating glimpse into Spain's rich heritage.

In terms of accessibility, Vistabella Golf Resort is approximately a 40-minute drive from Alicante Airport (ALC), which serves as the main international gateway to the region. Alicante Airport offers a wide range of domestic and international flights, making it convenient for residents and visitors to reach the resort easily.

Whether you're seeking a round of golf, relaxation by the beach, or cultural exploration, Vistabella Golf Resort and its surrounding area provide a wealth of opportunities for an exceptional lifestyle.
