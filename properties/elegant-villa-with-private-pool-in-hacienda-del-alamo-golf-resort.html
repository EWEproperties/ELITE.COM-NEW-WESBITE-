Elite World Estates presents a modern villa located within the prestigious Hacienda del Álamo Golf Resort in the Murcia region. This is an excellent opportunity for those seeking a comfortable home in a peaceful golf resort setting, offering privacy and a Mediterranean lifestyle all year round.

The property is designed on a single level and features a functional layout with 3 bedrooms and 2 elegant bathrooms. The bright living area combines a spacious lounge, dining area, and modern open-plan kitchen. Large floor-to-ceiling windows flood the interior with natural light and seamlessly connect the indoor living space with the terrace and garden.

The villa is situated on a spacious plot and features a private swimming pool, landscaped garden, private parking space, and an impressive rooftop solarium where you can enjoy beautiful views of the golf course and the surrounding mountains.

An additional advantage of the property is its high-quality finish, including a fully fitted kitchen with appliances, an energy-efficient aerothermal system for hot water production, and pre-installation for ducted air conditioning.

Hacienda del Álamo Golf Resort offers residents access to an 18-hole golf course, restaurants, sports facilities, a hotel, and extensive green areas. Murcia International Airport is located just approximately 15 minutes away by car.

Location

Fuente Álamo de Murcia is a charming Spanish town located in the Region of Murcia, renowned for its relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle, authentic local character, and excellent connections to the Costa Cálida coastline. Surrounded by picturesque mountains and countryside, it offers ideal conditions for year-round living.

The town provides all essential amenities for everyday life, including shops, restaurants, schools, medical centres, and a variety of sports facilities. Thanks to its convenient location, residents can enjoy both the tranquillity of a traditional Spanish town and easy access to the region’s main attractions.

Fuente Álamo is situated approximately 20 minutes from Murcia International Airport and around 25 minutes from the beautiful sandy beaches of the Costa Cálida and the Mediterranean Sea. Another advantage is its proximity to prestigious golf courses, natural parks, and historic cities such as Cartagena and Murcia.

It is an ideal location for those seeking a peaceful place to live, a second home in Spain, or an attractive investment property in a region that enjoys more than 300 days of sunshine each year.
