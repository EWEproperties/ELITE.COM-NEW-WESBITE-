Location

Torrevieja is one of the most well-known destinations on the Costa Blanca, highly regarded for its exceptional Mediterranean climate, beautiful beaches, and excellent quality of life throughout the year. The city is located approximately 40 minutes from Alicante International Airport, providing quick and convenient access for both residents and holidaymakers.

Torrevieja is famous for its wide sandy beaches and lively promenades lined with restaurants, cafés, and shops. Popular beaches such as La Mata, Playa del Cura, and Los Locos offer the perfect setting for relaxation, seaside walks, and water sports. One of the city's most iconic landmarks is the famous Pink Lagoon, which creates a unique microclimate considered one of the healthiest in Europe.

The city offers excellent infrastructure for everyday living, including shopping centres, international schools, private clinics, supermarkets, and a wide variety of restaurants. Several renowned golf courses, marinas, and recreational areas can also be found nearby, making Torrevieja an ideal place for both holidays and year-round living.

This is the perfect location for anyone looking for a property in sunny Spain, whether as a holiday home, a rental investment, or a permanent residence embracing the Mediterranean lifestyle.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa del Acequión, about 1.6 km by road.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio Público Inmaculada Conception, about 600 m.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 20 km, around 22 minutes by car.
International school: Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen, about 11 km, around 14 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Centro de Salud Patricio Pérez, about 500 m away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Quirón/USP Hospital San Jaime, about 4 km by road, around 6 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Dia, about 200 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 60 km by road, around 45 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 66 km, around 50 minutes by car.
