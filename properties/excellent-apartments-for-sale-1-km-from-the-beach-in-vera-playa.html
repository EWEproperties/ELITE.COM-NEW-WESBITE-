A new residential development located in Vera Playa on the Costa de Almería, just approximately 1 km from the beach. Its peaceful coastal setting, contemporary architecture and extensive communal facilities make it an attractive option both for permanent living and as a second home in Spain.

The complex comprises 146 modern apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms, designed with space, natural light and a comfortable Mediterranean lifestyle in mind. All properties feature spacious terraces, while apartments on the upper floors additionally offer private solariums of more than 60 m², providing an ideal outdoor space to enjoy views of the surroundings, the sea and beautiful sunsets.

The interiors have a bright and contemporary character. Each apartment offers 2 bathrooms and an open-plan living area, where the lounge connects seamlessly with the kitchen. Large windows allow plenty of natural light into the interiors and create a natural transition between the living space and the terrace.

One of the main advantages of the development is its extensive selection of communal areas designed for relaxation and outdoor activities. Residents will have access to 2 swimming pools, landscaped gardens, mini golf, an outdoor gym, pétanque and table tennis areas, as well as a children's playground. Each apartment also includes a parking space and storage room.

The estimated completion date for the development is the first quarter of 2028.

Location – Vera Playa, Costa de Almería

The development is situated in Vera Playa, one of the popular coastal areas of the Costa de Almería, approximately 1 km from the Mediterranean Sea and its wide sandy beaches. It is particularly appealing to those who want to live close to the sea while enjoying a quieter environment and a greater sense of space than in more crowded Mediterranean resorts.

Vera Playa provides excellent conditions for both holidays and year-round living. The surrounding area offers restaurants, bars, shops, supermarkets and everyday services, making daily life convenient without the need for long journeys. At the same time, the proximity to the coast makes it easy to enjoy days at the beach, seaside walks and the Mediterranean outdoor lifestyle throughout much of the year.

Another major advantage is the proximity to Mojácar, one of the most distinctive destinations in this part of Andalusia. The coastal area of Mojácar offers long beaches, restaurants and beach bars, while historic Mojácar Pueblo, perched on a hill, is known for its whitewashed Andalusian architecture, narrow streets and panoramic views. This allows property owners to enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of Vera Playa while remaining within easy reach of one of the area's most popular coastal destinations.

The centre of Vera can be reached by car in approximately 10–12 minutes, while Mojácar is around 15–20 minutes away. Convenient access to the AP-7 motorway also makes travelling along the Costa de Almería and towards the Murcia region straightforward. Almería Airport is approximately 60 minutes away by car, while Murcia Airport is around 75 minutes away.

An additional advantage for future property owners is the planned Vera AVE high-speed railway station, which is expected to further improve connections between this part of the coast and other major Spanish cities.
