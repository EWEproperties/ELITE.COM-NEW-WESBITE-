Location

Dolores is a small town in the province of Vega Baja, in its southern part, about 50 kilometers from Alicante. We can easily get here by the AP7 motorway, and by the road leading from Elche if traveling by plane, from the international airport of Alicante, which is only 25 minutes from Dolores.

Certainly, the great advantage of the town of Dolores are open and hospitable inhabitants, high quality of life, pleasant and healthy climate as well as close proximity to the sea (about 10 kilometers). All this makes this area at the top of the quality of life of the Mediterranean style.

The proximity of the El Hondo nature reserve gives a great space for relaxation and the joy of many paths to walk in the interesting company of various types of birds living in the reserve, which is their shelter).

Beaches

Guardamar del Segura Beach: Located about a 15-minute drive from Dolores, Guardamar del Segura Beach is a vast beach of golden sand and crystal clear waters. It has a wide range of services such as beach bars, restaurants and children's playgrounds.

La Marina Beach: Located south of Guardamar del Segura, La Marina Beach is another nearby option. It is a quieter and less crowded beach, surrounded by natural dunes and pine forests. Here you can enjoy a more natural and relaxing environment.

Golf courses

La Marquesa Golf: This golf course is located in the nearby town of Rojales, about a 10-minute drive from Dolores. It is an 18-hole course designed for players of all levels. It offers panoramic views of the countryside and offers services such as equipment rental, golf lessons and a restaurant.

La Finca Golf & Spa Resort: Located in Algorfa, about a 20-minute drive from Dolores, this 18-hole golf course offers a stunning setting overlooking a mountainous landscape. In addition to the golf course, the resort also has a spa, hotel and several restaurants.

Monuments

The Church of the Archduke Dolores is one of the greatest architectural icons of the 18th century with great purity of style and excellent quality. Paintings showing the local Saint Nuestra Senora de los Dolores (the most beautiful sculpture ever created by Salzillo) and Saint San Pascual Bailón together with frescoes further show the majesty of this temple. The city promenade is a place showing a bronze bust, a tribute to the founder of the city - Cardinal Luis Belluga y Moncada.

Kitchen

Dolores' traditional cuisine is always a big surprise for visitors, an explosion of intense flavors that alludes to complex dishes featuring classic local produce. We will especially highlight here artichokes, which are the main ingredient, homemade sausages and salted fish, as well as very sophisticated desserts created using traditional recipes. The proximity of the Mediterranean coast allows you to taste many fish and seafood: sea bream, grouper, shrimps and sardines. We can try gray berweons and eels in the El Hondo nature reserve.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Platja dels Tossals, about 14 km by road, around 18 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: Colegio Público Virgen de los Dolores, about 300 m.
Private school: Colegio Concertado Santa María de la Huerta, about 4 km, around 6 minutes by car.
International school: Colegio Noruego Den Norsken Skolen, about 12 km, around 12 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is the local public health centre (Centro de Salud), about 5 km away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario Vega Baja: Urgencias, about 13 km by road, around 14 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Hiperber, about 800 m away.

How far is the airport?

Alicante-Elche Airport is about 44 km by road, around 35 minutes by car. Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 75 km, around 55 minutes by car.
