Elite World Estates presents modern homes located in the peaceful town of Roldán, in the Region of Murcia. This is an excellent opportunity for those looking for a comfortable property in Spain that combines a functional layout, private outdoor space, and convenient access to the beaches of the Mar Menor, golf courses, and all the amenities needed for everyday living.

Each home has been thoughtfully designed on a single level, offering maximum comfort and an efficient use of space. The interior features a bright open-plan living and dining area with a modern kitchen, two comfortable bedrooms, and two fully equipped bathrooms. Large windows allow plenty of natural light to fill the home while seamlessly connecting the living area with the terrace and private garden.

Outside, each property offers a private swimming pool, off-road parking on the plot, and a spacious rooftop solarium with a summer kitchen—an ideal place to relax, sunbathe, and enjoy time with family and friends throughout the year. The development has been designed to modern standards, offering high-quality finishes and energy-efficient solutions.

The location provides easy access to renowned golf courses, shops, restaurants, schools, recreational facilities, and Murcia International Airport. The sandy beaches of both the Mar Menor and the Mediterranean Sea are just a short drive away, making these homes an excellent choice as a holiday residence, a rental investment, or a permanent home.

Location

The property is situated in the peaceful town of Roldán, in the Region of Murcia, on the sunny Costa Cálida. It is an ideal location for those seeking tranquillity and comfort while enjoying easy access to beaches, golf courses, and all essential services. The town centre offers supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, pharmacies, schools, and sports facilities, making it perfect for both holiday living and year-round residence.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to renowned courses such as La Torre Golf Resort and Terrazas de La Torre Golf Resort, while the beaches of the Mar Menor and the Mediterranean Sea are only around 15–20 minutes away by car. Murcia International Airport is approximately 25 minutes from the development, while Alicante Airport can be reached in just over an hour. Excellent motorway connections also provide easy access to the cities of Cartagena, Murcia, and Los Alcázares.
