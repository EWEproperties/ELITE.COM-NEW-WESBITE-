Location

Mijas is one of the largest towns in the province of Malaga, consisting of 3 different districts: Mijas Pueblo, Mijas Costa and Mijas la Cala. The most popular, attracting numerous tourists and fans of Andalusian white villages, are Mijas Pueblo.

It is a picturesque town located several kilometers from the coast, on the side of the well-known town of Fuengirola. The nearest metropolis is Malaga, approximately 35 km away. It is a similar distance from the city of Marbella. Mijas is about half an hour by car from Malaga airport.

Mijas la Cala offers many interesting pubs and restaurants where you can refresh yourself with cool sangria or tapas, or eat typical Andalusian dishes, such as gazpacho or seafood dishes. However, La Cale de Mijas does not live only in Andalusian cuisine. You will also find Greek and Chinese restaurants here, as well as one Thai and several Indian restaurants.

One of the more popular places in La Cala de Mijas is Legends Bar La Cala. You can not only drink good drinks and eat something nice here, but above all, you can spend a truly fantastic evening listening to live music. It is an intimate place where thematic events are often held. The place has good opinions among tourists and it is worth stopping by at least for a moment.

In Mijas, it is worth visiting several observation decks, which offer a beautiful view of the coast and the town itself. One of the most famous is located on Avenida del Compas, right next to the sanctuary of the patron saint of Mijas. The second point with a panorama can be found in the Gardens on the Walls (Jardin de las Murallas).

Another unusual attraction of the town is the Mayan Monkey Mijas Chocolate Factory, called the smallest chocolate factory in the world, at Plaza Virgen de la Peña 15. In addition to producing chocolate from natural, local products, it is also famous for its workshops where you can produce your own chocolate.
