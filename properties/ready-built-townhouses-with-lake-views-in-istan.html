An exceptional residential development located in the hills of Istán, near Marbella, surrounded by mountains and Mediterranean nature. Its elevated position provides beautiful panoramic views across the Costa del Sol coastline, the Mediterranean Sea, the surrounding mountains and La Concepción reservoir.

The development comprises modern homes featuring elegant contemporary architecture that blends naturally into the mountainous landscape. The properties are arranged over several levels, offering spacious and bright interiors, private terraces and outdoor areas designed for relaxation.

At the heart of each home is a spacious living and dining area with an open-plan kitchen. Large windows provide plenty of natural light and create a seamless connection between the interior, terrace and garden. Modern kitchens, light colour schemes, wood accents and carefully selected materials give the interiors an elegant and timeless character.

The bedrooms have been designed with comfort and privacy in mind, while the modern bathrooms feature spacious walk-in showers and high-quality finishes. Generous terraces make the most of the exceptional setting, allowing residents to enjoy the beautiful views throughout the year.

One of the main advantages of the development is its peaceful setting and proximity to nature. At the same time, the location provides convenient access to Nueva Andalucía, Puerto Banús and Marbella, combining the privacy of a mountain setting with easy access to beaches, restaurants, golf courses and the extensive amenities of the Costa del Sol.

This is an attractive choice both as a holiday home in Spain and as a permanent residence for those looking for space, nature and spectacular views close to Marbella.

Estimated completion date: 4 November 2026.

Location

The development is situated in Istán, in a picturesque mountainous area of the Costa del Sol, close to Marbella. Its elevated position offers exceptional views of the surrounding mountains, La Concepción reservoir and the Mediterranean coastline.

The location combines peace and proximity to nature with convenient access to some of the most renowned destinations in the Marbella area. Nueva Andalucía, Puerto Banús, beaches, golf courses, restaurants and a wide range of amenities are all within a short drive.

It is an ideal location for those who wish to live in a peaceful, natural environment while remaining close to the vibrant lifestyle of Marbella and the Costa del Sol.
