This luxurious detached villa with private pool is situated on a large plot of over 530 m2 in a beautiful, safe residential area in Quesada.

Live or relax in a house with an excellent location. Your new home is within walking distance of Quesada town center which has many amenities including a supermarket, pharmacy, shops, bars and restaurants.

This modern villa with 4 bedrooms and 5 bathrooms is distinguished by high quality workmanship and an attractive price. It has a private swimming pool measuring 10 x 3 meters and a spacious terrace perfect for warm days. The property has a beautiful open plan living room with patio doors leading out to the garden. It has a modern kitchen equipped with household appliances, including an oven, hob, hood, fridge-freezer, washing machine, dishwasher and microwave. On the same level there is a dining area and a guest bathroom.

The large and fully developed basement area, accessible by lift or stairs, comprises a double garage, a large bedroom with built-in wardrobes and an en-suite bathroom, and a further double bedroom with fitted wardrobes and an en-suite bathroom. Additionally, you will find utility rooms here.

The stairs lead to the first floor where there is a beautiful master bedroom with built-in wardrobes, en-suite bathroom and private solarium. On the same level there is another double bedroom with built-in wardrobes, en-suite bathroom and terrace.

On the roof you will find a double private solarium where you can enjoy the beautiful open view and sun all day long.

Location

Ciudad Quesada is located on the south of the Costa Blanca, just 8 km north of the main city, Torrevieja. Nearby towns are Rojales and San Fulgencio. You can get to Ciudad Quesada straight from the nearby airport, where you will find many connections. The city is just a 30-minute drive from Alicante Airport, which is well connected to major cities in Europe. Come and experience the best of the southern Costa Blanca in this vibrant city.

Beaches

The city is a short distance from golden sandy beaches, including El Moncayo en Guerdanar del Segura, and is surrounded by wonderful natural parks and picturesque landscapes.

Laguna La Mata

Lovers of nature and peace like nothing else than to grab a backpack, put on sports or comfortable shoes and hit the road. In Ciudad Quesada you can go on a walking tour of amazing places. There are many hiking trails, but one of the most captivating for nature lovers and those who enjoy bird watching is to visit La Mata Lagoon.

Golf

Golf enthusiasts can enjoy the prestigious La Marquesa Golf Club, which offers breathtaking views of the surrounding area. Meanwhile, nature lovers can visit the nearby salt lakes of Torrevieja, which are home to many species of birds. For those who want .water.

History

The city has a rich history dating back to the early 18th century and has become a vibrant community with a unique mix of Spanish, British and other European cultures. In Ciudad Quesada you can experience the city's rural past in its charming old town, while the modern and cosmopolitan area around the city center reflects its more recent development.

Beauty ritual and relaxation

Another plan not to miss in Ciudad Quesada is to go to the spa and let someone take care of your well-being for the morning, afternoon or all day. In the spa of Hotel la Laguna you can enjoy the so-called "Sensory Circuit", thanks to which you will gain access to a wellness zone full of contrasts of hot and cold, a relaxation zone and a saltwater jacuzzi, where you can work on all your senses through various areas: sight, smell, taste, touch and hearing. To relieve tension, you can have a bamboo or chocolate massage or take part in anti-stress therapy. You will leave here feeling relaxed like never before. The spa at Hotel La Laguna has an important advantage: you don't have to be a hotel guest to use its facilities.
