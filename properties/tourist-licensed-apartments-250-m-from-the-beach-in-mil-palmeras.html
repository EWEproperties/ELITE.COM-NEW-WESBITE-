Elite World Estates proudly presents an exclusive boutique development of 24 luxury tourist apartments located in one of the most desirable areas of the southern Costa Blanca – Mil Palmeras, just next to Torre de la Horadada. This is an excellent opportunity for buyers looking for an investment property in Spain that combines premium finishes, an exceptional location just a short walk from the beach, and outstanding short-term rental potential.

This intimate residential development consists of 24 fully furnished tourist apartments, each offering 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom, distributed across a contemporary six-storey building. Buyers can choose from ground-floor apartments with spacious terraces, apartments on the upper floors, and exclusive penthouses featuring large private rooftop solariums with panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea.

The apartments offer living areas ranging from 71 to 81 m², while the outdoor terraces vary from 19 up to an impressive 146 m², providing generous outdoor living space to enjoy the Mediterranean climate all year round.

Each apartment is delivered fully finished, furnished, and ready to move into or rent out immediately. The standard specification includes air conditioning, complete furniture package, fully equipped kitchen with premium appliances, electric blinds, video intercom system, and high-quality porcelain flooring. Every property also includes a private underground parking space and a storage room.

Residents will enjoy beautifully designed communal facilities, including a swimming pool with a sunbathing area, landscaped Mediterranean gardens, an elegant reception area, and an exclusive residents' lounge, creating the perfect environment for both holidays and year-round living.

One of the development's greatest advantages is its unbeatable location, just 200–250 metres from the sandy beach of Mil Palmeras. Restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, shops, the beachfront promenade, and all essential amenities are within easy walking distance. Several renowned golf courses, marinas, and shopping centres are also only a short drive away.

Thanks to their official tourist apartment status, premium specifications, and complete furnishing package, these properties represent an outstanding opportunity both as a holiday home on the Mediterranean coast and as a high-yield investment for the holiday rental market.

Estimated completion date: 1 April 2027

Location

The development is located in Mil Palmeras, one of the most prestigious and peaceful seaside resorts on the southern Costa Blanca, within the municipality of Pilar de la Horadada. The area is renowned for its wide Blue Flag sandy beaches, crystal-clear waters, and Mediterranean climate with more than 300 days of sunshine each year.

The apartments are situated only 200–250 metres from Mil Palmeras Beach, allowing residents to reach the sea within just a few minutes on foot. Nearby you will find a wide selection of restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, pharmacies, shops, and a beautiful seaside promenade connecting Mil Palmeras with Torre de la Horadada, perfect for walking and cycling throughout the year.

Golf enthusiasts and active lifestyle lovers will appreciate the proximity of several renowned golf courses, marinas, cycling routes, and a wide range of water sports. The popular La Zenia Boulevard Shopping Centre, with over 150 shops, restaurants, and leisure facilities, is only a short drive away.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de las Mil Palmeras, about 500 m by road.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: CEIP Martín Artigot, about 4 km, around 6 minutes by car.
International school: British School (San Miguel de Salinas), about 11 km, around 15 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?

The nearest health centre is Pilar de Horadada Mil Palmeras Consultorio, about 300 m away. The nearest hospital is Hospital Universitario de Torrevieja, about 15 km by road, around 16 minutes by car.

Where are the nearest shops and supermarket?

The nearest supermarket is Supersaura, about 500 m away.

How far is the airport?

Murcia International Airport (Corvera) is about 42 km by road, around 30 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche Airport is about 79 km, around 60 minutes by car.
