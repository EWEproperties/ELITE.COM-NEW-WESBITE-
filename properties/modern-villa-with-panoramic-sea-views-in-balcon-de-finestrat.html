Situated in a picturesque neighbourhood of Balcón de Finestrat surrounded by mountains, natural greenery and sea views , this modern villa offers a range of amenities designed to meet your needs and enhance your living experience. Live in a magical place, a short drive (about 10 minutes) from the golf course and the beach, which offers its residents an exclusive lifestyle in close proximity to nature.

The design of this unique villa is characterized by a subdued, built-up front, emphasizing the balanced form of the building, and an open, glass facade on the sea side, visually enlarging the space. The main entrance to this modern villa is located at the bottom of the plot, with ample parking for two cars.

It is a modern villa with a contemporary style, with a clean and elegant design, with a layout oriented towards the Mediterranean light that permeates the entire interior of the house. With open living areas where the kitchen connects to the living room, creating a continuous environment bounded by a central island, it creates an amazing space for dining every day. The living-dining room connects to the veranda and pool terrace, creating one space.

Bedrooms create a quiet and peaceful space for rest and sleep. All are equipped with built-in wardrobes that provide plenty of storage space. The bedrooms and bathrooms are spacious and bright thanks to the orientation and large windows letting in plenty of natural light.

The swimming pool in shades of aquamarine and covered with modern, top-quality tiles will certainly make your hot days more pleasant. On your private terrace there is also a barbecue area, configurable according to the user's needs, with access from the paved side through the natural terrain.

Location

Finestrat is a town in the province of Alicante that is located just 8 km from the popular, large city of Benidorm, with a very quiet area that makes it an ideal place to live. The city is located 40 kilometers from the picturesque Alicante and its international airport. Known for its stunning mountain views, pristine beaches and rich cultural heritage, Finestrat is the perfect destination for anyone looking for a relaxing and unforgettable experience.

Moreover, it is very close to shopping centers, golf courses, hotels and SPAs. The good location makes it a growing area and makes Finestrat an ideal city for real estate investment. Finestrat is situated on the slopes of Puig Campana, with stunning views of the mountains, coast and Mediterranean Sea.

Cala Finestrat is a seaside resort located just three kilometers from busy Benidorm. The resort offers adequate facilities, a large selection of restaurants, bars and shops. Everything you need is available in Benidorm, which is easily accessible by bus and taxi. La Cala Beach is quite quiet, as most people looking for a more upbeat atmosphere head to the beaches surrounding Benidorm. The beach is a clean stretch of golden sand, perfect for children and safe for swimming. There are several lovely restaurants along the promenade, and there is an arts and crafts market in the afternoons during the summer months.

Nearby there are the Terre Mitica and Terra Natura theme parks, the Aqualandia water park and the Mundomar marine and exotic animal park, which offer fun for the whole family. However, if you prefer to spend your holiday with a round or two of golf, Finestrat will be pleased with the nearby Real Faula Golf Club, which offers two excellent golf courses.

Both Finestrat and Cala Finestrat are quiet destinations compared to some of the neighboring resorts. Both offer a selection of local bars and restaurants where you can enjoy good food and a relaxed atmosphere.
