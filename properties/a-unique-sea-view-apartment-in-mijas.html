Location

Mijas is a charming town located on the Costa del Sol which, thanks to its unique position, remains free from high-rise buildings and large-scale resort developments. Much of the town is set along the slopes of the surrounding mountains, creating a picturesque layout of whitewashed houses. The views — especially from the sea — are truly spectacular. Surrounded by fragrant pine forests, Mijas offers stunning panoramas of the Mediterranean, providing an unforgettable living experience.

Mijas Costa is particularly popular among international buyers thanks to its excellent microclimate, renowned golf courses and proximity to Málaga Airport, ensuring convenient travel connections. The airport is just 20 minutes to the east, while world-class destinations such as Marbella and Puerto Banús are only 20 minutes to the west. Nearby golf courses include Mijas Golf, Santana Golf and El Chaparral.

In Mijas, it is worth visiting several viewpoints offering breathtaking views of both the coastline and the town itself. One of the most well-known is located along Avenida del Compás, next to the sanctuary of Mijas’ patron saint. Another beautiful viewpoint can be found at the Gardens of the Walls (Jardín de las Murallas).

A unique attraction in the town is the so-called smallest chocolate factory in the world — Mayan Monkey Mijas Chocolate Factory, located at Plaza Virgen de la Peña 15. In addition to producing chocolate from natural, local ingredients, it is famous for its workshops where visitors can create their own chocolate.
