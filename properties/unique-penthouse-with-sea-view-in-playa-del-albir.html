Brand new project of modern apartments located in Playa Albir, l'Alfas del Pi (Alicante), offers an exceptional environment for both permanent residence and holiday-making. Occupying a fully consolidated environment, complex is situated on a 30,000 square meter plot in the prime location of Albir.

Apartment's distinguishing feature is its capacious ground floor gardens and extensive terraces, ranging from 20 to over 130 square meters, that provide stunning views of the sea and Sierra Helada Natural Park.

Apartments comes with open plan living and kitchen area which is spacious and comfortable area designed for relaxation and entertainment. Location

Albir is a picturesque coastal town situated in the province of Alicante, Spain. It is a popular destination for tourists seeking to enjoy the warm Mediterranean climate, stunning scenery, and relaxed way of life. Albir is surrounded by natural beauty, with the Sierra Helada Natural Park to the north and the Mediterranean Sea to the south.

Albir is located approximately 60 kilometers from Alicante Airport, which is the nearest international airport to the town. The journey from the airport to Albir takes approximately 45-50 minutes by car, depending on traffic conditions. There are several transportation options available, including rental cars, taxis, and public buses, which provide easy access to and from the airport.

Albir is home to some of the most beautiful beaches in the Costa Blanca region. Racó de l'Albir Beach in l'Alfàs del Pi is an exceptional vantage point from which to admire the beauty of the Mediterranean Sea. Located adjacent to the famous Paseo de las Estrellas, this beach exudes a cinematic aura, reminiscent of the annual film festival hosted here. Adding to the idyllic ambiance, a bustling craft market operates during the summer months, bringing vibrant color and liveliness to the area. As an urban beach, a plethora of dining options are available, providing visitors with a diverse selection of refreshments, from chilled drinks to steaming coffee.

Things to Do or Visit: Albir has plenty of attractions for visitors to enjoy. The town is famous for its lively Sunday market, where locals and tourists alike can browse through a wide variety of goods, including handmade crafts, fresh produce, and clothing. The town also has several art galleries and museums, including the Museo al Aire Libre, which features a collection of outdoor sculptures and artwork.

This charming seaside town of Albir is graced by the majestic Sierra Helada mountain range, which serves as a stunning natural backdrop and demarcates the town from its larger neighbor, Benidorm. The Sierra Helada range is part of a natural park that spans over 5,600 hectares and is now a highly protected nature reserve - the first maritime land park in the Valencian Community.

Finally, Albir is renowned for its food and wine culture. The town has a wide variety of restaurants and bars, serving traditional Spanish cuisine and locally produced wines. Whether you're looking for a romantic dinner for two or a lively night out with friends, Albir has something to offer everyone.
