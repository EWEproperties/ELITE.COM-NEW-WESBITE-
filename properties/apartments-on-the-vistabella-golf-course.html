Location

Located along the magnificent Costa Blanca coastline, Orihuela Costa is a picturesque region in the province of Alicante, Spain, offering a harmonious combination of sun, sea and relaxation. Despite its name, Orihuela Costa is located about 20 kilometres from the main town of Orihuela, near the border with Murcia and close to the famous La Manga and the picturesque Mar Menor.

This charming location is home to the captivating Vistabella golf course, a paradise for golf enthusiasts seeking both natural beauty and a challenging game. Set against a backdrop of Mediterranean landscapes, this 18-hole course promises an unforgettable experience for golfers of all levels. With well-equipped facilities, including practice areas and clubhouse amenities, Vistabella serves not only as a premier golfing destination but also as a vibrant community hub where players gather to indulge their passion for the game.

Beyond the greens, Orihuela Costa tempts with 16 kilometres of varied coastline, making it the perfect destination for a family beach holiday. But it's not just about sun and sand – this region caters to all needs, offering a wealth of amenities, facilities and leisure activities.

From the lively coastal resorts of Punta Prima, Playa Flamenca and La Zenia to the tranquil charm of Cabo Roig, Orihuela Costa offers a range of seaside experiences to suit every preference. Each of these locations boasts Blue Flag beaches, stunning mountain views and a range of leisure options, from water sports to golf, ensuring an unforgettable holiday experience.

For those seeking a taste of authentic Spanish culture, the charming town of Campoamor offers green forests, pristine beaches and a peaceful atmosphere, while the prestigious Villamartin golf resort exudes the charm of a Mediterranean village, complete with luxury amenities and two 18-hole golf courses. -hole golf courses.

Further south, the inviting seaside resort of Mil Palmeras captivates with its sandy beaches, rocky coves and a wealth of water sports opportunities, complementing Orihuela Costa's appeal as a premier destination for leisure and recreation.

With three renowned golf courses – Villamartin, Campoamor and Las Ramblas – Orihuela Costa is a golfer's paradise, inviting enthusiasts to play in stunning surroundings and warm Spanish hospitality. Whether you are looking for sun-drenched beaches, championship golf courses or simply a peaceful escape, Orihuela Costa offers a slice of paradise on the Costa Blanca.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the nearest beach?

The nearest beach is Playa de Los Náufragos, about 18 km by road, around 18 minutes by car.

Which schools are nearby?

Public school: IES Benejúzar, about 16 km, around 20 minutes by car.
Private school: Colegio San José de Calasanz, about 10 km, around 13 minutes by car.

Where is the nearest hospital or health centre?
