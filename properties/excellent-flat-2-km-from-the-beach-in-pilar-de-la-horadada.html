Location

Pilar de la Horadada has everything you need to enjoy the sea, pine forests and Spanish sun. The coastline of Pilar de la Horadada is over 4 kilometres long and offers a wide variety of scenery. There are wide beaches with fine white sand, small stone ledges suspended over the water and small, intimate, hidden coves with golden sand.

The resort is located in the south-eastern part of Spain and borders Murcia (about 15 minutes away). Alicante is about a 40-minute drive away.

The sea here is usually calm, the beaches are safe, and there are no steep slopes, which allows even the smallest children to swim safely. In addition, the clear water and excellent visibility invite divers to observe the beauty of aquatic life.

The cleanliness of the beaches in Pilar de la Hordada has been recognised with the European Union's Blue Flag, which guarantees a healthy environment.

Inland, there are various interesting excursions to be had near the towns of Sierra and Escalona. There are two marked hiking trails along the Rio Seco riverbed, 19 kilometres long, which are definitely worth visiting. One of the routes is 4 kilometres long, while the other is 10 kilometres long, stretching from the natural area of Pilar de Campoverde to the mouth of the Mil Palmeras beach.

Not far from the town of Pilar de la Horadada, there is also an 18-hole golf course in Lo Romero. Both professionals and beginners will feel at home there.

Gastronomy has a privileged place in the culture of this beautiful town. Typical dishes of Pilar de la Horadada include potatoes with meat stew, meatballs, rabbit in garlic and Gachas Migas (a flour and garlic pancake), as well as excellent rice dishes and soups. Sweets and cakes also deserve a mention here, with confectionery such as meringue cake called ‘milhojas’ or almond sweets called ‘cordiales’ being the most popular.
