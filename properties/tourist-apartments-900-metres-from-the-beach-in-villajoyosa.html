Location – Villajoyosa

Villajoyosa is one of the most picturesque towns on the Costa Blanca, located between Benidorm and El Campello. The town is famous for its colourful fishermen’s houses, beautiful beaches and authentic Spanish atmosphere that is difficult to find in larger tourist resorts.

The town offers more than 3 kilometres of sandy beaches and charming coves with crystal-clear waters. Along the coastline, you will find numerous restaurants, cafés and promenades where you can enjoy the Mediterranean lifestyle throughout the year.

Villajoyosa is also renowned for its chocolate-making tradition. It is home to the famous Valor chocolate factory, one of Spain’s most iconic brands. The town remains lively throughout the year thanks to its cultural events, excellent gastronomy and well-developed infrastructure.

The combination of beautiful beaches, authentic character, excellent location and Mediterranean climate has made Villajoyosa one of the most sought-after destinations for property buyers on the Costa Blanca.
