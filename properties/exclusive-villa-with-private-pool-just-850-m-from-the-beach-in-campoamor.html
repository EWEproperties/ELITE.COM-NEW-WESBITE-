Elite World Estates proudly presents an exceptional development consisting of only two luxury villas located in the prestigious area of Dehesa de Campoamor on Orihuela Costa. This unique project is designed for the most discerning buyers seeking modern architecture, privacy, and an outstanding location close to the beach and golf courses.

The development is situated just 850 metres from the sandy Playa de La Glea beach, allowing residents to reach the Mediterranean Sea in approximately 10 minutes on foot.

This boutique project comprises two independent premium villas set on spacious plots exceeding 700 m². The properties have been designed to perfectly combine elegant design, functionality, and the Mediterranean lifestyle.

Each villa offers approximately 245–247 m² of contemporary living space distributed over two levels. The standard layout includes 3 spacious bedrooms, 3 elegant bathrooms, and 2 additional guest toilets. The master suite is conveniently located on the ground floor, while the remaining bedrooms are situated on the upper level, ensuring privacy and comfort.

The heart of each home is an impressive open-plan living area of more than 50 m², combining a spacious lounge, dining area, and modern kitchen. Large floor-to-ceiling windows fill the interiors with natural light and create a seamless connection between the indoor and outdoor living spaces.

Outside, residents can enjoy a private swimming pool with LED lighting and a dedicated hydromassage area inspired by a jacuzzi. The beautifully landscaped garden with a pergola provides the perfect setting for relaxation and outdoor living throughout the year.

An additional highlight is the spacious private rooftop solarium featuring a summer kitchen and BBQ area. From here, owners can enjoy beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding area while experiencing complete privacy and spectacular sunsets.

The villas are finished to an exceptional standard and include zoned air conditioning powered by an aerothermal system, Bosch kitchen appliances, large-format porcelain flooring, advanced thermal and acoustic insulation, and an automatic entrance gate with video intercom.

One of the greatest advantages of this development is its location. Playa de La Glea beach and the marina are only around 850 metres away. Restaurants, shops, services, and renowned golf courses, including Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, are located nearby. Alicante and Murcia airports can be reached in approximately 45 minutes by car.

This is a unique opportunity for those looking for a modern luxury villa in one of the most prestigious locations on the southern Costa Blanca.

Expected completion date: December 2026 or early 2027.

Location – Dehesa de Campoamor

Dehesa de Campoamor is one of the most prestigious and exclusive residential areas on the southern Costa Blanca, located within Orihuela Costa in the province of Alicante. The area is renowned for its elegant villas, wide sandy beaches, and exceptional Mediterranean climate, offering more than 300 days of sunshine per year.

Campoamor perfectly combines tranquillity, privacy, and a luxurious lifestyle. Surrounded by greenery, parks, and natural coastal landscapes, it also provides quick access to all essential amenities. Residents can enjoy a wide selection of restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, shops, a marina, and sports facilities.

One of Campoamor’s greatest attractions is its beautiful coastline featuring fine golden sand and crystal-clear Mediterranean waters. Playa de La Glea, Aguamarina, and Campoamor beaches are considered among the finest on the Orihuela Costa.

Golf enthusiasts will appreciate the proximity to renowned golf courses such as Las Colinas Golf & Country Club, Real Club de Golf Campoamor, and Villamartín Golf, all recognised among the best golf resorts in Spain.

Excellent transport links provide easy access to Alicante International Airport in approximately 45 minutes and Murcia Airport in less than 40 minutes. Thanks to its prime location, excellent infrastructure, and proximity to the sea, Campoamor remains one of the most desirable places to live and invest on the Costa Blanca.
