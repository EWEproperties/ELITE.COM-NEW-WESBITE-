Location

Welcome to Benijofar, a small town situated on the southern coast of the Costa Blanca, in the province of Alicante, Spain. This charming town is renowned for its friendly residents, traditional Spanish architecture and idyllic setting, surrounded by citrus groves and vegetable fields.

Location and history

Benijofar is situated a short distance from the coastal towns of Guardamar del Segura and Torrevieja, and just 30 minutes from Alicante International Airport. The town has a rich history dating back to Moorish times, and traces of its past are still visible in the old town, with its narrow streets, whitewashed houses and beautiful church.

Things to do

Despite being a small town, Benijofar has plenty to do and see. The town also boasts many bars and restaurants serving traditional Spanish cuisine as well as international dishes.

If you’re interested in outdoor activities, you can take a stroll along the River Segura, which flows through the town, or go on an excursion or a picnic to the nearby La Mata Nature Park. Golf enthusiasts will be pleased to know that there are several golf courses within easy reach of Benijofar, including the famous La Finca Golf Resort.

Beaches

Of course, no trip to the Costa Blanca would be complete without a visit to the beach. Benijofar is just a short drive from some of the best beaches in the area, including those at Guardamar and La Mata.
