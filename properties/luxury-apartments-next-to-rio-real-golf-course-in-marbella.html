Elite World Estates presents an exceptional residential development located in one of the most prestigious areas of Costa del Sol – the exclusive Río Real district in eastern Marbella. This boutique project has been designed for discerning buyers seeking luxury, privacy, and an outstanding location close to the sea, golf course, and city center.

The development consists of only 16 exclusive apartments with 2 and 3 bedrooms, ensuring a private atmosphere and a high level of comfort. The contemporary architecture blends harmoniously with the surrounding landscape, while the spacious interiors have been carefully designed with attention to every detail. Large floor-to-ceiling windows provide abundant natural light and offer panoramic views of the golf course and Mediterranean surroundings.

Buyers can choose from elegant apartments with spacious terraces, ground-floor residences with private gardens, and spectacular penthouses featuring terraces of up to 200 m² and private swimming pools. Each property has been thoughtfully designed to maximize space, functionality, and everyday comfort.

Residents will enjoy access to premium communal facilities including an outdoor swimming pool with sun deck, a modern spa, sauna, and a fully equipped gym. Each apartment also includes underground parking spaces and private storage rooms.

One of the project's greatest advantages is its exceptional location. The complex is situated within the prestigious Río Real Golf community, just a few minutes from Marbella town center and beautiful sandy beaches, including the renowned Playa de Los Monteros. The development is located next to the famous Río Real Golf Course and the future Four Seasons Marbella Resort, further enhancing the prestige and investment potential of the area.

This is an ideal opportunity for those seeking an exclusive apartment in one of the most desirable locations on Costa del Sol, whether as a permanent residence or a high-potential investment.

Expected completion date: Q2 2028.

Location: Marbella – The Luxury Capital of Costa del Sol

Marbella is one of the most prestigious and internationally recognized destinations on Spain’s Costa del Sol. For decades, the city has attracted those seeking an exceptional lifestyle, outstanding climate, and luxury properties surrounded by the sea, golf courses, and Mediterranean nature.

With more than 320 days of sunshine per year, wide sandy beaches, elegant promenades, and world-class infrastructure, Marbella is one of Europe's most sought-after locations for living and investing. The city is home to prestigious golf courses, fine dining restaurants, luxury hotels, renowned international schools, and modern shopping centers.

At the heart of Marbella lies its charming Old Town, with picturesque streets, traditional restaurants, and the famous Plaza de los Naranjos. Lovers of luxury living will appreciate Puerto Banús, home to exclusive yachts, designer boutiques, and sophisticated beach clubs.

Marbella also enjoys excellent transport connections to Málaga Airport, located approximately 40 minutes away by car. Combining luxury, security, quality of life, and a stable property market, Marbella remains one of the most attractive places to purchase real estate on the Costa del Sol.
