Your new Spanish residence is located in one of the most beautiful areas of the Costa Blanca - south, in the picturesque village of Guardamar del Segura. This modern development has been built in a beautiful location right next to Las Lagunas de La Mata National Park, a protected natural area, an ideal place for relaxation and hiking.

It is a modern residential complex consisting of 175 extremely spacious flats with 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and large terraces. The ground floor flats will have spacious gardens and the penthouses will have a private solarium on the roof of the building.

All flats will have an associated parking space in the underground car park. The flats have a modern finish and are built with the highest quality materials. They are ideal for those looking for a picturesque and unique place to live and relax.

A great asset of the development is the large, exclusive and extensive common areas, where you can relax in the large summer pool or the heated spa. Children will enjoy the large green park with playgrounds and adults will enjoy the gym with bio-healthy machines and ‘Putting Green’ golf.
