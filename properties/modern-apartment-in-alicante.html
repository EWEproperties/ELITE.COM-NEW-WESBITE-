Location

Located on Spain's southeastern coast, Alicante is a true gem of the Costa Blanca region, offering the perfect combination of natural beauty, rich history and a warm Mediterranean climate. Its magnificent beaches and tranquil Mediterranean waters provide the perfect setting for water sports and beachcombing, making it a favorite destination for locals and tourists alike.

Golf enthusiasts from around the world flock to Alicante for its famous courses, and Alicante Golf is one of the best. Located just a few kilometers from the city center, this 18-hole course was designed by Seve Ballesteros and offers breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea. It is known for its challenging layout and is a must-see for golf enthusiasts.

Alicante's rich history is evident in its many magnificent monuments, such as the Castle of St. Barbara. This historic fortress sits atop Benacantil Mountain and offers panoramic views of the city and the Mediterranean Sea. The castle is a testament to the many civilizations that settled in Alicante over the centuries, including Romans, Moors and Christians, and is a popular destination for history buffs and tourists.

When it comes to recreation, Alicante's coastline is undoubtedly a favorite of locals and tourists alike. The promenade Explanada de España, located across from the port, is a lively boulevard with palm trees, colorful flowers and charming street vendors. Here you can enjoy a cup of coffee or a delicious meal while taking in the magnificent views of the sea and the city.
