Property Prices in Spain 2026: Apartments and Luxury Villas

By Aleksandra Kobus · 11 April 2025 · updated 24 October 2026 · 4 min read

In short: In September 2026 the average asking price in Spain was about €2,930 per m² (idealista), almost 12% more than a year earlier. Prices differ hugely by place: Madrid is around €6,500 per m², Málaga city close to €4,000, and many Costa Blanca towns well below the national average. On the coast, a resale apartment can still be found for under €250,000, new sea-view apartments mostly cost €300,000 to €700,000, and luxury villas start around €1 million and go far beyond €5 million in Marbella. Budget another 10 to 13% for taxes and fees on top of the price.

"How much does a flat in Spain cost?" has no single answer. The same money buys a three-bedroom apartment with a pool in Torrevieja or a one-bedroom flat in central Marbella. Here are the current figures, what each budget buys, and the factors behind the price.

What drives the price

  • Location: the town, the neighbourhood, and above all the distance to the sea. Frontline and walking-distance-to-the-beach homes carry a clear premium.
  • Views and orientation: an open sea view and a south-facing terrace can add a large share to the price of the same apartment.
  • New or resale: new builds usually cost more per m² but come with modern insulation, a warranty and lower repair costs. See new build or resale.
  • Condition: a home to renovate is cheaper, but works easily cost several hundred to over a thousand euros per m².
  • Size and layout: bedrooms with their own bathrooms, a large terrace or a garden raise the price more than extra square metres alone.
  • Amenities: communal pools, gardens, gym, security, parking and storage. They also raise the community fees.
  • Surroundings: golf, marinas, schools, airports within an hour, and towns that are lively all year.

How much does a luxury villa cost?

On the Costa Blanca, a modern villa with a pool and sea views usually starts around €1 million in Jávea, Moraira, Benissa or Cumbre del Sol. Larger plots, frontline positions and designer architecture take prices to €3–5 million.

On the Costa del Sol, prices are higher. Villas in Estepona and Benahavís start around €1.5 million, and in Marbella's best areas, such as Sierra Blanca, the Golden Mile and La Zagaleta, prices of €5–20 million and more are common.

For what to check when buying at this level, see our guide to buying a luxury villa.

Why Spain is still good value

Even after strong rises, buyers from northern and western Europe usually get more for their money in Spain than on comparable coasts in France or Italy, or in their own big cities. The main reasons:

  • Large supply: Spain built a great deal in the 2000s, and prices fell sharply after 2008. The coast is still catching up from that fall.
  • Land and building costs: outside the big cities, land and construction are cheaper than in most of western Europe.
  • Local incomes: Spanish salaries are lower than in northern Europe, which limits how far prices can rise in areas bought mainly by locals.
  • Competition: many developers and a large resale market keep prices in check.

The gap is closing, though. Prices rose about 12% in the last year, and the best coastal locations have little new land to build on. Waiting for prices to fall has not paid off in recent years.

Rent or buy?

Rents on the coast have risen fast too, and good long-term rentals are hard to find. If you plan to spend several months a year in Spain for many years, buying usually works out cheaper than renting, and you can let the home while you are away. If you are not yet sure where you want to live, renting for a season first is a sensible way to test an area. For rental income figures, see our guide to buying to rent out.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an apartment cost in Spain?

The average asking price was about €2,930 per m² in September 2026. On the coast, resale apartments start below €250,000, and new sea-view apartments mostly cost €300,000 to €700,000.

How much does a luxury villa cost in Spain?

On the Costa Blanca, sea-view villas usually start around €1 million. On the Costa del Sol they start around €1.5 million, and Marbella's best addresses reach €5–20 million or more.

Are property prices in Spain still rising?

Yes. The INE index rose 12.2% in the year to the second quarter of 2026, and idealista asking prices were up almost 12% a year in September 2026.

What costs are added to the purchase price?

Roughly 10 to 13%: transfer tax or VAT and stamp duty, notary, land registry and legal fees.

Where is the cheapest coastal property?

On the southern Costa Blanca, around Torrevieja and Orihuela Costa, and inland from the coast.
