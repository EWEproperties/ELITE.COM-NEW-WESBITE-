The golf courses

Golf courses on the Costa Cálida and the homes near them

The Costa Cálida is the coast of the Region of Murcia, around the Mar Menor lagoon. It has more than 20 golf courses, most of them flat resort courses with lakes, a short drive from the beaches and from Región de Murcia airport at Corvera.

Prices are lower than on the Costa Blanca, so it is popular with buyers who want a villa with a private pool near golf for a moderate budget. Below, each golf course has the properties we sell nearest to it.

La Serena Golf · Roda Golf · La Torre Golf and Mar Menor Golf · Hacienda del Álamo Golf · El Valle Golf · La Manga Club

Golf course

La Serena Golf, Los Alcázares

18 holes · par 72 · by Manuel Piñero · opened 2006
La Serena Golf is a flat championship course on the edge of Los Alcázares, designed by the Spanish Ryder Cup player Manuel Piñero. Water comes into play on 16 of the 18 holes, so lakes are the main challenge. The new villas and apartments around the course are about 1 km from the beaches of the Mar Menor, and Los Alcázares has shops, restaurants and a promenade open all year.

Golf course

La Manga Club, Cartagena

3 courses × 18 holes · 560-hectare resort
La Manga Club is one of the best-known golf resorts in Spain: 560 hectares with three 18-hole courses. The North and South courses were designed by Robert Dean Putman, and the West course by Dave Thomas in 1996. The resort also has a hotel, tennis, football pitches and restaurants, and the beaches of the Mediterranean and La Manga are a few minutes away.

Questions

Golf properties on the Costa Cálida: questions and answers

How much does a golf property cost on the Costa Cálida?

Our golf properties on the Costa Cálida start at €207,900 for an apartment at Hacienda del Álamo. Most apartments cost between €250,000 and €400,000, and villas with a private pool from about €275,000 in Roldán up to about €720,000 at La Serena.

Which golf course is closest to the beach?

La Serena Golf in Los Alcázares and Roda Golf in San Javier are about 1 km from the Mar Menor. La Manga Club is a few minutes from the Mediterranean beaches.

What costs are there on top of the price?

In the Region of Murcia you pay 10% VAT and 1.5% stamp duty on a new build, or 7.75% transfer tax on a resale home. Add about 1.5–2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer. We give you the exact figure for the home you like.
