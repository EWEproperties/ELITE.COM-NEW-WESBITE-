Properties near the best golf courses on the Costa Blanca

Most golf resorts on the Costa Blanca are in the south, between Alicante and Murcia: Las Colinas, La Finca, Vistabella and La Marquesa. They have new villas and apartments, sunny weather all year and sandy beaches 10–20 minutes away.

Around Alicante itself, Bonalba, Font del Llop and Alenda put you close to the city and the airport. Further north, Puig Campana in Finestrat has mountain and sea views next to Benidorm.

Questions

Golf properties on the Costa Blanca: questions and answers

Which is the best golf resort on the Costa Blanca?

It depends on what you want. Las Colinas is the most exclusive and has won many awards. La Finca has a five-star hotel and lots of choice. Font del Llop and Bonalba are close to Alicante and the airport, and La Marquesa is inside a town with shops open all year.

How much does a golf property cost on the Costa Blanca?

Our golf properties on the Costa Blanca start at €260,900. Most new apartments cost between €270,000 and €450,000, villas with a private pool from about €350,000 in Vistabella to over €2 million in Las Colinas.

What costs are there on top of the price?

For a new build you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty. For a resale home you pay 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million). Add about 1.5–2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer. We give you the exact figure for the home you like.
