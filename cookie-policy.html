Cookie policy

This website does not use cookies.

  1. What cookies are
  2. Our website does not use cookies
  3. Other websites
  4. If this changes

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser, for example to remember your settings or to count visits. Similar technologies, such as local storage or tracking pixels, work in the same way.

2. Our website does not use cookies

This website does not store cookies or similar technologies in your browser, and it does not use statistics, advertising or social media trackers. That is why you do not see a cookie banner.

Our fonts are stored on our own server, so loading a page does not send your data to font services. The property photos come from the image servers of our property system (eGO Real Estate); they do not set cookies.

3. Other websites

When you follow a link to another site, such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or YouTube, that site may use its own cookies under its own policy.

4. If this changes

If we add website statistics, a map, a chat or any other tool that uses cookies, we will update this page with a list of the cookies, and ask for your consent before setting any cookie that is not strictly necessary, as Article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE requires.

You can always delete cookies and block them in your browser settings.

Questions? Write to info@eliteworldestates.com. More about how we use personal data is in our privacy policy.
