The course

Golf at Las Colinas Golf & Country Club

The 18-hole, par-71 championship course was designed by the American architect Cabell B. Robinson. It follows the natural contours of the valley, between hills, pine trees and open countryside.

Las Colinas has won many awards, including several World Golf Awards as Spain's best golf course. Players also have a golf academy, a grass practice area, a putting green and a 5,000 m² short-game area designed with Miguel Ángel Jiménez.

Living here

Why buy a property at Las Colinas Golf?

The resort is planned at low density, so homes have space, privacy and views. Inside the resort you find restaurants, a racquet club, a gym and wellness areas, and the resort's beach club sits on La Glea beach in Campoamor.

The beaches of Campoamor and Orihuela Costa are a short drive away, and both Alicante-Elche and Murcia-Corvera airports are within easy reach. It suits buyers looking for a quiet, high-end second home or a permanent home on the southern Costa Blanca.

Questions

Las Colinas Golf: questions and answers

How much does a property cost in Las Colinas?

Our properties in Las Colinas start at €480,000 for a new apartment. Penthouses with a private pool cost from about €550,000, and villas from about €1.09 million up to about €2.5 million.

Where is Las Colinas Golf?

In the municipality of Orihuela, inland from Campoamor on the southern Costa Blanca. The beaches of Campoamor and Orihuela Costa are a short drive away, and the resort is between Alicante-Elche and Murcia-Corvera airports.

What costs are there on top of the price?

For a new build you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty. For a resale home you pay 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million). Add about 1.5–2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer. We give you the exact figure for the home you like.
