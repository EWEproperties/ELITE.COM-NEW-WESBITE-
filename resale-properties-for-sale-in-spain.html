Why buy resale

See exactly what you are buying

  • Move in now

    No waiting two years for a build to finish. Most resale homes can be yours in weeks.

  • Established areas

    Mature gardens, open shops and neighbours who already live there all year round.

  • More space for the money

    Older homes often come with bigger plots and terraces than new builds at the same price.

  • Room to negotiate

    You deal with a private owner, not a price list, so there is often room on the price.

How we help

Every home is checked before you sign

  • We read the Nota Simple: who owns the home and whether there are mortgages or charges on it
  • We check that the home is legal and registered as it really is
  • We ask for unpaid community fees, IBI and utility bills to be settled before completion
  • We work with independent lawyers and stay with you until the notary
