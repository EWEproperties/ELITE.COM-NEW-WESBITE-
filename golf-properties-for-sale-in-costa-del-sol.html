Where to buy

The golf areas of the Costa del Sol

Mijas

La Cala Resort has three 18-hole courses in the hills behind La Cala de Mijas, and Calanova Golf is close by. New apartments and townhouses with golf views.

Marbella

Nueva Andalucía is known as the Golf Valley, with Los Naranjos, Las Brisas and Aloha. Apartments and villas close to Puerto Banús.

Benahavís

Inland from Marbella, with courses such as La Quinta and Los Arqueros. Large villas with sea views in the hills.

Estepona

Estepona Golf and El Paraíso Golf, with new developments between the courses and the sea. Good value compared with Marbella.

Casares

Finca Cortesín, which hosted the Solheim Cup in 2023, and Doña Julia Golf on Casares Costa. Apartments with sea and golf views.

Questions

Golf properties on the Costa del Sol: questions and answers

How much does a golf property cost on the Costa del Sol?

Our golf properties on the Costa del Sol start at €239,000 for a new apartment. Most apartments with golf or sea views cost between €350,000 and €900,000, and villas from about €950,000 to almost €7 million.

Which area is best for golf on the Costa del Sol?

Marbella's Golf Valley in Nueva Andalucía has the most courses close together. Mijas and Estepona have newer homes at lower prices, and Casares and Benahavís suit buyers who want space and views.

What costs are there on top of the price?

In Andalusia you pay 10% VAT and 1.2% stamp duty on a new build, or 7% transfer tax on a resale home. Add about 1.5–2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer. We give you the exact figure for the home you like.
