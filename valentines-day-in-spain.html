Valentine's Day in Spain: 5 Romantic Coastal Towns

By Lucyna Gacek · 3 February 2026 · updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: February is a lovely month for a romantic trip to the Spanish coast: mild, sunny days, quiet streets and restaurants open for locals. Our five favourites are Altea on the Costa Blanca, with its white old town and blue-domed church; Sitges near Barcelona; Cadaqués on the Costa Brava; Nerja and its Balcón de Europa on the Costa del Sol; and the white hill village of Mojácar in Almería. Spaniards celebrate San Valentín on 14 February with dinner and flowers, and in Catalonia lovers also exchange books and roses on Sant Jordi, 23 April.

Valentine's Day (San Valentín) in Spain is simple: a good dinner, flowers or a small gift, and time together. On the coast, February adds sunshine, empty beaches and long walks at sunset.

Here are five towns where romance comes naturally.

Altea – romance at a slower pace

Altea is often called one of the prettiest towns on the Costa Blanca. White houses, cobbled lanes and the blue-tiled dome of the church crown the old town, and the viewpoint by the church square looks out over the bay. In February the small restaurants of the old town are calm and welcoming. See our homes for sale in Altea.

Sitges – elegance by the sea

About 40 minutes from Barcelona, Sitges combines a seaside promenade, an old town and lively restaurants all year. A walk along Sant Sebastià beach to the church on the headland is the perfect start to the evening.

Cadaqués – quiet beauty on the Costa Brava

At the far end of the Costa Brava, Cadaqués is a whitewashed fishing village surrounded by rocky coast, once home to Salvador Dalí in nearby Portlligat. In winter it is peaceful and intimate, ideal for couples who want to switch off.

Nerja – light and open horizons

On the eastern Costa del Sol, Nerja's Balcón de Europa is a palm-lined terrace above the sea, perfect for a quiet moment before dinner. February is bright and mild in Andalusia, and the nearby caves and the white village of Frigiliana make a lovely day out. See our Costa del Sol top 10.

Mojácar – a white village above the sea

In Almería, Mojácar's white houses tumble down a hill a few kilometres from the beach. Its viewpoints are some of the best places in Spain to watch the sunset together. Nearby, see our homes in Vera.

How Spaniards celebrate love

San Valentín on 14 February is a normal working day, celebrated in the evening with dinner, flowers and small gifts; book restaurants in advance. In Catalonia the most romantic day is Sant Jordi on 23 April, when men traditionally give roses and women give books, and today couples exchange both.

Frequently asked questions

Is Valentine's Day celebrated in Spain?

Yes, on 14 February, with dinner, flowers and small gifts. It is not a public holiday.

What is the most romantic town on the Costa Blanca?

Many couples choose Altea, with its white old town, sea views and calm restaurants.

What is the weather like in Spain in February?

On the Mediterranean coast it is usually mild and sunny, around 17–18 °C during the day, with cool evenings.

What is Sant Jordi?

Catalonia's day of love on 23 April, when couples exchange roses and books.
