La Zagaleta – The Most Exclusive Place in Europe

By Lucyna Gacek · 1 August 2025 · updated 24 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: La Zagaleta is a private, gated estate of about 900 hectares in the hills of Benahavís, a short drive from Marbella and Puerto Banús. It has only detached villas on large plots, two private 18-hole golf courses, an equestrian centre and security around the clock. Homes there usually cost several million euros.

Marbella is famous for luxury, but one address in its hills stands above the rest. La Zagaleta is often called the most exclusive residential estate in Europe. Here is what it offers, how life there works and what to know if you are thinking of buying.

What is La Zagaleta?

La Zagaleta is a private estate in the municipality of Benahavís, on the Costa del Sol. It covers about 900 hectares of hills, woodland and valleys between the coast and the Serranía de Ronda, with views to the Mediterranean and, on clear days, to Gibraltar and Africa.

It was developed from the 1990s on a former hunting estate. From the start the idea was simple: very few homes, very large plots and plenty of nature between them. Even so, Marbella's centre and the marina of Puerto Banús are only about 20 minutes away by car.

The villas

La Zagaleta has only detached villas. There are no apartments or townhouses. Plots usually range from about 3,000 m² to more than 10,000 m², so neighbours are far apart and the views stay open.

Every villa is an individual design, many by well-known architects, from classic Andalusian estates to minimalist glass houses. Large homes often have a spa, cinema room, gym, wine cellar, staff quarters and garages for car collections. Prices usually start at several million euros and the largest estates cost far more.

Security and privacy

Privacy is the main reason many owners choose La Zagaleta. The estate has:

  • a fenced perimeter with guarded entrance gates,
  • a security team on duty 24 hours a day,
  • camera surveillance and patrols,
  • its own private roads, closed to the public.

Visitors can only enter if a resident has registered them. Together with the low density of homes, this gives a level of calm and discretion that is hard to find anywhere else on the coast.

Life inside the estate

Residents and their guests have access to:

  • two private 18-hole golf courses, both for members only,
  • a clubhouse with a restaurant,
  • an equestrian centre and riding trails,
  • tennis and padel courts,
  • walking and mountain-bike routes through the hills,
  • a helipad and concierge services, including care of the home while owners are away.

Outside the gates, the coast is close: Puerto Banús and Marbella in about 20 minutes, Ronda in about 45 minutes and Málaga airport in under an hour.

Is it a good investment?

Supply is very limited, because the number of plots is fixed and few villas come up for sale each year. At the same time, demand for the top end of the Marbella market has stayed strong. That combination has helped values hold up well over time.

Buying here is still a long-term decision. Running costs are high, from estate fees to staff and maintenance, and the pool of buyers for a home of this size is small. Get a clear picture of all costs before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

Where is La Zagaleta?

In the hills of Benahavís, on the Costa del Sol in Málaga province, about 20 minutes by car from Marbella and Puerto Banús.

How much does a villa in La Zagaleta cost?

Villas usually start at several million euros. The largest estates with sea views cost considerably more.

Can anyone visit La Zagaleta?

No. It is a private estate with guarded gates. Visitors need to be registered by a resident, and viewings of villas for sale are arranged through an agent.

What facilities does La Zagaleta have?

Two private 18-hole golf courses, a clubhouse, an equestrian centre, tennis and padel courts, walking trails, a helipad and 24-hour security.
