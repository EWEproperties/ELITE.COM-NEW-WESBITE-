The city

Living in Estepona

Estepona is a town of about 75,000 people on the western Costa del Sol, between Marbella and Gibraltar. Its old town is known as the garden of the Costa del Sol, with white streets full of flower pots, small squares and painted murals, and it has kept the feel of a real Andalusian town.

Along the coast runs a long promenade with sandy beaches such as La Rada and El Cristo, and a marina with restaurants. Inland rise the hills of the Sierra Bermeja, and between the sea and the mountains lie golf courses and new residential areas with views over the sea towards Gibraltar and Africa.

Estepona is lively all year, with shops, markets, international schools and a hospital. Málaga airport is about an hour away by car, Gibraltar about 45 minutes, and Marbella and Puerto Banús about 25 minutes. Prices are lower than in Marbella, which is why many buyers look here first.

Where to buy

The main areas of Estepona

Old town and centre

White streets with flowers, shops and restaurants, a few minutes' walk from La Rada beach. Apartments for living all year.

Seafront and marina

The promenade, the beaches and the marina. Apartments close to the sea, many with views over the water.

New Golden Mile

The coast between Estepona and San Pedro de Alcántara, with modern beachside complexes, pools and gardens.

Selwo and the hills

Newer developments on the slopes east of the town, with wide terraces and open sea views. Mostly apartments and penthouses.

Golf valley

The area around Estepona's golf courses, a few minutes inland. Apartments on the course and villas with a private pool.

West of Estepona

Quieter residential areas towards Casares and Manilva, with townhouses and villas, often for less money.

Budget

What it costs to buy in Estepona

On top of the price you pay tax and fees. For a new apartment at €560,000, for example, that is €56,000 VAT and €6,720 stamp duty, plus notary, land registry and legal fees. Taxes on resale homes are different, so always ask us for the exact figure for the home you like.

Questions

Buying property in Estepona: questions and answers

How much does an apartment cost in Estepona?

Our new apartments in Estepona start at €239,000 today. Most apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms we sell cost between €400,000 and €750,000, depending on the views, the terrace and the distance to the beach. Villas cost from about €730,000 for a semi-detached villa to €2.35 million on the golf course.

Estepona or Marbella: which is better?

Marbella has the Golden Mile, Puerto Banús and the most famous addresses, and higher prices. Estepona is calmer, with a real old town and more space for the money. Marbella is about 25 minutes away by car.

Is Estepona good for living all year?

Yes. Estepona is a real town where most people live all year, with shops, markets, international schools and a hospital, and a mild climate in winter.

How far is Estepona from the airport?

Málaga airport is about 85 km away, around an hour by car. Gibraltar airport is about 45 minutes away.

What costs are there on top of the price?

In Andalusia you pay 10% VAT and 1.2% stamp duty on a new build, and 7% transfer tax on a resale home. Notary, land registry and legal fees add about 1.5–2.5%.

Can foreigners buy property in Estepona?

Yes. Anyone can buy property in Spain. You need a Spanish NIE tax number and usually a Spanish bank account, and we help you with both. See our guide to the NIE number.
