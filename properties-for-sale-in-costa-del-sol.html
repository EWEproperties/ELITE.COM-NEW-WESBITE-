The coast

Life on the Costa del Sol

Marbella and its neighbours Benahavís and Nueva Andalucía are the luxury heart of the coast, with Puerto Banús, the Golden Mile and the Golf Valley. Prices here are the highest in southern Spain.

To the west, Estepona, Casares and Manilva offer newer developments, a calmer pace and better value, many with sea and golf views. To the east, Mijas, Fuengirola and Benalmádena are close to Málaga and its airport, with a busy town life all year.

Málaga airport has direct flights to most European cities all year, and the AP-7 and A-7 roads connect the whole coast.

Questions

Questions and answers

Where is the best place to buy on the Costa del Sol?

For luxury and prestige, Marbella, Benahavís and Nueva Andalucía. For newer homes and better value with sea views, Estepona and Casares. For easy access to Málaga and the airport, Mijas, Fuengirola and Benalmádena.

How much does a property cost on the Costa del Sol?

Our homes on the Costa del Sol start at €235,000 today. Most new apartments in Estepona, Mijas and Casares cost between €400,000 and €650,000, while most villas in Marbella and Benahavís cost between €3.4 and €8 million.

What taxes do I pay when I buy on the Costa del Sol?

On a new build you pay 10% VAT (IVA) and 1.2% stamp duty (AJD). On a resale home you pay 7% transfer tax (ITP). Add about 1.5 to 2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer.

Which airport is closest?

Málaga airport. Fuengirola and Mijas are about 20 to 30 minutes away, Marbella about 40 minutes and Estepona about an hour. Gibraltar airport is also close to Manilva and Sotogrande.
