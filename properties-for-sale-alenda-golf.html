The course

Golf at Alenda Golf

Alenda Golf is an established course set in the landscape of the Sierra de las Águilas. Besides the main course, the club has three putting greens and a practice hole called Hole 19, and floodlights let you practise after sunset.

The clubhouse has changing rooms, a pro shop and a café with views over the course. There is also a gym with fitness classes and outdoor padel courts.

Living here

Why buy a property at Alenda Golf?

Alenda is a lived-in community, not just a holiday resort: it has restaurants, a supermarket, a gym, padel and other services. Magno International School, a bilingual international school, is within walking distance of the homes.

It sits between Alicante and Elche, with good roads to both cities, the airport and the coast. That suits families and buyers who want a quiet golf setting close to a city.
