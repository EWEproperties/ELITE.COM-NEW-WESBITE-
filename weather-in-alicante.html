Weather in Alicante – Month by Month

By Lucyna Gacek · 10 March 2026 · updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: Alicante is one of the sunniest and driest cities in Europe, with about 2,850 hours of sunshine and only around 300 mm of rain a year. Winter days are usually 17–18 °C, summer days 30–31 °C with warm nights. The sea is warmest in August (about 27 °C). For beaches go June to September; for sightseeing and viewing homes, spring and autumn are ideal.

Alicante is often described as having 300 days of sunshine a year. The official numbers are almost as good: the Spanish weather agency AEMET records about 2,850 hours of sun and very little rain. But each season feels different, and the best month depends on whether you come for the beach, for walking or to look at homes. Here is what to expect, month by month.

Alicante's climate in brief

Alicante has a dry Mediterranean climate. The mountains inland shelter the coast, and the sea keeps winters mild and summers less extreme than in inland Spain.

  • Sunshine: about 2,850 hours a year, among the highest in Europe.
  • Average temperature: about 18 °C over the year.
  • Rain: around 300 mm a year, falling on roughly 37 days, mostly in autumn.
  • Frost and snow: almost never on the coast.

Inland towns such as Elche or Orihuela are a few degrees hotter in summer and cooler on winter nights. Further north, around Jávea and Dénia, the climate is a little greener and wetter.

Spring (March to May)

Days of 20–25 °C, fresh evenings and the countryside at its greenest. Almond trees flower inland from late January. The sea is still cool (14–18 °C), so swimming is for the brave, but it is a perfect time for walking, golf, cycling and city breaks.

Summer (June to September)

Hot, sunny and almost dry. Days reach 30–31 °C on the coast, but humidity can make them feel warmer, and nights in July and August often stay above 21 °C. The sea warms to about 25–27 °C. The beaches and promenades are busiest in July and August; June and September are calmer and just as warm.

Autumn (October and November)

Many residents' favourite season. October days are still around 25 °C and the sea stays warm enough to swim. This is also when most of the year's rain falls, often in short, heavy storms known as a DANA or gota fría. They usually pass within a day or two, but they are one reason to check flood maps before buying a home in a low-lying area.

Winter (December to February)

Mild and bright. Daytime temperatures of 17–18 °C mean lunch on a sunny terrace is normal in January, though nights drop to 6–7 °C. Homes on the coast are built for heat, not cold, so good insulation and a heat pump (air conditioning that also heats) make a big difference to comfort. Winter is quiet, with no crowds and lower prices for flights and rentals.

When is the best time to visit Alicante?

  • Beach holiday: June to September, with June and September less crowded.
  • Sightseeing, walking and golf: March to May and October to November.
  • Escaping a northern winter: December to February.
  • Viewing properties: spring and autumn. You can see a home in pleasant weather and also test how warm it feels inside on a cooler day.

What the climate means for buying a home

The mild climate is a big reason why many people live on the Costa Blanca all year, not just in summer. When you look at a property, check its orientation (south-facing terraces get winter sun, but can be very hot in August), insulation, air conditioning with heating, and whether it lies in a flood zone. Frequently asked questions

Is Alicante warm in winter?

Mild rather than warm. Days are usually 17–18 °C and sunny, nights around 6–7 °C. You can often sit outside at lunchtime, but you need a jacket in the evening.

When is the sea warm enough to swim in Alicante?

From about mid-June to October, when the sea is 22–27 °C. It is warmest in August.

Which is the rainiest month in Alicante?

Usually September, followed by October. Rain often comes in short, heavy storms rather than long wet spells.

Does it snow in Alicante?

Almost never on the coast. Snow can fall on the inland mountains in some winters.

Every season has its charm

There is no single perfect month in Alicante, but there are very few bad ones. Choose the season that fits what you want to do, and if you are thinking of staying longer, tell us what you are looking for. We will show you homes that are comfortable all year round.
