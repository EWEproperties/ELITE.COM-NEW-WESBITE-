La Hoya, Torrevieja: A Guide to Lagoons Village

By Lucyna Gacek · 13 January 2026 · updated 25 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: La Hoya is the largest new residential area in Torrevieja: almost 2 million m² between the pink lagoon, La Mata natural park and the sea, built from scratch to a single urban plan with low buildings, wide streets and lots of green space. At its centre, developer TM Grupo Inmobiliario is building Lagoons Village: about 1,800 homes over 264,000 m² in 11 phases, from apartments and bungalows to villas, with a private sport and wellness club for owners. La Mata beach, the Habaneras shopping centre and Quirónsalud hospital are a few minutes away. It suits buyers who want a modern, year-round home and are happy to buy into a district still being built.

Torrevieja grew quickly and not always tidily. La Hoya is different: a whole new district designed in one go. Here is what it is, what Lagoons Village offers and what to think about if you are considering buying there.

La Hoya: a new district built to one plan

La Hoya is described locally as the largest area of building land in Alicante province, covering almost two million square metres on the north side of Torrevieja. Because it is being built from scratch under a single partial plan (plan parcial), it has lower buildings, wider streets, modern utilities and much more green space than older parts of the town. Several developers hold licences in the area, and La Hoya will grow over many years into a neighbourhood of several thousand homes.

Location: between the lagoons and the sea

  • Next to the Lagunas de La Mata y Torrevieja natural park and the famous pink lagoon, with walking and cycling paths.
  • A few minutes from La Mata beach, the Habaneras shopping centre and the private Quirónsalud Torrevieja hospital.
  • About 45 minutes by car from Alicante-Elche airport and around 50 minutes from Murcia airport.

The salt lagoons give the area a dry, mild microclimate that many people find good for their health. See what else the town offers in our Costa Blanca guide.

Lagoons Village by TM

In the middle of La Hoya, TM Grupo Inmobiliario, a developer with decades of experience on the Costa Blanca, is building Lagoons Village. When it launched the project in 2024, TM announced about 1,800 homes on 264,000 m², built in 11 phases, with an investment of around €375 million. TM is also building roads, avenues, green areas and the owners' sports centre.

The project is planned as a complete district rather than a single complex, for people who live here all year, spend long winters here or use it as a second home.

The owners' sport and wellness club

The Lagoons Village Sport & Wellness Club is for owners only. It is planned to include:

  • heated pools for adults and children, partly powered by solar panels, and a large beach-style pool area,
  • indoor and outdoor gyms,
  • tennis and padel courts, a football pitch and a mini basketball court,
  • children's play areas, co-working space, a spa and treatment rooms,
  • cycle paths, including towards La Mata beach, a shuttle bus to the beach, and private security.

Facilities on this scale are rare in Costa Blanca developments, but they come with community fees. Ask for the expected fees per property type before you reserve.

The homes

Lagoons Village offers apartments, bungalows, semi-detached houses and detached villas, mostly with 1 to 3 bedrooms. The designs have large windows, open-plan kitchens and plenty of outdoor space: gardens, terraces and roof solariums. Homes face two ways for cross-ventilation, use aerothermal heat pumps for hot water and climate control, and the landscaping uses native plants that need little water. Parking and storage are planned for each home.

What to consider before buying

  • A district in progress: La Hoya will be a building site for years. Check which phases and services will be finished when you move in.
  • Off-plan protection: every payment before completion must be backed by a bank guarantee. See new build or resale.
  • Running costs: ask for the community fees, which will reflect the club and security.
  • Letting: if you plan holiday lets, check the community rules and the regional tourist registration. See short-term rental rules.
  • Long-term value: new, well-planned districts tend to keep their appeal better than older areas that are hard to modernise, and Torrevieja's new supply is concentrated here.

Frequently asked questions

Where is La Hoya in Torrevieja?

On the north side of the town, between the pink lagoon, the La Mata natural park and the sea, a few minutes from La Mata beach and the Habaneras shopping centre.

How big is Lagoons Village?

TM Grupo Inmobiliario plans about 1,800 homes on 264,000 m², built in 11 phases.

What types of homes are there?

Apartments, bungalows, semi-detached houses and detached villas, mostly with 1 to 3 bedrooms, with gardens, terraces or solariums.

Is the sport and wellness club open to everyone?

No. It is planned for Lagoons Village owners only, and its costs are reflected in the community fees.

How far is the airport?

About 45 minutes by car to Alicante-Elche airport and around 50 minutes to Murcia airport.
