Wellness Retreats on the Costa Blanca

By Lucyna Gacek · 17 February 2026 · updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: The Costa Blanca has wellness for every style. SHA Wellness Clinic in L'Albir offers medical detox and longevity programmes; MasQi in the Sierra de Mariola is a quiet mountain yoga retreat; Ritual de Terra in Jávea is a boutique spa hotel; Asia Gardens near Benidorm is pure luxury relaxation; and Thalasia in San Pedro del Pinatar, just south in Murcia, focuses on seawater and mud treatments.

Mornings by the Mediterranean feel different: soft light, warm air even in winter and no rush. That is why the Costa Blanca has become one of Europe's favourite places for a real reset, from medical programmes to simple spa weekends. Here are five very different retreats, and who each one suits.

SHA Wellness Clinic – L'Albir

Set on the hillside of the Serra Gelada between Altea and Benidorm, SHA is one of the best-known medical wellness clinics in Europe. Stays combine medical check-ups, personalised nutrition, fitness and natural therapies, with programmes for detox, sleep, weight and healthy ageing. Days are structured and results are measured.

Best for: guided detox, preventive health and people ready for a real lifestyle change. It feels more like a clinic than a holiday, and that is the point.

MasQi, The Energy House – Sierra de Mariola

Inland, in the forested Sierra de Mariola natural park, MasQi offers yoga, meditation, walks and plant-based food in small groups. There is no nightlife, no beach club and very little phone signal.

Best for: a digital detox and a deeper, quieter reset. Many guests find a week in the mountains more restful than any seaside spa.

Ritual de Terra – Jávea

A boutique hotel with Mediterranean architecture, a spa and a calm, understated style in the countryside of Jávea. It works well for short breaks that mix yoga, treatments and coastal walks, without a strict programme. Outside July and August, Jávea is one of the most peaceful towns on the coast.

Best for: couples and anyone who values design and a balanced spa stay.

Asia Gardens Hotel & Thai Spa – Finestrat

A five-star resort in the hills above Benidorm with tropical gardens, several pools and a Thai spa. It is not a medical retreat: the focus is pure relaxation, good food and a massage, in a beautiful setting.

Best for: luxury spa weekends and switching off completely.

Thalasia – San Pedro del Pinatar

Just south of the Costa Blanca, on the Mar Menor in Murcia, Thalasia specialises in seawater hydrotherapy (thalassotherapy). Nearby, at Lo Pagán, the salt flats' natural mud baths have been used for generations for joints and skin.

Best for: circulation, joints, gentle recovery and short health breaks.

How to choose

  • Want measurable results and medical support? Choose a clinic programme such as SHA.
  • Need silence and nature? Head inland to a mountain retreat.
  • Looking for a short, beautiful break? A boutique or luxury spa hotel.
  • Have joint or circulation problems? Try thalassotherapy and mud baths on the Mar Menor.

Book early for spring and autumn, the most popular seasons, and check what is included: medical tests and some treatments often cost extra. On the Costa del Sol, see our guide to wellness retreats in Marbella.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best wellness retreat on the Costa Blanca?

It depends on your goal. SHA in L'Albir is the leading medical wellness clinic; MasQi offers a quiet mountain yoga retreat; Asia Gardens and Ritual de Terra are spa hotels for relaxing breaks.

Where is SHA Wellness Clinic?

In L'Albir (L'Alfàs del Pi), on the slopes of the Serra Gelada between Altea and Benidorm, about 50 minutes from Alicante airport.

When is the best time for a wellness retreat on the Costa Blanca?

Spring and autumn, when it is warm but not hot. Winter is also mild and quieter.

Are there mud baths on the Costa Blanca?

The best known are just south, at Lo Pagán in San Pedro del Pinatar on the Mar Menor, where the salt flats' mud is free to use.
