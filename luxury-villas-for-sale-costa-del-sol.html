What taxes do I pay on a luxury villa on the Costa del Sol?

On a new build, 10% VAT (IVA) and 1.2% stamp duty (AJD). On a resale villa, 7% transfer tax (ITP). Add about 1.5 to 2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer.
