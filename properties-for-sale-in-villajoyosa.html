The city

Living in Villajoyosa

Villajoyosa, or La Vila Joiosa in Valencian, is a seaside town of about 37,000 people between Alicante and Benidorm. It is best known for the brightly painted houses of its old town, which rise above the river mouth and the beach, and for its long tradition of making chocolate.

The town has several beaches along its coast: the main beach in front of the colourful houses, the long Playa del Paraíso, and smaller coves in between. The fishing port is still active, and the old town has narrow streets, small squares and local restaurants.

Villajoyosa is lively all year, as most people live and work here, and prices are often lower than in Benidorm, just 10 minutes away. The TRAM links the town with Benidorm and Alicante, and Alicante-Elche airport is about 35 minutes away by car.

Buying property in Villajoyosa: questions and answers

How much does an apartment cost in Villajoyosa?

Our apartments in Villajoyosa start at €254,000 today. Most apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms we sell cost between €350,000 and €580,000, depending on the distance to the beach and the views. Penthouses with sea views cost from about €535,000, and a frontline penthouse €1,540,000.

Villajoyosa or Benidorm: which is better?

Benidorm is a holiday town with tall towers and nightlife. Villajoyosa is a real Spanish town with a quieter pace and its own old town, and prices are often lower. Benidorm is only 10 minutes away by car or TRAM.

Can I rent out an apartment in Villajoyosa?

Yes, and some of our apartments already have a tourist licence. Holiday letting needs a tourist licence in the Valencian register, and some buildings restrict it, so check before you buy. See our guide to the short-term rental rules.

How far is Villajoyosa from the airport?

Alicante-Elche Miguel Hernández airport is about 35 minutes by car. You can also take the TRAM to Alicante.

What costs are there on top of the price?

For a new build you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty. For a resale home you pay 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million). Notary, land registry and legal fees add about 1.5–2.5%.

Can foreigners buy property in Villajoyosa?

Yes. Anyone can buy property in Spain. You need a Spanish NIE tax number and usually a Spanish bank account, and we help you with both. See our guide to the NIE number.
