About this property

Three-bedroom villa on an 800 m² plot in the sought-after Santa Clara area of Altea, with a private pool, a covered terrace and a top-floor master suite with mountain and partial sea views.

A detached villa in the peaceful and popular Santa Clara area of Altea, easy to look after and suited to year-round living or holidays. It has 228 m² built on an 800 m² plot and faces south-east.

The main floor has an entrance hall, a bright living and dining room with high ceilings, a fitted kitchen, two double bedrooms and a bathroom. Large sliding doors open onto a covered terrace overlooking the private pool.

The whole upper floor is the master suite, with its own bathroom and a private terrace with views of the Sierra de Bernia and partial sea views. There is a garage, parking, a storage room, air conditioning and gas central heating. IBI is about €849 a year.
