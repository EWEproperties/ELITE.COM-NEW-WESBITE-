About this property

Contemporary villa with Dutch-designed interiors on a 6,000 m² plot in El Planet, Altea, with five bedrooms including a separate guest apartment, a private pool and panoramic views.

A designer villa in El Planet, Altea, in a peaceful and exclusive setting with panoramic views. It sits on an impressive 6,000 m² plot, has 258 m² built and was renovated in 2022, with interiors by a Dutch designer that mix clean lines and natural materials.

There are five bedrooms, two of them in a separate guest apartment for extra privacy. Bright open-plan living spaces flow outside through large windows and glass doors, with a designer kitchen and a statement fireplace.

Mature landscaping surrounds several terraces, some of them heated, with areas for dining and relaxing, an outdoor fireplace, a summer kitchen and a private pool. The large grounds give real privacy. There is a garage, parking and central air conditioning and heating.
