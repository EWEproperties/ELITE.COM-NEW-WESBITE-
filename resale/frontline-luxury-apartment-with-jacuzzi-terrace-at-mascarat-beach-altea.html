About this property

Furnished four-bedroom apartment of 305 m² right on the sea at Mascarat Beach, Altea, with a 109 m² covered panoramic terrace with jacuzzi, direct beach access, two garage spaces and two storage rooms.

A luxury apartment on the frontline at Mascarat Beach in Altea, about 30 m from the sea with direct beach access, next to the Luis Campomanes marina (Marina Greenwich) and a few minutes from restaurants, cafés and the yacht club. The complex is one of the most exclusive on the Costa Blanca, with 24-hour security.

It has 305 m² built, including about 197 m² inside and a 109 m² covered panoramic terrace with a jacuzzi. There are four bedrooms, two of them suites with dressing rooms, an extra full bathroom and a large living and dining room with an open kitchen. Finishes are high quality, with central air conditioning and underfloor heating in the bathrooms.

It is ready to move into and sold fully furnished, with two underground garage spaces and two large storage rooms. It works well as a main home or an investment.
