About this property

One-bedroom apartment renovated to a high standard on the 17th floor in Benidorm, with a large open balcony, parking and a gated communal pool.

A renovated one-bedroom apartment of 50 m² on the 17th floor, with views over Benidorm, the sea and the mountains. The height keeps it quiet even though the centre is close.

The bright living area opens onto a large balcony. There is a bedroom, a bathroom with walk-in shower and a fitted kitchen, and it comes furnished.

Parking is included, the building has several lifts and a gated communal pool. The beach is about ten minutes on foot. It works as a holiday home or a rental investment.
