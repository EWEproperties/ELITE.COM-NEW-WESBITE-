About this property

Fully furnished penthouse in a small, modern complex near Benidorm, with a private solarium looking out over the sea, the mountains and the Benidorm skyline.

A fully furnished penthouse in a small, modern complex close to Benidorm. From the private solarium and the terrace you look out over the sea, the mountains and the Benidorm skyline.

There are two bedrooms and two bathrooms in 62 m². The kitchen has Bosch appliances and comes fully equipped. A shaded parking space and the communal pool are included.

The penthouse is ready to move into, as a home for the whole year, a holiday home or a rental. The beach is about 5 km away and Alicante airport about 60 km (45 minutes).
