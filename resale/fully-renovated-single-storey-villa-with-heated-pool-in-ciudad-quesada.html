About this property

Five-bedroom single-level villa on a 620 m² corner plot in Ciudad Quesada, Rojales, renovated to an as-new standard, with a heated private pool, a roof solarium and a 50 m² open-plan living area.

A modern single-storey villa on a 620 m² corner plot in Ciudad Quesada, Rojales, in a quiet, popular residential area close to shops, schools, restaurants and golf about 2 km away. It has 177 m² built, with five bedrooms and four full bathrooms.

The house was completely renovated a few months ago to a high standard, with new wiring, plumbing, floors, walls, façade and outdoor areas, so it feels like a new build. The 50 m² living and dining room has an open kitchen with Bosch appliances, and all the bedrooms are large with big windows.

Outside are a heated private pool, a roof solarium with views, terraces, mature trees and parking for four cars behind an electric gate. There is air conditioning, underfloor heating, electric shutters and double glazing. The beach is about 11 km and Alicante airport about 40 km away.
