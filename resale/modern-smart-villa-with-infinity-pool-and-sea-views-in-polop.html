About this property

Contemporary villa built in 2021 on four levels, with 448 m² built, an infinity pool, a jacuzzi and panoramic views of the Mediterranean and the Sierra de Bernia.

A contemporary villa built in 2021 on a private 538 m² plot in one of Polop's most exclusive residential communities. It has 448 m² built over four levels, with premium finishes, smart home technology and views of the Mediterranean and the Sierra de Bernia.

Floor-to-ceiling windows fill the open-plan living and dining areas with light and open onto several terraces with sea and mountain views. There are three bedrooms, four bathrooms, two guest toilets and a designer kitchen. The lower level is fully finished and adds flexible extra space.

Outside, landscaped gardens surround an infinity pool, with sun terraces, lounge areas and a private jacuzzi. There is a garage and parking, air conditioning and underfloor heating with a heat pump. It is sold unfurnished.
