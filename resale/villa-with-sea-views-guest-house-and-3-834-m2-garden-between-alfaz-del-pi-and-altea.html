About this property

Four-bedroom villa renovated in 2023 on a 3,834 m² landscaped plot between Alfaz del Pi and Altea, with sea and mountain views, a private pool and a separate guest house.

A villa of 376 m² on a 3,834 m² plot in a private residential area halfway between Alfaz del Pi and Altea, with views of the sea and the mountains. The garden has mature and fruit trees, stone paths and sculptures. Built in 1980, renovated in 2023.

The street-level floor has a bright living room with open kitchen, three bedrooms and two bathrooms, and a large terrace with panoramic views. The middle floor has two lounges, an en-suite bedroom with walk-in wardrobe, a second kitchen, an art studio and direct access to the pool. The lowest floor is partly built and ready to be finished.

A separate guest house sits on the plot. There are a private pool, heating, air conditioning and parking. IBI is about €630 a year.
