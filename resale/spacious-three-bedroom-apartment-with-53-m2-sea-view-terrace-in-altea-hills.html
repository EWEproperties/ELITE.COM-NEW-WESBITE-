About this property

200 m² apartment in the gated Altea Hills community with 24-hour security, panoramic sea views, a 53 m² terrace, a fireplace, underfloor heating and a garage space.

A spacious apartment in the gated Altea Hills community, with well-kept gardens, 24-hour security and panoramic sea views in a quiet, green setting. Altea centre, the beaches, Don Cayo golf club and Marina Greenwich are a few minutes away, the AP-7 is 1 km and Alicante airport about 45 minutes.

It has 200 m² built with three large bedrooms, one with its own bathroom and dressing room, a fully equipped kitchen with a separate laundry, and a bright living and dining room with a fireplace and sea views. The 53 m² terrace, part covered and part open, is reached from all the main rooms.

There is underfloor heating and central hot and cold air conditioning, and a space in the underground garage is included. The building is well built and has communal pools and gardens.
