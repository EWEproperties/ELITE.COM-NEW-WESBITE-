About this property

Three-bedroom detached villa on a 679 m² plot in Benissa with sea views from the Peñón de Ifach to Cabo de Moraira, ready to modernise, with space for a pool.

A detached villa in a privileged spot in Benissa, with Mediterranean views stretching from the Peñón de Ifach to Cabo de Moraira. It has 187 m² built on a 679 m² plot, with three bedrooms and two bathrooms, and the beach is five to ten minutes by car.

Well kept and facing south-east, it gets plenty of light all day. It is a sound base for a comfortable home or a stylish renovation. The large terrace is ideal for meals outside with the views, and the mature garden gives privacy.

The lower terrace is a natural place to add a private pool. There are fitted wardrobes, a storage room, private parking and two entrances to the plot. IBI is about €655 a year.
