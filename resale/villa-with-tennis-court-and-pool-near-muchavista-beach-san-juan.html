About this property

Five-bedroom villa on a flat 1,816 m² plot in a private enclave of five villas, with a pool, a tennis court with frontón, a garden studio and EV charging, 1.8 km from Muchavista beach.

A well-kept Mediterranean villa in a quiet cul-de-sac enclave of just five detached houses in San Juan de Alicante. It is about 1.1 km from the Lycée Français, 1.8 km from Muchavista beach and a short drive from Playa de San Juan and Alicante city centre.

The flat, south-east facing 1,816 m² plot has mature gardens with palm and fruit trees around an 8 x 4 m pool, a covered terrace, a barbecue area and a pool bathroom. A private tennis court with frontón has its own entrance, and a detached garden studio with power and fast internet works as an office, gym or guest room.

The house has 249 m² over two floors, with five bedrooms and five bathrooms, a bright living and dining room with solid teak floors, and a recently renovated kitchen. There is a covered carport with EV charging plus parking for three more cars. IBI is about €590 a year.
