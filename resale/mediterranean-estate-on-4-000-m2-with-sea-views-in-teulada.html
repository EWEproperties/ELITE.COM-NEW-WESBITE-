About this property

Six-bedroom villa with 404 m² of living space on a 4,000 m² corner plot in Teulada, with views along the coast to Jávea and Calpe, a heated pool with jacuzzi and a summer kitchen.

A detached Mediterranean estate on a private 4,000 m² corner plot in Teulada, with 815 m² built including 404 m² of living space. It mixes traditional architecture and rustic touches with sea views, and suits a family home, a private retreat or a boutique guest house.

Gated entrance, a central fountain and landscaped gardens set the tone. There are six bedrooms, four bathrooms and a guest toilet, with wooden beams, terracotta floors, arches and an antique front door. It was updated in 2024 and is sold furnished.

Terraces and mature gardens look along the coast towards Jávea, Benissa and Calpe. The heated pool has a jacuzzi and massage jets, and there is a summer kitchen, a barbecue, a garage and parking. IBI is about €1,464 a year.
