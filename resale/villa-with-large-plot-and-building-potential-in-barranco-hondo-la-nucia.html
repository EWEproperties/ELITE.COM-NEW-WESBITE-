About this property

Three-bedroom villa from 1975 on a 1,170 m² plot among pine trees in La Nucía, ready to modernise, with an adjoining 1,100 m² plot where a second house could be built.

A detached villa in the Barranco Hondo area of La Nucía, among pine trees and open countryside, a short drive from the coast. Built in 1975, it has 109 m² with three bedrooms and two bathrooms on a 1,170 m² plot, with sea and mountain views.

It is habitable now, with bright living spaces opening to the garden, but would benefit from modernising into a contemporary Mediterranean home. The plot leaves room to extend the house, landscape the garden or add a pool, subject to permits.

An adjoining plot of about 1,100 m² is included, where a second house of around 109 m² over two floors could be built, subject to planning permission. That opens up options for resale, rental income or family use. La Nucía has good sports facilities and international schools.
