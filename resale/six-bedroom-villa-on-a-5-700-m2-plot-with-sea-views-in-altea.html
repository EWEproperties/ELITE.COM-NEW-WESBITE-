About this property

Substantial villa of about 675 m² on a private plot of over 5,700 m² in one of Altea's most prestigious areas, with panoramic sea views, a pool and great potential for a high-end renovation.

A substantial villa in one of Altea's most prestigious residential areas, on a private plot of more than 5,700 m² with about 675 m² built, panoramic views towards the sea and a south-facing aspect. Altea's old town, beaches, marina and restaurants are close by.

It currently has six bedrooms and seven bathrooms, generous living spaces with high ceilings and large windows, and plenty of light. Mature gardens give exceptional privacy. There is a lift, a garage, parking and a storage room.

The house is an excellent base for a high-end renovation into a contemporary villa or Mediterranean estate. Large terraces, a private pool and extensive grounds leave room for landscaping and leisure facilities, subject to permits. This much land, space and view is rare in Altea.
