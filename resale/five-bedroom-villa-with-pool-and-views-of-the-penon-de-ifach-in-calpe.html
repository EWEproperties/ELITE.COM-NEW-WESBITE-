About this property

Private villa of 385 m² on a 1,100 m² plot at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in Calpe, with two independent floors, a pool and views of the sea and the Peñón de Ifach.

A detached villa of 385 m² on a 1,100 m² plot at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac on a hilltop in Calpe, with no direct neighbours and views of the Mediterranean and the Peñón de Ifach. It borders a protected green area.

Each of the two floors has its own entrance, living room and access to the pool, which makes it easy to host family or guests. There are four en-suite bedrooms plus a children's room, a modern kitchen with breakfast bar, a utility room and a guest WC. The master bathroom has a jacuzzi.

Outside are a large terrace, a private pool, a garden with a gazebo and lemon and orange trees, and parking for two cars. It is sold furnished.
