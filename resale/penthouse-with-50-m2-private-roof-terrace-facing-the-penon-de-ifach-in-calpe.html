About this property

One-bedroom penthouse from 2015 in Calpe with a 50 m² private roof terrace and open views of the Peñón de Ifach and the sea, plus a garage space and storage room.

A penthouse built in 2015 in a sought-after part of Calpe, with the beach and the town's amenities close by. Its best feature is a 50 m² private roof terrace with open views of the Peñón de Ifach and the sea.

The bright living and dining area faces east and gets the morning sun. There is one large bedroom and one bathroom, and the layout allows a second bedroom to be added. It has 81 m² of usable space, an extra terrace and balcony, fitted wardrobes and air conditioning.

A garage space, a storage room, a lift and a communal pool are included, and it is sold furnished. IBI is about €335 a year and community fees about €44 a month.
