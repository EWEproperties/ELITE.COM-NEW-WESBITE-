About this property

Three-bedroom semi-detached house of 103 m² built in 2010 in Balcón de Bernia, Calpe, with a private garden, communal pool, padel court and parking.

This semi-detached house in the quiet La Empedrola area of Calpe, in the Balcón de Bernia development, was built in 2010 and offers 103 m² of living space.

It has three bedrooms and two bathrooms, and a private garden where you can enjoy the open views of the Sierra de Bernia.

Residents share a communal pool and a padel court, and the house has its own parking. The beaches and the centre of Calpe are a short drive away.
