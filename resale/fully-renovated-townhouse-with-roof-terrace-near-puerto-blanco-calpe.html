About this property

Three-bedroom townhouse renovated in 2025 in a gated complex with two pools in Calpe, with a private roof terrace with panoramic views, 1.2 km from Puerto Blanco beach and marina.

A townhouse built in 2009 and fully renovated in 2025, in a secure gated complex 1.2 km from Puerto Blanco beach and its recently renovated marina. It has 165 m² built over three levels on a 143 m² plot and is sold fully furnished and ready to move into.

There are three bedrooms and three modern bathrooms, with electric underfloor heating and split air conditioning. A covered parking space for one car is included.

The private roof terrace has panoramic views and room for a summer kitchen or jacuzzi. The complex has two communal pools and well-kept grounds. IBI is about €583 a year and community fees about €9 a month.
