About this property

Four-bedroom villa from 2005 in a quiet area of La Nucía, with 188 m² over two floors and a basement, a large pool, a summer kitchen and sea and mountain views.

A well-kept detached Mediterranean villa in a peaceful residential area of La Nucía. Built in 2005, it has 188 m² over two floors and a spacious basement on a 251 m² plot, and faces south for plenty of light.

The ground floor has a bright living room opening to a covered terrace, a large separate kitchen with its own door to the garden, a double bedroom and a bathroom. The first floor holds more bedrooms, for four in total and three bathrooms. The basement has a double garage and storage.

Outside, a large private pool is surrounded by sunny terraces, with a fully equipped summer kitchen, barbecue, outdoor shower and a chill-out lounge, plus an easy-care garden. It has air conditioning and central heating and is sold unfurnished. IBI is about €770 a year.
