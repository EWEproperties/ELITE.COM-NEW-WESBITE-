About this property

Four-bedroom villa renovated in 2021 and 2025 in a sought-after part of Moraira, on two plots totalling 2,970 m², with open sea views, a 32 m² pool and an Ibiza-style corner.

A villa in one of Moraira's most sought-after areas, with a peaceful feel, great privacy and open views of the sea and greenery. It sits on two plots totalling 2,970 m²: the house on 1,360 m² and an adjoining 1,610 m² plot included in the sale, which keeps the surroundings open and unoverlooked.

The house has 157 m² inside plus a 27 m² veranda. It was renovated with taste inside in 2021 and outside in 2025, and every room has reversible air conditioning. The main floor faces the sea and the pool, with three bedrooms and the living areas opening onto the terraces.

Upstairs, the master suite has its own bathroom and dressing room. Outside are a 32 m² pool, a separate Ibiza-style corner, terraces, a barbecue and parking. IBI is about €584 a year.
