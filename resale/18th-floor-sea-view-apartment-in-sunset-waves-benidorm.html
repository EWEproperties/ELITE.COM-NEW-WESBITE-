About this property

Renovated and newly furnished two-bedroom apartment on the 18th floor of Sunset Waves at Poniente beach, with panoramic sea views and resort-style facilities.

This apartment is on the 18th floor of the second tower of Sunset Waves, one of the best-known residential complexes on Benidorm's Poniente beach. It has been recently renovated and decorated with all-new furniture.

It has two bedrooms and two bathrooms with a bright, modern layout. From the living room and private terrace you look out over the sea, the city skyline and the mountains, especially beautiful at sunset.

The complex has over 10,000 m² of shared grounds with outdoor pools, a heated pool, jacuzzis, a gym, sauna, tennis and padel courts, tropical gardens and private security. Restaurants, beach clubs and the town centre are close by.
