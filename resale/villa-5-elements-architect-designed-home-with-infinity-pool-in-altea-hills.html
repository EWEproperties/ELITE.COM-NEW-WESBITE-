About this property

Four-level villa by architect Gilardi in the gated Altea Hills community, with a private lift, an angular infinity pool with sea views, a master level with jacuzzi and sauna, and a mature fruit garden.

Villa 5 Elements was designed by architect Gilardi together with the owner and sits in the prestigious gated community of Altea Hills. It is spread over four levels with 789 m² built on a 1,100 m² plot. You enter at the top floor with private parking, and a floating staircase and a private lift link every level.

The main floor has a designer kitchen with a peninsula and a dining area, with sliding glass walls that open fully to the terraces, an outdoor barbecue kitchen and an angular infinity pool with private sea views. There is also a glazed all-season lounge and a Bali-style lounge with living walls. The level below has two large suites with their own bathrooms and terraces with sea and mountain views.

The lowest floor is a private master level with a large suite, a jacuzzi, a sauna and a lawned garden, plus an atelier that can be an office, guest room or gym. The mature, low-maintenance garden has lemon, fig, avocado and pomegranate trees and automatic watering.
