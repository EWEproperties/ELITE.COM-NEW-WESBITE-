About this property

Large 147 m² apartment in Altea a minute's walk from the sea and the promenade, with three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a balcony with open views and a handy interior patio.

A spacious 147 m² apartment in Altea, about 100 m from the sea, with the promenade, cafés, restaurants and everyday shops all within an easy walk. It is close to everything yet quiet and private.

There are three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a bright living room and a practical layout. The balcony has open views and is a calm place to relax, and an interior patio next to the kitchen is a rare and very useful extra.

It suits a family home, a holiday base or a secure investment. The building has a lift. IBI is about €450 a year and community fees about €60 a month. Alicante airport is about 45 minutes by car.
