About this property

Fully renovated villa on a 1,022 m² plot within walking distance of Albir centre, with six en-suite style bedrooms, a pool, outdoor kitchen and a rooftop wellness terrace with sauna and jacuzzi.

A villa of 464 m² on a 1,022 m² plot in a sought-after part of Albir, within walking distance of the town centre. It was fully renovated in 2024 and is sold furnished and ready to move into.

The first floor has the living room with mountain views, an open-plan kitchen, a large balcony, two bedrooms including the master and a guest WC. The ground floor has four more bedrooms, each with its own bathroom and terrace onto the garden, plus a laundry room, garage and a covered entertaining area.

The mature garden has a large south-facing pool, an outdoor kitchen, stone barbecue and orange, lemon, mango and avocado trees on automatic irrigation. The rooftop terrace has a sauna, jacuzzi and sun area with open sea views. IBI is about €2,014 a year.
