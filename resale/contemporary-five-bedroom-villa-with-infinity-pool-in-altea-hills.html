About this property

Modern villa on three levels in the gated community of Altea Hills, with five en-suite bedrooms, a lift, an infinity pool and wide sea views.

A contemporary villa of 350 m² on an 817 m² plot in Altea Hills, one of the best-known gated communities on the Costa Blanca. The three floors are linked by a staircase and a lift.

The main floor has an open-plan living and dining area and a kitchen with high-end appliances, with floor-to-ceiling glass doors opening to the terraces and the sea view. A fifth en-suite bedroom on this level works as a second master suite or a guest room. Upstairs are four more en-suite bedrooms; the main suite has its own sea view terrace.

Outside, the infinity pool is surrounded by terraces, seating areas and an outdoor kitchen with barbecue. There is space to add a gym or a separate guest studio. Altea's seafront, restaurants and marina are a short drive away.
