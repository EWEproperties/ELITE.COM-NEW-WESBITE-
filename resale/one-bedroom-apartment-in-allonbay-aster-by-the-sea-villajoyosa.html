About this property

Stylish one-bedroom apartment of 55 m² in Allonbay Aster, Villajoyosa, with a 13.6 m² terrace, communal pool, spa and garage, a few metres from the beach.

This elegant one-bedroom apartment is in Allonbay Aster, one of the most popular residential developments on the Costa Blanca, right by the sea in Villajoyosa.

The layout is modern and practical, with an open kitchen joined to the living room. The bedroom is spacious and the bathroom has a contemporary finish. A 13.6 m² terrace adds outdoor space.

The private development has a communal pool with gardens, a wellness area with spa and jacuzzi, and a garage with an electric door. A compact, easy home for living, holidays or rental.
