About this property

Bright two-bedroom apartment on Plaza Triangular in central Benidorm, about 300 m from Levante beach, in a gated complex with a communal pool and a private parking space.

An apartment on Plaza Triangular, one of the most central and sought-after spots in Benidorm, about 300 m from Levante beach and surrounded by shops and services. It has 85 m² built.

There are two bedrooms, two bathrooms, a kitchen and a spacious living and dining room with large windows. All rooms face outwards, with plenty of natural light and views of the sea and the Benidorm skyline from several rooms. It is in very good condition and ready to move into.

The apartment is in a private gated complex with a communal pool, gardens and a lift, and the price includes a private parking space, which is rare this close to the centre. IBI is about €763 a year and community fees about €110 a month.
