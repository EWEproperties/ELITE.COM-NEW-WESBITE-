About this property

Four-bedroom Mediterranean villa on an 850 m² plot in Altea Hills, facing south-east, with sweeping views of the bay, a private pool and most rooms on one floor.

A Mediterranean-style villa with character in Altea Hills, one of the Costa Blanca's most prestigious communities. It faces south-east, is very private and has spectacular panoramic views of the sea, the Bay of Altea, the coast and the mountains. It has 330 m² built on a plot of about 850 m².

Most rooms are on the main floor: a large bright living room opening to the terraces, a separate fitted kitchen, three bedrooms and two bathrooms. The main bedroom has its own bathroom and a 20 m² mezzanine used as an office. Built in 2001, the house was renovated and extended in 2013 with a tower room upstairs holding a fourth bedroom with its own bathroom and sea views.

Wooden ceilings and varied volumes give it personality. In front of the view sits a private pool of about 6 x 4 m, and a pergola area offers more space for summer dining or relaxing.
