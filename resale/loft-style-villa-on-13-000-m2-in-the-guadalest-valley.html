About this property

Modern loft-style villa built in 2021 on a 13,319 m² plot in the green Guadalest valley, 20 minutes from the coast, with 1,000 m² of terraces and garden, a step-free pool and solar power.

A modern villa built in 2021 in the green and peaceful Guadalest valley, within the municipality of Callosa, about 20 minutes from the coast and 15 minutes from the A7. It sits on a 13,319 m² plot with plenty of water and around 300 days of sun a year.

The loft-style house has 406 m² built, with three bedrooms, two bathrooms and a large open-plan living area. The 240 m² ground floor has high ceilings and lots of possibilities, and a fireplace and underfloor heating keep it warm in winter.

Inside and outside flow into 1,000 m² of terraces and garden, with a step-free private pool, a summer kitchen and a barbecue. Solar panels and its own water source make it largely self-sufficient. There is a garage and parking, and shops and a medical centre are nearby.
