About this property

Three-bedroom apartment in gated Altea Hills, renovated in 2023, with a large terrace with private jacuzzi and open views over the sea and Altea.

A three-bedroom apartment in the gated community of Altea Hills, fully renovated in 2023, with 135 m² of living space (187 m² built).

The open-plan living and dining area and modern kitchen open through large glass doors onto a generous terrace with a private jacuzzi and clear views across the Mediterranean and Altea. The master bedroom has an en-suite bathroom, and there is a second bathroom and a laundry and storage room.

A private parking space and a large storage room are included, with a communal pool and tennis courts in the community. IBI is about €578 a year.
