About this property

High-floor two-bedroom apartment from 2019 at Blue Infinity, Cumbre del Sol, with a south-facing sea view terrace, smart home system and resort facilities.

A two-bedroom, two-bathroom apartment of 100 m² on a high floor of Blue Infinity in Cumbre del Sol, built in 2019. The south-facing 55 m² terrace looks out over the Mediterranean.

The open-plan living area has a modern fitted kitchen with breakfast bar. The main bedroom has an en-suite bathroom, a dressing area and terrace access. It has ducted air conditioning, underfloor heating, a smart home system and an alarm, plus two storage rooms on the same floor.

Residents have indoor and outdoor pools, a gym, sauna and steam room, and secure parking. Jávea and Moraira are about 15 minutes away. Community fees are about €2,330 a year.
