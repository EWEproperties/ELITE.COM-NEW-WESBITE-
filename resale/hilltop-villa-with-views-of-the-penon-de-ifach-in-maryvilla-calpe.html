About this property

Three-bedroom villa at the highest point of Maryvilla in Calpe, on a 793 m² plot with a private pool, summer kitchen and open views of the coast and the Peñón de Ifach.

A villa of 172 m² on a 793 m² plot at the top of the Maryvilla residential area in Calpe, with privacy and open views over the coast and the Peñón de Ifach. Facing north-east, it gets morning sun and sunrises over the sea, while the terraces stay shaded in the afternoon.

There are three bedrooms and three bathrooms. It was built in 2003, updated in 2013 and is sold furnished, with air conditioning and central heating.

Outside are a private pool, garden, several terraces, a barbecue and summer kitchen, and parking. Calpe's beaches and town centre are about 3 km away. IBI is about €560 a year.
