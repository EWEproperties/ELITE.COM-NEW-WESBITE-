Exclusive Golf Clubs on the Costa del Sol

By Lucyna Gacek · 1 August 2025 · updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: The Costa del Sol has around 70 golf courses between Málaga and Sotogrande, which is why it is called the "Costa del Golf". The best known include Real Club Valderrama (host of the 1997 Ryder Cup), Finca Cortesín (host of the 2023 Solheim Cup), Real Club Las Brisas, Los Naranjos and La Quinta. Visitors pay a green fee per round, usually from about €60 to several hundred euros in high season. Residents can buy annual memberships, and some of the most exclusive clubs are by membership or invitation only.

Mild winters, more than 300 days of sun and courses with views of the sea and the mountains have made the western Costa del Sol one of the most popular golf destinations in Europe. For many of our clients golf is not just a hobby: it decides where they buy, and it becomes the centre of their social life in Spain.

Here is an overview of the most prestigious clubs, how playing and membership work, and what to look for in a home on a golf course.

Why the Costa del Sol is a golf paradise

You can play here all year. Winter days are often 17–19 °C and sunny, which is why the high season for golf runs from October to May, not in summer. The courses are close together, mostly within 30 minutes of each other along the A-7, and Málaga airport has direct flights to most European cities. Many courses were designed by famous names such as Robert Trent Jones, Seve Ballesteros, Jack Nicklaus and Tom Fazio.

The most prestigious clubs

  • Real Club Valderrama (Sotogrande) – one of Europe's most famous courses, host of the 1997 Ryder Cup, the first held in continental Europe. Tee times for visitors are very limited.
  • Finca Cortesín (Casares) – a luxury resort course that hosted the Solheim Cup in 2023 and has staged professional match-play events.
  • Real Club de Golf Las Brisas (Nueva Andalucía) – a classic Robert Trent Jones course in Marbella's Golf Valley, known for its mature trees and fast greens.
  • Los Naranjos and Aloha (Nueva Andalucía) – long-established courses just behind Puerto Banús, popular with residents.
  • La Quinta Golf (Benahavís) – 27 holes with views of the sea and La Concha, surrounded by residential estates.
  • La Zagaleta (Benahavís) – two private courses inside one of Europe's most exclusive gated estates, for residents and their guests only.

Green fees and membership: how it works

There are two ways to play:

  • Green fee – a price per round, booked in advance. On most courses it ranges from about €60 to €200, and well above that at the most famous courses in high season. Twilight rates and summer prices are lower, and a buggy usually costs extra.
  • Membership – an annual fee, sometimes with an entry fee or a share in the club. Members get priority tee times, tournaments, the clubhouse and often the pool, gym and academy. Some clubs offer family, weekday or non-resident packages, or a trial period.

Prices change every season, so check each club's website. If you play more than two or three times a week, a membership is usually cheaper than green fees.

Buying a home on a golf course

Golf-front homes range from apartments in gated complexes to penthouses and large villas with views of the fairways. Their advantages:

  • open, green views that cannot be built on,
  • quiet surroundings and, in many estates, 24-hour security,
  • steady demand from buyers and holiday renters,
  • sometimes discounted or included membership for owners.

Before you buy, check the community fees, whether the course is public or private, the direction of the fairway (stray balls), and the licence situation if you plan to let the home to tourists.

Golf as a way into the community

For many newcomers the golf club is where friendships start. Clubs organise weekly competitions, social dinners and junior academies, and members come from all over Europe. Frequently asked questions

How many golf courses are on the Costa del Sol?

Around 70, mostly between Málaga and Sotogrande, which is why the area is often called the Costa del Golf.

What is the most famous golf course on the Costa del Sol?

Real Club Valderrama in Sotogrande, which hosted the 1997 Ryder Cup. Finca Cortesín, Las Brisas and La Quinta are also very well known.

How much is a green fee on the Costa del Sol?

Usually from about €60 to €200 a round, and more at the most prestigious courses in high season. Twilight and summer rates are lower.

When is the best time to play golf on the Costa del Sol?

From October to May. Summer is very hot in the middle of the day, so most people play early in the morning or in the evening.
