Top Hotels in Alicante

By Anita Orłowska · 10 November 2024 · updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: For a city break in Alicante, stay in the old town or by the marina: Hospes Amérigo (5-star, in a former convent), Casa Alberola by Hilton and the adults-only Palacete S.XVII are the most stylish. For the beach, Meliá Alicante and Porta Maris sit next to El Postiguet, and Port Alicante City & Beach is at Playa de San Juan. Eurostars Pórtico and NH Alicante are practical central options. Book early for June (Hogueras), Easter and summer.

Alicante has hotels for every kind of trip, from historic boutique stays in the old town to big beach hotels with pools. Where you stay matters more than the stars: the old town and the marina are best for walking and restaurants, Playa de San Juan for a beach holiday.

Here is our shortlist, often recommended to clients who come to view homes on the Costa Blanca.

Hospes Amérigo – 5-star in a former convent

In the heart of the old town, a converted Dominican convent with modern interiors, a rooftop pool and spa with castle views, and a well-known restaurant. Ideal for a stylish city break on foot.

Meliá Alicante – on Postiguet beach

A large hotel right between the marina and El Postiguet beach. Many rooms have sea views, and there is an outdoor pool, spa and gym. Good for families and for those who want the beach on the doorstep.

Hotel Boutique Alicante Palacete S.XVII – adults only

A small adults-only hotel in a 17th-century palace in the old town, with individually designed rooms, a cocktail bar and a wine cellar. Perfect for couples.

Casa Alberola, Curio Collection by Hilton

A historic neoclassical building facing the marina and the Explanada, restored as a boutique hotel with a rooftop terrace and a seafood-focused restaurant.

Eurostars Pórtico Alicante

A comfortable city hotel next to the town hall, with a rooftop terrace overlooking the old town. A practical base for sightseeing.

Hotel Spa Porta Maris by Meliá

A 4-star hotel on the marina, a short walk from El Postiguet, with sea-view rooms and a spa with a heated indoor pool.

Port Alicante City & Beach

A 4-star superior hotel at Playa de San Juan, close to the long beach and the tram to the centre, with a sky bar and an outdoor pool. A good choice for a beach holiday with city access.

NH Alicante

A reliable business and city hotel near the centre, with easy access to the train station and the road to the airport.

Which area to choose

  • Old town and Explanada – everything on foot, best for a first visit.
  • Postiguet and the marina – beach and city together.
  • Playa de San Juan – a long beach, calmer evenings and the tram to the centre.
  • Near the station – practical for trains to Madrid and Valencia.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Alicante?

The old town and the Explanada for sightseeing and restaurants, El Postiguet for the beach in the centre, and Playa de San Juan for a quieter beach holiday.

Are there 5-star hotels in Alicante?

Yes. Hospes Amérigo in the old town is the best known, in a converted convent with a rooftop pool and spa.

Which Alicante hotels are on the beach?

Meliá Alicante and Porta Maris are next to El Postiguet, and Port Alicante City & Beach is at Playa de San Juan.

When should I book a hotel in Alicante?

Early for Easter, the Hogueras de San Juan in June and July–August, when prices are highest.
