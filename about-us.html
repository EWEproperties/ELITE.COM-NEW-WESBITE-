Why buy with us

A calm, honest way to buy in Spain

We live here

Our team lives on the Costa Blanca. We know the towns, the developers and the everyday details that matter when you buy, from the best streets to the community fees.

New homes, ready to move in

We work directly with trusted developers. New homes in Spain come finished, with kitchen, bathrooms and wardrobes, so you only add the furniture.

No fee for buyers

Our commission is paid by the seller, so our help costs you nothing. You pay the same price as buying directly.

With you until the keys

We speak English, Spanish, German and Polish, and work with lawyers, banks and translators who guide you through every document.

How we help

From the first idea to the keys

  1. 1

    Tell us what you want

  2. 2

    Video calls and plans

    We show you homes and areas on video, so you only travel for the best ones.

  3. 3

    Viewing trip
