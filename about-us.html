Meet Elite World Estates – real estate agency in Spain

We are a real estate agency that operates in Spain. For years (our real estate agency started working in 2018) we have been helping investors, as well as people looking for their place on earth, to find the perfect property in Spain. We accompany our clients right from the stage of selecting an offer and then guide them through the entire purchase process. Right up to receiving the keys to their dream house or apartment in sunny Spain.

We are your guide through the formalities. Not knowing the language will not be a barrier for you. Our team speaks not only Polish and Spanish, but also English, German and Italian.

We take an individual approach to each client. Our aim is not to sell an apartment in Spain at any price, but to satisfy the client. We know very well that this is often one of the most important financial decisions of their lives, so we are keen to ensure that it is properly chosen and considered. For some it will be houses for sale in Spain with sea views, and for others it will be flats in the city centre. We believe that a satisfied customer is more advertising than any campaign. And for us, this is the best proof that the work we do is of the highest quality.

What is our range of properties in Spain?

We focus on properties from the new build market – we have direct contact with the best developers in Spain. Properties in our offer are turnkey finished.

In Spain, new build apartments for sale are fully finished. You don't have to worry about expensive renovations and finding the right professionals. Properties from developers have tiled floors, painted walls and finished terraces. Also, the kitchen and bathroom are furnished and the bedrooms have built-in wardrobes. Depending on the offer, sometimes there is even white goods already in place. Usually only movable furniture has to be purchased. The price usually also includes a garage and a storage room.

What sets us apart from other real estate agencies?

First and foremost, we live here. Housing and apartments for sale in Spain are not our next project to add to our property portfolio. We are 100% focused on Spain, where we are very familiar with the industry – both the formalities and the unwritten rules. We know what surprises expats when dealing with Spaniards and we can guide you through this path. We focus on the Costa Blanca and Costa del Sol regions, which we can confidently call our home.

We carry out local expertise. We know the properties on offer not just from Wikipedia and online maps. We have been to the places where we offer houses and flats in person and are very familiar with the actual characteristics of the locations. Every customer is an individual story to us. Depending on whether he is looking for a property for investment or for his own use (or if he wants to combine both), we advise which location will be best for him.

What problems do we solve when buying a property in Spain?

Getting down to specifics. In addition to the actual purchase of a property in Spain, we also help our clients with organisational matters:

  • We help you book a hotel or flat before your first arrival.
  • We pick up from the airport or help with car rental.
  • We organise visits to selected properties.
  • We cooperate with interior designers.
  • We work with legal advisers and banking specialists who will help with the formalities necessary to buy a house in Spain (e.g. setting up an identification number for a foreigner or opening a Spanish bank account).
