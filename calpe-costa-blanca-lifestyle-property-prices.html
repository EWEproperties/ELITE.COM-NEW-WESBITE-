Calpe on the Costa Blanca – Lifestyle, Property Prices and Who Buys Here

By Lucyna Gacek · 16 March 2026 · updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: Calpe is a lively but relaxed town under the Peñón de Ifach, with two long beaches, a fishing port and a big international community. In September 2026 asking prices averaged about €3,600 per m² (idealista), around 13% higher than a year earlier, with flats near Arenal-Bol beach well above €5,000 per m². Seafront apartments, penthouses with terraces and sea-view villas on the hills are the most sought after.

Calpe sits between the mountains and the Mediterranean, guarded by the 332-metre Peñón de Ifach, the symbol of the Costa Blanca. It combines the feel of an old fishing town with everything you need for year-round living. Here is what life is like, what homes cost in 2026 and where to look.

Life in Calpe

Daily life revolves around the sea. Mornings start with coffee on the promenade, and in the late afternoon the fishing boats come in and the catch is auctioned at the port, where you can watch from a public gallery. Restaurants along the harbour serve that fish the same evening.

For nature, you can climb the Peñón de Ifach inside its natural park (in busy months you book the free entry online), walk round Las Salinas lagoon, where flamingos feed, or swim in the coves on the north side. The old town, with its painted steps and small squares, hosts markets and fiestas, including the Moors and Christians in October.

Calpe is busy in summer but calmer than nearby Benidorm, and it stays alive all year with shops, health centres, schools and a large international population.

Property prices in 2026

According to idealista, the average asking price in Calpe in September 2026 was about €3,600 per m², up around 13% on a year earlier. Flats averaged about €4,000 per m² and houses and villas about €3,250 per m².

  • Playa Arenal-Bol (town centre beach): the most expensive area, around €5,500 per m².
  • Calpe Pueblo (old town): around €3,100 per m².
  • Hillside villa areas, such as Oltamar and Cucarres: from about €2,800 per m².

As a rough guide, a two-bedroom flat near the beach costs €300,000–500,000, a sea-view apartment or penthouse €450,000–700,000, and a modern villa from around €800,000 up to several million. See our current properties for sale in Calpe.

Who buys in Calpe?

Calpe is one of the most international towns on the Costa Blanca. Buyers come mostly from the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, Scandinavia and the UK, alongside Spanish families from Madrid and Valencia who keep a second home here.

  • Second-home owners who want a beach town that works all year.
  • Retirees who like the flat seafront and services within walking distance.
  • Remote workers drawn by the climate and good internet.
  • Investors in holiday lets, although rules have tightened (see below).

Areas and beaches

  • Arenal-Bol: the main beach in the centre, with a promenade full of cafés and restaurants. Mainly apartments.
  • La Fossa (Levante): a long sandy beach at the foot of the Peñón, with high-rise sea-view apartments.
  • Old town and Las Salinas: local life, shops and newer developments around the lagoon.
  • Maryvilla, Empedrola, Canuta and Gran Sol: hillside villa areas with sea views, quieter but steeper, so a car is useful.

Buying to rent out: what to know

Since 2024 the Valencian Community has tightened the rules for holiday lets. Each tourist home needs a registration number, town halls can limit them by zone, and in an apartment building the owners' association can restrict or ban new ones. Before you buy for rental, check the town's current zoning, the building's statutes and whether the flat already has a valid licence. Our guide to short-term rental rules explains the details.

What is next for Calpe

The town is investing in public spaces, including the renewal of the La Fossa promenade, and is updating its urban plan to keep growth under control. New homes still come to the market, mainly around Las Salinas and in the hillside villa areas. Limited land between the sea, the Peñón and the mountains supports prices over the long term.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a property cost in Calpe?

In September 2026 the average asking price was about €3,600 per m². A two-bedroom flat near the beach typically costs €300,000–500,000, and a modern villa starts at around €800,000.

Is Calpe a good place to live all year?

Yes. It has shops, health centres, schools, a fishing port and a big international community, and it stays lively in winter.

Which is the most expensive area of Calpe?

The area around Arenal-Bol beach in the centre, at around €5,500 per m².

Can I rent out my flat in Calpe to tourists?

Possibly, but you need a tourist registration and the town's zoning and your building's rules must allow it. Check before you buy.
