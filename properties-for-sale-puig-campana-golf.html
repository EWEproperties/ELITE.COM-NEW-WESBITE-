The course

Golf at Puig Campana Golf

Puig Campana Golf is a 9-hole course in Finestrat, designed by Stirling & Martín. Its setting makes it special: you play with the Puig Campana mountain on one side and views of the Mediterranean on the other.

The club has a modern clubhouse with a restaurant, a café, a pro shop and changing rooms. For longer rounds, several 18-hole courses around Benidorm and Finestrat are a short drive away.

Living here

Why buy a property at Puig Campana Golf?

You live in a quieter area with mountain and sea views, while Benidorm, Finestrat and Villajoyosa are a short drive away, with shopping centres, international schools and restaurants.

The beaches and big shopping areas are about 10 minutes away by car, and Alicante-Elche airport about 45 minutes along the AP-7 motorway.

Puig Campana Golf: questions and answers

How much does a property cost near Puig Campana Golf?

Where is Puig Campana Golf?

In Finestrat, on the hills behind Benidorm. The beaches are about 10 minutes away and Alicante-Elche airport about 45 minutes by car.

What costs are there on top of the price?

For a new build you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty. For a resale home you pay 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million). Add about 1.5–2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer. We give you the exact figure for the home you like.
