The coast

North and south: two sides of one coast

The northern Costa Blanca, from Dénia and Jávea down to Altea and Benidorm, is greener and hillier, with rocky coves, mountains close to the sea and many villas with sea views. Prices are higher here, especially in Jávea, Moraira, Cumbre del Sol and Altea.

The southern Costa Blanca, from Alicante and Santa Pola to Torrevieja, Orihuela Costa and Pilar de la Horadada, is flatter and sunnier, with long sandy beaches, salt lakes and several golf resorts. This is where you find most new apartments and bungalows at more modest prices.

Alicante-Elche airport sits in the middle, so most towns are 20 to 60 minutes by car from the terminal, with direct flights to most European cities all year.

Questions

Questions and answers

Where is the best place to buy on the Costa Blanca?

It depends on how you will use the home. For sea views, coves and a quieter, greener coast, look at the north: Jávea, Moraira, Cumbre del Sol, Altea and Calpe. For long sandy beaches, golf and lower prices, look at the south: Torrevieja, Orihuela Costa and Guardamar. Alicante city suits buyers who want to live all year in a real city.

How much does a property cost on the Costa Blanca?

Our homes on the Costa Blanca start at €116,000 today. Most new apartments with 2 or 3 bedrooms in the south cost between €250,000 and €400,000, while most sea-view villas in the north cost between €1.4 and €2.5 million.

What taxes do I pay when I buy on the Costa Blanca?

On a new build you pay 10% VAT (IVA) and 1.4% stamp duty (AJD). On a resale home you pay transfer tax (ITP) of 9%, or 11% above €1 million. Add about 1.5 to 2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer.

Which airport is closest?

Alicante-Elche airport. Most Costa Blanca towns are 20 to 60 minutes away by car. For the far north, Valencia airport is also an option.
