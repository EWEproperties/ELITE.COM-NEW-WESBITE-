Luxury villas

Our luxury villas

On the Costa del Sol, most of our luxury villas are in Marbella, Benahavís, Estepona and Casares. On the Costa Blanca, they are in Jávea, Moraira, Cumbre del Sol, Altea and Calpe, where the coast is greener and the views are wide.

Questions

Questions and answers

What does a luxury villa cost in Spain?

Our luxury villas start at €1,025,000. On the Costa Blanca, most sea-view villas cost between €1.4 and €2.5 million. In Marbella and Benahavís, most villas cost between €3.4 and €8 million.

What taxes do I pay on a new luxury villa?

10% VAT (IVA) plus stamp duty (AJD) of 1.4% in the Valencian Community or 1.2% in Andalusia. On a resale villa you pay transfer tax instead: 11% above €1 million in the Valencian Community and 7% in Andalusia.
