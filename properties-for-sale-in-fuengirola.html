The city

Living in Fuengirola

Fuengirola is a lively seaside town of about 85,000 people on the Costa del Sol, between Málaga and Marbella. It has about 7 km of sandy beaches along a long seafront promenade, and Sohail Castle stands on a hill by the river at the western end of the town.

It is a practical place to live all year, with everything on foot: shops, markets, restaurants, schools and health centres. The local train runs along the coast to Málaga airport and the city of Málaga, so many people live here without a car.

Above the town, El Higuerón is a newer residential area on the hills, with modern homes and wide views over the sea. Málaga airport is about 25 minutes away by train or car, and Marbella about 30 minutes.

El Higuerón

A newer area on the hills above Fuengirola, with modern townhouses and villas with private pools and wide sea views.

Budget

What it costs to buy in Fuengirola

On top of the price you pay tax and fees. For a new home at €545,000, for example, that is €54,500 VAT and €6,540 stamp duty, plus notary, land registry and legal fees. Taxes on resale homes are different, so always ask us for the exact figure for the home you like.

Questions

Buying property in Fuengirola: questions and answers

How much does an apartment cost in Fuengirola?

Our apartments in Fuengirola start at €450,000 today, and most with 2 or 3 bedrooms cost between €450,000 and €800,000, depending on the views and the distance to the beach. Townhouses in El Higuerón cost from about €800,000, and a large villa with panoramic sea views €3.2 million.

Is Fuengirola good for living without a car?

Yes. Fuengirola is compact and flat near the sea, with shops and services on foot, and the local train runs to Málaga airport and Málaga city.

How far is Fuengirola from the airport?

Málaga airport is about 25 minutes away by car or by the local train.

What costs are there on top of the price?

In Andalusia you pay 10% VAT and 1.2% stamp duty on a new build, and 7% transfer tax on a resale home. Notary, land registry and legal fees add about 1.5–2.5%.

Can foreigners buy property in Fuengirola?

Yes. Anyone can buy property in Spain. You need a Spanish NIE tax number and usually a Spanish bank account, and we help you with both. See our guide to the NIE number.
