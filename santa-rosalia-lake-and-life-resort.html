Santa Rosalía Lake and Life Resort: A Buyer's Guide

By Anita Orłowska · 1 December 2024 · updated 25 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short: Santa Rosalía Lake and Life Resort is a gated residential resort in Torre-Pacheco, Murcia, on the Costa Cálida, about 5 minutes from Los Alcázares and the Mar Menor. Its centrepiece is a turquoise swimming lagoon of about 1.6 hectares with white sand beaches and two islands, built with Crystal Lagoons technology, set in a park of about 12 hectares. Owners have beaches, a beach bar, water sports, courts, an outdoor gym and adventure golf, plus 24-hour security. Homes range from apartments to villas with private gardens. Murcia airport is about 25 minutes away and Alicante about an hour.

Few developments in Spain offer a beach in the middle of the estate. Santa Rosalía does, and it has become one of the best-known resorts on the Costa Cálida. Here is what it offers, who it suits and what to check before buying.

Location

  • Between Los Alcázares and Torre-Pacheco in the Region of Murcia, about 5 km from the Mar Menor and the seven beaches of Los Alcázares.
  • About 25 minutes by car to Región de Murcia International Airport (Corvera) and a little over an hour to Alicante-Elche.
  • About 25 minutes to Cartagena, with its Roman theatre, port and shopping streets.

The Mar Menor is a shallow salt lagoon with warm, calm water. It has suffered serious water-quality problems in recent years, and large recovery plans are under way; the resort's own lagoon is independent of it.

The crystal lagoon

At the heart of the resort is a turquoise lagoon of about 1.6 hectares, built and run with Crystal Lagoons technology, which keeps the water clear with much less energy and chemicals than a conventional pool. White sand beaches face south, east and west, and two islands sit in the water, one with a beach bar. The lagoon and its gardens form a park of about 12 hectares, planted with palm trees. It was the largest lagoon of its kind in Europe when it opened.

Facilities for owners

  • Three beaches, a beach club and chill-out terraces
  • Kayaks, paddleboards and windsurfing on the lagoon (no motor boats)
  • Beach volleyball, multi-sports courts and an outdoor gym
  • Yoga and Pilates areas, picnic lawns and children's playgrounds
  • Adventure golf, disc golf, pétanque, chess and table tennis
  • Controlled access and 24-hour security

The facilities are for residents, and they are paid for through the community fees. Ask for the current fees before you reserve.

The homes

The resort is planned for well over a thousand homes, built in phases: apartments with large windows onto green avenues, townhouses and villas with private gardens and pools. Newer phases are still being built and sold, so both new and resale homes are available. Homes closest to the lagoon cost the most.

Who it suits, and what to check

Santa Rosalía suits families, buyers who want a holiday home with resort facilities and those who prefer a calm, secure setting over a town centre. It is less suited to those who want to walk to shops and restaurants every day.

  • Off-plan phases: insist on a bank guarantee for each payment. See new build or resale.
  • Community fees: the lagoon and security cost money; compare fees between phases.
  • Holiday letting: the Region of Murcia has its own tourist-rental register. Check the resort's rules too. See short-term rental rules.
  • Taxes: purchase taxes in Murcia differ from those in the Valencian Community and Andalusia. See how to buy in Spain.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Santa Rosalía Resort?

In Torre-Pacheco, Region of Murcia, between Los Alcázares and Torre-Pacheco, about 5 km from the Mar Menor.

How big is the lagoon?

About 1.6 hectares of water with white sand beaches and two islands, in a park of about 12 hectares.

Can anyone use the lagoon?

No. The lagoon and facilities are for residents and their guests, and they are funded through the community fees.

Which airport is closest?

Región de Murcia International Airport at Corvera, about 25 minutes by car. Alicante-Elche is a little over an hour away.

Are there homes for sale now?

Yes, both in newer phases and on the resale market. Contact us for current availability and prices.
