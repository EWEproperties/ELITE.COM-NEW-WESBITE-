The course

Golf at Bonalba Golf Resort

Bonalba is an 18-hole, par-72 course designed by Ramón Espinosa. It has wide fairways, large greens and well-placed bunkers, and five large lakes come into play on several holes.

Practice facilities include putting greens, an approach area, a practice bunker and a driving range, and the clubhouse restaurant looks out over the course.

Living here

Why buy a property at Bonalba Golf?

Bonalba combines a quiet golf setting with the city close by. Mutxamel, El Campello and Sant Joan d'Alacant have supermarkets, shops, restaurants, health centres and international services.

The beaches of El Campello and San Juan are about 10–15 minutes away by car, Alicante city centre about 20 minutes and Alicante-Elche airport about 25–30 minutes.

Questions

Bonalba Golf: questions and answers

How much does a property cost in Bonalba Golf?

Our properties in Bonalba start at €282,000 for an apartment. A penthouse over the course costs about €437,000, and villas from about €460,000.

Where is Bonalba Golf?

In Mutxamel, just north of Alicante, next to El Campello. The beaches are about 10–15 minutes away, Alicante city about 20 minutes and the airport about 25–30 minutes.

What costs are there on top of the price?

For a new build you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty. For a resale home you pay 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million). Add about 1.5–2.5% for the notary, land registry and lawyer. We give you the exact figure for the home you like.
