The city

Living in Vera

Vera is a town in the province of Almería, in Andalusia, a few kilometres inland from the sea. Its coast, Vera Playa, has long sandy beaches, a promenade and modern residential complexes, and is a popular place for buyers from all over Europe.

The area is dry and sunny for most of the year, with a mild winter. The old town of Vera has a church, squares and local shops, and the nearby villages of Mojácar and Garrucha add white houses, a fishing port and good restaurants.

Prices are lower than on the better known coasts, so many buyers find a new home near the beach on a modest budget. Almería airport is about an hour away by car, and Región de Murcia airport about an hour and a half.

Budget

What it costs to buy in Vera

On top of the price you pay tax and fees. For a new home at €280,000, for example, that is €28,000 VAT and €3,360 stamp duty, plus notary, land registry and legal fees. Taxes on resale homes are different, so always ask us for the exact figure for the home you like.

Questions

Buying property in Vera: questions and answers

How much does an apartment cost in Vera?

Our apartments in Vera start at €180,000 today, and most cost between €180,000 and €300,000. Bungalows cost from about €270,000, and villas with a private pool near the beach from about €430,000 to €475,000.

Can I rent out an apartment in Vera Playa?

Yes. Some of our apartments in Vera Playa already come with a tourist licence. Holiday letting in Andalusia needs registration in the regional tourist register, so check the licence before you buy.

How far is Vera from the airport?

Almería airport is about an hour by car, and Región de Murcia airport about an hour and a half.

What costs are there on top of the price?

In Andalusia you pay 10% VAT and 1.2% stamp duty on a new build, and 7% transfer tax on a resale home. Notary, land registry and legal fees add about 1.5–2.5%.

Can foreigners buy property in Vera?

Yes. Anyone can buy property in Spain. You need a Spanish NIE tax number and usually a Spanish bank account, and we help you with both. See our guide to the NIE number.
