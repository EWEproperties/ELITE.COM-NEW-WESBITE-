The city

Living in Ciudad Quesada

Ciudad Quesada is a large residential area in the municipality of Rojales, in the south of the Costa Blanca, about 10 km inland from the sea. It was one of the first places on the coast built for international buyers, and today it has a big community of people who live here all year.

The area has its own shops, restaurants and the La Marquesa golf course, and the old town of Rojales with its bridge and caves is a few minutes away. The sandy beaches of Guardamar and La Mata are about 15 minutes away by car.

Prices are lower than on the coast, so buyers get a villa with a pool and a garden for the price of an apartment by the sea. Alicante-Elche airport is about 35 minutes away by car, and Torrevieja about 15 minutes.

Where to buy

The main areas around Ciudad Quesada

Ciudad Quesada

The main residential area, with shops, restaurants and services. Villas, bungalows and apartments on quiet streets.

La Marquesa Golf

The golf course in the middle of Ciudad Quesada. Villas with golf views and a private pool.

Doña Pepa

The newer part of Ciudad Quesada, with modern villas and apartments and a shopping area close by.

Rojales town

The old town by the Segura river, with a historic bridge, caves and local life. Bungalows and villas nearby.

La Finca Golf, Algorfa

A golf resort about 10 minutes away, with a hotel and a course. Villas, semi-detached houses and bungalows.

Algorfa village

A small Spanish village near La Finca, with local shops and bars and villas on the edge of the village.

Budget

What it costs to buy in Ciudad Quesada

On top of the price you pay tax and fees. For a new home at €549,000, for example, that is €54,900 VAT and €7,686 stamp duty, plus notary, land registry and legal fees. Taxes on resale homes are different, so always ask us for the exact figure for the home you like.

Questions

Buying property in Ciudad Quesada: questions and answers

How much does a villa cost in Ciudad Quesada?

Our villas in Ciudad Quesada cost from about €394,000 to €1.2 million today, most of them with 3 or 4 bedrooms and a private pool. Apartments and bungalows start at €260,900. At La Finca Golf in Algorfa, villas cost from about €450,000 to €1.8 million.

Is Ciudad Quesada good for living all year?

Yes. Ciudad Quesada has a large international community who live here all year, with shops, restaurants, health centres and golf close by, and the coast about 15 minutes away.

How far is Ciudad Quesada from the airport?

Alicante-Elche airport is about 35 minutes by car, and Región de Murcia airport about 45 minutes.

What costs are there on top of the price?

In the Valencian Community you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty on a new build, and 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million) on a resale home. Notary, land registry and legal fees add about 1.5–2.5%.

Can foreigners buy property in Ciudad Quesada?

Yes. Anyone can buy property in Spain. You need a Spanish NIE tax number and usually a Spanish bank account, and we help you with both. See our guide to the NIE number.
