The city

Living in Cumbre del Sol

Cumbre del Sol is a residential area on the cliffs of Benitachell, on the northern Costa Blanca between Jávea and Moraira. It is known for sea views, quiet streets and modern villas built on the slopes above the Mediterranean.

Below the cliffs lie small coves with very clear water. The best known is Cala del Moraig, with a pebble beach, a sea cave and deep blue water. Paths along the coast lead to viewpoints and other quiet coves.

The area has its own restaurants, shops and an international school. For larger shops, hospitals and weekly markets, Jávea, Moraira and Teulada are 10 to 15 minutes away by car. Alicante airport is about 75 km away, around an hour by car, and Valencia airport about 100 km.

Questions

Buying property in Cumbre del Sol: questions and answers

How much does a villa cost in Cumbre del Sol?

Most villas with sea views we sell in Cumbre del Sol cost between €1.3 million and €3.1 million, depending on the size, the plot and the view. Our most exclusive villa is €6,057,000. Apartments start at €612,000.

Where is Cumbre del Sol?

Cumbre del Sol is in Benitachell (El Poble Nou de Benitatxell), on the northern Costa Blanca between Jávea and Moraira. Calpe is about 12 km away and Benidorm about 30 km.

Is Cumbre del Sol good for living all year?

Yes. Many owners live here all year or for long periods. The area has restaurants, shops and an international school, and Jávea, Moraira and Teulada have hospitals, larger shops and markets 10 to 15 minutes away.

How far is Cumbre del Sol from the airport?

Alicante-Elche airport is about 75 km away, around an hour by car. Valencia airport is about 100 km away.

What costs are there on top of the price?

For a new build you pay 10% VAT and 1.4% stamp duty. For a resale home you pay 9% transfer tax (11% above €1 million). Notary, land registry and legal fees add about 1.5–2.5%.

Can foreigners buy property in Cumbre del Sol?

Yes. Anyone can buy property in Spain. You need a Spanish NIE tax number and usually a Spanish bank account, and we help you with both. See our guide to the NIE number.
