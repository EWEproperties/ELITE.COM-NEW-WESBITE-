The Best-Paid Jobs in Spain

By Aleksandra Kobus · 3 June 2025 · updated 24 October 2026 · 4 min read

In short: The average gross salary in Spain was €29,540 a year in 2024. Managers and directors earn the most, around €63,900 on average, followed by people in energy, finance and insurance. Pay is highest in the Basque Country and Madrid. Newcomers may also pay a flat 24% income tax for up to six years under the so-called Beckham Law.

Some people buy in Spain to rent out or to retire. Others come for work. If you are thinking of a career move, here is which jobs pay best, how much people earn by region, and what else to weigh up before you go.

Medicine and finance

Doctors are among the best-paid professionals in Spain, above all specialists such as surgeons, anaesthetists and radiologists. Dentists and orthodontists in private practice also earn well. As in most of Europe, doctors can work in the public health system, in private clinics, or in both.

In finance, the top earners are chief financial officers (CFOs), who answer for a company's stability, followed by experienced financial analysts and advisers. According to Spain's statistics office (INE), finance and insurance was the second best-paid sector in 2024, with an average of €51,863 a year.

Management and technology

The single best-paid group in the INE figures is managers and directors, with an average of €63,865 a year in 2024. That is more than double the national average. Chief executives, operations directors and supply chain managers in industry are in this group, and their pay rises steadily with experience.

Technology pays well too. Besides IT directors, experienced data analysts, cybersecurity specialists and software engineers are in demand, and many work remotely for companies outside Spain.

Other well-paid jobs

Other professions with good salaries include:

  • Engineers, especially in design, construction and energy. Electricity and gas supply was the best-paid sector in 2024, at €57,932 a year on average.
  • Lawyers, above all those working in international or Spanish property and tax law.
  • Architects, who are busy with the steady flow of new developments on the coast.

Some trades are short of workers even if the pay is lower, for example mechanics and maintenance technicians. At the other end of the scale, hospitality is the lowest-paid sector, at €17,653 a year on average.

Salaries by region

Where you work makes a big difference. In 2024 the highest average salaries were in:

  • the Basque Country: €35,170
  • the Madrid region: €34,410
  • Navarre: €32,605

The lowest were in Extremadura (€24,979) and the Canary Islands (€25,120). The national average was €29,540 gross a year, 5.3% more than in 2023. The minimum wage in 2026 is €1,221 a month, paid 14 times a year, or €17,094 a year.

Tax rules that help newcomers

Two rules are worth knowing if you move to Spain for work:

  • The Beckham Law (the special regime for people moving to Spain). If you have not been tax resident in Spain in the previous five years, you can pay a flat 24% on Spanish employment income up to €600,000. It applies in the year you arrive and the five years after. You have to apply for it, so speak to a tax adviser early.
  • The digital nomad visa. People from outside the EU who work remotely for foreign companies can live in Spain with this visa. You need to earn at least twice the minimum wage, about €2,850 a month in 2026. EU citizens do not need a visa.

Pay is not everything

Many people choose Spain for the way of life as much as for the salary:

  • Time off. By law, employees get at least 30 calendar days of paid holiday a year, up to 14 public holidays, and 15 days off when they marry.
  • Climate. Alicante and Málaga get close to 3,000 hours of sunshine a year, so you can spend your free time outdoors almost all year.
  • Pace of life. Long lunches, evenings outside and time with family and friends are part of everyday life.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average salary in Spain?

In 2024 the average gross salary was €29,540 a year, according to the INE. Men earned €32,058 on average and women €26,905.

Which job pays the most in Spain?

Managers and directors earn the most, around €63,900 a year on average. Specialist doctors, CFOs, engineers in the energy sector and senior IT professionals are also among the top earners.

Which region pays the highest salaries?

The Basque Country (€35,170 a year on average) and the Madrid region (€34,410), followed by Navarre.

What is the minimum wage in Spain in 2026?

€1,221 gross a month, paid 14 times a year. That is €17,094 a year.

When is a good time to move?

Many people move at the peak of their career, when their experience lets them negotiate a strong salary and their savings let them buy a home outright. Others bring a remote job with them and enjoy a foreign salary with Spanish living costs.
